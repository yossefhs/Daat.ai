The Issachar & Zevulun model

In the Torah, two brothers struck a pact. Issachar sat in the tent of study. Zevulun went out to trade — and supported his brother materially. The merit of the Torah being learned is shared equally between them.

Today, thousands of pages of halakha need to be studied, translated, brought online, explained by the Daat AI. All of this costs money — hosting, research, translation, content, AI — but all of it can be given freely to talmidim if Zevuluns choose to carry their share.

By supporting DAAT, you are not making a donation. You are taking your seat in the study.

שאלות נפוצות

Frequently asked questions

Is the donation tax-deductible?
Yes, for French taxpayers. The association is a registered loi 1901 non-profit of general interest. 66 % of your donation is deductible from income tax, up to 20 % of taxable income. HelloAsso sends the tax receipt automatically. For donations via Qonto, the receipt is sent on request to contact@daattorah.com. For other countries, please check local rules.
How can I cancel or modify a monthly donation?
Anytime. For monthly donations via Qonto, email us at contact@daattorah.com with "Cancel Tomeit Daat" and we'll stop the debit within 5 days. For HelloAsso, log in to your HelloAsso donor space and disable the recurring payment directly.
What's the difference between Qonto and HelloAsso?
Qonto is our association bank account — direct card payment with no intermediary or commission. HelloAsso is the leading French platform for non-profit donations — it handles tax receipts automatically and offers an optional tip (never required) that funds their service. Both transfer 100 % of the donation to DAAT.
Can I dedicate a study after donating?
Yes. Email us at contact@daattorah.com with the reference of your donation (date, amount, platform) and the dedication text. We'll add it to the corresponding Siman page within 48h.
How does my donation fund the Daat AI?
Each question asked to Daat triggers a call to the Claude API (Anthropic), costing about €0.50 on average. A €18 donation funds ~36 free conversations offered to other talmidim. Beyond the AI, your donation also covers hosting (Vercel), the database and the work of writing the halakhic content.
