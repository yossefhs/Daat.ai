## Week 1 — Siman 247 · Having a package delivered on Shabbos
**Article:** https://daattorah.com/blog/livraison-colis-shabbat-en.html · **Visual:** assets/img/og/siman-247.svg

### 🔵 LinkedIn — Tue 9am
> **Can you hand a package to a carrier on Friday, knowing it will be in transit during Shabbos?**
>
> The question feels modern — Amazon, USPS, FedEx — yet the Shulchan Aruch already settles it in Siman 247.
>
> Three cumulative conditions to entrust a letter or item to a non-Jew before Shabbos:
> • **ketzitzah (קציצה)** — a price agreed in advance
> • **kvi'us (קביעות)** — a fixed, established collection/delivery service
> • the **time margin** for the non-Jew to set out before Shabbos begins
>
> The principle that lights up the whole sugya: **adata d'nafshei (אדעתא דנפשיה)** — the non-Jew acts in *his own* interest (for his wage), not as your agent on Shabbos.
>
> 👉 The full study: https://daattorah.com/blog/livraison-colis-shabbat-en.html
> *This is not a halachic ruling — ask your Rav.*
>

## Week 2 — Siman 318 · Reheating food on Shabbos
**Article:** https://daattorah.com/blog/rechauffer-plat-shabbat-en.html · **Visual:** assets/img/og/siman-318.svg

### 🔵 LinkedIn — Tue 9am
> **Friday night's dish has gone cold. May you put it back on the hot plate on Shabbos?**
>
> Cooking (bishul / מבשל) is one of the 39 melachot. But a food that is **solid, fully cooked, and then cooled** is not "re-cooked" — the principle **ein bishul achar bishul (אין בישול אחר בישול)**, "there is no cooking after cooking."
>
> Everything then turns on three parameters:
> • the food (solid vs. liquid)
> • the heat source (hot plate, fire, hot water)
> • the vessel — **kli rishon** (was on the fire, still cooks) vs. **kli sheni** (transferred, cooks less)
> …and on the threshold **yad soledet bo (יד סולדת בו)** (the hand recoils, often estimated around 45°C).
>
> 👉 The full study: https://daattorah.com/blog/rechauffer-plat-shabbat-en.html
> *This is not a halachic ruling — ask your Rav.*

## Week 4 — Siman 326 · Showering / bathing on Shabbos
**Article:** https://daattorah.com/blog/se-doucher-laver-shabbat-en.html · **Visual:** assets/img/og/siman-326.svg

### 🔵 LinkedIn — Tue 9am
> **May you take a shower on Shabbos?**
>
> Siman 326 draws a sharp line between **hot water** and **cold water**:
> • Washing the **whole body** (and even limb by limb) with water **heated by fire** — even heated before Shabbos → forbidden.
> • Washing **face, hands, feet** → permitted.
> • **Cold water** is treated far more leniently.
>
> Hence the question of the **modern shower** (water heater = water heated by fire), plus the issue of wringing out hair.
>

## Week 5 — Siman 261 · What time to light Shabbos candles
**Article:** https://daattorah.com/blog/bougies-shabbat-horaire-allumage-en.html · **Visual:** assets/img/og/siman-261.svg

### 🔵 LinkedIn — Tue 9am
> **At exactly what time do you light Shabbos candles?**
>
> Siman 261 goes to the very heart of the onset of Shabbos:
> • **bein hashmashot (בין השמשות)** — twilight, a moment of *doubt* (day or night?) in which one no longer does melachah.
> • **tosefet Shabbat (תוספת שבת)** — the mitzvah of **adding** mundane time to the sacred, i.e., accepting Shabbos *before* nightfall.
> • Hence the widespread custom of lighting **~18 minutes before sunset** (times vary by community and locale).
>

## Week 6 — Siman 319 · Sorting food (borer) on Shabbos
**Article:** https://daattorah.com/blog/trier-aliments-borer-shabbat-en.html · **Visual:** assets/img/og/siman-319.svg

### 🔵 LinkedIn — Tue 9am
> **Making a salad, sorting cutlery: may you do it on Shabbos?**
>
> **Borer (בורר)** — sorting — is a Torah-level melachah. But sorting *to eat* remains possible under **three cumulative conditions**:
> • **okhel mitokh psolet (אוכל מתוך פסולת)** — take what you want (the food), not the waste
> • **b'yad (ביד)** — by hand, with no sorting tool (sieve, strainer, etc.)
> • **le'altar (לאלתר)** — for **immediate** consumption, not for later (sorting to store, le-otzar, makes one liable)
>

> • Perfume raises its own questions depending on the product and how it's applied.
