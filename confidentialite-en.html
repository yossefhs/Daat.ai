Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, for how long, and how to delete your account. Applies to the website, the Daat assistant and the mobile app.

In one page

Daat Torah collects little, and only to make the service work: an email address if you create an account, your questions in order to answer them, your progress so you can find it again. We sell nothing to anyone and show no advertising, and you can delete your account yourself, from the app or on simple request.

This policy applies to the website daattorah.com, the Daat AI assistant and the Daat Torah mobile app (iOS and Android).

Last updated: 2 November 2026.

The details

Who is responsible for processing

Daat Torah (DAAT), a halakha study project founded and led by Rav Yossef Haim Samama. For any question about your data: Daattorah.com@gmail.com. The website and API are hosted on Vercel; data is stored in a Redis database at Upstash.

What we collect, and when

  • Without an account. An anonymous identifier (the daat_guest_id cookie on the website, an installation identifier in the app) used only to count free questions. Your preferences (language, study level, local history) stay in your browser or device. Technical counters tied to your IP address limit abuse; they erase themselves within 35 days at most.
  • With an account. Your email address (sign-in uses a one-time code sent by email: there is no password), creation and last sign-in dates, your conversations with the assistant so you can find them across devices, your study progress and personal study plan, and your question counters (daily and monthly).
  • If you support the project. On the website, donations go through HelloAsso, which passes on your email, the form chosen, the amount and the date. In the app, plans are purchased through Apple's App Store or Google Play, which confirm to us the product, start and expiry dates and a transaction identifier, tied to your account email. In both cases, no banking details ever reach us.
  • Newsletter and notifications. Your email if you subscribe to the study newsletter, with one-click unsubscribe in every message. For notifications, the technical subscription address provided by your browser or device, and your language; no email.
  • What you write to us. The content of your error reports and feedback, with your email if you provide it; and, if you look for a study partner (khavroutha), the form details (name, city, email, WhatsApp), to put you in touch.

The AI assistant and your questions

To answer you, the text of your question and the thread of the conversation are sent to language-model providers: Anthropic (Claude) for the answers, and DeepSeek for light tasks (greetings, summarising the older turns of a long conversation). Source-text requests are sent to the Sefaria library. We send them neither your email address nor your identity: only the text of the exchange leaves.

Questions asked are also written to our technical usage log, with the account identifier, to track costs and spot abuse. This log keeps only the last 500 entries and then erases the oldest.

The assistant is a study tool: do not entrust it with sensitive personal information, yours or anyone else's.

Why we use them

  • Providing the requested service (answering, syncing, giving access to the chosen plan): performance of the contract.
  • Protecting the service against abuse and fraud (question limits, code-sending limits): legitimate interest.
  • Sending you the newsletter and notifications: your consent, withdrawable at any time.
  • Keeping donation records: legal and accounting obligation.

No automated decision with legal effects, no advertising profiling.

For how long

  • Sign-in code: 10 minutes. Session: 30 days without signing in again.
  • Usage and abuse counters: from 24 hours to 35 days, then automatic erasure.
  • Account, conversations, progress, study plan: as long as your account exists.
  • Technical usage log: the last 500 entries.
  • Donations and payments: the period required by accounting law.

Where the data goes: our providers

  • Vercel — hosting, server functions and cookieless audience measurement.
  • Upstash — database.
  • Resend — sending sign-in emails and the newsletter.
  • Anthropic and DeepSeek — the assistant's language models.
  • Sefaria — source texts.
  • HelloAsso — donations on the website.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play, where applicable with their subscription-management intermediary — in-app purchases.

Some of these providers are established outside the European Union. We send them only what is strictly necessary for their role. We do not sell, rent or share your data for commercial or advertising purposes.

Cookies and local storage

  • daat_session — your signed-in session (HttpOnly, 30 days).
  • daat_guest_id — your anonymous identifier for free questions (1 year).
  • Browser or device local storage — language, level, local conversation history, offline progress.
  • Audience measurement — Vercel Web Analytics, with no cookie or persistent identifier, from aggregated data.

No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers.

Your rights and how to exercise them

You may access your data, correct it, have it erased, request a copy, object to processing or withdraw consent.

  • Delete your account — in the app, from the account settings; on the website, on request to Daattorah.com@gmail.com. Deletion erases your account, your plan, your counters, your conversations, your progress, your study plan, your newsletter subscription and your khavroutha profile, and removes your address from the usage log. Only donation records are kept for the period required by law.
  • Newsletter — unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email.
  • Notifications — disable them in your browser or device.
  • Copy of your data — on request by email, you receive your conversations and your progress in a readable format.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you may contact the data-protection authority of your country (in France, the CNIL).

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Sign-in relies on a one-time code, with no password to store. Sessions are carried by an HttpOnly cookie or a signed token. Administration access is restricted and logged.

Minors

Daat Torah is aimed at a study audience and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data without parental consent, write to us: we will erase it.

Changes

The date at the top of the page indicates the latest version. A significant change is announced on the website before it takes effect.

A question about your data?

Write to us: we answer personally, and we erase on request.
