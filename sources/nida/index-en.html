Hilkhot Nida — Taharas haMishpacha

The laws of family purity: hefsek tahara, the seven clean days, harchakos, chafifah and tevila. Study in 4 levels, with the nossei keilim — Shach (ש״ך), Taz (ט״ז), Pithei Teshuva — and the Daat HaRav (Chabad) level.

The Hilkhot Nida (taharas hamishpacha) are, within Yoreh De'ah, the heart of Jewish married life: hefsek tahara, the seven clean days, the harchakos, chafifah and tevila. The study follows the order of the Mehaber, with the Shach, Taz and Pithei Teshuva, and a Daat HaRav (Chabad) level.
