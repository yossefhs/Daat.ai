Siman 103 — A Taste That Spoils (Notein Taam Lifgam) Does Not Forbid the Mixture

1. The axiom: a taste that spoils does not forbid — notein taam lifgam

The starting point:

Siman 103 states a great principle of issour ve-heter: any forbidden substance whose taste is spoiled (טעמו פגום) does not forbid its mixture. And even if the forbidden substance is not spoiled in itself — it is savory and excellent on its own — but it spoils the mixture into which it fell → it is permitted (seif 1). The source (Taz s.k. 1) is derived from the verse of the גר: "לֹא תֹאכְלוּ כָל נְבֵלָה לַגֵּר אֲשֶׁר בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָ תִּתְּנֶנָּה" — only a נבילה fit for the גר (edible) is called נבילה; a taste that spoils is like an inedible נבילה.
CaseTaste imparted to the mixtureResult
The prohibition spoils the mixture (טעם לפגם)Spoiled tastePermitted (seif 1)
The prohibition improves the mixture (טעם לשבח)Savory tasteForbidden (unless nullified by 60)
Davar hashouv (בריה) not spoiled in itselfSpoils the food, yet importantNot nullified even in a thousand (Rama)
💡 The marker: this siman no longer speaks of "60 / nullification" as the previous simanim, but of the quality of the taste: שבח (improvement) → forbidden; פגם (spoilage) → permitted. The נטל״פ need not even spoil completely; spoiling a little (פוגם קצת) suffices (seif 2). But the davar hashouv (בריה and the like, Rama) that is not spoiled in itself is not nullified, even in a thousand.

2. The 3 reflex questions

■ DOES IT SPOIL? (פוגם / משביח) — does the forbidden substance impart to the mixture a spoiled taste (פגם → permitted) or an improved / neutral taste (שבח → forbidden)? Even spoiling a little suffices to permit (seif 1-2).
↓ we look at the nature of the prohibition
■ TASTE ALONE OR SUBSTANCE? (גוף האיסור) — is there only the taste of the prohibition (טעמו בלבד)? Then permitted if it spoils a little, even a majority prohibition. But if there is its substance (ממש) and it is the majority or מחצה → a complete פגם is required (seif 2). The ציר (brine) counts as the substance (Taz s.k. 3).
↓ we look at the duration of the פגם
■ START TO FINISH? (מתחילתו ועד סופו) — does it spoil without interruption? Yes → permitted. But if it improved then spoiled (השביח ולבסוף פוגם) or spoiled then improved (פוגם ולבסוף השביח) → forbidden (seif 2).

⚖ The pot / the spoon / the יורה (Rama seif 2)

Even if the prohibition is נ״ט לפגם and the food is permitted, the pot (קדירה) remains forbidden: if within 24h one cooks in it a 2nd food that the 1st prohibition improves, the 2nd food is forbidden for lack of 60 against the 1st prohibition. But if one stirred the 1st food with a spoon (כף) and then plunged the spoon into a 2nd food that it also spoils → the pot is not forbidden (this is נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא, Taz s.k. 6). Likewise a thing with no taste at all, such as the יורה in which honey is melted, even though bees' legs (רגלי הדבורים) are found in it → the cauldron is not forbidden.

3. The master grid: degree of the פגם, majority and the pot

An absolute must to memorize. Basis: Mehaber seifim 1-2, read with the Rama, the Taz (s.k. 1-6) and the Shach (s.k. 1-3).

SituationDecisive criterionResult
Prohibition that spoils the mixture, even a little Spoiled taste (start to finish) 🟢 Permitted (נטל״פ)
Small amount of prohibition, abundant היתר, spoils a little פוגם קצת 🟢 Permitted
Prohibition with substance, majority or מחצה על מחצה Is a complete פגם needed? 🔴 Forbidden until complete פגם (inedible)

Davar hashouv (בריה) not spoiled in itself Importance (Rama seif 1) 🔴 Not nullified even in a thousand
📌 Key reading: in נטל״פ, first ask does the prohibition spoil (a little suffices). Then distinguish taste alone (permitted even as a majority) from majority substance (a complete פגם is needed). And look at the duration: it must spoil start to finish, otherwise → forbidden.

4. The 5 golden rules

  1. נותן טעם לפגם מותר. A prohibition that imparts to the mixture only a spoiled taste does not forbid it (source: the נבילה fit for the גר, Taz s.k. 1) — seif 1.
  2. A little suffices, but not the majority with substance. פוגם קצת permits; but a majority prohibition (or מחצה) with substance requires a complete פגם; taste alone → permitted even as a majority (seif 2).
  3. מתחילתו ועד סופו. The פגם must last from start to finish; משביח ולבסוף פוגם (or the reverse) → forbidden (seif 2).
  4. בת יומה / אינה בת יומה (24h). A pot not used for 24h gives a spoiled taste and does not forbid — provided it is rinsed (no fat בעין on its surface); 60 against the stuck-on residue suffices (seif 5).
  5. דבר חריף משוי לשבח. A sharp body (peppers / spices) "improves" the spoiled taste → nullifies the נטל״פ and forbids (seif 6); and in bassar be-halav the water heated within 24h becomes נבילה (חנ״נ, seif 7).

5. Mnemonic — the "PAGUM" memory aid

"PAGUM" — after the פגם, the heart of the siman
  • Pogem (spoils): a spoiled taste does not forbid — even פוגם קצת suffices.
  • Amount of substance (ממשות): majority substance → complete פגם required; taste alone → permitted even as a majority.
  • Goes start to finish: it must spoil מתחילתו ועד סופו, otherwise forbidden.
  • Utensil not בת יומה: 24h → spoiled taste; rinsed + 60 against the stuck-on residue.
  • Makes sharp (חריף): sharpness "improves" → forbidden (seif 6).
The ladder of the pot and the taste (seif 2-6)
  • קדירה (pot) → forbidden even if the food is permitted (Rama)
  • כף → קדירה that also spoils → נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא: not forbidden
  • יורה of honey + bees' legs → no taste: not forbidden
  • אינה בת יומה (24h) → spoiled taste → permitted (rinsed + 60 against the stuck-on)
  • דבר חריף in the pot → the sharpness improves → forbidden

6. The 4 classic pitfalls

❌ Pitfall 1 — Thinking "a little suffices" always: פוגם קצת permits when a small amount of prohibition falls into abundant permitted food. But yesh mi she'omer: an abundant prohibition in a reduced permitted food, and even half-and-half (מחצה על מחצה), is not permitted by נטל״פ — a complete פגם (inedible) is required. Except if there is no substance, only the taste (טעמו בלבד) → there, permitted even as a majority (seif 2).
❌ Pitfall 2 — Forgetting "מתחילתו ועד סופו": it is not enough that the prohibition ends up spoiling. If it improved then spoiled (השביח ולבסוף פוגם) — or spoiled then improved (פוגם ולבסוף השביח) — it is forbidden (Taz s.k. 5 example: vinegar of yayin nesech in groats). The פגם is valid only if it is continuous. And the חוכך (Taz s.k. 4): if one benefits more from the quantity it added than what it spoils in taste → forbidden.
❌ Pitfall 3 — The pot, the spoon and the honey: even when the food is permitted (נטל״פ), the pot remains forbidden and a 2nd food improved within 24h is forbidden for lack of 60. But a spoon (כף) that passes from a spoiled food to another food that it also spoils is נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא → permitted; and a thing with no taste at all, such as the honey יורה with bees' legs, is not forbidden. Do not confuse the pot (forbidden) with the spoon / יורה (not forbidden).
❌ Pitfall 4 — אינה בת יומה without rinsing, and the חריף: a pot אינה בת יומה (past 24h since the prohibition was cooked in it) "spoils" and does not forbid — provided it is rinsed (מודחת), with no fat בעין on its surface (the בעין never spoils, Taz s.k. 8). And beware of the דבר חריף: a sharp body (peppers) in a pot that is not בת יומה "improves" (לשבח) the spoiled taste → everything is forbidden (seif 6, ruled by the Beit Yossef).

For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.

7. Recap of the 7 seifim — one line each

SeifTopicThe essential
1Notein taam lifgam permittedAny forbidden substance whose taste is spoiled does not forbid its mixture; even if it is savory in itself but spoils the mixture into which it fell → permitted. Rama: a davar hashouv (בריה and the like) not spoiled in itself is not nullified even in a thousand (או״ה ל״ב).
2Degree of the פגם; majority; duration; potNo need to spoil completely: פוגם קצת suffices. Yesh mi she'omer: a majority prohibition (or מחצה) with substance → a complete פגם is required; but taste alone → permitted even as a majority. Yesh she'choHekh: benefit from the quantity → forbidden. All this start to finish; השביח ולבסוף פוגם (or the reverse) → forbidden. Rama: the pot is forbidden; spoon = נ״ט בר נ״ט permitted; honey יורה = not forbidden.
3Spoiling with the help of salt / spicesEven if the prohibition alone has no power to spoil, but it spoils with the concurrence of salt or ground spices (abundant) in the pot → it is nevertheless permitted.
4Oil and honey of a non-JewThe oil (שמן) and honey (דבש) of a non-Jew, even cooked, are permitted: meat spoils the oil and makes it stink, and likewise the honey. Rama: י״א that meat does not spoil the honey itself, only a beverage made from honey (Maharil); where there is no great loss, be strict. Meat or milk in wine = נטל״פ → permitted.
5Pot that is אינה בת יומה (24h)A pot not "of its day": its taste is deemed spoiled → it does not forbid. בת יומה as long as 24h (מעת לעת) have not passed since the prohibition was cooked; afterward the food is permitted (נטל״פ), provided it is rinsed (no fat בעין on its surface, which would be like a piece of unspoiled prohibition). Yesh matirin even without rinsing. Rama: 60 against the stuck-on → all permit; such is the custom.
6Sharp body (peppers) in a pot not בת יומהYesh mi she'omer: if one put peppers (פלפלין) in a pot of prohibition that is not בת יומה, everything is forbidden, because its sharpness (חורפיה) "improves" (לשבח) and nullifies the פגם (ruled by the Beit Yossef).
7Pot of meat and milk + water; חתיכה נעשית נבילהA pot saturated with meat and milk (together or one after the other) that, before a night passed, was used to heat water → deemed בת יומה until 24h since the heating of the water. Rama: but if a night passed → permitted; likewise if a night passed between cooking the meat and the milk; and likewise for all prohibitions per חתיכה נעשית נבילה (cf. siman 92); and in case of loss one may permit for other prohibitions, provided 24h have passed since the prohibition was cooked.

⚖ The reflex in 3 questions

  1. Does it spoil? Spoiled taste → permitted (נטל״פ), even a little; improved taste → forbidden.
  2. Taste alone or majority substance? Taste alone → permitted even as a majority; majority substance → a complete פגם is needed; and it must spoil start to finish.
  3. Pot? בת יומה? חריף? The pot is forbidden but the spoon / יורה are not; אינה בת יומה (24h) rinsed → permitted; a sharp body improves → forbidden.
For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.
