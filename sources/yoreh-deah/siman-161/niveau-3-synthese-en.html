1. The axiom: forbidding and recovering are two questions

The whole siman rests on one distinction. A transaction may be forbidden without the court taking back what it yielded. Forbidding belongs to the Torah or the Sages; taking back belongs to a positive commandment, and has conditions of its own. Never read an answer from the first column as though it held for the second.

The sentence that announces it is the first of the siman, and it bears on the smallest case:

« בפחות משוה פרוטה יש איסור רבית אבל אין מוציאין אותו בדיינין » (שו״ע יו״ד קס״א:א)
A prohibition that is not recovered is not half a prohibition. Seif 2 says so expressly of dust of interest: the court does not take it back, and whoever would discharge his duty toward Heaven must return it.

2. The three reflex questions

Before any case in the block of interest, three questions in this order and in no other.

#The questionWhat it decides
1Does what is paid pay for waiting? (אגר נטר)If so, it is interest — whatever the wrapper. If not, the rest does not arise.
2Is it by way of a loan, and for a sum fixed at the time of the loan?Both together: interest of the Torah. One missing: dust of interest, rabbinic.
3Did the borrower give willingly?This decides the recovery of dust of interest (seifim 3 and 4) and what is accepted from the penitent (seif 7).
« אָמַר רַב נַחְמָן: כְּלָלָא דְרִיבִּיתָא, כֹּל אֲגַר נְטַר לֵיהּ – אָסוּר » (בבא מציעא ס״ג ע״ב)
« רבית דאורייתא שהוא בדרך הלואה בדבר קצוב יוצאה בדיינים » (שו״ע יו״ד קס״א:ה)

3. Master table — the eleven seifim clause by clause

SeifWhat it decidesThe governing word
1The prohibition bears on anything, down below a peruta — but it is not extracted. The gloss: whatever is a wage for waiting is forbidden; by way of sale it is only dust of interest.כל דבר · אגר נטר
2Dust of interest does not come out through the judges; but one who would discharge his duty toward Heaven must return it. The gloss excepts interest paid in advance or afterwards.לצאת ידי שמים
3If the borrower seized from the lender’s property, it is taken back from him.תפס לוה
4The rule of seif 2 holds only where the lender consumed it with the consent of the borrower. Coercion or an erroneous ruling: it is recovered.מדעת הלוה
5Torah interest — loan and fixed sum — is recovered: coercion of the person to the utmost, but no seizure of property. Likewise for free dwelling in the pledged courtyard.בדבר קצוב
6The lender having died, the sons owe nothing — except an identifiable object, and if the father had repented.דבר מסויים
7One who comes of his own accord: an identifiable object is accepted; otherwise, if interest was his trade, it is not accepted, to open repentance to him.לפתוח לו דרך לתשובה
8Paid in five measures of wheat for a dinar: he returns the five measures, or their price — for the sale stands despite the prohibition.שהמקח קיים
9Paid with a cloak or a vessel: he returns the object, because it is identifiable.גלימא או כלי
10A rental paid a dinar when it was worth half: the whole dinar is taken back.כל הדינר
11A deed carrying interest: the principal alone, provided the interest is recognizable — and the deed is torn. Principal and interest merged: nothing, not even the principal.שיהא ניכר

5. יוצאה בדיינים — a mitzva, not a debt

The seif says two things together, and their assembly is the doctrine.

« שהיו כופין ומכין אותו עד שתצא נפשו אבל אין בית דין יורדין לנכסיו » (שו״ע יו״ד קס״א:ה)
The Beit Yosef, in the name of the Rashba and the Ran, explains: this is not the execution of a debt, it is the coercion a court applies to bring about the fulfilment of a positive commandment.
« דהא דאמרינן יוצאה בדיינים היינו לומר דכופין אותו משום מ״ע דוחי אחיך עמך ומכין אותו עד שתצא נפשו כדי שיקיים המצוה אבל אין ב״ד יורדין לנכסיו » (בית יוסף יו״ד קס״א)
« כדין שאר מצות עשה דכתיב וחי אחיך עמך » (ש״ך יו״ד קס״א ס״ק ז)
Three consequences to remember. (1) No seizure of property. (2) According to the Taz, a claim by the borrower is required — disputed by the Panim Meirot and the Knesset HaGedola. (3) The Shach bounds the immunity: a lender who returned and then seized by force is a thief, and there one does descend into his property.
« נראה דאם כבר החזיר הרבית על פי ב״ד או מעצמו ואח״כ חזר ותפס משל לוה בעד הרבית יורדין לנכסיו » (ש״ך יו״ד קס״א ס״ק ח)

6. דעת הלוה — the thread linking seifim 2, 3, 4 and 7

A single word runs through four seifim: what the borrower willed.

  • Seif 2 — dust of interest is not recovered.
  • Seif 4 — and here is why: because the lender consumed it מדעת הלוה. Remove the consent, and it is recovered.
  • Seif 3 — but that consent was given once for all: the borrower may not go back on it by helping himself.
  • Seif 7 — and it is again what distinguishes interest from theft before the penitent: what was received with another’s consent is returned only in kind.
« לא אמרו דאבק רבית אינה יוצאה בדיינים אלא בשאכל המלוה מדעת הלוה » (שו״ע יו״ד קס״א:ד)
« וטעם הדבר דרבית כיון דבא לידו מדעת הלוה בעצמו אין לקבל ממנו אלא בדבר מסוים משא״כ בגזלה » (נקודות הכסף יו״ד קס״א)
Remember the Nekudot HaKesef’s formulation: theft and interest are not restored by the same rule, because interest came into his hand with the consent of the one who gave it.

7. דבר מסויים — the heir and the penitent

Two seifim, one word, and one must know which of the two regimes applies.

« לקח רבית קצוצה ומת אין הבנים צריכים להחזיר אלא אם כן היה דבר מסויים כגון פרה וטלית ועשה אביהם תשובה ולא הספיק להחזיר עד שמת » (שו״ע יו״ד קס״א:ו)
« אם בא לעשות תשובה מעצמו להחזיר הרבית אם הוא דבר מסויים מקבלים ממנו » (שו״ע יו״ד קס״א:ז)
WhoIdentifiable objectNot identifiable
The sons, after the lender’s deathThey return it — if the father had repented without managing toThey owe nothing
The lender himself, coming of his own accordIt is accepted from himIt is not accepted, if interest was his trade — to open repentance to him

« אבל עומד במרדו מוציאין ממנו בע » (ש״ך יו״ד קס״א ס״ק י״ב)
To know. The Perisha, reported by the Shach, holds that “identifiable” is not to be taken strictly. The Nekudot HaKesef — that is, the Shach himself, correcting his own note — hold the opposite, and prove it from the Rambam.
« כתב הפרישה דל״ד אלא כל דבר שהוא בעין כמו שגזלו » (ש״ך יו״ד קס״א ס״ק י״א)
« מיהו לפענ״ד דברי הפרישה דחוקים דדוחק לומר דדבר מסוים לאו דוקא הוא » (נקודות הכסף יו״ד קס״א)

« אבל אם אינו מפורש אלא שכולל הקרן עם הרבית אינו גובה בו אפילו הקרן » (שו״ע יו״ד קס״א:י״א)
« ארישא קאי אבל כשלא נכתב בשטר רק הקרן פשיטא דלא יקרענו » (ש״ך יו״ד קס״א ס״ק י״ח)
The underlying question. Why must the interest be recognizable? For the Taz, because otherwise the witnesses would be disqualified. For the Shach and the Nekudot HaKesef, because one would fear collecting the interest under the name of principal — and the witnesses are not disqualified.
« וגבי אפילו ממשעבדי והעדים לא נפסלו אפילו ברבית דאורייתא דלא תשימון עליו נשך לא משמע לאינשי אלא בלוה ומלוה כן כתבו התוספות והרא״ש ורוב הפוסקים » (ש״ך יו״ד קס״א ס״ק י״ז)
« וכשאין רבית מפורש בשטר מודו רבנן דלא גבי הקרן דחיישינן שמא יגבה הרבית בתורת קרן » (נקודות הכסף יו״ד קס״א)

9. The vocabulary of the block — from 159 to 177

The termWhat it denotes
רבית קצוצהStipulated interest: loan + fixed sum. Of the Torah, recoverable.
אבק רביתDust of interest: every other wage for waiting. Of the Sages, not recoverable.
נשך ותרביתThe two words of the verse: the bite, from the payer’s side; the increase, from the receiver’s.
אגר נטרThe wage of waiting — the material criterion of the whole block.
קציצהThe fixing of the sum at the time of the loan. It is what shifts a case from one category to the other.
יוצאה בדייניםRecovered at law — by coercion of the person, not by seizure of property.
דבר מסוייםAn object still identifiable in kind. It governs seifim 6, 7 and 9.
רבית מוקדמת ומאוחרתInterest paid before the loan, or after repayment. Excepted by the gloss of seif 2.
צד אחד ברביתAn arrangement where interest is incurred on one side only; the Gemara makes it a dispute among tannaim.
הערמת רביתThe contrivance of interest: a licit form for a forbidden effect. Named at simanim 163 and 177.
מלוה · לוה · ערב · קרןLender, borrower, guarantor, principal.
« צַד אֶחָד בְּרִבִּית מוּתָּר » (בבא מציעא ס״ג ע״א)

10. Golden rules, mnemonic and classic pitfalls

Five golden rules.
  1. “It is only dust of interest” has never meant “one may keep it”.
  2. The criterion is not the name of the transaction but what the money pays for: אגר נטר.
  3. Two conditions make Torah interest, and both are needed: the way of a loan, and a sum fixed at the time of the loan.
  4. Recovery of interest is the performance of a mitzva, not of a debt: hence the absence of seizure, and every difficulty that follows.
  5. After the fact — heirs, penitent, cloak — the question becomes: is the object still there?
Mnemonic — the four words of the siman.
אגר נטר: is it interest? · קצוב: is it of the Torah? · מדעת: is it taken back? · מסויים: is it accepted?
Four classic pitfalls.
  1. Thinking that “not recoverable” means “permitted”. Seif 2 says exactly the opposite in its second half.
  2. Confusing coercion of the person with seizure of property. Seif 5 states the one and refuses the other in a single sentence.
  3. Attributing the Ramban’s view to the Rema at seif 11: that is the Taz’s reading, and the Nekudot HaKesef refuse it in two words.
  4. Taking “identifiable” as a synonym of “still existing”. That is the Perisha’s reading, and the Shach himself corrected it.
For practice. This level is a study recapitulation. A real financial arrangement is judged on its exact terms, and the classification it receives decides real sums. For the application to your own case, ask your Rav.
