1. The axiom: two registers, not two degrees

The axiom. The siman does not judge powers: it judges bearings, and it distributes them into two registers that are not measured against one another. Asking runs against a demand — תמים תהיה — which lists no act. Acting by augury, divination or sorcery runs against a Torah prohibition. Almost every permission in the siman lodges in the first register, or in an act of which one can say that it is not the act aimed at.
« הוא משום תמים תהיה אסור לשאול בהן אבל העושה מעשה בקסמים וניחוש וכישוף איסור דאורייתא הוא » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק א)
Three consequences, and they order the siman. (1) A permission may bear on an act that avails nothing — seif ו says so itself of the incantation over a bite. (2) A prohibition may bear on a sentence — seif י״ז forbids a table because of what is said about it. (3) And where the siman does permit, a rule of bearing almost always follows the permission.

2. The three reflex questions

1. Is it speech or an act?
Speech → תמים תהיה, and the siman is more yielding.
Act → a Torah prohibition, and it is far less so.
↓
2. Is the reason stated?
Stated → the act takes the standing of what is said of it.
Unstated → the gloss of seif ג permits, the Taz explains why, the Pit’hei Teshuva leaves the question open for refraining.
↓
3. Is there danger?
Danger to life → all is permitted: incantation, verse, scroll.
No danger → one returns to the first two questions, and the Maharshal refuses even danger to a limb.

4. What one does not enquire of — and the question of the sick man

Seif א names four categories: those who gaze at the stars and lots in the body of the seif, diviners, readers of omens and sorcerers in the gloss. The source is single and it is a demand. The question the A’haronim worked on is that of the sick man, and it arises precisely because the source is a demand and not a prohibition of act.

The Shakh reports these views and does not explicitly decide. Ascribe to him no ruling he did not write — and likewise for the Pit’hei Teshuva, whose defence ends in a clause of caution.

5. ניחוש against סימן — seifim ב to ד

Three consecutive seifim set up three distinct standings, and they must be held apart. Seif ב: a recorded custom, of which the gloss says that there is no augury but there is a sign — and which at once closes by forbidding investigation. Seif ג: augury proper, in nine forms. Seif ד: the sign, permitted, which the Shakh widens to the point of permitting action upon it.

« כי אע״פ שאין ניחוש יש סימן » (רמ״א יו״ד קע״ט:ב)
« אלא שאין לחקור אחר זה משום תמים תהיה » (רמ״א יו״ד קע״ט:ב)
« משמע דעת הרב והפוסקים דאפי׳ לעשות מעשה ולסמוך עליו לעתיד על הפסוק מותר דחשיב קצת נבואה » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק ה)
The criterion the Rambam gives in chapter י״א has three members, and it lights up the three seifim: he did not direct his acts by the sign, he did not refrain from acting, and he made himself a sign about a thing that had already happened.
« הוֹאִיל וְלֹא כִּוֵּן מַעֲשָׂיו וְלֹא נִמְנַע מִלַּעֲשׂוֹת אֶלָּא עָשָׂה זֶה סִימָן לְעַצְמוֹ לְדָבָר שֶׁכְּבָר הָיָה הֲרֵי זֶה מֻתָּר » (רמב״ם הלכות עבודה זרה וחוקות הגויים פרק י״א הלכה ה)

6. The word that makes the prohibition — the stated reason

This is the siman’s most singular mechanism, and it works twice in opposite directions. At seif ג the gloss permits slaughtering the hen on condition that he not say why. At seif י״ז the table on the night of the circumcision is forbidden because they say that it is for the child’s mazal. The same act in both cases, and the sentence decides.

« אלא אומר סתם שחטו תרנגולת זו מותר לשחטה כשקראה כתרנגול » (רמ״א יו״ד קע״ט:ג)
« ואומרים שהוא למזל התינוק אסור » (שו״ע יו״ד קע״ט:י״ז)
« אלא די״ל דשאני הכא כיון שיש שינוי טבע לפניו ויש מקצת חרדה על האדם הותר לו לעשות בלי אמירה משא״כ בהנך דלעיל שאין בהם שינוי טבע » (ט״ז יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק ב)
The symmetry is not complete, and that is exactly the Pit’hei Teshuva’s point at sק 3: one who says nothing but refrains because of a portent is not treated by the gloss. He brings a view that permits it and a source that seems to contradict it, and raises the difficulty against the Rama himself.

7. חובר חבר and the two breaches of danger — seifim ה to ז

Seif ה defines the prohibition at its widest: the Shakh adds that an intention to protect changes nothing. Then two seifim open the only breach the siman knows here, and they are not of equal force. Seif ו protects the mind of a man in danger, by an act the seif itself says avails nothing. Seif ז protects his life, and the Shakh there invokes the general rule.

« דאין לך דבר שעומד בפני פיקוח נפש חוץ מעבודת כוכבים וג״ע וש״ד » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק ט)
« משמע כאן דאם פיקוח נפש תלוי בעבירה שכשעושה עבירה יכול להנצל מותר לו לעשות כן מעצמו עבירה ההיא » (ט״ז יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק ד)

8. The verses — healing, protecting, writing: seifim ח to י״ב

Five seifim, and one rule runs through them: words of Torah protect, they do not heal the body. It explains why seif ט forbids the verse over the stricken child, why seif י permits it to the healthy man, and why seif י״ב restricts verse-amulets to protection alone. Seif ח, upstream, grades the offence when the verse is used all the same; and seif י״א recalls that some practices are permitted by name.

« דאז צריך לרפוא׳ ודברי תורה לא ניתן לרפואת הגוף אלא לרפואת הנפש » (ט״ז יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק ח)

9. The dead — seifim י״ג and י״ד

Two very short seifim, and two opposite directions. Seif י״ג defines the דורש אל המתים by three joint acts, which makes the definition narrow — and that is why the Shakh can report that the custom of going to the cemetery is established and carries no prohibition. Seif י״ד permits adjuring the sick man to return after his death, because the adjuration is addressed to a living man; and the view reported in parentheses goes further, distinguishing the body of the dead from his spirit.

« והב״ח כתב לקמן ס״ס רי״ז שכבר החזיקו במנהג זה ואין איסור בדבר » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק ט״ו)
« וא״כ ע״כ צ״ל דמה דעבד שמואל היינו לרוח ולא לגוף כדברי רא״ם » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק ט״ז)

10. Illusion, Sefer Yetzira and demons — seifim ט״ו and ט״ז

Seif ט״ו is the most commented in the siman: eleven words, four apparatus entries, and two competing solutions to the Rambam’s contradiction — the Beit Yosef’s, which maintains that there is an act, and the Taz’s, which accepts that there is none and distinguishes by what the eyes receive. The second half of the seif permits Sefer Yetzira; the Shakh and the Ba’er Hetev close it with conditions. Seif ט״ז gives the same movement concerning demons, and it is the Rama himself who closes.

« ונ״ל דכל אוחז את העינים הוא עושה מעשה כדי לאחוז את העינים » (בית יוסף יו״ד קע״ט)
« ולע״ד נראה דהרמב״ם ס״ל דיש מלקות בלאו זה אף על פי שאין בו מעשה כיון שלעיני הרואים נחשב שעושה מעשה » (ט״ז יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק י)
« וכתב הלבוש אבל צריך לזה קדושה וטהרה אשר לא נמצא זה בעו״ה בדורות הללו ועל זה נאמר ודאשתמש בתגא חלף על כן המונע יבורך » (באר היטב יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק י״א)
« מ״מ רוב העוסקים בזה אינן נפטרים מהם בשלום על כן שומר נפשו ירחק מהם » (רמ״א יו״ד קע״ט:ט״ז)

11. The table, the incense and the אמגושי — seifim י״ז to י״ט

The last three seifim run from the lightest to the gravest. Seif י״ז forbids a table because of a sentence. Seif י״ח forbids fumigating the house with a fragrant herb, and the Shakh gives the reason: it appears like fumigating for a demon — so it is seif י״ט that explains seif י״ח. And seif י״ט itself carries two clauses, which the Shakh takes up one after the other.

« מפני שנראה כמקטיר לשד » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק כ״א)
« ונפקא מיניה דאם חייב משום עבודת כוכבים הוי מומר לכל התורה כולה וכדלעיל סימן ג׳ וסימן קי״ט משא״כ אם חייב משום בעל אוב דלא הוי אלא מומר לדבר אחד » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק כ״ב)
« ולפע״ד נראה עיקר בש״ס דשבת (פ׳ כלל גדול דף ע״ה עמוד א׳) דמותר ללמוד מאמגושי מכשף דברי תורה ועניני כישוף להבין ולהורות » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ט ס״ק כ״ג)

12. Vocabulary, golden rules, mnemonic and classic pitfalls

The vocabulary

The wordWhat it denotes
תמים תהיהThe demand that opens the siman. It forbids no act: it forbids consulting. Everything the siman permits on the side of speech is decided in relation to it.
ניחושMaking a rule of conduct out of a portent. Nine forms at seif ג, and always the same verb.
סימןIts exact opposite, and the two words touch in one sentence. A marker that obliges nothing.
חובר חברTo gather beasts by an incantation. The Shakh adds that a protective intention changes nothing.
אוחז את העיניםIllusion — including produced by tricks and quickness of hand, with no sorcery at all.
קמיעThree regimes not to be confused: healing by Names, carrying verses for protection, writing verses.
דורש אל המתיםTo starve oneself, to lodge in the cemetery, and to do so for that purpose. Three joint acts — which is what lets the custom of going to the cemetery stand.
סגולהThe word is not in the seifim: it enters through the Pit’hei Teshuva at sק 6, and enters as a limit.
דרכי האמוריIt too is not in the seifim. The Pit’hei Teshuva uses it twice, to characterise practices the seifim had not foreseen.

The golden rules

1. Asking is not doing. The Shakh lays it down at the first sק, and that line serves in almost every seif.

2. Danger to life lifts everything — seif ח says it, the Shakh gives the rule at sק 9, and the Taz draws at sק 4 that the man may act himself.

3. Words of Torah protect and do not heal. It is the Rambam, then the Taz at sק 8 and the Shakh at sק 12; and it is what reconciles seifim ט, י and י״ב.

4. The stated reason changes the standing of the act. The gloss of seif ג makes a permission of it, seif י״ז makes a prohibition of it.

5. A permission in this siman is rarely a bare permission: the Rama, the Shakh and the Pit’hei Teshuva each close it with a rule of bearing.

Mnemonic

S · R · D — Speech, Reason, Danger.
What is spoken belongs to תמים תהיה; the reason stated tips the act; and danger to life erases everything. The siman’s three reflex questions hold in these three words.

Classic pitfalls

Seif ד is not a licence to divine. The Shakh explains at sק 4 that these are mere markers, and adds at sק 5 that one may even act on the child’s verse. But seif ג stands entire, and the gloss ends on a rule of bearing, not on a criterion.
Seif י״ב does not contradict itself. The first member speaks of amulets bearing Names; the restriction “only to protect” refers to amulets bearing verses. The Beit Yosef gives the Rashba’s responsum from which the seif is drawn, and the division is explicit there.
The Maharshal is stricter than expected. He refuses consultation even where a limb is in danger — whereas elsewhere danger to a limb often suffices. Do not align him with the general rule.
Sefer Yetzira is permitted, and closed. The Tur writes “ab initio”, and the seif adds “even to perform an act”. But the Shakh at sק 18 and the Ba’er Hetev at sק 11 set out the conditions and conclude that he who refrains shall be blessed. To quote the permission without the clause is to quote half the source.
The Shakh does not explicitly decide the question of the sick man. He reports four positions and stops. The Ba’er Hetev sums up the strictest, the Pit’hei Teshuva defends the most lenient and ends with שומר נפשו ירחק מהם. Do not ascribe to any of them a ruling he did not write.
For practical application, consult your Rav. This level organises what the sources say; it decides nothing in their place.
