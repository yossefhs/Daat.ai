Siman 199 — The Chafifah and the Examination of the Hidden Places (Chafifah uVedikat Beit haStarim)

בְּדִיקַת בֵּית הַסְּתָרִים וְדִינֵי חֲפִיפָה

Preparing for the טבילה: washing the בית הסתרים and the חפיפה of the hair (hot water, without נתר), close to the טבילה (סמוך); the timing on Shabbat and Yom Tov and the תרי קולי; the בדיעבד; and a חציצה found after the טבילה (תולין / לא עלתה)

1. The axiom: the חפיפה prepares the טבילה — wash, comb, examine

The starting point:

Before immersing, the woman performs the חפיפה — חפיפה: she washes, combs and examines herself, so that at the time of the טבילה nothing interposes (חוצץ) between the water and her body (that is the preceding siman, 198). This siman governs the conduct of that preparation: to wash the בית הסתרים (the hidden / folded places) with water, to perform the חפיפה of the hair with hot water (not cold, which tangles it; and without נתר/טפל which dries it out); to do it close to the טבילה (סמוך); to manage the timing on Shabbat and Yom Tov; and to know what counts בדיעבד and what to do with a חציצה found after the טבילה.
ConceptWhat it meansPractical consequence

סמוך לטבילהThe חפיפה must, לכתחילה, be close to the טבילה — one does not separate themWorthy minhag: begin מבעוד יום, finish at night (§3)
בית הסתריםThe hidden places: they need no ביאת מים, only רחיצה (washing)A distinct status בדיעבד and for the תולין (§9, 12-13)
💡 The marker: this siman is למעשה — it describes the conduct of the חפיפה (material, timing, בדיעבד) and the fate of a חציצה discovered after the fact. The act is learned from a balanit or a moret kalla, and every concrete case — the night on which to immerse on Shabbat, a חציצה found upon emerging — is a שאלה to put to a Rav: one does not decide alone from a summary.

2. The grid: בית הסתרים vs כל הגוף — which needs ביאת מים?

■ TWO REGIMES TO DISTINGUISH — the whole exposed body (כל הגוף) needs the water to touch it (ביאת מים); the בית הסתרים (the hidden / folded places) need only to be washed (רחיצה), not for water to enter them.
↓ נ״מ for the בדיעבד
■ כל הגוף — if the water did not touch an exposed point → the טבילה does not count (link to sim. 198).
↓
■ בית הסתרים — not washed beforehand; examined afterward and nothing found → עלתה (§9, end).
↓
■ חפיפה not סמוך — forbidden לכתחילה, but בדיעבד (חפפה by day, immersed a later night) → עלתה (§8).

⚖ The rule "אין מרחיקין" (§3, link to sim. 197)

The חפיפה must, לכתחילה, be close to the טבילה (סמוך לטבילה): one does not separate the preparation from the immersion, so that no חציצה arises in the interval. The worthy minhag: to begin washing and combing from the daytime (מבעוד יום), continue until nightfall, then immerse — and even after washing, to check again at the moment of immersing.

3. The master grid: the 5 families of this siman

To memorize. Basis: Mehaber and Rama, the 13 seifim, read with the Taz, the Shach, the Sidrei Tahara, the Chochmat Adam, the Aroukh haShulchan and contemporary pesika (Taharat haBayit, Shevet haLevi, Badei haShulchan).

FamilyThe essentialThe marker
1 · Washing the בית הסתרים & the material of the חפיפה Rinse the armpit and the בית הסתרים with water (מים), not other liquids; the חפיפה of the hair with hot water (cold tangles the שיער; even water warmed by the sun suffices); without נתר ("טפל") which dries it out The correct material (§1-2)
2 · The חפיפה סמוך & the daytime minhag The חפיפה לכתחילה close to the טבילה (סמוך); worthy minhag: begin מבעוד יום, continue until night, immerse, and re-check at the moment of immersing אין מרחיקין (§3)

📌 Key reading: the five families come down to a single thread — the חפיפה (washing, combing, examining) prepares a טבילה free of חציצה, and must remain close to it. Two distinctions decide everywhere: כל הגוף (ביאת מים) vs בית הסתרים (רחיצה), and לכתחילה (סמוך) vs בדיעבד. The timing on Shabbat (§4-7) and the fate of a חציצה found afterward (§10-13) are merely the application of these two distinctions.

4. The 5 golden rules

  1. Wash the בית הסתרים with water, perform the חפיפה of the hair with hot water — without נתר. Rinse the armpit and the folds with water (מים); hair with hot water (cold tangles); no נתר/טפל which dries it out (Mehaber seif 1-2).
  2. The חפיפה close to the טבילה (סמוך) — one does not separate them. Worthy minhag: begin מבעוד יום, finish at night, immerse, and re-check at the moment of immersing (Mehaber seif 3).
  3. The timing follows the night of the טבילה. מוצאי שבת → חוף at night (Rama: also ערב שבת); ליל שבת → חוף by day; Yom Tov → חוף ערב שבת; and one does not combine two leniencies — תרי קולי בהדדי לא מקילינן (Mehaber seif 4-7).
  4. בדיעבד, a חפיפה not close may count. חפפה and examined by day, immersed a later night → עלתה; for the whole body (ביאת מים); and the בית הסתרים not examined beforehand, examined afterward and nothing found → עלתה (Mehaber seif 8-9).
  5. A חציצה found afterward: one תולין when one can. Within the עונה of the חפיפה → does not re-immerse; one imputes (תולין) to the material handled after the טבילה → does not re-immerse; but חוצצין handled between חפיפה and טבילה → לא עלתה (Mehaber seif 10-13).

5. Mnemonic — the "ḤAFIFA" memory aid

"Ḥ-A-F-I-F-A" — after the 5 families
  • Ḥot water: wash the בית הסתרים with water, חפיפה of the hair with hot water — without נתר.
  • Adjacent to the טבילה: סמוך — one does not separate them; worthy minhag from מבעוד יום.
  • Feast & Shabbat: מוצ״ש → at night; ליל שבת → by day; Yom Tov → ערב שבת; no תרי קולי.
  • Indulgence (בדיעבד): a חפיפה not סמוך → עלתה; כל הגוף (ביאת מים) vs בית הסתרים (רחיצה).
  • Found a חציצה afterward: עונה / תולין → does not re-immerse; handled between חפיפה and טבילה → לא עלתה.
  • Aid of the balanit: the בדיקת בית הסתרים and the final check.
The ladder of the בדיעבד & the תולין
  • חפיפה by day, טבילה a later night → עלתה בדיעבד (§8)
  • בית הסתרים examined afterward, nothing found → עלתה (§9)
  • חציצה within the עונה / imputed to the after-טבילה → does not re-immerse (§10-12)
  • חוצצין handled between חפיפה and טבילה → לא עלתה (§13)

6. The 5 classic pitfalls

❌ Pitfall 1 — Believing one washes the חפיפה however one wishes: the בית הסתרים are rinsed with water (מים), not other liquids, and the hair with hot water — because cold water tangles (מסבכת) the שיער. And one does not use נתר ("טפל"), which dries out the hair (seif 1-2).
❌ Pitfall 2 — Separating the חפיפה from the טבילה: the חפיפה must, לכתחילה, be close to the טבילה (סמוך) — אין מרחיקין. The worthy minhag is to begin מבעוד יום and finish at night; and even after washing, she checks again at the moment of immersing (seif 3).
❌ Pitfall 3 — Combining two leniencies on Shabbat (§7): where one is wary of immersing at night, one does not combine two leniencies (תרי קולי) — the leniency of סרך בתה (immersing by day) and the leniency of distancing the חפיפה from the טבילה. The exact timing (מוצ״ש, ליל שבת, Yom Tov) is a שאלה to the Rav (seif 4-7).
❌ Pitfall 4 — Confusing כל הגוף and בית הסתרים בדיעבד: the whole exposed body (כל הגוף) needs ביאת מים; but the בית הסתרים need only to be washed (רחיצה). Hence: not examined beforehand, examined afterward and nothing found → עלתה (§9); and a חפיפה not close to the טבילה may count בדיעבד (§8).
❌ Pitfall 5 — Re-immersing systematically when a חציצה is found afterward: found within the עונה of the חפיפה → does not re-immerse (§10); one imputes (תולין) the חציצה to the material handled after the טבילה → does not re-immerse (§11-12); but חוצצין handled between the חפיפה and the טבילה (or having handed a sticky dish to her child) → לא עלתה, even if she checked immediately afterward (§13).

For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav, your balanit or your moret kalla.

8. The חפיפה in practice and the timing on Shabbat

⚖ The חפיפה (preparation) — the practical act

Before immersing, the woman performs the חפיפה: she washes (the hair with hot water, the folds and בית הסתרים), combs, removes every חציצה (cf. sim. 198), and examines herself — all close to the טבילה (אין מרחיקין). The act is learned from a balanit or a moret kalla, and the בדיקת בית הסתרים and the final check fall to her.

💡 The role of the balanit: at the mikvé, the balanit performs the בדיקת בית הסתרים and the final check (back, hair, hard-to-see points) before the טבילה. The timing on Shabbat / Yom Tov and the fate of a חציצה found afterward are שאלות settled with a Rav; one does not decide alone.

⚖ The reflex in 3 questions

  1. Is the חפיפה correct? Washed (בית הסתרים with water, hair with hot water, without נתר), close to the טבילה (סמוך)?
  2. Which regime? כל הגוף (ביאת מים) or בית הסתרים (רחיצה)? לכתחילה (סמוך) or בדיעבד? — that is what decides.
  3. Concrete case (timing on Shabbat, a חציצה found afterward…)? It is a שאלה — settled with a Rav, a balanit or a moret kalla.
For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav, your balanit or your moret kalla.
