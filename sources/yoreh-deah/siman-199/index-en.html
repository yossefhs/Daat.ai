בְּדִיקַת בֵּית הַסְּתָרִים וְדִינֵי חֲפִיפָה

Siman 199 — The Chafifah and the Examination of the Hidden Places (Chafifah uVedikat Beit haStarim): Preparing for the Tevilah, the Shabbat Timing, and the Bedi'avad

The preparation for the טבילה that completes siman 198 (חציצה): she rinses the armpit and the בית הסתרים (the hidden and folded places) with water (מים, and not other liquids), and the חפיפה of the hair is done in hot water (cold water tangles the שיער ; even sun-warmed water suffices), without נתר (« טפל ») which dries it out ; the חפיפה must לכתחילה be close to the טבילה (סמוך לטבילה), and the מנהג כשר is to begin מבעוד יום and to re-check at the moment of immersion ; then the timing on Shabbat and Yom Tov (מוצאי שבת, ליל שבת, two festival days) and the תרי קולי (one does not combine סרך בתה and distancing the חפיפה), the בדיעבד when the חפיפה was not סמוך (כל הגוף vs בית הסתרים), and the תולין of a חציצה found upon emerging — a practical and modest subject to be learned from the Rav, the balanit and the moret kalla (Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De'ah 199 — 13 se'ifim)

צְרִיכָה לְמַשְׁמֵשׁ בְּבֵית הַשֶּׁחִי וּבְבֵית הַסְּתָרִים, וְלָחֹף שְׁעָרָהּ. וַחֲפִיפַת הָרֹאשׁ צָרִיךְ שֶׁתִּהְיֶה בְּחַמִּין, שֶׁהַצּוֹנְנִין מְסַבְּכִין אֶת הַשֵּׂעָר, וַאֲפִלּוּ בְּחַמֵּי חַמָּה ; וְלֹא תָּחֹף בְּנֶתֶר וְלֹא בְּטָפֵל מִפְּנֵי שֶׁמְּסַבְּכִין אֶת הַשֵּׂעָר.

She must pass her hand (למשמש) over the armpit and the בית הסתרים and wash (לחוף) her hair. The חפיפה of the head must be done in hot water (בחמין), because cold water tangles (מסבכין) the שיער — and even sun-warmed water suffices ; one washes neither with נתר nor with טפל, since they dry out and tangle the hair.

Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De'ah 199:1-2 (summary)

Frequently asked questions — Siman 199

What is the חפיפה, and how does one wash before the טבילה?

The חפיפה is the preparation that precedes the טבילה (completing siman 198 on the חציצה): to wash, to comb and to examine. She must rinse the armpit and the בית הסתרים (the hidden and folded places of the body) with water (מים), and not with other liquids. The חפיפה of the hair is done in hot water, because cold water tangles (מסבך) the שיער ; even sun-warmed water suffices. One does not do the חפיפה with נתר or with טפל (alkaline substances that dry out and mat the hair). The concrete conduct of the חפיפה is learned from the balanit, the moret kalla and the Rav.

Must the חפיפה be close to the טבילה (סמוך לטבילה)?

Yes — לכתחילה the חפיפה must be close to the טבילה (סמוך לטבילה), so that nothing interposing arises between the preparation and the immersion. The מנהג כשר reported is to begin washing and combing from the day (מבעוד יום), to continue until nightfall, and then immerse at night ; and even after having washed and combed, to re-check again at the moment of immersion. Thus the preparation remains attached to the טבילה. The practical detail (the order, the final examination) is settled with the balanit and the Rav.

How is the חפיפה conducted on Shabbat and Yom Tov, and what is the תרי קולי?

The siman settles several cases according to the time of the טבילה. If it falls מוצאי שבת (where one cannot do the חפיפה properly on Shabbat day), she does the חפיפה that very night, at the conclusion of Shabbat (the Rama adds a מנהג יפה: to wash and comb well ערב שבת, then re-do the חפיפה and re-comb a little מוצאי שבת). If the טבילה falls ליל שבת (Friday night), she does the חפיפה by day, before the entry of Shabbat. On Yom Tov or for two festival days, she does the חפיפה ערב שבת or the day before. And where one is wary of immersing at night, one does not apply two leniencies together (תרי קולי): one does not combine the קולא of סרך בתה (immersing by day) with the קולא of distancing the חפיפה from the טבילה. Each calendar configuration is a שאלה to the Rav.

What if the חפיפה was not close, or a חציצה is found after the טבילה?

בדיעבד: if she did the חפיפה and examined by day and immersed a later night, עלתה לה טבילה בדיעבד, even though the חפיפה was not סמוך to the טבילה ; this applies to the whole body (which needs ביאת מים), whereas the בית הסתרים (which do not need ביאת מים) — if she did not examine them before, then examines them after and finds nothing → עלתה. חציצה found upon emerging: if it is in the עונה in which she did the חפיפה, she does not re-immerse ; one imputes (תולין) the חציצה to the material she handled after the טבילה (between the טבילה and the בדיקה). But if, between the חפיפה and the טבילה, she handled דברים החוצצין → לא עלתה, even if she checked herself immediately after. All this is subject to the Rav's ruling and the balanit's check.
