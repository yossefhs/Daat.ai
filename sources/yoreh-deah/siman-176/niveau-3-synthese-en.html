Siman 176 — Which hiring is permitted

איזה השכרה מותרת ואיזה אסורה
Eight seifim, reduced to one question: what remains with the owner?

1. The axiom: rent pays for what use takes away

The axiom. Rent is lawful when the thing remains its owner’s — it wears, it may lose value, and he bears that. It becomes interest the moment only a claim remains with the owner: nothing wears with him any more, and what he receives pays only for time.
« אבל בסעיף ב׳ בכלים אמרינן דאין השכר משום הלואה אלא משום שנפחת הכלי במה שנשתמש בו ואותו הפחת המשכיר מקבלו עליו » (ט״ז יו״ד קע״ו ס״ק א)
Three consequences, and they order the siman. (1) Money is the limiting case: once spent it leaves nothing — hence seif 1. (2) Things are the normal case, and the more they wear, the clearer the hiring — hence seifim 2–4. (3) Land and the worker are special cases, not because wear is absent but because their rent has a maturity of its own — hence seifim 6–8.

2. The three reflex questions

1. What remains with the owner? The thing, with its risk and its wear — this is a hiring. A claim only — this is a loan.
▼
2. What does the extra payment pay for? Property made better — permitted. A mere delay — forbidden.
▼
3. Has a debt already arisen? No — a discount for immediate payment is lawful. Yes — any supplement is a wage of waiting.

5. Things — from the vessel to the beast

The ordinary vessel — it wears, the owner bears the wear: this is a hiring. One may even permit selling it, provided a like one returns.
▼
The ship — the name of loan appears only at the breaking. Until then it is at the owner’s risk.
▼
The copper pot — the wear begins with the first use, and yet rent is taken, because what remains is diminished. This is the strong case of the siman.
▼
The cow — valued for after its death, not while alive: the owner keeps the fall in value and the wasting.
▼
The vessel that does not wear — silver, gold: if the risk passes to the taker, this is no longer a hiring.
« אלא אפי׳ בסיר נחושת שמיד שמתחיל להשתמש בו מיד מתחיל הקלקול שהנחושת הולך ופוחת תמיד ממילא הוה תכף הלואה אפ״ה מותר ליקח שכר » (ט״ז יו״ד קע״ו ס״ק ה)
« ול״ד לצאן ברזל דלקמן ר״ס קע״ז דהתם אינו עושה בהן מלאכה שיכחישו מחמתן והוא מקבל עליו אחריות מעתה » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ו ס״ק ה)

6. Money handed to the tenant — into the property or into trade

Two hundred zuz, an increased rent: the form is identical on either side of seif 5, and the outcome is opposite. The criterion is the destination — the Tur says it in a word: בגוף השדה. Invested in the hired thing, the money makes it better, and the supplement pays for a better thing; used in trade, it does nothing to the thing, and the supplement pays only for a delay.
« אע״פ שהמאה שלוקח ממנו בתורת הלואה לוקח אותם שהרי מחזירה לו בסוף ונמצא שמוסיף לו שני כורין בשכר הלואת המאה אפי׳ הכי מותר כיון שמוציא אותם בגוף השדה » (טור יו״ד קע״ו)
« נמצא מעלה לו שכירות ספינה טובה יותר מספינה רעה » (ש״ך יו״ד קע״ו ס״ק ו)
The pitfall. The Tur himself concedes that the money is received as a loan and will be returned at the end. It is therefore not the legal characterisation of the payment that saves the transaction, but its use. An identical arrangement whose money served working capital falls back under the prohibition, whatever name is given to it.

8. The man — why he must begin today

The Rambam sets the two rules side by side and opposes them: it is permitted to increase the rent of land, it is forbidden to increase a man’s wage. The Nekudot HaKesef give the reason: the courtyard is acquired in its body at once, and the tenant may do with it as he wishes; the worker is acquired by nobody, he may withdraw — as long as he has not begun, the money received is a loan and the discount is its price.
« מֻתָּר לְהַרְבּוֹת בִּשְׂכַר הַקַּרְקַע. כֵּיצַד. הִשְׂכִּיר לוֹ אֶת הֶחָצֵר וְאָמַר לוֹ אִם מֵעַכְשָׁו אַתָּה נוֹתֵן לִי הֲרֵי הִיא לְךָ בְּעֶשֶׂר סְלָעִים בְּכָל שָׁנָה. וְאִם תִּתֵּן שְׂכַר חֹדֶשׁ בְּחֹדֶשׁ הֲרֵי הִיא בְּסֶלַע בְּכָל חֹדֶשׁ. הֲרֵי זֶה מֻתָּר » (רמב״ם הלכות מלווה ולווה פרק ז׳ הלכה ח׳)
« אָסוּר לְהַרְבּוֹת בִּשְׂכַר הָאָדָם. כֵּיצַד. לֹא יֹאמַר לוֹ עֲשֵׂה עִמִּי הַיּוֹם מְלָאכָה זוֹ שֶׁהִיא שָׁוָה כֶּסֶף וַאֲנִי אֶעֱשֶׂה עִמְּךָ בְּשָׁבוּעַ אַחֵר מְלָאכָה שֶׁהִיא שָׁוָה שְׁתַּיִם » (רמב״ם הלכות מלווה ולווה פרק ז׳ הלכה י׳)
« דטעמא דמסתבר הוא דדווקא בפועל שאין גופו קנוי לו שהרי יכול לחזור בו מה שאין כן בחצר שמיד נקנה לו גוף החצר » (נקודות הכסף יו״ד קע״ו)

10. Golden rules, mnemonic and classic pitfalls

Five golden rules.
  • 1. The name does nothing: calling interest “rent” does not change it.
  • 2. Rent pays for wear; what does not wear cannot be hired out once the risk passes to the taker.
  • 3. What is paid in addition must pay for a thing made better, not for a delay.
  • 4. As long as no debt has arisen, a discount for immediate payment is lawful; afterwards, never.
  • 5. Every wage of waiting is forbidden, with no distinction between rent money and loan money.
Mnemonic — R · W · D · C · A
Risk: who bears it? · Wear: does the thing wear? · Destination: does the money enter the property? · Claim: has a debt arisen? · Acquisition: is the thing acquired at once?
Five classic pitfalls.
  • Believing that the vocabulary of hiring suffices: this is exactly the error the Rivash analyses.
  • Hiring out a precious object that does not wear while placing all the risk on the taker.
  • Confusing money invested in the property with money that feeds the business.
  • Renegotiating a rent already running against early payment — the discount becomes a wage of waiting.
  • Adding a supplement to a dowry already owed: the Taz and the Pit’hei Teshuva see genuine interest in it.

Flash card

One sentence: rent pays for the wear of a thing that stays at its owner’s risk.
The limiting case: money, which neither wears nor stays.
The strong case: the copper pot, which wears from the first moment and is hired out all the same.
The date that decides: the one on which the debt arises.
