Siman 233 — synthesis and revision (יורה דעה רל״ג)

עונת נדרים לזכר ולנקבה
Three ages, one changing condition — laid out for revision

1. The rule, in one sentence

A year before his majority — twelve for a boy, eleven for a girl — the child enters the עונת נדרים: his word binds, on condition that he knows in whose name he vowed. Before that it does not bind even if he knows; after it, it binds even if he does not.
What changes at each boundary. Not the degree of the rule but its condition. Period 1: nothing binds. Period 2: knowledge makes the vow. Period 3: age makes the vow.

2. The three ages

Up to 12 (boy) / 11 (girl): אפילו יודעים — אינו נדר
▼
12 years + 1 day / 11 years + 1 day: the vow binds if he knows לשם מי
▼
And he is examined — all year, and at every vow (Shakh ס״ק א)
▼
13 years + 1 day / 12 years + 1 day: אע״פ שאין יודעים — נדריהם נדר
▼
And at neither end are the שתי שערות required

3. Where it comes from

The three ages — the mishnah of נדה מ״ה ע״ב, in the same order. The name of the regime — the Rambam, הלכות נדרים פרק י״א: there it is written “וזו היא עונת נדרים האמורה בכל מקום”. The repetition of the examination — the Rambam, same chapter halakha ב, taken up by the Shakh. The Rama’s gloss — the Rambam, הלכות שבועות פרק י״ב, with the Semag and the Kol Bo.
« בֶּן שְׁתֵּים עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה וְיוֹם אֶחָד — נְדָרָיו נִבְדָּקִין, בֶּן שְׁלֹשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה וְיוֹם אֶחָד — נְדָרָיו קַיָּימִין, וּבוֹדְקִין כׇּל שְׁלֹשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה » (נדה מ״ה ע״ב)
« וְזוֹ הִיא עוֹנַת נְדָרִים הָאֲמוּרָה בְּכָל מָקוֹם » (רמב״ם הלכות נדרים פרק י״א הלכה ג׳)
« וְדָבָר זֶה מִדִּבְרֵי תּוֹרָה שֶׁהַמֻּפְלָא הַסָּמוּךְ לְאִישׁ הֶקְדֵּשׁוֹ וְנִדְרוֹ נֵדֶר » (רמב״ם הלכות נדרים פרק י״א הלכה ד׳)

The Taz and the Nekudot HaKesef have no entry on this siman. Nothing about the halakha follows: the siman is brief and the Shakh says all there is. The Taz has seventeen entries at siman 229, thirty-four at 232, sixty-six at 234. Nothing has been manufactured here to fill a column.

6. What remains open

The status of the מופלא הסמוך לאיש. The Rambam writes “ וְדָבָר זֶה מִדִּבְרֵי תּוֹרָה שֶׁהַמֻּפְלָא הַסָּמוּךְ לְאִישׁ הֶקְדֵּשׁוֹ וְנִדְרוֹ נֵדֶר ” (רמב״ם הלכות נדרים פרק י״א הלכה ד׳) — from the Torah — and at once adds that he is not flogged. The Shulḥan Arukh does not characterise the regime, and the Pitḥei Teshuva refers to the Aḥaronim who argue it. We flag the debate and do not settle it.
The missing clause. The Tur writes “ ומיהו אינן לוקין אם חללו נדרן ועבדו על שבועתן עד שיגדלו ” (טור יורה דעה רל״ג); the Shulḥan Arukh did not carry it over. A silence is not a divergence, and disagreement must not be read into that omission.
What the parent must do. The Rama says to rebuke, and to hold the child to the light word. The line between “light” and “afflicting” is not written, and yet it decides. It is weighed before a Rav, and not in the heat of the moment.

7. Vocabulary

עונת נדרים — the year preceding majority, in which a child’s word can bind. · מופלא הסמוך לאיש — the child’s name in that year. · בדיקה — the examination, bearing on one point and repeated at every vow. · שתי שערות — the signs of majority, not required here. · עינוי נפש — the limit of ḥinukh in the Rama.

8. Recapitulation

י for יודעים: during the year of examination everything turns on that word. — ב for בודקין: and it is verified, every time. — ג for גדלות: after which knowledge is no longer tested. Three letters, three ages.
Golden rules. (1) Never presume discernment: it is ascertained. (2) Never carry a finding from one vow to another. (3) A child below the boundary makes no vow — but he acquires habits, and that is what the Rama is aiming at.

The line of psak

The vow of a boy of twelve years and a day, or of a girl of eleven years and a day, may bind — provided it is established that they know in whose name they vowed, and that establishing is repeated at every vow. Before that age there is no vow, and nothing to release; what remains is a matter of education, which the Rama frames by the measure of a דבר קטן וקל and the limit of עינוי נפש. After thirteen and twelve the vow binds unconditionally, and to undo it the route of siman 228 is required. All these questions, and in particular the ascertaining of discernment, are put to a Rav.
