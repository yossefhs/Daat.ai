Siman 188 — The Colors of Blood

1. The axiom: the color decides

The starting point:

Siman 188 asks neither "how much" nor "from where," but "which color?". דאורייתא (by Torah law), only five kinds of מראות render the woman טמאה. But the Sages tightened the matter, lest one confuse one דם with another דם: they forbade every shade that tends toward red and permitted every shade with no suspicion of redness (Shach: "אסרו כל הנוטה לאדמימות, הכשירו כל שאין לספקו באדמימות"). Hence the major practical conclusion: today one no longer judges a doubtful color oneself — one shows the בדיקה to a מורה הוראה (Rav), who examines the shade.
LevelWhat renders טמאWhat stays טהור
דאורייתא (Torah)Five מראות onlyEvery other shade

💡 The marker: this siman rests entirely on the color. Red (any shade) and black → טמא; white and green (ירוק) → טהור. The boundary (brown, beige, yellow…) is subtle and the modern eye is no longer expert: that is precisely why a doubtful shade is never decided alone, but with a Rav.

2. The 3 reflex questions

■ WHICH SHADE? (מראה) — does the color tend toward red (light or dark) or is it black? → טמא. Is it white or green (ירוק) — even dense, even like wax or gold, all the more so like leek or grass? → טהור (seif 1).
↓ we look at the source
■ WHERE DOES IT COME FROM? (שפופרת / חתיכה / מכה) — seen in an abnormal way (through a שפופרת, inside a חתיכה of flesh, when the מקור is known to be detached) → we attribute it to an innocent cause / a מכה → טהורה (seifim 3, 5-6).
↓ we decide with the authority
■ WHAT DOES THE RAV SAY? (מורה הוראה) — no longer being expert in distinguishing shades, a doubtful color is shown to a Rav; and for a מפלת (peels, hairs, a בריה), the nature of the blood is verified by the שרייה (soaking) under a ruling (seif 4).

⚖ The golden rule of our time

The Mehaber decides by color, but the Acharonim (גבעת שאול, של״ה, מהר״ם מינץ, PT s.k. 1-3) warn: the light (day / night), the wet / dry state and the fineness of the shades make the eye fallible. אינה סומכת אף על עצמה (PT s.k. 6) — the woman does not rely even on her own judgment: a doubtful בדיקה is shown to a מורה הוראה, who examines it and rules. This is the practical halachic translation of this entire siman.

📌 Key reading: first ask the color (red / black = טמא; white / green = טהור), then the source (שפופרת, חתיכה, detached מקור → we attribute it to a מכה), finally, for a מפלת, the שרייה (dissolves = דם; does not dissolve = בריה / מכה). And everywhere, the doubtful shade is shown to the Rav.

4. The 5 golden rules

  1. The color decides. Red (any shade) and black → טמא; white and green (ירוק) → טהור, even dense, even at an open מקור (Mehaber seif 1).
  2. "אסרו כל הנוטה לאדמימות, הכשירו כל שאין לספקו באדמימות". The Sages tightened (red) and permitted (no suspicion of redness) — this is the Shach's yesod (s.k. 1).
  3. Limited נאמנות. She is believed to say "כזה ראיתי ואבדתיו" if white / green; but for a טמא/doubtful blood, "the Sage permitted such a one" does not suffice → it must be shown (Mehaber seif 2).
  4. תליה במכה. שפופרת, חתיכה of flesh, detached מקור → seen in an abnormal way → we attribute it to a מכה → טהורה (Mehaber seifim 3, 5-6).
  5. שרייה for the מפלת; חזקה after 3×. A בריה (peels/hairs) → טמא if it dissolves in 24 h (פושרים); checked 3× without dissolving → חזקה of a מכה, no need to check again (Mehaber + Rama seifim 4-6).

5. Mnemonic — the "MAR'OT" memory aid

"MAR'OT / מראות" — the siman's chain of decision
  • Mar'eh (the color): red / black → טמא; white / green → טהור — this is the first criterion.
  • Admimut (redness): the Sages forbade everything tending toward red, permitted everything with no suspicion of redness.
  • Rav: a doubtful shade is not judged alone — it is shown to a מורה הוראה.
  • Otza'ah / source (יציאה): שפופרת, חתיכה, detached מקור → seen abnormally → we attribute it to a מכה.
  • Tliya / שרייה: a בריה that dissolves in 24 h = טמא; 3× without dissolving → חזקה of a מכה.
The ladder of colors (seif 1)
  • אדום (red, any shade) → טמא
  • שחור (black) → טמא (it is "faded" red)
  • לבן (white) → טהור
  • ירוק (green: wax, gold, leek, grass, "בלוא") → טהור — even dense

6. The 4 classic pitfalls

❌ Pitfall 1 — Judging a doubtful color oneself: one believes one can decide a "borderline" shade (brown, beige, yellow…) by eye. But no longer being expert in distinguishing shades, אינה סומכת אף על עצמה (PT s.k. 6): the light, the wet / dry state and the fineness of the shade deceive. A doubtful בדיקה is always shown to a מורה הוראה — one never decides alone.
❌ Pitfall 2 — Believing green is always simple: green (ירוק) is טהור, but the debate is real. מהר״ם מינץ, של״ה and the PT (s.k. 2) warn "אין להקל במהירות": a greenish shade tending toward red, or a doubtful green, is not an automatic leniency. Clear ירוק is טהור; doubtful green is shown to the Rav.
❌ Pitfall 3 — Forgetting the תליה במכה: when the מקור has detached and pieces of flesh fall, the Rama teaches that we attribute the blood to the מכה as long as the pieces are there — even if she sees blood. To ignore this תליה is to declare טמא what the halacha attributes to a known wound (Mehaber seif 5, Rama). But the application remains with the Rav.
❌ Pitfall 4 — Confusing חתיכת דם and בריה: the soaking test (שרייה, 24 h) applies only to a מפלת of peels / hairs: if it dissolves, it is טמא. But a חתיכת דם (a clot of blood), even hard and undissolved, is טמא — it does not "pass" the שרייה test like a בריה. A י״א adds that a small piece (the size of a thin שפופרת) also requires the שרייה (Mehaber seif 6).

For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.

⚖ The reflex in 3 questions

  1. Which color? Red / black → טמא; white / green → טהור. The doubtful shade is not judged alone.
  2. Where does it come from? שפופרת, חתיכה, detached מקור → seen in an abnormal way → we attribute it to a מכה.
  3. בריה or clot? A מפלת of peels / hairs → שרייה (dissolves = טמא); a hard חתיכת דם → טמא; 3× without dissolving → חזקה of a מכה.
For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.
