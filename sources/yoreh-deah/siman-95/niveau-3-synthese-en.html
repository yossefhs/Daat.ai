1. The axiom: נותן טעם בר נותן טעם דהיתרא — the taste of a permitted taste

The starting point:

Siman 95 no longer deals with a forbidden substance falling into a permitted one, but with a permitted taste transmitted twice. Meat gives its taste to the pot; the pot gives it to the fish cooked inside. Yet each of these tastes is permitted: this is נותן טעם בר נותן טעם דהיתרא (the taste of a permitted taste). The taste is קלוש (faint) and was never forbidden → the fish stays heter, permitted to eat with כותח (dairy). The Mehaber (seif 1) permits "מִשּׁוּם דַּהֲוֵי נוֹתֵן טַעַם בַּר נוֹתֵן טַעַם דְּהֶתֵּרָא". But the Rama is stringent le-chatchila in cooking and roasting (יש מחמירין) while permitting bedieved: this is the Sephardi / Ashkenazi machloket that structures the whole siman.
PathMehaber (Sephardi)Rama (Ashkenazi custom)
נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא, eating with the other kindPermitted even le-chatchilaForbidden le-chatchila (cooking/roasting); permitted bedieved
Putting in THEIR dishes / עלו (without cooking)PermittedPermitted le-chatchila
Stuck fat (שומן) > 1/60, or דבר חריףForbidden (60 required)Forbidden (60 required)
💡 The marker: this siman no longer speaks of "cold / hot / salted" nor of "tasting / 60" against a prohibition, but of a repeated permitted taste (נ״ט בר נ״ט). Everything flips to forbidden the moment there is (a) fat in substance (ממש) at more than one sixtieth, (b) a real איסור (even several נ״ט בר נ״ט stay forbidden), or (c) a דבר חריף that carries the taste like substance.

2. The 3 reflex questions

■ PERMITTED OR FORBIDDEN? (היתרא / איסורא) — is the transmitted taste permitted (kosher meat → pot → fish) or is there an איסור / fat ממש > 1/60? Permitted taste → נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא, permitted; issur or fat → 60 required (seif 1).
↓ we look at the mode of contact
■ COOKED/ROASTED OR "RISEN" (עלו)? — cooked or roasted (נתבשלו / נצלו) → true נ״ט בר נ״ט, which the Rama disputes le-chatchila but permits bedieved. Only "risen" (עלו), hot placed on cold without cooking → lighter, permitted even le-chatchila for all (seif 2; Taz s.k. 3-4).
↓ we look at the nature of the food
■ DAVAR CHARIF OR NOT? (דבר חריף) — a sharp / pungent food (onion, garlic, spices ground in a מדוך, חריף גמור רובו ככולו) → it carries the taste like substance: forbidden even bedieved up to 60 against the absorbed meat. A little spice does not make it charif (end of seif 2; cross-ref siman 96).

⚖ Why נ״ט בר נ״ט permits (seif 1)

At each step the taste is already permitted and weakens (קלוש): the meat is kosher, the pot carries only a meat taste, and the fish receives only a taste of a taste. Eating that fish with כותח does not recreate "meat and milk" in the full sense. But with an איסור (real נבילה, etc.), even several נ״ט בר נ״ט stay forbidden (Taz s.k. 1) — the leniency applies only to a permitted taste at each link.

3. The master grid: permitted (נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא) vs forbidden

An absolute must to memorize. Basis: Mehaber seifim 1-4, read with the Rama, the Taz (s.k. 1, 3-4, 8-9, 13, 15) and the Shach (s.k. 1).

SituationDecisive criterionResult

📌 Key reading: first ask is the taste permitted or is there an איסור / fat ממש; then cooked/roasted or only עלו; then is the food charif. And keep in mind the machloket: the Mehaber permits, the Rama is stringent le-chatchila (cooking/roasting) but permits bedieved, the use of the dishes, the עלו and the non-ben-yomo.

4. The 5 golden rules

  1. "נותן טעם בר נותן טעם דהיתרא". A permitted taste transmitted twice stays permitted; but with an איסור, even several נ״ט בר נ״ט stay forbidden (Mehaber seif 1; Taz s.k. 1).
  2. Fat ממש > 1/60 = forbidden. A poorly washed pot with substance of fat → 60 required; we presume that a pot in which one cooked had no stuck fat (Shach s.k. 1).
  3. Mehaber / Rama machloket. The Mehaber permits eating with the other kind; the Rama forbids le-chatchila in cooking/roasting, permits bedieved, the dishes, the עלו and the non-ben-yomo (Mehaber + Rama seif 2).
  4. Dishwashing: two ben yomo + together + כלי ראשון = forbidden. One non-ben-yomo, or separately, or in a כלי שני → permitted. עירוי of meat = כלי ראשון; neutral water poured = permitted even with stuck fat (Mehaber + Rama seif 3).
  5. דבר חריף cancels the leniency. A fully sharp food (רובו ככולו) carries the taste like substance → forbidden even bedieved up to 60 (Rama seif 2, cross-ref siman 96).

5. Mnemonic — the "DAGIM" memory aid

"DAGIM" (דגים, the fish of seif 1)
  • Double taste: נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא — permitted because the taste is permitted and faint.
  • Ashkenazi / Sephardi: Rama stringent le-chatchila (cooking/roasting), Mehaber permits.
  • Grease (fat ממש) > 1/60 → forbidden; 60 required against the fat.
  • Interdicted if charif: a דבר חריף carries the taste → even bedieved up to 60.
  • Merely risen (עלו) → light, permitted even le-chatchila.
The dishwashing matrix — הדחת כלים (seif 3-4)
  • 2 ben yomo + together + כלי ראשון → forbidden (and such is the custom)
  • one non-ben-yomo / separately / כלי שני → permitted
  • עירוי of meat onto milk → like כלי ראשון, forbidden if ben yomo
  • neutral water poured on both → permitted even with stuck fat
  • ash (אפר) in the water in advance → נ״ט לפגם → permitted even with stuck fat

6. The 4 classic pitfalls

❌ Pitfall 1 — Reading seif 1 as a general leniency: נ״ט בר נ״ט applies only to a taste permitted at each link. With an איסור (real נבילה, forbidden חֵלֶב fat, etc.), even several נ״ט בר נ״ט stay forbidden (Taz s.k. 1). And if fat in substance (ממש) at more than one sixtieth is stuck on a poorly washed pot → forbidden, 60 required.
❌ Pitfall 2 — Confusing עלו with נתבשלו/נצלו: a food merely "risen" (עלו) — hot placed on a cold dish of the other kind, without cooking — is permitted even le-chatchila in both directions (Taz s.k. 4). But cooked or roasted (נתבשלו / נצלו) → this is the true נ״ט בר נ״ט that the Rama disputes le-chatchila (cooking and roasting) and permits only bedieved. Do not apply the leniency of עלו to a real bishul.
❌ Pitfall 3 — Forgetting the דבר חריף: a fully sharp food (חריף גמור, רובו ככולו) — pungent things cooked in a meat dish even non ben yomo, or spices ground in a מדוך (mortar) of meat — cancels the leniency of נ״ט בר נ״ט: it carries the taste like substance and is forbidden even bedieved up to 60 against the absorbed meat (cross-ref siman 96). A little spice does not make the food charif — only if it is רובו ככולו sharp.
❌ Pitfall 4 — Getting the dishwashing matrix wrong: for meat dishes washed with milk dishes, the Mehaber permits by נ״ט בר נ״ט (if no fat, "ברי לי"), but the Rama (Tour, many Rishonim) forbids if the two are ben yomo, washed together in a כלי ראשון (and such is the custom). Permitted if one is non-ben-yomo, or washed one after the other, or in a כלי שני. עירוי from a meat כלי ראשון = like כלי ראשון; but neutral boiling water poured on both → permitted even with stuck fat (the עירוי is not a true כלי ראשון making them absorb from each other).

For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.

7. Recap of the 7 seifim — one line each

SeifTopicThe essential
1נ״ט בר נ״ט: fish in a meat potFish cooked/roasted in a well-washed meat pot, no stuck fat → permitted with כותח (נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא). Poorly washed pot with fat ממש > 1/60 of the fish → forbidden (60 required).
2Egg; Mehaber / Rama machloket; דבר חריףEgg cooked in water in a dairy pot → permitted in poultry even le-chatchila. Cooked WITH meat even a קליפה → forbidden with כותח. Rama: יש מחמירין in cooking/roasting, custom forbids le-chatchila / permits bedieved; their dishes, עלו, non-ben-yomo permitted. דבר חריף (רובו ככולו) → forbidden even bedieved up to 60 (siman 96).
3הדחת כלים (dishwashing — central case)Meat dishes washed in a dairy יורה, water יד סולדת, even both ben yomo → permitted (נ״ט בר נ״ט), if "ברי לי" no fat; otherwise 60. Rama: forbidden if 2 ben yomo + together + כלי ראשון; permitted if one non-ben-yomo / one after the other / כלי שני. עירוי of meat = כלי ראשון; neutral water poured = permitted even with stuck fat.
4אפר (ash)Ash put into the hot water of the יורה before placing the utensils → permitted even with stuck fat, because the ash renders the taste נותן טעם לפגם (degraded taste). (Taz s.k. 15: בורית/fat-based soap = פגום.)
5Proximity: כותח / salt / vinegarOne does not place a vessel of כותח next to a vessel of salt (visible, does not nullify); permitted next to vinegar (חומץ) (nullifies in 60). Rama: only if uncovered; and bedieved (one transgressed) → permitted, we do not fear it fell into the salt.
6Meat jug next to a milk jugPermitted to store in a chest a meat jug (כד) next to a milk one. Rama: some are stringent le-chatchila; good to refrain where unnecessary.
7Salt from a meat dish into milkSalt placed in a meat bowl → permitted to put it into milk (the salting does not absorb enough). Rama: one who is stringent on this too, blessing upon him, for some forbid le-chatchila (Tossafot, Sma"g, Or Zaroua).

⚖ The reflex in 3 questions

  1. Permitted or forbidden taste? נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא → permitted; איסור / fat ממש > 1/60 / charif → 60 required.
  2. Cooked/roasted or only עלו? עלו → permitted even le-chatchila; cooked/roasted → Rama stringent le-chatchila, permits bedieved.
  3. Which dishwashing, which proximity? 2 ben yomo + together + כלי ראשון = forbidden; neutral water / ash / non-ben-yomo = permitted; כותח near salt = no, near vinegar = yes.
For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.
