Siman 94 — Dipping a Dairy Spoon into a Meat Pot

דִּין הַתּוֹחֵב כַּף חוֹלֶבֶת בִּקְדֵרָה שֶׁל בָּשָׂר

1. The axiom: the spoon (כף) that is dipped — we assess against what was dipped

The starting point:

Siman 94 deals with a utensil (no longer a food) that carries the taste of one kind into the other: one dips a dairy spoon (כף חולבת) into a meat pot — or the reverse. The governing question: against what do we assess the sixty, and is the spoon ben yomo? The Mehaber (seif 1) rules: we assess the sixty against only the part that was dipped into the pot (מְשַׁעֲרִים בְּכָל מַה שֶּׁנִּתְחַב), provided the spoon is ben yomo, i.e. used in a kli rishon within 24 hours. One view ("some say") assesses against the whole metal spoon on the strength of "חַם מִקְצָתוֹ חַם כֻּלּוֹ" (partly hot = wholly hot) — but the Rama rules: the first opinion is the עיקר, "וכן נוהגין".

💡 The marker: this siman no longer speaks of a "piece" nor of the "צירוף of the pot" as in 92, but of a spoon / a utensil that carries the taste. Two axes govern everything: (a) against what is the 60 measured — against the dipped part; and (b) is the spoon ben yomo — used in a kli rishon within 24 hours. The whole siman declines these two axes (spoon, new pot, honey, knife, cheese).

2. The 3 reflex questions

■ BEN YOMO? (בן יומו) — was the spoon used in a kli rishon within 24 hours? Ben yomo → it forbids even bedieved (seif 1, 3). Not ben yomo → forbidden only lechatchila; bedieved it does not forbid (seif 4).
↓ we look at the measure
■ IS THERE 60? (ששים נגד הנתחב) — is there 60 against the dipped part of the spoon? With 60 → pot and dish permitted, but the spoon stays forbidden (seif 3). Without 60 → everything forbidden even בהנאה; one may nonetheless put fruit / cold food in it (seif 3).
↓ we look at the number of dippings
■ HOW MANY DIPPINGS? (פעם / פעמיים) — dipped twice with nothing known in between → one requires 2 × 60; but the custom (Rama / Teroumat ha-Deshen) follows those content with a single 60 (seif 2).

⚖ Against what do we measure (seif 1, Shach s.k. 1)?

We assess the sixty against the part of the spoon dipped into the pot, since we do not know exactly how much it emits (לא ידעינן כמה נפיק מינה). If one does not know how far the spoon was dipped → we assume it was dipped up to its top (עד ראש הכף, Shach in the name of the Maharchal). We do not rule by "חם כולו": the metal is not deemed hot in its entirety here (וכן נוהגין).

3. The master grid: ben yomo / not ben yomo, 60 or not, נ״ט בר נ״ט

An absolute must to memorize. Basis: Mehaber seifim 1-5, read with the Rama, the Taz (s.k. 1-2, 6, 12) and the Shach (s.k. 1, 3).

📌 Key reading: first ask is the spoon ben yomo (if not, forbidden lechatchila only). Then is there 60 against the dipped part (if not, forbidden even בהנאה, fruit/cold aside). And whenever a permitted taste has passed through a neutral intermediary (water, pot, utensil) before reaching the other kind, it is נותן טעם בר נותן טעם דהיתרא → bedieved permitted.

4. The 5 golden rules

  1. "מְשַׁעֲרִים בְּכָל מַה שֶּׁנִּתְחַב". We assess the 60 against the dipped part of the spoon, not against the whole spoon; we do not rule "חם כולו" (Mehaber + Rama seif 1, Shach s.k. 1, see siman 98).
  2. בן יומו = the bedieved criterion. A spoon used in a kli rishon within 24 h → forbidden even bedieved; not ben yomo → forbidden lechatchila only (seif 1, 3, 4).
  3. 60 → everything permitted except the spoon. With 60 against the dipped part: pot and dish permitted, but the spoon stays forbidden (saturated with bassar be-halav). Without 60 → everything forbidden even בהנאה, but fruit / cold permitted (seif 3).
  4. נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא. A permitted taste passed through a neutral intermediary (water, new pot, pan) does not forbid bedieved; the theme of seifim 5 and 9 (new pot, honey).
  5. סכין: hot meat → 60 against the spot of the knife; otherwise קליפה. A dairy knife cutting boiling meat (kli rishon): without 60 against the spot → the whole slice forbidden, and the knife requires הגעלה; a knife not ben yomo → קליפה only; in kli sheni → קליפה for the meat, נעיצה for the knife (seif 7).

5. Mnemonic — the "KAPaF" memory aid

"K-A-P-a-F" — after the כף (spoon) of seif 1
  • Kli ben yomo: used in a kli rishon within 24 h → the bedieved criterion.
  • A plate: with 60, pot and dish permitted — only the spoon stays forbidden.
  • Part dipped: we assess the 60 against what was dipped (שנתחב), not against everything.
  • Filter the permitted taste: נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא (water, new pot, honey) → bedieved permitted.
The ladder of the knife (סכין, seif 7)
  • kli rishon, ben yomo, no 60 → all forbidden; knife → הגעלה
  • kli rishon, ben yomo, 60 against the spot → permitted
  • not ben yomo → קליפה only (and a little for the שמנונית)
  • kli sheni → meat: קליפה · knife: נעיצה (thrust it into the ground)

6. The 4 classic pitfalls

❌ Pitfall 1 — Assessing against the whole spoon: the "some say" view (חם מקצתו חם כולו, the whole metal spoon) is not the halakha. We assess the 60 against the dipped part only (Rama: "עיקר · וכן נוהגין"). And if one does not know how far the spoon was dipped → we assume up to the top of the spoon (Shach s.k. 1, in the name of the Maharchal), no further.
❌ Pitfall 2 — Confusing ben yomo and not ben yomo: only a bat yoma spoon (kli rishon, 24 h) forbids even bedieved (seif 3). If it is not ben yomo, it is forbidden lechatchila only: bedieved, pot and dish are permitted, and so is the spoon (seif 4). Never treat a non-ben yomo spoon as though it had forbidden everything.
❌ Pitfall 3 — Applying חתיכה נעשית נבילה here: in seif 6, onions / vegetables saturated with meat cooked in a dairy pot → if one knows how much meat is saturated in them, one measures only 60 against that meat, not against everything. One does not say חנ״נ (Rama), since everything is still heter; one assesses only against what was saturated.
❌ Pitfall 4 — Forgetting the נ״ט בר נ״ט דהיתרא and the knife: (a) honey cooked in a bat yoma meat pan then poured hot into a bat yoma dairy bowl → permitted (taste of a permitted taste, seif 9); a new pot with two spoons → bedieved permitted (seif 5). (b) A dairy knife cutting boiling meat: without 60 against the spot → the whole slice forbidden; a knife not ben yomo → קליפה, and even then one peels a little on account of the שמנונית (grease) of the knife (seif 7).

For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.

⚖ The reflex in 3 questions

  1. Ben yomo? Kli rishon within 24 h → forbidden even bedieved; otherwise, lechatchila only.
  2. Is there 60 against the dipped part? With 60 → everything permitted except the spoon; without 60 → forbidden even בהנאה (fruit/cold aside).
  3. A neutral intermediary? A knife? A permitted taste via water/pot/pan = נ״ט בר נ״ט → bedieved permitted; a knife on hot meat = 60 against the spot, otherwise קליפה / הגעלה / נעיצה.
For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.
