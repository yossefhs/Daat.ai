Yoreh De'ah — Issur ve-Heter

The kashrus of food: bassar be-chalav, taarovos, hag'alas keilim. Study in 4 levels, with the nossei keilim of Yoreh De'ah — Shach (ש״ך), Taz (ט״ז), Pri Megadim, Pithei Teshuva.

After Hilchos Shabbat (Orach Chaim), the platform opens the Yoreh De'ah section — the heart of kashrus. Unlike Orach Chaim, Yoreh De'ah has no Mishna Berura: its principal commentaries are the Shach, the Taz, the Pri Megadim and the Pithei Teshuva.
