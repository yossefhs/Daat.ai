Yoreh De'ah · Issur ve-Heter

דִּין תּוֹלִין לְהָקֵל וְהַכֹּל נִכְנָס בְּסָפֵק

Siman 111 — Attribution (Tolin / Shani Omer) and Mixtures That Combine in Doubt (Hakol Nichnas be-Safek)

The direct continuation of siman 110: when a piece of forbidden and a piece of permitted fall into two pots (one permitted, one forbidden), one attributes (תולין / שאני אומר) the forbidden to the forbidden side → permitted — but only for a דרבנן prohibition (or a דאורייתא prohibition nullified by the majority), and as long as the prohibition is not the majority; and the rule of הכל נכנס בספק: two pots that "enter into doubt" combine to reach 60 — even far apart (one in the cellar, one in the attic), provided they belong to one and the same person (Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De'ah 111 — 7 se'ifim)

שְׁתֵּי קְדֵרוֹת, אַחַת שֶׁל הֶתֵּר וְאַחַת שֶׁל אִסּוּר, וּלְפָנָיו שְׁתֵּי חֲתִיכוֹת, אַחַת שֶׁל הֶתֵּר וְאַחַת שֶׁל אִסּוּר — אִם הַחֲתִיכָה הִיא מֵאִסּוּר דְּרַבָּנָן, כְּגוֹן שׁוּמָּנוֹ שֶׁל גִּיד, וְנָפְלוּ אֵלּוּ לְתוֹךְ אֵלּוּ — מֻתָּרִים, שֶׁאָנוּ תּוֹלִין לוֹמַר הָאִסּוּר נָפַל לְתוֹךְ הָאִסּוּר וְהַהֶתֵּר לְתוֹךְ שֶׁל הֶתֵּר.

Two pots, one of permitted and one of forbidden, and before him two pieces, one of permitted and one of forbidden: if the piece is a rabbinic prohibition, such as the fat of the gid (שומנו של גיד), and they fell into one another, they are permitted, because we attribute (תולין) by saying: the forbidden fell into the forbidden and the permitted into the permitted.

Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De'ah 111:1

Frequently asked questions — Siman 111

What is תולין / שאני אומר (attributing the doubt)?

According to the Shulchan Aruch (YD 111:1), when there are two pots — one of permitted, one of forbidden — and before them two pieces — one of permitted, one of forbidden — that fall into one another, one attributes (תולין) by saying: the forbidden fell into the forbidden and the permitted into the permitted (שאני אומר). It is permitted even if the permitted in the pot is not greater than the piece, but only for a דרבנן prohibition (e.g. שומנו של גיד). The basis is ספק דרבנן לקולא (Yevamot 82). For practical halacha, consult your Rav.

When does one NOT attribute leniently?

Three limits (se'ifim 2-4): (1) one attributes as long as the prohibition is not the majority; if the forbidden is greater than the permitted, one does not attribute (se'if 2). (2) For a דאורייתא prohibition, a majority of permitted is required to nullify it מן התורה (se'if 3). (3) When the permitted is the majority: in מין במינו the majority suffices (מן התורה בטל ברוב), but in אינו מינו one needs sixty times (se'if 4). For practical halacha, consult your Rav.

What is הכל נכנס בספק (the combination / צירוף)?

This is the principle of se'if 7: if a prohibition falls into one of two pots of permitted without knowing which, and neither alone contains enough to nullify it, but the two together do, then the two combine (מצטרפות) to nullify it. And even one at home and one in the attic (אחת בבית ואחת בעלייה) combine, and so up to a hundred — provided they belong to a single person (destined to be mixed); for two persons, they do not combine. The Rama adds: יש מחמירין even for a single person, and in a case of loss one only eats it after mixing them together. For practical halacha, consult your Rav.
