Siman 97 — Not Kneading Dough with Milk

שֶׁלֹּא לָלוּשׁ עִיסָּה בְּחָלָב

The גזירה "lest one come to eat it with meat" (שמא יבוא לאכלה עם בשר), the exceptions of small quantity (כדי אכילה בבת אחת) and distinct shape (שינה צורת הפת), the oven smeared with fat (אליה) and cooking bread, roast and fish together with meat (ריחא)

1. The axiom: not an absorption, but a גזירה

The starting point:

Siman 97 deals neither with a mixture nor with a real absorption of a forbidden substance: it lays down a גזירה (rabbinic safeguard). One does not knead dough with milk (אין לשין עיסה בחלב), "שֶׁמָּא יָבוֹא לְאָכְלָהּ עִם בָּשָׂר" — lest one come to eat it with meat. The bread becomes forbidden by decree, not by a real taste: "if one kneaded → all the bread is forbidden, even to eat it alone" (אֲפִלּוּ לְאָכְלָהּ לְבַדָּהּ). Only two היכרים lift the גזירה: a small quantity, enough to eat in one sitting (כְּדֵי אֲכִילָה בְּבַת אַחַת), or a changed shape of the bread (שִׁנָּה צוּרַת הַפַּת).
LogicThe engine of siman 97What is NOT at stake
The nature of the prohibitionA rabbinic גזירה of confusion (Shach)Not an absorption / טעם כעיקר
The remedyהיכר: small quantity or distinct shapeNot 60 nor ביטול (the bread is נבילה by decree)
The scopeForbidden even eaten alone (אפילו לבדה)Not only "with meat"
💡 The marker: this siman does not ask "how much taste / how much to nullify," but "could one err and eat it with meat?". As long as there is a היכר (little bread, or a recognizable shape), no confusion is possible → permitted. Without a היכר, the bread is forbidden even alone.

2. The 3 reflex questions

■ IS THERE A גזירה? (שמא יבוא לאכלה עם בשר) — was bread kneaded with milk (or baked in an oven smeared with fat)? If so, without a היכר → all the bread is forbidden, even alone (seif 1).
↓ we look for the היכר
■ IS THERE A היכר? (כדי אכילה בבת אחת / שינה צורת הפת) — a small quantity eaten in one sitting, or a recognizable distinct shape → permitted. But: the shape works only at home (not for the market) and only at the time of baking (seif 1).
↓ we look at the fat
■ IS THERE FAT בעין OR AN ODOR? (אליה / ריחא) — an oven smeared with tail-fat (אליה) → permission only by ליבון (heating until it whitens), not by wiping. Joint cooking (bread, roast, fish with meat) → ריחא forbidden with milk, mitigated by a large open oven or by covering (seif 2-3).

⚖ The customs of the year (Rama, seif 1)

This is why the custom is to knead bread with milk on Shavuot, and with fat (שומן) in honor of Shabbat: all this is considered a small quantity and their shape differs from other breads. All the more so פלאד״ן (a cheese pastry) or פשטיד״א (a meat pie) are permitted. But one does not bake any bread with a fladen/pashtida in an oven, lest the fat ooze (יזוב) onto the bread; and if it oozed beneath it (תחתיו) → it is as if kneaded with it. The custom is to place the tofela (טפילה, the rim of dough) at the mouth of the oven; and even in a pan (מחבת), the custom is to be stringent le-chatchila.

3. The master grid: the dough with milk, its exceptions and their limits

An absolute must to memorize. Basis: Mehaber seifim 1-3, read with the Rama, the Taz (s.k. 1-5) and the Shach.

SituationDecisive criterionResult
Dough kneaded with milk, a lot, ordinary shape גזירה without a היכר 🔴 All the bread forbidden, even eaten alone
Small quantity, eaten in one sitting היכר: כדי אכילה בבת אחת 🟢 Permitted (no risk of forgetting)
Shape of the bread changed, recognizable היכר: שינה צורת הפת 🟢 Permitted — at home and at baking only
Bread made for the market, with a distinct shape The shape היכר does not work (Mahari"t) 🔴 Forbidden (buyers will not recognize it)
Dividing a bread already forbidden A שינוי after the fact does not help 🔴 Forbidden (already נעשה נבילה)
Bread baked in an oven smeared with fat (אליה) Fat בעין → ליבון required 🟡 Status of dough with milk; no היתר by wiping
בוכיא heated from below External heating does not help 🔴 No היתר without internal ליבון
Bread + roast / fish + meat, small oven ריחא transfers the taste 🔴 Forbidden with milk
Large oven (12 esronim), mouth open / roast covered ריחא dissipated / cut off 🟢 Permitted (cross-ref siman 108)
📌 Key reading: for the dough with milk, first ask is there a היכר (quantity or shape) — and check its two limits (home only, baking only, no dividing after the fact). For the smeared oven, see whether the fat is בעין (→ ליבון). For joint cooking, look at the size of the oven and whether there is covering (ריחא).

4. The 5 golden rules

  1. "אין לשין עיסה בחלב שמא יבוא לאכלה עם בשר". This is a גזירה: if one kneaded, all the bread is forbidden even eaten alone (Mehaber seif 1, Shach).
  2. Two היכרים lift the גזירה. A small quantity (כדי אכילה בבת אחת) or a recognizable distinct shape (שינה צורת הפת) → permitted (Mehaber seif 1).
  3. The shape היכר has two limits. It works only at home (not to sell at the market) and only at the time of baking; one does not divide a bread already נבילה (Pithei Teshuva s.k. 3, Mahari"t).
  4. Fat בעין → ליבון, not קינוח. An oven smeared with אליה cannot be wiped clean; one must heat it "עד שיתלבן". Even a בוכיא has no היתר by an external heating (Mehaber seif 2, Taz s.k. 4-5).
  5. ריחא in joint cooking. Bread + roast, fish + meat in an oven → forbidden with milk. Mitigated by a large open oven (12 esronim) or by covering (Mehaber seif 3, cross-ref siman 108).

5. Mnemonic — the "BREAD" memory aid

"B-R-E-D" — the thread of the siman
  • Bread with milk = גזירה: forbidden even eaten alone (שמא יבוא לאכלה עם בשר).
  • Ration in one sitting (כדי אכילה בבת אחת): little bread → היכר → permitted.
  • Edited shape (שינה צורת הפת): distinct form → permitted (home + baking only).
  • Don't smear the oven (אליה): fat בעין → ליבון; and ריחא in joint cooking.
The ladder of the leniencies (seif 1-3)
  • כדי אכילה בבת אחת → permitted (small quantity, no forgetting)
  • שינה צורת הפת → permitted — at home and at baking only
  • תנור באליה → ליבון only; wiping / external heating do not help
  • ריחא → large open oven / covered roast → permitted (siman 108)

6. The 4 classic pitfalls

❌ Pitfall 1 — Thinking it is an absorption: bread kneaded with milk is not forbidden because it "contains" milk nor by a taste computation. It is a גזירה of confusion (Shach). That is why 60 does not help to permit it when the decree applies, and it is forbidden even eaten alone — unlike a real mixture. Do not reason "ביטול בס׳" here.
❌ Pitfall 2 — Extending the shape היכר without its limits: changing the shape of the bread helps only at home — bread made for the market remains forbidden, since buyers will not recognize the mark (Mahari"t, Pithei Teshuva s.k. 3). Moreover the שינוי must be made at the time of baking: a shape / a פשטיד״א made after the bread is already forbidden does not help, and one does not divide a bread already נבילה.
❌ Pitfall 3 — Confusing fat בעין with a mere בליעה: an oven smeared with אליה keeps the fat בעין (a present substance, not wipeable) → היתר only by ליבון (heating until it whitens), not by קינוח (Taz s.k. 4). By contrast, bread that has only an absorbed taste (without fat בעין) remains נ״ט בר נ״ט → bedieved permitted even with milk, and with meat permitted even le-chatchila (Pithei Teshuva s.k. 4, link siman 95).
❌ Pitfall 4 — Underestimating "זב תחתיו" and ריחא: one does not bake bread with a fladen/pashtida in an oven, lest the fat ooze onto the bread; it need not be visible beneath the bread — once it overflows and soaks into the oven, one suspects it reached it (Taz s.k. 2). And the ריחא (bread + roast, fish + meat) forbids with milk, except in a large open oven or with covering.

For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.

(See siman 108 how the custom is to conduct oneself.)

⚖ The reflex in 3 questions

  1. גזירה? Dough with milk (or a smeared oven) → forbidden even eaten alone, by decree "שמא יבוא לאכלה עם בשר".
  2. Is there a היכר? Small quantity or distinct shape → permitted; but at home and at baking only, and not by dividing after the fact.
  3. Fat בעין or ריחא? Smeared oven → ליבון; joint cooking → large open oven / covering (siman 108).
For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.
