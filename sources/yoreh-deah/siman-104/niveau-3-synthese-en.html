Siman 104 — A Mouse Found in Wine or Beer (Akhbar), and Repulsive Creatures

דִּין עַכְבָּר שֶׁנִּמְצָא בְּיַיִן אוֹ בְּשֵׁכָר

Field mouse (נ״ט לשבח) / town mouse (safek), כבוש כמבושל 24h, נחתך שאי אפשר לבררו → all forbidden, wine / oil / fat נ״ט לפגם, נטילת מקום + ספק ספיקא, הקריבהו נא לפחתך, the repulsive creatures

1. The axiom: the field mouse improves, the town mouse is a doubt

The starting point:

Siman 104 continues the נ״ט לשבח / נ״ט לפגם debate of Siman 103, applied to the mouse (עכבר). The field mouse (עכברא דדברא) imparts a good taste (נותן טעם לשבח) — "it comes up on the table of kings" — therefore it forbids. But the town mouse (עכברא דמתא) is a doubt (ספק): "מְסֻפָּק לָנוּ אִם מַשְׁבִּיחַ בְּשֵׁכָר וְחֹמֶץ אוֹ אִם הוּא פּוֹגֵם" — does it improve the beer and the vinegar, or spoil them? The whole siman flows from this doubt, from the measure of time (24h) and from separability.
MouseTasteConsequence
עכברא דדברא (field)נ״ט לשבח — improves (kings' food)Forbidden (it gives taste)
עכברא דמתא (town)ספק — improves or spoils?Decided by cold < 24h, intactness, separability
In wine / oil (seif 2)פוגם בודאי — spoils for sureNo need for 60
💡 The marker: this siman no longer speaks of "contact" (Siman 91) or of the classic "taste + 60" (Siman 92), but of a doubt about the nature of the taste (improves or spoils) and of the separability of the substance. The hinge of all of seif 1 is: fallen cold, removed whole, before 24h → permitted; otherwise 60 is required; and if the substance is recognizable but inseparable → nothing nullifies it.

2. The 3 reflex questions

■ WHICH MOUSE / WHICH LIQUID? (דדברא / דמתא ; wine-oil or beer-vinegar) — field mouse → improves → forbidden. Town mouse in wine / oil (seif 2) → spoils for sure → no 60. Town mouse in beer / vinegar (seif 1) → safek, continue (seif 1).
↓ we look at cold and time
■ COLD, WHOLE, < 24h? (בצונן · שלם · מעת לעת) — fallen cold, removed whole, and without a stay of 24h → permitted (כבוש כמבושל: 24h cold = as cooked). But if hot (רותח), or 24h even cold → 60 is required (seif 1).
↓ we look at separability
■ CAN THE SUBSTANCE BE FILTERED? (אפשר לבררו במסננת) — cut into fine particles that can be filtered out with nothing remaining, or fully melted (נימוח) → nullified by 60. But inseparable (mixed into a thick food) → all forbidden, without nullification (seif 1).

⚖ נחתך שאי אפשר לבררו — the recognizable, inseparable prohibition (seif 1)

If the mouse was cut into particles impossible to filter — for example the wine / vinegar mixed into a thick food (מאכל עב) — all is forbidden, without nullification (אין לו ביטול), for we fear encountering the very substance of the forbidden thing (ממש) without noticing. [Rama: this stringency applies only to a שרץ (creature), of which we fear that it remains and cannot be extracted; for other prohibitions, there is no concern (או״ה כלל ל״ב).]

3. The master grid: absolutely forbidden, 60, or no 60 at all

An absolute must to memorize. Basis: Mehaber seifim 1-3, read with the Rama, the Taz (s.k. 1-6) and the Shach (s.k. 1, 3-9).

SituationDecisive criterionResult
Field mouse (עכברא דדברא) נ״ט לשבח (kings' food) 🔴 Forbidden (it improves)
Town mouse, beer / vinegar, cold, whole, < 24h כבוש כמבושל not reached 🟢 Permitted
Hot (רותח), or 24h even cold; but filterable / melted Sixty (ס׳) against the mouse 🟡 Permitted if there is 60 of permitted
Cut fine, inseparable (thick food) נחתך-אי אפשר לבררו → ממש 🔴 All forbidden, without nullification
Fallen into wine, oil (and honey) פוגם בודאי — spoils for sure 🟢 Permitted without 60
Fallen into fat (שומן), arriving hard before us נטילת מקום + ספק ספיקא (כבוש) 🟢 Remove the spot (Rama)
Fat: poured hot over it, doubt if the mouse was there יש מחמירים (stringent) 🔴 All forbidden even with 60
Repulsive creatures (ants, flies, mosquitoes), melted דברים המאוסים + majority 🟢 Nullified in the majority; but filter the filterable
📌 Key reading: in seif 1, first ask which mouse (field = forbidden), then cold / whole / < 24h (otherwise 60), then can the substance be filtered (otherwise all forbidden). In seif 2, the wine and the oil spoil (no 60); hard fat → נטילת מקום. In seif 3, the repulsive creatures are nullified in the majority, but one filters the filterable and is wary of vinegar / beer.

4. The 5 golden rules

  1. "עכברא דדברא נותן טעם לשבח". The field mouse is kings' food → it improves and forbids; the town mouse is a safek improves / spoils (Mehaber seif 1).
  2. כבוש כמבושל = 24h cold. Fallen cold, removed whole, without a stay of 24h (מעת לעת) → permitted; otherwise (hot, or 24h) 60 is required (Mehaber seif 1, cross-ref siman 105).
  3. נחתך שאי אפשר לבררו → אין לו ביטול. Substance recognizable but inseparable (thick food) → all forbidden, for one might swallow the ממש (Mehaber seif 1; Rama: only for a שרץ).
  4. נ״ט לפגם in wine / oil → no 60. The mouse spoils the wine and the oil (which "must be fragrant"); no need for 60 against what it released (Mehaber seif 2).
  5. דברים המאוסים nullified in the majority. Ants, flies, mosquitoes, from which a person recoils → nullified even melted if the permitted is the majority; but one filters the filterable and is wary of vinegar / beer (Mehaber seif 3).

5. Mnemonic — the "עכבר" memory aid

"ע-כ-ב-ר" — the 4 hinges of the siman
  • עכברא דדברא / דמתא: field = improves (forbids); town = safek.
  • כבוש כמבושל: cold, whole, < 24h → permitted; otherwise 60.
  • בirur (separating): can it be filtered? Yes → 60. No (thick food) → all forbidden.
  • רotev / oil and spoiled: wine, oil → spoil, no 60; repulsive creatures → majority.
The ladder of the fat (Rama seif 2)
  • fat arriving hard → נטילת מקום: remove the spot (ספק ספיקא, כבוש)
  • poured hot, doubt if the mouse was there → all forbidden even with 60 (יש מחמירים)
  • fat repulsive to eat → forbidden to kindle it (הקריבהו נא לפחתך, OH 124)
  • great loss (הפסד מרובה) → one may rely on the lenient

6. The 4 classic pitfalls

❌ Pitfall 1 — Confusing the two mice: the field mouse (דדברא) improves for sure (kings' food) → forbidden. Only the town mouse (דמתא) is a safek decided by cold < 24h, intactness, separability. And this "improves / spoils" safek is not a countable doubt for a ספק ספיקא: the gemara itself was uncertain (Shach s.k. 4).
❌ Pitfall 2 — Believing 60 always saves: when the mouse was cut into particles impossible to filter in a thick food, there is no nullification — neither לח בלח by 60, nor יבש ביבש by majority — for one might swallow the ממש without noticing (seif 1). Machloket Taz / Rama-Shach: the Rama sees here a stringency unique to a שרץ (כעדשה, like a בריה); the Taz disputes: this rule applies to every prohibition that is recognizable-inseparable (and חצי שיעור is forbidden מן התורה even < כעדשה). The Shach defends the Rama, but concedes that if totally ground in it is nullified like יבש ביבש (Ran / Rashba, resp. 101).
❌ Pitfall 3 — Requiring 60 for wine and oil: in wine, oil (and honey), the mouse spoils for sure (פוגם בודאי) — these liquids "must be fragrant" (Rambam) — therefore no 60 is needed against what it released (seif 2). For fat (שומן), however, there is a machloket: the Rama is lenient like wine, but some are stringent (like beer / vinegar).
❌ Pitfall 4 — Confusing נ״ט לפגם and דברים המאוסים: the repulsive creatures (ants, flies, mosquitoes) are nullified in the majority even when melted — for things spoiled in themselves (מאוס) are unanimously nullified, unlike the machloket of נ״ט לפגם (siman 103:2; Shach s.k. 9). But one filters everything filterable, and in vinegar / beer one is wary as with the mouse (ב״י לדעת הרשב״א).

For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.

⚖ The reflex in 3 questions

  1. Which mouse, which liquid? Field = improves (forbidden); town = safek; wine / oil = spoils (no 60).
  2. Cold, whole, < 24h? Can it be filtered? Yes to all three → permitted; filterable → 60; inseparable → all forbidden.
  3. Fat or a repulsive creature? Hard fat → נטילת מקום; repulsive → no kindling; ants / flies → majority, but filter.
For the halacha le-ma'aseh, consult your Rav.
