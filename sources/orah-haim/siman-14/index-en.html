Siman 14 — Who May Make Tzitzit? (Tzitzit sheAsaan Akum): the Woman, the Non-Jew, and the Borrowed Talit

The Siman — Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim siman 14 (5 seifim)
דִּינֵי צִיצִית שֶׁעֲשָׂאָן עַכּוּ״ם וְנָשִׁים וְטַלִּית שְׁאוּלָה. צִיצִית שֶׁעֲשָׂאוֹ עַכּוּ״ם — פָּסוּל, דִּכְתִיב דַּבֵּר אֶל בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל — לְאַפּוּקֵי עַכּוּ״ם ; הָאִשָּׁה כְּשֵׁרָה לַעֲשׂוֹתָן. הַגָּה: וְיֵשׁ מַחְמִירִין לְהַצְרִיךְ אֲנָשִׁים שֶׁיַּעֲשׂוּ אוֹתָן, וְטוֹב לַעֲשׂוֹת כֵּן לְכַתְּחִלָּה. (סעיף א) · הֵטִיל יִשְׂרָאֵל צִיצִיּוֹת בְּבֶגֶד בְּלֹא כַּוָּנָה — אִם אֵין צִיצִיּוֹת אֲחֵרִים מְצוּיִים לְהַכְשִׁירוֹ, יֵשׁ לִסְמֹךְ עַל הָרַמְבָּ״ם שֶׁמַּכְשִׁיר, אֲבָל לֹא יְבָרֵךְ עָלָיו. (סעיף ב) · הַשּׁוֹאֵל מֵחֲבֵרוֹ טַלִּית שֶׁאֵינָהּ מְצֻיֶּצֶת, פָּטוּר מִלְּהָטִיל בָּהּ צִיצִית כָּל שְׁלֹשִׁים יוֹם, דִּכְתִיב כְּסוּתְךָ — וְלֹא שֶׁל אֲחֵרִים ; אֲבָל אַחַר שְׁלֹשִׁים יוֹם חַיָּב מִדְּרַבָּנָן, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁנִּרְאֵית כְּשֶׁלּוֹ. הַגָּה: וְאִם הֶחֱזִירוֹ תּוֹךְ שְׁלֹשִׁים יוֹם וְחָזַר וּלְקָחוֹ, אֵינוֹ מִצְטָרֵף, רַק בָּעִינָן שְׁלֹשִׁים יוֹם רְצוּפִים. שְׁאָלָהּ כְּשֶׁהִיא מְצֻיֶּצֶת — מְבָרֵךְ עָלֶיהָ מִיָּד. (סעיף ג) · מֻתָּר לִטֹּל טַלִּית חֲבֵרוֹ וּלְבָרֵךְ עָלֶיהָ, וּבִלְבַד שֶׁיְּקַפֵּל אוֹתָהּ אִם מְצָאָהּ מְקֻפֶּלֶת. הַגָּה: וְהוּא הַדִּין בִּתְפִלִּין ; אֲבָל אָסוּר לִלְמֹד מִסְּפָרִים שֶׁל חֲבֵרוֹ בְּלֹא דַּעְתּוֹ, דְּחָיְשִׁינַן שֶׁמָּא יִקְרַע אוֹתָם בְּלִמּוּדוֹ. (סעיף ד) · טַלִּית שֶׁל שֻׁתָּפִין חַיֶּבֶת בְּצִיצִית, דִּכְתִיב עַל כַּנְפֵי בִגְדֵיהֶם. (סעיף ה)
The laws of tzitzit made by a non-Jew or by women, and of the borrowed talit — and it contains 5 seifim: (1) Tzitzit made by a non-Jew (עכו״ם) are פסול, for it is written דבר אל בני ישראל — to exclude the non-Jew; a woman is כשרה to make them. (Rama: some are stringent — יש מחמירין — and require that men make them, and it is good to do so לכתחילה.) (2) If a Jew attached tzitzit to a garment בלא כוונה (without the intention לשמה): if no other tzitzit are available to make it valid, one relies on the Rambam who validates — but one does not bless on it. (3) One who borrows from his fellow a talit without tzitzit is exempt from attaching them for 30 days, for it is written כסותך — and not that of others; but after 30 days he is חייב מדרבנן, for it appears his own (נראית כשלו). (Rama: if he returned it within the 30 days and took it again, it does not combine — 30 consecutive days are required. If he borrowed it already fitted with tzitzit, he blesses on it at once.) (4) It is מותר to take one's fellow's talit and bless on it, provided one folds it back if he found it folded. (Rama: likewise for tefillin; but it is forbidden to study from one's fellow's books without his consent, lest he tear them while studying.) (5) A talit owned by partners is obligated in tzitzit, for it is written על כנפי בגדיהם.

The main themes of the Siman

צִיצִית שֶׁעֲשָׂאוֹ עַכּוּ״ם
Tzitzit made by a non-Jew
Tzitzit made by a non-Jew are פסול: it is written דבר אל בני ישראל — the Torah entrusts the making of tzitzit to Israel, to exclude the non-Jew (לאפוקי עכו״ם).
הָאִשָּׁה כְּשֵׁרָה
The woman is כשרה
A woman is כשרה to make the tzitzit. The Rama: some are stringent (יש מחמירין) and require men — וטוב לעשות כן לכתחילה.
תַּעֲשֶׂה — לִשְׁמָהּ
The kavana לשמה
Tzitzit attached בלא כוונה: if no other tzitzit are available, one relies on the Rambam who validates — but בלא ברכה (the Rosh invalidates: תעשה לך — לשמה).
כְּסוּתְךָ — וְלֹא שֶׁל אֲחֵרִים
The borrowed talit
A borrowed talit without tzitzit is exempt for 30 days — כסותך, and not that of others; after 30 days, חייב מדרבנן, for it appears his own (נראית כשלו).
נִיחָא לֵיהּ דְּלֶעֱבֵיד מִצְוָה בְּמָמוֹנֵיהּ
A fellow's talit
It is permitted to take a fellow's talit and bless on it — it pleases him that a mitzva be done with his property — provided one folds it back. Likewise the tefillin; but not the sefarim.
טַלִּית שֶׁל שֻׁתָּפִין
The talit of partners
A jointly owned talit is obligated in tzitzit: it is written על כנפי בגדיהם — in the plural, to include a garment belonging to several partners.

Frequently asked questions — Siman י״ד

Are tzitzit made by a non-Jew or by a woman valid, according to Siman 14?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 14:1, tzitzit made by a non-Jew (עכו״ם) are פסול, for it is written דבר אל בני ישראל — to exclude the non-Jew from the making of tzitzit. A woman, however, is כשרה to make them. The Rama adds that some are stringent (יש מחמירין) and require that men make them — and it is good to do so לכתחילה.

What if a Jew attached the tzitzit without kavana לשמה, according to Siman 14?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 14:2), if a Jew attached tzitzit to a garment בלא כוונה (without the intention לשמה), and no other tzitzit are available to make it valid, one relies on the Rambam who validates — but one does not bless on it (אבל לא יברך עליו), for the Rosh and Tosafot invalidate (תעשה — לשמה) and ספק ברכות להקל.

Must a borrowed talit carry tzitzit, according to Siman 14?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 14:3), one who borrows from his fellow a talit without tzitzit is exempt from attaching them for 30 days, for it is written כסותך — and not that of others. But after 30 days he is חייב מדרבנן, for the talit appears his own (נראית כשלו). The Rama specifies: if he returned it within the 30 days and took it again, it does not combine — 30 consecutive days are required; and if he borrowed it already fitted with tzitzit, he blesses on it at once.

May one take a fellow's talit without asking him and bless on it, according to Siman 14?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 14:4), it is מותר to take a fellow's talit and bless on it — for it pleases him that a mitzva be accomplished with his property (ניחא ליה דליעביד מצוה בממוניה) — provided one folds it back if he found it folded. The Rama adds that the same applies to tefillin; but it is forbidden to study from a fellow's books without his consent, lest he tear them while studying. And the talit of partners is obligated in tzitzit — על כנפי בגדיהם (seif 5).
