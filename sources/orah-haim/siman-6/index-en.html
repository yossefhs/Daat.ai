The Siman — Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim siman 6 (4 seifim)
כְּשֶׁיֵּצֵא מִבֵּית הַכִּסֵּא יְבָרֵךְ אֲשֶׁר יָצַר אֶת הָאָדָם בְּחָכְמָה, שֶׁבְּרִיאַת הָאָדָם הִיא בְּחָכְמָה נִפְלָאָה; הַגּוּף דּוֹמֶה לְנוֹד מָלֵא רוּחַ וְהוּא מָלֵא נְקָבִים, רוֹפֵא חוֹלֵי כָל בָּשָׂר וּמַפְלִיא לַעֲשׂוֹת. (סעיף א) · יֵשׁ נוֹהֲגִין לְהַמְתִּין לְבָרֵךְ עַל נְטִילַת יָדַיִם עַד בּוֹאָם לְבֵית הַכְּנֶסֶת, וּבְנֵי סְפָרַד לֹא נָהֲגוּ כֵן; וְעַל כָּל פָּנִים לֹא יְבָרֵךְ שְׁתֵּי פְעָמִים. (סעיף ב) · בִּרְכַּת אֱלֹהַי נְשָׁמָה אֵינָהּ פּוֹתַחַת בְּבָרוּךְ, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁהִיא בִּרְכַּת הַהוֹדָאָה. (סעיף ג) · יֵשׁ נוֹהֲגִין שֶׁאַחַר שֶׁבֵּרַךְ אֶחָד בִּרְכַּת הַשַּׁחַר וְעָנוּ אַחֲרָיו אָמֵן, חוֹזֵר אֶחָד מֵהָעוֹנִים וּמְבָרֵךְ. (סעיף ד)
Laws of the blessings Asher Yatzar and Elokai Neshama — and it contains 4 seifim: (1) on leaving the bathroom, one recites אשר יצר את האדם בחכמה — for the creation of man is of a wondrous wisdom; the body is like a flask full of breath, pierced with orifices (נקבים נקבים חלולים חלולים) which must remain as they are, lest man not survive an hour; one concludes רופא חולי כל בשר ומפליא לעשות. (2) Some wait until the synagogue to say על נטילת ידים with the other blessings; Sephardim do not — and one does not bless twice. (3) אלהי נשמה does not open with ברוך because it is a ברכת ההודאה, like the birkat hagueshamim. (4) The custom of blessing and answering amen in turn, in a group.

The main themes of the Siman

אֲשֶׁר יָצַר בְּחָכְמָה
The wisdom of the body
The creation of man is of a wondrous wisdom: the body as a flask full of breath, pierced with orifices which must remain as they are.
נְקָבִים נְקָבִים חֲלוּלִים חֲלוּלִים
Orifices and hollow organs
If one of the orifices that must stay open were to close, or one of the hollow organs were to open, man could not survive even a single hour.
רוֹפֵא וּמַפְלִיא לַעֲשׂוֹת
Healer and wondrous maker
The body expels its waste (רופא חולי כל בשר) and keeps its breath within it as in an intact flask (ומפליא לעשות): the health of the body every day.
סֵדֶר בִּרְכוֹת הַשַּׁחַר
The order of the morning blessings
Where to say על נטילת ידים and the morning blessings (home or synagogue); not to bless twice; Ashkenazi vs Sephardi custom.
בִּרְכַּת הַהוֹדָאָה
The blessing of thanksgiving
אלהי נשמה does not open with ברוך because it is a blessing of thanksgiving; blessings of thanksgiving do not open with ברוך, like the birkat hagueshamim.
עֲנִיַּת אָמֵן בְּצִבּוּר
Answering amen in a group
The custom where each one blesses in turn and the others answer amen: the one who blesses does not intend to discharge the others, and the others do not intend to be discharged.

Structure of the Siman — the 4 seifim

Frequently asked questions — Siman ו׳

What does the blessing Asher Yatzar mean, according to Siman 6?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 6:1, on leaving the bathroom one recites אשר יצר את האדם בחכמה — for the creation of man is of a wondrous wisdom. The body is compared to a flask full of breath, pierced with orifices (נקבים נקבים חלולים חלולים): if one of those that must stay open were to close, or one of those that must stay closed were to open, man could not survive even a single hour.

Where should one say על נטילת ידים and the birkhot hashachar, according to Siman 6?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 6:2), some have the custom of waiting until the synagogue to say על נטילת ידים together with the other morning blessings; Sephardim do not have this custom. The common rule: one should not bless twice. Whoever says them at home does not repeat them in the synagogue, and vice versa.

Why does אלהי נשמה not open with ברוך, according to Siman 6?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 6:3), אלהי נשמה does not open with ברוך because it is a ברכת ההודאה, a blessing of thanksgiving; and blessings of thanksgiving do not open with ברוך, as we find with the birkat hagueshamim (the blessing over rain).

What is the custom of blessing and answering amen in a group, according to Siman 6?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 6:4), some have the custom that after one person has said a birkat hashachar and the others answered amen, one of those who answered then blesses in turn, and so on. One does not object to this custom: we do not say that they have already discharged their obligation through the amen, because the one who blesses does not intend to discharge the others, and they too do not intend to be discharged by his blessing.

Why is Asher Yatzar said after each visit to the bathroom, according to Siman 6?

Because אשר יצר praises the wisdom of the body that expels its waste and keeps its breath within it (רופא חולי כל בשר ומפליא לעשות). It is the blessing of the health of the body: each time the organs perform their function, one acknowledges the wonder of creation — not only in the morning, but after each visit to the bathroom, every day.
