Siman 8 — Donning the Tzitzit and its Blessing (Hilkhot Tzitzit)

Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 8 — 17 seifim

The Siman — Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim siman 8 (17 seifim)
הִלְכוֹת צִיצִית וַעֲטִיפָתוֹ. יִתְעַטֵּף בַּצִּיצִית וִיבָרֵךְ מְעֻמָּד. (סעיף א) · סֵדֶר עֲטִיפָתוֹ כְּדֶרֶךְ בְּנֵי אָדָם שֶׁמִּתְכַּסִּים בִּכְסוּתָן, וְנָכוֹן שֶׁיְּכַסֶּה רֹאשׁוֹ בַּטַּלִּית. (סעיף ב) · טַלִּיתוֹת קְטַנִּים שֶׁלָּנוּ — אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵין בָּהֶם עִיטּוּף, יוֹצְאִים בָּהֶם יְדֵי חוֹבַת צִיצִית. (סעיף ג) · מַחֲזִיר שְׁתֵּי צִיצִיּוֹת לְפָנָיו וּשְׁתַּיִם לַאֲחוֹרָיו כְּדֵי שֶׁיְּהֵא מְסֻבָּב בַּמִּצְוָה. (סעיף ד) · מְבָרֵךְ לְהִתְעַטֵּף בַּצִּיצִית. (סעיף ה) · עַל טַלִּית קָטָן — וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁמְּבָרְכִין עָלָיו עַל מִצְוַת צִיצִית, וְכֵן נוֹהֲגִין. (סעיף ו) · צָרִיךְ לְהַפְרִיד חוּטֵי הַצִּיצִית זֶה מִזֶּה. (סעיף ז) · יְכַוֵּן בְּהִתְעַטְּפוֹ שֶׁצִּוָּנוּ הקב״ה לְהִתְעַטֵּף בּוֹ כְּדֵי שֶׁנִּזְכֹּר כָּל מִצְוֹתָיו. (סעיף ח) · קֹדֶם שֶׁיְּבָרֵךְ יְעַיֵּן בְּחוּטֵי הַצִּיצִית שֶׁלֹּא יְבָרֵךְ לְבַטָּלָה. (סעיף ט) · עִיקַּר מִצְוַת טַלִּית קָטָן לְלָבְשׁוֹ עַל בְּגָדָיו כְּדֵי שֶׁתָּמִיד יִרְאֵהוּ וְיִזְכֹּר הַמִּצְווֹת. (סעיף יא) · דִּין הֶפְסֵק וְכַוָּנָה בְּכַמָּה טַלִּיתוֹת, וְדִין הַלָּן בְּטַלִּיתוֹ. (סעיפים יב-טז)
Laws of the tzitzit and its wrapping — and it contains 17 seifim: (1) one wraps oneself in the tzitzit and blesses standing (מעומד). (2) The order of the wrapping is done as people cover themselves with their garment, and it is proper to cover one's head with the talit. (3) Our talitot katan: even without a real עיטוף, one fulfills the obligation of tzitzit; it is good to place it on one's head, to wrap oneself for the time it takes to walk 4 amot, then to lower it. (4) One brings two tzitziyot in front and two behind, so as to be surrounded by the mitzva. (5) One blesses להתעטף בציצית. (6) Over the talit katan, the Rama: some bless על מצות ציצית, and this is the custom (וכן נוהגין). (7) One must separate the strings. (8) Kavana: He commanded us to wrap ourselves so that we remember all His mitzvot (למען תזכרו). (9) Check the strings before blessing, so as not to bless in vain (לבטלה). (11) The essence of the mitzva of the talit katan: to wear it over one's clothes so as always to see it and remember. (12-16) The laws of הפסק and כוונה (one or several berakhot), and the case of the הלן בטליתו.

The main themes of the Siman

יִתְעַטֵּף מְעֻמָּד
Wrapping standing
One wraps oneself (עיטוף) in the talit standing, as people cover themselves with their garment, and it is proper to cover one's head too.
לְהִתְעַטֵּף בַּצִּיצִית
The talit gadol blessing
One blesses להתעטף בציצית; with several people, all bless, or one blesses and the others answer amen.
עַל מִצְוַת צִיצִית
The talit katan blessing
Over the talit katan, the Rama: some bless על מצות ציצית, and this is the custom (וכן נוהגין), which is not to be changed.
לְמַעַן תִּזְכְּרוּ
The kavana of the mitzva
One bears in mind that the Holy One blessed be He commanded us to wrap ourselves so that we remember all His mitzvot.
בְּדִיקַת הַצִּיצִית
Checking the strings
Before blessing, one examines the strings of the tzitzit to ensure they are valid, so as not to bless in vain (לבטלה).
דִּין הֶפְסֵק וְכַוָּנָה
Hefsek and kavana
Several four-cornered garments: the initial kavana and the הפסק (speech, walking, removal) determine one or several berakhot.

Structure of the Siman — landmarks in the 17 seifim

סעיף א-ד עיטוף The wrapping One wraps oneself standing (מעומד), as people cover themselves; proper to cover the head. The talit katan fulfills the obligation even without עיטוף. One brings 2 tzitziyot in front and 2 behind, to be surrounded by the mitzva.

סעיף ח-יא כוונה ובדיקה Kavana and checking Kavana: to remember all the mitzvot (למען תזכרו). Check the strings before blessing (לבטלה). The essence of the talit katan: to wear it over one's clothes so as always to see it.
סעיף יב-טז הפסק וכוונה ✦ Le-maaseh Several talitot: the initial kavana and the הפסק (speech, walking, removal) determine one or several berakhot; talit removed or fallen entirely → bless again; the הלן בטליתו blesses in the morning.

Frequently asked questions — Siman ח׳

How does one wrap oneself in the talit and what blessing does one say, according to Siman 8?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 8:1-2, one wraps oneself (עיטוף) in the talit standing (מעומד) and recites להתעטף בציצית. The order of the wrapping is done as people cover themselves with their garment, and it is proper to cover one's head with the talit as well.

What blessing does one say over the talit katan, according to Siman 8?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 8:6), over the talit katan one may bless להתעטף בציצית, even though one merely puts it on without a real עיטוף. The Rama reports that some bless על מצות ציצית, and this is the custom (וכן נוהגין), which is not to be changed.

What intention must one have when wrapping oneself in the tzitzit, according to Siman 8?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 8:8), one should bear in mind, while wrapping oneself, that the Holy One blessed be He commanded us to wrap ourselves in the tzitzit so that we remember all His mitzvot to perform them (למען תזכרו).

Must one check the strings of the tzitzit before blessing, according to Siman 8?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 8:9), before blessing one examines the strings of the tzitzit to make sure they are valid (כשרים), so as not to utter a blessing in vain (ברכה לבטלה).

If one removed one's talit, must one bless again, according to Siman 8?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh (Orach Chaim 8:14), if one removed one's talit, even intending to put it back on at once, one must bless again upon putting it back. The Rama reports opinions that do not require a new blessing when one intended to return, or when a talit katan remained on the body (and this is the custom).
