Our Sages taught (ברכות ט׳:): כל הסומך גאולה לתפלה אינו ניזוק כל אותו היום — whoever joins Redemption to prayer is not harmed the entire day. The Amida is the moment of standing before the King right after evoking the Redemption ; therefore nothing may intervene between גאל ישראל and the Amida. We study here at the level of principle ; for personal conduct, one refers to the custom and to the ruling of the Rav.

A. Joining Redemption to the Amida — the ג׳ seifim

סְמִיכַת גְּאֻלָּה לִתְפִלָּה — joining Redemption to prayer — the blessing that closes the ק״ש is sealed with גאל ישראל (« who redeemed Israel ») ; one must immediately join the Amida, interposing between them neither אמן (per the Mechaber) nor any verse — חוץ מה׳ שפתי תפתח, which is itself an enactment of the Sages and is part of the prayer. The siman adds the conduct of the חזן and the priority of this סמיכה over praying with the congregation.

Séif 1 — סמיכת גאולה לתפלה: interpose nothing

צריך לסמוך גאולה לתפלה, ולא יפסיק ביניהם אפילו באמן אחר גאל ישראל, ולא בשום פסוק, חוץ מה׳ שפתי תפתח. הגה: ויש אומרים שמותר לענות אמן על גאל ישראל, וכן נוהגין; ויש אומרים הא דצריך לסמוך גאולה לתפלה היינו דוקא בחול או ביו״ט אבל בשבת אין צריך, וטוב להחמיר אם לא במקום שצריך לכך.
Séif 1: one must join Redemption to prayer (לסמוך גאולה לתפלה), and not interrupt between them, not even with אמן after גאל ישראל, nor with any verse — except ה׳ שפתי תפתח. Rama: and some say it is permitted to answer אמן after גאל ישראל, and such is the custom ; and some say this obligation applies only on a weekday or Yom Tov, but not on Shabbat, and it is good to be stringent, except where one has need.
No interruption between גאל ישראל and the Amida — per the Mechaber, not even אמן. One exception only: ה׳ שפתי תפתח (« Lord, open my lips »), which the Sages set at the threshold of the Amida ; it is no interruption because it is already part of the prayer. The Rama records two additions of custom: one may answer אמן after גאל ישראל (Ashkenazi practice), and there is a view that on Shabbat the סמיכה is not required — yet טוב להחמיר.

Séif 2 — החזן בקול רם: the officiant repeats ה׳ שפתי תפתח

החזן כשמתחיל י״ח בקול רם, חוזר ואומר ה׳ שפתי תפתח ופי יגיד תהלתך.
Séif 2: the officiant (חזן), when he begins the Amida (י״ח) aloud, again says ה׳ שפתי תפתח ופי יגיד תהלתך.
The חזן has already said ה׳ שפתי תפתח in his silent Amida. When he opens the repetition aloud (חזרת הש״ץ) he says it again: since this verse is an integral part of the Amida, the prayer he opens aloud too begins with ה׳ שפתי תפתח ופי יגיד תהלתך.

Séif 3 — מצא ציבור מתפללין: the סמיכה over praying with the congregation

אם עד שלא קרא ק״ש מצא ציבור מתפללין, לא יתפלל עמהם אלא קורא ק״ש ואחר כך יתפלל, דמסמך גאולה לתפלה עדיף.
Séif 3: if, before he has read the ק״ש, he finds the congregation praying — he does not pray with them, but reads the ק״ש and then prays, for joining Redemption to prayer is preferable (דמסמך גאולה לתפלה עדיף).
A man arrives at the synagogue and the congregation is already starting the Amida, while he has not yet read the ק״ש. Should he not pray with them for the merit of communal prayer? No: he first reads the ק״ש and its blessings (up to גאל ישראל) and then joins the Amida, for joining Redemption to prayer prevails (at Shacharit) over praying with the congregation.
The siman in one sentence: one must join גאל ישראל to the Amida without interruption — per the Mechaber not even אמן, except ה׳ שפתי תפתח ; the Rama permits answering אמן (custom) and reports that on Shabbat some do not require the סמיכה (וטוב להחמיר) ; the חזן repeats ה׳ שפתי תפתח when beginning aloud ; and if the congregation prays before he has read the ק״ש, he reads the ק״ש and then prays, for מסמך גאולה לתפלה עדיף.

Practical cases

Case 1 — Answering אמן after גאל ישראל

Situation: the officiant seals גאל ישראל ; may I answer אמן before beginning my Amida?
Conduct: per the Mechaber, one does not interrupt אפילו באמן: answering אמן to one's own blessing (or even to the חזן's) would be an interruption between Redemption and prayer. Per the Rama, ויש אומרים שמותר לענות אמן על גאל ישראל, וכן נוהגין — this is the Ashkenazi custom. Each follows his minhag and the ruling of his Rav.

Case 2 — The סמיכה on Shabbat

Situation: on Shabbat, must one still take care to join גאל ישראל to the Amida without interruption?
Conduct: the Rama records a view: the obligation applies only on a weekday or Yom Tov, but not on Shabbat, for this סמיכה is derived from יענך ה׳ ביום צרה (adjacent to יהיו לרצון אמרי פי), and Shabbat is not a day of distress ; Yom Tov, however, is like a weekday, for they are ימי הדין (days of judgment). Even so, וטוב להחמיר — it is good to be stringent, except where one has need. One follows one's Rav.

Case 3 — The congregation prays before I have read the ק״ש

Situation: I arrive late, the congregation has already started the Amida, and I have not yet read the ק״ש. Should I pray with them?
Conduct: no — לא יתפלל עמהם. He first reads the ק״ש and its blessings, then joins the Amida to גאל ישראל, for (at Shacharit) מסמך גאולה לתפלה עדיף — joining Redemption to prayer prevails over praying with the congregation. For time-pressed cases and the distinction between Shacharit and Arvit, one follows one's Rav.

Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. What does סמיכת גאולה לתפלה mean, and what must one not interpose between גאל ישראל and the Amida (séif 1)?
  2. Why does ה׳ שפתי תפתח not count as an interruption?
  3. Per the Rama, may one answer אמן after גאל ישראל, and what does he say about Shabbat?
  4. Why does the חזן repeat ה׳ שפתי תפתח when beginning the Amida aloud (séif 2)?
  5. I arrive and the congregation prays before I have read the ק״ש — what do I do, and why (séif 3)?
