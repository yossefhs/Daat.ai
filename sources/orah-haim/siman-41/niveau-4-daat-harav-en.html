Orah Haim מ״א · Level 4 · שיטת אדמו״ר הזקן

דעת הרב

Level 4 — Daat HaRav (the Alter Rebbe) — Siman מ״א · דִּין הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשּׂאוֹי אֵיךְ יִנְהֹג בַּתְּפִלִּין

The Alter Rebbe's way on one who carries a load and his conduct with tefilin: removing the שֶׁל רֹאשׁ while carrying a load (גְּנַאי, דֶּרֶךְ בִּזָּיוֹן) ; a מִטְפַּחַת forbidden but a כּוֹבַע and a מִצְנֶפֶת permitted (דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה) ; and a heavy load of ד׳ קַבִּין that presses the tefilin (נִדְחָקוֹת) — in the Chabad sensibility.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe is not a commentary on the Mechaber (מחבר) — it is a complete, self-standing Shulchan Aruch, written by the Alter Rebbe (Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, founder of Chabad, disciple of the Maggid (מגיד) of Mezeritch). Its distinctiveness: it fuses halacha (הלכה) + the reasons of the mitzvot + the inner dimension in a single work, and rules with a unique Talmudic rigour.

For Chabad, the Alter Rebbe is הפוסק האחרון — his psak is the decisive authority. The Rebbe explicitly instructed to study the Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe (שולחן ערוך הרב) seriously. This page gathers the Rav's way on siman מ״א — דִּין הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשּׂאוֹי אֵיךְ יִנְהֹג בַּתְּפִלִּין (how one who carries a load should conduct himself with tefilin). In the Rav, the siman has only a single seif (סָעִיף אֶחָד), in which the Alter Rebbe unfolds: the duty to remove the שֶׁל רֹאשׁ as long as one carries a load on the head ; the fact that even a light object like a מִטְפַּחַת is forbidden on the head that bears tefilin (for it is a גְּנַאי), whereas a כּוֹבַע or a מִצְנֶפֶת — whose way is to be on the head (דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה) — are permitted ; and a heavy load of ד׳ קַבִּין that presses the tefilin (נִדְחָקוֹת) and compels their removal.

1 · טקסט אדמו״ר הזקן

שולחן ערוך הרב — סימן מ״א — הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשּׂאוֹי אֵיךְ יִנְהֹג בַּתְּפִלִּין

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe

שולחן ערוך הרב, סימן מ״א — הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשּׂאוֹי אֵיךְ יִנְהֹג בַּתְּפִלִּין — וּבוֹ סָעִיף אֶחָד
The Alter Rebbe's own text (Sefaria source Shulchan_Arukh_HaRav,_Orach_Chayim.41), followed by our English translation. The siman contains only a single seif (סָעִיף אֶחָד).

סעיף א הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשּׂאוֹי עַל רֹאשׁוֹ חוֹלֵץ שֶׁל רֹאשׁ עַד שֶׁיָּסִיר ; מִטְפַּחַת אֲסוּרָה, כּוֹבַע וּמִצְנֶפֶת מֻתָּרִים ; מַשּׂאוֹי כָּבֵד ד׳ קַבִּין וְנִדְחָקוֹת — לַהֲסִירָן.

דִּין הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשְּׂאוֹי אֵיךְ יִנְהֹג בַּתְּפִלִּין וּבוֹ סָעִיף אֶחָד:הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשּׂאוֹי עַל רֹאשׁוֹ — צָרִיךְ לַחֲלֹץ תְּפִלִּין שֶׁל רֹאשׁ עַד שֶׁיָּסִיר הַמַּשׂאוֹי. וַאֲפִלּוּ דָּבָר קַל, כְּגוֹן מִטְפַּחַת — אָסוּר לְהַנִּיחַ עַל הָרֹאשׁ שֶׁיֵּשׁ בּוֹ תְּפִלִּין, שֶׁגְּנַאי הוּא כְּשֶׁיֵּשׁ עַל הָרֹאשׁ דָּבָר אַחֵר שֶׁאֵינָהּ מַלְבּוּשׁ וְאֵין דַּרְכָּהּ לִהְיוֹת עַל הָרֹאשׁ. אֲבָל דָּבָר שֶׁדַּרְכָּהּ לִהְיוֹת עַל הָרֹאשׁ כְּגוֹן כּוֹבַע וּמִצְנֶפֶת — מֻתָּר, אֲפִלּוּ אִם מֻנָּח עַל הַתְּפִלִּין. וּמִכָּל מָקוֹם, אִם הוּא מַשּׂאוֹי כָּבֵד אַרְבָּעָה קַבִּין וְהַתְּפִלִּין נִדְחָקוֹת — צָרִיךְ לַהֲסִירָן:

How one carrying a load should conduct himself. One who carries a load (מַשּׂאוֹי) on his head must remove the תְּפִלִּין שֶׁל רֹאשׁ until he sets the load down. And even a light object, such as a מִטְפַּחַת (cloth) — it is forbidden to place it on the head that bears tefilin, for it is a disgrace (גְּנַאי) that there be on the head another object which is not a garment (מַלְבּוּשׁ) and whose way is not to be on the head. But an object whose way is to be on the head, such as a כּוֹבַע (hat) or a מִצְנֶפֶת (turban) — is permitted, even if it rests on the tefilin. And nevertheless, if it is a heavy load of four קַבִּין (ד׳ קַבִּין) and the tefilin are pressed (נִדְחָקוֹת) — he must remove them.

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim מ״א (Kehot edition, a single seif / סָעִיף אֶחָד). English translation: DAAT. No passage is cited outside this verifiable text.

2 · היסוד

היסוד — כְּבוֹד הַתְּפִלִּין בִּנְשִׂיאַת מַשּׂאוֹי

The yesod: carrying a load and the honour of the tefilin

The Alter Rebbe's reading of this siman unfolds in three movements. First the קְדֻשָּׁה and the כָּבוֹד due to tefilin: carrying a load on the head is a גְּנַאי / דֶּרֶךְ בִּזָּיוֹן and a הֶסֵּחַ הַדַּעַת, hence the duty to remove the שֶׁל רֹאשׁ until the load is set down. Then the criterion of דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה: what is a garment whose way is to be on the head (כּוֹבַע, מִצְנֶפֶת) is permitted even on the tefilin, but a מִטְפַּחַת — which is not a מַלְבּוּשׁ — is forbidden. Finally the שִׁעוּר ד׳ קַבִּין: a heavy load that presses the tefilin (נִדְחָקוֹת) compels their removal, lest they shift from their מָקוֹם.

כְּבוֹד הַתְּפִלִּין וְאִסּוּר הַגְּנַאי

The sanctity of tefilin and the prohibition of גְּנַאי

The yesod. Tefilin are תַּשְׁמִישֵׁי קְדֻשָּׁה ; carrying a load on the head that bears them is a גְּנַאי, and involves also a הֶסֵּחַ הַדַּעַת — hence the duty to remove the שֶׁל רֹאשׁ until the load is set down.

The consequence. An object that is not a garment (מַלְבּוּשׁ) and whose way is not to be on the head — even a light one, such as a מִטְפַּחַת — is forbidden. On the גּוּף נָקִי and the הֶסֵּחַ הַדַּעַת in tefilin, cf. siman 28 and siman 44.

דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה — כּוֹבַע וּמִצְנֶפֶת

The criterion of the "manner of wearing"

The principle. The decisive criterion is the דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה: an object whose way is to be worn on the head — a כּוֹבַע or a מִצְנֶפֶת — is permitted, even resting on the tefilin, for there is here neither גְּנַאי nor loss of honour.

The extension. Conversely, what is not a מַלְבּוּשׁ (the מִטְפַּחַת, a load) places on the head a foreign object alien to the קְדֻשָּׁה — hence the prohibition. The dividing line is not weight alone, but the nature of the object and its relation to the head.

מַשּׂאוֹי כָּבֵד ד׳ קַבִּין וְנִדְחָקוֹת

The heavy load and the pressed tefilin

The principle. Even a permitted כּוֹבַע or מִצְנֶפֶת may become problematic: if it is a heavy load of ד׳ קַבִּין and the tefilin are pressed (נִדְחָקוֹת), they must be removed.

The conduct. The concern is twofold: the weight that shifts the tefilin from their מָקוֹם, and the injury to honour. The שִׁעוּר of ד׳ קַבִּין sets the threshold beyond which the leniency of דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה no longer holds.

Referral to study. The reach of the yesod set here — the כָּבוֹד due to the תַּשְׁמִישֵׁי קְדֻשָּׁה, the דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה and the שִׁעוּר ד׳ קַבִּין — touches both conduct (how one carries, what one places on the head, when one removes) and the Chabad sensibility on the קְדֻשָּׁה that surrounds the mitzva. This page presents it at the level of the principle attested in the Rav's text, without citing a chapter or a precise formulation, and without adding anything invented to the spiritual meaning. For in-depth study of this dimension — and for personal conduct on the load, the מִטְפַּחַת and the שִׁעוּר — study with a Chabad Rav and in the texts themselves.

הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשּׂאוֹי אֵיךְ יִנְהֹג בַּתְּפִלִּין — at the principle · for study, see a Chabad Rav

4 · קווי היסוד של הרב

קווי היסוד של שיטת הרב

The defining lines of the Rav's way on this siman

Three orientations that characterise the Alter Rebbe's sensibility on this siman — beyond the mere application of the rule. Each follows the structure: principle of the siman → the Rav's reading → practical consequence. Each line rests on the actual text of the Rav (§1).

קו א — כְּבוֹד הַתְּפִלִּין וְאִסּוּר גְּנַאי

The honour of tefilin and the prohibition of גְּנַאי

Principle of the siman: removing the שֶׁל רֹאשׁ as long as one carries a load (single seif).

The Rav's reading: the load on the head is a גְּנַאי and a הֶסֵּחַ הַדַּעַת ; one removes the שֶׁל רֹאשׁ until the load is set down.

Practical consequence: do not carry a load on the head while keeping the שֶׁל רֹאשׁ ; remove it beforehand.

קו ב — דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה, כּוֹבַע וּמִצְנֶפֶת

The "manner of wearing": hat and turban

Principle of the siman: what is permitted and what is forbidden on the head (single seif).

The Rav's reading: the criterion is the דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה — a כּוֹבַע or a מִצְנֶפֶת (a מַלְבּוּשׁ) is permitted even on the tefilin ; a מִטְפַּחַת, a non-garment, is forbidden.

Practical consequence: a usual head-covering is placed without concern ; a cloth or any foreign object on the head that bears tefilin, not.

קו ג — שִׁעוּר ד׳ קַבִּין וְהַתְּפִלִּין נִדְחָקוֹת

The shiur of ד׳ קבין and the pressed tefilin

Principle of the siman: the heavy load that presses the tefilin (single seif).

The Rav's reading: beyond the שִׁעוּר ד׳ קַבִּין, when the tefilin are נִדְחָקוֹת, the leniency of the head-covering no longer holds — lest they shift from their מָקוֹם.

Practical consequence: remove the tefilin under a heavy load ; rely on the Rav for assessing the שִׁעוּר.

5 · למעשה

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

Practical Chabad conduct

Points of conduct that flow directly from siman מ״א in the sensibility of the Alter Rebbe and Chabad — presented at the level of the principle, referring to the Rav for the detail of cases (the load, the head-covering, the שִׁעוּר).

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman מ״א (הַנּוֹשֵׂא מַשּׂאוֹי אֵיךְ יִנְהֹג בַּתְּפִלִּין)

  • ① Carrying a load. One who carries a load (מַשּׂאוֹי) on his head removes the שֶׁל רֹאשׁ until he has set it down — for it is a גְּנַאי and a הֶסֵּחַ הַדַּעַת. Basis: SA HaRav מ״א seif א.
  • ② The מטפחת. Even a light object like a מִטְפַּחַת (cloth) is forbidden on the head that bears tefilin, for it is not a מַלְבּוּשׁ and its way is not to be on the head. Basis: SA HaRav מ״א seif א.
  • ③ Hat and turban. What whose way is to be on the head — כּוֹבַע, מִצְנֶפֶת (דֶּרֶךְ לְבִישָׁה) — is permitted, even resting on the tefilin. Basis: SA HaRav מ״א seif א.
  • ④ The heavy load. If it is a heavy load of ד׳ קַבִּין and the tefilin are נִדְחָקוֹת (pressed, shifted) — they must be removed. Basis: SA HaRav מ״א seif א.

⚠ This section presents the Chabad conduct grounded in the Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe, siman מ״א (actual text reproduced in §1). It does not replace a rabbinic decision for a particular case. For any concrete question — the load, the head-covering, the שִׁעוּר ד׳ קַבִּין — consult your Rav, and for Chabad, a Chabad Rav.
