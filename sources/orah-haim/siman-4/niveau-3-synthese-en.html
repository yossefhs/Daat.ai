1. The Central Axiom

Universal principle of Siman ד׳:

On waking, a person receives a renewed נשמה — חדשים לבקרים: he becomes like a new creation. He then sanctifies his hands like the Kohen מקדש ידיו מן הכיור before the service. Siman ד׳ unfolds the morning netila under its two intertwined reasons: to remove the רוח רעה that rests on the hands during sleep (hence the 3 times alternated, into a כלי), and to prepare the body for the tefila through nekiut. One pours, one blesses על נטילת ידים (and אשר יצר), the right hand first.
💡 The universal key: the morning netila is not mere hygiene — it is the first avoda of the day. נוטל ידיו ומברך על נטילת ידים.
  • The berakha → על נטילת ידים (Rama: also אשר יצר)
  • The רוח רעה → pour 3 times, alternated, into a כלי, the right first
  • Nekiut → prepare the body to stand before its Master in the tefila

⚖ The structure of the siman at a glance

The heart of the siman holds in a few families: (1) the berakha — על נטילת ידים and אשר יצר (seif א'); (2) the רוח רעה and the 3 times — pour three times, do not touch before (seifim ב', ג'); (3) order and the כלי — no רביעית, into a vessel, the right first (seifim ו'–י״ב); (4) the ספקות — awake at night, before dawn, a nap: without a berakha (seifim י״ג–ט״ז); (5) the דברים הצריכים נטילה and their dangers (seifim י״ח–י״ט); (6) without water (seif כ״ב); (7) cleanliness before the tefila — the מקומות מכוסים, the ברכות השחר (seifim כ״א, כ״ג). Seifim ד'–ה', י״ז, כ' are ancillary conducts of health.

📚 The yesod of the siman: two reasons intertwine throughout the morning netila — the רוח רעה (Kabbalah, Zohar: the בת מלך that rests on the hands) and the kedusha / kohen (the renewed soul, כבריה חדשה, sanctifying one's hands like the Kohen at the kiyor). From this double cause flow all the nafka mina of the siman: the ספקות, the כלי, the רביעית, and the berakha.

4. The berakha and the 3 times — the renewed soul

Q: How does one perform the morning netila?
↓
🟢 Into a כלי — Do not pour on the ground, but into a vessel; no need for a רביעית (seifim ו', ח').
🟢 The right first — Take the vessel in the right hand, pass it to the left, pour onto the right first (seif י').
🟢 3 times alternated — Pour three times to remove the רוח רעה that rests on the hands (seif ב').
🟢 The berakha — על נטילת ידים, and אשר יצר (Rama, Abudraham, seif א').
🔴 Touch nothing before — Neither mouth, nor nose, nor ears, nor eyes before the netila (seif ג').

The renewed soul on waking

The morning netila is not a mere gesture of hygiene: חדשים לבקרים — each morning a person is restored to himself like a new creation. He sanctifies his hands like the Kohen מקדש ידיו מן הכיור before the Temple service. The 3 pourings and the berakha are thus not formalities: they mark that the first avoda of the day is to prepare oneself, body and soul renewed, to stand before one's Master.

5. The ספקות and the berakha — overview

#CaseConductSource
① Awake all night? Do the netila, but without a berakha (ספק). Seif י״ג · Rama (Rosh, Rashba)
② Rose before dawn? One is in doubt whether to repeat by day for the רוח רעה; repeat without a berakha. Seif י״ד · Rama
③ Slept during the day (nap)? One is in doubt whether to pour 3 times; do it without a berakha. Seif ט״ו
④ שכשוך (dipping into a vessel)? Counts for ק״ש / tefila, but not to remove the רוח רעה. Seif י״ב
⑤ Without water? Clean (צרור, earth) and bless על נקיות ידים; it avails for the tefila, not for the רוח רעה. Seif כ״ב
📚 The overall logic: the berakha על נטילת ידים is said only in the clear case (waking after a night of sleep). As soon as a doubt weighs on the very reason for the netila — awake all night, before dawn, a nap, without water — one applies ספק ברכות להקל: one performs the act, but without pronouncing the berakha. For the precise case, one studies Level 4 and consults the Rav.

6. The דברים הצריכים נטילה — cleanliness before the tefila

Q: When is a new netila needed during the day?
↓
🟢 On rising — Rising from bed, leaving the bathroom or the bathhouse (seif י״ח).
🟢 The body — Cutting one's nails, removing one's shoes, touching one's feet, washing one's head (seif י״ח).
🟢 The covered places — Touching a normally covered place of the body (seif י״ח).
🔴 The danger of omission — Bloodletting without netila: seven days; shaving: three days; nails: one day (seif י״ט).
🔴 Before the tefila — Do not touch calf, thigh, מקומות מכוסים (siman 92, seif כ״א).

Nekiut, the prelude to the tefila

Beyond the netila of waking, the siman enumerates the acts that demand a new netila over the course of the day (seif י״ח) and recalls the nekiut required before praying: not touching the covered places of the body, where the מלמולי זיעה are found (seif כ״א, cf. siman 92). The ברכות השחר may, for their part, precede the netila — except if one slept naked, where one does not mention the Name before having cleaned oneself (seif כ״ג). The cleanliness of the body is thus the condition for standing worthily before one's Master.

7. Golden rules — Memory Aid

The 5 golden rules of Siman ד׳
  • The berakha — על נטילת ידים and אשר יצר from waking.
  • 3 times for the רוח רעה — pour three times, alternated, into a כלי.
  • The right first — vessel from the right to the left, one pours onto the right.
  • The doubt → without a berakha — night, dawn, nap, without water: ספק ברכות להקל.
  • Nekiut before the tefila — the דברים הצריכים נטילה, the מקומות מכוסים (siman 92).

3 foundational principles to memorize:

  • ג' פעמים — 3 times to remove the רוח רעה, the right first (seifim ב', י')
  • ספק ברכות להקל — in doubt, netila without a berakha (seifim י״ג–ט״ו)

9. Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ Pitfall 1: Believing it suffices to wash once.
→ False. Seif ב': ידקדק לערות עליהם מים ג' פעמים — one pours 3 times to remove the רוח רעה that rests on the hands.
❌ Pitfall 2: Pouring indifferently onto the left hand first.
→ Seif י': one takes the vessel in the right hand, passes it to the left, and pours onto the right first.
❌ Pitfall 3: Saying the berakha in every case of doubt.
→ Seifim י״ג–ט״ו: awake all night, before dawn, a daytime nap — one does the netila without a berakha (ספק ברכות להקל).
❌ Pitfall 4: Thinking that dipping the hands (שכשוך) always suffices.
→ Seif י״ב: the שכשוך counts for ק״ש and the tefila, but not to remove the רוח רעה (and one is in doubt even in three separate waters).
❌ Pitfall 5: Neglecting a new netila after the דברים הצריכים נטילה.
→ Seifim י״ח–י״ט: rising, bathroom, nails, shoes, covered places… require a netila; the omission carries a danger. For the precise conduct, consult the Rav.

✦ ❖ ✦ The 5 Practical Commandments of Siman ד׳ in Brief

  1. Wash and bless — על נטילת ידים and אשר יצר from waking.
  2. Pour 3 times — alternated, into a כלי, to remove the רוח רעה.
  3. The right first — vessel from the right to the left.
  4. In doubt, without a berakha — night, dawn, nap, without water.
  5. Be clean before praying — the דברים הצריכים נטילה, the מקומות מכוסים → consult the Rav.
