Siman 34 — The Order of the Parshiyot and Rabbeinu Tam Tefillin (Seder ha-Parshiyot ve-Tefillin de-Rabbeinu Tam)

Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 34 — 4 seifim

The Siman — Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim siman ל״ד (4 seifim)
וּבוֹ ד׳ סְעִיפִים. סֵדֶר הַנָּחָתָן בַּבָּתִּים לְרַשִׁ״י וְהָרַמְבַּ״ם: קֹדֶשׁ מִשְּׂמֹאל הַמַּנִּיחַ בַּבַּיִת הַחִיצוֹן, וְאַחֲרָיו וְהָיָה כִּי יְבִיאֲךָ בַּבַּיִת הַשֵּׁנִי, וּשְׁמַע בַּבַּיִת הַשְּׁלִישִׁי, וְהָיָה אִם שָׁמֹעַ בַּבַּיִת הָרְבִיעִי שֶׁהוּא הַבַּיִת הַחִיצוֹן לִימִינוֹ ; וּלְרַבֵּנוּ תָּם בַּבַּיִת הַג׳ וְהָיָה אִם שָׁמֹעַ וּבַבַּיִת הַד׳ שְׁמַע ; וּמִנְהַג הָעוֹלָם כְּרַשִׁ״י וְהָרַמְבַּ״ם.
And it contains four seifim (ד׳ סעיפים): for רש״י and the רמב״ם, the order is קדש in the outer bayit (to the wearer's left), then והיה כי יביאך, שמע, and finally והיה אם שמוע in the outer bayit to his right; for רבינו תם the last two are reversed (והיה אם שמוע then שמע), and מנהג העולם כרש״י והרמב״ם. The following seifim address the ירא שמים who dons the two pairs (שתי זוגות) with כוונה and the question of the ברכה, the practice reserved for the מוחזק ומפורסם בחסידות (יוהרא), and two separate cases (של חול).

The main themes of the Siman

סֵדֶר הַפָּרָשִׁיּוֹת
the order of the parshiyot
קדש · והיה כי יביאך · שמע · והיה אם שמוע set into the four בתים. One follows the vantage of the reader facing the wearer, even for a left-handed person (אטר).
רַשִׁ״י וְרַבֵּנוּ תָּם
Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam
The machloket concerns the order of the last two parshiyot (שמע / והיה אם שמוע): the meaning of "משמאל" in מנחות. רש״י and the רמב״ם on one side, רבינו תם on the other.
מִנְהַג הָעוֹלָם כְּרַשִׁ״י
the widespread custom
מנהג העולם follows רש״י and the רמב״ם: nearly everyone dons the tefillin of רש״י alone. The שי״נין and the הויות סמוכות illuminate the writing כסדרן.
שְׁתֵּי זוּגוֹת תְּפִלִּין
the two pairs
The ירא שמים fulfills both opinions: he dons two pairs (רש״י and רבינו תם) intending to fulfill his obligation with the one that is אליבא דהלכתא, the rest being כרצועות בעלמא.
מוּחְזָק בַּחֲסִידוּת
the recognized pious one
This public practice is legitimate only for one who is established and renowned for his piety — otherwise it is יוהרא (spiritual arrogance).
שְׁנֵי כִּיסִין
two separate cases
One does not place the two pairs in a single כיס, for one is חול: two cases, with a סימן for each, so as never to confuse them.

Frequently asked questions — Siman ל״ד

What is the order of the parshiyot in the בתים of the tefillin, according to Siman 34?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 34:1, for רש״י and the רמב״ם the order is: קדש in the outer bayit (to the wearer's left), then והיה כי יביאך in the second, שמע in the third, and והיה אם שמוע in the fourth — the outer bayit to his right. For רבינו תם, the last two are reversed: והיה אם שמוע in the third and שמע in the fourth (outer). מנהג העולם — the widespread custom — follows רש״י and the רמב״ם.

Who dons the two pairs of tefillin (רש״י and רבינו תם), and what of the ברכה, according to Siman 34?

According to the Mechaber (Orach Chaim 34:2), the ירא שמים fulfills both opinions: he makes two pairs of tefillin (שתי זוגות) and dons them, intending (כוונה) to fulfill his obligation with the pair that is אליבא דהלכתא, the rest being mere straps (כרצועות בעלמא). If he knows how to place both together, he does so; otherwise he dons רש״י with the ברכה, wears them during ק״ש and the tefila, then dons רבינו תם without a ברכה after the tefila and reads שמע and והיה אם שמוע in them.

Why is the practice of the two pairs reserved for certain people, according to Siman 34?

According to the Mechaber (Orach Chaim 34:3), only one who is established and renowned for his piety (מוחזק ומפורסם בחסידות) does so in public. For one who is not, wearing the two pairs conspicuously would be יוהרא (spiritual arrogance) — the appearance of a piety one does not possess.

May the two pairs be kept in a single case, according to Siman 34?

According to the Mechaber (Orach Chaim 34:4), one does not place the two pairs in a single case (כיס), for one of them is חול (mundane) and it is forbidden to place it in a tefillin case. One therefore makes two cases, with a sign (סימן) for each, so as never to put one in place of the other.
