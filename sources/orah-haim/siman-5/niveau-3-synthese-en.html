1. The Central Axiom

Universal principle of Siman ה׳:

A blessing is not a formula recited out of habit: it is a word addressed to a present Master. Thus the Mechaber opens with יְכַוֵּן בַּבְּרָכוֹת פֵּרוּשׁ הַמִּלּוֹת — in the blessings, intend the meaning of the words one pronounces. And when the speech touches the divine Names, the kavana sharpens: at the הוי״ה, to intend its reading אדנות — אדון הכל, Master of all — and its writing in yod-hé — היה הוה ויהיה, He was, is, and will be; at the אלהים, to intend that He is תקיף בעל הכחות, the Mighty One, Master of all the forces. The single seif of this siman thus unfolds a ladder of kavana: from the meaning of the words up to the awareness of Whom one blesses.
💡 The universal key: to bless is to stand before Hashem knowing to Whom one speaks. יכוין בברכות פירוש המלות.
  • The meaning of the words → intend what one pronounces
  • The Name הוי״ה → אדון הכל (reading) + היה הוה ויהיה (writing)
  • The Name אלהים → תקיף בעל הכחות, the Mighty One, Master of all forces

⚖ The structure of the seif at a glance

The single seif of the Mechaber holds in a three-rung ladder: (1) the meaning of the words — the base kavana, in every blessing פירוש המלות; (2) the Name הוי״ה — a double intention: its reading אדון הכל and its writing היה הוה ויהיה; (3) the Name אלהים — תקיף בעל הכחות, the Mighty One, Master of all forces. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav (the Alter Rebbe) unfolds this same seif into 3 seifim: adding the conduct when the Name is written א-ד (intention "אדון הכל" only) and the דקדוק of the vocalization of the aleph (חטף פתח / שבא).

📚 The yesod of the siman: the entire kavana rises by degrees. First the words (to understand what one says); then the Names, where one no longer stops at the literal sense but at what the Name reveals of Hashem: הוי״ה, the Master above time (transcendence, אדון הכל + היה הוה ויהיה), and אלהים, the Master of all the forces (the power deployed in the world, בעל הכחות). To bless thus becomes an act of knowledge — דעת — and of service of the heart.

4. The Name הוי״ה — reading and writing

Q: What must one bear in mind when saying the Name הוי״ה?
↓
🟢 The reading (אדנות) — One reads אד-ני: intend אדון הכל, that He is the Master of all (Kingship, malchut).
🟢 The writing (יו״ד ה״א) — The Name is written in yod-hé: intend היה הוה ויהיה, He was, is, and will be (eternity, above time).
🟢 Both together — The kavana of the הוי״ה unites reading and writing: the present Master and the Being above time.
🔴 Do not pronounce the Name as it is written — One always reads אד-ני; the writing is only an object of thought.

Why two intentions?

The Name הוי״ה carries a double treasure. As it is read (אד-ני), it speaks of Kingship: Hashem is אדון הכל, the Master of all, to Whom everything belongs — this is the near aspect, the one before Whom I stand. As it is written (yod-hé), it speaks of eternity: היה הוה ויהיה, He who was, is, and will be, above time — this is the transcendent aspect. The blessing unites the two: I address the present Master who is also the eternal Being. For the deepening of this relation (transcendence / immanence, הוי״ה and אלהים), the path of the שער היחוד והאמונה of the Tanya illuminates the principle — one will study it with a Chabad Rav.

5. The Name אלהים — בעל הכחות

Q: What must one bear in mind when saying the Name אלהים?
↓
🟢 תקיף — Intend that He is the Mighty, the Strong (the Mechaber: תקיף; the SA HaRav adds ואמיץ).
🟢 בעל הכחות — Master of the capacity (בעל היכולת) and of all the forces (בעל הכחות כולם).
🟢 בעליונים ובתחתונים — The SA HaRav specifies: to Whom belongs the capacity in the upper and lower worlds.

הוי״ה and אלהים — the principle

Where הוי״ה speaks of the Being above time (transcendence), אלהים speaks of the power deployed in the world, בעל הכחות — all the forces of creation have their source in Him. Jewish thought teaches that these two Names are not two powers, but a single unity: the יחוד of הוי״ה and אלהים. This is precisely the subject of the שער היחוד והאמונה of the Tanya, to be studied in principle with a Chabad Rav. For the practical kavana of this siman, it suffices to retain: at אלהים, intend that He is תקיף בעל הכחות כולם.

For the case where one pronounced a blessing without kavana, one distinguishes according to the place: one studies Level 4 and consults the Rav for the practical conduct.

7. Golden rules — Memory Aid

The 5 golden rules of Siman ה׳
  • The meaning of the words — פירוש המלות: to understand what one pronounces.
  • הוי״ה reading — אדון הכל: Master of all (Kingship).
  • הוי״ה writing — היה הוה ויהיה: He was, is, and will be (eternity).
  • אלהים — תקיף בעל הכחות: the Mighty One, Master of all forces.
  • Aim, without blocking — the kavana is the ideal everywhere, מעכב in certain places (simanim 60, 101) → the Rav.

8. Mnemonic — Memory Aid

Acrostic: M-R-W-P — the 4 aims of the kavana
  • Meaning — of what one pronounces (פירוש המלות).
  • Reading of the הוי״ה — אדון הכל, Master of all.
  • Writing of the הוי״ה — היה הוה ויהיה, above time.
  • Power (אלהים) — תקיף בעל הכחות, Master of all forces.
3 foundational principles to memorize:
  • פירוש המלות — the kavana of the meaning of the words, in every blessing
  • אדון הכל + היה הוה ויהיה — the double intention of the Name הוי״ה
  • בעל הכחות — the intention of the Name אלהים, the Mighty One

9. Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ Pitfall 1: Reciting the blessings mechanically, without thinking of the meaning.
→ The seif: יכוין בברכות פירוש המלות — the kavana of the meaning of the words is the very heart of the blessing.
❌ Pitfall 2: Believing there is a single intention for the Name הוי״ה.
→ The seif: two intentions — the reading אדון הכל and the writing היה הוה ויהיה.
❌ Pitfall 3: Confusing the kavana of the Name הוי״ה with that of the Name אלהים.
→ הוי״ה = above time (היה הוה ויהיה, אדון הכל); אלהים = תקיף בעל הכחות, the Master of all forces.
❌ Pitfall 4: Wanting to pronounce the Name as it is written.
→ One always reads אד-ני; the writing in yod-hé is only an object of thought, never of pronunciation.
❌ Pitfall 5: Turning the kavana into a scrupulous block that paralyzes prayer.
→ The kavana is the ideal everywhere, מעכב in certain places only (1st berakha, 1st verse of the Shema — simanim 60, 101). For the case where one missed the kavana, consult the Rav.

✦ ❖ ✦ The 5 Practical Commandments of Siman ה׳ in Brief

  1. Understand what you say — פירוש המלות: the kavana of the meaning of the words.
  2. הוי״ה, read the Master of all — אדון הכל (reading adnut).
  3. הוי״ה, intend the eternity — היה הוה ויהיה (writing yod-hé).
  4. אלהים, intend the power — תקיף בעל הכחות, Master of all forces.
  5. Aim without blocking — מעכב in certain places (simanim 60, 101) → consult the Rav.
