1. The Central Axiom

Universal principle of Siman י״ז:

The siman classifies who is obligated in tsitsit — four categories, four statuses. The סומא (blind man) is חייב: although it is written וראיתם אותו, he is included by אשר תכסה בה — and « וראיתם אותו » comes to exclude the כסות לילה (night garment) — see סי׳ י״ח. Women and slaves are פטורים, for it is a מצות עשה שהזמן גרמא; the Rama: if they wish to wrap themselves and recite the blessing, they may — but it appears as יוהרא (ostentation), all the more so since tsitsit is not a חובת גברא (one is not bound to buy a talit — cf. סי׳ י״ט). The טומטום ואנדרוגינוס are חייבין מספק: they wrap themselves בלא ברכה. And the קטן היודע להתעטף: his father must buy him tsitsit to educate him — חינוך.
💡 The universal key: the siman is a table of statuses — for each person, ask: is he obligated (חייב), exempt (פטור), or in doubt (ספק)? And for the child: where does חינוך begin?
  • סומא → חייב — included by אשר תכסה בה; wears and blesses fully
  • נשים ועבדים → פטורים (מ״ע שהזמן גרמא); Rama: possible but מחזי כיוהרא
  • טומטום ואנדרוגינוס → חייבין מספק → they wrap themselves בלא ברכה
  • קטן היודע להתעטף → his father buys him tsitsit — חינוך (2 in front / 2 behind)

2. Master table of the ג׳ seifim

The ג׳ סעיפים of the Mechaber unfold four categories: (A) the סומא — חייב, included by אשר תכסה בה; (B) women and slaves — exempt (מ״ע שהזמן גרמא), with the Rama's יוהרא caveat; (C) the טומטום ואנדרוגינוס — חייבין מספק, they wrap themselves בלא ברכה; (D) the קטן היודע להתעטף — the father must buy him tsitsit for חינוך.

⚖ The structure of the siman at a glance

The ג׳ סעיפים of the Mechaber decide who is obligated in tsitsit: (A) the סומא — חייב, included by אשר תכסה בה, « וראיתם אותו » excluding the כסות לילה; (B) women and slaves — פטורים (מ״ע שהזמן גרמא); Rama: they may wrap themselves and bless, but מחזי כיוהרא, all the more so since it is not a חובת גברא; (C) the טומטום ואנדרוגינוס — חייבין מספק, בלא ברכה; (D) the קטן היודע להתעטף — חינוך, with the Rama's threshold (2 in front / 2 behind, holding the tsitsit during the Shema). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav (the Alter Rebbe) unfolds this siman into ד׳ סעיפים: the blind man, women and slaves, טומטום ואנדרוגינוס, and the קטן.

3. סומא חייב — the blind man is fully obligated

Q: Is the blind man obligated in tsitsit, when it is written « וראיתם אותו »?
↓
⚠️ וראיתם אותו — At first sight, « and you shall see it » would seem to exclude one who does not see (seif 1).
🟢 נתרבה מאשר תכסה בה — He is included by « אשר תכסה בה »: every garment with which one covers oneself → the סומא is חייב (seif 1).
📚 וראיתם אותו — to exclude the כסות לילה — The verse comes to exclude the כסות לילה (night garment), not the blind man — see סי׳ י״ח (seif 1).

The law of the סומא

The siman opens on an apparent paradox: the Torah says וראיתם אותו — « and you shall see it » — and yet the סומא is חייב in tsitsit. The resolution (Menachot 43a): the blind man is included by « אשר תכסה בה » — « with which you cover yourself » — which embraces every person wearing a four-cornered garment, whether he sees or not; and « וראיתם אותו » did not come to exclude the blind man but to exclude the כסות לילה, the night garment, which is not subject to tsitsit — that is the subject of סימן י״ח. The blind man therefore wears the talit with a blessing, fully obligated. The precise drasha (sight as טעם or as תנאי) is developed in Level 2; the psak, in Level 4 (refer to the Rav).

4. נשים ועבדים — exempt, the יוהרא and the חובת גברא

Q: Are women and slaves obligated in tsitsit?
↓
🟢 נשים ועבדים פטורים — Exempt, for it is a מצות עשה שהזמן גרמא (seif 2).
🟡 Rama — הרשות בידם — If they wish to wrap themselves and bless, they may, as with the other מ״ע שהזמן גרמא (תוס׳, הרא״ש, הר״ן) (seif 2).
🔴 אך מחזי כיוהרא — But it appears as יוהרא (ostentation) → women should not wear tsitsit (seif 2).
📚 הואיל ואינו חובת גברא — All the more so since it is not an obligation on the person: no one is bound to buy a talit — cf. סי׳ י״ט (seif 2).
📚 The yesod of seif 2: tsitsit is a מצות עשה שהזמן גרמא — a positive commandment bound to time (the night, the כסות לילה, delimits its scope) — from which women and slaves are exempt. The Rama sets two markers, with measure: on one hand, הרשות בידם — those who wish to wrap themselves and bless may do so, as with the other מ״ע שהזמן גרמא; on the other, specifically for tsitsit, it appears as יוהרא — for it is not a חובת גברא: no one, not even a man, is bound to buy a talit in order to become obligated (it is the four-cornered garment that triggers the obligation — cf. סי׳ י״ט); publicly taking on a mitzvah from which one is exempt, where no one is ever bound in itself, carries a scent of ostentation. The machloket on women's blessing (the Ashkenazi and the Sephardi custom) is developed in Level 2; in practice — respect for the communities and referral to the Rav (Level 4).

5. טומטום ואנדרוגינוס — the ספק, wrapping בלא ברכה

Q: What is the status of the טומטום and the אנדרוגינוס?
↓
📚 Definitions — The טומטום: it is not known whether male or female; the אנדרוגינוס: presents both (seif 2).
🟡 חייבין מספק — Perhaps male → obligated out of doubt: they wrap themselves (seif 2).
🔴 ויתעטפו בלא ברכה — But without a blessing: the ספק compels the act, not the blessing (seif 2).
🟢 Rama — According to the custom where women bless on the מ״ע שהזמן גרמא, they too would bless (seif 2).

The yesod of the ספק

The טומטום (whose sex remains indeterminate) and the אנדרוגינוס (who bears both characteristics) are perhaps male: they are therefore חייבין מספק — the doubt compels one to fulfil the act, and they wrap themselves. But that same doubt withholds the blessing: בלא ברכה. The Rama adds a nuance of consistency: in communities where women bless on the מ״ע שהזמן גרמא, the טומטום and the אנדרוגינוס — who are not « less » than women, whatever their status — would bless too. The foundation (the ספק, the rules on blessings in case of doubt) is developed in Level 2; the psak, in Level 4 (refer to the Rav).

6. קטן היודע להתעטף — the חינוך and its threshold

Q: From when must one give tsitsit to a child?
↓
🟢 קטן היודע להתעטף — As soon as the child knows how to wrap himself: אביו צריך ליקח לו ציצית לחנכו — his father must buy him tsitsit to educate him (seif 3).
📚 Rama — the threshold — Precisely: that he knows how to wrap 2 tsitsit in front and 2 behind (הגהת מיימוני פ״ג) (seif 3).
📚 Rama — during the Shema — And that he knows how to hold the tsitsit in his hand during the ק״ש (מרדכי סוף פרק לולב הגזול) (seif 3).
📚 The yesod of seif 3: the קטן is exempt in himself, but the duty of חינוך — education in the mitzvot (Sukka 42a) — rests on the father: as soon as the child knows how to wrap himself, his father must buy him tsitsit. The Rama gives the operational criterion: this « knowing how to wrap » means knowing how to arrange two tsitsit in front and two behind, and knowing how to hold the tsitsit in his hand during the Shema — that is, an intelligent wearing of the mitzvah, not a mere gesture. It is thus not a fixed age but an ability: as soon as it is there, the talit katan enters the child's life. The detail (whose חינוך, the age, the modern טלית קטן) is developed in Level 2; the psak, in Level 4 (refer to the Rav).

7. Golden rules — Memory Aid

The 4 golden rules of Siman י״ז
  • סומא חייב — included by אשר תכסה בה; « וראיתם אותו » excludes the כסות לילה (סי׳ י״ח) — he wears and blesses.
  • נשים ועבדים פטורים — מ״ע שהזמן גרמא; Rama: possible with a blessing, but מחזי כיוהרא (no חובת גברא — סי׳ י״ט).
  • טומטום ואנדרוגינוס — חייבין מספק → they wrap themselves בלא ברכה.
  • קטן היודע להתעטף — his father buys him tsitsit: חינוך (2 in front / 2 behind + holding during the ק״ש).

9. Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ Pitfall 1: Believing that « וראיתם אותו » exempts the blind man.
→ The seif: the סומא is חייב — נתרבה מאשר תכסה בה; the verse excludes the כסות לילה (seif 1).
❌ Pitfall 2: Turning the women's exemption into an absolute prohibition.
→ The seif: Rama — הרשות בידם to wrap themselves and bless; it is the יוהרא that restrains, not a prohibition (seif 2).
❌ Pitfall 3: Believing that tsitsit is a חובת גברא — that one would be bound to buy a talit.
→ The seif: אינו חובת גברא — it is the four-cornered garment that triggers the obligation (cf. סי׳ י״ט) (seif 2).
❌ Pitfall 4: Having the טומטום or the אנדרוגינוס bless like a certain male.
→ The seif: חייבין מספק → they wrap themselves בלא ברכה (Rama: except according to the custom where women bless) (seif 2).
❌ Pitfall 5: Waiting for the bar mitzvah to give one's son a talit katan.
→ The seif: קטן היודע להתעטף — as soon as he knows how to wrap 2 in front / 2 behind and hold the tsitsit during the ק״ש: חינוך (seif 3).

✦ ❖ ✦ The 3 Practical Commandments of Siman י״ז in Brief

  1. Honour the blind man's obligation — the סומא חייב: he wraps himself and blesses fully, included by אשר תכסה בה.
  2. Respect the custom for women — exempt (מ״ע שהזמן גרמא); no יוהרא: the conduct follows the community and its Rav; the טומטום ואנדרוגינוס wraps himself בלא ברכה.
  3. Educate your son — קטן היודע להתעטף: buy him a talit katan as soon as he manages 2 in front / 2 behind (SA HaRav, ד׳ סעיפים).
