Siman 36 — The Precise Form of the Letters (Dikduk Tsurat ha-Otiyot)

Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 36 — 3 seifim

The Siman — Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim siman ל״ו (3 seifim)
וּבוֹ ג׳ סְעִיפִים. צָרִיךְ לְדַקְדֵּק בִּכְתִיבַת הָאוֹתִיּוֹת שֶׁלֹּא תִשְׁתַּנֶּה צוּרַת שׁוּם אַחַת מֵהֶן וְלֹא תִדְמֶה לְאַחֶרֶת. (הגה) וּלְכַתְּחִלָּה יִכְתּוֹב בִּכְתִיבָה תַּמָּה, וְהוּא יָדוּעַ אֵצֶל הַסּוֹפְרִים ; מִיהוּ אִם שִׁנָּה בְּצוּרַת הַכְּתָב אֵינוֹ פָּסוּל. כָּל אוֹת צְרִיכָה לִהְיוֹת גֹּלֶם אֶחָד ... חוּץ מֵה״א וְקוּ״ף שֶׁאֵין לִיגַּע הָרֶגֶל בַּגַּג. וְצָרִיךְ לְתַיֵּג שעטנ״ז ג״ץ.
And it contains three seifim (שלשה סעיפים): one must be precise in writing the letters so that none changes its form (שלא תשתנה צורת שום אחת מהן) or resembles another ; לכתחילה one writes in כתיבה תמה, but if one changed the form of the כתב it is not פסול (as long as the letter remains recognizable). The second seif: each letter must be a single body (גולם אחד) — the נקודות of the א, the יודי״ן of the שי״ן and עי״ן, and the back of the צד״י must touch, except the leg of the ה״א and קו״ף. The third: the תגין (crowns) on שעטנ״ז ג״ץ.

The main themes of the Siman

דִּקְדּוּק צוּרַת הָאוֹתִיּוֹת
the precision of the form
Writing with precision so no letter changes its form or resembles another — a ד that becomes a ר, a ה that becomes a ח.
כְּתִיבָה תַּמָּה
the whole writing
The Rama: לכתחילה one writes in כתיבה תמה, the form known to the sofrim ; but if one changed the form of the כתב, it is not פסול.
שִׁנָּה בְּצוּרָה
changing the form
As long as the letter remains recognizable (תינוק דלא חכים ולא טיפש) it is kosher ; פסול only if the form is destroyed (הפסיד צורת האות).
גֹּלֶם אֶחָד
a single body
Each letter is one body: the נקודה of the א (like a יו״ד), the יודי״ן of the שי״ן and עי״ן, the back of the צד״י must touch — otherwise פסולה.
הֵ״א וְקוּ״ף
the hei and the qof
Exception to the rule of נגיעות: the leg (רגל) of the ה״א and קו״ף must not touch the roof (גג) ; if it touches, פסול.
תָּגִין — שעטנ״ז ג״ץ
the crowns
One must crown the letters שעטנ״ז ג״ץ ; the sofrim tag others. If one did not tag, it is not פסול (not מעכב).

Frequently asked questions — Siman ל״ו

What must the sofer take care of in the form of the letters, according to Siman 36?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 36:1, one must be precise in writing the letters so that none changes its form (שלא תשתנה צורת שום אחת מהן) or resembles another (ולא תדמה לאחרת). The Rama adds: לכתחילה one writes in כתיבה תמה, the form known to the sofrim ; but if one changed the form of the כתב, it is not פסול as long as the letter remains recognizable.

What does it mean that each letter must be a single body (גולם אחד), according to Siman 36?

According to the Mechaber (Orach Chaim 36:2), each letter must be a single body (גולם אחד). One must therefore take care that the נקודה on the א (formed like a יו״ד) and the one below it, the יודי״ן of the שי״ן and עי״ן, and the back of the צד״י do touch the body of the letter ; one whose strokes do not touch is פסולה. So too for the other letters — except the ה״א and קו״ף, whose leg (רגל) must not touch the roof (גג): if it touches, פסול.

Which letters bear the תגין (crowns), and are they indispensable, according to Siman 36?

According to the Mechaber (Orach Chaim 36:3), one must crown (לתייג) the letters שעטנ״ז ג״ץ, and the sofrim have the custom of tagging other letters. But if one did not tag — even the letters שעטנ״ז ג״ץ — it is not פסול: the תגין are not מעכב bediavad.
