1. The Central Axiom

Universal principle of Siman כ״ב:

The siman — a single seif (סעיף אחד) — treats the ברכת שהחיינו on a new talit: one who bought a talit and made tsitsit on it (קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית) recites שהחיינו, for this talit is no less than new garments (דלא גרע מכלים חדשים) — the berakha springs from the joy of the new (Berakhot 59b), not from the mitsva as such. And the Rama adds: if one did not bless at the time of the making (בשעת עשייה), one blesses at the first wrapping (בשעת עטוף ראשון — Maimoni, Nimukei Yosef). The joy of the mitsva is thus not lost: it has a second appointment.
💡 The universal key: the whole siman holds in three markers — what (שהחיינו on the new talit), why (the joy of the new), when (the making, otherwise the first wrapping).
  • קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית → מברך שהחיינו — the new talit calls for the berakha
  • דלא גרע מכלים חדשים → the foundation: the joy of the new garment (Berakhot 59b), not the mitsva as such
  • בשעת עשייה → the primary moment: the making of the tsitsit
  • Rama — בשעת עטוף ראשון → forgotten at the making → one blesses at the first wrapping

2. Master table of the סעיף אחד

The סעיף אחד of the Mechaber unfolds into three axes: (A) the din — קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית → מברך שהחיינו; (B) the foundation — דלא גרע מכלים חדשים: the berakha comes from the joy of the new; (C) the Rama — forgotten בשעת עשייה → one blesses בשעת עטוף ראשון.

AxisSeifHeart of the halakhaMarker
A. קנה טלית
ועשה בו ציצית		 ① One who bought a talit and made tsitsit on it recites שהחיינו. The primary moment of the berakha is the making (בשעת עשייה) — that is where the joy of the new garment become a talit of mitsva is born. מברך שהחיינו
B. דלא גרע
מכלים חדשים		 ① The foundation: this talit is no less than new garments, over which one says שהחיינו (Berakhot 59b). The berakha thus bears on the joy of the new (the קנין), not on the mitsva as such — hence the absence of an equivalent שהחיינו on tefillin (machloket, developed in Level 2). כלים חדשים
C. Rama —
עטוף ראשון		 ① (הגה) Rama: ואם לא בירך בשעת עשייה מברך בשעת עטוף ראשון — if one did not bless at the time of the making, one blesses at the first wrapping (sources: Maimoni and Nimukei Yosef). The berakha then joins the first fulfilment of the mitsva, just before לְהִתְעַטֵּף בַּצִּיצִית. בשעת עטוף ראשון

⚖ The structure of the siman at a glance

The סעיף אחד of the Mechaber decides the ברכת שהחיינו on a new talit: (A) קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית → מברך שהחיינו; (B) the foundation — דלא גרע מכלים חדשים (Berakhot 59b); (C) the Rama — forgotten בשעת עשייה → בשעת עטוף ראשון (Maimoni, Nimukei Yosef). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav (the Alter Rebbe) likewise treats this siman in a single seif (סעיף אחד) and refines the moments: a talit fit to wear as it is at the purchase → שהחיינו at the purchase; cloth made into a garment → שהחיינו at the attaching of the tsitsit (הטלת הציצית), and failing that at the first wearing — see Level 4.

3. קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית — the berakha at the making

Q: One bought a new talit and made tsitsit on it — does one say שהחיינו?
↓
🟢 קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית — Yes: מברך שהחיינו (seif 1).
📚 בשעת עשייה — The primary moment of the berakha: the making of the tsitsit (seif 1, as presupposed by the Rama).
🟢 גדול או קטן — Talit gadol or talit katan: the joy of the new garment holds for both (practical case — detail in Level 4).

The law of the new talit

The siman holds in one line: one who bought a talit and made tsitsit on it recites שהחיינו. The Mechaber places the berakha at the moment the talit becomes a talit — the making of the tsitsit (בשעת עשייה): the instant where the joy of the purchase meets the joy of the mitsva, and the halakha wants that joy to be sanctified by a berakha. The question of the exact moment of the obligation (עשייה or עיטוף ראשון) and its relation to the principle עובר לעשייתן are developed in Level 2; the psak, in Level 4 (refer to the Rav).

4. דלא גרע מכלים חדשים — the yesod: the joy of the new

Q: What does this שהחיינו bear on — the mitsva of tsitsit or the new garment?
↓
📚 דלא גרע מכלים חדשים — The talit is no less than new garments (seif 1).
📚 Berakhot 59b — One who buys new garments says שהחיינו: the berakha of renewal and joy.
🟡 Not the mitsva as such — Hence: no equivalent שהחיינו on tefillin (machloket — brief reference in Level 2).
📚 The yesod of the seif: the formula דלא גרע מכלים חדשים is the key to the whole siman. The Mechaber does not say « one blesses שהחיינו on the mitsva of tsitsit », but « this talit is no less than new garments »: the berakha is that of כלים חדשים (Berakhot 59b) — the joy of the new, of the קנין — applied to the talit. This is why the mitsva of tsitsit, which is not periodic (unlike the sukka or the lulav), does not of itself engender a שהחיינו — and why, on tefillin (which are not a garment of joy), the question is a machloket. The full chakira (שהחיינו על המצוה או על הקנין) is developed in Level 2.

5. The Rama — forgotten → the first wrapping

Q: One forgot to bless שהחיינו at the making — is the berakha lost?
↓
🟢 Rama — מברך בשעת עטוף ראשון — No: one blesses at the first wrapping (seif 1, הגה).
📚 The sources — מיימוני ונימוקי יוסף: Hagahot Maimoni and Nimukei Yosef (seif 1, הגה).
🟡 First wrapping gone by without a berakha — ספק ברכות: refer to the Rav (Level 4).

The yesod of the Rama

The Rama (in the name of the Hagahot Maimoni and the Nimukei Yosef) teaches that the berakha missed at the making is made up at the first wrapping (בשעת עטוף ראשון): the joy of the new talit is still whole at the moment one wraps oneself in it for the first time, and the berakha then joins the first fulfilment of the mitsva. Behind this din lies the chakira of the moment of the obligation — is the berakha due בשעת עשייה (at the making, over the new) or בשעת עיטוף ראשון (at the first wearing, over the use)? — and its relation to the principle עובר לעשייתן: all of it developed in Level 2; the psak, in Level 4.

6. The ladder of moments — when to say שהחיינו

Q: At which moment, concretely, does one say שהחיינו on the new talit?
↓
① בשעת עשייה — The primary moment: at the making of the tsitsit (seif 1).
② Forgotten → בשעת עטוף ראשון — Rama: at the first wrapping (seif 1, הגה).
③ SA HaRav (single seif) — Talit fit to wear at the purchase → at the purchase; cloth → at the attaching of the tsitsit, otherwise at the first wearing (Level 4).
④ Current practice — Talit katan, new clothing, ספק ברכות → refer to the Rav.
📚 The yesod of the moments: the berakha of שהחיינו follows the joy — it is said at the moment when the joy of the new talit is most whole. First appointment: the making (בשעת עשייה). Make-up appointment: the first wrapping (בשעת עטוף ראשון — Rama). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav refines further: if the talit was fit to wear as it is at the purchase, the joy (and the berakha) is at the purchase itself; if one bought cloth to make a garment of it, the joy blossoms only at the attaching of the tsitsit, and failing that at the first wearing. In current practice (talit katan, ספק ברכות להקל), one consults one's Rav — Level 4.

7. Golden rules — Memory Aid

The 4 golden rules of Siman כ״ב
  • קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית — new talit + tsitsit → מברך שהחיינו.
  • דלא גרע מכלים חדשים — the foundation: the joy of the new (Berakhot 59b), not the mitsva as such.
  • Forgotten בשעת עשייה — Rama: one blesses בשעת עטוף ראשון (Maimoni, Nimukei Yosef).
  • Current practice — talit katan, clothing, ספק ברכות → refer to the Rav (Level 4).

8. Mnemonic — Memory Aid

Acrostic: A-K-I — the three markers of Siman כ״ב
  • Assiya (בשעת עשייה) — the berakha at the making of the tsitsit.
  • Kelim chadashim (דלא גרע מכלים חדשים) — the foundation: the joy of the new garment.
  • Ittuf rishon (בשעת עטוף ראשון) — forgotten → one blesses at the first wrapping (Rama).
3 foundational formulas to memorize:
  • קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית מברך שהחיינו — the new talit calls for the berakha
  • דלא גרע מכלים חדשים — it is no less than new garments
  • ואם לא בירך בשעת עשייה מברך בשעת עטוף ראשון — the Rama's make-up

9. Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ Pitfall 1: Believing that this שהחיינו bears on the mitsva of tsitsit itself.
→ The seif: דלא גרע מכלים חדשים — it is the berakha of the joy of the new (Berakhot 59b), applied to the talit (seif 1).
❌ Pitfall 2: Systematically waiting for the first wearing to bless.
→ The seif: the primary moment is the making (בשעת עשייה); the first wrapping is only the make-up (seif 1).
❌ Pitfall 3: Believing the berakha lost if one forgot at the making.
→ The seif: Rama — מברך בשעת עטוף ראשון (Maimoni, Nimukei Yosef) (seif 1, הגה).
❌ Pitfall 4: Deducing from the talit an automatic שהחיינו on tefillin.
→ The seif: the berakha comes from כלים חדשים — tefillin are not a garment of joy: machloket, brief reference in Level 2.
❌ Pitfall 5: Deciding alone the current cases (talit katan, first wrapping gone by without a berakha).
→ The seif does not decide them: ספק ברכות — refer to the Rav (Level 4).

✦ ❖ ✦ The 3 Practical Commandments of Siman כ״ב in Brief

  1. Bless at the making — קנה טלית ועשה בו ציצית מברך שהחיינו: the new talit calls for the berakha already at the עשייה.
  2. Remember the foundation — דלא גרע מכלים חדשים: it is the joy of the new (Berakhot 59b) that the berakha sanctifies.
  3. Make up at the first wrapping — Rama: forgotten at the making → מברך בשעת עטוף ראשון; beyond that, ספק ברכות → the Rav (SA HaRav, סעיף אחד).
