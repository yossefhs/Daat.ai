Siman 37 — The Time for Tefillin and Who Is Obligated (Zman Hanachat ha-Tefillin u-Mi Hem ha-Chayavim)

The Siman — Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim siman ל״ז (3 seifim)
וּבוֹ שְׁלֹשָׁה סְעִיפִים. גָּדוֹל שְׂכַר מִצְוַת תְּפִלִּין, וְכָל מִי שֶׁאֵינוֹ מַנִּיחָם הוּא בִּכְלַל פּוֹשְׁעֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל בְּגוּפָן. מִצְוָתָן לִהְיוֹתָם עָלָיו כָּל הַיּוֹם, אֲבָל מִפְּנֵי שֶׁצְּרִיכִים גּוּף נָקִי — שֶׁלֹּא יָפִיחַ בָּהֶם וְשֶׁלֹּא יַסִּיחַ דַּעְתּוֹ מֵהֶם — נָהֲגוּ שֶׁלֹּא לְהַנִּיחָם כָּל הַיּוֹם, וּמִכָּל מָקוֹם צָרִיךְ כָּל אָדָם לִיזָּהֵר לִהְיוֹתָם עָלָיו בִּשְׁעַת קְרִיאַת שְׁמַע וּתְפִלָּה. וְקָטָן הַיּוֹדֵעַ לִשְׁמֹר תְּפִלִּין — חַיָּב אָבִיו לִקְנוֹת לוֹ תְּפִלִּין לְחַנְּכוֹ.
And it contains three seifim (ג׳ סעיפים): great is the reward of the mitsva of tefillin, and whoever does not don them is among the פושעי ישראל בגופן; their mitsva would be to wear them all day (מצוותן כל היום), but since they require a clean body (גוף נקי — not to pass wind, not to divert one's mind from them) and not everyone can be careful, the custom became to wear them only at ק״ש ותפלה; finally the חינוך of the קטן who knows to guard his tefillin — his father must buy them for him to train him (Rama: וי״א from י״ג שנים ויום אחד, בעל העיטור, וכן נהגו ואין לשנות).

The main themes of the Siman

גֹּדֶל שְׂכַר הַמִּצְוָה
the reward of the mitsva
גדול שכר מצות תפילין: the mitsva of tefillin bears an immense reward — the sign the Jew wears on his arm and head throughout the prayer.
פּוֹשְׁעֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל בְּגוּפָן
"with their body"
Whoever does not don tefillin is counted among the פושעי ישראל בגופן — the sin cleaves to the body itself (קרקפתא דלא מנח תפילין, ראש השנה יז.).
מִצְוָתָן כָּל הַיּוֹם
all day
מצוותן להיותם עליו כל היום: by right, the mitsva would be to be in tefillin all day — a continual mitsva, מצווה תמידית.
גּוּף נָקִי
a clean body
Tefillin require a גוף נקי: not to pass wind while wearing them (שלא יפיח) and not to divert one's mind (שלא יסיח דעתו). Hence נהגו שלא להניחם כל היום.
בִּשְׁעַת ק״ש וּתְפִלָּה
at ק״ש and תפלה
Though the custom is not to wear them all day, everyone must take care to have them on at least בשעת ק״ש ותפלה — the heart of the day.
חִינּוּךְ הַקָּטָן
the child's training
A קטן who knows to guard his tefillin in purity — his father must buy him tefillin לחנכו. Rama: וי״א from י״ג שנים ויום אחד (בעל העיטור), וכן נהגו ואין לשנות.

Frequently asked questions — Siman ל״ז

Why does one wear tefillin mainly at ק״ש and the תפלה, and not all day, according to Siman 37?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim 37:2, the mitsva would be to wear them all day (מצוותן להיותם עליו כל היום). But because tefillin require a clean body (גוף נקי) — not to pass wind while wearing them and not to divert one's mind from them — and not every person can be careful, the custom became not to wear them all day. Nevertheless everyone must take care to have them on at least during ק״ש and the תפלה.

From what age does a קטן don tefillin, according to Siman 37?

According to the Mechaber (Orach Chaim 37:3), a קטן who knows how to guard his tefillin in purity — not to sleep in them nor pass wind in them (the Rama adds: nor enter a lavatory with them) — his father must buy him tefillin to train him (לחנכו). The Rama brings another view (וי״א): this קטן is precisely one who is י״ג שנים ויום אחד (thirteen years and one day, בעל העיטור), and such is the custom, וכן נהגו ואין לשנות.

How grave is it not to don tefillin, according to Siman 37?

According to the Mechaber (Orach Chaim 37:1), great is the reward of the mitsva of tefillin (גדול שכר מצות תפילין), and whoever does not don them is counted among the פושעי ישראל בגופן — the transgressors of Israel "with their body." The gravity is that the sin cleaves to the body itself, for failing to bear the sign of the mitsva.
