Siman 248 — Traveling by Ship or Caravan in the Days Before Shabbos

The Alter Rebbe's shitah on the 14 seifim that codify the three-day rule before Shabbos (תלת-יומא קודם השבת) — when one may begin a sea or caravan journey if one knows it will extend into Shabbos.
