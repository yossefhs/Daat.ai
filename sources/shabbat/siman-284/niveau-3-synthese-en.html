1. The central Axiom

Siman רפ"ד in one sentence.
The haftarah is a twin-city of the Torah reading on Shabbos: it lives through and after the Torah, preserves the memory of an era of persecution where the Neviim replaced the Torah, and counts 21 pesukim minimum (3 × 7 olim) + 7 brachos (2 before, 5 after) — always mei-inyana shel parashah (in thematic connection with the parashah).

5. The heart of the siman — the haftarah as indivisible unit

The most subtle point of siman רפ"ד is neither the number of brachos nor the calendar of the haftaros, but a structural idea which governs several halachos of seif ה: the haftarah is read as a single block, analogous to a complete aliyah. Three borderline cases flow from this and must be resolved by this single principle.

Borderline caseApplication of the "indivisible unit" principle
The maftir is interrupted in the middle (nishtatek)The replacement does not start where the other stopped: he restarts from the first pasuk. A haftarah "half said by one, half by the other" is not a haftarah — just as one does not split an aliyah between two olim.
Two people want to read together (to "help")Assur: תְּרֵי קָלֵי לָא מִשְׁתַּמְעֵי (Megillah 21b) — two simultaneous voices are inaudible. The haftarah must come from one mouth, otherwise the tzibbur is not yotzei anything.
How many pesukim: 21, or less?21 pesukim (3 × 7 olim) are the standard; but if the prophetic topic concludes earlier (slik inyana), one stops — because what makes the unit is the closed thematic content, not a mechanical count.
The principle to take away. Everything that appears to be an accumulation of disparate rules (restarting, single voice, length) flows from one and the same intuition: the reading of the Neviim is a single, continuous public utterance. It is also dependent on the Torah — hence the halachah that without a sefer Torah read with a minyan, we read the haftarah but without its seven brachos: the liturgical "frame" only holds if the Torah has preceded it.

6. Mnemonic — "21 · 7 · 7"

21 — minimum pesukim on Shabbos (3 × 7 olim). On Yom Tov: 15 (3 × 5).

7 — brachos of the haftarah: 2 before + 5 after.

7 — seifim of the Mechaber: structure, R"Ch, Amen, Katan, Nishtatek, Golel, Mechubaros.

Mnemonic of the Rama's 4: 15 · אחר · יחיד · יקום

15 — 15 pesukim ביו"ט (instead of 21).

אחר — another may read for the one who does not know.

יחיד — one maftir at a time (trei kali).

יקום — Yekum Purkan + Av HaRachamim (Ashkenazi minhag).

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — Mentioning Rosh Chodesh in the maftir's brachos: on Shabbos-R"Ch we read "HaShamayim kis'i" but we do not modify the brachos to mention R"Ch (except by mistake, we do not redo).
Pitfall 2 — Restarting the haftarah in the middle: if nishtatek, the replacement must restart from the beginning, never in the middle. The haftarah is treated as an indivisible unit, analogous to a complete aliyah.
Pitfall 3 — Two people reading together: trei kali lo mishtam'i. Even if one follows poorly and the other wants to "help", it is assur. One at a time.
Pitfall 4 — Reading the haftarah without having read the Torah: without Torah (read with a minyan), we do not say the 7 brachos. One may read the haftarah alone, but without liturgical framing.
Pitfall 5 — Starting before the golel has finished: out of kavod for the sefer Torah and concentration of the mispalelim, wait for the end of the rolling.
Pitfall 6 — Haftarah of the 1st parashah (instead of the 2nd): when parshiyos mechubaros, we read the haftarah of the second (except for the exceptions in siman 428).

10. The 7 practical commandments of Siman רפ"ד

For the maftir and the kehillah

  1. Choose a haftarah mei-inyana shel parashah — according to the luach of the kehillah.
  2. Read at least 21 pesukim on Shabbos (15 on Yom Tov), unless the topic concludes earlier.
  3. Always after the Torah reading — no brachos without Torah read.
  4. On Shabbos-R"Ch: do not modify the brachos to mention Rosh Chodesh.
  5. Answer Amen with kavanah to the brachos of the olim and of the maftir — to complete the 100 brachos.
  6. In case of nishtatek: one single replacement who restarts from the beginning; never in two voices.
  7. Wait until the golel has finished rolling the sefer Torah before starting.
