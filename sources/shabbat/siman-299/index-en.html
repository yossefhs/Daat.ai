Siman 299 — No eating, drinking, or melachah before Havdalah

The Alter Rebbe's shitah on the 21 se'ifim codifying the issurim before Havdalah on Motzei Shabbos — no eating, no drinking (except water), and no melachah. Conceptual framework: Shabbos is not yet fully over.
