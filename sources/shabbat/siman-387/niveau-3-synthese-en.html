The seif number on this page is that of the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber (a single seif, with a gloss of the Rema). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav, studied in Level 4, writes this siman in 2 seifim: when it is cited, its name is given next to the number.

1. The central axiom

Siman שפ״ז in one sentence.
Those who join in a shituf in the mavoy must make the eruv in the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv: « המשתתפין במבוי צריכים לערב בחצירו׳ כדי שלא ישכחו התינוקות תורת עירוב שהרי אין התינוקות מכירים מה נעשה במבוי » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ז:א). If they made the shituf with bread, one relies on the shituf, with no eruv in the courtyards: « לפיכך אם נשתתפו במבוי בפת סומכין עליו ואין צריכין לערב בחצירו׳ שהרי התינוקו׳ מכירים בפת » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ז:א) — but the seif at once cites a view (“some say”): if each courtyard did not make the eruv for itself, one does not rely on the shituf; on this view, the commentators diverge (Level 2). The gloss of the Rema cites a view on a shituf of wine, and the custom of a single shituf, with flour (seif א).

3. The course of the seif — from the shituf of the mavoy to the gloss of the Rema

Seif א, first clause — the eruv of the courtyards: those who join in a shituf in the mavoy must make the eruv in the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv, for the children do not know what is done in the mavoy.
↓
Seif א, second clause — bread: therefore, if they joined in the mavoy with bread, one relies on it, and they need not make the eruv in the courtyards, for the children recognize bread.
↓
Seif א, third clause — “some say”: if each courtyard did not make the eruv for itself, one does not rely on the shituf.
↓
Seif א, fourth clause — the shituf in place of the eruv: the seif continues: when each courtyard made the eruv for itself, and then they joined in a shituf in the mavoy without making the eruv through the openings between them, they use the courtyards through those openings.
↓
Seif א, last clause — the eruv in place of the shituf: if they made the eruv through the openings and did not join in a shituf in the mavoy, the courtyards are permitted to use the mavoy.
↓
Gloss of the Rema — wine: some say that one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv even with wine; and likewise on the eruv in place of the shituf.
↓
Gloss of the Rema — a single shituf: “we are accustomed”, lechatchilah, to make only a single shituf, with flour; the reason he proposes: our eruv, to which each of the people of the courtyard gives for the shituf, is like eruv and shituf together, and the requirement of shituf and eruv belonged to the time of the Gemara; in the case he describes (בכה״ג), if one makes more and recites the blessing over it, it is a blessing in vain.

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes this siman in two seifim: 387:1 — the eruv in every courtyard that has two houses, courtyards open to one another, the shituf of bread, and the custom of his time; 387:2 — one who forgot to make the eruv with the residents of his courtyard, in a mavoy joined with wine or another kind that is not bread, and the case where all forgot (Level 4).

4. Decision tree — is the eruv of the courtyards still needed?

Q1: Did the courtyards make the shituf of the mavoy with bread? (seif א)
↓ NO → they must make the eruv in the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv (seif א). The gloss of the Rema cites a view (“some say”) that relies on the shituf in place of the eruv even with wine; the commentators discuss its scope (Level 2).
↓ YES → one relies on it, with no eruv in the courtyards (seif א) — according to the Mishnah Berurah, a whole loaf, placed in a house of the courtyard (ס״ק ו). But according to the view the seif at once cites (“some say”), if each courtyard did not make the eruv for itself, one does not rely on the shituf; the commentators diverge on this view (Level 2).
Q2: Did each courtyard make the eruv for itself, then the shituf of the mavoy, without an eruv through the openings between the courtyards? (seif א)
↓ YES → they use the courtyards through those openings: one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv (seif א).
Q3: Did they make the eruv through the openings, without joining in a shituf in the mavoy? (seif א)
↓ YES → the courtyards are permitted to use the mavoy: one relies on the eruv in place of the shituf (seif א).
Q4: Is a separate shituf needed, in addition to the eruv to which each one gave? (gloss of the Rema, seif א)
↓ The Rema writes “we are accustomed”, lechatchilah, to make only a single shituf, with flour; in the case he describes (בכה״ג), if one makes more and recites the blessing over it, it is a blessing in vain; the reason, he proposes (ונ״ל הטעם), and the commentators discuss it (Level 2).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

On the “וי״א” of the seif, and on the scope of the view the Rema cites on wine, the commentators diverge: Level 2; Level 4, points ג and ה. This page does not decide: consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic ת-פ-ל-פ-י-ק

Words of the seif and of the gloss of the Rema, in their order:

ת — התינוקות: the eruv of the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv.

פ — בפת: with a shituf of bread, one relies on it — then “some say”.

ל — לעצמה: according to that view, without an eruv of each courtyard for itself, one does not rely on the shituf.

פ — פתחים: the shituf in place of the eruv, through the openings between the courtyards; the eruv through the openings, in place of the shituf.

י — ביין: the view the Rema cites, even with wine.

ק — בקמח: the custom of a single shituf, with flour.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Overlooking what the seif requires of those who join in the mavoy. The seif: « המשתתפין במבוי צריכים לערב בחצירו׳ כדי שלא ישכחו התינוקות תורת עירוב שהרי אין התינוקות מכירים מה נעשה במבוי » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ז:א) — according to the Mishnah Berurah, with a shituf made of foods other than bread (ס״ק א). For wine, see the view that the Rema's gloss cites (Pitfall 3).
Pitfall 2: Presenting the shituf of bread as sufficient without the view that follows. The seif continues at once: « וי״א שאם לא עירבה כל חצר לעצמה אין סומכין על השיתוף אבל כשכל חצר עירבה לעצמה ואח״כ נשתתפו במבוי ולא עירבו דרך פתחים שביניהם מותרים להשתמש בחצירו׳ שבמבוי דרך פתחים שביניהם שסומכים על שיתוף במקום עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ז:א). On this “וי״א”, the commentators diverge (Level 2). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav does not write this “וי״א” (387:1; Level 4, point ג). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 3: Presenting the Rema's view on wine as the rule. The Rema introduces it with « ויש אומרים » (רמ״א או״ח שפ״ז:א); the commentators discuss its scope (Level 2).
Pitfall 4: Presenting the Rema's reason as established. He writes « ונ״ל הטעם » (רמ״א או״ח שפ״ז:א): it is a reason he proposes. And the blessing in vain concerns, in the case he describes, one who makes more and recites the blessing over it: « אבל בכה״ג לכ״ע אין עושין רק שתוף א׳ ואי עביד יותר ובירך עליו הוי ברכה לבטלה » (רמ״א או״ח שפ״ז:א).
Pitfall 5: Reading the custom the Rav reports as today's practice. He writes « עַכְשָׁו » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ז:א) — the custom of his time —, with a condition: « אִם מֻתָּר לְטַלְטֵל מֵהֶן לְבֵית הַכְּנֶסֶת » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ז:א). For today, consult your Rav.
Pitfall 6: Mixing the two numberings. Levels 1 to 3 follow the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber, which writes this siman in a single seif, with a gloss of the Rema; Level 4 follows the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, which writes it in 2 seifim (387:1, 387:2).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שפ״ז

For daily conduct

  1. Those who join in a shituf in the mavoy must make the eruv in the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv (seif א) — according to the Mishnah Berurah, with a shituf made of foods other than bread (ס״ק א); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav: in every courtyard that has two houses (387:1).
  2. With a shituf of bread, one relies on it, with no eruv in the courtyards (seif א) — according to the Mishnah Berurah, a whole loaf, placed in a house of the courtyard (ס״ק ו); but the seif at once cites a view (“some say”): if each courtyard did not make the eruv for itself, one does not rely on the shituf; the commentators diverge (Level 2): consult your Rav.
  3. When each courtyard made the eruv for itself, then the shituf of the mavoy without an eruv through the openings, they use the courtyards through those openings; and if they made the eruv through the openings without joining in a shituf in the mavoy, the courtyards are permitted to use the mavoy (seif א).
  4. The Rema writes “we are accustomed”, lechatchilah, to a single shituf, with flour; in the case he describes (בכה״ג), if one makes more and recites the blessing over it, it is a blessing in vain; the reason, he proposes (gloss, seif א). The Rav writes the custom of his time: to rely on the shituf in the synagogue, made of bread, if it is permitted to carry from the courtyards to the synagogue (387:1).
  5. The Rav, on one who forgot: in a mavoy all of whose courtyards made the eruv, each for itself, and which then joined in a shituf with wine or another kind that is not bread, one who forgot to make the eruv with the residents of his courtyard has lost nothing; if all forgot to make the eruv in the courtyards, and they are not particular about their pieces of bread (to the measure of two meals of the eruv), they are as though joined by eruv, and one relies on the shituf of the mavoy for the first Shabbos only; this is permitted to them only under pressing need (387:2).
  6. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
