1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שפ״ז contains a single seif of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo), followed by a gloss of the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles), in small print. Topic of the siman, according to its title: partners in a mavoy must make the eruv in the courtyards. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe has 2 seifim for this siman: its numbering is that of Level 4, not that of this page.

Seif א

שותפין במבוי צריכין לערב בחצירות. ובו סעיף אחד:
המשתתפין במבוי צריכים לערב בחצירו' כדי שלא ישכחו התינוקות תורת עירוב שהרי אין התינוקות מכירים מה נעשה במבוי לפיכך אם נשתתפו במבוי בפת סומכין עליו ואין צריכין לערב בחצירו' שהרי התינוקו' מכירים בפת וי״א שאם לא עירבה כל חצר לעצמה אין סומכין על השיתוף אבל כשכל חצר עירבה לעצמה ואח״כ נשתתפו במבוי ולא עירבו דרך פתחים שביניהם מותרים להשתמש בחצירו' שבמבוי דרך פתחים שביניהם שסומכים על שיתוף במקום עירוב ואם עירבו דרך פתחים שביניהם ולא נשתתפו במבוי מותר להשתמש החצרות במבוי שסומכים על עירוב שעירבו החצרו' דרך פתחים במקום שיתוף: הגה ויש אומרים דסומכין אשיתוף במקום עירוב אפי' לא נשתתפו אלא ביין וכן סומכין אעירוב במקום שיתוף (הרא״ש ומרדכי פ' הדר וטור) ולעיל סי' שס״ו ס״ו בהגהה כתבתי דאנו נוהגין לכתחילה שלא לעשות רק שתוף א' בקמח ונ״ל הטעם כי עירוב שלנו שכל א' מבני החצר נותן לשיתוף הוי כעירוב ושיתוף ביחד ולכתחילה אין לעשות יותר והא שהצריכו שיתוף ועירוב היינו בזמן הגמרא שבני החצר לא נתנו לשתוף רק החצר עירבו ביחד וא' מיני החצר נתן לשתוף אבל בכה״ג לכ״ע אין עושין רק שתוף א' ואי עביד יותר ובירך עליו הוי ברכה לבטלה (ד״ע ותשובת מהר״ם שהביא הב״י):
Translation: Those who make a partnership in the mavoy [the shared alley onto which the courtyards open; not with bread, but with other foods or drinks — Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א] must make the eruv in the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv, for the children do not know what is done in the mavoy. Therefore, if they made the partnership in the mavoy with bread, one relies on it, and they need not make the eruv in the courtyards, for the children know the bread. And some say: if each courtyard did not make the eruv for itself, one does not rely on the shituf; but when each courtyard made the eruv for itself, and afterwards they made the partnership in the mavoy, and they did not make the eruv through the openings between them, they are permitted to use the courtyards of the mavoy through the openings between them, for one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv; and if they made the eruv through the openings between them and did not make the partnership in the mavoy, the courtyards are permitted to use the mavoy, for one relies on the eruv that the courtyards made through the openings, in place of the shituf. Gloss of the Rema: and some say that one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv even if they made the partnership only with wine; and likewise one relies on the eruv in place of the shituf [the Rema's references, as printed: הרא״ש ומרדכי פ׳ הדר וטור]. And above, in siman 366, seif 6, in the gloss, I wrote that we are accustomed, a priori, to make only one shituf, with flour. And it seems to me that the reason is that our eruv, where each of the members of the courtyard gives to the shituf, is like eruv and shituf together, and a priori one does not make more; and that [the Sages] required shituf and eruv was in the time of the Gemara, when the members of the courtyard did not give to the shituf: only the courtyard made the eruv together, and one from the courtyard gave to the shituf; but in such a case [ours], according to all, one makes only one shituf, and if one makes more and recites the blessing over it, it is a blessing in vain [the Rema's references, as printed: ד״ע ותשובת מהר״ם שהביא הב״י].
What this seif says:
  • Those who make the partnership in the mavoy must also make the eruv in the courtyards, for the children: המשתתפין במבוי צריכים לערב בחצירו׳ כדי שלא ישכחו התינוקות תורת עירוב שהרי אין התינוקות מכירים מה נעשה במבוי. According to the Mishnah Berurah, this is a shituf made not with bread but with other foods or drinks (ס״ק א); and by strict law there would be no need to make the eruv in the courtyards, since they are already joined by the shituf of the mavoy — only so that the children do not forget (ס״ק ג), and do not say: our fathers did not make the eruv (ס״ק ד).
  • With a shituf of bread, one relies on it, without eruv in the courtyards: לפיכך אם נשתתפו במבוי בפת סומכין עליו ואין צריכין לערב בחצירו׳ שהרי התינוקו׳ מכירים בפת. The Mishnah Berurah gives the reason — bread is the life of man, therefore one recognizes it when one partners with it — and adds that the case is where the bread was placed in a house in the courtyard, a whole loaf and not a slice; otherwise it does not help (ס״ק ו). The seif continues at once: “some say” that if each courtyard did not make the eruv for itself, one does not rely on the shituf (next point).
  • Some say: without each courtyard's own eruv, one does not rely on the shituf; the seif continues: when each courtyard made it, the shituf takes the place of the eruv between the courtyards: וי״א שאם לא עירבה כל חצר לעצמה אין סומכין על השיתוף אבל כשכל חצר עירבה לעצמה ואח״כ נשתתפו במבוי ולא עירבו דרך פתחים שביניהם מותרים להשתמש בחצירו׳ שבמבוי דרך פתחים שביניהם שסומכים על שיתוף במקום עירוב. The Mishnah Berurah explains this view: the shituf of the mavoy only joins the courtyards with the mavoy, not the houses with the courtyards (ס״ק ז); “to use the courtyards” means to carry from courtyard to courtyard objects that were in the house when Shabbos began (ס״ק ח). On this “וי״א”, the commentators diverge: see section 5 (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ב) and Level 2.
  • The eruv made through the openings takes the place of the shituf: ואם עירבו דרך פתחים שביניהם ולא נשתתפו במבוי מותר להשתמש החצרות במבוי שסומכים על עירוב שעירבו החצרו׳ דרך פתחים במקום שיתוף.
  • Gloss of the Rema — even with wine, and the reverse as well: ויש אומרים דסומכין אשיתוף במקום עירוב אפי׳ לא נשתתפו אלא ביין וכן סומכין אעירוב במקום שיתוף. The Mishnah Berurah notes that the second half (one relies on the eruv in place of the shituf) is already in the words of the Mechaber, and that the Rema repeated it in passing (ס״ק י). On the scope of this “יש אומרים”, the commentators discuss (Level 2).
  • Gloss of the Rema — a single shituf, with flour: ולעיל סי׳ שס״ו ס״ו בהגהה כתבתי דאנו נוהגין לכתחילה שלא לעשות רק שתוף א׳ בקמח; ונ״ל הטעם כי עירוב שלנו שכל א׳ מבני החצר נותן לשיתוף הוי כעירוב ושיתוף ביחד ולכתחילה אין לעשות יותר; והא שהצריכו שיתוף ועירוב היינו בזמן הגמרא שבני החצר לא נתנו לשתוף רק החצר עירבו ביחד וא׳ מיני החצר נתן לשתוף אבל בכה״ג לכ״ע אין עושין רק שתוף א׳ ואי עביד יותר ובירך עליו הוי ברכה לבטלה. The Rema writes “it seems to me that the reason is” (ונ״ל הטעם): it is a reason he proposes; the commentators discuss it (section 5, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״א-י״ב; Level 2).

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Several courtyards open onto one mavoy. The siman deals with two institutions, for carrying on Shabbos: the eruv of the courtyards (עירוב חצרות), which joins the houses of a courtyard, and the shituf of the mavoy (שיתוף מבואות), which joins the courtyards to the mavoy. The Beit Yosef brings, in the chapter Ha-dar, the baraisa: « מערבין בחצירות ומשתתפין במבוי שלא לשכח תורת עירוב מן התינוקות שיאמרו אבותינו לא עירבו דר״מ וחכמים אומרים מערבין או משתתפין » (בית יוסף או״ח שפ״ז) — one makes the eruv in the courtyards and the shituf in the mavoy, so that the law of eruv is not forgotten by the children, who would say: our fathers did not make the eruv — the words of Rabbi Meir; and the Sages say: one makes the eruv or the shituf. The Beit Yosef then brings the discussion of the Amoraim and the Rishonim on bread and wine, and on the halachah (Level 2).

The Tur opens this siman with « סומכין על עירוב במקום שיתוף ועל שיתוף במקום עירוב » (טור או״ח שפ״ז) — one relies on the eruv in place of the shituf, and on the shituf in place of the eruv; and he writes further on: « והרמב״ם ז״ל כתב שאין סומכין על שיתוף בשביל עירוב אלא א״כ שתפו בפת וא״א הרא״ש ז״ל לא כתב כן » (טור או״ח שפ״ז) — the Rambam writes that one relies on the shituf for the eruv only if they made the partnership with bread, and the Rosh, his father, does not write so. The Mechaber's seif speaks of a shituf of bread; the gloss of the Rema brings the view that relies on the shituf even with wine, and names the Rosh, the Mordechai and the Tur.

The fundamental question: when the courtyards of a mavoy have made the shituf of the mavoy, is the eruv still needed in each courtyard? The seif answers: yes, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv; but if they made the shituf with bread, one relies on it. It then brings a view (“some say”) according to which, without each courtyard's own eruv, one does not rely on the shituf, and sets out when the shituf takes the place of the eruv between the courtyards, and the eruv through the openings takes the place of the shituf. The Rema adds a view for a shituf of wine, and the custom of a single shituf.

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. The gloss of the Rema refers to siman 366, seif 6: the gloss of the Rema there brings the custom of collecting a little flour from each house, making one whole challah from it, and making the eruv with it (רמ״א או״ח שס״ו:ו). In siman 366, seif 3, another gloss of the Rema refers to simanim 386 and 387: « והמנהג בזמן הזה להניח העירוב בבית הכנסת וכן נהגו הקדמונים ונראה לי הטעם דעירובין שלנו יש להם דין שתוף ואין צריך להניח בבית דירה וע׳ לקמן סי׳ שפ״ו ושפ״ז » (רמ״א או״ח שס״ו:ג) — the custom, in his time, is to place the eruv in the synagogue, and so did the earlier authorities; and he proposes as the reason that our eruvin have the status of a shituf.

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman, in the order of the seif:

The essential notions of siman שפ״ז:
  • המשתתפין במבוי — those who make the partnership in the mavoy: « ולא מיירי בפת אלא בשאר מיני אוכלין ומשקין » (משנה ברורה שפ״ז ס״ק א) — it is not about bread, but about other foods and drinks.
  • כדי שלא ישכחו התינוקות תורת עירוב — for the children: « ר״ל דמדינא לא היו צריכין לערב בחצרות הפתוחות למבוי כלל שהרי מעורבין ומשותפין כבר ממילא ע״י שיתוף שבמבוי אלא כדי וכו » (משנה ברורה שפ״ז ס״ק ג) — by strict law there would be no need to make the eruv in the courtyards that open onto the mavoy, since they are already joined by the shituf of the mavoy; only “so that…”. And the Mishnah Berurah adds what they would say: « ויאמרו אבותינו לא עירבו » (משנה ברורה שפ״ז ס״ק ד).
  • בפת — the shituf of bread: « מתוך שהפת היא חיי האדם לפיכך מכירים בו כשמשתתפים בו » (משנה ברורה שפ״ז ס״ק ו); and « ומיירי שהניחו הפת בבית שבחצר והיה פת שלמה ולא פרוסה ואי לא״ה לא מהני » (משנה ברורה שפ״ז ס״ק ו) — the bread placed in a house in the courtyard, a whole loaf and not a slice; otherwise it does not help.
  • שיתוף במקום עירוב — the shituf in place of the eruv; what it permits between the courtyards: « היינו להוציא מחצר לחצר כלים ששבתו בבית » (משנה ברורה שפ״ז ס״ק ח).
  • עירוב במקום שיתוף — the eruv that the courtyards made through the openings between them, in place of the shituf: the courtyards are permitted to use the mavoy (seif א).
  • שתוף א׳ בקמח — a single shituf, with flour: the custom the Rema brings, and the reason he proposes (gloss of the Rema, seif א).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אThose who make the partnership in the mavoy must make the eruv in the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv. If they made the partnership with bread, one relies on the shituf, without eruv in the courtyards. Some say: if each courtyard did not make the eruv for itself, one does not rely on the shituf. The seif continues: if each courtyard did, and then they made the partnership in the mavoy without an eruv through the openings between the courtyards, they use the courtyards through those openings (the shituf in place of the eruv); and if they made the eruv through the openings without the partnership in the mavoy, the courtyards use the mavoy (the eruv in place of the shituf). Gloss of the Rema: some say that one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv even with wine, and likewise on the eruv in place of the shituf; he writes “we are accustomed” to make, a priori, only one shituf, with flour — he proposes a reason for it —, and, in the case he describes (בכה״ג), making more with the blessing is a blessing in vain.
The heart of the siman: the shituf of the mavoy does not, in principle, exempt from the eruv of the courtyards, because of the children (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג); a shituf of bread does exempt, for the children know the bread (seif א) — a whole loaf, placed in a house in the courtyard (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו). A view in the seif (“some say”) requires, in order to rely on the shituf, each courtyard's own eruv; the commentators diverge on this view (Level 2). The Rema adds a view for a shituf of wine, and brings the custom of a single shituf, with flour (gloss of the Rema, seif א).

6. Position of the Rema

In this siman, the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles, הגה) has a gloss, on seif א, after the Mechaber's last sentence.

The gloss of the Rema:
  • Seif א, a view for wine — « ויש אומרים דסומכין אשיתוף במקום עירוב אפי׳ לא נשתתפו אלא ביין וכן סומכין אעירוב במקום שיתוף » (רמ״א או״ח שפ״ז:א) — some say that one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv even if they made the partnership only with wine; and likewise on the eruv in place of the shituf. On the scope of this view, the commentators discuss (Level 2).
  • Seif א, the custom of a single shituf — « ולעיל סי׳ שס״ו ס״ו בהגהה כתבתי דאנו נוהגין לכתחילה שלא לעשות רק שתוף א׳ בקמח » (רמ״א או״ח שפ״ז:א) — the Rema refers to his gloss in siman 366, seif 6, and writes: “we are accustomed”, a priori, to make only one shituf, with flour.
  • Seif א, the reason he proposes — « ונ״ל הטעם כי עירוב שלנו שכל א׳ מבני החצר נותן לשיתוף הוי כעירוב ושיתוף ביחד ולכתחילה אין לעשות יותר » (רמ״א או״ח שפ״ז:א) — our eruv, where each gives to the shituf, is like eruv and shituf together; in the time of the Gemara, one from the courtyard gave to the shituf; and, in the case he describes (בכה״ג), one who makes more with the blessing makes a blessing in vain (same gloss). On this reason, the commentators discuss (section 5, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״א-י״ב; Level 2).
  • Chabad → the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שפ״ז, in 2 seifim, with its own numbering (see Level 4 — Daat HaRav).

7. Modern practical cases

The situations below are applications by the site: the seif does not name them, and each is tied to the part of the seif it involves.

SituationQuick analysis
The courtyards of a mavoy made the shituf with a food other than breadApplication by the site, seif א: the seif writes that those who make the partnership in the mavoy must make the eruv in the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv — according to the Mishnah Berurah, this is a shituf made not with bread but with other foods or drinks (ס״ק א); the gloss of the Rema brings a view that relies on the shituf in place of the eruv even with wine, and the commentators discuss its scope (Level 2). Consult your Rav.
The shituf was made with breadApplication by the site, seif א: one relies on it, without eruv in the courtyards — a whole loaf, placed in a house in the courtyard (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו); but according to the view the seif then brings (“some say”), if each courtyard did not make the eruv for itself, one does not rely on the shituf, and the commentators diverge on this view (Level 2). Consult your Rav.
Each courtyard made its eruv, the mavoy its shituf, but no eruv through the openings between the courtyardsApplication by the site, seif א: they use the courtyards through the openings between them, for one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv — carrying from courtyard to courtyard objects that were in the house when Shabbos began (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ח). Consult your Rav.
Is a separate shituf needed, besides the eruv to which everyone gave?Application by the site, gloss of the Rema on seif א: the Rema writes “we are accustomed” to make, a priori, only one shituf, with flour; in the case he describes (בכה״ג), making more and reciting the blessing over it is a blessing in vain; the reason he gives, he proposes (“it seems to me”), and the commentators discuss it (Level 2). Consult your Rav.
For precise practical cases: the seif makes the ruling depend, among other things, on the food of the shituf, on each courtyard's own eruv and on the eruv through the openings between the courtyards; and the commentators discuss its scope. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שפ״ז:
  1. The shituf of the mavoy does not exempt from the eruv of the courtyards, so that the children do not forget the law of eruv (seif א) — a shituf made with a food other than bread (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א).
  2. A shituf of bread does exempt, for the children know the bread (seif א) — a whole loaf, placed in a house in the courtyard (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו).
  3. Some say: without each courtyard's own eruv, one does not rely on the shituf; the commentators diverge on this view (Level 2).
  4. The seif continues: when each courtyard made the eruv for itself, the shituf takes the place of the eruv between the courtyards; and the eruv made through the openings takes the place of the shituf (seif א).
  5. Gloss of the Rema: a view for a shituf of wine; the custom of a single shituf, with flour; in the case he describes (בכה״ג), a blessing over more is in vain (seif א; Level 2, section 6).
  6. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Why must those who make the partnership in the mavoy still make the eruv in the courtyards, when by strict law the courtyards are already joined (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג-ד)?
  2. Why does a shituf of bread exempt from the eruv of the courtyards, and on what conditions according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ו)?
  3. According to the Mishnah Berurah's explanation of the view “וי״א”, what does the shituf of the mavoy join, and what does it not join (ס״ק ז)?
  4. What does the view the Rema brings add, and what does the Mishnah Berurah say about its second half (ס״ק ט-י)?
  5. According to the reason the Rema proposes, why does one make only one shituf, and what of a blessing over more?
