1. The central axiom

Siman שמ"ה in one sentence.
The issur of הוצאה (transferring) is moving an object from one reshus to another — so one must first define the reshuyos. The siman sets out four: reshus hayachid (RH"Y) and reshus harabim (RH"R), mid'Oraisa; karmelis, the intermediate one instituted by Chazal; and makom petour, where one carries freely. It all turns on geometry: mechitzos, area, height.

2. The four reshuyos

ReshusDefinitionCarrying status
רְשׁוּת הַיָּחִיד (RH"Y)Mechitzos of 10 tefachim surrounding 4×4 tefachimReshus mid'Oraisa; internal carrying is free
רְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים (RH"R)Open way of 16 amos, uncovered, public thoroughfareReshus mid'Oraisa
כַּרְמְלִיתIntermediate: neither enclosed nor a public way (sea, plain)Assur mid'rabbanan
מְקוֹם פְּטוּרLess than 4×4, raised more than 3Exempt — carrying is free

3. The key measurements

MeasurementRole

4×4 tefachimMinimal area of a significant reshus (RH"Y, karmelis)
10 tefachimHeight of mechitzos for an RH"Y; ceiling of RH"R and karmelis
16 amosWidth of an RH"R thoroughfare
600,000Per the "yesh omrim": number of passersby required for an RH"R

4. Decision tree — classifying a place

Q1: Is the place surrounded by mechitzos of 10 tefachim over a 4×4 area?
↓ YES → reshus hayachid (RH"Y)
Q2: Is it an open way of 16 amos meant for the public to pass through?
↓ YES → reshus harabim (RH"R) — see the 600,000 opinion
Q3: Is the place 4×4, while being neither enclosed nor a public way?
↓ YES → karmelis (assur mid'rabbanan)
Q4: Is the place less than 4×4 and raised more than 3?
↓ YES → makom petour (carrying is free)
→ At the slightest doubt about the measurements → consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic רְשׁוּת

ר — רְשׁוּת הַיָּחִיד: reshus hayachid — mechitzos of 10 tefachim surrounding 4×4; one carries freely within.

ש — שֵׁשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה אַמּוֹת: reshus harabim — an open way of 16 amos meant for the public to pass.

ו — וּבֵינוֹנִי: the karmelis — an "intermediate" reshus instituted by Chazal (sea, plain); carrying is assur mid'rabbanan.

ת — תֵּל קָטָן: makom petour — less than 4×4, raised; carrying is free.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking a reshus hayachid requires private ownership. "Yachid" means a space delimited by mechitzos — not property belonging to an individual.
Pitfall 2: Confusing karmelis and makom petour. The karmelis is 4×4 and carrying there is assur mid'rabbanan; the makom petour is less than 4×4 and carrying there is free.
Pitfall 3: Forgetting that an RH"R "holds" only up to 10 tefachim. Above 10, it is no longer a reshus harabim.
Pitfall 4: Ruling on one's own that a street is an RH"R mid'Oraisa. The question of 600,000 passersby carries heavy consequences (eruv) — it is a matter for a Rav.

10. The practical takeaways of Siman שמ"ה

For daily conduct

  1. Before carrying, identify the reshus of origin and of destination.
  2. Classify a place by three criteria: mechitzos, area (4×4), height (10).
  3. Remember the thresholds: 3 (lavud to the ground), 4×4 (a significant reshus), 10 (mechitzah / ceiling).
  4. The karmelis (sea, plain, open street per the opinions) — carrying is assur mid'rabbanan.
  5. In a doubt about classifying an actual place — consult your Rav.
  6. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
