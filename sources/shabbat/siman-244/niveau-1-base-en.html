Siman רמ״ד · 22 Seifim

Kablanus work by a non-Jew for a Jew — kablanus, maaras ayin, talush vs mechubar
סימן רמ״ד
איזו מלאכות יכול הנכרי לעשות בעד הישראל
🌱 Introduction Level · Beginners
✦ ❖ ✦

The great siman of kablanus (contracted work of the non-Jew). 22 seifim that codify when a non-Jew may perform a melakha for a Jew. Key distinction: שכיר יום (day-hire — agent of the Jew, forbidden) vs קבלן (kablan / contractor — acts in his own interest, permitted). Limits: at his place and not at the Jew's home, talush (movable object) and not mechubar (land), no מראית עין.

Topic: When a non-Jew may perform a melakha for a Jew on Shabbos
Source: שולחן ערוך אורח חיים סימן רמ״ד · 22 seifim · Avodah Zarah 21b

1. The text of the Shulchan Aruch

Seif Alef — Fundamental distinction שכיר יום / קבלן

אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאָסוּר לְהַנִּיחַ לְנָכְרִי שֶׁיַּעֲשֶׂה מְלָאכָה לְיִשְׂרָאֵל בְּשַׁבָּת אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁעוֹשֶׂה בְּשָׂכָר אִם הוּא עוֹשֶׂה בְּתוֹרַת שְׂכִיר יוֹם... אֲבָל אִם הוּא עוֹשֶׂה בְּתוֹרַת קַבְּלָנוּת שֶׁקִּבֵּל עָלָיו לַעֲשׂוֹת כָּךְ וְכָךְ בְּעַד שָׂכָר כָּךְ וְכָךְ — מֻתָּר לְהַנִּיחוֹ שֶׁיַּעֲשֶׂה כָּל מְלַאכְתּוֹ בְּשַׁבָּת.
Fundamental distinction:
  • שְׂכִיר יוֹם = day-hire. The Jew pays per day of work. The non-Jew is the Jew's agent (שלוחו). Forbidden that he work on Shabbos.
  • קַבְּלָן = kablan, contractor by piecework. The Jew pays for a defined job (so much for the whole). The non-Jew acts in his own interest (finish quickly to get paid). Permitted that he work on Shabbos.
The fundamental chiddush: in the case of kablanus, the non-Jew "אֵינוֹ עוֹשֶׂה בִּשְׁבִיל טוֹבַת הַיִּשְׂרָאֵל אֶלָּא בִּשְׁבִיל עַצְמוֹ" (he acts for himself, not for the Jew). He is no longer שלוחו של ישראל (the Jew's agent). The rabbinic prohibition of amira le-akum (telling the non-Jew) does not apply.

Seif Beis — Distinction תלוש / מחובר

בַּמֶּה דְּבָרִים אֲמוּרִים שֶׁמֻּתָּר לְהַנִּיחַ לְקַבְּלָן נָכְרִי לַעֲשׂוֹת בְּשַׁבָּת? כְּשֶׁעוֹשֶׂה מְלָאכָה בְּדָבָר הַתָּלוּשׁ מִן הַקַּרְקַע, שֶׁהַנָּכְרִי יָכוֹל לַעֲשׂוֹתָהּ בְּבֵיתוֹ אוֹ בְּמָקוֹם אַחֵר שֶׁאֵינוֹ רְשׁוּת הַיִּשְׂרָאֵל... אֲבָל כְּשֶׁעוֹשֶׂה בִּמְחֻבָּר לַקַּרְקַע — אָסוּר.
2 categories of work:
  • תָּלוּשׁ = movable object (cloth to sew, book to print). The non-Jew takes it home. Permitted on Shabbos because no one knows it is for a Jew.
  • מְחֻבָּר לַקַּרְקַע = land/construction (building a house, harvesting a field). The land is visible and public (the land being known as the Jew's). Forbidden on Shabbos — maaras ayin.

2. The general context

What is this siman about?

Siman רמ״ד is the great siman of kablanus — the densest of the laws of amira le-akum. It sets the conceptual structure that will allow handling all Jew/non-Jew interactions on Shabbos.

QuestionAnswer
Under what contract does the non-Jew work?Day-hire (forbidden) or kablanus (permitted)
On what does he work?Movable/talush (permitted) or real estate/mechubar (forbidden)
Where?At his place (permitted) or at the Jew's home (forbidden)
Publicly visible?No maaras ayin
The founding talmudic sugya — Avodah Zarah 21b and Moed Katan 12a: "נכרי שעוסק במלאכת ישראל בקבלנות — מותר". Discussion on the limits: where, what, how. The conclusion: 4 cumulative conditions.

3. Key concept 1 — שכיר יום vs קבלן

שְׂכִיר יוֹם = day-hire. Wages by time. קַבְּלָן = kablan / contractor by piecework. Wages by result. The halakha distinguishes these two modes by agency (שליחות):
  • Sechir yom: the Jew rents the non-Jew's time → the non-Jew is his agent
  • Kablan: the Jew rents a result → the non-Jew acts in his own interest (finish quickly = get paid)
Criterionשכיר יוםקבלן
Payment modeBy time (per day, per hour)By result (lump sum for the task)
Agency (שליחות)The Jew is employer — direct agentThe non-Jew acts for himself
Work on ShabbosForbiddenPermitted (with conditions)
Modern applicationFull-time salaried employee, temp worker paid hourlyCraftsman with quote, freelancer per project, fixed-fee delivery

4. Key concept 2 — תלוש vs מחובר

תָּלוּשׁ = "detached" — movable object (cloth, paper, portable machine). מְחֻבָּר לַקַּרְקַע = "attached to the ground" — land, construction, plantings. The talush can be taken to the non-Jew's place (= invisible). The mechubar is necessarily public (= visible).
Logic: the danger is not the work itself (already resolved by the kablanus) but the maaras ayin — the public image. If passersby see a non-Jew building the Jew's house on Shabbos, they will think he is sechir yom (employee) → bad example.

5. Key concept 3 — מראית עין and publicity

מַרְאִית עַיִן = "visual appearance". Halakha forbids certain actions even permitted in themselves if they give a false impression to the public. Here: a non-Jew working for a Jew on Shabbos in a public place may give the impression he is a direct employee.
CriterionNo maaras ayinRisk of maaras ayin
PlaceAt the non-Jew's placeAt the Jew's place (visible)
Type of workTalush (movable)Mechubar (visible land)
DistanceOutside techum (= 1km + no Jews)Within techum
NotorietyAnonymousPublicly known (e.g. ship on riverbank)

6. The 4 cumulative conditions of permitted kablanus

For a non-Jew to be able to work on Shabbos for a Jew:
  1. קַבְּלָנוּת — contract by piecework, not by the day
  2. בְּבֵיתוֹ — at the non-Jew's place (not at the Jew's home)
  3. תָּלוּשׁ — movable object (not land or visible construction)
  4. בְּלִי אֲמִירָה — without explicitly telling him to work on Shabbos; no maaras ayin
+ Conditions of siman רנ״ב: for melakhos that began on Friday.

8. Modern practical cases

Case 1 — Non-Jewish print shop printing a Jewish book

  • If the printer = kablan (paid per print run, not by the hour) ✓
  • Work in his workshop (at his place) ✓
  • Talush (book = movable) ✓
  • No maaras ayin if the workshop is not known as serving a Jew
→ Permitted for the print shop to run on Shabbos.

Case 2 — Construction of a building by a non-Jewish contractor

  • Kablan: yes (lump sum for the project)
  • Mechubar (construction site) ✗
  • Publicly visible ✗
→ Forbidden on Shabbos. The contract must stipulate the Shabbos halt.

Case 3 — Amazon delivery / Shabbos package

  • Delivery service = global kablan (delivers per package, not by the hour)
  • Not at the Jew's home (truck, road)
  • Talush (movable package)
  • No maaras ayin (anonymous delivery)
→ Permitted to receive a package on Shabbos. But do not order Friday knowing the Shabbos delivery will be specifically for oneself.

Case 4 — Non-Jewish cleaning lady

  • If paid hourly / daily = sechir yom → forbidden on Shabbos
  • If paid by task (flat rate per house cleaned) = kablan → permitted if at the Jew's home possible?
Problem: she works at the Jew's home (not mechubar, but visible). Maaras ayin conceivable. Usual practice: do not have the cleaning lady come on Shabbos.

Case 5 — Vehicle driven by a non-Jewish driver (Uber)

  • Uber = kablan per ride (flat rate per trip)
  • The driver's vehicle (not at the Jew's home)
  • Talush
→ But getting in the car on Shabbos = equivalent to initiating the melakha (= explicit request). Forbidden in practice.

9. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The 4 teachings of Siman רמ״ד:
  1. Kablan/sechir-yom distinction — agency vs own interest
  2. Talush/mechubar — movable at his place OK, immovable public NOT
  3. Maaras ayin — blocks even the talush if too visible
  4. No explicit amira — never ask on Shabbos

Practical decision table

The 5 practical mitzvos of Siman רמ״ד

  1. Verify kablanus — contract by piecework, not by the hour
  2. Work at the non-Jew's place — not at the Jew's home
  3. Talush only — no land or visible construction
  4. Do not ask on Shabbos — let the non-Jew decide when
  5. Check maaras ayin — not too publicly known as serving a Jew
