דיני ברכת עירוב

The Blessing of the Eruv over the Shituf of the Mavoy

1 seif in the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber, with a gloss of the Rema introduced by הגה; 1 in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, under its own numbering (Level 4). Over the shituf of the mavoy, which blessing does one say, with which formula, and at what time? The seif answers: it is a mitzvah to pursue it; one blesses “al mitzvas eruv” and says “by this shituf…”; one blesses at the time one collects it or at the time one transfers its ownership — on the meaning of these two times, the commentators differ (Level 2). The Rema adds: collected for the sake of the eruv, it remains an eruv if they forgot to bless — where the custom is to collect flour, the commentators differ (Level 2).
