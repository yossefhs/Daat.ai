דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on Siman 395

The blessing of the eruv over the shituf of the mavoi: the mitzvah to pursue the shituf, the blessing and the formula, what is added when the shituf is made with bread and one relies on it in place of an eruv, making no eruv at all in the courtyards, the time of the blessing, and the case where no blessing was said. The single seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, compared with the Mechaber's single seif and the Rema's gloss.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav is not a commentary on the Mechaber: it is a Shulchan Aruch in its own right, written by the Alter Rebbe, with its own division and its own numbering of the seifim. Here the Shulchan Aruch HaRav has one seif, and the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has one seif too; the references on this page follow the Rav's numbering, except where the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch is named.

This page gathers, for Siman 395, the full text of the Rav with its translation, where his text differs from the Mechaber's and the Rema's, and the practical conduct that follows from it.

1 · Text of the Alter Rebbe

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן שצ״ה

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav of the Alter Rebbe

סימן שצ״ה — דִּינֵי בִּרְכַּת עֵרוּב — וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד
Source: Kehot edition, as reproduced on Sefaria. 1 seif (the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has 1 too, under its own numbering).

סעיף א דִּינֵי בִּרְכַּת עֵרוּב וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד: מִצְוָה לְחַזֵּ(ו)ר אַחַר שִׁתּוּפֵי…

דִּינֵי בִּרְכַּת עֵרוּב וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד:

מִצְוָה לְחַזֵּ(ו)ר אַחַר שִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת כְּמוֹ אַחַר עֵרוּבֵי חֲצֵרוֹת.

וּמְבָרֵךְ עָלָיו "עַל מִצְוַת עֵרוּב", וְאוֹמֵר "בְּזֶה הַשִּׁתּוּף יְהֵא מֻתָּר לְכָל בְּנֵי הַמָּבוֹי לְהוֹצִיא וּלְהַכְנִיס מֵחֲצֵרוֹת לְמָבוֹי".

וְאִם מִשְׁתַּתְּפִים בְּפַת וְסוֹמְכִין עַל שִׁתּוּף בִּמְקוֹם עֵרוּב, וְאֵין מְעָרְבִין בַּחֲצֵרוֹת כְּלָל, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ז — יֹאמַר גַּם כֵּן "מֵחֲצֵרוֹת לְבָתִּים".

אֵימָתַי מְבָרֵךְ עָלָיו? בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁמְּקַבֵּץ אוֹתוֹ מִבְּנֵי הַמָּבוֹי אוֹ בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁמְּזַכֶּה לָהֶם, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שס״ו.

וְאִם לֹא בֵרַךְ וְלֹא אָמַר עָלָיו כְּלוּם — אֵינוֹ מְעַכֵּב, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם:

The laws of the blessing of the eruv; it contains one seif:

It is a mitzvah to pursue the shitufei mevo'os (partnerships of the mavoi), as [one pursues] the eruvei chatzeros.

And one recites the blessing over [the shituf] "… the mitzvah of eruv", and says: "By this shituf may it be permitted to all the residents of the mavoi to carry out and to bring in [objects] from the courtyards to the mavoi".

And if they make the shituf with bread, rely on the shituf in place of an eruv, and make no eruv at all in the courtyards, as was explained in Siman 387, he shall also say: "from the courtyards to the houses".

When does one recite the blessing over it? At the time one collects it from the residents of the mavoi, or at the time one confers it upon them, as was explained in Siman 366.

And if one did not recite the blessing and said nothing over it, this does not hold it back [from being valid], as was explained there:

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim 395. Sefaria gives no Kuntres Acharon entry for this siman.

2 · The Rav's text and the Mechaber's

לשון הרב מול סעיף המחבר והרמ״א

Where the Rav's text differs from the Mechaber's seif and the Rema's

Each point sets side by side the single seif of the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch, or the Rema's gloss, and the single seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, in the order of the Rav's text, and notes only what the comparison of the two texts shows. A point noted is not thereby unique to the Rav: the page sometimes names its source; it notes neither all the sources nor all the divergences (see also Level 2).

נקודה א — מצוה לחזר אחר שיתופי מבואות (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א)

Point א — the mitzvah to pursue the shitufei mevo'os (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1)

The mitzvah to pursue the shituf

What the Mechaber writes: « מצוה לחזור אחר שיתופי מבואו' » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ה:א).

What the Rav writes: « מִצְוָה לְחַזֵּ(ו)ר אַחַר שִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת כְּמוֹ אַחַר עֵרוּבֵי חֲצֵרוֹת » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א).

The difference: the Rav's text compares the mitzvah to pursue the shitufei mevo'os to that of pursuing the eruvei chatzeros; the Mechaber's seif does not write this comparison.

What is found elsewhere: the Mechaber himself, for the eruv of courtyards: « מצוה לחזור אחר עירובי חצרות » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ו:יג); the Rav himself, in Siman 366, writes for the eruv of courtyards: « מִצְוָה לַחֲזוֹר אַחַר עֵרוּבֵי חֲצֵרוֹת, כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יָבֹאוּ לְטַלְטֵל בְּאִסּוּר » (שו״ע הרב שס״ו:יח). The Mishnah Berurah, later than the Rav, writes on this seif: « פן ישכחו ויבואו לידי איסור טלטול » (משנה ברורה שצ״ה ס״ק א).

נקודה ב — הברכה, הנוסח, והתוספת לבתים (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א)

Point ב — the blessing, the formula, and the addition for the houses (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1)

The blessing and the formula

What the Mechaber writes: « ומברך עליו על מצו' עירוב ואומר בזה השיתוף יהיה מותר לכל בני המבוי להוציא ולהכניס מחצירו' למבוי (ומהם לבתים) (ב״י בשם שבילי לקט) בשבת » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ה:א). The words « (ומהם לבתים) » are printed there in parentheses, in small type, without הגה, followed by their source; the seif does not write in which case they are said.

What the Rav writes: « וּמְבָרֵךְ עָלָיו "עַל מִצְוַת עֵרוּב", וְאוֹמֵר "בְּזֶה הַשִּׁתּוּף יְהֵא מֻתָּר לְכָל בְּנֵי הַמָּבוֹי לְהוֹצִיא וּלְהַכְנִיס מֵחֲצֵרוֹת לְמָבוֹי" » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א); then « וְאִם מִשְׁתַּתְּפִים בְּפַת וְסוֹמְכִין עַל שִׁתּוּף בִּמְקוֹם עֵרוּב, וְאֵין מְעָרְבִין בַּחֲצֵרוֹת כְּלָל, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ז — יֹאמַר גַּם כֵּן "מֵחֲצֵרוֹת לְבָתִּים" » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א).

The difference: the Rav's text gives the formula of the shituf up to the words “from the courtyards to the mavoi”, and writes separately in which case one adds “from the courtyards to the houses”: when the shituf is made with bread, one relies on the shituf in place of an eruv, and no eruv at all is made in the courtyards; for this it refers to Siman 387. The Mechaber's seif prints the addition in parentheses, with no condition written.

What is found elsewhere: the Magen Avraham, before the Rav, places these words of the Mechaber's seif in the case where one relies on the shituf in place of an eruv: « אף על גב דהכא מיירי בשיתופי מבואות מ״מ הא סומכין על שתוף במקום עירוב כמ״ש סי' שפ״ז » (מגן אברהם שצ״ה ס״ק א); the Mishnah Berurah, after the Rav, reads them the same way (משנה ברורה שצ״ה ס״ק ג). The Rav himself, in Siman 366, after the formula of the eruv of courtyards, writes: « וְנוּסַח שֶׁלָּנוּ יִתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שצ״ה » (שו״ע הרב שס״ו:יח).

נקודה ג — זמן הברכה (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א)

Point ג — the time of the blessing (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1)

The time of the blessing

What the Mechaber writes: « אימתי מברך עליו בשעה שמקבץ אותו מבני המבוי או בשעה שמזכה להם » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ה:א).

What the Rav writes: « אֵימָתַי מְבָרֵךְ עָלָיו? בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁמְּקַבֵּץ אוֹתוֹ מִבְּנֵי הַמָּבוֹי אוֹ בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁמְּזַכֶּה לָהֶם, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שס״ו » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א).

The difference: both texts name the same two times; the Rav adds the reference to Siman 366. On the meaning of these two times, the commentators differ: see Level 2, section 4.

What is found elsewhere: in Siman 366, the Rav writes for the eruv of courtyards: « וְאֵימָתַי מְבָרֵךְ? בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁמְּקַבֵּץ אוֹתוֹ מִבְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר אוֹ בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁמְּזַכֶּה לָהֶם, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁכָּל הַמִּצְוֹת מְבָרֵךְ עֲלֵיהֶם עוֹבֵר לַעֲשִׂיָּתָן » (שו״ע הרב שס״ו:יח), and then he deals with the custom in which the shammash makes the eruv with a challah baked from the flour collected: see point ד. See also Level 4 of Siman 366.

נקודה ד — אם לא בירך (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א)

Point ד — if he did not recite the blessing (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1)

If he did not recite the blessing

What the Rema writes: in his gloss, introduced by הגה at the end of the Mechaber's seif א: « ואם גבו אותו לשם עירוב ושכחו ולא ברכו עליו הוי עירוב כי אין הברכה מעכבת » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ה:א).

What the Rav writes: « וְאִם לֹא בֵרַךְ וְלֹא אָמַר עָלָיו כְּלוּם — אֵינוֹ מְעַכֵּב, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א).

The difference: the Rav's text writes that this does not hold it back [from being valid] even if, besides the blessing, nothing was said over the shituf; it does not write here « גבו אותו לשם עירוב » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ה:א) nor « ושכחו » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ה:א), and refers, by « כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א), to Siman 366, which it has just named. On the forgotten blessing where the custom is to collect flour, the commentators differ: see Level 2, section 5; what the Rav writes in Siman 366 for this custom is quoted below.

What is found elsewhere: in Siman 366, the Mechaber writes for the eruv of courtyards: « ואם גבו העירוב ולא ברכו עליו אין הברכה מעכבת ומותרים לטלטל » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ו:טו). The Rav, in Siman 366, for the custom in which the shammash makes the eruv with a challah baked from the flour collected, writes: « וּלְמִנְהַג מְדִינוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁהַשַּׁמָּשׁ עוֹשֶׂה הָעֵרוּב בְּחַלָּה שֶׁאוֹפֶה מֵהַקֶּמַח שֶׁקִּבֵּץ, אִם אֵינוֹ מְזַכֶּה לָהֶם הֲרֵי הַבְּרָכָה הִיא אַחַר שֶׁנַּעֲשָׂה הָעֵרוּב מִמֵּילָא כְּשֶׁנֶּאֱפָה הַחַלָּה, וְאַף עַל פִּי כֵן הִיא עוֹבֵר לַעֲשִׂיָּתָן, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁכָּל שֶׁלֹּא נִכְנַס שַׁבָּת עֲדַיִן לֹא קָנָה עֵרוּב, שֶׁקְּנִיַּת עֵרוּב הִיא בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת … אֲבָל הָעִקָּר כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר לְמַעְלָה, שֶׁהַשַּׁמָּשׁ יְזַכֶּה לָהֶם הָעֵרוּב, וִיבָרֵךְ בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁמְּזַכֶּה אוֹ הוּא אוֹ הַזּוֹכֶה לָהֶם (וַאֲפִלּוּ אֵינוֹ דָר בְּעִיר זוֹ) » (שו״ע הרב שס״ו:יח) — and according to the custom of these lands, in which the shammash makes the eruv with a challah he bakes from the flour he collected, if he does not confer it upon them, the blessing comes after the eruv has been made of itself when the challah was baked, and nevertheless it still precedes the act, because as long as Shabbat has not begun the eruv has not yet been acquired, the acquisition of the eruv taking place at twilight … but the main view is, as explained above, that the shammash confer the eruv upon them, and that, at the time it is conferred, either he or the one who acquires on their behalf recite the blessing (even if he does not live in this town); then he writes: « וְאִם גָּבוּ הָעֵרוּב וְלֹא בֵרְכוּ עָלָיו וְלֹא אָמְרוּ עָלָיו כְּלוּם — אֵין הַבְּרָכָה וְהָאֲמִירָה מְעַכֶּבֶת, וּמֻתָּרִים לְטַלְטֵל מִבָּתִּים לֶחָצֵר » (שו״ע הרב שס״ו:יח). The Mishnah Berurah, after the Rav, writes on the Rema's gloss: « ה״ה אפילו לא אמרו עליו ג״כ הנוסח של בזה השתוף הנ״ל » (משנה ברורה שצ״ה ס״ק ה).

4 · Lemaaseh

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

The practical Chabad conduct

Points of halachic conduct that flow directly from the Shulchan Aruch HaRav Siman 395, in the Chabad sensibility. Each point refers to its seif of the Rav, in the order of the text.

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman 395

  • ① The mitzvah to pursue the shituf: it is a mitzvah to pursue the shitufei mevo'os, as the eruvei chatzeros (395:1). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1.
  • ② The blessing and the formula: one recites the blessing over the shituf "… the mitzvah of eruv", and says: "By this shituf may it be permitted to all the residents of the mavoi to carry out and to bring in [objects] from the courtyards to the mavoi". If the shituf is made with bread, one relies on the shituf in place of an eruv, and no eruv at all is made in the courtyards, one also says "from the courtyards to the houses"; the Rav refers to Siman 387 (395:1). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1.
  • ③ The time of the blessing: at the time one collects the shituf from the residents of the mavoi, or at the time one confers it upon them; the Rav refers to Siman 366 (395:1). On the meaning of these two times, the commentators differ: see Level 2, section 4; what the Rav writes in Siman 366 for the custom of the challah baked from the flour collected is quoted at point ד. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1.
  • ④ If he did not recite the blessing: if one did not recite the blessing and said nothing over the shituf, this does not hold it back [from being valid]; the Rav refers to Siman 366 (395:1). In Siman 366, for the custom of the challah baked from the flour collected, the Rav writes that, if the shammash does not confer the eruv upon them, the blessing comes after the eruv has been made of itself when the challah is baked, and that it nevertheless still precedes the act, since the eruv is acquired only at twilight; but that the main view is that the shammash confer it upon them, and that one bless at the time of conferring (point ד). On the forgotten blessing where the custom is to collect flour, the commentators differ: see Level 2, section 5. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1.

⚠ This section presents the Chabad halachic conduct. For any concrete question: consult your Rav.
