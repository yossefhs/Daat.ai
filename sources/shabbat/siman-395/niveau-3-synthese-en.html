The seif number on this page is that of the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber (a single seif, with a gloss of the Rema introduced by הגה). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav, studied in Level 4, also writes this siman in a single seif, in its own words: when it is cited, its name is given next to the number.

1. The central axiom

Siman שצ״ה in one sentence.
The shituf of the mavoy is pursued as a mitzvah, and one blesses over it “al mitzvas eruv” — « מצוה לחזור אחר שיתופי מבואו׳ ומברך עליו על מצו׳ עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ה:א) — saying the formula “by this shituf…”; one blesses at the time one collects it or at the time one transfers its ownership (seif א; on the meaning of these two times, the commentators differ: Level 2, section 4). The Rema's gloss adds: « ואם גבו אותו לשם עירוב ושכחו ולא ברכו עליו הוי עירוב כי אין הברכה מעכבת » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ה:א) — collected for the sake of the eruv, it remains an eruv even if they forgot to bless; where the custom is to collect flour, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 5).

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes this siman in a single seif (395:1), in the same order: the mitzvah, which it compares to that of the eruv of the courtyards; the blessing, and the formula it gives up to the words “from the courtyards to the mavoy”; the case in which one adds “from the courtyards to the houses”; the time of the blessing; and the case where one neither blessed nor said anything — Level 4.

4. Decision tree — what does one say over the shituf, and when?

Q1: Are the residents of the mavoy making the shituf? (seif א)
↓ It is a mitzvah to pursue it (seif א) — according to the Mishnah Berurah, lest they forget and come to the prohibition of carrying (ס״ק א).
Q2: What does one say? (seif א)
↓ The blessing “al mitzvas eruv”, then the formula “by this shituf…” (seif א).
Q3: Does one rely on the shituf in place of the eruv?
↓ The formula of the seif carries, in small print, “and from them to the houses”; the Mishnah Berurah explains it by this case (ס״ק ג, referring to siman 387). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav: if the shituf is made with bread, one relies on it in place of the eruv, and no eruv at all is made in the courtyards, one also says “from the courtyards to the houses”; the formula it gives first ends with the words “from the courtyards to the mavoy” (395:1, referring to siman 387).
Q4: When does one bless? (seif א)
↓ At the time one collects it from the residents of the mavoy, or at the time one transfers its ownership to them (seif א; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1, referring to siman 366; Level 4, point ג). On the meaning of these two times, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 4).
Q5: Did they forget to bless? (the Rema's gloss)
↓ According to the Rema's gloss, collected for the sake of the eruv, it is an eruv, for the blessing is not indispensable (seif א); the same holds if they did not say the formula (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה). Where the custom is to collect flour, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 5). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav: if one did not bless and said nothing over it, this does not hold it back [from being valid] (395:1, referring to siman 366; Level 4, point ד).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic מ-ב-א-ה

Words of the seif, in its order:

מ — מצוה לחזור: it is a mitzvah to pursue the shituf of the mavoy (seif א).

ב — בזה השיתוף: the formula said after the blessing “al mitzvas eruv” (seif א); the Mishnah Berurah explains the parenthesis “and from them to the houses” by the case where one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv (ס״ק ג).

א — אימתי מברך עליו: at the time one collects it, or at the time one transfers its ownership (seif א); on the meaning of these two times, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 4).

ה — הגה … אין הברכה מעכבת: collected for the sake of the eruv, it remains an eruv if they forgot to bless (the Rema's gloss); where the custom is to collect flour, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 5).

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that the blessing is a condition of the shituf. The Rema's gloss: « ואם גבו אותו לשם עירוב ושכחו ולא ברכו עליו הוי עירוב כי אין הברכה מעכבת » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ה:א) — the gloss deals with a shituf collected for the sake of the eruv; the Mishnah Berurah: the same holds if they did not say the formula (ס״ק ה). Where the custom is to collect flour, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 5). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav: « וְאִם לֹא בֵרַךְ וְלֹא אָמַר עָלָיו כְּלוּם — אֵינוֹ מְעַכֵּב, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א).
Pitfall 2: Adding “and from them to the houses” without looking at the case. The seif prints these words in parentheses, in small print, without הגה, and does not write in which case they are said; the Mishnah Berurah explains them by the case where one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv (ס״ק ג, referring to siman 387); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes the condition: « וְאִם מִשְׁתַּתְּפִים בְּפַת וְסוֹמְכִין עַל שִׁתּוּף בִּמְקוֹם עֵרוּב, וְאֵין מְעָרְבִין בַּחֲצֵרוֹת כְּלָל, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ז — יֹאמַר גַּם כֵּן "מֵחֲצֵרוֹת לְבָתִּים" » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ה:א).
Pitfall 3: Giving the two times of the blessing a settled meaning. The seif writes: « אימתי מברך עליו בשעה שמקבץ אותו מבני המבוי או בשעה שמזכה להם » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ה:א); on the meaning of these two times, the commentators differ: Level 2 (section 4) gives each one in his own words. This page does not decide: consult your Rav.
Pitfall 4: Mixing the two texts. The Mechaber and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav each write this siman in a single seif, but not in the same words: Levels 1 to 3 cite seif א of the Mechaber and the Rema's gloss, Level 4 the seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (395:1); where they differ is in Level 4 (section 2).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שצ״ה

For daily conduct

  1. It is a mitzvah to pursue the shituf of the mavoy (seif א) — according to the Mishnah Berurah, lest they forget and come to the prohibition of carrying (ס״ק א); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav compares it to that of the eruv of the courtyards (395:1).
  2. One blesses over it “al mitzvas eruv” and says the formula “by this shituf…” (seif א); the name eruv also applies to a shituf (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב).
  3. “And from them to the houses”, in small print in the formula of the seif: the Mishnah Berurah explains it by the case where one relies on the shituf in place of the eruv (ס״ק ג, referring to siman 387); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav: if the shituf is made with bread, one relies on it in place of the eruv, and no eruv at all is made in the courtyards, one also says “from the courtyards to the houses” (395:1).
  4. One blesses at the time one collects it from the residents of the mavoy, or at the time one transfers its ownership to them (seif א; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 395:1, referring to siman 366; Level 4, point ג); on the meaning of these two times, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 4): consult your Rav.
  5. Collected for the sake of the eruv, it remains an eruv if they forgot to bless, for the blessing is not indispensable (the Rema's gloss); the same without the formula (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה); where the custom is to collect flour, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 5): consult your Rav. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav: if one neither blessed nor said anything, this does not hold it back (395:1, referring to siman 366; Level 4, point ד).
  6. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
