1. Central axiom

Siman ש"ס in one sentence.
An open space enclosed by "inferior" partitions is a reshus hayachid min haTorah, but Chazal treat it as a karpef — carrying limited to two se'ah. This limit falls when three adult Yidden camp there: they form a caravan and the place becomes a true dwelling, where one carries without area limit — provided the whole space genuinely serves their needs.

3. Hierarchy of concepts

Level 1 — min haTorah: even inferior partitions (shesi or erev) form a valid reshus hayachid.
↓
Level 2 — rabbinic decree: a non-dwelling enclosure = karpef → carrying limited to two se'ah.
↓
Level 3 — caravan exception: three adult Yidden → the place is a dwelling → unlimited carrying.
↓
Level 4 — caravan condition: no two-se'ah area unused.

4. Decision tree

Q1: How many adult Yidden camp in the enclosure?
↓ ONE or TWO → carrying limited to two se'ah (beyond: 4 amos); THREE or more ↓
Q2: Does the enclosed area exceed six se'in?
↓ NO → permitted even with two se'ah unused; YES ↓
Q3: Does any two-se'ah area remain unused?
↓ NO → carrying permitted throughout; YES → limited to 4 amos

6. Mnemonic שָׁלֹשׁ

ש — שְׁלֹשָׁה עוֹשִׂים שַׁיָּרָא: three adult Yidden form the caravan and lift the two-se'ah limit.

ל — לֹא יוֹתֵר מִכְּדֵי צָרְכָּם: the caravan must not leave two se'ah unused.

שׁ — שַׁבָּת קוֹבַעַת: status is fixed at entry of Shabbos.

7. Pitfalls

Pitfall 1: Believing an inferior partition suffices for everything. Min haTorah yes, but Chazal limit carrying to two se'ah unless three are present.
Pitfall 2: Counting minors (or a non-Jew) among the "three." The Rema excludes a non-Jew and, per many, a minor.
Pitfall 3: Enclosing too large a space "for safety." An unused two-se'ah area nullifies the caravan benefit.
Pitfall 4: Wanting to "correct" the situation on Shabbos. Status is fixed at entry — everything must be settled before.

10. Practical commandments of Siman ש"ס

For daily conduct

  1. One or two campers: carrying limited to two se'ah.
  2. Three adult Yidden: caravan — carrying throughout, however large.
  3. No two se'ah unused (unless total enclosure ≤ six se'in).
  4. Settle everything before Shabbos: status is fixed at entry.
  5. In doubt — consult your Rav.
  6. Deep pilpul — Level 2; Shitah of the Alter Rebbe (Chabad) — Level 4.
