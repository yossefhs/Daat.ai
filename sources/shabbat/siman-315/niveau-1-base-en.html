1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שט"ו contains 13 seifim of the Mechaber (Rav Yosef Karo), codifying the halachos pertaining to making an ohel (roof/shelter) on Shabbos.

Seif א — Making an ohel: roof forbidden, walls permitted

דברים האסורים משום אוהל בשבת. ובו יג סעיפים:
אסור לעשות אהל בשבת ויו"ט אפי' הוא עראי ודוקא גג אבל מחיצות מותר ואין מחיצה אסורה אא"כ נעשית להתיר סוכה או להתיר טלטול: הגה אבל מחיצה הנעשית לצניעות בעלמא שרי (טור) ולכן מותר לתלות וילון לפני הפתח אף על גב שקבוע שם (א"ז וב"י) וכן פרוכת לפני ארון הקודש ובלבד שלא יעשה אהל בגג טפח (ב"י וכלבו) וכן מותר לעשות מחיצה לפני החמה או הצינה או בפני הנרות שלא יכבה אותן הרוח (מרדכי ריש פרק כירה) אבל אסור לעשות מחיצה בפני אור הנר כדי שישמש מטתו (ד"ע) וכן לפני ספרים כדי לשמש או לעשות צרכיו אם לא שהיה מבעוד יום טפח שאז מותר להוסיף עליה בשבת (מרדכי ריש פ' כירה):
Translation: It is forbidden to make an ohel (shelter) on Shabbos and Yom Tov — even a temporary one (ohel arai). And this applies only to a roof (gag); making walls (mechitzos) is permitted. A mechitzah is forbidden only when it renders a sukkah valid or enables carrying (by creating a halachic domain). Rama's gloss: A mechitzah made purely for tznius is permitted — therefore it is permitted to hang a curtain before a doorway, or a paroches before the aron kodesh, provided one does not form a roof of a tefach at the top. So too it is permitted to set up a partition against the sun, against the cold, or to shield the candles from the wind. But it is forbidden to set up a partition before a candle for marital purposes, or before sefarim in order to be intimate or to relieve oneself — unless a tefach was already in place from before Shabbos (then one may complete it on Shabbos).

Seif ב — Tosefes ohel: completing a pre-existing shelter

עצים שתוקעין ראשן האחד בדופן הספינה וכופפין ראשם השני בדופן השני של הספינה ופורסין מחצלת עליהם לצל אם יש ברחבן טפח או אפי' אין ברחבן טפח אם אין בין זה לזה ג' טפחים חשיבי כאוהל ומותר לפרוס עליהם בשבת מחצלות דהוה ליה תוספת אהל עראי ושרי ומטעם זה מחצלת פרוסה כדי טפח מותר לפרוס שאר המחצלת בשבת וטפח שאמרו חוץ מן הכריכה:
Translation: Poles whose one end is stuck into one side of a boat and whose other end is bent into the opposite side, over which a mat is spread for shade: if the poles are a tefach wide, or even less but spaced less than three tefachim apart — they are already considered an ohel. One may therefore spread mats over them on Shabbos, since this is only tosefes ohel arai (addition to an existing temporary shelter), which is permitted. For the same reason: a mat already spread out a tefach from before Shabbos — one may unroll the rest on Shabbos. The tefach mentioned is measured excluding the rolled-up portion.

2. The general context — the melachah of ohel

What is this siman about?

Siman שט"ו deals with ohel (אהל — roof, shelter, canopy). Making a roof on Shabbos is a toladah (derivative) of the melachah of boneh (building). The siman lays out a subtle and very practical distinction: the roof is forbidden, but the walls are permitted.

The key principle: "ודוקא גג אבל מחיצות מותר" — the issur of ohel applies only to the roof (gag, the horizontal plane that covers / casts shade). Vertical walls (mechitzos) are in principle permitted — unless the mechitzah renders a sukkah valid or creates a domain for carrying.
Ohel keva vs ohel arai:
  • אהל קבע (ohel keva) — a permanent shelter → forbidden מן התורה (toladah of boneh).
  • אהל עראי (ohel arai) — a temporary shelter → forbidden מדרבנן (gezeirah, "lest one come to ohel keva").
  • תוספת אהל (tosefes ohel) — completing an already-existing shelter (at least a tefach) → permitted!

Its place within Hilchos Shabbos

Siman שט"ו concludes the "building bloc" (313–314–315) by dealing with covering. This is the siman that governs umbrellas, awnings, crib netting, strollers' canopies — anything that "covers." Especially practical in the summer and for young parents.

3. The key halachic concepts

1. אהל (ohel) — shelter, roof. Toladah of boneh. This is the horizontal plane that covers and casts shade — not the supports.
2. גג (gag) — the roof. Only the roof is subject to the issur. A horizontal surface a tefach (≈ 8 cm) wide already qualifies as a significant "roof."
3. מחיצה (mechitzah) — a vertical wall. In principle permitted on Shabbos — unless it renders a sukkah valid or creates a domain for carrying (mechitzah hamateres).
4. תוספת אהל (tosefes ohel) — addition to an existing shelter. If a roof of at least a tefach was in place before Shabbos, one may complete it on Shabbos. This is the great permissive mechanism of the siman.
5. אהל עראי and the gezeirah. Even a temporary shelter is forbidden מדרבנן — a gezeirah lest one come to ohel keva. Hence the importance of the pre-existing tefach for avoiding the issur.

  2. If a wall → generally permitted

6. The Rama's position

The Rama adds a rich gloss to seif 1, codifying the cases of permitted walls:

Main glosses of the Rama (seif 1):
  • Tznius wall — a partition made for simple tznius is permitted. Hence: hanging a curtain before a doorway, or a paroches before the aron kodesh → permitted.
  • Condition: not to form a roof of a tefach at the top of the curtain.
  • Permitted: a partition against the sun, the cold, or to shield the candles from the wind.
  • Forbidden: a partition before light for marital purposes, or before sefarim to relieve oneself — unless a tefach was already in place from before Shabbos.
Difference between Mechaber and Rama:
  • Mechaber → lays out the gag/mechitzah principle.
  • Rama → details the permitted walls (tznius) and their limits.
  • Chabad → Shulchan Aruch HaRav, Siman שט"ו.

7. Modern practical cases

SituationHalachic analysis
Umbrella (opening / closing)Forbidden according to most poskim — this is making an ohel arai. An umbrella already open before Shabbos = permitted to stand under it.
Stroller canopy / sunshadeIf the canopy has a tefach extended before Shabbos → one may unfold it further (tosefes ohel). Otherwise → borderline case, consult your Rav.
Mosquito net over a cribIf it forms a roof → ohel problem. If a tefach was set up before Shabbos → one may complete it.
Curtain, room divider, partitionPermitted — it is a wall (mechitzah), not a roof. Unless it renders a sukkah valid or creates a domain for carrying.
Covering a pot, a dishPermitted — a cover almost touching the contents is not a significant "ohel." The issue arises when there is an empty space of a tefach beneath.
Retractable awning / patio coverExtending = making an ohel. If a tefach was extended before Shabbos → completing is permitted.
Children's play tentSetting up a tent on Shabbos = ohel, forbidden. Leaving it set up from before Shabbos = OK.
For halachah l'maaseh: The golden rule: a roof (awning, umbrella, canopy, tent) is not made on Shabbos. The practical solution: leave a tefach extended before Shabbos — one will then be able to complete it (tosefes ohel). Vertical walls (curtains, partitions) remain permitted.

8. Practical summary of the Siman

Key teachings of Siman שט"ו:
  1. Making an ohel (roof/shelter) on Shabbos = toladah of boneh, forbidden — even temporarily (gezeirah).
  2. Only the roof (gag) is involved; the walls (mechitzos) are permitted.
  3. Tosefes ohel: if a tefach of roof was in place before Shabbos, one may complete it.
  4. Tznius wall (curtain, paroches): permitted — without forming a roof at the top.
  5. Mechitzah hamateres (rendering a sukkah valid / creating a domain): forbidden.
  6. Practical solution: extend a tefach before Shabbos (umbrella, canopy, awning).

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Of which melachah is ohel a toladah?
  2. What is the fundamental distinction between gag and mechitzah?
  3. What is the difference between ohel keva and ohel arai? Why is arai also forbidden?
  4. What is tosefes ohel? What is the condition of the tefach?
  5. Why is a tznius curtain permitted, but a partition that renders a sukkah valid forbidden?
  6. May one open an umbrella on Shabbos? And a stroller canopy?
  7. What is the practical solution for using an awning / umbrella on Shabbos?
