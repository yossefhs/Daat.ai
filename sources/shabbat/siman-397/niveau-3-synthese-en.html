1. The central axiom

Siman שצ״ז in one sentence.
Every person has two thousand amos in every direction: « כל אדם יש לו אלפים אמה לכל רוח חוץ מארבע אמותיו או מהמקום ששבת בו » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ז:א); one on whom the holy day began in a valley, and who does not know the techum, walks two thousand paces (seif ב). His belongings and his animal do not go beyond his two thousand amos: « כך כליו ובהמתו אין יכול שום אדם להוליכ׳ חוץ לאלפים אמה של בעליה׳ » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ז:ג). Seifim ד to יח say, case by case, whose techum a thing follows — for example the father's (seif ד), the borrower's or the lender's (seif יא), the one who draws (seifim יד to טז); on some of these cases, the commentators differ (section 5).

4. Decision tree — whose techum does a thing follow?

Q1: Is it the person himself? (seifim א and ב)
↓ YES → two thousand amos in every direction, beyond one's four amos, or from the place where one took one's rest (seif א); the holy day began on him in a valley, and he does not know the techum → two thousand paces (seif ב). NO → Q2.
Q2: Is it a utensil or an animal that has an owner? (seif ג)
↓ They do not go beyond the two thousand amos of their owners, nor, if these made an eruv toward one direction, toward the other, even one step, where they cannot go (seif ג). The following seifim say, case by case, whose techum the thing follows → Q3.
Q3: Was the animal handed over, or the ox sold? (seifim ד to ז)
↓ To the son → like the feet of the father (seif ד); to a shepherd, even on Yom Tov → of the shepherd; to two shepherds → of the owner (seif ה) — on these two seifim, the commentators differ (section 5). A fattener's ox → of the one who bought it to slaughter it on Yom Tov; slaughtered and sold on Yom Tov → each buyer takes his portion to the place he is going (seif ו). A herdsman's ox → of the people of the town (seif ז).
Q4: Does the thing belong to several people, or come from another? (seifim ח to יג)
↓ The brothers' utensils: set aside for one → his feet; otherwise → where all can go (seif ח). A garment borrowed by two → where both can go; eruvin at the end of the two thousand, one to the east, the other to the west → it is not moved (seif ט). An animal bought in partnership and slaughtered on Yom Tov → all the meat, of both, even though each took his portion (the commentators differ: section 5); a barrel of wine divided on Yom Tov → each one's share, his feet (seif י). Borrowed on Erev Yom Tov, even if taken only at night → of the borrower; on Yom Tov, even by habit → of the lender (seif יא). The dough and dish of the woman who borrowed on Yom Tov → of both (seif יב). A coal taken on Yom Tov → of the one who gives it; lit from the other's flame → of the one who lit (seif יג).
Q5: Is it water? (seifim יד to טז)
↓ An individual's cistern → its owner; the town's → the people of the town; an ownerless one → the one who draws (seif יד). Flowing rivers and gushing springs → the one who draws; coming from outside the techum into the techum → one draws from them on Shabbos, and needless to say on Yom Tov (seif טו). Drawn for a fellow from an ownerless cistern → the one who drew it; the commentators differ (seif טז; section 5).
Q6: Is it deposited produce, or guests' portions? (seifim יז and יח)
↓ Produce deposited in another town, far away, whose inhabitants made an eruv to come to its owner → when a corner was set aside for it, like its owner, and they do not bring it to him; otherwise, like the feet of the one with whom it is deposited; the commentators differ on what decides (seif יז; section 5). Portions of guests invited on Yom Tov → not to a place where the host of the meal cannot go, unless he gave them title on Erev Yom Tov, through another person (seif יח).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic א-כ-ר-ש-ש-ג-מ-פ-א

Nine markers taken from the seifim, in their order:

א — אלפים: two thousand amos in every direction, beyond one's four amos or the place where one took one's rest (seif א); two thousand paces in the valley, for one who does not know the techum (seif ב).

כ — כליו ובהמתו: not beyond the two thousand amos of their owners (seif ג).

ר — רועה: an animal handed over to the son follows the father (seif ד); to a shepherd, even on Yom Tov, the shepherd; to two shepherds, the owner (seif ה) — the commentators differ (section 5); the fattener's ox follows the one who bought it to slaughter it on Yom Tov (seif ו), the herdsman's the people of the town (seif ז).

ש — שותפות: the brothers' utensils (seif ח), the garment borrowed by two (seif ט), the animal and the barrel bought in partnership (seif י — on the animal, the commentators differ).

ש — שואל: borrowed on Erev Yom Tov, the borrower; on Yom Tov, the lender (seif יא); the dough and dish of the woman who borrowed on Yom Tov, both (seif יב).

ג — גחלת: a coal taken on Yom Tov follows the one who gives it; what one lights from the other's flame, the one who lit (seif יג).

מ — ממלא: the ownerless cistern (seif יד), rivers and springs (seif טו), water drawn for a fellow (seif טז — the commentators differ).

פ — פירות: produce deposited in another town, far away, like its owner when a corner was set aside for it — the inhabitants who made an eruv to come to him do not bring it to him —, otherwise like the depositary (seif יז — the commentators differ on what decides).

א — אורחים: no portions to a place where the host of the meal cannot go, unless he gave them title on Erev Yom Tov, through another (seif יח).

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Forgetting the four amos. Seif א gives the two thousand amos « חוץ מארבע אמותיו או מהמקום ששבת בו » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ז:א) — beyond one's four amos, or from the place where one took one's rest.
Pitfall 2: Attributing to the Rema the parenthesis of seif ב. (בינוניות) (טור) is in small type, without הגה: it is attributed to no one (Level 1). The gloss of the Rema introduced by הגה is in seif ג.
Pitfall 3: Thinking that an animal handed over to the son follows the son. Seif ד writes: « המוסר בהמתו לבנו הרי היא כרגלי האב » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ז:ד); the commentators differ on this seif (section 5).
Pitfall 4: Thinking that the meat of an animal bought in partnership and slaughtered on Yom Tov follows each partner, because each took his portion. Seif י makes it like the feet of both, even though each took his portion; it is in a barrel of wine divided on Yom Tov that each one's share follows his feet. On the animal, the commentators differ (section 5).
Pitfall 5: Thinking that an object borrowed on Yom Tov follows the borrower when one is used to borrowing it every Yom Tov. Seif יא makes it like the feet of the lender; borrowed on Erev Yom Tov, even if taken only at night, it follows the borrower.
Pitfall 6: Treating a coal like a flame. A coal taken on Yom Tov follows the one who gives it; a lamp or wood lit from the other's flame follows the one who lit (seif יג).
Pitfall 7: Mixing the two numberings. The Mechaber has 18 seifim, the Shulchan Aruch HaRav 20: the Rav treats the herdsman's ox before the fattener's (397:6 and 397:7, for seifim ז and ו), divides seif טו between its seifim 15 and 16, writes the rain in its seif 17, and its seifim 18 to 20 correspond to seifim טז to יח (section 3).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שצ״ז

For daily conduct

  1. The two thousand amos: every person has two thousand amos in every direction, beyond one's four amos or the place where one took one's rest (seif א); if the holy day began on him in a valley, and he does not know the techum, he walks two thousand paces (seif ב).
  2. Belongings and the animal: no one takes them beyond the two thousand amos of their owners, nor, if these made an eruv toward one direction, toward the other, even one step, where they cannot go (seif ג); on the gloss of the Rema, see section 3.
  3. The animal handed over: to the son, like the feet of the father (seif ד); to a shepherd, even on Yom Tov, like the feet of the shepherd; to two shepherds, like the feet of the owner (seif ה). The commentators differ on these two seifim (section 5): consult your Rav.
  4. The two oxen: the fattener's, like the feet of the one who bought it to slaughter it on Yom Tov, and, slaughtered and sold on Yom Tov, each buyer takes his portion to the place he is going (seif ו); the herdsman's, like the feet of the people of the town (seif ז).
  5. What belongs to several people: the brothers' utensils (seif ח), the garment borrowed by two (seif ט), the animal bought in partnership and slaughtered on Yom Tov — all the meat like the feet of both, even though each took his portion — and the barrel of wine divided on Yom Tov — each one's share like his feet (seif י). On the animal, the commentators differ (section 5): consult your Rav.
  6. What one borrows or takes from another: borrowed on Erev Yom Tov, even if taken only at night, like the feet of the borrower; on Yom Tov, even by habit, like the feet of the lender (seif יא); the dough and dish of the woman who borrowed on Yom Tov, like the feet of both (seif יב); a coal taken on Yom Tov, like the feet of the one who gives it, what one lights from the other's flame, like the feet of the one who lit (seif יג).
  7. Water: an individual's cistern, the town's, an ownerless one (seif יד); rivers and springs, and what comes from outside the techum (seif טו); water drawn from an ownerless cistern for a fellow, like the feet of the one who drew it (seif טז). On water drawn for a fellow, and on rain that falls on Yom Tov, which the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes (397:17), the commentators differ (section 5): consult your Rav.
  8. Deposited produce: in another town, far away, it is like its owner when a corner was set aside for it, and the inhabitants of that town who made an eruv to come to him do not bring it to him; otherwise, like the feet of the one with whom it is deposited (seif יז). The commentators differ on what decides this case (section 5): consult your Rav.
  9. The guests: invited on Yom Tov, they do not carry portions to a place where the host of the meal cannot go, unless he gave them title to these portions on Erev Yom Tov, through another person (seif יח).
  10. When in doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
