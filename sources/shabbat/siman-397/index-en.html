Orach Chaim · Hilchos Techumin

דין שביתת היחיד וכליו ומהלך אלפים אמה

The resting place of the individual and his belongings; walking two thousand amos

18 seifim in the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber, with a gloss of the Rema introduced by הגה in seif ג; 20 in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, under its own numbering (Level 4). How far may one go on Shabbos and Yom Tov, and how far may one take what is one's own? The siman answers: two thousand amos in every direction, beyond one's four amos or the place where one took one's rest (seif א); in a valley, for one who does not know the techum, two thousand paces (seif ב); belongings and the animal do not go beyond the two thousand amos of their owners (seif ג); seifim ד to יח say, case by case, whose techum a thing follows, and on several cases, for example the animal handed over to the son or to shepherds (seifim ד and ה), the commentators differ (Level 2).
