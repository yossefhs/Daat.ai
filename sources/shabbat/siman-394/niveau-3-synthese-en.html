The seif numbers on this page are those of the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber (3 seifim, with a gloss of the Rema introduced by הגה in seif ב). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav, studied in Level 4, also writes this siman in 3 seifim, under its own numbering: when it is cited, its name is given next to the number.

1. The central axiom

Siman שצ״ד in one sentence.
What the eruv is at twilight. If it is doubtful whether it existed then, it is permitted, provided it has a presumption of validity: he placed it there, and a doubt arose; if it is doubtful whether it was placed, the seif writes that it is not, and the commentators diverge on this case (seif א; section 5). At twilight, the eruv must be where it is possible to take it: if a heap fell on it and he cannot take it without a hoe and an axe, it is forbidden (seif ב); the gloss of the Rema, introduced by הגה in the same seif, deals with when the eruv must exist and when it is eaten. If he put it in a migdal and locked it, and the key was lost before dark, and it is impossible to take it out without doing a complete melachah at twilight, it is not an eruv (seif ג).

4. Decision tree — the eruv at twilight

Q1: Is it doubtful whether the eruv existed at twilight? (seif א)
↓ YES, and it had a presumption of validity — he placed it there, and a doubt arose → it is permitted (seif א). It is doubtful whether it was placed → the seif writes that it is not; the commentators diverge on this case (section 5).
Q2: At twilight, was the eruv where it is possible to take it? (seif ב)
↓ A heap fell on it, and he cannot take it without a hoe and an axe → it is forbidden (seif ב). Taking it requires only an act forbidden as a shevus → the commentators diverge (section 5).
Q3: When is the eruv eaten? (seif ב, gloss of the Rema)
↓ The gloss: one may eat it when it is certainly dark; one should break bread over it on Shabbos morning; and this only where it is customary to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos; but it is much preferable to make it for the whole year at one time (seif ב); on this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4). What this restriction refers to, the page does not decide (Level 4, point ג).
Q4: Was the eruv put in a locked migdal whose key was lost before dark? (seif ג)
↓ YES, and it is impossible to take it out unless he does a complete melachah at twilight → it is as if it were lost, and it is not an eruv (seif ג). A migdal of bricks laid without mortar → the commentators diverge (section 5). The key found on Shabbos → seif ג does not deal with this case; the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes it (394:3), and the commentators diverge on its conditions (section 5).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

5. The doubt whether it was placed (seif א), what is forbidden only as a shevus (seifim ב and ג) and the key found on Shabbos — the commentators diverge

summary: each table gives the text of the Mechaber and that of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav; the commentators diverge, and Level 2 cites each by his own text.

The doubt whether it was placed (seif א)

WorkWhat it writes
Shulchan Aruch, seif א« ספק עירוב כגון ספק אם היה קיים בין השמשו׳ אם לאו מותר והוא שיהיה לו חזקת כשרו׳ כגון שהניחו שם ואירע בו ספק אבל אם לא היה לו חזקת כשרו׳ כגון ספק אם הונח שם אם לאו לא » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:א) — without a presumption of validity, for example if it is doubtful whether it was placed there, it is not
Shulchan Aruch HaRav (394:1)« סְפֵק עֵרוּב, כְּגוֹן סָפֵק אִם הָיָה קַיָּם בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת אִם לָאו — מֻתָּר, שֶׁסְּפֵק דִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים לְהָקֵל. וְהוּא שֶׁהִנִּיחוֹ שָׁם וְאֵרַע בּוֹ סָפֵק, אֲבָל סָפֵק אִם הֻנַּח שָׁם אִם לָאו — אָסוּר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:א) — a doubt about the eruv, for example whether it existed at twilight or not, is permitted, because a doubt in a rabbinic rule is decided leniently, provided he placed it there and a doubt arose; a doubt whether it was placed there is forbidden (Level 4, point א)
The commentatorsThey diverge. The Biur Halachah writes on this rule: « ודע דדין זה לאו דין ברור הוא דרבים מהראשונים פליגי עליה » (ביאור הלכה שצ״ד) — it is not a clear rule, for many of the Rishonim disagree with it. Each is cited by his own text in Level 2 (section 2)

What is forbidden only as a shevus (seifim ב and ג)

WorkWhat it writes
Shulchan Aruch, seifim ב and גIn seif ב, the eruv that cannot be taken without a hoe and an axe; in seif ג, the eruv that cannot be taken out of the migdal unless he does a complete melachah at twilight
Shulchan Aruch HaRav (394:2)After writing that digging with a hoe and an axe is a complete melachah of the Torah, forbidden even at twilight: « אֲבָל דָּבָר שֶׁהוּא מִשּׁוּם שְׁבוּת לֹא גָזְרוּ עָלָיו בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת לְצֹרֶךְ מִצְוָה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רס״א, וְהָעֵרוּב גַּם כֵּן הוּא צֹרֶךְ מִצְוָה, כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יְטַלְטְלוּ בְּאִסּוּר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שס״ו » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ב) — something forbidden as a shevus, the Sages did not decree against at twilight for the sake of a mitzvah, and the eruv too is for the sake of a mitzvah, so that people do not carry in a forbidden way (Level 4, point ב)
The commentatorsThey diverge: each is cited by his own text in Level 2 (section 6), where the migdal of bricks laid without mortar is also treated

The key found on Shabbos (seif ג)

WorkWhat it writes
Shulchan Aruch, seif גThe seif stops at the key lost before dark: it does not deal with the key found on Shabbos
Shulchan Aruch HaRav (394:3)After the rule of the migdal: « וְאִם נִמְצָא הַמַּפְתֵּחַ בְּשַׁבָּת, אִם הָיָה בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁיָּכוֹל לַהֲבִיאוֹ בְּלֹא מְלָאכָה שֶׁל תּוֹרָה — הֲרֵי זֶה עֵרוּב. וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה הַמַּפְתֵּחַ בְּיָדוֹ בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, כֵּיוָן שֶׁמָּצוּי הוּא שֶׁיִּמְצָאֶנּוּ — אָנוּ חוֹשְׁבִים אוֹתוֹ כְּשֶׁמְּצָאוֹ כְּאִלּוּ הָיָה בְּיָדוֹ לְמַפְרֵעַ » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ג) — if the key is found on Shabbos, in a place from which he can bring it without a melachah forbidden by the Torah, it is an eruv; although it was not in his hand at twilight, since it is common that he finds it, once found we consider it as if it had been in his hand retroactively (Level 4, point ד)
The commentatorsThey diverge on the conditions of this case: each is cited by his own text in Level 2 (section 7)

For a concrete case, the page does not decide: consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic ס-ח-ג-ב-מ

Five words of the seifim, in their order:

ס — ספק: if it is doubtful whether the eruv existed at twilight, it is permitted (seif א) — under the condition of the next letter.

ח — חזקת כשרות: the presumption of validity — he placed it there, and a doubt arose; if it is doubtful whether it was placed, the seif writes that it is not, and the commentators diverge on this case (seif א; section 5).

ג — גל: a heap fell on the eruv, and he cannot take it without a hoe and an axe: it is forbidden (seif ב); for an act forbidden only as a shevus, the commentators diverge (section 5).

ב — בין השמשות: the gloss of the Rema — the eruv need exist only at twilight; one may eat it when it is certainly dark; one should break bread over it on Shabbos morning; and this only where it is customary to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos; but it is much preferable to make it for the whole year at one time (seif ב); on this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4).

מ — מפתח: the key of the migdal lost before dark: if it is impossible to take out the eruv unless he does a complete melachah at twilight, it is not an eruv (seif ג); for the key found on Shabbos, see section 5.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that every doubt about the eruv is permitted. Seif א sets a condition, the presumption of validity: he placed it there, and a doubt arose. If it is doubtful whether it was placed, the seif writes that it is not; the commentators diverge on this case (section 5).
Pitfall 2: Presenting the doubt whether it was placed as settled. Seif א writes that it is not, and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (394:1) that it is forbidden; but the Biur Halachah writes: « ודע דדין זה לאו דין ברור הוא דרבים מהראשונים פליגי עליה » (ביאור הלכה שצ״ד); each is cited by his own text in Level 2 (section 2). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 3: Extending the prohibition of seif ב to any obstacle. Seif ב forbids when he cannot take the eruv without a hoe and an axe; for what is forbidden only as a shevus, the commentators diverge (section 5).
Pitfall 4: Attributing to the Mechaber what the gloss writes. That the eruv need exist only at twilight, that one may eat it when it is certainly dark, that one should break bread over it on Shabbos morning, and the end, on the eruv of every Erev Shabbos and the eruv for the whole year: this is the gloss of the Rema, introduced by הגה in seif ב, not the Mechaber.
Pitfall 5: Making the end of the gloss say more than its text. It writes: and this only where it is customary to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos; but it is much preferable to make it for the whole year at one time, as above, siman 368 (seif ב). On this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4); what this restriction refers to, the page does not decide (Level 4, point ג): consult your Rav.
Pitfall 6: Thinking that the lost key is enough to cancel the eruv. Seif ג sets the condition: if it is impossible to take out the eruv unless he does a complete melachah at twilight. And the key found on Shabbos, the seif does not deal with; the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes it (394:3), and the commentators diverge on its conditions (section 5).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שצ״ד

For daily conduct

  1. The doubtful eruv: if it is doubtful whether it existed at twilight, it is permitted, provided it has a presumption of validity — he placed it there, and a doubt arose; if it is doubtful whether it was placed, the seif writes that it is not (seif א). The commentators diverge on this last case (section 5): consult your Rav.
  2. The place of the eruv at twilight: where it is possible to take it; if a heap fell on it and he cannot take it without a hoe and an axe, it is forbidden (seif ב). For what is forbidden only as a shevus, the commentators diverge (section 5).
  3. The gloss of the Rema: the eruv need exist only at twilight; one may eat it when it is certainly dark; one should break bread over it on Shabbos morning; and this only where it is customary to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos; but it is much preferable to make it for the whole year at one time (seif ב; siman 368); on this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4).
  4. The migdal whose key was lost before dark: if it is impossible to take the eruv out of it unless he does a complete melachah at twilight, it is not an eruv (seif ג). For a migdal of bricks laid without mortar, the commentators diverge; for the key found on Shabbos, the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes the case (394:3), and the commentators diverge on its conditions (section 5).
  5. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
