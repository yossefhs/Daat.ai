דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on Siman 394

The doubtful eruv; the eruv on which a heap fell; the eruv locked in a migdal whose key was lost, and the key found on Shabbos. The 3 seifim of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, compared with the 3 seifim of the Mechaber.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav is not a commentary on the Mechaber: it is a Shulchan Aruch in its own right, written by the Alter Rebbe, with its own division and numbering of the seifim. Here the Shulchan Aruch HaRav announces 3 seifim at the head of the siman, and the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber has 3 as well. Sefaria serves the Rav's text in 3 blocks, which this page reproduces and numbers as they stand (section 1). Each seif of the Rav corresponds to the seif of the same rank in the Mechaber: the doubtful eruv in seif 1 (the Mechaber's seif א), the eruv on which a heap fell in seif 2 (ב), the eruv locked in a migdal whose key was lost in seif 3 (ג). The Mechaber's seif ב carries a gloss of the Rema introduced by הגה; the Rav's seif 2 does not write it, and the Rav writes in Siman 393, in his seif 3, what it says about the eruv that need exist only at bein hashmashos and about the bread broken on it, without the end of the gloss; on the preference it states, he writes in Siman 368, seif 4 (section 2, point ג). In the English text of this page, the Rav's seifim are designated by their number (394:3, seif 3), the Mechaber's by their Hebrew letter (the Mechaber's seif ג); in the Hebrew references in parentheses, both numberings are in letters, and the reference always names the work (שו״ע או״ח for the Mechaber, שו״ע הרב for the Rav).

This page gathers, for Siman 394, the full text of the Rav with its translation, where his text differs from that of the Mechaber and the Rema, and the practical conduct that follows from it.

1 · Text of the Alter Rebbe

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן שצ״ד

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav of the Alter Rebbe

סימן שצ״ד — סְפֵק עֵרוּב מַה דִּינוֹ — וּבוֹ ג׳ סְעִיפִים
Source: Kehot edition, as reproduced on Sefaria. The heading announces 3 seifim; Sefaria serves the text in 3 blocks (the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has 3 seifim, under its own numbering).

סעיף א סְפֵק עֵרוּב מַה דִּינוֹ וּבוֹ ג' סְעִיפִים: סְפֵק עֵרוּב, כְּגוֹן סָפֵק…

סְפֵק עֵרוּב מַה דִּינוֹ וּבוֹ ג' סְעִיפִים:

סְפֵק עֵרוּב, כְּגוֹן סָפֵק אִם הָיָה קַיָּם בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת אִם לָאו — מֻתָּר, שֶׁסְּפֵק דִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים לְהָקֵל. וְהוּא שֶׁהִנִּיחוֹ שָׁם וְאֵרַע בּוֹ סָפֵק, אֲבָל סָפֵק אִם הֻנַּח שָׁם אִם לָאו — אָסוּר:

A doubtful eruv, what is its law; it contains 3 seifim:

A doubtful eruv — for example, a doubt whether it existed at bein hashmashos (twilight) or not — is permitted, for a doubt in [a law] of rabbinic origin (sfek divrei sofrim) [is resolved] leniently. And this is provided that he placed it there and a doubt arose concerning it; but a doubt whether it was placed there or not — it is forbidden:

סעיף ב צָרִיךְ שֶׁיְּהֵא הָעֵרוּב בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת…

צָרִיךְ שֶׁיְּהֵא הָעֵרוּב בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁרָאוּי לִטְּלוֹ וּלְאָכְלוֹ. לְפִיכָךְ אִם נָפַל עָלָיו גַּל, וְאֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לִטְּלוֹ עַד שֶׁיַּחְפּוֹר בְּמָרָא וְקַרְדֹּם — אֵינוֹ עֵרוּב, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁחֲפִירָה זוֹ הִיא מְלָאכָה גְמוּרָה שֶׁל תּוֹרָה וַאֲסוּרָה אֲפִלּוּ בְּבֵין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת. אֲבָל דָּבָר שֶׁהוּא מִשּׁוּם שְׁבוּת לֹא גָזְרוּ עָלָיו בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת לְצֹרֶךְ מִצְוָה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רס״א, וְהָעֵרוּב גַּם כֵּן הוּא צֹרֶךְ מִצְוָה, כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יְטַלְטְלוּ בְּאִסּוּר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שס״ו:

The eruv must be, at bein hashmashos, in a place where it is fit to be taken and eaten. Therefore, if a heap (gal) fell on it and he cannot take it unless he digs with a pick and an axe, it is not an eruv, because this digging is a complete labor (melachah gemurah) of the Torah and is forbidden even at bein hashmashos. But a thing that is [forbidden] as a shevus — [the Sages] did not decree against it at bein hashmashos for the sake of a mitzvah, as was explained in Siman 261; and the eruv too is for the sake of a mitzvah, so that people not carry in a forbidden manner, as was explained in Siman 366:

סעיף ג נְתָנוֹ בְּמִגְדָּל וְנָעַל בְּפָנָיו…

נְתָנוֹ בְּמִגְדָּל וְנָעַל בְּפָנָיו, וְאָבַד הַמַּפְתֵּחַ קֹדֶם שֶׁחָשֵׁכָה, אִם אִי אֶפְשָׁר לְהוֹצִיא מִשָּׁם הָעֵרוּב אֶלָּא אִם כֵּן יַעֲשֶׂה מְלָאכָה גְמוּרָה — הֲרֵי זֶה כְּמִי שֶׁאָבַד, וְאֵינוֹ עֵרוּב, שֶׁהֲרֵי אִי אֶפְשָׁר לְאָכְלוֹ.

וְאִם נִמְצָא הַמַּפְתֵּחַ בְּשַׁבָּת, אִם הָיָה בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁיָּכוֹל לַהֲבִיאוֹ בְּלֹא מְלָאכָה שֶׁל תּוֹרָה — הֲרֵי זֶה עֵרוּב. וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה הַמַּפְתֵּחַ בְּיָדוֹ בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, כֵּיוָן שֶׁמָּצוּי הוּא שֶׁיִּמְצָאֶנּוּ — אָנוּ חוֹשְׁבִים אוֹתוֹ כְּשֶׁמְּצָאוֹ כְּאִלּוּ הָיָה בְּיָדוֹ לְמַפְרֵעַ:

If he put it in a migdal, locked it, and the key was lost before nightfall: if it is impossible to take the eruv out of there unless he does a complete labor (melachah gemurah), it is like [an eruv] that was lost, and it is not an eruv, since it is impossible to eat it.

And if the key was found on Shabbos: if it was in a place from which he can bring it without a labor [forbidden] by the Torah, it is an eruv. And even though the key was not in his hand at bein hashmashos, since it is common that he will find it, we regard it, once he has found it, as if it had been in his hand retroactively:

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim 394. Sefaria gives no Kuntres Acharon entry for this siman.

2 · The Rav's text and the Mechaber's

לשון הרב מול סעיף המחבר והרמ״א

Where the Rav's text differs from the Mechaber's seif and the Rema's

Each point sets side by side a seif of the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch (its numbering, 3 seifim, designated by their letter) and the corresponding seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (its own numbering, also 3 seifim), in the order of the Rav's seifim, and notes only what the comparison of the two texts shows; in point ג, the comparison is with the gloss of the Rema introduced by הגה in the Mechaber's seif ב. A point noted is not thereby peculiar to the Rav: the page sometimes names its source; it notes neither all the sources nor all the divergences.

נקודה א — ספק עירוב (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:א)

Point א — the doubtful eruv (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 394:1)

The doubtful eruv

What the Mechaber writes: « ספק עירוב כגון ספק אם היה קיים בין השמשו' אם לאו מותר והוא שיהיה לו חזקת כשרו' כגון שהניחו שם ואירע בו ספק אבל אם לא היה לו חזקת כשרו' כגון ספק אם הונח שם אם לאו לא » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:א).

What the Rav writes: « סְפֵק עֵרוּב, כְּגוֹן סָפֵק אִם הָיָה קַיָּם בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת אִם לָאו — מֻתָּר, שֶׁסְּפֵק דִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים לְהָקֵל. וְהוּא שֶׁהִנִּיחוֹ שָׁם וְאֵרַע בּוֹ סָפֵק, אֲבָל סָפֵק אִם הֻנַּח שָׁם אִם לָאו — אָסוּר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:א).

The difference: the Rav's text gives the reason for permitting, « שֶׁסְּפֵק דִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים לְהָקֵל » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:א); the Mechaber's seif א gives none. The Mechaber states the condition with the words « חזקת כשרו' » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:א), with an example for each side, introduced by כגון; the Rav's text does not use these words, and writes the condition through the cases themselves: he placed it there and a doubt arose; a doubt whether it was placed there. For this last case, the Mechaber writes « אבל אם לא היה לו חזקת כשרו' כגון ספק אם הונח שם אם לאו לא » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:א), and the Rav « אָסוּר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:א).

Among the commentators: on the eruv that has no « חזקת כשרו' » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:א) — the doubt whether it was placed —, the views diverge: see Level 2.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, a doubtful eruv — for example, a doubt whether it existed at bein hashmashos or not — is permitted, provided that he placed it there and a doubt arose concerning it; a doubt whether it was placed there or not is forbidden (394:1). On this last case, the views diverge (above): consult your Rav. See section 4, point ①.

נקודה ב — עירוב שנפל עליו גל (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ב)

Point ב — the eruv on which a heap fell (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 394:2)

Torah labor and shevus

What the Mechaber writes: « צריך שיהא העירוב בין השמשו' במקום שראוי ליטלו הילכך אם נפל עליו גל ואינו יכול ליטלו בלא מרא וחצינא אסור » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:ב).

What the Rav writes: « צָרִיךְ שֶׁיְּהֵא הָעֵרוּב בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁרָאוּי לִטְּלוֹ וּלְאָכְלוֹ. לְפִיכָךְ אִם נָפַל עָלָיו גַּל, וְאֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לִטְּלוֹ עַד שֶׁיַּחְפּוֹר בְּמָרָא וְקַרְדֹּם — אֵינוֹ עֵרוּב, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁחֲפִירָה זוֹ הִיא מְלָאכָה גְמוּרָה שֶׁל תּוֹרָה וַאֲסוּרָה אֲפִלּוּ בְּבֵין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת. אֲבָל דָּבָר שֶׁהוּא מִשּׁוּם שְׁבוּת לֹא גָזְרוּ עָלָיו בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת לְצֹרֶךְ מִצְוָה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רס״א, וְהָעֵרוּב גַּם כֵּן הוּא צֹרֶךְ מִצְוָה, כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יְטַלְטְלוּ בְּאִסּוּר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שס״ו » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ב).

The difference: the Rav's text writes « לִטְּלוֹ וּלְאָכְלוֹ » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ב) where the Mechaber's seif ב writes « ליטלו » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:ב), and « אֵינוֹ עֵרוּב » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ב) where it writes « אסור » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:ב). It gives the reason: this digging is a complete labor of the Torah, forbidden even at bein hashmashos. Then, introduced by « אֲבָל דָּבָר שֶׁהוּא מִשּׁוּם שְׁבוּת » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ב), it writes that the Sages did not decree against a thing [forbidden] as a shevus at bein hashmashos for the sake of a mitzvah, and that the eruv is for the sake of a mitzvah, so that people not carry in a forbidden manner; the Rav refers to his Simanim 261 and 366. The Mechaber's seif ב writes neither this reason nor this distinction.

Among the commentators: the Magen Avraham, earlier than the Rav, writes on the Mechaber's seif ג « אבל דבר שהוא משום שבות לא גזרו עליו לפי שצורך מצוה היא (מ״מ ספ״א) » (מגן אברהם שצ״ד ס״ק ב), then reports that the Tur holds otherwise: « והטור ס״ל דגזרו עליו משום דמחזי כסותר אוהל שכ' נתנו במגדל שהוא לבנים מסודרים וכו' בשבת אינו עירוב וזה אינו אלא משום מוקצה כמ״ש התו' » (מגן אברהם שצ״ד ס״ק ב). On this point, the views diverge: see Level 2.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, the eruv must be, at bein hashmashos, in a place where it is fit to be taken and eaten; if a heap fell on it and he cannot take it unless he digs with a pick and an axe, it is not an eruv (394:2). What the Rav's distinction between Torah labor and shevus permits in a given case, this page does not decide, and the views diverge on this point (above): consult your Rav. See section 4, point ②.

נקודה ג — הגהת הרמ״א בסעיף ב של המחבר (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ב)

Point ג — the Rema's gloss on the Mechaber's seif ב (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 394:2)

What the Rav writes in Siman 393

What the Rema writes: on the Mechaber's seif ב, the gloss introduced by הגה writes: « והעירוב א״צ להיות קיים רק בין השמשות (טור) ויוכל לאכלו כשודאי חשיכה (הגהות אשירי ומרדכי פרק בכל מערבין) ויש לבצוע עליו בשחרית בשבת (מנהגים) ודוקא במקום שנוהגים לערב כל ערב שבת אבל עדיף טפי לערב על כל השנה בפעם אחת כדלעיל סי' שס״ח (ב״י) » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ד:ב).

What the Rav writes: his seif 2, in Siman 394, does not write this gloss. In Siman 393, his seif 3 writes — summary: the eruv need exist only at bein hashmashos, and one may eat it once it is certainly dark; one should break bread on it on Shabbos morning, or at the night meal if one eats it once it is certainly dark; the Rav gives the reasons (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג).

The difference: the Rav's text writes in Siman 393 what the beginning of the Rema's gloss writes in Siman 394. It writes the end of the gloss neither in seif 3 of Siman 393 nor in the seifim of Siman 394: « ודוקא במקום שנוהגים לערב כל ערב שבת אבל עדיף טפי לערב על כל השנה בפעם אחת כדלעיל סי' שס״ח (ב״י) » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ד:ב). On the preference this end states, the Rav writes in Siman 368, seif 4, that in truth it is better to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos, and that one who does so should eat it the next day (שו״ע הרב שס״ח:ד); see Level 4 of Siman 368, point ה. The detailed comparison is in Level 4 of Siman 393, section 2, point ד.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, the eruv need exist only at bein hashmashos, and one may eat it once it is certainly dark (393:3). On the preference the end of the Rema's gloss states, the Rav writes that in truth the eruv of every Erev Shabbos is better (368:4); on this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4). What the restriction that opens this end of the gloss (ודוקא…) refers to, this page does not decide: consult your Rav. See section 4, point ②.

נקודה ד — נמצא המפתח בשבת (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ג)

Point ד — the key found on Shabbos (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 394:3)

The key that was found

What the Mechaber writes: « נתנו במגדל ונעל בפניו ואבד המפתח קודם שחשכה אם אי אפשר להוציא העירוב אלא אם כן עשה מלאכה גמורה בין השמשו' הרי זה כמו שאבד ואינו עירוב שהרי אי אפשר לאכלו » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ד:ג).

What the Rav writes: after the law of the locked migdal whose key was lost, « וְאִם נִמְצָא הַמַּפְתֵּחַ בְּשַׁבָּת, אִם הָיָה בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁיָּכוֹל לַהֲבִיאוֹ בְּלֹא מְלָאכָה שֶׁל תּוֹרָה — הֲרֵי זֶה עֵרוּב. וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה הַמַּפְתֵּחַ בְּיָדוֹ בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, כֵּיוָן שֶׁמָּצוּי הוּא שֶׁיִּמְצָאֶנּוּ — אָנוּ חוֹשְׁבִים אוֹתוֹ כְּשֶׁמְּצָאוֹ כְּאִלּוּ הָיָה בְּיָדוֹ לְמַפְרֵעַ » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:ג).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif ג ends with the lost key; the Rav's text adds the case of the key found on Shabbos: if it was in a place from which he can bring it without a labor [forbidden] by the Torah, it is an eruv, even though the key was not in his hand at bein hashmashos, since it is common that he will find it.

Among the commentators: the Magen Avraham, earlier than the Rav, writes this case in the name of the Tur and Tosafos: « ואם נמצא המפתח בשבת במקום שיכול להביאו בלא מלאכה דאורייתא הוי עירוב אף על פי שלא היה בידו ב״ה כיון שמצוי הוא שימצאנו חשבי' ליה כאלו הוא בידו (טור ותוס') » (מגן אברהם שצ״ד ס״ק ב). On the conditions of this case, the views diverge: see Level 2.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, if the eruv was put in a locked migdal, the key was lost before nightfall, and the eruv cannot be taken out unless a complete labor is done, it is not an eruv; if the key is found on Shabbos in a place from which he can bring it without a labor [forbidden] by the Torah, it is an eruv, even though the key was not in his hand at bein hashmashos, since it is common that he will find it (394:3). On the conditions of this case, the views diverge (above): consult your Rav. See section 4, point ③.

4 · Lemaaseh

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

The practical Chabad conduct

Points of halachic conduct that flow directly from the Shulchan Aruch HaRav Siman 394, in the Chabad sensibility. Each point refers to its seif of the Rav, in the order of the text.

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman 394

  • ① The doubtful eruv: a doubtful eruv — for example, a doubt whether it existed at bein hashmashos or not — is permitted, for a doubt in a law of rabbinic origin is resolved leniently; provided that he placed it there and a doubt arose concerning it. But a doubt whether it was placed there or not is forbidden (394:1). On this last case, the views diverge (section 2, point א): consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 394:1.
  • ② The eruv on which a heap fell: the eruv must be, at bein hashmashos, in a place where it is fit to be taken and eaten; if a heap fell on it and he cannot take it unless he digs with a pick and an axe, it is not an eruv, because this digging is a complete labor of the Torah, forbidden even at bein hashmashos (394:2). The Rav also writes that the Sages did not decree against a thing [forbidden] as a shevus at bein hashmashos for the sake of a mitzvah, and that the eruv is for the sake of a mitzvah (394:2); on this point, the views diverge (section 2, point ב): for a concrete case, consult your Rav. What the Rema's gloss writes on the Mechaber's seif ב, the Rav writes in Siman 393, without the end of the gloss; on the preference it states, he writes in Siman 368, seif 4, that in truth the eruv of every Erev Shabbos is better (section 2, point ג). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 394:2.
  • ③ The locked eruv whose key was lost: if he put the eruv in a migdal, locked it, and the key was lost before nightfall, and it is impossible to take the eruv out of there unless he does a complete labor, it is not an eruv, since it is impossible to eat it. If the key was found on Shabbos, and it was in a place from which he can bring it without a labor [forbidden] by the Torah, it is an eruv, even though it was not in his hand at bein hashmashos, since it is common that he will find it (394:3). On the conditions of this case, the views diverge (section 2, point ד): consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 394:3.

⚠ This section presents the Chabad halachic conduct. For any concrete question: consult your Rav.
