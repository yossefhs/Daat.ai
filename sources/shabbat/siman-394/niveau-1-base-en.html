1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שצ״ד contains 3 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo). The text Sefaria serves carries a gloss of the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) introduced by הגה, in seif ב. Topic of the siman, according to its title: a doubtful eruv, what is its law. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe also has 3 seifim for this siman: its numbering is that of Level 4, not that of this page.

Seif א

ספק עירוב מה דינו. ובו ג סעיפים:
ספק עירוב כגון ספק אם היה קיים בין השמשו' אם לאו מותר והוא שיהיה לו חזקת כשרו' כגון שהניחו שם ואירע בו ספק אבל אם לא היה לו חזקת כשרו' כגון ספק אם הונח שם אם לאו לא:
Translation: A doubtful eruv — for example, if it is doubtful whether it [still] existed at twilight (bein hashmashos) or not — is permitted; and that is provided it has a presumption of validity (chezkas kashrus), for example, he placed it there and a doubt arose about it; but if it had no presumption of validity, for example, if it is doubtful whether it was placed there or not, [it is] not.
What this seif says:
  • If it is doubtful whether the eruv existed at twilight, it is permitted, provided it has a presumption of validity: he placed it there, and a doubt arose about it: ספק עירוב כגון ספק אם היה קיים בין השמשו׳ אם לאו מותר והוא שיהיה לו חזקת כשרו׳ כגון שהניחו שם ואירע בו ספק. The doubt whether it existed, explains the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק א): the eruv was eaten or burned, and one does not know whether this was before twilight began or whether it still existed at the time of twilight.
  • But without a presumption of validity — if it is doubtful whether it was placed there — not: אבל אם לא היה לו חזקת כשרו׳ כגון ספק אם הונח שם אם לאו לא. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ב) explains how this case differs from the eruv of courtyards placed at twilight (simanim 393 and 261), which has no presumption of validity either: a doubt whether it was placed at all is worse. He derives from this that if one placed, for the partnership of alleys, meat that is doubtfully treifah, it is not an eruv. On this last case of the seif — the doubt whether it was placed — the commentators differ (Biur Halachah; Level 2).

Seif ב

צריך שיהא העירוב בין השמשו' במקום שראוי ליטלו הילכך אם נפל עליו גל ואינו יכול ליטלו בלא מרא וחצינא אסור: הגה והעירוב א״צ להיות קיים רק בין השמשות (טור) ויוכל לאכלו כשודאי חשיכה (הגהות אשירי ומרדכי פרק בכל מערבין) ויש לבצוע עליו בשחרית בשבת (מנהגים) ודוקא במקום שנוהגים לערב כל ערב שבת אבל עדיף טפי לערב על כל השנה בפעם אחת כדלעיל סי' שס״ח (ב״י):
Translation: The eruv must be, at twilight, in a place where it is possible to take it; therefore, if a heap [of rubble] (gal) fell on it and he cannot take it without a hoe and an axe (mara vechatzina), it is forbidden. Gloss (hagah): And the eruv need exist only at twilight (Tur), and one may eat it when it is certainly dark (Hagahos Asheri and Mordechai, chapter Bechol me'arvin); and one should break bread over it on Shabbos morning (Minhagim). And this only in a place where it is customary to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos; but it is much preferable to make the eruv for the whole year at one time, as above, siman 368 (Beis Yosef).
What this seif says:
  • At twilight, the eruv must be where it is possible to take it: צריך שיהא העירוב בין השמשו׳ במקום שראוי ליטלו.
  • A heap fell on it, and he cannot take the eruv without a hoe and an axe: it is forbidden: הילכך אם נפל עליו גל ואינו יכול ליטלו בלא מרא וחצינא אסור. But, notes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ג), if no hoe and axe are needed, but only moving a muktzeh object, this was not prohibited at twilight, as written in siman 261. For what is forbidden only as a shevus, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 6).
  • Gloss of the Rema: the eruv need exist only at twilight, and one may eat it when it is certainly dark: והעירוב א״צ להיות קיים רק בין השמשות (טור) ויוכל לאכלו כשודאי חשיכה. The Biur Halachah explains why the Rema writes that one eats the eruv when it is certainly dark, although in siman 393, seif ג, an eruv eaten at twilight is valid: there it is after the fact; from the outset, one should not put oneself even into a rabbinic doubt (section 6).
  • Gloss of the Rema: one should break bread over it on Shabbos morning: ויש לבצוע עליו בשחרית בשבת (מנהגים). The reason, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ד): since one mitzvah was performed with it, let another mitzvah be performed with it; and although one may eat it at the evening meal as well, it is better to break bread over it in the morning, because one sometimes eats the evening meal before dark.
  • Gloss of the Rema: this only where it is customary to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos; it is much preferable to make it for the whole year at one time: ודוקא במקום שנוהגים לערב כל ערב שבת אבל עדיף טפי לערב על כל השנה בפעם אחת כדלעיל סי׳ שס״ח (ב״י). On this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4). And in this way, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ו), when the eruv is eaten on Shabbos, it permits only for that Shabbos, not for the following Shabbos.

Seif ג

נתנו במגדל ונעל בפניו ואבד המפתח קודם שחשכה אם אי אפשר להוציא העירוב אלא אם כן עשה מלאכה גמורה בין השמשו' הרי זה כמו שאבד ואינו עירוב שהרי אי אפשר לאכלו:
Translation: If he put it in a migdal and locked it, and the key was lost before dark: if it is impossible to take out the eruv unless he does a complete melachah (melachah gemurah) at twilight, it is as if it were lost, and it is not an eruv, since it is impossible to eat it.
What this seif says:
  • The eruv put in a locked migdal, the key lost before dark: נתנו במגדל ונעל בפניו ואבד המפתח קודם שחשכה. The migdal: a built one, or even a movable one, like a large chest holding forty se'ah, whose dismantling involves demolishing a tent, forbidden by the Torah (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ז, which refers to siman 314). The key lost: only when it was lost from where it was put and one does not know where it is; but if it lies in its place and he only forgot where he put it, it is an eruv, for he is apt to remember (ס״ק ח, in the name of the Rashba and the Ritva). Before dark: before twilight begins (ס״ק ט).
  • If it is impossible to take out the eruv without doing a complete melachah at twilight, it is as if lost, and it is not an eruv: אם אי אפשר להוציא העירוב אלא אם כן עשה מלאכה גמורה בין השמשו׳ הרי זה כמו שאבד ואינו עירוב שהרי אי אפשר לאכלו. For example, if one must demolish the migdal or the door, which involves demolishing a tent (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י). If the key is found on Shabbos where it can be brought without a Torah melachah, with no public domain separating the place of the key from the migdal, it is an eruv, although it was not in hand at twilight: since it is common to find it, it is considered as if it had been in hand (ס״ק י״א); on the conditions of this case, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 7). For a migdal of bricks laid without mortar, whose dismantling is forbidden only by the Sages, the commentators differ (Biur Halachah; Level 2).

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Siman שצ״ד deals with the eruv about which there is a doubt (seif א), with the place where the eruv must be at twilight — and, in the Rema's gloss, with when it is eaten (seif ב) — and with the locked-up eruv whose key was lost (seif ג).

For seif א, the Mishnah deals with the eruv [of techumin] that rolled outside the limit, on which a heap fell, which was burned, or which, being terumah, became impure: by day, it is not an eruv; after dark, it is an eruv; and in case of doubt: « אִם סָפֵק, רַבִּי מֵאִיר וְרַבִּי יְהוּדָה אוֹמְרִים: הֲרֵי זֶה חַמָּר גַּמָּל. רַבִּי יוֹסֵי וְרַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן אוֹמְרִים: סְפֵק עֵירוּב כָּשֵׁר » (עירובין ל״ה ע״א). The Beis Yosef writes: « וידוע דהלכה כמותם לגבי ר״מ ורבי יהודה » (בית יוסף או״ח שצ״ד), and that, although the Mishnah is taught for the eruv of techumin, the same holds for the eruv of courtyards and the partnership of alleys. For the end of the seif, he writes that the Gemara (daf 36) implies it; and the Biur Halachah: « ודע דדין זה לאו דין ברור הוא דרבים מהראשונים פליגי עליה » (ביאור הלכה שצ״ד). On this case, the commentators differ: see Level 2.

Seif ב comes from the same Mishnah (the heap that fell on the eruv). The Gemara asks whether the Mishnah does not follow Rebbi, for whom a rabbinic prohibition (shevus) was not decreed at twilight, and answers: « אֲפִילּוּ תֵּימָא כְּרַבִּי, לָא צְרִיכָא, דְּבָעֵי מָרָא וַחֲצִינָא » (עירובין ל״ה ע״א). The Rema's gloss, on when the eruv is eaten, is studied in section 6.

Seif ג comes from the Mishnah: « נְתָנוֹ בְּמִגְדָּל וְאָבַד הַמַּפְתֵּחַ — הֲרֵי זֶה עֵירוּב. רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר אוֹמֵר: אִם אֵינוֹ יוֹדֵעַ שֶׁהַמַּפְתֵּחַ בִּמְקוֹמוֹ — אֵינוֹ עֵירוּב » (עירובין ל״ד ע״ב), whose cases the Gemara discusses (ל״ד ע״ב – ל״ה ע״א). The Biur Halachah writes that the words מלאכה גמורה are the language of the Rambam; for a migdal of bricks laid without mortar, the commentators differ (Level 2).

The fundamental question: does the eruv work when there is a doubt about it, or when it is out of reach at twilight? The siman answers for the doubt (seif א), for the eruv that cannot be taken without a hoe and an axe (seif ב), and for the locked-up eruv whose key was lost (seif ג).

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. Siman שצ״ד follows siman שצ״ג · 393, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דיני עירוב ביום טוב שחל בערב שבת » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:א), and precedes siman שצ״ה · 395, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דיני ברכת עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ה:א). The Rema's gloss refers to siman 368. The Mishnah Berurah refers, among others, to simanim 393 and 261 (ס״ק ב), to siman 261 (ס״ק ג) and to siman 314 (ס״ק ז).

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman:

The essential notions of siman שצ״ד:
  • ספק עירוב — the doubtful eruv: if it is doubtful whether it existed at twilight, it is permitted, when it has a presumption of validity (seif א); « ר״ל שנאכל או נשרף העירוב ואינו יודע אם היה זה קודם שהתחיל הבין השמשות או שהיה קיים בזמן ביה״ש » (משנה ברורה שצ״ד ס״ק א).
  • חזקת כשרות — the presumption of validity: one placed the eruv there, and a doubt arose (seif א). If it is doubtful whether it was placed, the seif writes that it is not; on this case the commentators differ (Level 2).
  • בין השמשות — twilight: the eruv must then be where it is possible to take it (seif ב); according to the Rema's gloss, it need exist only at that time (seif ב).
  • מרא וחצינא — a hoe and an axe: if one cannot take the eruv without them, it is forbidden (seif ב); if one need only move a muktzeh object, « לא גזרו עליו ביה״ש כמ״ש סימן רס״א » (משנה ברורה שצ״ד ס״ק ג); for what is forbidden only as a shevus, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 6).
  • מלאכה גמורה — a complete melachah: if one must do one to take the eruv out of the migdal, it is not an eruv (seif ג); « כגון שצריך לסתור המגדול או הדלת שיש בו משום סתירת אהל » (משנה ברורה שצ״ד ס״ק י).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אA doubtful eruv — if it is doubtful whether it existed at twilight — is permitted, provided it has a presumption of validity, for example, he placed it there and a doubt arose; if it has none, for example, if it is doubtful whether it was placed, not (on this last case, the commentators differ: Level 2).
בAt twilight, the eruv must be where it is possible to take it: if a heap fell on it and he cannot take it without a hoe and an axe, it is forbidden. Gloss of the Rema: the eruv need exist only at twilight; one may eat it when it is certainly dark; one should break bread over it on Shabbos morning; and this only where it is customary to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos — but it is much preferable to make it for the whole year at one time (siman 368); on this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4).
גThe eruv put in a locked migdal, the key lost before dark: if it is impossible to take it out without doing a complete melachah at twilight, it is as if lost, and it is not an eruv, since it cannot be eaten.
The heart of the siman: what the eruv is at twilight. If it is doubtful whether it existed then, it works, when it had a presumption of validity; if it is doubtful whether it was placed, the seif writes that it does not, and the commentators differ (seif א; Level 2). It must be where it is possible to take it: not under a heap, when he cannot take it without a hoe and an axe (seif ב), not in a migdal whose key was lost, when it can be taken out only by a complete melachah (seif ג).

6. Position of the Rema

In this siman, a gloss of the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles, הגה) is introduced by הגה in seif ב. Here, seif by seif, are remarks of the commentators, and this gloss part by part.

The gloss of the Rema, and remarks:
  • Seif א, the doubt whether it was placed — the seif writes that it is not permitted; on this case the commentators differ: see Level 2.
  • Seif ב, when the eruv must exist, and when it is eaten — « והעירוב א״צ להיות קיים רק בין השמשות (טור) ויוכל לאכלו כשודאי חשיכה (הגהות אשירי ומרדכי פרק בכל מערבין) » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ד:ב). The Biur Halachah: « ואף דלעיל בסימן שצ״ג ס״ג פסק דאם נאכל בין השמשות ג״כ עירובו עירוב התם הוא לענין דיעבד אבל לכתחלה אין כדאי להכניס עצמו אפילו בספק דרבנן » (ביאור הלכה שצ״ד).
  • Seif ב, breaking bread over it in the morning — « ויש לבצוע עליו בשחרית בשבת (מנהגים) » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ד:ב). The Mishnah Berurah gives the reason (ס״ק ד, in section 5).
  • Seif ב, the weekly eruv and the yearly eruv — « ודוקא במקום שנוהגים לערב כל ערב שבת אבל עדיף טפי לערב על כל השנה בפעם אחת כדלעיל סי׳ שס״ח (ב״י) » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ד:ב). In siman 368, seif ה, a gloss of the Rema writes: « וזה טוב יותר מלערב כל שבת ושבת שלא ישכחו מלערב מיהו אם רוצים לערב כל ע״ש ולאכול העירוב כל שבת הרשות בידם » (רמ״א או״ח שס״ח:ה). And the Mishnah Berurah: « ובאופן זה כשנאכל עירובו בשבת אינו מותר רק לאותה שבת אבל לא לשבת הבאה » (משנה ברורה שצ״ד ס״ק ו). On this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4).
  • Chabad → Level 4 — Daat HaRav studies this siman in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שצ״ד, under its own numbering, in 3 seifim. The Rav writes there, in his seif א, the reason for the permission: « שֶׁסְּפֵק דִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים לְהָקֵל » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ד:א), and, in his seif ג, the case of the key found on Shabbos: see Level 4.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שצ״ד:
  1. If it is doubtful whether the eruv existed at twilight, it is permitted, provided it has a presumption of validity: he placed it there, and a doubt arose (seif א).
  2. If it is doubtful whether it was placed, the seif writes that it is not; on this case the commentators differ (seif א; Level 2).
  3. At twilight, the eruv must be where it is possible to take it; if a heap fell on it and he cannot take it without a hoe and an axe, it is forbidden (seif ב).
  4. Gloss of the Rema: the eruv need exist only at twilight; one may eat it when it is certainly dark; one should break bread over it on Shabbos morning; and this only where it is customary to make the eruv every Erev Shabbos — but it is much preferable to make it for the whole year at one time (seif ב); on this preference, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 4).
  5. In a locked migdal whose key was lost before dark: if it is impossible to take out the eruv without doing a complete melachah at twilight, it is not an eruv (seif ג).
  6. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Which doubt does seif א permit, and on what condition (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א)?
  2. Why, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ב), is the doubt whether it was placed worse than an eruv placed at twilight?
  3. What does the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ג) say when, to take the eruv, one need only move a muktzeh object, and do the commentators agree on this case (Level 2, section 6)?
  4. Why does one break bread over the eruv on Shabbos morning, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ד)?
  5. Why does the gloss of the Rema in seif ב judge it preferable to make the eruv for the whole year at one time (ס״ק ה), and do the commentators agree on this preference (Level 2, section 4)?
  6. According to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ח and י״א), when does the lost key not prevent the eruv, and on what do the commentators differ (Level 2, section 7)?
