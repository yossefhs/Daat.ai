1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שצ״ו contains 2 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo); the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) adds a gloss, introduced by הגה in seif א. Topic of the siman, according to its title: the law of the four amos that every person has on Shabbos. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe has 4 seifim for this siman: its numbering is that of Level 4, not that of this page.

Seif א

דין ארבע אמו' שיש לכל אדם בשבת. ובו ב' סעיפים:
שבו איש תחתיו מכאן שכל אדם יש לו ד' אמות בכל מקום אפי' יצא חוץ לתחום ומודדי' לכל אדם באמה שלו ואם היה ננס באיבריו נותנים לו ארבע אמות בינוני' של כל אדם שכל אחת מהן ששה טפחי': הגה ואלו ד' אמות מודדין לו מרווחת והוא באמצען (טור) ויש אומרים דמותר להלך ארבע אמות לכל צד אבל לטלטל אין לו רק ארבע אמות עם אלכסונן כמו שנתבאר לעיל סי' שמ״ט: (טור בשם הרז״ה פי״ב בשם רשב״א):
Translation: “Remain every man in his place (shevu ish tachtav, literally “beneath him”)” [Shemos 16:29]: from here, every person has four amos in every place, even if he went out beyond the techum. One measures for every person with his own amah; and if he was a dwarf in his limbs, one gives him four average amos of any person, each of which is six tefachim. Gloss of the Rema: and these four amos, one measures them for him generously, and he is in their middle (Tur); and some say that it is permitted to walk four amos to every side, but to carry, he has only four amos with their diagonal, as was explained above in siman 349 [the Rema's references, as printed: טור בשם הרז״ה פי״ב בשם רשב״א].
What this seif says:
  • Every person has four amos in every place, even outside the techum: שבו איש תחתיו מכאן שכל אדם יש לו ד׳ אמות בכל מקום אפי׳ יצא חוץ לתחום. “Beneath him” (תחתיו), explains the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק א), is like the place of his body: three amos, and one amah to stretch out his hands and feet; it refers to siman 349, which deals with carrying an object in the public domain, whereas here it is about one who went out beyond the techum, who is allowed to walk four amos. For Moshe said this to those who had gone out beyond the techum (ס״ק ב, citing the Tur).
  • One measures for each person with his own amah; for a dwarf in his limbs, average amos: ומודדי׳ לכל אדם באמה שלו ואם היה ננס באיבריו נותנים לו ארבע אמות בינוני׳ של כל אדם שכל אחת מהן ששה טפחי׳. A dwarf in his limbs is, according to the Mishnah Berurah, one whose body is average and whose arm is short (ס״ק ד, referring to siman 349).
  • Gloss of the Rema: these four amos are measured for him generously: ואלו ד׳ אמות מודדין לו מרווחת והוא באמצען. Generously, that is, not measured tightly (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו); and this applies whether one measures for him with his own amah or gives him average amos (ס״ק ה). On how to read the words והוא באמצען, and therefore on the measure according to each of the two opinions that the gloss brings, the commentators diverge (section 6; Level 2).
  • Gloss of the Rema: some say he may walk four amos to every side; to carry, four amos with their diagonal: ויש אומרים דמותר להלך ארבע אמות לכל צד אבל לטלטל אין לו רק ארבע אמות עם אלכסונן כמו שנתבאר לעיל סי׳ שמ״ט. For practice between the two opinions that the gloss brings, see Level 2, and consult your Rav.

Seif ב

בכל מקום שקדש היום עליו אם הוא מוקף לדירה אפי' אין בו עתה דיורין חשוב כולו כד' אמו' ואם אינו מוקף לדירה עד סאתים חשוב כולו כד' אמו' ואפילו שבת בתל גבוה ובקמה קצורה ושבלי' מקיפו' אותה חשיבי כד' אמות עד סאתים:
Translation: In any place where the day [of Shabbos] sanctified him (kadash hayom alav): if it is enclosed for dwelling (mukaf ledirah), even if there are no residents in it now, it is all considered as four amos; and if it is not enclosed for dwelling, up to [an area of] two se'ah (sasayim), it is all considered as four amos. And even if he established his Shabbos on a high mound (tel gavoha), or in a harvested field of grain surrounded by stalks, they are considered as four amos, up to two se'ah.
What this seif says:
  • A place enclosed for dwelling is all considered as four amos, even without residents: בכל מקום שקדש היום עליו אם הוא מוקף לדירה אפי׳ אין בו עתה דיורין חשוב כולו כד׳ אמו׳. For example, writes the Mishnah Berurah, if he established his Shabbos in a city or in another place enclosed for dwelling (ס״ק י״א); without residents: a city emptied of its inhabitants whose wall still stands (ס״ק י״ב); and even if that place is as large as Antioch, he counts two thousand amos outside that place (ס״ק י״ג).
  • If it is not enclosed for dwelling: up to two se'ah: ואם אינו מוקף לדירה עד סאתים חשוב כולו כד׳ אמו׳. If the enclosure is larger than two se'ah, he has, according to the Mishnah Berurah, only two thousand amos in every direction, from the four amos where he was standing when the day sanctified him (ס״ק י״ד).
  • And even a high mound, or a harvested field surrounded by stalks, up to two se'ah: ואפילו שבת בתל גבוה ובקמה קצורה ושבלי׳ מקיפו׳ אותה חשיבי כד׳ אמות עד סאתים. The Mishnah Berurah specifies: a mound ten tefachim high, and the same for the stalks (ס״ק ט״ו); stalks that one left unharvested all around, according to Rashi, and on condition that one tied them together so that an ordinary wind does not move them, for any partition that the wind moves is not a partition (ס״ק ט״ז); although [this enclosure of stalks] came about by itself, it is a partition, and the mound, although it has no enclosure on top, counts as a partition because it is ten tefachim high (ס״ק י״ז, referring to siman 345).

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Siman שצ״ו first sets out the four amos that every person has on Shabbos in every place, even outside the techum, and how they are measured (seif א, with the gloss of the Rema), then the place where the day sanctified him, which is all considered as four amos (seif ב). The following siman starts from there: « כל אדם יש לו אלפים אמה לכל רוח חוץ מארבע אמותיו או מהמקום ששבת בו » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ז:א).

Seif א starts from the words of the verse: « שְׁבוּ אִישׁ תַּחְתָּיו אַל יֵצֵא אִישׁ מִמְּקֹמוֹ בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי » (שמות ט״ז:כ״ט). The Tur, on this siman, writes: « תניא שבו איש תחתיו מכאן שכל אדם יש לו ד״א בכ״מ אפילו יצא חוץ לתחום שהרי משה אמר כן לאותן שיצאו חוץ לתחום ללקוט » (טור או״ח שצ״ו).

The fundamental question: how much space does a person have on Shabbos, where he is? The siman answers: four amos in every place, even outside the techum, measured with his own amah (seif א); and the place where the day sanctified him is all considered as four amos, if it is enclosed for dwelling, even without residents, or, if it is not, up to two se'ah (seif ב).

Place in Hilchos Techumin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות תחומין of the Shulchan Aruch runs from siman 396 to siman 407: siman שצ״ו opens it. It follows siman שצ״ה · 395, which closes הלכות עירובין, and whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דיני ברכת עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ה:א); it precedes siman שצ״ז · 397, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דין שביתת היחיד וכליו ומהלך אלפים אמה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ז:א). The gloss of the Rema refers to siman 349, on the four amos in the public domain; and siman 358 deals with the places called enclosed for dwelling.

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman:

The essential notions of siman שצ״ו:
  • ד׳ אמות — the four amos that every person has in every place, even outside the techum (seif א); “beneath him”, according to the Mishnah Berurah: « גופו שלש אמות ואמה כדי לפשוט ידיו ורגליו » (משנה ברורה שצ״ו ס״ק א).
  • אמה — the amah: one measures for each person with his own, and the average amah is six tefachim (seif א).
  • תחום — the Shabbos boundary: every person has two thousand amos in every direction, outside his four amos or the place where he established his Shabbos (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ז:א); one who went out beyond the techum still has his four amos (seif א).
  • קדש היום עליו — the day sanctified him: the place where he was when Shabbos began (seif ב); the Mishnah Berurah writes « בעת שקידש היום » (משנה ברורה שצ״ו ס״ק י״ד).
  • מוקף לדירה — enclosed for dwelling: it is all considered as four amos, even without residents (seif ב); which places are so is the subject of siman 358, « דין איזה מקומות נקראים מוקפים לדירה » (שו״ע או״ח שנ״ח:א).
  • סאתים — two se'ah: the limit for a place that is not enclosed for dwelling (seif ב); siman 358 gives its measure, « שהוא שיעור ע׳ אמה וד׳ טפחים על ע׳ אמה וד׳ טפחים » (שו״ע או״ח שנ״ח:א).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אEvery person has four amos in every place, even if he went out beyond the techum. They are measured with his own amah; to a dwarf in his limbs one gives four average amos, of six tefachim each. Rema: they are measured for him generously, and he is in their middle (Tur); some say he may walk four amos to every side, but to carry he has only four amos with their diagonal (siman 349); on how to read this gloss, the commentators diverge (section 6).
בThe place where the day sanctified him: enclosed for dwelling, even without residents, it is all considered as four amos; if it is not enclosed for dwelling, up to two se'ah; and even a high mound, or a harvested field surrounded by stalks, are considered as four amos up to two se'ah (for the height and the stalks, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט״ו-ט״ז).
The heart of the siman: wherever he is, even outside the techum, every person has four amos, measured with his own amah (seif א); and the place where the day sanctified him is all considered as four amos, if it is enclosed for dwelling, even without residents, or, if it is not, up to two se'ah (seif ב). How the four amos are measured according to the two opinions that the Rema brings, the commentators read differently (section 6).

5. The Mishnah Berurah — first entries

The Mishnah Berurah of Rabbi Yisrael Meir Kagan (the Chafetz Chaim) has 17 entries on this siman. Here are the first four, on the words of the Mechaber in seif א — to better understand the meaning of the text:

משנה ברורה (א) — (א) שבו איש תחתיו מכאן וכו' - פי' דכתיב תחתיו כתחתיו גופו שלש אמות ואמה כדי לפשוט ידיו ורגליו וכמבואר בסימן שמ״ט עי״ש במ״ב אך דשם מיירי לענין טלטול איזה חפץ בר״ה והכא מיירי לענין יצא חוץ לתחום דיש לו רשות להלך ד' אמות:
משנה ברורה (ב) — (ב) אפילו יצא וכו' - שהרי משה אמר זה לאותן שיצאו חוץ לתחום שבו איש תחתיו [טור]:
משנה ברורה (ג) — (ג) באמה שלו - עיין לעיל שמ״ט במ״ב סק״ג:
משנה ברורה (ד) — (ד) ננס באבריו - ר״ל שגופו בינוני וזרועו קצר וכמו שביארתי לעיל בשמ״ט סק״ד ובשה״צ שם:

6. Position of the Rema

In this siman, the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles, הגה) has a gloss, introduced by הגה in seif א, on the measurement of the four amos. The detail of what the commentators say on it is at Level 2.

The gloss of the Rema:
  • Seif א, the text of the gloss — « ואלו ד׳ אמות מודדין לו מרווחת והוא באמצען (טור) ויש אומרים דמותר להלך ארבע אמות לכל צד אבל לטלטל אין לו רק ארבע אמות עם אלכסונן כמו שנתבאר לעיל סי׳ שמ״ט » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ו:א).
  • Seif א, generously — the Mishnah Berurah: « שיהיו מודדין אותו בריוח ולא בצמצום » (משנה ברורה שצ״ו ס״ק ו).
  • Seif א, the two opinions — the commentators diverge on how to read the words והוא באמצען, and therefore on the measure that the gloss gives to each of the two opinions it brings; see Level 2. For practice, consult your Rav.
  • Chabad → Level 4 — Daat HaRav studies this siman in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שצ״ו, under its own numbering, in 4 seifim.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שצ״ו:
  1. Four amos: every person has four amos in every place, even if he went out beyond the techum (seif א).
  2. The measurement: with his own amah; for a dwarf in his limbs, four average amos of six tefachim (seif א).
  3. The gloss of the Rema brings two opinions on how these four amos are measured for him, and adds that to carry he has only four amos with their diagonal (siman 349); the commentators read the gloss differently (seif א; section 6).
  4. The place where the day sanctified him: enclosed for dwelling, even without residents, it is all considered as four amos; if it is not, up to two se'ah (seif ב).
  5. A high mound, a harvested field surrounded by stalks are also considered as four amos up to two se'ah (seif ב; for the height and the stalks, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט״ו-ט״ז).
  6. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. From where does seif א derive that every person has four amos, and what does “beneath him” mean according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק א)?
  2. Why do these four amos apply even to one who went out beyond the techum (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב)?
  3. With which amah does one measure, and what does one do for a dwarf in his limbs (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד)?
  4. Which two opinions does the gloss of the Rema bring, and what limit does it set for carrying (seif א)?
  5. What difference does seif ב make between a place enclosed for dwelling and a place that is not?
  6. Why does a high mound or stalks count as an enclosure (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט״ו-י״ז)?
