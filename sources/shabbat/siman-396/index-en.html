Orach Chaim · Hilchos Techumin

דין ארבע אמו׳ שיש לכל אדם בשבת

The Four Amos of Every Person, and the Place Counted as Four Amos

2 seifim in the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber, with a gloss of the Rema introduced by הגה on seif א; 4 in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, under its own numbering (Level 4). What space does each person have on Shabbos, where he is? The siman answers: four amos in every place, even if he went out beyond the techum, measured with his own amah; the gloss of the Rema brings two opinions on how they are measured for him, and the commentators read it differently (seif א; Level 2); the place where the day sanctified him is all considered as four amos, if it is enclosed for dwelling, even without residents, or, if it is not, up to two se'ah, and likewise a high mound or a harvested field surrounded by stalks, up to two se'ah, with the specifics of the Mishnah Berurah and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav on the height of ten tefachim and the tied stalks (seif ב).
