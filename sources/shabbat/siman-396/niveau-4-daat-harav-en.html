דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on Siman 396

The four amos that every person has on Shabbos: the root of the techum and the two thousand amos; the shape of the four amos; their measure, tight or generous; the place enclosed by partitions, the mound, the harvested field and the city counted as four amos. The 4 seifim of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, compared with the 2 seifim of the Mechaber.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav is not a commentary on the Mechaber: it is a Shulchan Aruch in its own right, written by the Alter Rebbe, with its own division and numbering of the seifim. Here the Shulchan Aruch HaRav announces 4 seifim at the head of the siman, and the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber has 2. Sefaria serves the Rav's text in 4 blocks, which this page reproduces and numbers as they stand (section 1). The two divisions do not coincide: the Rav's seif 1 takes up the beginning of the Mechaber's seif א, the four amos of every person, and the rule of the enclosed place, which the Mechaber writes in seif ב; the Rav's seifim 2 and 3 correspond to the rest of seif א and to the Rema's gloss introduced there by הגה; the Rav's seif 4 corresponds to the end of seif ב (the mound and the harvested field), and adds the city. In the English text of this page, the Rav's seifim are designated by their number (396:3, seif 3), the Mechaber's by their Hebrew letter (the Mechaber's seif ב); in the Hebrew references in parentheses, both numberings are in letters, and the reference always names the work (שו״ע או״ח for the Mechaber, רמ״א או״ח for the Rema's gloss, שו״ע הרב for the Rav).

This page gathers, for Siman 396, the full text of the Rav with its translation, where his text differs from that of the Mechaber and the Rema, and the practical conduct that follows from it.

1 · Text of the Alter Rebbe

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן שצ״ו

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav of the Alter Rebbe

סימן שצ״ו — דִּין אַרְבַּע אַמּוֹת שֶׁיֵּשׁ לְכָל אָדָם בְּשַׁבָּת — וּבוֹ ד׳ סְעִיפִים
Source: Kehot edition, as reproduced on Sefaria. The heading announces 4 seifim; Sefaria serves the text in 4 blocks (the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has 2 seifim, under its own numbering).

סעיף א דִּין אַרְבַּע אַמּוֹת שֶׁיֵּשׁ לְכָל אָדָם בְּשַׁבָּת וּבוֹ ד' סְעִיפִים: "שְׁבוּ אִישׁ תַּחְתָּיו אַל יֵצֵא אִישׁ מִמְּקֹמוֹ בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי"…

דִּין אַרְבַּע אַמּוֹת שֶׁיֵּשׁ לְכָל אָדָם בְּשַׁבָּת וּבוֹ ד' סְעִיפִים:

"שְׁבוּ אִישׁ תַּחְתָּיו אַל יֵצֵא אִישׁ מִמְּקֹמוֹ בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי". יֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁזּוֹ אַזְהָרָה לְיוֹצֵא חוּץ לַתְּחוּם, וְשִׁעוּר תְּחוּם שֶׁל תּוֹרָה הוּא י״ב מִיל, כְּשִׁעוּר מַחֲנֵה יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁהָיְתָה י״ב מִיל עַל י״ב מִיל. וַחֲכָמִים אָסְרוּ לָצֵאת יוֹתֵר מֵאַלְפַּיִם אַמּוֹת, שֶׁהֵן מִיל א'.

וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁאֵין לִתְחוּמִין עִקָּר מִן הַתּוֹרָה כְּלָל, אֶלָּא אֲפִלּוּ יוֹתֵר מִי״ב מִיל אֵינוֹ אָסוּר מִן הַתּוֹרָה כְּלָל. וְזֶה שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "אַל יֵצֵא אִישׁ מִמְּקֹמוֹ" — זוֹ הִיא אַזְהָרָה עַל הוֹצָאָה, שֶׁהִזְהִיר מֹשֶׁה שֶׁלֹּא לָצֵאת עִם הַכְּלִי מֵרְשׁוּת הַיָּחִיד לִרְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים לִלְקוֹט הַמָּן. וּ"שְׁבוּ אִישׁ תַּחְתָּיו" — קִבְּלוּ חֲכָמִים שֶׁזּוֹ אַזְהָרָה שֶׁלֹּא לְטַלְטֵל בִּרְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים אֶלָּא כְּשִׁעוּר תַּחְתָּיו שֶׁל אָדָם, שֶׁהוּא ד' אַמּוֹת, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שמ״ט.

אֶלָּא שֶׁחֲכָמִים סָמְכוּ אִסּוּר הַתְּחוּמִין שֶׁל דִּבְרֵיהֶם לְמִקְרָא זֶה "אַל יֵצֵא אִישׁ מִמְּקֹמוֹ", שֶׁלֹּא יֵצֵא יוֹתֵר מֵאַלְפַּיִם מֵהַמָּקוֹם שֶׁשָּׁבַת בּוֹ, כִּי עַד אַלְפַּיִם נִקְרָא מְקוֹמוֹ, כְּמוֹ שֶׁמָּצִינוּ בְּעָרֵי מִקְלָט, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "וְשַׂמְתִּי לְךָ מָקוֹם וְגוֹ'", וְהֵן קוֹלְטוֹת אוֹתוֹ גַּם כְּשֶׁהוּא חוּצָה לָהֶן, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "וּמָצָא אוֹתוֹ מִחוּץ לִגְבוּל עִיר מִקְלָטוֹ", וְנֶאֱמַר לְהַלָּן "וּמַדֹּתֶם מִחוּץ לָעִיר אֶת פְּאַת קֵדְמָה אַלְפַּיִם בָּאַמָּה וְגוֹ'". מַה "חוּץ" הָאָמוּר לְהַלָּן — אַלְפַּיִם, אַף "חוּץ" הָאָמוּר כַּאן — אַלְפַּיִם.

וּ"שְׁבוּ אִישׁ תַּחְתָּיו" — כְּשִׁעוּר תַּחְתָּיו. מִכַּאן סָמְכוּ חֲכָמִים שֶׁכָּל אָדָם יֵשׁ לוֹ ד' אַמּוֹת בְּכָל מָקוֹם שֶׁהוּא, אֲפִלּוּ יָצָא חוּץ לַתְּחוּם — יָכוֹל לְהַלֵּךְ שָׁם ד' אַמּוֹת, וְכֵן יָכוֹל לְטַלְטֵל בָּהֶן. וְכֵן כְּשֶׁמּוֹדְדִים אַלְפַּיִם מִמְּקוֹם שְׁבִיתָתוֹ — מוֹדְדִים חוּץ מִד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ, שֶׁהֵן כְּשִׁעוּר תַּחְתָּיו (וְיָכוֹל לְטַלְטֵל בָּהֶן. לְפִיכָךְ) אִם שָׁבַת, דְּהַיְנוּ שֶׁקִּדֵּשׁ עָלָיו הַיּוֹם, בְּמָקוֹם הַמֻּקָּף מְחִצּוֹת בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁמֻּתָּר לְטַלְטֵל בְּכֻלּוֹ — הֲרֵי כֻּלּוֹ נֶחְשָׁב לוֹ כְּד' אַמּוֹת, וְנוֹתְנִים לוֹ אַלְפַּיִם סָבִיב לְהֶקֵּף זֶה, עַל דֶּרֶךְ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שצ״ט, אֲפִלּוּ יֵשׁ בּוֹ כַּמָּה מִילִין.

וְהוּא שֶׁיְּהֵא מֻקָּף לְדִירָתוֹ מִתְּחִלָּתוֹ, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵין בּוֹ עַתָּה דִּיּוּרִין וְאֵין מִשְׁתַּמְּשִׁין בּוֹ כְּלָל, כְּמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן ת״ח, אוֹ אֲפִלּוּ אֵינוֹ מֻקָּף לְדִירָה אֶלָּא שֶׁאֵין בּוֹ יוֹתֵר מִבֵּית סָאתַיִם. אֲבָל אִם יֵשׁ בּוֹ יוֹתֵר מִבֵּית סָאתַיִם — כְּשֵׁם שֶׁאֵין מְחִצּוֹתֶיהָ מוֹעִילוֹת שֶׁיְּהֵא מֻתָּר לְטַלְטֵל בְּכֻלּוֹ, כָּךְ אֵינָן מוֹעִילוֹת שֶׁיְּהֵא כֻלּוֹ נֶחְשָׁב כְּד' אַמּוֹת לְעִנְיַן תְּחוּמִין:

Law of the four amos that every person has on Shabbos; it contains 4 seifim:

“Let each man remain in his place; let no man go out of his place on the seventh day.” Some say that this is a warning (azharah) against one who goes out beyond the techum, and that the measure of the techum of the Torah is 12 mil, like the measure of the camp of Israel, which was 12 mil by 12 mil; and the Sages forbade going out more than two thousand amos, which are one mil.

And others say that the techumin have no root at all in the Torah; even beyond 12 mil it is not forbidden by the Torah at all. And what is said, “let no man go out of his place”, is a warning about carrying out (hotza'ah): Moshe warned not to go out with the vessel from the private domain to the public domain to gather the manna. And “let each man remain in his place (tachtav)”: the Sages received that this is a warning not to carry (letaltel) in the public domain except within the measure of a person's “place”, which is four amos, as was explained in Siman 349.

Only, the Sages based the prohibition of techumin, which is of their words, on this verse, “let no man go out of his place”: that he not go out more than two thousand [amos] from the place where he established his Shabbos residence (shavas), for up to two thousand it is called “his place”, as we find with the cities of refuge, as it is said: “I will appoint you a place, etc.”; and they protect him also when he is outside them, as it is said: “and finds him outside the border of his city of refuge”; and it is said further on: “You shall measure, outside the city, on the east side, two thousand amos, etc.” Just as the “outside” said further on is two thousand, so too the “outside” said here is two thousand.

And “let each man remain in his place”: within the measure of his “place”. From here the Sages based [the rule] that every person has four amos wherever he is: even if he went out beyond the techum, he may walk four amos there, and likewise he may carry in them. And likewise, when one measures two thousand [amos] from the place of his Shabbos residence (shevisaso), one measures them apart from these four amos, which are the measure of his “place” (and he may carry in them. Therefore), if he established his Shabbos residence, that is, if the day became holy upon him, in a place surrounded by partitions (mechitzos) in such a way that it is permitted to carry in all of it, all of it is counted for him as four amos, and one gives him two thousand [amos] around this enclosure, in the manner that will be explained in Siman 399, even if it contains several mil.

And this is provided that it was enclosed for his dwelling from the outset (mukaf ledirato), even though it now has no residents and is not used at all, as will be explained in Siman 408; or even if it is not enclosed for dwelling, but it has no more than a beis sa'asayim. But if it has more than a beis sa'asayim — just as its partitions do not avail to permit carrying in all of it, so they do not avail for all of it to be counted as four amos with regard to techumin:

סעיף ב ד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן תְּחוּמִין…

ד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן תְּחוּמִין — בֵּין ד' אַמּוֹת שֶׁבִּמְקוֹם שְׁבִיתָתוֹ, בֵּין ד' אַמּוֹת שֶׁיֵּשׁ לוֹ כְּשֶׁיָּצָא חוּץ לַתְּחוּם — יֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁהֵן כְּד' אַמּוֹת שֶׁבִּרְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל, שֶׁהוּא בּוֹרֵר לוֹ ד' אַמּוֹת לְאֵיזוֹ רוּחַ שֶׁיִּרְצֶה, וּכְשֶׁהָלַךְ לְרוּחַ אַחַת ד' אַמּוֹת — שׁוּב אֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לַחֲזוֹר וְלֵילֵךְ לְרוּחַ הַשֵּׁנִית (שֶׁכְּנֶגְדָּהּ) אֲפִלּוּ כָּל שֶׁהוּא אִם הוּא חוּץ לַתְּחוּם, כְּמוֹ שֶׁאֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לְטַלְטֵל חֵפֶץ אֶחָד יוֹתֵר מִד' אַמּוֹת, שֶׁאֵין נוֹתְנִים אֲלַכְסוֹן לְעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל אֶלָּא מִפְּנֵי שֶׁאֵין שָׁם רִבּוּעַ, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שמ״ט. וְכֵן בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁשָּׁבַת בּוֹ, אִם הָלַךְ לְרוּחַ אַחַת ד' אַמּוֹת וְאַלְפַּיִם — שׁוּב אֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לֵילֵךְ לְרוּחַ הַשֵּׁנִית (שֶׁכְּנֶגְדָּהּ) אֶלָּא אַלְפַּיִם. וְאִם לֹא נָטַל לוֹ כָּל ד' אַמּוֹת לְרוּחַ אַחַת, יָכוֹל לְהַשְׁלִימָן בְּרוּחַ הַב' (שֶׁכְּנֶגְדָּהּ).

וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן תְּחוּמִין אֵינוֹ כְּד' אַמּוֹת שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל, אֶלָּא הֵן ד' אַמּוֹת לְכָל רוּחַ, שֶׁהֵן ח' עַל ח' מְרֻבָּעוֹת, בֵּין בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁשָּׁבַת בּוֹ בֵּין כְּשֶׁיָּצָא חוּץ לַתְּחוּם (וּלְפִי סְבָרָא זוֹ כָּל שֶׁכֵּן שֶׁאֵין מוֹדְדִים לוֹ ד' אַמּוֹת עִם אֲלַכְסוֹנָם לְכָל רוּחַ, שֶׁכֵּיוָן שֶׁנּוֹתְנִים לוֹ ח' עַל ח' מְרֻבָּעוֹת — הֲרֵי יֵשׁ בָּהֶן אֲלַכְסוֹן מַמָּשׁ שֶׁל ד' אַמּוֹת לְכָל רוּחַ, דְּהַיְנוּ מֵאֶמְצָעִיתוֹ לְאַרְבַּע הַזָּוִיּוֹת. מַה שֶּׁאֵין כֵּן בְּד' אַמּוֹת שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל, שֶׁמּוֹדְדִים לוֹ ד' אַמּוֹת בְּיֹשֶׁר אֶל עֵבֶר פָּנָיו לְאֵיזוֹ רוּחַ שֶׁיִּרְצֶה, וְכֵיוָן שֶׁלֹּא שַׁיָּךְ בָּהֶן אֲלַכְסוֹן מַמָּשׁ — צָרִיךְ לִתֵּן לוֹ הֵן וַאֲלַכְסוֹנָם, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שמ״ט). וְיֵשׁ לִסְמוֹךְ עַל דִּבְרֵיהֶם לְהָקֵל בְּדִבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים:

These four amos that concern techumin — both the four amos at the place of his Shabbos residence and the four amos he has when he went out beyond the techum — some say that they are like the four amos of the public domain that concern carrying (tiltul): he chooses for himself four amos in whichever direction he wishes; and once he has walked four amos in one direction, he can no longer return and walk in the second direction (the one opposite it), even the slightest amount, if he is beyond the techum, just as he cannot carry one object more than four amos; for the diagonal (alachson) is given for carrying only because there is no square there, as was explained in Siman 349. And likewise, at the place where he established his Shabbos residence, if he walked four amos and two thousand in one direction, he can no longer walk in the second direction (the one opposite it) more than two thousand. And if he did not take all four amos in one direction, he may complete them in the second direction (the one opposite it).

And others say that these four amos that concern techumin are not like the four amos that concern carrying, but are four amos in every direction, which make eight by eight, squared, both at the place where he established his Shabbos residence and when he went out beyond the techum (and according to this view, all the more so one does not measure for him four amos with their diagonal in every direction: since one gives him eight by eight squared, there is in them a true diagonal of four amos in every direction, that is, from their middle to the four corners; which is not the case with the four amos that concern carrying, where one measures for him four amos in a straight line before him, in whichever direction he wishes; and since there is no true diagonal in them, one must give him [these amos] and their diagonal, as was explained in Siman 349). And there is ground to rely on their words to be lenient in [a rule] of rabbinic order (divrei soferim):

סעיף ג ד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁלְּכָל רוּחַ וְרוּחַ…

ד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁלְּכָל רוּחַ וְרוּחַ, שֶׁהֵן שִׁעוּר תַּחְתָּיו שֶׁל אָדָם — מוֹדְדִים אוֹתוֹ לְכָל אָדָם בְּאַמָּה שֶׁלּוֹ. וְאִם הוּא נַנָּס בְּאֵבָרִים, שֶׁגּוּפוֹ גָדוֹל וְאַמָּתוֹ קְטַנָּה — נוֹתְנִים לוֹ ד' אַמּוֹת בֵּינוֹנִית שֶׁל כָּל אָדָם, שֶׁכָּל אַחַת מֵהֶן ו' טְפָחִים, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שמ״ט.

וּכְבָר נִתְבָּאֵר שָׁם שֶׁד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ מוֹדְדִים אוֹתָן מְצֻמְצָמוֹת. וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁמּוֹדְדִים אוֹתָן מְרֻוָּחוֹת, וְיֵשׁ לִסְמוֹךְ עַל דִּבְרֵיהֶם בְּד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן תְּחוּמִין, שֶׁעִקָּרָן אֵינָן אֶלָּא מִדִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים:

These four amos in every direction, which are the measure of a person's “place”, are measured for each person by his own amah. And if he is a dwarf in his limbs (nannas be'evarim), whose body is large and whose amah is small, one gives him four average amos, those of every person, each of which is six tefachim, as was explained in Siman 349.

And it was already explained there that these four amos are measured tight (metzumtzamos). And some say that they are measured generously (merubachos); and there is ground to rely on their words for these four amos that concern techumin, which in their root are only of rabbinic order:

סעיף ד שָׁבַת בְּתֵל גָּבוֹהַּ י' טְפָחִים…

שָׁבַת בְּתֵל גָּבוֹהַּ י' טְפָחִים, אִם אֵין בּוֹ אֶלָּא בֵּית סָאתַיִם — הֲרֵי כֻּלּוֹ נֶחְשָׁב לוֹ כְּד' אַמּוֹת, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁהוּא כְּמֻקָּף מְחִצּוֹת מִלְמַעְלָה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שמ״ה, אֶלָּא שֶׁאֵינָן מֻקָּפוֹת לְדִירָה.

וְכֵן אִם שָׁבַת בְּקָמָה קְצוּרָה וְשִׁבֳּלִים גְּבוֹהִים י' טְפָחִים מַקִּיפִים אוֹתָהּ. וְהוּא שֶׁקָּשַׁר הַשִּׁבֳּלִים יַחַד בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁאֵין רוּחַ הַמְּצוּיָה מְנִידָה אוֹתָן, שֶׁכָּל מְחִצָּה שֶׁהָרוּחַ מְנִידָהּ — אֵינָהּ מְחִצָּה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שס״ב.

אֲבָל הַשּׁוֹבֵת בְּעִיר — כָּל הָעִיר נֶחְשֶׁבֶת כְּד' אַמּוֹת, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵינָהּ מֻקֶּפֶת מְחִצּוֹת כְּהִלְכָתָן, כְּגוֹן שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהּ פְּרָצוֹת הַרְבֵּה בְּמִלּוּאָן וּבְיוֹתֵר מֵעֶשֶׂר, דְּהוֹאִיל וּבָתֵּי הָעִיר מֻבְלָעִים בְּתוֹךְ שִׁבְעִים אַמָּה וְשִׁירַיִם שֶׁל זֶה — הֲרֵי כֻּלָּם כְּדִירָה אַחַת, כְּמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שצ״ח:

If he established his Shabbos residence on a mound (tel) ten tefachim high, if it has only a beis sa'asayim, all of it is counted for him as four amos, because it is as if surrounded by partitions from above, as was explained in Siman 345; only, they are not enclosed for dwelling.

And likewise if he established his Shabbos residence in a harvested field of grain (kamah ketzurah) surrounded by stalks ten tefachim high. And this is provided that he tied the stalks together in such a way that an ordinary wind does not move them, for any partition that the wind moves is not a partition, as was explained in Siman 362.

But one who establishes his Shabbos residence in a city — the whole city is counted as four amos, even though it is not surrounded by partitions in accordance with the law (kehilchasan), for example if it has many breaches of their full height (bimlo'an) and of more than ten [amos]: since the houses of the city are included within seventy amos and a remainder (shirayim) of one another, they are all as one dwelling, as will be explained in Siman 398:

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim 396. Sefaria gives no Kuntres Acharon entry for this siman.

2 · The Rav's text and the Mechaber's

לשון הרב מול סעיף המחבר והרמ״א

Where the Rav's text differs from the Mechaber's seif and the Rema's

Each point sets side by side a passage of the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch (its numbering, 2 seifim, designated by their letter), or of the Rema's gloss introduced by הגה in seif א, and the corresponding seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (its own numbering, 4 seifim), in the order of the Rav's seifim, and notes only what the comparison of the two texts shows. A point noted is not thereby peculiar to the Rav: the page sometimes names its source; it notes neither all the sources nor all the divergences.

נקודה א — שורש התחום ואלפים אמה (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:א)

The root of the techum

What the Mechaber writes: at the beginning of seif א, « שבו איש תחתיו מכאן שכל אדם יש לו ד' אמות בכל מקום אפי' יצא חוץ לתחום » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ו:א). In Siman 396, he does not write what the root of the techum is.

What the Rav writes: « יֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁזּוֹ אַזְהָרָה לְיוֹצֵא חוּץ לַתְּחוּם, וְשִׁעוּר תְּחוּם שֶׁל תּוֹרָה הוּא י״ב מִיל, כְּשִׁעוּר מַחֲנֵה יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁהָיְתָה י״ב מִיל עַל י״ב מִיל. וַחֲכָמִים אָסְרוּ לָצֵאת יוֹתֵר מֵאַלְפַּיִם אַמּוֹת, שֶׁהֵן מִיל א » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:א); then « וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁאֵין לִתְחוּמִין עִקָּר מִן הַתּוֹרָה כְּלָל, אֶלָּא אֲפִלּוּ יוֹתֵר מִי״ב מִיל אֵינוֹ אָסוּר מִן הַתּוֹרָה כְּלָל » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:א).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif א derives from the verse the four amos of every person, even beyond the techum. The Rav's seif 1 first reports two opinions on the root of the techum: according to some, the verse warns one who goes out beyond the techum, whose Torah measure is 12 mil, and the Sages forbade going beyond two thousand amos; according to others, the techumin have no root in the Torah, and the verse is a warning about carrying out. He then writes that the Sages based the prohibition of techumin, which is of their words, on this verse, and derives the two thousand amos from the cities of refuge; then, that in these four amos one may walk and carry, and that the two thousand amos are measured apart from these four amos. The Rav's seif 1 does not write which of the two opinions is the main one.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, every person has four amos wherever he is, even if he went out beyond the techum: he may walk in them and carry in them; and when the two thousand amos are measured from the place of his Shabbos residence, they are measured apart from these four amos (396:1). Between the two opinions on the root of the techum, this page does not decide: consult your Rav. See section 4, point ①.

נקודה ב — ארבע אמות לרוח אחת או לכל רוח (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ב)

The shape of the four amos

What the Rema writes: in his gloss introduced by הגה in seif א, « ואלו ד' אמות מודדין לו מרווחת והוא באמצען (טור) ויש אומרים דמותר להלך ארבע אמות לכל צד אבל לטלטל אין לו רק ארבע אמות עם אלכסונן כמו שנתבאר לעיל סי' שמ״ט » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ו:א). He reports the opinion introduced by ויש אומרים without writing whether one may rely on it.

What the Rav writes: his seif 2 reports two opinions; the second: « וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן תְּחוּמִין אֵינוֹ כְּד' אַמּוֹת שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל, אֶלָּא הֵן ד' אַמּוֹת לְכָל רוּחַ, שֶׁהֵן ח' עַל ח' מְרֻבָּעוֹת, בֵּין בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁשָּׁבַת בּוֹ בֵּין כְּשֶׁיָּצָא חוּץ לַתְּחוּם » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ב). And he concludes: « וְיֵשׁ לִסְמוֹךְ עַל דִּבְרֵיהֶם לְהָקֵל בְּדִבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ב).

The difference: the Rav's seif 2 also sets out the first opinion: these four amos are like those of carrying in the public domain; he chooses four amos in whichever direction he wishes, and once he has walked four amos in one direction, he can no longer walk in the opposite direction, if he is beyond the techum; at the place of his Shabbos residence, if he walked four amos and two thousand in one direction, he can walk in the opposite direction only two thousand; and if he did not take all four amos in one direction, he may complete them in the opposite direction. On the second opinion — these four amos of the techumin are not like those of carrying objects: four amos in every direction, eight by eight — the Rav writes that there is ground to rely on their words to be lenient in [a rule] of rabbinic order; the Rema's gloss does not write this.

Among the commentators: on the words « והוא באמצען » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ו:א) in the Rema's gloss, which precede the opinion introduced by ויש אומרים, the commentators differ: see Level 2.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, there is ground to rely on the opinion according to which these four amos of the techumin are not like those of carrying objects, and which gives four amos in every direction, eight by eight, both at the place of one's Shabbos residence and beyond the techum, to be lenient in [a rule] of rabbinic order (396:2). See section 4, point ②.

נקודה ג — מרווחות או מצומצמות (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ג)

The measure of the four amos

What the Mechaber and the Rema write: seif א, « ומודדי' לכל אדם באמה שלו ואם היה ננס באיבריו נותנים לו ארבע אמות בינוני' של כל אדם שכל אחת מהן ששה טפחי » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ו:א); the Rema's gloss, introduced by הגה, « ואלו ד' אמות מודדין לו מרווחת והוא באמצען » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ו:א).

What the Rav writes: after each person's own amah and the dwarf in his limbs, « וּכְבָר נִתְבָּאֵר שָׁם שֶׁד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ מוֹדְדִים אוֹתָן מְצֻמְצָמוֹת. וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁמּוֹדְדִים אוֹתָן מְרֻוָּחוֹת, וְיֵשׁ לִסְמוֹךְ עַל דִּבְרֵיהֶם בְּד' אַמּוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁלְּעִנְיַן תְּחוּמִין, שֶׁעִקָּרָן אֵינָן אֶלָּא מִדִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ג).

The difference: the Mechaber and the Rav both measure the four amos for each person by his own amah, and give the dwarf in his limbs average amos of six tefachim; the Rav specifies that the dwarf in his limbs is one whose body is large and whose amah is small. The Rema's gloss writes that they are measured generously, without reporting another opinion on this measure. The Rav writes that he explained in Siman 349 that they are measured tight, reports the generous measure as the opinion of some, and writes that there is ground to rely on that opinion for these four amos of techumin, which in their root are only of rabbinic order.

Among the commentators: on the words that follow « מודדין לו מרווחת » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ו:א) in the Rema's gloss, see point ב.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, these four amos are measured for each person by his own amah; the dwarf in his limbs is given four average amos of six tefachim; and for these four amos of techumin, which in their root are only of rabbinic order, there is ground to rely on the opinion that measures them generously (396:3). See section 4, point ③.

נקודה ד — תל וקמה קצורה (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ד)

The mound and the stalks

What the Mechaber writes: at the end of seif ב, « ואפילו שבת בתל גבוה ובקמה קצורה ושבלי' מקיפו' אותה חשיבי כד' אמות עד סאתים » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ו:ב).

What the Rav writes: « שָׁבַת בְּתֵל גָּבוֹהַּ י' טְפָחִים, אִם אֵין בּוֹ אֶלָּא בֵּית סָאתַיִם — הֲרֵי כֻּלּוֹ נֶחְשָׁב לוֹ כְּד' אַמּוֹת, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁהוּא כְּמֻקָּף מְחִצּוֹת מִלְמַעְלָה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שמ״ה, אֶלָּא שֶׁאֵינָן מֻקָּפוֹת לְדִירָה » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ד); then « וְכֵן אִם שָׁבַת בְּקָמָה קְצוּרָה וְשִׁבֳּלִים גְּבוֹהִים י' טְפָחִים מַקִּיפִים אוֹתָהּ. וְהוּא שֶׁקָּשַׁר הַשִּׁבֳּלִים יַחַד בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁאֵין רוּחַ הַמְּצוּיָה מְנִידָה אוֹתָן, שֶׁכָּל מְחִצָּה שֶׁהָרוּחַ מְנִידָהּ — אֵינָהּ מְחִצָּה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שס״ב » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ד).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif ב writes « בתל גבוה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ו:ב) without a measure, and the harvested field surrounded by stalks without a condition. The Rav's text gives the mound and the stalks a height of ten tefachim; it gives the reason for the mound — it is as if surrounded by partitions from above, only they are not enclosed for dwelling —; and it sets a condition for the stalks: that he tied them together in such a way that an ordinary wind does not move them, for any partition that the wind moves is not a partition.

Among the commentators: the Mishnah Berurah, later than the Rav, writes on the mound « עשרה טפחים וה״ה בשבלים » (משנה ברורה שצ״ו ס״ק ט״ו), and on the stalks « והוא שקשר השבלים יחד בענין שאין רוח מצויה מנידה אותן שכל מחיצה שרוח מניד אותה אינה מחיצה » (משנה ברורה שצ״ו ס״ק ט״ז).

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, for one who established his Shabbos residence on a mound ten tefachim high, if it has only a beis sa'asayim, all of it is counted for him as four amos; and likewise in a harvested field surrounded by stalks ten tefachim high, provided that he tied the stalks together in such a way that an ordinary wind does not move them (396:4). See section 4, point ④.

נקודה ה — השובת בעיר (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ד)

The city

What the Mechaber writes: at the beginning of seif ב, « בכל מקום שקדש היום עליו אם הוא מוקף לדירה אפי' אין בו עתה דיורין חשוב כולו כד' אמו » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ו:ב).

What the Rav writes: his seif 1 writes the rule of the place enclosed for dwelling from the outset, or that has no more than a beis sa'asayim (section 1); and, at the end of seif 4, « אֲבָל הַשּׁוֹבֵת בְּעִיר — כָּל הָעִיר נֶחְשֶׁבֶת כְּד' אַמּוֹת, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵינָהּ מֻקֶּפֶת מְחִצּוֹת כְּהִלְכָתָן, כְּגוֹן שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהּ פְּרָצוֹת הַרְבֵּה בְּמִלּוּאָן וּבְיוֹתֵר מֵעֶשֶׂר, דְּהוֹאִיל וּבָתֵּי הָעִיר מֻבְלָעִים בְּתוֹךְ שִׁבְעִים אַמָּה וְשִׁירַיִם שֶׁל זֶה — הֲרֵי כֻּלָּם כְּדִירָה אַחַת, כְּמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שצ״ח » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ו:ד).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif ב writes the place enclosed for dwelling, even if it now has no residents. The Rav's seif 4 writes that the whole city is counted as four amos even if it is not surrounded by partitions in accordance with the law — for example if it has many breaches of their full height and of more than ten [amos] — because its houses are included within seventy amos and a remainder of one another, and are all as one dwelling; he refers to his Siman 398.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, for one who establishes his Shabbos residence in a city, the whole city is counted as four amos, even if it is not surrounded by partitions in accordance with the law, because its houses are included within seventy amos and a remainder of one another (396:4); the Rav refers to his Siman 398. See section 4, point ⑤.

4 · Lemaaseh

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

The practical Chabad conduct

Points of halachic conduct that flow directly from the Shulchan Aruch HaRav Siman 396, in the Chabad sensibility. Each point refers to its seif of the Rav, in the order of the text.

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman 396

  • ① The four amos of every person: every person has four amos wherever he is, even if he went out beyond the techum: he may walk in them and carry in them; the two thousand amos are measured from the place of his Shabbos residence, apart from these four amos. If he established his Shabbos residence in a place surrounded by partitions in such a way that it is permitted to carry in all of it, all of it is counted for him as four amos, and one gives him two thousand amos around it, even if it contains several mil — provided that it was enclosed for dwelling from the outset, even though it now has no residents, or, if it was not, that it has no more than a beis sa'asayim; a place that is not enclosed for dwelling and has more than a beis sa'asayim is not counted as four amos (396:1). On the root of the techum, the Rav reports two opinions (section 2, point א). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 396:1.
  • ② The shape of the four amos: the Rav reports two opinions — according to the first, one chooses four amos in one direction; according to the second, these four amos of the techumin are not like those of carrying objects: one has four amos in every direction, eight by eight — and writes that there is ground to rely on the second to be lenient in [a rule] of rabbinic order, both at the place of one's Shabbos residence and beyond the techum (396:2). On the Rema's gloss, see section 2, point ב. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 396:2.
  • ③ The measure of the four amos: these four amos are measured for each person by his own amah; the dwarf in his limbs, whose body is large and whose amah is small, is given four average amos of six tefachim. The Rav explained in Siman 349 that they are measured tight; some say that they are measured generously, and there is ground to rely on them for these four amos of techumin, which in their root are only of rabbinic order (396:3). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 396:3.
  • ④ The mound and the harvested field: for one who established his Shabbos residence on a mound ten tefachim high, if it has only a beis sa'asayim, all of it is counted for him as four amos; and likewise in a harvested field surrounded by stalks ten tefachim high, provided that he tied the stalks together in such a way that an ordinary wind does not move them (396:4). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 396:4.
  • ⑤ The city: for one who establishes his Shabbos residence in a city, the whole city is counted as four amos, even if it is not surrounded by partitions in accordance with the law — for example if it has many breaches of their full height and of more than ten [amos] — because its houses are included within seventy amos and a remainder of one another (396:4); the Rav refers to his Siman 398. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 396:4.
