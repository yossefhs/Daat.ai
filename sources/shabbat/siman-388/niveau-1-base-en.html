1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שפ״ח contains a single seif of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo), without a gloss of the Rema. Topic of the siman, according to its title: the law when the courtyards did not make an eruv together and also did not make a partnership in the mavoy. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe also has a single seif for this siman: its text is studied at Level 4.

Seif א

דין אם לא עירבו החצירו' יחד וגם לא נשתתפו במבוי. ובו סעיף אחד:
אם לא עירבו החצירו' יחד וגם לא נשתתפו במבוי מותר לטלטל בכל המבוי כלים ששבתו בתוכו בין עירבו חצירו' עם הבתים בין לא עירבו ולהרב רבינו משה בר מיימון דוקא כשלא עירבו חצירו' עם הבתים אבל אם עירבו חצירו' עם הבתים אין מטלטלין במבוי אלא בארבע אמות:
Translation: If the courtyards did not make an eruv together, and also did not make a partnership in the mavoy [the shared alley onto which the courtyards open; the partnership in the mavoy is the shituf], it is permitted to carry throughout the mavoy the objects that rested in it [when Shabbos began], whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or did not. And according to the Rav, our master Moshe son of Maimon [the Rambam], only when the courtyards did not make an eruv with the houses; but if the courtyards made an eruv with the houses, one carries in the mavoy only within four cubits.
What this seif says:
  • Neither eruv between the courtyards nor shituf: objects that rested in the mavoy are carried throughout the mavoy: אם לא עירבו החצירו׳ יחד וגם לא נשתתפו במבוי מותר לטלטל בכל המבוי כלים ששבתו בתוכו בין עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים בין לא עירבו. According to the Mishnah Berurah, the meaning is “even if they did not make an eruv”, for if they did, it is also like a shituf of the mavoy, as in the previous siman (ס״ק א); it is likewise permitted to take out from the courtyard to the mavoy, and from the mavoy to the courtyard, objects that rested within (בתוכו), and all this when the mavoy itself is fixed according to the rule with a lechi or a korah (ס״ק ב); and when the courtyards made an eruv with the houses, one might fear that a person would come to take house objects out to the mavoy, and it is nevertheless permitted (ס״ק ג).
  • The view of the Rambam: only if the courtyards did not make an eruv with the houses; otherwise, four cubits: ולהרב רבינו משה בר מיימון דוקא כשלא עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים אבל אם עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים אין מטלטלין במבוי אלא בארבע אמות. On this view, the commentators discuss: see section 5 (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד) and Level 2.

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Several courtyards open onto one mavoy. The previous siman (387) dealt with the shituf of the mavoy and the eruv of the courtyards; this one deals with the case where the courtyards made neither an eruv together nor the shituf of the mavoy. The Beit Yosef brings the source, at the beginning of the chapter Rabbi Eliezer de-Milah: « א״ר זירא אמר רב מבוי שלא נשתתפו בו אין מטלטלין בו אלא בד״א ופירשו בגמרא דהיינו כשעירבו חצירות עם בתים אבל אם לא עירבו חצירות עם בתים מותר לטלטל בכולו » (בית יוסף או״ח שפ״ח) — Rabbi Zeira said in the name of Rav: a mavoy in which they did not make a partnership, one carries in it only within four cubits; and the Gemara explains it: when the courtyards made an eruv with the houses; but if the courtyards did not make an eruv with the houses, it is permitted to carry throughout the mavoy.

The Beit Yosef then writes: « ממילא בטלה הא דרב ולעולם מותר לטלטל במבוי בין עירבו חצירות בין לא עירבו וכ״כ התוספות בהדיא וזהו דעת הרי״ף והרא״ש שהשמיטו הא דרב אבל הרמב״ם פוסק בפ״ה מה״ע כרב » (בית יוסף או״ח שפ״ח) — as a result [since we do not hold like Rav in a neighbouring question, but like Rabbi Yochanan: siman 372], the statement of Rav falls away, and it is always permitted to carry in the mavoy, whether the courtyards made an eruv or not; so the Tosafos write explicitly, and this is the view of the Rif and the Rosh, who omitted the statement of Rav; but the Rambam rules like Rav, in chapter 5 of the laws of eruv. The seif of the Mechaber first states the rule anonymously, then brings the view of the Rambam.

The fundamental question: when the courtyards of a mavoy made neither an eruv together nor the shituf of the mavoy, may one carry in the mavoy the objects that rested in it? The seif answers: yes, throughout the mavoy, whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not. It then brings the view of the Rambam: only if the courtyards did not make an eruv with the houses; if they did, one carries in the mavoy only within four cubits.

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. On this seif, the Mishnah Berurah refers to the previous siman (ס״ק א) and to siman 372, seif 1 (ס״ק ב).

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman, in the order of the seif:

The essential notions of siman שפ״ח:
  • אם לא עירבו החצירו׳ יחד — if the courtyards did not make an eruv together: « ר״ל אף אם לא עירבו וכו׳ דאם עירבו הרי הוא ג״כ כעין שיתוף מבוי וכבסימן הקודם » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק א) — that is: even if they did not make an eruv; for if they did, it is also like a shituf of the mavoy, as in the previous siman.
  • כלים ששבתו בתוכו — the objects that rested in the mavoy: « וה״ה דמותר להוציא מחצר למבוי כלים ששבתו בתוכו וכן מן המבוי לחצר » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק ב) — likewise, it is permitted to take out from the courtyard to the mavoy objects that rested within (בתוכו), and from the mavoy to the courtyard; and the reason: « דלא תקנו חכמים שיתופי מבואות אלא להתיר להוציא למבוי כלים ששבתו בבית אבל לענין חצרות ומבואות עצמן לא תקנו ורשות אחד הוא דמותר לטלטל מזה לזה » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק ב) — the Sages instituted the shituf of the mavoy only to permit taking out to the mavoy objects that rested in the house; but for the courtyards and the mavoy themselves, they did not institute it: it is one domain, and it is permitted to carry from one to the other.
  • The fixed mavoy — « ופשוט דכ״ז מיירי שהיה המבוי עצמו מתוקן בלחי וקורה כדין » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק ב) — it is simple that all this concerns the case where the mavoy itself was fixed with a lechi or a korah [לחי וקורה], according to the rule.
  • בין עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים בין לא עירבו — whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not: « דמצויין כלי הבית בחצר ושייך למגזר דלמא אתי לאפוקי כלי הבית למבוי אפ״ה שרי » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק ג) — house objects are found in the courtyard, and there would be room to decree lest a person come to take house objects out to the mavoy; it is nevertheless permitted.
  • אין מטלטלין במבוי אלא בארבע אמות — one carries in the mavoy only within four cubits: according to the view of the Rambam, when the courtyards made an eruv with the houses (seif א). On this view, see section 5 (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד) and Level 2.

The heart of the siman: without an eruv between the courtyards and without a shituf of the mavoy, objects that rested in the mavoy are carried throughout the mavoy, whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not (seif א) — when the mavoy is fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב). The seif then brings the view of the Rambam: if the courtyards made an eruv with the houses, one carries in the mavoy only within four cubits (seif א); on this view, the commentators discuss (section 5, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד; Level 2).

6. Position of the Rema

In the text Sefaria serves (Maginei Eretz edition), the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) adds no gloss (הגה) to this seif.

Without a gloss of the Rema:
  • Seif א — no gloss of the Rema; the commentators explain the seif: see sections 1, 3 and 5, and Level 2.
  • Chabad → the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שפ״ח, in a single seif, with its own numbering (see Level 4 — Daat HaRav).

7. Modern practical cases

The situations below are applications by the site: the seif does not name them; each one is linked to the statement of the seif that it involves.

SituationQuick analysis
An object rested in the mavoy when Shabbos began; the courtyards made neither an eruv together nor the shituf, and none made an eruv with its housesApplication by the site, seif א: it is permitted to carry it throughout the mavoy — when the mavoy is fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב). Consult your Rav.
Same situation, but each courtyard made an eruv with its housesApplication by the site, seif א: the seif permits carrying throughout the mavoy; according to the view of the Rambam that it then brings, one carries in the mavoy only within four cubits; on this view, the commentators discuss (section 5, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד; Level 2). Consult your Rav.
The courtyards of the mavoy made an eruv together, without a shitufApplication by the site, Mishnah Berurah on seif א (ס״ק א): if they made an eruv, it is also like a shituf of the mavoy, as in the previous siman (siman 387). Consult your Rav.
For precise practical cases: the anonymous rule of the seif holds whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not; the Rambam's view, which the seif brings next, limits it to the case where they did not; and the Mishnah Berurah adds the fixing of the mavoy with a lechi or a korah (ס״ק ב). For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שפ״ח:
  1. Without an eruv between the courtyards and without a shituf, objects that rested in the mavoy are carried throughout the mavoy, whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not (seif א) — when the mavoy is fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב).
  2. The view of the Rambam: only if the courtyards did not make an eruv with the houses; otherwise, one carries in the mavoy only within four cubits (seif א); the commentators discuss it (section 5, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד; Level 2).
  3. If the courtyards made an eruv together, it is also like a shituf of the mavoy, as in the previous siman (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א).
  4. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Which objects does the seif permit carrying throughout the mavoy, and in what situation (seif א)?
  2. What might one have feared when the courtyards made an eruv with the houses, and why is it nevertheless permitted according to the anonymous rule of the seif (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג)?
  3. What does the view of the Rambam say, and how far does one then carry in the mavoy (seif א)?
  4. What condition on the mavoy itself does the Mishnah Berurah set (ס״ק ב), and what does it write on the view of the Rambam (ס״ק ד)?
