The seif number on this page is that of the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber (a single seif, with no gloss of the Rema). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav, studied in Level 4, also writes this siman in a single seif, in its own words: when it is cited, its name is given next to the number.

1. The central axiom

Siman שפ״ח in one sentence.
The courtyards of a mavoy made neither an eruv together nor the shituf of the mavoy: it is permitted to carry throughout the mavoy the objects that rested in it, whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not — « אם לא עירבו החצירו׳ יחד וגם לא נשתתפו במבוי מותר לטלטל בכל המבוי כלים ששבתו בתוכו בין עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים בין לא עירבו » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ח:א); according to the Mishnah Berurah, when the mavoy itself is fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule (ס״ק ב), a condition the Shulchan Aruch HaRav also sets (388:1). The seif then brings the view of the Rambam: « ולהרב רבינו משה בר מיימון דוקא כשלא עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים אבל אם עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים אין מטלטלין במבוי אלא בארבע אמות » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ח:א); on this view, the commentators discuss (section 5).

3. The course of the seif — from the unattributed rule to the Rambam's view

Seif א, first clause — the unattributed rule: if the courtyards did not make an eruv together, and also did not make a partnership in the mavoy, it is permitted to carry throughout the mavoy the objects that rested in it, whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not — according to the Mishnah Berurah, when the mavoy is fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule (ס״ק ב).
↓
Seif א, second clause — the Rambam's view: only when the courtyards did not make an eruv with the houses; if they did, one carries in the mavoy only within four cubits.

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes this siman in a single seif (388:1): what the shituf is for, and the objects that rested in the mavoy or in the courtyards; the condition of the lechi or the korah; the objects that rested in the house and were taken out. He does not write the Rambam's view — Level 4.

4. Decision tree — may one carry in the mavoy?

Q1: Did the courtyards make an eruv together? (Mishnah Berurah on seif א, ס״ק א)
↓ YES → it is also like a shituf of the mavoy, as in the previous siman (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א). NO, and no shituf of the mavoy → Q2.
Q2: Did the object rest in the mavoy? (seif א)
↓ YES → it is carried throughout the mavoy (seif א) — when the mavoy is fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 388:1). Likewise, from the courtyard to the mavoy and from the mavoy to the courtyard (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav also names the objects that rested in the courtyards, provided one does not bring them into the house (388:1).
Q3: Did the courtyards make an eruv with the houses? (seif א)
↓ According to the seif's unattributed rule, this changes nothing (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג). According to the Rambam's view, which the seif brings next, one then carries in the mavoy only within four cubits (seif א). The commentators discuss it (section 5).
Q4: Did the object rest in the house?
↓ The Mechaber's seif does not speak of it. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav: the shituf only serves to permit taking out to the mavoy objects that rested in the houses; if they were taken out into a mavoy without shituf or into a courtyard without eruv, in the manner of wearing a garment, or in a forbidden way, they may be carried there beyond four amos, but not from the courtyard to the mavoy or the reverse (388:1; he refers to siman 372).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

5. The Rambam's view — where the commentators discuss it

The seif first sets the unattributed rule, then brings the view of the Rambam: « ולהרב רבינו משה בר מיימון דוקא כשלא עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים אבל אם עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים אין מטלטלין במבוי אלא בארבע אמות » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ח:א). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes the rule « בֵּין שֶׁעֵרְבוּ הַחֲצֵרוֹת לְעַצְמָן בֵּין שֶׁלֹּא עֵרְבוּ » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א), and does not write the Rambam's view (Level 4, point ב). summary: the table condenses what the two texts write.

TextWhen the courtyards made an eruv with the houses
Seif א, first clause (unattributed rule)It is permitted to carry throughout the mavoy the objects that rested in it, whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not; the Mishnah Berurah sets here the condition of the lechi or the korah (ס״ק ב)
Seif א, second clause (the Rambam's view)One carries in the mavoy only within four cubits; the permission holds only when the courtyards did not make an eruv with the houses
Shulchan Aruch HaRav (388:1)The mavoy fixed with a lechi or a korah is one domain with the courtyards for the objects that rested in them, whether the courtyards made an eruv for themselves or not; he does not write the Rambam's view

On the Rambam's view, the commentators discuss: Level 2 (section 4) cites each in his own words, and Level 1 (section 5) gives the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ד) in full. This page does not decide: consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic כ-ב-א

Three words of the seif, in its order:

כ — כלים ששבתו בתוכו: the objects that rested in the mavoy are carried throughout the mavoy — when it is fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב).

ב — בין עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים בין לא עירבו: according to the seif's unattributed rule, whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not.

א — בארבע אמות: according to the Rambam's view, if the courtyards made an eruv with the houses — the commentators discuss it (section 5).

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that the shituf of the mavoy is needed for objects that rested in the mavoy. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav: « כָּל שִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת אֵינָם אֶלָּא לְהַתִּיר לְהוֹצִיא לְתוֹכוֹ כֵּלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בַּבָּתִּים. אֲבָל כֵּלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בְּתוֹכוֹ אוֹ בַּחֲצֵרוֹת — מֻתָּר לְטַלְטְלָם בּוֹ בְּיוֹתֵר מִד׳ אַמּוֹת וּמִמֶּנּוּ לַחֲצֵרוֹת, וּבִלְבָד שֶׁלֹּא יַכְנִיסֵם לַבַּיִת, אֲפִלּוּ עֵרְבוּ הַבָּתִּים שֶׁבֶּחָצֵר » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א) — under the condition of the lechi or the korah (pitfall 2). That the shituf serves only objects that rested in the house, the Mishnah Berurah also writes (ס״ק ב).
Pitfall 2: Thinking that the seif permits carrying in any mavoy. According to the Mishnah Berurah, all this concerns a mavoy fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule: « ופשוט דכ״ז מיירי שהיה המבוי עצמו מתוקן בלחי וקורה כדין » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק ב); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav sets the same condition: « וּבִלְבָד שֶׁיְּהֵא הַמָּבוֹי מְתֻקָּן בְּלֶחִי אוֹ קוֹרָה כְּהִלְכָתוֹ » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א). The Mechaber's seif does not write it (Level 4, point ב).
Pitfall 3: Thinking that, according to the seif's unattributed rule, the courtyards' eruv with the houses prevents carrying in the mavoy. The seif writes « בין עירבו חצירו׳ עם הבתים בין לא עירבו » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ח:א); the Mishnah Berurah: one might fear that a person would come to take house objects out to the mavoy, « אפ״ה שרי » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק ג) — in a mavoy fixed with a lechi or a korah (pitfall 2). The seif then brings the Rambam's view: section 5.
Pitfall 4: Presenting the Rambam's view as the seif's rule, or the unattributed rule as its only voice. The seif writes both: the unattributed rule first, the Rambam's view after it (seif א); the commentators discuss it: section 5. Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 5: Thinking that objects that rested in the house, once taken out into a mavoy without shituf or into a courtyard without eruv, are carried from the courtyard to the mavoy. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav: « וְכֵלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בְּבֵיתוֹ, וְהוֹצִיאָם לְמָבוֹי שֶׁאֵינוֹ מְשֻׁתָּף אוֹ לְחָצֵר שֶׁאֵינָהּ מְעֹרֶבֶת בְּדֶרֶךְ מַלְבּוּשׁ אוֹ בְּאִסּוּר — מֻתָּר לְטַלְטְלָם שָׁם בְּיוֹתֵר מִד׳ אַמּוֹת, אֲבָל אָסוּר לְטַלְטְלָם מֵחָצֵר לְמָבוֹי אוֹ לְהֵפֶךְ » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א). The Mechaber's seif does not speak of this case.
Pitfall 6: Mixing the two texts. The Mechaber and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav each write this siman in a single seif, but not in the same words: Levels 1 to 3 cite seif א of the Mechaber, Level 4 the seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (388:1); where they differ is in Level 4 (section 2).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שפ״ח

For daily conduct

  1. If the courtyards made an eruv together, it is also like a shituf of the mavoy, as in the previous siman (Mishnah Berurah on seif א, ס״ק א).
  2. Without an eruv between the courtyards and without shituf, the objects that rested in the mavoy are carried throughout the mavoy, whether the courtyards made an eruv with the houses or not (seif א) — when the mavoy is fixed with a lechi or a korah, according to the rule (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 388:1); likewise, from the courtyard to the mavoy and from the mavoy to the courtyard (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב).
  3. The Rambam's view, which the seif brings next: only if the courtyards did not make an eruv with the houses; otherwise, one carries in the mavoy only within four cubits (seif א); the commentators discuss it (section 5): consult your Rav.
  4. Objects that rested in the house and were taken out into a mavoy without shituf or into a courtyard without eruv, in the manner of wearing a garment, or in a forbidden way: they may be carried there beyond four amos, but not from the courtyard to the mavoy or the reverse (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 388:1, which refers to siman 372); the Mechaber's seif does not speak of this case.
  5. When in doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
