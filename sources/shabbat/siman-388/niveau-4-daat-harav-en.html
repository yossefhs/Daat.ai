דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on Siman 388

When the courtyards did not make an eruv together and no shituf was made in the mavoi: what the shituf is for, objects that rested in the mavoi or in the courtyards, the condition of the lechi or the korah, and objects taken out of the house. The single seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, compared with the Mechaber's single seif.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav is not a commentary on the Mechaber: it is a Shulchan Aruch in its own right, written by the Alter Rebbe, with its own division and its own numbering of the seifim. Here the Shulchan Aruch HaRav has one seif, and the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has one seif too; the references on this page follow the Rav's numbering, except where the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch is named.

This page gathers, for Siman 388, the full text of the Rav with its translation, where his text differs from the Mechaber's, and the practical conduct that follows from it.

1 · Text of the Alter Rebbe

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן שפ״ח

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav of the Alter Rebbe

סימן שפ״ח — דִּין אִם לֹא עֵרְבוּ הַחֲצֵרוֹת יַחַד וְגַם לֹא נִשְׁתַּתְּפוּ בַּמָּבוֹי — וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד
Source: Kehot edition, as reproduced on Sefaria. 1 seif (the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has 1 too, under its own numbering).

סעיף א דִּין אִם לֹא עֵרְבוּ הַחֲצֵרוֹת יַחַד וְגַם לֹא נִשְׁתַּתְּפוּ בַּמָּבוֹי וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד: כָּל שִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת אֵינָם אֶלָּא…

דִּין אִם לֹא עֵרְבוּ הַחֲצֵרוֹת יַחַד וְגַם לֹא נִשְׁתַּתְּפוּ בַּמָּבוֹי וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד:

כָּל שִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת אֵינָם אֶלָּא לְהַתִּיר לְהוֹצִיא לְתוֹכוֹ כֵּלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בַּבָּתִּים. אֲבָל כֵּלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בְּתוֹכוֹ אוֹ בַּחֲצֵרוֹת — מֻתָּר לְטַלְטְלָם בּוֹ בְּיוֹתֵר מִד' אַמּוֹת וּמִמֶּנּוּ לַחֲצֵרוֹת, וּבִלְבָד שֶׁלֹּא יַכְנִיסֵם לַבַּיִת, אֲפִלּוּ עֵרְבוּ הַבָּתִּים שֶׁבֶּחָצֵר.

וּבִלְבָד שֶׁיְּהֵא הַמָּבוֹי מְתֻקָּן בְּלֶחִי אוֹ קוֹרָה כְּהִלְכָתוֹ, שֶׁאָז אֵינוֹ כַּרְמְלִית אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁלֹּא נִשְׁתַּתְּפוּ בּוֹ, וּרְשׁוּת אַחַת הוּא עִם הַחֲצֵרוֹת לְכֵלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בְּתוֹכָן, בֵּין שֶׁעֵרְבוּ הַחֲצֵרוֹת לְעַצְמָן בֵּין שֶׁלֹּא עֵרְבוּ.

וְכֵלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בְּבֵיתוֹ, וְהוֹצִיאָם לְמָבוֹי שֶׁאֵינוֹ מְשֻׁתָּף אוֹ לְחָצֵר שֶׁאֵינָהּ מְעֹרֶבֶת בְּדֶרֶךְ מַלְבּוּשׁ אוֹ בְּאִסּוּר — מֻתָּר לְטַלְטְלָם שָׁם בְּיוֹתֵר מִד' אַמּוֹת, אֲבָל אָסוּר לְטַלְטְלָם מֵחָצֵר לְמָבוֹי אוֹ לְהֵפֶךְ, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שע״ב:

The law when the courtyards did not make an eruv together, and also did not make a shituf in the mavoi; it contains one seif:

All shitufei mevo'os (partnerships of the mavoi) serve only to permit taking out into [the mavoi] objects that rested [at the onset of Shabbos] in the houses. But objects that rested in [the mavoi] or in the courtyards, it is permitted to carry them in it beyond four amos, and from it to the courtyards, provided one does not bring them into the house, even if the houses in the courtyard made an eruv.

Provided that the mavoi is fitted with a lechi or a korah according to its law, for then it is not a karmelis, even though no shituf was made in it, and it is one domain with the courtyards for the objects that rested in them, whether the courtyards made an eruv for themselves or did not.

And objects that rested in his house, which he took out into a mavoi without shituf or into a courtyard without eruv, in the manner of wearing a garment, or in a forbidden way: it is permitted to carry them there beyond four amos, but it is forbidden to carry them from the courtyard to the mavoi or the reverse, as was explained in Siman 372:

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim 388. Sefaria gives no Kuntres Acharon entry for this siman.

2 · The Rav's text and the Mechaber's

לשון הרב מול סעיף המחבר

Where the Rav's text differs from the Mechaber's seif

This survey compares the Rav's text with the Mechaber's single seif, quoted side by side, in the order of the Rav's text, and notes only what the comparison of the two texts shows. A point noted is not thereby peculiar to the Rav: it may already be written by the Mechaber himself, in another siman, or by one of the commentators — the Tur; the Magen Avraham and the Taz, before the Rav; the Mishnah Berurah, later than the Rav — and the page sometimes names where, without noting every such source (see also Level 2).

נקודה א — שיתופי מבואות, וכלים ששבתו במבוי או בחצרות (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א)

Point א — what the shituf of the mavoi is for, and objects that rested in the mavoi or in the courtyards (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 388:1)

Objects that rested in the mavoi or in the courtyards

What the Mechaber writes: « מותר לטלטל בכל המבוי כלים ששבתו בתוכו בין עירבו חצירו' עם הבתים בין לא עירבו » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ח:א), and the seif continues with the Rambam's view (point ב). He names here only objects that rested in the mavoi, and writes neither what the shituf is for, nor carrying from the mavoi to the courtyards, nor that one does not bring them into the house.

What the Rav writes: « כָּל שִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת אֵינָם אֶלָּא לְהַתִּיר לְהוֹצִיא לְתוֹכוֹ כֵּלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בַּבָּתִּים. אֲבָל כֵּלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בְּתוֹכוֹ אוֹ בַּחֲצֵרוֹת — מֻתָּר לְטַלְטְלָם בּוֹ בְּיוֹתֵר מִד' אַמּוֹת וּמִמֶּנּוּ לַחֲצֵרוֹת, וּבִלְבָד שֶׁלֹּא יַכְנִיסֵם לַבַּיִת, אֲפִלּוּ עֵרְבוּ הַבָּתִּים שֶׁבֶּחָצֵר » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א); and he continues at once with a condition, « וּבִלְבָד שֶׁיְּהֵא הַמָּבוֹי מְתֻקָּן בְּלֶחִי אוֹ קוֹרָה כְּהִלְכָתוֹ » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א) (point ב).

The difference: the Rav's text first states that the shituf of the mavoi serves only for objects that rested in the houses; it permits, together with objects that rested in the mavoi, those that rested in the courtyards, and carrying from the mavoi to the courtyards; and it writes that one does not bring them into the house. The Mechaber's seif writes none of this.

What is found elsewhere: the Mechaber himself, in seif א of Siman 372, names the fitted mavoi among the domains between which one may carry: « או למבוי שמתוקן בלחי או קורה אפי' לא עירבו בו » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ב:א). The Mishnah Berurah, later than the Rav, writes on this seif: « וה״ה דמותר להוציא מחצר למבוי כלים ששבתו בתוכו וכן מן המבוי לחצר וכדלעיל בסימן שע״ב ס״א דלא תקנו חכמים שיתופי מבואות אלא להתיר להוציא למבוי כלים ששבתו בבית אבל לענין חצרות ומבואות עצמן לא תקנו ורשות אחד הוא דמותר לטלטל מזה לזה » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק ב); the end of the same ס״ק sets the condition of the lechi and the korah (point ב).

נקודה ב — מתוקן בלחי או קורה, ודעת הרמב״ם (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א)

Point ב — the lechi or the korah, and the Rambam's view (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 388:1)

The lechi or the korah, and the Rambam's view

What the Mechaber writes: « מותר לטלטל בכל המבוי כלים ששבתו בתוכו בין עירבו חצירו' עם הבתים בין לא עירבו » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ח:א), then « ולהרב רבינו משה בר מיימון דוקא כשלא עירבו חצירו' עם הבתים אבל אם עירבו חצירו' עם הבתים אין מטלטלין במבוי אלא בארבע אמות » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ח:א). He thus brings two views, and does not write in this seif the condition of the lechi or the korah.

What the Rav writes: « וּבִלְבָד שֶׁיְּהֵא הַמָּבוֹי מְתֻקָּן בְּלֶחִי אוֹ קוֹרָה כְּהִלְכָתוֹ, שֶׁאָז אֵינוֹ כַּרְמְלִית אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁלֹּא נִשְׁתַּתְּפוּ בּוֹ, וּרְשׁוּת אַחַת הוּא עִם הַחֲצֵרוֹת לְכֵלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בְּתוֹכָן, בֵּין שֶׁעֵרְבוּ הַחֲצֵרוֹת לְעַצְמָן בֵּין שֶׁלֹּא עֵרְבוּ » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א). He does not write the Rambam's view.

The difference: the Rav's text sets the condition that the mavoi be fitted with a lechi or a korah according to its law, and gives the reason: it is then not a karmelis, and it is one domain with the courtyards. It writes that the rule holds whether the courtyards made an eruv for themselves or did not, without bringing the Rambam's view, which the Mechaber writes second.

What is found elsewhere: for the condition, the Mechaber's seif א of Siman 372 quoted in point א; for the single domain, whether or not the courtyards made an eruv, the Taz, before the Rav: « דקי״ל חצירות ומבוי רשות א׳ הם אפי׳ בעירבו בחצירו׳ » (ט״ז או״ח שפ״ח ס״ק א), who then brings the Rambam's view; and the Mishnah Berurah, after the Rav: « ופשוט דכ״ז מיירי שהיה המבוי עצמו מתוקן בלחי וקורה כדין » (משנה ברורה שפ״ח ס״ק ב). On the Rambam's view, which the Mechaber brings second, the commentators discuss it: see Level 2, sections 2 and 4.

נקודה ג — כלים ששבתו בבית והוציאם (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א)

Point ג — objects that rested in the house and were taken out (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 388:1)

Objects taken out of the house

What the Mechaber writes: the Mechaber's seif does not deal with this case.

What the Rav writes: « וְכֵלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בְּבֵיתוֹ, וְהוֹצִיאָם לְמָבוֹי שֶׁאֵינוֹ מְשֻׁתָּף אוֹ לְחָצֵר שֶׁאֵינָהּ מְעֹרֶבֶת בְּדֶרֶךְ מַלְבּוּשׁ אוֹ בְּאִסּוּר — מֻתָּר לְטַלְטְלָם שָׁם בְּיוֹתֵר מִד' אַמּוֹת, אֲבָל אָסוּר לְטַלְטְלָם מֵחָצֵר לְמָבוֹי אוֹ לְהֵפֶךְ » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א), and he refers to Siman 372, « כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שע״ב » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ח:א).

The difference: the Rav's text writes this case, and distinguishes carrying there, beyond four amos, which is permitted, from carrying from the courtyard to the mavoi or the reverse, which is forbidden.

4 · Lemaaseh

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

The practical Chabad conduct

Points of halachic conduct that flow directly from the Shulchan Aruch HaRav Siman 388, in the Chabad sensibility. Each point refers to its seif of the Rav, in the order of the text.

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman 388

  • ① Objects that rested in the mavoi or in the courtyards: the shituf of the mavoi serves only to permit taking out into the mavoi objects that rested in the houses. Objects that rested in the mavoi or in the courtyards may be carried in the mavoi beyond four amos, and from the mavoi to the courtyards, provided one does not bring them into the house; on condition that the mavoi is fitted with a lechi or a korah according to its law, even though no shituf was made in it; whether the courtyards made an eruv for themselves or did not (388:1). The Mechaber's seif also brings the Rambam's view, which the Rav does not write (section 2, point ב): consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 388:1.
  • ② Objects taken out of the house: objects that rested in the house and were taken out into a mavoi without shituf or into a courtyard without eruv, in the manner of wearing a garment, or in a forbidden way, may be carried there beyond four amos, but it is forbidden to carry them from the courtyard to the mavoi or the reverse; the Rav refers to Siman 372 (388:1). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 388:1.

⚠ This section presents the Chabad halachic conduct. For any concrete question: consult your Rav.
