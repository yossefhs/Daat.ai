1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שפ״ט contains a single seif of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo), without a gloss of the Rema. Topic of the siman, according to its title: a non-Jew who has a window open to the alley. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe also has one seif for this siman, studied at Level 4.

Seif א

עכו״ם שיש לו חלון פתוח למבוי. ובו סעיף אחד:
עכו״ם הדר במבוי ויש לו חלון אחורי ביתו פתוח לבקעה או לקרפף אפי' אין בו אלא ד״ט על ד״ט אינו אוסר על בני מבוי אפי' מכניס ומוציא גמלים וקרונו' דרך המבוי כל היום שחפץ יותר באותו שפתוח לו מאחוריו לבדו שיש לו אויר והוי פתחו למבוי כמו שאינו רגיל שאינו אוסר והוא שיש בבקעה או בקרפף יותר מסאתים אבל אם אין בו אלא סאתים קטן הוא ולא ניחא ליה ביה וישראל שיש לו פתח למבוי ופתח מאחוריו לקרפף ושכח ולא עירב אם הקרפף יותר מסאתים אוסר לפי שאינו ראוי לו ואינו מסתלק מן המבוי ואם הוקף לדירה אפי' יותר מסאתי' אינו אוסר כיון שראוי לו ויש מי שאומר דאדרבה אם הוקף לדירה אפי' בית סאתים אוסר שכיון שהוקף לדירה אין בו אויר ואינו חפץ בו להסתלק בשבילו מן המבוי:
Translation: A non-Jew (akum) who lives in an alley (mavoy) and has, behind his house, a window open to a valley (bik'ah) or to an enclosure (karpef) — even if it is only four tefachim by four tefachim — does not forbid [carrying] for the residents of the alley, even if he brings camels and wagons in and out by way of the alley all day, for he prefers the one that opens to him behind, for himself alone, where he has open space (avir), and his door to the alley is like [a door] that is not regularly used, which does not forbid. And this is when the valley or the enclosure has more than [the area of] two se'ah; but if it has only two se'ah, it is small, and he does not care for it. And a Jew who has a door to the alley and, behind [his house], a door to an enclosure, and forgot and did not make the eruv: if the enclosure is more than two se'ah, he forbids, because [the enclosure] is not usable for him, and he does not withdraw from the alley; and if it was enclosed for dwelling, even [if it is] more than two se'ah, he does not forbid, since it is usable for him. And there is one who says: on the contrary, if it was enclosed for dwelling, even [an area of] two se'ah forbids, for since it was enclosed for dwelling it has no open space, and he does not care for it so as to withdraw from the alley on its account.
What this seif says:
  • The non-Jew whose window, behind his house, opens to a valley or an enclosure does not forbid the residents of the alley: עכו״ם הדר במבוי ויש לו חלון אחורי ביתו פתוח לבקעה או לקרפף … אינו אוסר על בני מבוי אפי׳ מכניס ומוציא גמלים וקרונו׳ דרך המבוי כל היום. Even if the window is only four tefachim by four, and provided the valley or the enclosure has more than two se'ah (continuation of seif א); and all the more so if it is a full door (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א). "By way of the alley": by way of the door that opens to the alley (ס״ק ב). The Biur Halachah writes that it seems to him (נ״ל) that an enclosure was permitted only if, like the valley, it is open to another place, and he cites the Ritva: see section 3.
  • The reason the seif gives: שחפץ יותר באותו שפתוח לו מאחוריו לבדו שיש לו אויר והוי פתחו למבוי כמו שאינו רגיל שאינו אוסר. The Mishnah Berurah compares this to a courtyard open to two alleys, which does not forbid the alley through whose door one is not used to going in and out; it refers to siman 386 (ס״ק ג).
  • The condition: more than two se'ah: והוא שיש בבקעה או בקרפף יותר מסאתים אבל אם אין בו אלא סאתים קטן הוא ולא ניחא ליה ביה. According to the Mishnah Berurah, in this case even a large door open to [the valley or the enclosure] does not cancel his door in the alley, and he forbids the residents of the alley — provided he uses the door on the alley side more (ס״ק ד).
  • The Jew who forgot the eruv, with a door to an enclosure of more than two se'ah, forbids: וישראל שיש לו פתח למבוי ופתח מאחוריו לקרפף ושכח ולא עירב … אם הקרפף יותר מסאתים אוסר לפי שאינו ראוי לו ואינו מסתלק מן המבוי. The enclosure is a karmelis, where one carries only within four amos (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה). The seif does not state outright the case of an enclosure of two se'ah: the Tur states it (section 2), and the Magen Avraham and the Mishnah Berurah explain it (section 3).
  • Enclosed for dwelling, the enclosure, even of more than two se'ah, does not forbid: ואם הוקף לדירה אפי׳ יותר מסאתי׳ אינו אוסר כיון שראוי לו.
  • A contrary opinion, reported by the seif: ויש מי שאומר דאדרבה אם הוקף לדירה אפי׳ בית סאתים אוסר שכיון שהוקף לדירה אין בו אויר ואינו חפץ בו להסתלק בשבילו מן המבוי. The seif first writes the rule without a name, then this opinion under "יש מי שאומר". The commentators discuss its scope: see Level 2 (section 5). Consult your Rav.

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

The Beit Yosef writes: « כל זה הסימן מימרות דאמוראי בפרק הדר » (בית יוסף או״ח שפ״ט) — this whole siman consists of statements of Amora'im in the chapter Ha-dar [of tractate Eruvin]; and he writes that the dispute between Rashi and Ri, which the Tur reports, is found there, in Tosafos.

The Tur carries the same teaching. After the Jew whose enclosure is more than two se'ah, he writes: « ואם אינו אלא סאתים אינו אוסר דכיון שראוי לו מסתלק מן המבוי » (טור או״ח שפ״ט) — and if it is only two se'ah, he does not forbid, for since it is usable for him, he withdraws from the alley; the Mechaber's seif does not include this sentence. And the Tur names the two opinions on the enclosure enclosed for dwelling, Rashi and Ri, while the seif writes the first without a name and the second under "יש מי שאומר".

The fundamental question: a resident of the alley has two exits: his door to the alley, and, behind his house, an opening to a valley or an enclosure. Does he forbid [carrying] for the residents of the alley? The seif answers for the non-Jew: no, if the valley or the enclosure has more than two se'ah, for he prefers that opening. And for the Jew who forgot the eruv: an enclosure of more than two se'ah is not usable for him, and he forbids; enclosed for dwelling, he does not forbid — and the seif reports a contrary opinion.

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. Siman 382 (seif 1) explains when a non-Jew who lives in a courtyard with Jews forbids [carrying] for them, and that one must then rent from him. For the door that is not regularly used, the Mishnah Berurah refers to siman 386 (ס״ק ג); and the Magen Avraham refers to siman 372 for the prohibition of taking [an object] out of the house into the enclosure (ס״ק א).

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman, in the order of the seif:

The essential notions of siman שפ״ט:
  • פתוח לבקעה או לקרפף — the opening to a valley or an enclosure. The Mishnah Berurah: « וכ״ש אם הוא פתח גמור » (משנה ברורה שפ״ט ס״ק א) — all the more so if it is a full door. The Biur Halachah: « נ״ל שלא התירו אפילו בשיש לו קרפף אלא בקרפף דומיא דבקעה שהוא פתוח למקום אחר » (ביאור הלכה שפ״ט) — it seems to me that, even when he has an enclosure, they permitted only an enclosure like the valley, which is open to another place; for if he has an opening only toward his house, he can go out to the market only by way of the alley, he explains, and he cites the Ritva.
  • כמו שאינו רגיל — the door that is not regularly used: the seif says it does not forbid; the Mishnah Berurah compares it to the courtyard open to two alleys (ס״ק ג).
  • יותר מסאתים — more than two se'ah: for the non-Jew, an enclosure of two se'ah is small, and he does not care for it (seif א). For the Jew, the Mishnah Berurah writes, on « שאינו ראוי לו » (משנה ברורה שפ״ט ס״ק ה): « שהוא כרמלית ואין מטלטלין בו אלא בתוך ד״א » (משנה ברורה שפ״ט ס״ק ה) — it is a karmelis, where one carries only within four amos; « אבל אם הוא רק בית סאתים או פחות כיון דמותר לטלטל בתוכו ניחא ליה ביה טפי » (משנה ברורה שפ״ט ס״ק ה) — but if it is only two se'ah or less, since one may carry within it, he cares for it more; and it adds: « ואע״ג דלגבי עכו״ם חשיב בית סאתים זוטרא לגבי ישראל נפיש » (משנה ברורה שפ״ט ס״ק ה) — although for the non-Jew an area of two se'ah counts as small, for the Jew it is large. The Magen Avraham had raised the question: « צ״ע דאפי׳ בית סאתים אסור דאינו ראוי לו דאסור להוציא מבית לקרפף » (מגן אברהם שפ״ט ס״ק א) — this needs examination: even an area of two se'ah should forbid, since it is not usable for him, for it is forbidden to take out from the house into the enclosure; and he answers: « ואפשר כיון דשרי לטלטל בתוכו ניחא ליה ביה משא״כ ביותר מסאתים » (מגן אברהם שפ״ט ס״ק א) — and possibly, since one may carry within it, he cares for it; which is not so with more than two se'ah.
  • ואינו מסתלק מן המבוי — he does not withdraw from the alley: together with « שאינו ראוי לו » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ט:א), this is the reason the seif gives for the prohibition of the Jew whose enclosure is more than two se'ah.
  • הוקף לדירה — the enclosure enclosed for dwelling: according to the rule the seif writes without a name, it is usable for him, and the Jew does not forbid, even beyond two se'ah; according to "יש מי שאומר", it forbids, even at two se'ah, for it has no open space. The commentators discuss its scope: each is cited in his own words at Level 2 (section 5).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אA non-Jew who lives in an alley and has, behind his house, a window open to a valley or an enclosure, even four tefachim by four, does not forbid the residents of the alley, even if he brings camels and wagons through the alley all day: he prefers the opening behind, and his door to the alley is like a door that is not regularly used — provided the valley or the enclosure has more than two se'ah; at two se'ah, it is small. A Jew who has a door to the alley and, behind, a door to an enclosure, and forgot the eruv: if the enclosure is more than two se'ah, he forbids, for the enclosure is not usable for him and he does not withdraw from the alley; if it was enclosed for dwelling, even more than two se'ah, he does not forbid. And there is one who says: on the contrary, enclosed for dwelling, even two se'ah forbids.
The heart of the siman: the non-Jew who has two exits, to the alley and to a valley or an enclosure, does not forbid the residents of the alley, for he prefers the other exit (seif א) — provided the valley or the enclosure has more than two se'ah (seif א). For the Jew who forgot the eruv, this depends on whether the enclosure is usable for him: at more than two se'ah it is not, and he forbids; enclosed for dwelling, it is, and he does not forbid — and the seif reports a contrary opinion (seif א; Level 2).

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שפ״ט:
  1. The non-Jew of the alley who has, behind his house, a window to a valley or an enclosure does not forbid the residents of the alley, even at four tefachim by four, and even if he passes through the alley all day (seif א).
  2. The reason: he prefers the opening behind, and his door to the alley is like a door that is not regularly used (seif א).
  3. The condition: the valley or the enclosure has more than two se'ah; at two se'ah, it is small (seif א).
  4. The Jew who forgot the eruv, with a door to an enclosure of more than two se'ah, forbids, for the enclosure is not usable for him; enclosed for dwelling, he does not forbid — and the seif reports a contrary opinion (seif א; Level 2).
  5. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Why does the non-Jew's door to the alley not forbid, when he has behind his house an opening to a valley of more than two se'ah (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג)?
  2. Why is an area of two se'ah small for the non-Jew, and large for the Jew (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה)?
  3. In what way is an enclosure of more than two se'ah not usable for the Jew (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה; Magen Avraham, ס״ק א)?
  4. According to each of the two opinions of the seif, does an enclosure enclosed for dwelling forbid, and why?
  5. What reservation does the Biur Halachah raise about the enclosure, and in whose name?
