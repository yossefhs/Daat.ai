Siman 249 — Specific Laws of Erev Shabbos (Friday)

The Alter Rebbe's shitah on the 13 seifim that codify the obligations and prohibitions of Friday: the three-parsa rule, taanis on erev Shabbos, eating sparingly, and devoting oneself exclusively to Shabbos preparation.

The 4 levels of study

LEVEL 01

רמת המתחילים

Foundation — Introduction

Pedagogical introduction: Hebrew text of the Mechaber and Mishnah Berurah, clear English rendering, key concepts explained, practical cases and summary.

📖 Study

LEVEL 02

רמת הלמדן

Lamdan — Advanced Pilpul

In-depth pilpul: Talmudic sources, שיטות of the Rishonim and Acharonim, fundamental חקירות, classical מחלוקות, and practical נפקא מינות.

📖 Study

LEVEL 03

חזרה וסיכום

Master Synthesis

Master synthesis: central axiom, hierarchy of concepts, decision trees, mnemonics, pitfalls to avoid, and a comprehensive summary table.

📖 Study

LEVEL 04

דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — Shitah of the Alter Rebbe

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on this siman: all 13 seifim complete and 5 entries of Kuntres Acharon. The strength and originality of the Alter Rebbe's psak, his own chiddush, sichos and igros of the Rebbe that illuminate the topic, and conduct למעשה.
