1. The text of the Shulchan Aruch

Siman רצ"ד contains 5 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo) codifying the laws relating to havdalah in tefillah.

Seif א — Havdalah in the berachah of "Chonen ha-Da'as"

דיני הבדלה בתפלה. ובו ה סעיפים:
אומרים הבדלה בחונן הדעת ואם טעה ולא הבדיל משלים תפלתו ואינו חוזר מפני שצריך להבדיל על הכוס ואם טעם קודם שהבדיל על הכוס צריך לחזור ולהבדיל בתפלה:
Translation: "One says havdalah in the berachah of "Chonen ha-Da'as" ("Atta chonen le-adam da'as", the 4th berachah of the Amidah). If one forgot to say it, one completes the tefillah and does not repeat — since one must make havdalah on the cup [so one will make up for it that way]. But if one tasted something before making havdalah on the cup, one must go back and say havdalah in tefillah."
Place of havdalah in the Amidah: in the 4th berachah "Atta chonen", one inserts "Atah Chonantanu" ("You graced us with knowledge of Your Torah... You distinguished between holy and mundane, between light and darkness, between Israel and the nations, between the 7th day and the 6 days of work...") just before "ve-chonnenu mei'itcha".
Rationale of "no need to repeat": havdalah is really 2 parallel mitzvos: (1) in tefillah and (2) on the cup. If one misses the first, one can rely on the second. But if one tastes before havdalah on the cup — one has lost the second, so one must redo the first.

Seif ב — Forgot + no cup available

טעה ולא הבדיל בתפלה ואין לו כוס בלילה וסבור שאף למחר לא יהיה לו צריך לחזור ולהתפלל:
Translation: "If one forgot and did not say havdalah in tefillah, and one has no cup [of wine] at night, and one thinks one will not have one tomorrow either — one must repeat the tefillah."
Inverse case of seif א: without a cup available (neither tonight nor tomorrow), the only way to fulfill havdalah is in tefillah. So one must absolutely repeat it — even to the point of redoing the entire Amidah.

Seif ג — Tisha b'Av falling on Sunday

תשעה באב שחל להיות באחד בשבת טעה ולא הבדיל בתפלה א"צ לחזור ולהתפלל כיון שמבדיל על הכוס במוצאי תשעה באב:
Translation: "Tisha b'Av that falls on Sunday [so Tisha b'Av begins Saturday night, motzaei Shabbat] — if one forgot and did not say havdalah in the tefillah of Maariv, one does not need to repeat the tefillah, since one will make havdalah on the cup at the conclusion of Tisha b'Av [Sunday night]."
Special case: Tisha b'Av on Sunday → one must fast all day Sunday, so no havdalah on the cup Saturday night (prohibited to drink wine during the fast). It is pushed off to motzaei Tisha b'Av (Sunday night, end of the fast). So no urgency to say it in tefillah — it will come anyway Sunday night.

Seif ד — No going back even before the next berachah

במקום שאמרו שאינו חוזר להתפלל מיד כשסיי' הברכ' אין לו לחזור אע"פ שלא פתח בברכה שלאחריה:
Translation: "In a case where one said that one does not repeat the tefillah (= case of seif א, where one has a cup), as soon as one has concluded the berachah [of Atta chonen], one must not go back [to add Atah Chonantanu] — even if one has not yet begun the following berachah."
Strict rule of irreversibility: normally, in tefillah, if one missed an insertion, one can go back as long as the next berachah has not begun. Here (havdalah) it is different: as soon as one concludes "Atta chonen", it is too late, even if one has not begun "Re'eh be-onyeinu". The make-up will be done on the cup.

Seif ה — Optional stringency

במקום שאמרו שאינו חוזר אם רצה להחמיר על עצמו לחזור אם סיים תפלתו רשאי אבל אם עדיין לא סיים תפלתו אינו רשאי לחזור:
Translation: "In a case where one said that one does not repeat, if one wishes to be stringent on oneself and repeat — if the tefillah is entirely completed, one is permitted to do so. But if one has not yet completed one's tefillah, one is not permitted to repeat."
2 strategies for the "hachmara":
  • If tefillah is finished: one may redo the entire Amidah for havdalah — permitted.
  • In the middle: one may not go back — forbidden (= berachah le-vatalah).
Full text: these 5 seifim constitute the entire codification of the Mechaber on this topic. Each one specifies a case, a condition, or an exception.

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Our siman codifies havdalah in the tefillah of Maariv of motzaei Shabbat — the insertion of "Atah Chonantanu" in the 4th berachah of the Amidah ("Atta chonen"). It is the 1st of the 2 havdalos of motzaei Shabbat — the 2nd being on the cup (wine/juice) at home.

Source of havdalah in tefillah: Berachos 33a — "the Anshei Knesses HaGedolah instituted Kiddush and havdalah in tefillah. When they became wealthy, they placed it on the cup; when they became impoverished, they returned it to tefillah. Therefore one says both".

Place in Hilchos Shabbos

  • Siman 293 — Maariv of motzaei Shabbat
  • Siman 294 — Havdalah in tefillah (our siman)
  • Siman 295-298 — Havdalah on the cup, besamim, candle
  • Siman 299-300 — Not eating before havdalah, end of Shabbat

3. Key halachic concepts

1. הבדלה בתפלה (Havdalah in tefillah): insertion of "Atah Chonantanu" in the 4th berachah of the Amidah of Maariv of motzaei Shabbat. First of the 2 havdalos.
2. חונן הדעת (Chonen ha-Da'as): 4th berachah of the Amidah ("Atta chonen le-adam da'as"). Place where havdalah is inserted.
3. אתה חוננתנו (Atah Chonantanu): text of havdalah in tefillah. Mentions 4 separations: kodesh/chol, light/darkness, Israel/nations, 7th day/6 days.
4. הבדלה על הכוס (Havdalah on the cup): second havdalah — on wine/juice, with berachos on the cup, besamim, ner. At home.
5. משלים תפלתו ואינו חוזר: basic rule: if one forgot, one completes the tefillah without repeating — since havdalah on the cup will make up for it. But: exceptions (seif ב, ג).
6. אם טעם קודם שהבדיל: if one tasted before havdalah on the cup → one must repeat the tefillah — since havdalah on the cup is no longer possible (one had to be fasting anyway).

משנה ברורה (ב) — (ב) בחונן הדעת - וקבעוה בברכה זו מפני שאסור לתבוע צרכיו קודם הבדלה. מנהג פשוט לומר אתה חונן וכו' עד לאנוש בינה ואח"כ אתה חוננתנו וכו' וחננו מאתך וכו' [אחרונים] ואם התחיל מאתה חוננתנו ואילך יצא. אם שכח להתפלל מתפלל שחרית שתים ואינו מזכיר אתה חוננתנו לא בראשונה ולא בשניה בד"א כשכבר הבדיל מאתמול על הכוס אבל אם לא הבדיל כלל דיש עליו עדיין חיוב מצות הבדלה יזכיר אתה חוננתנו בשחרית בתפלה שניה שהיא להשלמה…

6. The Rama's position

Wherever the Rama (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) adds a הגהה (gloss), he generally clarifies the Ashkenazi nuances compared to the Sephardi Mechaber. Carefully check the Hebrew text above to identify passages introduced by הגה.

The Rama's position in this siman: the Mechaber and the Rama follow the same halachah on this siman — no significant Ashkenazi gloss. Practice is uniform.

7. Modern practical cases

SituationPractical halachah
Forgot Atah Chonantanu and continued tefillahNo going back; finish tefillah normally. Havdalah on the cup makes up for it.
Realizes in the middle of Atta chonenAs long as one has not said "Hashem chonen ha-da'as" at the end, one may add Atah Chonantanu.
Realizes after "Hashem chonen ha-da'as"Too late, even if one has not yet begun "Re'eh be-onyeinu". Continue (seif ד).
Forgot + no wine or grape juice at homeIf also none for tomorrow, repeat the tefillah (seif ב).
Forgot + no wine but will have some tomorrow morningFinish tefillah normally. Havdalah tomorrow morning on the cup.
Tasted before havdalah on the cupPotential berachah le-vatalah. Repeat the tefillah for havdalah (seif א).
Tisha b'Av on Sunday, forgotNo going back; havdalah Sunday night at the end of the fast (seif ג).
Wants to be stringent and redo everythingOnly if the entire tefillah is finished (seif ה). In the middle = forbidden.
Woman at home, doing havdalah without tefillahFor women who do not say Maariv — havdalah is done only on the cup. No Atah Chonantanu.
Practical rule: in case of forgetting, the priority is havdalah on the cup at home. Tefillah is only repeated if havdalah on the cup is impossible.

8. Practical summary of the Siman

The 5 key lessons of Siman רצ"ד:
  1. Havdalah in tefillah: insertion of "Atah Chonantanu" in the 4th berachah of the Amidah (Chonen ha-Da'as).
  2. Forgot = no going back if one has a cup — havdalah on the cup makes up for it.
  3. Forgot + no cup = repeat the tefillah.
  4. Special case of Tisha b'Av Sunday = no going back (havdalah pushed to Sunday night).
  5. Optional stringency: redo the entire tefillah OK; in the middle forbidden.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. In which berachah of the Amidah is havdalah added? What is the name of the text?
  2. If I forget Atah Chonantanu, must I repeat the tefillah? What is the condition?
  3. In what case must I absolutely repeat the tefillah for havdalah?
  4. Why on Tisha b'Av falling on Sunday do we not repeat even if we forgot?
  5. How far can one go back to add Atah Chonantanu in the current berachah?
  6. If one wants to be stringent and repeat the tefillah, in what case is it permitted?
