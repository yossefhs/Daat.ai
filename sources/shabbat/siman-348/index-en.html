Siman 348 — Handing an object from one reshus to another

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe in the 3 seifim that codify the case of handing an object from one reshus to another on Shabbos (over a border) — fine distinctions based on whether the hand remains in the first reshus or crosses over to the second.
