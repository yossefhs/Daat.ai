1. The Central Axiom

Siman שס"א in one sentence.
A reshus is not defined only by its metzius (10 tefachim of height, mechitzos): it is preserved or degraded also by shimush and by the form of its pirtzos. A roof that is reshus hayachid mide'oraisa, but used by the rabbim, is treated as reshus harabbim — until a sulam kavua demonstrates that the baal habayis has "removed" it from public use.

3. The three statuses of a gag by height

High by 10 tefachim also on the street side → full reshus hayachid → no sulam necessary, the rabbim has no access.
↓
Low (less than 10) on street side, high (10+) on chatzer side → reshus hayachid mide'oraisa, but treated "like" reshus harabbim because the rabbim places kelim on it → assur to use from the chatzer, except with a sulam kavua.
↓
Low (less than 10) on all sides → karmelis → the sulam is useless: the gag was never reshus hayachid.

4. Decision tree

Q1: Is the gag higher than 10 tefachim on the street side?
↓ YES → reshus hayachid, use it; NO ↓
Q2: Is it higher than 10 tefachim on the chatzer side?
↓ NO → karmelis, the sulam is useless; YES ↓
Q3: Has a kavua sulam been installed (be'kvius, for chol and for Shabbos)?
↓ YES → the rabbim is "removed," one may use the gag; NO → assur
→ At the slightest doubt → consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic סֻלָּם

ס — סָמוּךְ וְנָמוּךְ: a roof adjacent to the street and low on that side — the rabbim uses it, it is treated as reshus harabbim.

ל — לְמַעְלָה מֵעֲשָׂרָה לֶחָצֵר: high by 10 tefachim on the chatzer side, it is reshus hayachid mide'oraisa — otherwise it is only karmelis.

ם — מַעֲשֵׂה קֶבַע: a sulam kavua, placed be'kvius for chol and Shabbos, removes the rabbim and permits use.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that a gag treated "like" reshus harabbim actually is one. It is only a derabbanan degradation due to shimush — precisely why a sulam can repair it.
Pitfall 2: Placing a sulam on a gag too low. If the gag does not reach 10 tefachim on the chatzer side, it is karmelis and the sulam changes nothing.
Pitfall 3: Settling for a sulam placed only for Shabbos. A "kavua" sulam must be so for chol as for Shabbos — otherwise it remains arai.
Pitfall 4: Applying פִּי תִקְרָה יוֹרֵד וְסוֹתֵם to a pirtzah in a corner, or under a slanted ceiling. In a corner, there is no "pesach"; under a slanted roof, the tikrah has no "edge" that can be extended.

10. Practical mitzvos of Siman שס"א

For daily conduct

  1. Gag adjacent to the street, low on that side, high on chatzer side: treated as reshus harabbim — assur to use from the chatzer.
  2. The sulam kavua demonstrates that the baal habayis "removes" the gag from public use: it permits use.
  3. "Kavua" means placed be'kvius — for chol as for Shabbos; a one-time sulam does not suffice.
  4. Chatzer breached in its full width or for more than 10 amos toward the street: becomes karmelis; pirtzah in a corner — assur even below 10.
  5. In case of doubt — consult your Rav.
  6. Deep pilpul — Level 2; Shitah of the Alter Rebbe — Level 4.
