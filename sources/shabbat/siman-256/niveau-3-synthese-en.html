1. The central axiom

Shabbos is a communal matter.
Siman רנ״ו codifies the communal dimension of entering Shabbos. Everyone must enter together into the kodesh. For this, a signal is needed — anciently the shofar (6 precise tekiyos per Shabbos 35b), today the shaliach tzibbur's announcement or the municipal siren. The function ("להבדיל את העם מן המלאכה") remains invariant; the means (shofar / voice / siren / SMS) adapts. This is the only siman in Hilchos Shabbos devoted to the public dimension of the transition.

4. Mnemonic — S-K-H

S — Shesh (שש = six): six tekiyos in the ancient practice. Three "function" + three "exit".

K — Kruz (כרוז = announcement): today the verbal announcement replaces the shofar.

H — Hakharazah (הכרזה): the shaliach tzibbur or shamash announces ~30-60 min before Shabbos.

To memorize: "S-K-H for siman 256: Shesh tekiyos in antiquity, Kruz today, Hakharazah of the shaliach tzibbur one hour before."

7. 5 pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that "the communal announcement doesn't concern me if I know the time". False — the collective dimension is the essence of the siman. Even if you know the time, the announcement is your way of entering together with others.
Pitfall 2: Making the announcement just before candle lighting (5 min before). Too late — the Rema recommends 30-60 min, to allow time for preparations. This is the practical spirit of the siman.
Pitfall 3: If you are responsible for a family / shul, forgetting to signal. Missed — this is precisely the role of the modern shaliach tzibbur. Making the announcement is performing the mitzvah.
Pitfall 4: Setting an SMS/app at the exact time of candle lighting. Too late — the alert must arrive before to allow preparation. 30 min before minimum.
Pitfall 5: Traveling or in a hotel — relying on an unverified calendar. Risk — the local sunset time varies according to latitude/longitude. Always check on a reliable Jewish calendar + adjust the alarm accordingly.

10. The 5 practical mitzvos of Siman רנ״ו

For daily conduct

  1. Know the exact time of sunset and candle lighting — reliable Jewish calendar, not estimation.
  2. Allow a margin of 30-60 min before candle lighting for preparations (spirit of the Rema).
  3. Announce collectively — if you are responsible for a family / group / kehillah, make the announcement. It is not redundant — it is the mitzvah.
  4. Listen to communal announcements (sirens, shaliach tzibbur, sms, app) — this is the living practice of siman 256.
  5. Adapt the form to the place — voice, bell, siren, SMS — no matter the instrument, the essential is the announcement function.
