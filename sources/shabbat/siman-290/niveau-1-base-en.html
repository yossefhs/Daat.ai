1. The text of the Shulchan Aruch

Siman ר"צ contains 2 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo) codifying the laws of completing the 100 berachos of Shabbos with fruits.

Seif א — Completing the 100 berachos with fruits, delicacies and fragrances

בשבת ישלים מאה ברכות בפירות. ובו ב סעיפים:
ירבה פירות ומגדים ומיני ריח כדי להשלים מנין מאה ברכות: הגה ואם רגיל בשינת צהרים אל יבטלנו כי עונג הוא לו (טור):
Translation: "One should increase fruits, delicacies and fragrant spices in order to complete the count of 100 berachos [a day]. Rema: And one who is accustomed to the afternoon nap — he should not cancel it, for it is oneg for him (Tur)."
The problem of the 100 berachos on Shabbos: during the week, one easily reaches 100 berachos (3 Amidos × 19 berachos ≈ 57, + birchos hashachar, + meals, + berachos hanehenin). On Shabbos, the Amidah has only 7 berachos — a shortfall of around 18-22 berachos. The siman proposes 3 strategies:
  1. Fruits — beracha borei peri haetz or borei peri haadamah per category.
  2. Delicacies (megadim) — mezonos cakes, candies, etc.
  3. Fragrances (minei reiach) — spices, flowers, incense: hanosen reiach tov bapeiros, borei atzei besamim, etc.
"But do not cancel the nap": the Rema adds an important note. Many mistakenly thought one had to push to eat more and more in order to reach 100 berachos — at the risk of missing the nap, an essential element of oneg Shabbos ("Shabbos shenas taanug" — sleep on Shabbos is delight). The Rema rules: the nap is not to be cancelled.

Seif ב — The "beis midrash" of Shabbos afternoon

אחר סעודת שחרית קובעים מדרש לקרות בנביאים ולדרוש בדברי אגדה ואסור לקבוע סעודה באותה שעה: הגה ופועלים ובעלי בתים שאינן עוסקים בתורה כל ימי שבוע יעסקו יותר בתורה בשבת מתלמידי חכמים העוסקים בתורה כל ימי השבוע ותלמידי חכמים ימשיכו יותר בעונג אכילה ושתיה קצת שהרי מתענגים בלמודם כל ימי השבוע: [ב"י סי' רפ"ח בשם ירושלמי]:
Translation: "After the seudah of shacharis (= the morning meal), one establishes a beis midrash (kovim midrash) to read from the Neviim and expound on words of aggadah. And it is forbidden to set up a meal at that hour. Rema: And laborers and balei batim who do not learn Torah all week — they should learn more Torah on Shabbos than the talmidei chachamim who learn all week; and the talmidei chachamim may have more oneg in eating and drinking — for they delight in their learning all week (Beis Yosef siman 288 in the name of the Yerushalmi)."
The "beis midrash" of Shabbos afternoon: historically, after the morning meal, the community would gather to learn together Neviim and aggadic drashos — often led by the local Rav. This is the origin of today's shiurim of Shabbos afternoon in shuls.
Differential allocation by profile (Rema):
  • Laborers/balei batim (who do not learn during the week) → more Torah on Shabbos.
  • Talmidei chachamim (who learn all week) → more physical oneg on Shabbos (eating, drinking, sleep).
Logic: each one completes on Shabbos what he lacks during the week — the balance of nefesh + guf.
"No meal at that hour": one avoids organizing a true meal (seudah-style) in the middle of the afternoon — to leave time for Torah study. Seudah Shelishis will come later, after Minchah (Siman 291).
Full text: these 2 seifim constitute the entire Mechaber's codification on this topic. Each one specifies a case, a condition or an exception.

2. General context

What is this siman about?

Our short siman (2 seifim) covers 2 topics of Shabbos afternoon:

  1. Completing the 100 daily berachos — natural deficit on Shabbos (shortened Amidah); compensated through fruits, delicacies, fragrances.
  2. The Shabbos afternoon beis midrash — reading Neviim and learning aggadah; no meal at that hour.
The 100 daily berachos: halacha from Dovid HaMelech (Menachos 43b) — every Jew must say or answer Amen to 100 berachos a day. On Shabbos, natural shortfall ≈ 18-22 berachos (short Amidah: 7 berachos × 4 tefilos = 28 vs weekday Amidah: 19 × 3 = 57). The siman proposes to make up the difference.

Place in Hilchos Shabbos

  • Siman 289 — Seudas shacharis (morning)
  • Siman 290 — 100 berachos + afternoon beis midrash (our siman)
  • Siman 291 — Seudah Shelishis
  • Siman 292 — Minchah of Shabbos

3. The key halachic concepts

1. מאה ברכות בכל יום (100 daily berachos): halacha attributed to Dovid HaMelech (Menachos 43b). Every Jew must say or answer Amen to 100 berachos per day.
2. מגדים (Megadim): delicacies, sweets, fine cakes — to distinguish Shabbos.
3. מיני ריח (Minei Reiach): fragrant plants/spices. Beracha: borei atzei besamim, borei minei besamim, etc. Each scent category = one beracha.
4. שינת צהרים (Shenas Tzaharayim): afternoon nap — element of oneg Shabbos. The Rema insists: not to be cancelled.
5. קובעים מדרש (Kovim Midrash): establishing a communal shiur — the source for the Shabbos afternoon shiurim in shuls.
6. Differential allocation (Rema, Yerushalmi):
  • Those who do not learn during the week → more Torah on Shabbos.
  • Talmidei chachamim → more physical oneg on Shabbos.
Logic: nefesh + guf balance over the week.

5. The Mishnah Berurah — first entries

The Mishnah Berurah of Rabbi Yisroel Meir HaKohen (Chofetz Chaim) has 8 entries on this siman. Here are the first ones — to better understand the meaning of the seifim:

משנה ברורה (א) — (א) ירבה בפירות וכו' - וגם בליל ש"ק טוב שישלים בם המאה ברכות:
משנה ברורה (ב) — (ב) מנין מאה ברכות - כי בכל יום מברך אדם מאה ברכות וכדאיתא לעיל בסימן רמ"ו ס"ג עי"ש במ"ב ועתה שנחסר לו כמה ברכות שבתפלת שבת יש רק ז' ברכות וע"כ ישתדל להשלימם:
משנה ברורה (ג) — (ג) אל יבטלנו - ומ"מ לא ירבה בו יותר מדאי שלא יביאנו לידי ביטול תורה שאפילו ת"ח שלומדים כל השבוע שמצוה שיתענגו ביותר וכדלקמיה בהג"ה אין הפי' שיבלו כל היום בתענוגים רק ימשכו יותר בתענוג משאר בני אדם. כתבו הספרים בשם הזוהר שמצוה על האדם לחדש חידושי תורה בשבת. ומי שאינו בר הכי לחדש ילמוד דברים שלא למד עד הנה:

For the full text of the 8 entries, see Sefaria: Mishnah Berurah 290.

6. The position of the Rema

Where the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) adds a hagahah (gloss), he generally specifies Ashkenazi nuances vis-à-vis the Sephardi Mechaber. Check carefully the Hebrew text above to identify the passages introduced by הגה.

Rema's additions in this siman:
  • Seif א: do not cancel the afternoon nap — it is also oneg.
  • Seif ב: the differential allocation — laborers learn more on Shabbos; talmidei chachamim enjoy more physical oneg.

7. Modern practical cases

SituationPractical halacha
Calculating one's Shabbos berachosCount Shacharis + Mussaf + Minchah + Birchas HaMazon (3 seudos) + birchos hashachar + amen to olim and maftir (Siman 284). Often one reaches 80 — shortfall ≈ 20.
Fruits as a strategyApples (haetz), strawberries (haadamah), grapes (hagefen non-Kiddush) — each type one beracha. See Siman 211 on the order of berachos.
Fragrances (besamim, flowers)Smelling cloves → atzei besamim; fresh mint → isvei besamim. Each new category, new beracha.
Shabbos napPermitted and recommended (Rema). "Shabbos shenas taanug" (Pirkei deRabi Eliezer). But not to exceed to the point of missing Minchah + Seudah Shelishis.
Torah shiur after seudahIdeal (Seif ב). Many communities have a daf yomi shiur, Pirkei Avos (summer), aggadah, etc. on Shabbos afternoon.
One who doesn't enjoy learningFor laborers/professionals who don't learn during the week, it's more on Shabbos (Rema). At minimum: read one parashah, listen to a shiur.
Tired talmid chachamPermitted (per Rema) to prioritize physical oneg — nap, longer seudah — since he learns all week.
No fruits / fragrances availableCount berachos with care; answer Amen to every beracha heard (yotza ke-yotzei).
Practical rule: Shabbos afternoon = balance of Torah + oneg. Each one according to his profile. No extremism — neither "all Torah" that cancels the nap, nor "all nap" that cancels Torah study.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The 4 key teachings of Siman ר"צ:
  1. Completing the 100 berachos through fruits, delicacies, fragrances — to make up the Shabbos shortfall.
  2. Do not cancel the afternoon nap (Rema) — it is oneg.
  3. Shabbos afternoon beis midrash: Neviim + aggadah, no meal at that hour.
  4. Differential profile: laborers → more Torah; talmidei chachamim → more physical oneg.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Why is there a shortfall of berachos on Shabbos? How much is missing?
  2. What are the 3 ways to compensate? Give an example for each.
  3. Why does the Rema insist on the nap? What is the source?
  4. What is the "Shabbos afternoon beis midrash" and what is its content?
  5. What is the Rema's difference of approach between laborers and talmidei chachamim?
  6. May one have a real meal after the morning meal and before Minchah?
