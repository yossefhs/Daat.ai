Siman 288 — Fasting and Ta'anis Chalom on Shabbos

The Alter Rebbe's shitah on the 14 se'ifim that codify the general prohibition of fasting on Shabbos (oneg Shabbos!), with limited exceptions: ta'anis chalom (a fast after a distressing dream), Yom Kippur (which overrides oneg Shabbos), and more.
