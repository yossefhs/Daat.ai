Siman רמ״ו · Level 1 — Lending and Renting Kelim to a Non-Jew for Shabbat

Accessible version — text, translation and pedagogical explanation
Section 1
The text of the Shulchan Aruch
שולחן ערוך, אורח חיים, סימן רמ״ו, סעיף א׳ — הטקסט המלא
מֻתָּר לְהַשְׁאִיל וּלְהַשְׂכִּיר כֵּלִים לְגוֹי, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁעוֹשֶׂה בָּהֶם מְלָאכָה בְּשַׁבָּת, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁאֵין אָנוּ מְצֻוִּים עַל שְׁבִיתַת כֵּלִים. בַּמֶּה דְּבָרִים אֲמוּרִים? כְּשֶׁהִשְׁאִילָן וְהִשְׂכִּירָן לוֹ מִקֹּדֶם הַשַּׁבָּת. אֲבָל בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת, סָמוּךְ לַחֲשֵׁכָה, אָסוּר לְהַשְׂכִּירָן אוֹ לְהַשְׁאִילָן לוֹ, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁנִּרְאֶה כְּאִלּוּ הוּא שְׁלוּחוֹ לַעֲשׂוֹת מְלָאכָה. וְאָסוּר לְהַשְׂכִּירָן לוֹ אֲפִלּוּ מִקֹּדֶם הַשַּׁבָּת בִּסְכַר יוֹם, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁנִּרְאֶה כְּמַשְׂתַּכֵּר בְּשַׁבָּת, אֲבָל בְּהַבְלָעָה — מֻתָּר.
הגה׃ וְהַשְׁאָלָה מֻתֶּרֶת אֲפִלּוּ עֶרֶב שַׁבָּת סָמוּךְ לַחֲשֵׁכָה, רַק שֶׁלֹּא יְקַבֵּל שָׂכָר אִם הוּא כְּלִי שֶׁמְּלַאכְתּוֹ לְאִסּוּר. וְכָל זֶה בְּשֶׁאֵין הַיִּשְׂרָאֵל מַצְוֵהוּ עַל הַמְּלָאכָה.
Full English translation
It is permitted to lend (lehash'il / השאלה) and to rent out (lehaskir / השכרה) kelim to a non-Jew, even though he does melakha with them on Shabbat, because we are not commanded regarding the resting of kelim (shvitat kelim / שביתת כלים).

When does this apply? When they were lent or rented out to him before Shabbat. But on erev Shabbat, close to nightfall (samukh la-chasheikha), it is forbidden to rent or lend them out, because it would appear as though he is the Yid's shaliach (שלוחו) doing the melakha for him.

And it is forbidden to rent them out even before Shabbat at a daily rate (sekhar yom / סכר יום), because it appears as earning wages on Shabbat (mishtaker be-Shabbat / sekhar Shabbat). But through הבלעה (havla'ah — bundling several days into one combined fee) — it is permitted.
Hagahah of the Rema (for Ashkenazim): And lending (sheilah / השאלה) is permitted even on erev Shabbat close to nightfall, provided he does not receive payment, if it is a kli she-melakhto le-issur (כלי שמלאכתו לאיסור — a kli whose typical use is something forbidden on Shabbat). And all of this applies only when the Yid does not instruct the non-Jew to do the melakha.
⚖
Two sources of the issur — must be distinguished
① Samukh la-chasheikha (close to nightfall) — gezeira of nireh ki-shlucho (נראה כשלוחו, appearance of agency): it looks like the non-Jew is the Yid's agent.
② Sekhar yom (daily rate) — gezeira of sekhar Shabbat (Shabbat wages): the Yid earns money from melakha done on Shabbat.
Havla'ah (bundling) dissolves the second problem: when the rental fee covers several days and is not broken down day-by-day, the "Shabbat wages" disappear.
Section 2
Understanding the halakha

What is this Seif about?

This Seif deals with a very common situation: a Yid owns tools, a car, professional equipment. May he rent them out or lend them to a non-Jew, knowing the non-Jew will use them on Shabbat?

The answer depends on what the Yid knows at the time of the lending or rental. If the Yid knows the kelim will be used on Shabbat — it is forbidden. If he does not know — it is permitted.

💡
Why is it forbidden?
The main reason is sekhar Shabbat — the Yid receives rental money for a day when his object "worked" on Shabbat. Even though the Yid himself did nothing, benefiting from the melakha done on Shabbat is forbidden.
📜
The Rema's ruling for Ashkenazim
The Rema goes further than the Shulchan Aruch: not only is it assur to rent out, but also to lend free of charge — even though the Yid receives nothing. Why? Because even a free loan creates the appearance that the Yid is sending his shaliach to do melakha on Shabbat (nireh ki-shlucho).
⏰
When must the kli be handed over?
The issur applies even if the kli is handed over on erev Shabbat (Friday). The fact that the contract was made before Shabbat does not permit the Shabbat use. The key is the Yid's knowledge: did he know the kli would be used on Shabbat?
Section 3
The 3 fundamental concepts
①
שְׁבִיתַת כֵּלִים
Shvitat kelim (resting of kelim)
According to Beit Shammai, a Yid's kelim must "rest" on Shabbat, even in the hands of a non-Jew. Beit Hillel (whom we follow le-halakha) hold there is no chiyuv of shvitat kelim per se — but other issurim still apply.
②
נִרְאֶה כִּשְׁלוּחוֹ
Appearance of agency
If a Yid lends his object to a non-Jew who uses it on Shabbat publicly, people might think the non-Jew is acting as the Yid's shaliach — as if the Yid himself were having someone work in his place. This appearance is forbidden.
③
שְׂכַר שַׁבָּת
Sekhar Shabbat (Shabbat wages)
The issur of taking financial benefit from melakha performed during Shabbat. Even if the rental contract was signed before Shabbat, the Yid receives money for an "active" day of Shabbat — this is problematic.
Section 4
The practical pesak
פסק הלכה — Final ruling
אסור להשכיר או להשאיל כלים לגוי כשיודע שישתמש בהם בשבת
For all (Sephardim and Ashkenazim): It is forbidden to rent out one's kelim to a non-Jew when one knows he will use them on Shabbat.

For Ashkenazim (Rema): It is also forbidden to lend one's kelim, even free of charge, under the same conditions.
Section 5
The key concept: הבלעה (havla'ah)

Why havla'ah permits the rental

Sekhar Shabbat is assur because it "appears" as income directly tied to melakha done on Shabbat. But when the rental fee is bundled into an overall price covering several days, the Shabbat profit is "absorbed" into the mass — it is no longer perceived as a specific gain from Shabbat.

This is analogous to the principle of batel be-rov (בטל ברוב — nullification in the majority): something minor and indistinguishable is neutralized.

✅
Permitted example (successful havla'ah)
Renting out a tool for 7 days for €100 total — the Shabbat profit is bundled into the package, not isolated. Mutar.
❌
Forbidden example (sekhar yom)
Renting out the same tool at €15 per day, which means €15 for Shabbat alone. The Shabbat gain is isolated, perceptible — assur.
⚠
Limit: Shabbat must not be the main element
Renting for Shabbat alone, or for Shabbat + Sunday where Shabbat is the main part, is not considered true havla'ah — the essence of sekhar Shabbat remains dominant.
Section 6
Lending vs renting — the Rema's חידוש

The Rema's distinction: השאלה ≠ שכירות

The Rema clearly distinguishes two situations that do not follow the same rule:

  • Sheilah / השאלה (lending without compensation) — permitted even on erev Shabbat close to nightfall, provided it is not a kli she-melakhto le-issur (a kli whose typical use violates Shabbat).
  • Sekhirut / שכירות (renting with compensation) — always assur at sekhar yom (daily rate); permitted only with havla'ah.
Case Status
Lending (free) before Shabbat — neutral kli✅ Mutar
Lending (free) before Shabbat — כלי מלאכה⚠️ Disputed (Rema stricter)
Lending (free) close to nightfall — neutral kli✅ Mutar
Lending (free) close to nightfall — כלי מלאכה❌ ASSUR (Rema)
Renting at a daily rate (sekhar yom)❌ Always ASSUR
Renting via הבלעה (multi-day bundle)✅ Mutar
Section 7
The final pesak — Sephardi vs Ashkenazi
🕎
Sephardi — Mechaber
Follows Rambam / Rif / Rosh: no chiyuv of שביתת כלים. Thus fewer restrictions on כלי מלאכה. Permits lending close to nightfall even for tools typically "le-issur." Source: the Shulchan Aruch text as is.
❄️
Ashkenazi — Rema
Adds a stricter chiddush (following Tossafot in part): for כלי מלאכה (tools whose typical use violates Shabbat), it is assur to lend close to nightfall, even free of charge. And for renting, he keeps his chiddush on havla'ah.
Section 8
Modern practical cases
Assur
I rent out my car to my non-Jewish neighbor Friday night, knowing he will use it on Shabbat.
I know he will use it on Shabbat → sekhar Shabbat + nireh ki-shlucho. Assur even according to the Shulchan Aruch.
Assur (Ashkenazim)
I lend my tools for free close to nightfall to a non-Jew who uses them on Shabbat.
Even without compensation, assur per the Rema due to nireh ki-shlucho (for כלי מלאכה).
Mutar
I rent out my drill for the week to a contractor (flat 7-day package), and he uses it every day.
Rental via הבלעה — the Shabbat profit is bundled into the overall package. Mutar.
Mutar
I lend my book to a non-Jewish neighbor on Thursday, and he reads it on Shabbat.
Free loan, well before Shabbat, on a neutral object (not a כלי מלאכה). Mutar for all.
Mutar
I rent out my drill to a contractor, without knowing he will work on Shabbat.
If the Yid does not know the kli will be used on Shabbat, it is mutar (אם אינו יודע, מותר).
Assur
I rent out my camera at €30 per day to a non-Jew for Shabbat alone.
Daily rate (סכר יום) isolated on Shabbat → sekhar Shabbat. Assur even without knowing the precise use.
Ask a Rav
I rent an apartment to a non-Jew for several months (including Shabbatot).
Long-term rental — specific halakhot apply (השכרת בית), often mutar but consult a Rav.
Ask a Rav
I give my car to a non-Jew so he can make a paid delivery (which includes Shabbat).
More complex: issues of שליחות + sekhar Shabbat. Depends on the contract and the Yid's knowledge. Ask a Rav.
Section 9
To remember — golden rules
The 4 essential rules
① Lending before Shabbat = OK
No chiyuv of resting the kelim. Lending or renting in advance (Thursday, Wednesday…) is mutar according to Beit Hillel.

② Close to nightfall = ASSUR to rent out
So as not to appear to be sending the non-Jew to do melakha in our place (nireh ki-shlucho).

③ Sekhar yom = ALWAYS ASSUR
Daily rate = sekhar Shabbat. הבלעה (multi-day bundle) solves the problem.

④ The Rema adds a chumra for כלי מלאכה
For kelim whose typical use violates Shabbat, lending free of charge close to nightfall is also assur (Ashkenazi).
