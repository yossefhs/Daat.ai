Siman 301 — Going out on Shabbos with clothing and objects

The Alter Rebbe's shitah on the 63 se'ifim codifying the large corpus of going out into the reshus harabim on Shabbos with clothing, accessories, and items — distinguishing between hotza'ah (carrying) and an accessory worn in the normal way.
