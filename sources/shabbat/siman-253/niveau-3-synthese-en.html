1. The central axiom

שמא יחתה: the fear of the poker shapes the entire siman.
Siman רנ״ג codifies a single halakhic fear — שמא יחתה בגחלים — declined into three questions: (1) shehiya — may one leave a pot on the fire Friday for Shabbat? (2) hazara — may one return it on Shabbat? (3) hatmana — may one wrap it? The answer to each depends on three crossed variables: fuel (גפת/עצים vs קש/גבבא), vessel (kira / kupach / tanur), state of the tavshil (raw / ben drussai / fully cooked / מצטמק ויפה לו / מצטמק ורע לו). The modern plata ≈ gerufa u-ketuma — no embers to stoke, fixed heat.

2. The 3 questions of the siman (S-H-H)

Shehiya — Leaving on the fire Friday for Shabbat

  • Permitted without restriction if: (a) plata = gerufa u-ketuma, or (b) tavshil cooked fully (כל צורכו) and מצטמק ורע לו, or (c) tavshil entirely raw (חי לגמרי)
  • Mahloket (Hananya/Hakhamim): does מאכל בן דרוסאי suffice, or is כל צורכו required?
  • Practice: Rama+Chabad — minhag lehakel (Hananya); Sephardim — Hakhamim (כל צורכו)

Hazara — Returning the pot on Shabbat

  • 5 cumulative conditions (Mehaber + Rama)
  • Tavshil cooled completely → forbidden (= new cooking)
  • Inside the oven (לתוכה) → always forbidden, even gaduf v'katum

Hatmana — Burying/wrapping

  • Friday: permitted if not מוסיף הבל
  • Shabbat: forbidden even if not מוסיף הבל
  • Full detail: siman רנ״ז

4. Mnemonic — S-H-K

S — Shehiya: leave on Friday → check autonomy (plata = gerufa u-ketuma) and state of the tavshil (≥ ben drussai, or raw, or מצטמק ורע לו).

H — Hazara: return on Shabbat → 5 conditions (gerufa, rotech, in hand, intention, on the kira).

K — Ketuma (gerufa u-ketuma): the transverse condition of the siman — no active embers, no organ to adjust. The modern plata is assimilated to it.

(Original French mnemonic: S-H-K — Shehiya · Hazara · Kotoumah)

To memorize: "S-H-K for siman 253: Shehiya (leave) before Shabbat, Hazara (return) on Shabbat with 5 conditions, all on Ketuma (plata)."

5. Decision tree

Question 1: Is the tavshil cooked כל צורכו before the entry of Shabbat?
↓
If YES + plata → permitted לכל הדעות
↓
If NO but ≥ ben drussai → permitted per Hananya/Rama (minhag lehakel)
↓
If NO and < ben drussai → tavshil must be entirely raw (chai legamre); otherwise forbidden
↓
Question 2: Returning on Shabbat? Check the 5 conditions (gerufa / rotech / in hand / intention / on the kira)
↓
If YES to all 5 → permitted
↓
If cooled or placed on the floor → forbidden לכתחילה; bediavad per Rama
↓
Question 3: No burying on Shabbat (blankets, isothermal pot)
↓
Question 4: No liquid added on Shabbat to the hot tavshil (Seif Daled)
↓
✓ PERMITTED if all questions answered correctly

7. 5 pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that the plata solves everything. False — the plata = gerufa u-ketuma, but one still needs (a) the tavshil to have reached the cooking threshold before Shabbat, and (b) the conditions of hazara to be respected when returning.
Pitfall 2: Returning a cooled pot to the plata on Shabbat. Forbidden לכל הדעות — it is a new cooking (bishul belach / yavesh depending on the content).
Pitfall 3: Pouring hot water (from the מיחם) into the chamin/cholent that has reduced too much. Forbidden (Seif Daled) — בישול אחר בישול בלח.
Pitfall 4: Covering the pot on Shabbat with a blanket/pillow to keep heat. Forbidden — הטמנה במוסיף הבל in the middle of Shabbat.
Pitfall 5: Setting the slow cooker on Friday with raw meat that will be כמאכל בן דרוסאי after the entry of Shabbat. Forbidden — active cooking on Shabbat passes through שמא יחתה. Solution: start earlier on Friday so the tavshil is cooked כל צורכו before the entry.

10. The 5 practical commandments of Siman רנ״ג

For daily conduct

  1. Cook completely on Friday — reach at least מאכל בן דרוסאי before entry (Chabad/Rama); better כל צורכו (יוצא ידי כל הדעות).
  2. Plata = gerufa u-ketuma — non-adjustable, with no accessible organ. Prefer Shabbat-certified models.
  3. Hazara: 5 conditions — gerufa + rotech (hot) + in hand + intention + on the kira (not inside).
  4. No liquid poured on Shabbat into the hot tavshil (Seif Daled); no active cooking.
  5. No burying on Shabbat — blankets, pillows, isothermal pot: hatmana forbidden.
