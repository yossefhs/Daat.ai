1. The central axiom

Siman שס״ח in one sentence.
Once the eruv has been made, it may be spoiled, diminish or become moldy: the siman says when the residents must be informed in order to redo or complete it (seifim א and ב), how much food the eruv must contain at its outset (seif ג), and what becomes of a diminished eruv (seif ד) and of moldy eruv bread (seif ה). Beneath seifim א and ב, one notion recurs in the Mishnah Berurah: what is redone or added can count as an eruv made from the outset. For a consumed eruv redone from another kind, « דכיון שכבר כלה המין הראשון הו״ל כמערב לכתחלה » (משנה ברורה שס״ח ס״ק ד); for residents who were added, « דהוספה זו ג״כ כתחלת עירוב הוא » (משנה ברורה שס״ח ס״ק ט).

3. Informing the residents — the cases of seifim א and ב, in their order

The setting → according to the Mishnah Berurah, seif א speaks of the first Shabbos: « זה מיירי בשבת ראשון ונתקלקל קודם שנכנס השבת » (משנה ברורה שס״ח ס״ק א).
↓
Redone from the same kind → no need to inform: « אם בא לערב ממין הראשון אפי כלה א״צ להודיע ואפי׳ מערב עליהם משלהם » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:א).
↓
From another kind, the first being consumed → inform if one uses what is theirs: « צריך להודיע להם אם מערב משלהם » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:א); from one's own: « ואם משלו א״צ להודיעם כלל » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:א). Whom to inform: the Mishnah Berurah reports the Maamar Mordechai, who limits this duty to a house that opens onto two courtyards, and adds that other Acharonim write the same (ס״ק ד); it also reports that the starting rule is itself disputed: « יש פוסקים דסברי דדוקא אם מערב משלו » (משנה ברורה שס״ח ס״ק ט); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav: « צָרִיךְ לְהוֹדִיעַ לְכָל מִי שֶׁצָּרִיךְ לְהוֹדִיעַ לוֹ בִּתְחִלַּת עֵרוּב » (שו״ע הרב שס״ח:א), and earlier in the same seif he gives as an example « כְּגוֹן שֶׁבֵּיתוֹ פָּתוּחַ לִשְׁתֵּי חֲצֵרוֹת » (שו״ע הרב שס״ח:א).
↓
The first only diminished → no need to inform: « ואם לא כלה הראשון אלא נתמעט אע״פ שמערב משלהם א״צ להודיעם » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:א).
↓
Residents were added → « אם הבית פתוח לשתי חצרות צריך להודיעם מפני שאולי אינם רוצים לערב עם אלו » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:ב).

4. Decision tree — a spoiled or diminished eruv

Q1: Was the eruv spoiled, or did it diminish, after the first Shabbos had begun?
↓ YES → for this Shabbos, « א״צ שום תיקון לשבת זו » (משנה ברורה שס״ח ס״ק א); for the following ones, any remnant suffices (seif ד); when the eruv and the shituf are made together, the Magen Avraham, reported by the Mishnah Berurah, declares such a remnant invalid, and the Biur Halachah concludes that one may be lenient in a time of need (בשעת הדחק); in the first place, complete the measure (ס״ק ט״ז); if residents were added after it diminished below its measure, one must add for them (שו״ע או״ח שס״ו:ט); if it is entirely consumed, it must be made anew for the following ones (ס״ק א), and likewise if it became moldy and unfit to eat (seif ה; entirely moldy, according to the Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ט). NO, it was before Shabbos began ↓
Q2: Is it redone from the same kind?
↓ YES → no need to inform, even if the first is consumed and even from what is theirs (seif א); NO ↓
Q3: Is the first consumed?
↓ NO, only diminished → no need to inform, even from what is theirs (seif א); YES ↓
Q4: Does one use what belongs to the residents?
↓ YES → they must be informed; the Mishnah Berurah reports the Maamar Mordechai and other Acharonim: only a house that opens onto two courtyards (ס״ק ד), and it also reports poskim for whom an eruv without the residents' consent is made only from one's own (ס״ק ט); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav says: those whom one informs at the start of an eruv, and gives as an example, in that seif, one whose house opens onto two courtyards (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:1); NO, from one's own → no need to inform (seif א)
And if residents were added → if the house opens onto two courtyards, one must inform (seif ב). → The layout of the houses and courtyards also decides: consult your Rav.

5. The Mechaber and the Alter Rebbe — what the comparison of the texts shows

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav (of the Alter Rebbe) has 4 seifim for this siman, the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber 5: the Rav's seif 3 carries what the Mechaber says in his seifim ג and ד, and his seif 4 corresponds to seif ה and the Rema's gloss. The table sets side by side, point by point, the text of the Mechaber and the Rema and that of the Rav; it does not examine the commentators one by one, whom Level 2 studies. Not everything the Rav writes is a difference: when the Rav names, among those who need not be informed, one who must be informed at the start of the eruv, for example one whose house opens onto two courtyards (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:1), he spells out the Mechaber's seif א, which makes no distinction between people; and that an eruv which diminished below its measure before Shabbos began is invalid even if a small whole loaf remains (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:3) already follows from the Mechaber (שו״ע או״ח שס״ו:ו; שס״ח:ד). When a commentator already has a point, he is named — the Mishnah Berurah, later than the Rav, too —, as in Level 4. For the condition on the residents' bread, for the added residents, for remnants that are a piece and for a loaf broken before Shabbos began, some commentators already read what the Rav writes into the Mechaber's words, and others hold otherwise; and whom to inform when redoing from another kind, the Mishnah Berurah reports for this case the view of the Maamar Mordechai, while the Magen Avraham and the Mishnah Berurah write or report other distinctions for other cases, from which this page does not derive the answer for this one: this page does not decide whether it is a difference from the Mechaber or a reading of his text.

The limit itself, the onset of the first Shabbos, is already in the Mechaber's seif ד

The Taz already writes both (ט״ז או״ח שס״ח ס״ק ד)

6. Mnemonic מכם · שמע

The three distinctions of seif א, in the order the seif introduces them, form מכם (from you) — and that is the question: does one use what belongs to the residents?

מ — מין הראשון: from the same kind, no need to inform (seif א).

כ — כלה: from another kind, the first being consumed, one must inform if one uses what is theirs (see ם); only diminished, no (seif א).

ם — משלהם (final mem): one informs them if one uses what is theirs; from one's own, no (seif א).

Then, for seifim ג, ד and ה, שמע:

A device of the site: each letter is taken from the key word of the distinction, and is not always its initial (the final ם of משלהם, the ע of נתעפש).

ש — שיעור העירוב בתחלתו: the measure is set at the outset (seif ג).

מ — נתמעט מכשיעורו: after the first Shabbos has begun, any remnant suffices (seif ד).

ע — נתעפש פת העירוב: as if consumed, the eruv must be made anew (seif ה).

Seif ב, the added residents, stands apart: the house that opens onto two courtyards.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Extending the exemption from informing to every redone eruv. Seif א gives it notably for the same kind and for an eruv that is only diminished; but from another kind, the first being consumed, « צריך להודיע להם אם מערב משלהם » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:א).
Pitfall 2: Conversely, requiring that they be informed for a consumed eruv redone from another kind when one uses one's own: « ואם משלו א״צ להודיעם כלל » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:א); according to the Mishnah Berurah, because the residents give nothing (ס״ק ה).
Pitfall 3: Forgetting that seif ג reports another view, eight eggs, which the Mishnah Berurah considers proper to follow in the first place. The seif gives six without attribution, then eight under וי״א: « שהם כששה ביצים וי״א שהם כשמונה ביצים » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:ג); and the Mishnah Berurah: « ולכתחלה נכון לנהוג כן ובדיעבד די כששה ביצים » (משנה ברורה שס״ח ס״ק י״ב). The site does not convert these measures into present-day units: consult your Rav for their equivalent.
Pitfall 4: Applying seif ד to an eruv that diminished before the first Shabbos began. The seif says « נתמעט מכשיעורו אחר שנכנס שבת ראשונ׳ ונשתייר » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ח:ד); and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav: « אֲבָל אִם נִתְמַעֵט מִשִּׁעוּרוֹ קֹדֶם כְּנִיסַת הַשַּׁבָּת, אַף אִם הַמְּשֻׁיָּר הוּא כִּכָּר קָטָן שָׁלֵם — פָּסוּל » (שו״ע הרב שס״ח:ג).
Pitfall 5: Presenting the any-remnant rule of seif ד without the Mishnah Berurah's reservation. It reports the Magen Avraham, according to whom this rule held when the courtyard eruv and the alley shituf were each made separately, and not when they are made together; it notes that the Biur Halachah reports dissenting views, and concludes: « ולכתחלה בודאי צריך ליזהר להוסיף עד כשיעור » (משנה ברורה שס״ח ס״ק ט״ז).
Pitfall 6: Ascribing to the Rema a preference for the eruv every Erev Shabbos. The Rema considers the matzah better, « שלא ישכחו מלערב » (רמ״א או״ח שס״ח:ה), and leaves free whoever wants to make the eruv every week. The opposite view is that of the Taz (ט״ז או״ח שס״ח ס״ק ד), of the Acharonim reported by the Mishnah Berurah, « והאחרונים כתבו דיותר טוב לערב כל ע״ש » (משנה ברורה שס״ח ס״ק כ״א), and that of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:4).
Pitfall 7: Mixing the two numberings. The Mechaber has 5 seifim, the Shulchan Aruch HaRav 4: a reference 368:3 to the Rav covers the measure and the remnants, which are the Mechaber's seifim ג and ד; 368:4 to the Rav corresponds to seif ה and the Rema's gloss.

10. The practical commandments of Siman שס״ח

For daily conduct

  1. A spoiled eruv redone from the same kind: without informing the residents, even if the first is consumed and even from what is theirs (seif א). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes in this seif that one does not take their bread from their houses without their knowledge: the members of the household give it (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:1); the Mechaber lays down the same rule in the previous siman, without attaching it to this case (שו״ע או״ח שס״ז:א); the Taz connects it to seif א, and the Biur Halachah notes that many Acharonim contested him (ביאור הלכה שס״ח).
  2. From another kind, the first being consumed: inform if one uses what is theirs; from one's own, no (seif א). Whom to inform: the Mishnah Berurah reports the Maamar Mordechai and other Acharonim: only a house that opens onto two courtyards (ס״ק ד), and it also reports poskim for whom an eruv without the residents' consent is made only from one's own (ס״ק ט); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav says: those whom one informs at the start of an eruv, and gives as an example, in that seif, one whose house opens onto two courtyards (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:1).
  3. The first only diminished: without informing, even from what is theirs (seif א).
  4. Residents added to the courtyard: if the house opens onto two courtyards, inform (seif ב); according to the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, even if one grants them a share of one's own (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:2); the Mishnah Berurah also reports a more lenient opinion (ס״ק ט); and according to the Magen Avraham, when they are in a single alley and one makes the eruv from what is theirs, they must also be informed (מגן אברהם שס״ח ס״ק א).
  5. The measure at the outset: a dried fig per person up to eighteen; beyond that, two meals — six eggs, and another opinion says eight (seif ג); the Mishnah Berurah: it is proper in the first place to follow eight; six suffice after the fact (ס״ק י״ב).
  6. After the first Shabbos has begun, any remnant suffices for the following Shabbosos (seif ד); when the eruv and the shituf are made together, the Magen Avraham, reported by the Mishnah Berurah, declares it invalid, and the Biur Halachah concludes that one may be lenient in a time of need (בשעת הדחק); in the first place, complete the measure (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט״ז). Consult your Rav. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav: even a piece; a loaf broken before Shabbos began remains valid if its measure remains until Shabbos begins, and for this case the Mishnah Berurah reports stricter authorities (ס״ק י״ז); but if it diminished below its measure before Shabbos began, it is invalid as it stands (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:3): one completes it up to its measure before Shabbos begins (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:1). If residents were added after the eruv diminished below its measure, one must add for them (שו״ע או״ח שס״ו:ט; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 366:12).
  7. Moldy eruv bread is as if consumed: make the eruv anew (seif ה); when it is entirely moldy (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ט).
  8. The matzah or the eruv every Erev Shabbos: the Rema considers the matzah better and leaves the eruv every Erev Shabbos to whoever wants it (seif ה); the Taz (ט״ז או״ח שס״ח ס״ק ד), the Acharonim reported by the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״א) and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 368:4) prefer making the eruv every Erev Shabbos.
  9. In case of doubt — about the state of the eruv, the measure or the layout of the courtyards: consult your Rav.
