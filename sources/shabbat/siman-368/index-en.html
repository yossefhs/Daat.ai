When the Eruv Is Spoiled After It Was Made

5 seifim in the Shulchan Aruch, with a gloss of the Rema in seif ה; 4 seifim in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav. When must the residents be informed in order to redo or complete the eruv? How much must it contain, and what of its remnants and of moldy bread?
