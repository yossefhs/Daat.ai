The text of the Shulchan Aruch — selections

יִשְׂרָאֵל וְנָכְרִי שֶׁנִּשְׁתַּתְּפוּ — אִם הִתְנוּ בִּתְחִלָּה שֶׁהַשָּׂכָר שֶׁל יוֹם הַשַּׁבָּת יִהְיֶה לַנָּכְרִי לְבַדּוֹ — מֻתָּר. וְאִם לֹא הִתְנוּ — אָסוּר.

1. Introduction to the sougya — Avodah Zarah 22a

"יִשְׂרָאֵל וְנָכְרִי שֶׁקִּבְּלוּ שָׂדֶה בְּשֻׁתָּפוּת — לֹא יֹאמַר הַיִּשְׂרָאֵל לַנָּכְרִי טוֹל אַתָּה חֶלְקְךָ בְּשַׁבָּת וַאֲנִי בְּחֹל. וְאִם הִתְנוּ מִתְּחִלָּה — מֻתָּר".

(Avodah Zarah 22a)

Root of the sougya: The Beraisa establishes that the very partnership (shutfus) is not enough on its own — a tnai me'ikara (prior stipulation) is required. If one says only now "tol atah chelkecha b'Shabbos" — assur. If they agreed at the outset — muttar. The difference is when the contract was sealed. Tnai me'ikara structures the division from the foundation.

2. Chakira in tnai me'ikara — essential or technical?

Chakira: Why does the stipulation me'ikara make a difference?

  • Tzad alef — essential: The stipulation structures the shutfus. Without a tnai, the Shabbos profits belong to both — and the Jew becomes like a shaliach (agent). With a tnai, the Shabbos profits are solely the goy's — no shlichus.
  • Tzad beis — technical: The stipulation removes the Jew's desire for the Shabbos profits. Without a tnai, there is a desire for the profit, even if in practice he will get compensation. With a tnai, there is no desire.
The Beis Yosef leans toward Tzad alef: essential. Tnai me'ikara changes the very structure of the shutfus, not merely the intent.

The Ran combines them — Tzad alef as the foundation, Tzad beis as the justification.

3. Shittos when no prior stipulation was made — bedieved

Two bedieved scenarios:

  • Late stipulation: They say afterwards "Shabbos profits for you, X profits for me" — this helps only going forward, not retroactively.
  • No stipulation at all: The Shabbos profits are assur to the Jew. He must offset (lekazez).

4. Chakira in mar'is ayin in commerce

Kashya: If there is a tnai me'ikara, why is there still a problem of mar'is ayin with an open shop?

Teiruts: Two layers:

  1. Tnai — resolves the question of who is entitled to the profit. The Jew does not receive Shabbos profits.
  2. Mar'is ayin — remains. The public sees the shop bearing the Jew's name open on Shabbos — and will think he is working on Shabbos. A communal problem, even if the private dimension is resolved.

6. Joint shop on Shabbos — chiddush of the Beis Yosef

The Beis Yosef (end of Siman רמ״ה): He notes the minhag of Ashkenazic communities — joint commerce with a goy where the shop is open even on Shabbos and the goy sells. If a tnai me'ikara exists, it is muttar. But the Mishnah Berurah notes that one should be machmir on mar'is ayin in public commerce.

7. Modern automated partnership

Chakira: A shutfus in a business that runs automatically (e-commerce, automated campaign) — is a tnai me'ikara required?

  • Tzad alef — yes: Every commercial shutfus requires a tnai. Automation does not change the obligation.
  • Tzad beis — no: If no partner performs any personal melakha on Shabbos, there is no problem at all. The tnai is meant for a case where there is action.
Contemporary poskim (Chabad, R' Ovadia): They require a tnai me'ikara even in an automated shutfus — because the profit itself, even if produced automatically, enters a joint account.

8. Practical modern nafka minos

1. Jewish-goy restaurant partnership: Tnai me'ikara in the contract: 100% of the Shabbos profits to the goy, X profits (of comparable value) to the Jew. The Jew may not work / be in the restaurant on Shabbos.

2. E-commerce with a goy partner: Tnai me'ikara is critical. In addition — the Jew does not handle orders on Shabbos.

3. Market investment (stocks, funds): Not an operational shutfus — exempt from tnai me'ikara. The company's Shabbos profits are not performed by the Jew.

4. Startup with a goy investor: Depends on the structure. If the Jew holds 51% and is an active CEO — operational shutfus, tnai required. If he is only a passive founder — less so.

5. Physical shop with a goy partner, open 7/7: Tnai me'ikara + mar'is ayin → best to close on Shabbos, or keep it open only with a prominent goy name.

6. AirBnB / apartment rental through a partnership: Tnai me'ikara. Shabbos reservations = profit to the goy. The Jew does not intervene.

7. B2B electronic commerce: Tnai + Shabbos orders processed automatically.

8. Joint real estate management: Depends on whether the shutfus is active (collecting rent, maintenance) or passive. Active — tnai. Passive — not critical.
