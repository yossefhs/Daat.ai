1. The central axiom

The woman lights the Shabbos light — and enters Shabbos together with it.
Siman רס״ג codifies the central mitzvah of the Jewish home. Three axes: (1) Universal mitzvah (men and women obligated, even paupers), with feminine priority; (2) Technical act (2 wicks Zakhor/Shamor, brachah with covered eyes, position of the candles); (3) Acceptance — cardinal חידוש of the Bahag: the lighting is in itself a halachic acceptance of Shabbos. The woman enters Shabbos at the moment she recites the brachah. This unique synthesis between the physical (the flame) and the spiritual (entering kedushah) makes siman 263 the central pivot of Hilchos Shabbos.

2. The 3 axes of the siman

Axis A — Universal mitzvah (Se'ifim 1-3)

  • Beautiful candle; 2 wicks Zakhor/Shamor; 3-4 OK (Rema)
  • Men and women obligated; even a pauper begs to obtain oil
  • Women take priority; Shabbos candle before Chanukah candle (shalom bayis)

Axis B — Technical act (Se'ifim 4-9)

  • Not too early, not too late (unless one accepts Shabbos early ≥ פלג המנחה)
  • Brachah + minhag of covered eyes
  • Yeshivah bachur: lights + blesses in his room
  • Guest: פרוטה to join in
  • Several baalei batim: each one blesses
  • Candles too short (extinguish before nightfall) = ברכה לבטלה

Axis C — Acceptance (Se'ifim 10-17)

  • Bahag's חידוש: lighting = acceptance for the woman who lights
  • Conditioning "in the heart" possible (Mordechai)
  • Rest of the household: free until Barekhu (Rema)
  • Tefillas Maariv early = acceptance
  • Traveler in a town that has already accepted = dragged along
  • Cloudy-day error: community need not re-daven; individual must re-daven
  • Possibility of asking another Jew (who has not accepted) to do a melakha

4. Mnemonic — N-B-Q

N — Nér (נֵר): the candle. 2 wicks minimum (Zakhor/Shamor); 3 if previously forgotten; +1 per child in Chabad. Woman has priority.

B — Brachah (בְּרָכָה): minhag of covered eyes — light → cover → bless → uncover → look.

Q — Qabbalah (קַבָּלַת שַׁבָּת): the lighting = acceptance for the woman (Bahag). תנאי בלב possible.

To memorize: "N-B-Q for siman 263: Nér 2 wicks minimum, Brachah with covered eyes, Qabbalah through lighting."

Step 1: Prepare 2 candles (3 if previously forgotten, +1 per child in Chabad)

Step 3: Cover the eyes with the hands

7. 5 pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Lighting then wanting to continue a mundane act (text, close the fridge). Forbidden according to the Rema (acceptance through lighting). Solution: mentally condition before lighting (rare).
Pitfall 2: Lighting in the living room but eating in the dining room. Risk of ברכה לבטלה if the candles are short (extinguish before nightfall). Either use long candles, or light where one eats.
Pitfall 3: Lighting and then moving the candles to the table. Wrong according to the Rema — the lighting must be done where the candles will remain. Final position before lighting.
Pitfall 4: Yeshivah student who thinks his parents light for him at home. Wrong — he must light + bless in his own room.
Pitfall 5: Poverty preventing one from obtaining candles → skip the mitzvah. Wrong — the Mechaber orders one to beg at doorways to obtain oil. The mitzvah is universal, with no excuse of poverty.

10. The 5 practical commandments of Siman רס״ג

For daily conduct

  1. Light at least 2 candles (Zakhor/Shamor) — 3 if previously forgotten, +1 per child in Chabad. Woman has priority.
  2. Brachah with covered eyes — light → cover → bless → uncover → look → Mizmor.
  3. Acceptance through lighting — the woman enters Shabbos at the moment she recites the brachah. Prior conditioning possible but exceptional.
  4. Student / traveler — light + bless in the dedicated room; otherwise פרוטה to join in.
  5. Universality — the mitzvah is obligatory for everyone, even paupers beg to obtain oil.
