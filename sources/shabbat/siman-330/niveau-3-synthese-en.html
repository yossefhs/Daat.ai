1. The central axiom

Siman 330 in one sentence.
יוֹלֶדֶת כְּחוֹלֶה שֶׁיֵּשׁ בּוֹ סַכָּנָה — the yoledes has the status of a choleh in sakanah: one is mechalel Shabbos for all her needs. But two nuances: one acts with shinui whenever possible (birth being predictable), and severity decreases over time — 3 days, 7 days, 30 days.

3. Hierarchy of cases

During labor and the first 3 days: full sakanah — one is mechalel for all her needs, even if she says she needs nothing.
↓
From the 3rd to 7th day: one transgresses if she says "I need"; abstains if she says "I don't need".
↓
From the 7th to 30th day: status of choleh without sakanah; no Torah issur, but refuah and fire permitted.
↓
At all times: what can be done with shinui — without delay — is done with shinui.

4. Decision tree

Q1 — Has labor begun? (birthing stool / flowing blood / can no longer walk) → one is mechalel for all her needs.
↓
Q2 — How many days since birth? ≤3 → full sakanah; 3-7 → per her words; 7-30 → choleh without sakanah.
↓
Q3 — Can this action be done with shinui without delay? Yes → with shinui. No → normally.
↓
Q4 — Care for the newborn? Viable (7/9 months) → permitted. Doubt (8 months without signs) → consult your Rav.

5. The 3 / 7 / 30 day scale: descending severity

The yoledes has, initially, the full status of חולה שיש בו סכנה. But this status does not extinguish at once: it decreases in steps, and the practical difficulty is knowing, each passing day, which regime applies. This is the central mechanism of the siman — understanding that the same danger is also measured in time.

The three steps and their logic

  • From labor through completed 3 days. Full sakanah. One is mechalel for all her needs even if she declares she needs nothing: at this stage, her word does not lift the presumption of sakanah.
  • From the 3rd to 7th day. Sakanah weakens, and now her word decides: if she says "I need", one transgresses; if she says "I don't need", one abstains. The delicate point is this inversion — before 3 days one acts against her word, after 3 days one follows her word.
  • From the 7th to 30th day. No more presumed sakanah: status of חולה שאין בו סכנה. No Torah issur by a Jew, but refuah permitted — and notably fire remains permitted to warm her, since cold is dangerous to her until 30 days.
≤ 3 days → one is mechalel for everything, even against her word.
↓
3-7 days → her word decides: "need" → one transgresses; "don't need" → abstains.
↓
7-30 days → choleh without sakanah; fire permitted against cold.

Shinui: a transverse requirement at all stages

To this time scale a second rule applies, valid at any moment: what can be done with a change in the way of acting (שינוי) must be done so — but on the sole condition that this causes no delay or difficulty. Birth being predictable, many acts can be anticipated or arranged; but at the slightest risk of dragging, shinui falls and one acts normally, since saving overrides minimization.

Remember: two axes cross — the time elapsed (3/7/30) sets the regime; shinui modulates the manner, without ever slowing the essential.

6. Mnemonic

י — יוֹלֶדֶת = חוֹלֶה שֶׁיֵּשׁ בּוֹ סַכָּנָה: one is mechalel for all her needs.

ש — שִׁינּוּי: whatever can be done otherwise, without delay, is done otherwise.

ז — זְמַן: 3 / 7 / 30 day scale — severity decreases.

→ יש"ז: status, shinui, time scale.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — hesitating during labor. From signs of onset, the yoledes is a choleh in sakanah: one acts for all her needs, even against her word.
Pitfall 2 — confusing shinui and delay. Shinui applies only if it causes no delay or difficulty. At the slightest risk of dragging, one acts normally.
Pitfall 3 — not anticipating. A woman in her 9th month must prepare every erev Shabbos the necessary — not preparing in advance causes unnecessary transgression.
Pitfall 4 — refusing the lamp. One lights for the yoledes even if she is blind, even if she asks for nothing: her calming (yishuv ha-da'as) is part of pikuach nefesh.

10. The practical commandments of Siman 330

For daily conduct

  1. The yoledes is a choleh in sakanah — one is mechalel Shabbos for all her needs.
  2. With shinui whenever possible without delay.
  3. Time scale — 3 days (full sakanah), 7 (per her words), 30 (without sakanah).
  4. Anticipate — prepare the necessary before Shabbos from the 9th month.
  5. Calm — lighting a lamp, calling for help, is part of the rescue.
  6. In doubt — consult your Rav. Pilpul: Level 2; Chabad shitah: Level 4.
