1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שפ״ה contains 4 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo); in the text Sefaria serves (Maginei Eretz edition), the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) has no gloss on it introduced by the word הגה. Topic of the siman, according to its title: the law of the Tzeduki and the mumar in the eruv. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe also has 4 seifim for this siman: its numbering is that of Level 4, not that of this page.

Seif א

דין צדוקי ומומר בעירוב. ובו ד סעיפים:
צדוקי הרי הוא כישראל ומבטל רשות אבל עירוב אינו מועיל כיון שאינו מודה בעירוב:
Translation: The Tzeduki (Sadducee) is like a Jew, and he renounces [his] domain (mevatel reshus); but the eruv does not help [with him — even if he gave his share in the eruv, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב], since he does not acknowledge the eruv.
What this seif says:
  • The Tzeduki is like a Jew: he renounces his domain: צדוקי הרי הוא כישראל ומבטל רשות. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק א) describes the Tzedukim as those who incline to heresy and do not believe in the Oral Torah; and although he certainly desecrates Shabbos in public, at least through a rabbinic prohibition, the Tzeduki is different, it writes in the name of the Beis Yosef, for he is established so from the days of his fathers and holds the custom of his fathers. It adds that, according to several poskim, even a Tzeduki, when he desecrates Shabbos in public, has the law of a non-Jew (section 6).
  • But the eruv does not help, since he does not acknowledge the eruv: אבל עירוב אינו מועיל כיון שאינו מודה בעירוב. Even if he gave his share in the eruv, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק ב), it does not help, and he forbids them until he renounces his domain to them; and this is not limited to the Tzedukim and the like who do not acknowledge the Oral Torah: likewise one who merely does not acknowledge the mitzvah of eruv cannot give a share in the eruv.

Seif ב

כותי הרי הוא כשאר עכו״ם ואין לו תקנה אלא בשכירות:
Translation: The Kusi (Cuthean) is like other non-Jews (akum), and he has no remedy (takanah) except through renting (sechirus).
What this seif says:
  • The Kusi has the law of other non-Jews: כותי הרי הוא כשאר עכו״ם.
  • His only remedy is renting: ואין לו תקנה אלא בשכירות.

Seif ג

ישראל מומר לעבודת אלילים או לחלל שבתות בפרהסיא אפילו אינו מחללו אלא באיסור דרבנן הרי הוא כעכו״ם ואם אינו מחלל אלא בצינע' אפילו מחללו באיסור דאוריית' הרי הוא כישראל ומבטל רשות:
Translation: A Jew who is a mumar (apostate) for idolatry, or for desecrating Shabbos in public (befarhesya) — even if he desecrates it only through a rabbinic prohibition — is like a non-Jew; and if he desecrates it only in private (betzin'a), even if he desecrates it through a Torah prohibition, he is like a Jew, and he renounces [his] domain.
What this seif says:
  • The mumar for idolatry, or for desecrating Shabbos in public, even through a rabbinic prohibition, is like a non-Jew: ישראל מומר לעבודת אלילים או לחלל שבתות בפרהסיא אפילו אינו מחללו אלא באיסור דרבנן הרי הוא כעכו״ם. For idolatry, even in private (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג). For Shabbos, it writes (ס״ק ד), this sin is as grave as that of an idolater, for through it he becomes a mumar for the whole Torah; and only if he is accustomed to this sin: if he desecrated Shabbos once in public, he is not called a mumar, and some say that even once he is called a mumar. It calls public desecrating before ten Jews, or knowing that it will become known among them; and all this when he transgresses out of appetite (leteavon): out of provocation (lehach'is), even for other sins and even not in public, he has the law of a non-Jew. Like a non-Jew, it specifies (ס״ק ה), for this matter: renunciation of domain no longer helps, only renting; for other matters, some Acharonim hold that desecration through a rabbinic prohibition does not make him a mumar.
  • In private, even through a Torah prohibition, he is like a Jew and renounces his domain: ואם אינו מחלל אלא בצינע׳ אפילו מחללו באיסור דאוריית׳ הרי הוא כישראל ומבטל רשות. If he is ashamed to do it before a great man, even though he does it before several people, that too counts as in private (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו).

Seif ד

ישראל שהמיר והיו לו בתים בשכונת ישראל שעירבו לכל השנה אם אין לו פתח אחר כלל הוא אוסר בכל שבת שחל לאחר שהמיר אבל אם יש לו פתח אחר לשכונת העכו״ם אפילו הוא פתח קטן שלא היה רגיל בו מתחיל' דוחין אותו אצל פתח הפתוח לשכונת העכו״ם:
Translation: A Jew who changed his religion (heimir), and had houses in the Jewish neighborhood, whose residents made the eruv for the whole year: if he has no other entrance at all, he forbids on every Shabbos that falls after he changed his religion; but if he has another entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews, even a small entrance that he was not accustomed to use at first, we push him off to the entrance that opens onto the neighborhood of the non-Jews [so that he does not forbid them — Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״א].
What this seif says:
  • With no other entrance, he forbids on every Shabbos that falls after he changed his religion: ישראל שהמיר והיו לו בתים בשכונת ישראל שעירבו לכל השנה אם אין לו פתח אחר כלל הוא אוסר בכל שבת שחל לאחר שהמיר. We do not say, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק ז), that since [carrying] was permitted for the Shabbosos that fell before he changed his religion, it is permitted for the whole year: each Shabbos is a matter in itself; and since he changed his religion, his share in the eruv is annulled, and he forbids them on the following Shabbosos until they rent from him. If he changed his religion on Shabbos itself, it is permitted for that Shabbos, since what was permitted for part of Shabbos is permitted for all of Shabbos (ס״ק ח).
  • If he has another entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews, even a small one, we push him off to it: אבל אם יש לו פתח אחר לשכונת העכו״ם אפילו הוא פתח קטן שלא היה רגיל בו מתחיל׳ דוחין אותו אצל פתח הפתוח לשכונת העכו״ם. It must at least be four tefachim by four; less than that is not an entrance (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י׳, referring to siman 372). We compel him against the trait of Sodom, it writes (ס״ק י״א), so that he does not forbid them (referring to siman 386, end of seif ט); and also because, having become estranged and accustomed to the non-Jews, he prefers that entrance. If at first he had no entrance and opened it after changing his religion, it refers to siman 383 (ס״ק ט).

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Siman שפ״ה says, for those who live with Jews, whether they are treated like a Jew, through the renunciation of domain, or like a non-Jew, through renting: the Tzeduki (seif א), the Kusi (seif ב), the mumar (seif ג), and the Jew who changed his religion when the neighborhood had made the eruv for the whole year (seif ד).

The Gemara brings a baraisa: « יִשְׂרָאֵל מוּמָר, מְשַׁמֵּר שַׁבַּתּוֹ בַּשּׁוּק — מְבַטֵּל רְשׁוּת, שֶׁאֵינוֹ מְשַׁמֵּר שַׁבַּתּוֹ בַּשּׁוּק — אֵינוֹ מְבַטֵּל רְשׁוּת » (עירובין ס״ט ע״ב) — a Jewish mumar who keeps his Shabbos in the marketplace renounces his domain; one who does not keep his Shabbos in the marketplace does not renounce his domain.

The fundamental question: with whom, among those who live with Jews, does the renunciation of domain suffice, and from whom must one rent? The siman answers according to the person: the Tzeduki renounces, but the eruv does not help with him (seif א); the Kusi has no remedy except through renting (seif ב); the mumar according to what he is a mumar for, and according to whether he desecrates in public or in private (seif ג); one who changed his religion according to whether or not he has another entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews (seif ד).

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. Siman שפ״ה follows siman שפ״ד · 384, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « עכו״ם אכסנאי אם מעכב » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ד:א), and precedes siman שפ״ו · 386, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דיני שיתוף בעירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:א). The Mishnah Berurah refers here, among others, to siman 372 (ס״ק י׳), to siman 383 (ס״ק ט) and to siman 386 (ס״ק י״א).

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman:

The essential notions of siman שפ״ה:
  • צדוקי — the Tzeduki (seif א): « והם הנוטים לאפיקורסות ואינם מאמינים בתורה שבע״פ » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק א).
  • מודה בעירוב — one who acknowledges the eruv: the Tzeduki does not, and the eruv does not help with him (seif א); the Mishnah Berurah extends it: « ה״ה אם רק אינו מודה במצות עירוב אינו יכול ליתן חלק בעירוב » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק ב).
  • שכירות — renting: the only remedy for the Kusi (seif ב), and for the mumar who is like a non-Jew, « שאינו מועיל שוב ביטול רשות אלא דוקא שכירות » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק ה).
  • מומר לחלל שבתות בפרהסיא — the mumar for desecrating Shabbos in public (seif ג): « דעון זה חמור כמו עובד עכו״ם שנעשה בזה מומר לכל התורה ודוקא בשרגיל בעון זה אבל אם חילל שבת בחד זימנא בפרהסיא לא מיקרי מומר וי״א דאפילו בחד זימנא מקרי מומר » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק ד) — this sin is as grave as that of an idolater, for through it he becomes a mumar for the whole Torah; and only if he is accustomed to this sin: if he desecrated Shabbos once in public, he is not called a mumar; and some say that even once he is called a mumar.
  • פרהסיא — public: « ופרהסיא מקרי כשחילל בפני עשרה מישראל או שידע שיתפרסם ביניהם » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק ד).
  • לתיאבון and להכעיס — out of appetite and out of provocation: « ודע דכל זה כשהוא עובר לתיאבון אבל להכעיס אפילו בשאר עבירות ואפילו שלא בפרהסיא דינו כעכו״ם » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק ד).
  • פתח אחר — another entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews (seif ד): « ועכ״פ בעינן שיהיה בו ד״ט על ד״ט דבפחות מזה לא חשיב פתח » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק י׳).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אThe Tzeduki is like a Jew, and he renounces his domain; but the eruv does not help with him, since he does not acknowledge the eruv.
בThe Kusi is like other non-Jews: he has no remedy except through renting.
גThe mumar for idolatry, or for desecrating Shabbos in public, even if he desecrates only through a rabbinic prohibition, is like a non-Jew; if he desecrates only in private, even through a Torah prohibition, he is like a Jew and renounces his domain.
דThe Jew who changed his religion and had houses in the Jewish neighborhood, whose residents made the eruv for the whole year: if he has no other entrance at all, he forbids on every Shabbos that falls after he changed his religion; if he has another entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews, even small and one he was not accustomed to at first, we push him off to it.
The heart of the siman: the siman says who is treated like a Jew, through the renunciation of domain, and who like a non-Jew, through renting. The Tzeduki renounces his domain, but the eruv does not help with him (seif א); the Kusi has no remedy except through renting (seif ב); the mumar for idolatry, or for desecrating Shabbos in public, is like a non-Jew, and one who desecrates only in private is like a Jew (seif ג); one who changed his religion forbids on every Shabbos that follows, unless he has another entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews (seif ד).

5. The Mishnah Berurah — first entries

The Mishnah Berurah of Rabbi Yisrael Meir Kagan (Chafetz Chaim) has 11 ס״ק on this siman. Here they are, in order — to better understand the meaning of the text:

משנה ברורה (א) — (א) צדוקי - על שם צדוק וביתוס והם הנוטים לאפיקורסות ואינם מאמינים בתורה שבע״פ אעפ״כ לענין זה הוא כישראל שמועיל כשמבטל רשות ואע״פ שבוודאי מחלל שבת בפרהסיא עכ״פ באיסור דרבנן ואיתא בס״ב דאף בזה הרי הוא כעכו״ם צדוקי שאני שמוחזק כך מימי אבותיו ומנהג אבותיו בידו כ״כ ב״י ודעת כמה פוסקים שאף צדוקי כשמחלל שבת בפרהסיא דינו כעכו״ם ונ״מ בכל זה לענין קראים שבזמנינו שבודאי מחללים שבת בפרהסיא עכ״פ במילי דרבנן אין יכולים לבטל רשות עד שישכור מהם:
משנה ברורה (ב) — (ב) אינו מועיל וכו' - ר״ל אף אם נתן חלק בעירוב אינו מועיל ואוסר עליהן עד שיבטל רשותו להן ולאו דוקא מכת הצדוקים וכה״ג שאינם מודים בתורה שבע״פ ה״ה אם רק אינו מודה במצות עירוב אינו יכול ליתן חלק בעירוב:
משנה ברורה (ג) — (ג) מומר לע״ג - אפילו בצינעה:
משנה ברורה (ד) — (ד) או לחלל שבתות בפרהסיא - דעון זה חמור כמו עובד עכו״ם שנעשה בזה מומר לכל התורה ודוקא בשרגיל בעון זה אבל אם חילל שבת בחד זימנא בפרהסיא לא מיקרי מומר וי״א דאפילו בחד זימנא מקרי מומר. ופרהסיא מקרי כשחילל בפני עשרה מישראל או שידע שיתפרסם ביניהם. ודע דכל זה כשהוא עובר לתיאבון אבל להכעיס אפילו בשאר עבירות ואפילו שלא בפרהסיא דינו כעכו״ם:

משנה ברורה (יא) — (יא) דוחין אותו וכו' - דכופין אותו על מדת סדום כדי שלא יאסור עליהן וכדלקמן בסימן שפ״ו סוף ס״ט לגבי ישראל וגם דבודאי כיון שנתנכר ורגיל עם העכו״ם ניחא ליה בההוא פתחא טפי:

The text on Sefaria: Mishnah Berurah 385.

6. Position of the Rema

In the text served by Sefaria (Maginei Eretz edition), the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) has no gloss introduced by the word הגה on this siman. The Mishnah Berurah, for its part, adds remarks and differing views to the seifim of the Mechaber; here are several, seif by seif.

The remarks of the Mishnah Berurah, seif by seif:
  • Seif א, the Tzeduki who desecrates Shabbos in public — after giving the reason of the Beis Yosef, « צדוקי שאני שמוחזק כך מימי אבותיו ומנהג אבותיו בידו » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק א), it writes: « ודעת כמה פוסקים שאף צדוקי כשמחלל שבת בפרהסיא דינו כעכו״ם ונ״מ בכל זה לענין קראים שבזמנינו שבודאי מחללים שבת בפרהסיא עכ״פ במילי דרבנן אין יכולים לבטל רשות עד שישכור מהם » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק א) — and the view of several poskim is that even a Tzeduki, when he desecrates Shabbos in public, has the law of a non-Jew; and the practical difference of all this concerns the Karaites of our time [that of the Mishnah Berurah], who certainly desecrate Shabbos in public, at least in rabbinic matters: they cannot renounce their domain, until one rents from them.
  • Seif ג, a single desecration in public — the Mishnah Berurah brings two views: « אבל אם חילל שבת בחד זימנא בפרהסיא לא מיקרי מומר וי״א דאפילו בחד זימנא מקרי מומר » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק ד) — but if he desecrated Shabbos once in public, he is not called a mumar; and some say that even once he is called a mumar.
  • Seif ג, for matters other than the eruv — « ולענין שארי דברים יש מן האחרונים שסוברין שאינו חשוב כמומר ע״י חילול שבת באיסור דרבנן » (משנה ברורה שפ״ה ס״ק ה) — for other matters, some Acharonim hold that he is not considered a mumar through desecrating Shabbos by a rabbinic prohibition; it refers to the Pischei Teshuvah on Yoreh De'ah, siman 2, to the Beis Meir on this siman and to the Gaon Yaakov.
  • Chabad → Level 4 — Daat HaRav studies this siman in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שפ״ה, under its own numbering, in 4 seifim. To distinguish the Tzeduki from the mumar, he writes: « לְפִי שֶׁהַצְּדוֹקִים אֲנוּסִים הֵם, שֶׁמַּעֲשֵׂה אֲבוֹתֵיהֶם בִּידֵיהֶם » (שו״ע הרב שפ״ה:ג).

7. Modern practical cases

Each of the situations below ties a case to a named seif; when the situation is a present-day one, the row says so: application by the site, not the words of the source.

SituationQuick analysis
A resident of the courtyard gave his share in the eruv, but does not acknowledge the mitzvah of eruvSeif א: the eruv does not help with one who does not acknowledge it; according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ב), even if he gave his share, it does not help, and he forbids them until he renounces his domain to them — and this also applies to one who, without being a Tzeduki, merely does not acknowledge the mitzvah of eruv. Consult your Rav.
A resident of the courtyard desecrates ShabbosApplication by the site, seif ג: the seif distinguishes between desecration in public and in private, and the Mishnah Berurah according to habit (it brings two views on a single desecration), and between appetite and provocation (ס״ק ד); whether a given resident is, in the sense of the seif, like a Jew or like a non-Jew — and therefore whether it is enough for him to renounce his domain, or one must rent from him — this page does not decide: it is for your Rav to say.
One who changed his religion also has a small entrance on the other sideSeif ד: if he has another entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews, even small and one he was not accustomed to, we push him off to it; according to the Mishnah Berurah, it must be at least four tefachim by four (ס״ק י׳), and if he opened it after changing his religion, it refers to siman 383 (ס״ק ט). Consult your Rav.
For precise practical cases: the seifim make the rule depend, among other things, on whether the person acknowledges the eruv (seif א), on whether he is like a non-Jew (seifim ב and ג), on desecration in public or in private (seif ג) and on the entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews (seif ד); and the Mishnah Berurah adds remarks and differing views (section 6). For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שפ״ה:
  1. The Tzeduki is like a Jew and renounces his domain; but the eruv does not help with him, since he does not acknowledge the eruv (seif א). On a Tzeduki who desecrates Shabbos in public, the views diverge: the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק א) gives the Beis Yosef's reason, then the view of several poskim who give him the law of a non-Jew; see section 6 and Level 3, section 5.
  2. The Kusi is like other non-Jews: only renting resolves it (seif ב).
  3. The mumar for idolatry, or for desecrating Shabbos in public, even through a rabbinic prohibition, is like a non-Jew; if he desecrates only in private, even through a Torah prohibition, he is like a Jew and renounces his domain (seif ג).
  4. One who changed his religion, when the neighborhood had made the eruv for the whole year: with no other entrance, he forbids on every Shabbos that falls afterwards; with another entrance to the neighborhood of the non-Jews, even small, we push him off to it (seif ד).
  5. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Why can the Tzeduki renounce his domain, while the eruv does not help with him (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א and ב)?
  2. What is the only remedy for the Kusi (seif ב), and how does his law differ from that of the Tzeduki (seif א)?
  3. In seif ג, what does the seif distinguish between desecration in public and in private, and why, according to the Mishnah Berurah, is desecrating Shabbos as grave as idolatry (ס״ק ד)?
  4. According to the Mishnah Berurah, what is called public, and what does provocation change (ס״ק ד)?
  5. In seif ד, why does one who changed his religion forbid on every Shabbos, even though the neighborhood had made the eruv for the whole year (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ז)?
