1. The central axiom

Siman רפ״ו in one sentence.
The tefilah of Musaf on Shabbos — the 4th tefilah of the day, established in correspondence with the additional Shabbos korban (Bamidbar 28:9-10) — is davened immediately after Shacharis, up to the end of the 7th hour. Beyond this = "poshea" but valid. Missed for the entire day, no tashlumin — unique among the tefilos.

4. Decision tree — Minchah vs Musaf when delayed

Q1: What time is it?
↓ Before six and a half hours (chatzos) → Musaf only
↓ After six and a half hours → possible conflict
Q2: Am I alone (yachid) or in public?
↓ Yachid → Minchah first (tadir kodem)
↓ Public → Musaf after Shacharis (shelo yit'u)
Q3: If you reversed the order → yatza yedei chovaso (Rashba/Rema).
↓
Q4: If Minchah ketanah has arrived (9.5 zmanios hours) → Minchah first even in public (Yerushalmi/Rema).

5. The heart of the siman — Musaf, the tefilah without tashlumin

The property that distinguishes Musaf from all other tefilos — and which constitutes the true heart of Siman רפ״ו — is captured in four words: אֵין לָהּ תַּשְׁלוּמִין, "it has no compensation". One must understand why, since this governs all practical halachah.

The mechanism of ordinary tashlumin: one who misses Shacharis makes it up at Minchah; one who misses Minchah makes it up at Maariv. A missed tefilah is "replayed" at the following tefilah, because the daily tefilos follow one another in a chain.
↓ why Musaf escapes the rule
Musaf does not correspond to any chain: it is established corresponding to the korban musaf of the day (Bamidbar 28:9-10), a korban that can only be offered on that very day. Once the time has passed, the korban is lost — and with it the "content" that Musaf came to represent. There is nothing to defer.

The decisive borderline case follows from this: one who, thinking to "make up" Musaf, would daven a second Amidah at Minchah, utters a berachah levatalah — he fulfills no mitzvah and utters Hashem's Name in vain. Missed for the entire day, Musaf is missed definitively; one accepts this and does better the following week.

The principle to take away. The rest of the siman is derived from this uniqueness: one davens Musaf right after Shacharis (before the 7th hour) precisely because no safety net exists downstream; and the severity of the term "פּוֹשֵׁעַ" for one who delays beyond this is understood in light of this irreparability.

6. Mnemonic — "7.0.M"

7 — 7th hour = "lechatchilah" limit for Musaf (≈ 1:00 PM zmanios).

0 — Zero tashlumin: Musaf missed for the entire day = irreparable mitzvah.

M — Minchah tadir → kodem in case of time conflict (except in public).

"TPK" mnemonic for the 3 specificities of Musaf:

T — Tadir: must be davened before Minchah which is tadir.

P — Passe over: no tashlumin — irreparable.

K — Korban: zecher to the korban musaf of Shabbos.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — Voluntarily delaying Musaf past 1:00 PM: though valid, one is called "poshea". Do not make this a habit.
Pitfall 2 — Thinking that a missed Musaf is made up at Minchah: false. No tashlumin. If one davens Minchah twice thinking to make up Musaf — it is a berachah levatalah.
Pitfall 3 — Full meal between Shacharis and Musaf: seudah = forbidden. Only "liso'ad halev" (fruits, a little bread).
Pitfall 4 — Reversing the order in public: in shul one always follows Shacharis → Musaf → ... → Minchah, even if technically Minchah is tadir and should come first. "Shelo yit'u."
Pitfall 5 — Believing that Musaf requires a minyan: false. Individual chiyuv (Seif Beis), like any Amidah. With a minyan, chazaras hashatz is added.

10. The 5 practical rulings of Siman רפ״ו

To daven Musaf Shabbos properly

  1. Daven Musaf immediately after Shacharis — at the latest before the 7th zmanios hour (≈ 1:00 PM).
  2. Never eat a true meal before Musaf — fruits, cake, pas k'beitzah OK, seudah no.
  3. Even alone, daven Musaf — it is an individual chiyuv, not dependent on a minyan.
  4. If delayed past Minchah gedolah (6.5 hours): as a yachid, daven Minchah first; in public, follow the usual order.
  5. If missed for the entire day: no tashlumin — accept the loss and do better the following week.
