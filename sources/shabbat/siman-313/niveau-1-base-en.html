1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman 313 contains 10 se'ifim from the Mechaber codifying the halachos of moving doors, windows, locks on Shabbos.

Se'if 1 — Window plug, reed, peg-bolt: the conditions

טלטול דלת וחלון והמנעול בשבת. ובו י סעיפים:
פקק החלון כגון לוח או שאר כל דבר שסותמין בו החלון יכולים לסתמו אפי' אם אינו קשור ולא אמרינן דהוי כמוסיף על הבנין והוא שיחשוב עליו מערב שבת לסתמו בו וקנה שהתקינו להיות נועל בו אין צריך שיקשרנו בדלת ומ"מ דמי לבנין יותר מפקק החלון ולא סגי במחשבה שיחשוב עליו מאתמול וצריך שיתקננו לכך לרש"י היינו שיתקננו לשם כלי שיהא ראוי לשום תשמיש שיהא ראוי להפוך בו זתים או לפצוע בו אגוזים ולר"ת א"צ שיהא ראוי לדבר אחר אלא כיון שתקנו ועשה מעשה והכינו לכך סגי: הגה ומקרי ע"י כך כלי שמלאכתו להיתר ומותר לטלטלו כמו שנתבאר לעיל סי' ש"ח ס"ב (ב"י והרמב"ם) ונגר שהוא יתד שנועלים בו ותוחבין אותו באסקופה למטה ודומה טפי לבנין לא סגי בהכי ואין נועלים בו אא"כ יהא קשור...
Translation: A window plug (pekak ha-chalon) — board or any object used to stop up the window — may be used to plug the window even if unattached; we do not say this is "adding to the building" (boneh). Condition: one must have thought about it before Shabbos to use it that way.
A reed prepared as a bolt (kaneh): no need to attach it to the door, but since it resembles building more than the window plug, mere thought is insufficient — one must have prepared it (Rashi: shaped it as a k'li fit for another use; R"T: simply that it has been worked and readied for the function). Rama's gloss: It then becomes k'li she-melachto le-heter, permitted to move (cf. 308:2).
A peg-bolt (neger) driven into the threshold — resembles building still more: neither thought nor preparation suffices — it must be tied.

Se'if 2 — Bolt piercing the ground

כל נגר שאמרנו מיירי שאסקופה גבוה וכשנועץ אותו במפתן אינו נוקב בארץ אבל אם נפחתה האסקופה בענין שכשמכניס הנגר בחור נוקב תחתיו בארץ הוי בנין ואסור לנעול בו:
Translation: All said of the neger (peg-bolt) assumes a raised threshold — when driven in, it does not pierce the ground. But if the threshold is damaged so that driving the bolt pierces the ground beneath — it is building (boneh), and assur to lock with it.

2. General context — boneh and soser at home

What does this siman address?

Siman 313 applies the melachos of boneh (building) and soser (demolishing) to movable household objects: doors, plugs, windows, bolts, hinges. The big question: opening/closing a door or placing a plug — is this "building" on Shabbos?

The two melachos involved:
  • בונה (boneh) — assembling, fastening, adding to a structure. One of the 39 melachos. "Adding to the building" (מוסיף על הבנין) is a toldah of it.
  • סותר (soser) — undoing a structure. Twin melachah.
The pivotal principle: תורת כלי ("status of a k'li"). A plug or bolt with the status of a k'li is not "building" when in use — it is mere use. How does it obtain this status? According to its resemblance to building: (a) plug → mere prior thought suffices; (b) reed-bolt → requires preparation; (c) peg-bolt → must be tied.

Place in Hilchos Shabbos

Siman 313 is the daily-domestic application of boneh/soser. The underlying principles are in simanim 314-315. Siman 313 specifically deals with whatever closes an opening — the most practical of the group.

3. Key halachic concepts

1. בונה (boneh) — building. De'oraisa melachah. Includes mosif al ha-binyan. Driving a bolt that pierces the ground = actual building.
2. סותר (soser) — demolishing. Twin melachah. Undoing what is solidly assembled.
3. תורת כלי — "status of a k'li." An object that is a k'li — using it is not "building." Three levels of stringency to obtain this status, by resemblance to building.
4. ציר (tzir) — the hinge/pivot. A door on a real pivot = falls under boneh. A "door" without a pivot, sliding on the ground → simple object, less problematic.
5. מחשבה מערב שבת — prior thought. For the plug (less like building), simply having thought the day before to use it that way suffices. This is what confers status of a k'li.

Method of reading:
  1. Identify the object (plug, reed, bolt, door)
  2. Evaluate its resemblance to building
  3. Check the corresponding condition (thought / preparation / attachment)
  4. If the bolt pierces the ground or one re-mounts a real pivot → boneh, assur

6. The Rama's position

Main glosses of the Rama:
  • Se'if 1 — An object prepared as a bolt becomes k'li she-melachto le-heter, and may therefore be moved on Shabbos (ref. siman 308:2).
  • Se'if 3 — On doors with a pivot: all the more so if the pivot is still present, it is muttar — provided the pivot does not return to its socket (otherwise it is building; ref. siman 519 — yamim tovim).
Mechaber vs. Rama difference:
  • Mechaber → presents the 3 levels of toras k'li.
  • Rama → specifies the link with muktzeh (308:2) and the pivot limit.
  • Chabad → Shulchan Aruch HaRav siman 313.

7. Modern practical cases

SituationHalachic analysis
Open/close an ordinary door (on fixed hinges)Muttar — the door is integral to the house, its normal use is not "building."
Door that has come off its hingesPutting it back = potential boneh. Don't do it; ask a Rav.
Manual roller shutterGenerally muttar (shutter = pekak ha-chalon, k'li status). Electric shutter = assur (electricity).
Door bolt / latchMuttar — part of the door. A removable bolt must have k'li status.
Key in a lockLocking/unlocking = muttar (normal use). Problem only with electronic locks.
Folding bed / extension tableUnfolding a bed designed for that = muttar (use). Assembling separate pieces = boneh, assur.
Removable screenIf designed to be put up/removed regularly = muttar. Otherwise = edge case.
Parasol / awning to deployDeploying an awning = potential ohel. See siman 315.
For halachah l'maaseh: The golden rule: an object designed and installed to be regularly opened/closed (door, window, shutter, drawer) — its normal use is muttar, because it is "using," not "building." The problem arises when: (a) one assembles separate pieces; (b) one reseats a hinge; (c) a bolt pierces the ground; (d) there is electricity.

8. Practical summary

Key teachings of Siman 313:
  1. Boneh / soser apply also to movable household objects.
  2. Toras k'li: an object that is a k'li — its use is not "building."
  3. 3 levels of stringency: plug → prior thought; bolt → preparation; peg-bolt → attachment.
  4. Bolt piercing the ground = actual building, assur.
  5. The pivot (tzir): reseating a pivot in its socket = boneh.
  6. Normal use of an installed door / window / shutter = muttar.
  7. Assembling separate pieces = boneh, assur.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. What are the two central melachos of the siman? Define them.
  2. What is the status of toras k'li? Why is it decisive?
  3. What are the 3 levels (plug / reed / peg-bolt)? Why this gradation?
  4. Why is a bolt piercing the ground assur (se'if 2)?
  5. What role does the tzir (pivot) play in halachic evaluation?
  6. Is opening/closing an ordinary door on Shabbos muttar? Why?
  7. Why is assembling furniture from pieces assur, but unfolding a folding bed muttar?
