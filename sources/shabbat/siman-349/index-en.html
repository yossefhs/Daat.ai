Siman 349 — The personal 4 amos in reshus harabim (a traveling private space)

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe in the 6 seifim that codify the concept of the personal 4 amos — every person has 4 amos around himself in reshus harabim that are considered his own personal reshus hayachid. The framework for the traveler.
