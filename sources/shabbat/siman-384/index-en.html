עכו״ם אכסנאי אם מעכב

The Non-Jew Lodging Temporarily, and the Men of the King's Army

2 seifim in the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber, with no gloss of the Rema; 1 in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, under its own numbering (Level 4). Does a non-Jew who enters a courtyard to lodge temporarily forbid carrying to the Jews who live there? Seif א answers: entered without permission, he never forbids; entered with permission, he forbids immediately if he is accustomed to come, and otherwise only after thirty days. Seif ב deals with the men of the king's army who entered the houses of the Jews, by force or willingly: they do not forbid if the householders have, in those places, objects that it is forbidden to move on Shabbos; on several points, the commentators differ (Levels 2 and 3).
