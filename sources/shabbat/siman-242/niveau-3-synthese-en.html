1. The Central Axiom

Universal principle of Siman רמ״ב:

The oneg and kavod of Shabbat are obligations graduated according to one's means. Halakha does not impose a single standard but 3 levels of obligation, each adapted to the economic situation of the learner. No one is entirely exempt — even the poorest keeps a minimum.
💡 The universal key: "יש לו מעט משלו" — Does he have at least a little of his own?
  • If yes (even modest) → 3 seudot + כסא דהרסנא minimum
  • If no (nothing at all) → 2 seudot suffice (R. Akiva)
  • If wealthy → maakhalim chashuvim according to his means

2. The hierarchy of מחויב — 3 חלוקות (Tur)

CategorySituationHalakhic obligationSource חז״ל
העשיר
Wealthy		 Possesses abundantly מאכלים חשובים according to his means: fish, meat, refined garments. Not to be miserly. Maamar 1 — שבת קי״ח: (תרדין ודגים)
הבינוני
Middle		 Few means of his own 3 seudot + כסא דהרסנא. Must restrict himself on the other days. Maamar 2 + 4 — שבת קי״ח: + פסחים קי״ב.
העני המדוקדק
Utterly poor		 Nothing of his own — extremely tight situation Only 2 seudot. Not held to formal oneg — may "make his Shabbat ordinary". Maamar 3 — עשה שבתך חול (R. Akiva)

⚖ Pesak of the Tur (which becomes the pesak of the Shulchan Aruch)

The Tur resolves the 5 apparently contradictory מאמרי חז״ל by proposing these 3 חלוקות. Each מאמר applies to a different economic level. The decisive test is "does he have a little of his own?".

4. חקירת היסוד — דאורייתא or דרבנן?

Position 1 (Rashi, Rambam ad loc, Yereim, Ribash): True דאורייתא — based on "וקראת לשבת ענג" (Yeshayahu 58:13)
↓
Position 2 (Rambam Hilkhot Shabbat 30:1, Chatam Sofer): "מדברי סופרים" — דרבנן with asmakhta
↓
Position 3 (Tzemach Tzedek, Nechpeh Bekessef): דברי קבלה — Prophetic tradition, intermediate status
📚 Pesak lema'aseh: All agree it is binding. The Shulchan Aruch of the Admour HaZaken and the Shaar HaTziyun rule דרבנן, but add: "אף שכבוד ועונג שבת מדרבנן, מכל מקום צריך להיזהר בהם מאד, שדברי סופרים חמורים יותר מדברי תורה". The debate thus concerns the level of the obligation, not its existence.

3 interpretations of the Rambam (according to the Acharonim)

AcharonPirushEffective status
Chatam Sofer (שו״ת או״ח קסה) "דברי סופרים" = drasha, but דין כדאורייתא ממש Quasi-Torah
Tzemach Tzedek (סי' כח) Rambam agrees with Rashi: מדאורייתא, simply explained by the Prophets Full Torah
Nechpeh Bekessef + Shaloh + Rav Pealim Rambam truly derabbanan — חולק על Rashi Stringent derabbanan

6. Tikkun Ezra — Decision Tree

Q: When do you want to wash your laundry for Shabbat?
↓
🟢 Thursday (5th day) — Perfect lekhatchila. Tikkun Ezra in its plain sense.
🟢 Friday morning — Permitted if Thursday is not possible (Magen Avraham). Aliya Rabba: no.
🟡 Friday afternoon — Problematic: kavod Shabbat + risk of overflow.
🟢 Modern washing machine — If finished before Shabbat, OK even on Friday.
🔴 Shabbat itself — Forbidden (כיבוס is an av מלאכה דאורייתא).

Position of the Chut Shani (modern)

With the washing machine, the original sense of תקנת עזרא is nuanced. The aim was to avoid having to wash on Friday (manually). Today, starting a machine on Friday morning and finishing it well before Shabbat does not infringe upon the spirit of the תקנה.

7. Afiyat halechem — the 5 טעמים

Mnemonic: "C-T-P-L-T" (5 reasons for the minhag of afiyat halechem at home)
  • Challa — fulfill mitzvat hafrashat challa (Masat Binyamin)
  • Tikkun — repair the חטא of Chava (ירושלמי שבת פ״ב ה״ה)
  • Pat Yisrael — avoid the kashrut doubt over a gentile bakery's bread
  • Lechem mishneh — "homemade" bread for the בציעה (סי' רע״ד)
  • Tenderness — fresh bread is more mechubad than stale bread (Pri Megadim)
Question of the Taz: If the טעם is solely פת ישראל, why does the Rama say "אין לשנות" even when one has commercial פת ישראל?

Answer: The primary טעם is hafrashat challa. And this can only be fulfilled with homemade bread. Therefore, even with פת ישראל available, the minhag is upheld.

8. Mnemonics — Memory Aids

Acrostic: O-N-E-G-S — the 5 pillars of oneg Shabbat
  • Obligation graduated according to means
  • Non-negligible minimum level (כסא דהרסנא)
  • Embarking on a loan permitted with a pledge (לוו עלי)
  • Guard against "making Shabbat ordinary" except in absolute necessity
  • Sedaka possible for Shabbat needs
3 foundational sources to memorize:
  • וקראת לשבת ענג — Yeshayahu 58:13 (biblical source)
  • עשה שבתך חול ואל תצטרך לבריות — R. Akiva (Pesachim 113a)
  • לוו עלי וקדשו קדושת היום — Beitza 15b (Hashem guarantees)

9. Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ Pitfall 1: Believing that "עשה שבתך חול" authorizes anyone to dispense with oneg.
→ False. This applies only to the utterly poor, with nothing of his own. With even a little means → 3 seudot obligatory.
❌ Pitfall 2: Confusing "לוו עלי" with a blank check to go into debt.
→ Tosafot and the majority of the Rishonim restrict: there must be either a משכון, or confidence that one will be able to repay. There is no mitzvah to rely on a nes.
❌ Pitfall 3: Thinking that the washing machine nullifies תקנת עזרא.
→ The spirit remains: do not wash on Friday night / Shabbat. The timing changes (the manual מלאכה disappears) but the principle remains.
❌ Pitfall 4: Reducing the minhag of afiyat halechem to a "culinary tradition".
→ It is halakha — the Rama writes "אין לשנות". 5 serious טעמים underpin it (kavod, challa, חטא חוה, etc.).
❌ Pitfall 5: Discussing דאורייתא vs דרבנן as if it were an abstract question.
→ It has precise נפקא מינות: safek לחומרא or לקולא, validity of a minimal כסא דהרסנא, status of the third seuda. Read Level 2 — Lamdan for the details.

10. Modern Practical Cases

Student on a tight budget Category ② (Beinoni) 3 seudot. At least כסא דהרסנא for the third if means are limited. Restrict the weekdays for Shabbat.
Indebted family — borrow for Shabbat? Permitted if realistic repayment Prefer a loan with משכון. Avoid ribit, except in absolute necessity (consult a Rav).
Thursday evening — no time to do laundry Friday morning = ok Start the machine Friday morning with margin before Shabbat. If not possible → rewear already clean clothes.
No time to make challot Commercial pat Yisrael = OK Pri Megadim & Mishna Berura permit. But lekhatchila at home remains preferable (challa + tradition).
Receiving tzedaka for the seuda Permitted (Beit Yosef) No shame — it is a communal mitzvah. Do not deprive yourself of oneg out of false modesty.
Wealthy — minimum required? מאכלים חשובים Fish + meat + quality wine. Do not be content with the strict minimum when you have the means (minhag Magen Avraham: takkanat רשב״ג not to speculate on Shabbat fish prices).

✦ ❖ ✦ The 5 Practical Commandments of Siman רמ״ב in Brief

  1. Identify your economic category — wealthy, middle, or utterly poor — and adapt your oneg accordingly.
  2. Guarantee yourself the minimum — 3 seudot with at least כסא דהרסנא for the third, except in extreme situations.
  3. No false shame — tzedaka for Shabbat is a mitzvah, not an indignity. לוו עלי with a pledge is permitted.
  4. Organize the week — laundry on Thursday (lekhatchila), planned meals, Friday prep before 6 pm.
  5. Don't break the minhag — afiyat halechem at home remains ideal. הפרשת חלה is a treasure.
