Text of Seif Alef — Choulhan Aroukh with the glosses of the Rama
להזהר בכבוד שבת. ובו סעיף אחד: אֲפִלּוּ מִי שֶׁצָּרִיךְ לַאֲחֵרִים, אִם יֵשׁ לוֹ מְעַט מִשֶּׁלּוֹ, צָרִיךְ לְזָרֵז עַצְמוֹ לְכַבֵּד אֶת הַשַּׁבָּת. וְלֹא אָמְרוּ "עֲשֵׂה שַׁבַּתְּךָ חוֹל וְלֹא תִּצְטָרֵךְ לַבְּרִיּוֹת" אֶלָּא לְמִי שֶׁהַשָּׁעָה דְּחוּקָה לוֹ בְּיוֹתֵר. עַל כֵּן צָרִיךְ לְצַמְצֵם בִּשְׁאָר יָמִים כְּדֵי לְכַבֵּד אֶת הַשַּׁבָּת. מִתַּקָּנַת עֶזְרָא שֶׁיִּהְיוּ מְכַבְּסִים בְּגָדִים בַּחֲמִישִׁי בְּשַׁבָּת מִפְּנֵי כְּבוֹד הַשַּׁבָּת. הגה: נוֹהֲגִין לָלוּשׁ כְּדֵי שִׁעוּר חַלָּה בַּבַּיִת לַעֲשׂוֹת מֵהֶם לְחָמִים לִבְצֹעַ עֲלֵיהֶם בְּשַׁבָּת וְיוֹם טוֹב, וְהוּא מִכְּבוֹד שַׁבָּת וְיוֹם טוֹב, וְאֵין לְשַׁנּוֹת.
To take care for the kavod of Shabbat — one seif: Even one who depends on others, if he has a little of his own, must hasten to honor Shabbat. The Sages only said "make your Shabbat ordinary and do not depend on the creatures" with regard to one whose situation is extremely strained. One must therefore restrict himself the other days in order to honor Shabbat. It is from the takana of Ezra that one launders clothing on Thursday out of respect for Shabbat. — Hagahos HaRama: The custom is to knead at home the quantity of a challa and to make from it loaves for the betzi'ah of Shabbat and Yom Tov; this is part of the kavod of Shabbat and one should not deviate from it.

Chakiras HaYesod — Oneg Shabbat: דאורייתא or דרבנן?

Posek Era Position Reasoning
רש״י / יראים / ריב״ש Rishonim — 11th–14th c. Side ① — דאורייתא Anchored in "וקראת לשבת עונג" (Yeshayahu 58:13). Full Torah status.
רמב״ם Rishonim — 12th c. Side ② — דברי קבלה / דרבנן 3 peshatim among the Acharonim (Chasam Sofer, Tzemach Tzedek, Nechpeh BaKessef): דרבנן with an asmachta, or דברי קבלה (intermediate status).
שולחן ערוך הרב Acharonim — 19th c. דרבנן — takanas chachamim Rules like the Rambam: oneg Shabbat is a rabbinic institution supported by the words of the Neviim.
פסק למעשה Practice today ✦ All agree The effective obligation to honor and take pleasure in Shabbat is binding; the debate concerns the level (Torah / Sages / Prophets), with precise nafka minos for borderline cases.

The שלש חלוקות in the chiyuv of oneg Shabbat (Taz)

Level Situation Obligation Source
הָעָשִׁיר Wealthy — has the means מאכלים חשובים Fish, meat, fine clothing. One must not be stingy with the needs of Shabbat (Choulhan Aroukh + Mg״A).
הַבֵּינוֹנִי Middle — limited means כסא דהרסנא A simple dish suffices (Shabbat 118b). But one must restrict himself the other days to have at least that.
הֶעָנִי הַמְּדֻקְדָּק Dire poverty עשה שבתך חול When the situation is extremely strained, one may "make his Shabbat ordinary" rather than depend on others (Pesachim 113a).

Frequently asked questions — Siman רמ״ב

What is the essential obligation of Siman רמ״ב of the Choulhan Aroukh?

According to the Choulhan Aroukh, Orach Chaim רמ״ב:1, even one who depends on others must, if he has a little of his own, hasten to honor Shabbat (kavod Shabbat) and find his pleasure in it (oneg Shabbat). One must restrict himself the other days in order to honor Shabbat properly.

Is oneg Shabbat a Torah obligation (דאורייתא) or rabbinic (דרבנן)?

There is a fundamental mahloket: Rashi, the Yereim, and the Rivash hold that oneg Shabbat is de'oraisa, anchored in the pasuk "וקראת לשבת עונג" (Yeshayahu 58:13). The Rambam and the Choulhan Aroukh HaRav (of the Admour HaZaken) hold it is divrei kabala or derabanan. Practically, all agree that the obligation is binding.

What does "עשה שבתך חול ואל תצטרך לבריות" mean?

This dictum of the Gemara (Pesachim 113a) means: "Make your Shabbat like a weekday rather than depend on others." According to the Choulhan Aroukh, this applies only to one whose situation is extremely strained — not to the general case. The Taz and the Magen Avraham accordingly distinguish 3 chalukos (wealthy, middle, dire poverty).

What is Tikkun Ezra and does it apply to a modern washing machine?

Tikkun Ezra (Bava Kama 113) is the takana that one launders clothing on Thursday out of respect for Shabbat. The Chut Shani and other modern Acharonim discuss its application to the washing machine: should one prefer Thursday? See Level 2 (Lamdan) for the full pilpul.

May one borrow money for the needs of Shabbat (sougya of 'levu alai')?

The Yerushalmi reports in the name of Hashem Himself: "לוו עלי ואני פורע" ("Borrow on My account and I will repay"). The Acharonim debate: the Gra and the Mahari Halevi disagree on the practical scope. Level 2 (Lamdan) treats the sougya in detail, including the distinction between mashkon and halvaa.
