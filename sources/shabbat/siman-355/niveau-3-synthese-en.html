1. The central axiom

Siman 355 in one sentence.
Open water is a karmelis: transferring between it and a house requires a "partition." The siman codifies two leniencies: the suspended partition (mechitza teluyah), virtually "extended downward" toward the water (גוד אחית), and kocho — when the object or water arrives indirectly, not thrown directly. Kevod habriyos further widens these leniencies for the outhouse.

3. The two leniencies

Leniency 1 — suspended partition: a partition that does not touch the water is virtually "extended downward" (גוד אחית). Valid only above water (10 deep), and made to permit.
↓
Leniency 2 — kocho: when the object or water is not thrown directly but arrives indirectly (on a board, along a wall), it is permitted in any karmelis.
↓
Expansion — kevod habriyos: for the outhouse, we ease (broken board, outhouse between two houses without eruv b'dieved).

4. Decision tree

Q1: Do you wish to draw water from a gezuzṭra or ship?
↓ YES → partition around the hole (4×4); ship → perforated board suffices
Q2: Is it an outhouse above water?
↓ YES → suspended partition made to permit, or board (kocho)
Q3: Is the outhouse above a non-water karmelis (ditch)?
↓ YES → suspended partition useless; only a board (kocho) works
→ Water less than 10 tefachim → no "water" status. In doubt → consult your Rav.

5. Suspended partition: conditions

ConditionRequirement
PlaceOnly above water (not a dry ditch)
Water depthAt least 10 tefachim; otherwise no "water" status
IntentMade to permit transferring — not only for privacy
Alternative (kocho)A board / reed that receives indirectly — valid in any karmelis

6. Mnemonic תָּלוּי

ת — תְּלוּיָה עֲשׂוּיָה לְהַתִּיר: the suspended partition must be made to permit, and only above water.

ל — לִמְלֹא בִּמְחִיצָה: for drawing water, a partition around the 4×4 hole (on a ship, a perforated board suffices).

ו — וְכֹחוֹ בְּכַרְמְלִית: what arrives indirectly (kocho) is permitted in any karmelis.

י — יֵשׁ עֹמֶק עֲשָׂרָה: only water 10 tefachim deep has "water" status.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Believing an ordinary partition (made for privacy) suffices. It must be made to permit transferring.
Pitfall 2: Using a suspended partition above a dry ditch. This leniency is only above water.
Pitfall 3: Relying on shallow water. Water less than 10 tefachim has no "water" status.
Pitfall 4: Drawing from a gezuzṭra without a partition. A partition is needed, at least around the 4×4 hole.

10. The practical takeaways of Siman 355

For daily conduct

  1. Drawing water from a balcony: partition around the hole (4×4); on a ship, a perforated board suffices.
  2. Suspended partition: valid only above water (10 deep), made to permit.
  3. Kocho (indirect arrival): permitted in any karmelis.
  4. Outhouse: kevod habriyos widens leniencies.
  5. In doubt — consult your Rav.
  6. Deep pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
