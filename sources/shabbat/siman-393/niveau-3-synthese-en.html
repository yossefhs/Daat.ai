1. The central axiom

Siman שצ״ג in one sentence.
Two time limits for the eruv of courtyards and the partnership of alleys. On a Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos, one does not make them: « אין מערבין עירובי חצירו׳ ושיתופי מבואו׳ בי״ט שחל להיות בע״ש » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:א); if he forgot to make the eruv on Erev Yom Tov, and the two days of Yom Tov fall on Thursday and Friday, he makes it on Yom Tov conditionally — for the two days of Yom Tov of the Diaspora, not for those of Rosh Hashanah (seif א). At twilight, one makes them, even if one has already accepted the addition to Shabbos; and the seif reports the opinion of those who forbid it then (seif ב; section 5). Two people acquire the eruv when, for one, the eruv made by day was eaten at twilight and, for the other, it was made at twilight; not a single person, for whom it was made and eaten at twilight (seif ג).

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes this siman in 3 seifim (393:1 to 393:3), which correspond rank for rank to those of the Mechaber. Level 4 notes where its text differs: the reason for the prohibition (point א); the bread of the second day (point ב); the addition to Shabbos and the Arvis prayer (point ג); at the start of its seif 3, what the Rema writes in siman 394, in his gloss introduced by הגה in seif ב (point ד); the eruv eaten at twilight, and the eruv made for two (point ה).

4. Decision tree — the eruv of courtyards made close to the onset of Shabbos

Q1: Is the day on which one wants to make the eruv a Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos? (seif א)
↓ YES → one makes neither the eruv of courtyards nor the partnership of alleys (seif א) → Q2. NO → Q3.
Q2: Did he forget to make the eruv on Erev Yom Tov, and did the two days of Yom Tov fall on Thursday and Friday? (seif א)
↓ YES, two days of Yom Tov of the Diaspora → he makes the eruv on Yom Tov conditionally, with the two declarations (seif א); on the bread of the second day, the commentators diverge (section 5). Two days of Rosh Hashanah → the condition does not apply, for they are like one long day (seif א).
Q3: Is it twilight? (seif ב)
↓ One makes the eruv at twilight (seif ב). If one has already accepted the addition to Shabbos → the seif reports two opinions, and the commentators diverge; the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes that the main view is the first opinion for the mere acceptance of the addition, and distinguishes the case of one who has prayed the Arvis of Shabbos (393:2; section 5).
Q4: Was the eruv made, or eaten, at twilight? (seif ג)
↓ For two people — for one, made by day and eaten at twilight; for the other, made at twilight → both acquire the eruv (seif ג). For a single person, made at twilight and eaten at twilight → it is forbidden (seif ג). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav first writes, on its own, that even if the eruv was eaten at twilight, he has acquired the eruv, with, in parentheses, « וּבְדִיעֲבַד » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג); then the case of two, and that of a single person (393:3; Level 4, point ה).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

5. The bread of the second day (seif א) and the addition to Shabbos (seif ב) — the commentators diverge

The bread of the second day (seif א)

WorkWhat it writes
Shulchan Aruch, seif א« ולמחר יאמר אם היום קודש הרי עירבתי מאתמול ואם היום חול יהא זה עירוב והני מילי בשני י״ט של גליות אבל בשני ימים טובים של ר״ה לא דכיומא אריכתא דמי » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:א) — it does not say over which bread the next day's declaration is made
Shulchan Aruch HaRav (393:1)After the same declaration: « וְעֵצָה טוֹבָה שֶׁיְּעָרֵב לְמָחָר בְּפַת שֶׁעֵרֵב בָּהּ אֶתְמוֹל, שֶׁאִם יְעָרֵב בְּפַת אַחֶרֶת — יֵאָסֵר לֶאֱכוֹל שְׁתֵּיהֶן קֹדֶם שֶׁחָשְׁכָה לֵיל שַׁבָּת » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:א) — it is good advice to make the eruv the next day with the bread of the day before; with another bread, it would be forbidden to eat the two before nightfall on Shabbos night. Then it writes that the conditional eruv applies only to the two days of Yom Tov of the exile, not to those of Rosh Hashanah (Level 4, point ב)

After accepting the addition to Shabbos (seif ב)

WorkWhat it writes
Shulchan Aruch, seif ב« א׳ עירובי חצירו׳ וא׳ שתופי מבואו׳ מערבין אותם בין השמשו׳ ואפילו אם כבר קיבל עליו תוספ׳ שבת ויש אוסרים אם קיבל עליו תוספת שבת » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ב) — it reports the two opinions without writing which is the main one
Shulchan Aruch HaRav (393:2)After the same two opinions, in parentheses: « וְהָעִקָּר כִּסְבָרָא הָרִאשׁוֹנָה בְּקַבָּלַת תּוֹסֶפֶת שַׁבָּת בִּלְבָד. אֲבָל אִם הִתְפַּלֵּל תְּפִלַּת עַרְבִית שֶׁל שַׁבָּת — הֲרֵי קִבֵּל עָלָיו שַׁבָּת עַצְמָהּ, כֵּיוָן שֶׁהִתְפַּלֵּל תְּפִלַּת לֵיל שַׁבָּת, וְנֶאֱסַר בְּכָל הַשְּׁבוּתִים כְּמוֹ בְּשַׁבָּת עַצְמָהּ, לְבַד מִמַּה שֶּׁנַּעֲשֶׂה עַל יְדֵי אַחֵר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רס״א וְרס״ג. וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם הוּא לֹא הִתְפַּלֵּל, אִם רֹב הַצִּבּוּר הִתְפַּלְלוּ — הַמִּעוּט נִמְשָׁכִים אַחֲרֵיהֶם בְּעַל כָּרְחָן. וְלֹא מִשֶּׁגָּמְרוּ הַתְּפִלָּה, אֶלָּא אֲפִלּוּ מִשֶּׁהִתְחִילוּ אוֹתָהּ, דְּהַיְנוּ עֲנִיַּת "בָּרְכוּ", כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ב) — the main view is the first opinion, for the mere acceptance of the addition to Shabbos; but one who has prayed the Arvis prayer of Shabbos has accepted upon himself Shabbos itself, and is forbidden in all the shevusim as on Shabbos itself, except for what is done through another; and the minority who have not prayed are drawn after the majority of the congregation who have prayed, from when they began the prayer, that is, from the response of Barchu (Level 4, point ג)
The commentatorsThey diverge on the opinion that forbids: each is cited by his own text in Level 2 (section 5)

What the exception of what is done through another permits for the eruv, this page does not decide (Level 4, point ג). Consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic ת-ג-ב-א-ל

Five words of the seifim, in their order:

ת — תנאי: if he forgot to make the eruv on Erev Yom Tov, and the two days of Yom Tov fall on Thursday and Friday, he makes it conditionally (seif א) — within the limit of the next letter.

ג — גליות: for the two days of Yom Tov of the Diaspora only, not for those of Rosh Hashanah (seif א).

ב — בין השמשות: one makes the eruv at twilight, even if one has already accepted the addition to Shabbos (seif ב) — with the opinion of the next letter.

א — אוסרים: some forbid it if one has accepted the addition to Shabbos (seif ב; section 5).

ל — לשנים: the eruv made for two — for one, made by day and eaten at twilight; for the other, made at twilight — serves both; for a single person, made and eaten at twilight, it is forbidden (seif ג).

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that the conditional eruv applies to every Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos. Seif א gives it to one who forgot to make the eruv on Erev Yom Tov, when the two days of Yom Tov fall on Thursday and Friday, and limits it: « והני מילי בשני י״ט של גליות אבל בשני ימים טובים של ר״ה לא דכיומא אריכתא דמי » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:א).
Pitfall 2: Saying only one declaration. Seif א writes two: on the first day, if today is a weekday, let this be an eruv, and if it is holy, his words are nothing; the next day, if today is holy, he made the eruv yesterday, and if it is a weekday, let this be an eruv. On the bread of the second day, the commentators diverge (section 5).
Pitfall 3: Keeping only one opinion in seif ב. After the rule, the seif reports: « ויש אוסרים אם קיבל עליו תוספת שבת » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ב); the commentators diverge on this opinion (section 5). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 4: Summing up the Shulchan Aruch HaRav by the first opinion alone. The Rav holds the first opinion to be the main view for the mere acceptance of the addition to Shabbos; but, he then writes, one who has prayed the Arvis of Shabbos has accepted upon himself Shabbos itself, and is forbidden in all the shevusim as on Shabbos itself, except for what is done through another; and the minority who have not prayed are drawn after the majority of the congregation, from the response of Barchu (393:2; section 5). What the exception of what is done through another permits for the eruv, this page does not decide (Level 4, point ג): consult your Rav.
Pitfall 5: Extending to a single person the rule of two. Seif ג writes: « אבל אם עירב עליו בין השמשו׳ ונאכל עירובו בין השמשו׳ אסור » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ג).
Pitfall 6: Attributing to the Rema the parenthesis of seif א. « ועיין לקמן סי׳ תקכ״ח » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:א) is in small type, without הגה: it is attributed to no one (Level 1).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שצ״ג

For daily conduct

  1. The Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos: one makes on it neither the eruv of courtyards nor the partnership of alleys (seif א).
  2. Forgetting, when the two days of Yom Tov fall on Thursday and Friday: one who forgot to make the eruv on Erev Yom Tov makes it on Yom Tov conditionally, with one declaration on the first day and one the next day — for the two days of Yom Tov of the Diaspora, not for those of Rosh Hashanah (seif א); on the bread of the second day, the commentators diverge (section 5).
  3. Twilight: one makes the eruv of courtyards and the partnership of alleys at twilight, even if one has already accepted the addition to Shabbos; and some forbid it if one has accepted the addition to Shabbos (seif ב). The commentators diverge, and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav distinguishes the case of one who has prayed the Arvis of Shabbos (393:2; section 5): consult your Rav.
  4. The eruv made for two: for one, the eruv made by day was eaten at twilight; for the other, it was made at twilight: both acquire the eruv; for a single person, the eruv made at twilight and eaten at twilight: it is forbidden (seif ג).
  5. When in doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
