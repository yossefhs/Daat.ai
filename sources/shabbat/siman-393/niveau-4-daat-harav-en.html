DAAT · LEVEL 4 — DAAT HARAV · Shitah of the Alter Rebbe

דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on Siman 393

The eruv on a Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos; the conditional eruv of one who forgot, when two days of Yom Tov of the exile fall on Thursday and Friday; the eruv made at bein hashmashos and after accepting the addition to Shabbos; the eruv eaten during bein hashmashos. The 3 seifim of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, compared with the 3 seifim of the Mechaber.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav is not a commentary on the Mechaber: it is a Shulchan Aruch in its own right, written by the Alter Rebbe, with its own division and numbering of the seifim. Here the Shulchan Aruch HaRav announces 3 seifim at the head of the siman, and the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber has 3 as well. Sefaria serves the Rav's text in 3 blocks, which this page reproduces and numbers as they stand (section 1). Each seif of the Rav corresponds to the seif of the same rank in the Mechaber: the Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos in seif 1 (the Mechaber's seif א), bein hashmashos and the addition to Shabbos in seif 2 (ב), the eruv eaten during bein hashmashos and the eruv made for two in seif 3 (ג). The Rav's seif 3 also writes, on the eruv that need exist only at bein hashmashos and on the bread broken on it, what the Rema writes in Siman 394, in his gloss introduced by הגה in seif ב (section 2, point ד). In the English text of this page, the Rav's seifim are designated by their number (393:3, seif 3), the Mechaber's by their Hebrew letter (the Mechaber's seif ג); in the Hebrew references in parentheses, both numberings are in letters, and the reference always names the work (שו״ע או״ח for the Mechaber, שו״ע הרב for the Rav).

This page gathers, for Siman 393, the full text of the Rav with its translation, where his text differs from that of the Mechaber, and the practical conduct that follows from it.

1 · Text of the Alter Rebbe

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן שצ״ג

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav of the Alter Rebbe

סימן שצ״ג — דִּין עֵרוּב [בְּ]יוֹם טוֹב שֶׁחָל בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת וְדִין בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת לְעֵרוּב — וּבוֹ ג׳ סְעִיפִים
Source: Kehot edition, as reproduced on Sefaria. The heading announces 3 seifim; Sefaria serves the text in 3 blocks (the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has 3 seifim, under its own numbering).

סעיף א דִּין עֵרוּב [בְּ]יוֹם טוֹב שֶׁחָל בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת וְדִין בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת לְעֵרוּב וּבוֹ ג' סְעִיפִים: אֵין מְעָרְבִין עֵרוּבֵי חֲצֵרוֹת…

דִּין עֵרוּב [בְּ]יוֹם טוֹב שֶׁחָל בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת וְדִין בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת לְעֵרוּב וּבוֹ ג' סְעִיפִים:

אֵין מְעָרְבִין עֵרוּבֵי חֲצֵרוֹת וְשִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת בְּיוֹם טוֹב שֶׁחָל לִהְיוֹת בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁנִּרְאֶה כִּמְתַקֵּן דָּבָר.

וְאִם חָלוּ ב' יָמִים טוֹבִים בְּיוֹם ה' וּבְיוֹם ו', וְשָׁכַח וְלֹא עֵרֵב מֵעֶרֶב יוֹם טוֹב — יְעָרֵב בְּיוֹם טוֹב עַל תְּנַאי, שֶׁיֹּאמַר בְּיוֹם טוֹב א': אִם הַיּוֹם חֹל — יְהֵא זֶה עֵרוּב, וְאִם הַיּוֹם קֹדֶשׁ — אֵין בִּדְבָרַי כְּלוּם. וּלְמָחָר יֹאמַר: אִם הַיּוֹם קֹדֶשׁ — הֲרֵי עֵרַבְתִּי אֶתְמוֹל, וְאִם הַיּוֹם חֹל — יְהֵא זֶה עֵרוּב. וְעֵצָה טוֹבָה שֶׁיְּעָרֵב לְמָחָר בְּפַת שֶׁעֵרֵב בָּהּ אֶתְמוֹל, שֶׁאִם יְעָרֵב בְּפַת אַחֶרֶת — יֵאָסֵר לֶאֱכוֹל שְׁתֵּיהֶן קֹדֶם שֶׁחָשְׁכָה לֵיל שַׁבָּת.

בַּמֶּה דְּבָרִים אֲמוּרִים? בְּב' יָמִים טוֹבִים שֶׁל גָּלֻיּוֹת. אֲבָל בְּב' יָמִים טוֹבִים שֶׁל רֹאשׁ הַשָּׁנָה אֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לְעָרֵב עַל תְּנַאי, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁהֵם כְּיוֹם אֶחָד אָרֹךְ, כְּמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן ת״ר:

The law of the eruv on a Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos, and the law of bein hashmashos (twilight) for the eruv; it contains 3 seifim:

One does not make eruvei chatzeiros or shitufei mevo'os on a Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos, because it appears as if one is fixing (mesaken) something.

And if two days of Yom Tov fell on Thursday and on Friday, and he forgot and did not make the eruv on Erev Yom Tov, he makes the eruv on Yom Tov conditionally (al tenai), saying on the first day of Yom Tov: “If today is a weekday (chol), let this be an eruv; and if today is holy (kodesh), my words are nothing.” And the next day he says: “If today is holy, I [already] made the eruv yesterday; and if today is a weekday, let this be an eruv.” And it is good advice (eitzah tovah) that he make the eruv the next day with the bread with which he made it the day before, for if he makes the eruv with other bread, he will be forbidden to eat both of them before nightfall of Shabbos night.

When is this said? For the two days of Yom Tov of the exile (shel galuyos). But for the two days of Yom Tov of Rosh Hashanah he cannot make the eruv conditionally, because they are like one long day, as will be explained in Siman 600:

סעיף ב אֶחָד עֵרוּבֵי חֲצֵרוֹת וְאֶחָד שִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת…

אֶחָד עֵרוּבֵי חֲצֵרוֹת וְאֶחָד שִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת מְעָרְבִים אוֹתָם בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, מִטַּעַם שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רס״א.

וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם כְּבָר קִבֵּל עָלָיו תּוֹסֶפֶת שַׁבָּת. וְיֵשׁ אוֹסְרִין אִם קִבֵּל עָלָיו תּוֹסֶפֶת שַׁבָּת (וְהָעִקָּר כִּסְבָרָא הָרִאשׁוֹנָה בְּקַבָּלַת תּוֹסֶפֶת שַׁבָּת בִּלְבָד. אֲבָל אִם הִתְפַּלֵּל תְּפִלַּת עַרְבִית שֶׁל שַׁבָּת — הֲרֵי קִבֵּל עָלָיו שַׁבָּת עַצְמָהּ, כֵּיוָן שֶׁהִתְפַּלֵּל תְּפִלַּת לֵיל שַׁבָּת, וְנֶאֱסַר בְּכָל הַשְּׁבוּתִים כְּמוֹ בְּשַׁבָּת עַצְמָהּ, לְבַד מִמַּה שֶּׁנַּעֲשֶׂה עַל יְדֵי אַחֵר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רס״א וְרס״ג. וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם הוּא לֹא הִתְפַּלֵּל, אִם רֹב הַצִּבּוּר הִתְפַּלְלוּ — הַמִּעוּט נִמְשָׁכִים אַחֲרֵיהֶם בְּעַל כָּרְחָן. וְלֹא מִשֶּׁגָּמְרוּ הַתְּפִלָּה, אֶלָּא אֲפִלּוּ מִשֶּׁהִתְחִילוּ אוֹתָהּ, דְּהַיְנוּ עֲנִיַּת "בָּרְכוּ", כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם):

Both eruvei chatzeiros and shitufei mevo'os are made at bein hashmashos, for the reason explained in Siman 261.

And even if he has already accepted upon himself the addition to Shabbos (tosefes Shabbos). And some forbid (yesh osrin) if he has accepted upon himself the addition to Shabbos (and the main view is according to the first opinion, for the acceptance of the addition to Shabbos alone. But if he prayed the Shabbos Arvis prayer, he has accepted upon himself Shabbos itself, since he prayed the prayer of Shabbos night, and he is forbidden in all the shevusim as on Shabbos itself, except for what is done through another, as explained in Simanim 261 and 263. And even if he himself did not pray, if the majority of the congregation (rov hatzibbur) prayed, the minority is drawn after them against their will. And not from when they finished the prayer, but even from when they began it, that is, the response of “Barchu”, as explained there):

סעיף ג הָעֵרוּב אֵינוֹ צָרִיךְ לִהְיוֹת קַיָּם…

הָעֵרוּב אֵינוֹ צָרִיךְ לִהְיוֹת קַיָּם אֶלָּא בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, וְיָכוֹל לְאָכְלוֹ כְּשֶׁוַּדַּאי חֲשֵׁכָה (שֶׁכֵּיוָן שֶׁנִּקְבְּעָה דִירָתוֹ בִּמְקוֹם עֵרוּב בְּבֵין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, שֶׁהוּא תְּחִלַּת כְּנִיסַת הַשַּׁבָּת — שׁוּב אֵינָהּ נִפְקַעַת מִשָּׁם כָּל הַשַּׁבָּת, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁנּוֹטֵל מִשָּׁם עֵרוּבוֹ).

וְיֵשׁ לִבְצוֹעַ עָלָיו בְּשַׁבָּת שַׁחֲרִית (אוֹ בִּסְעֻדַּת הַלַּיְלָה אִם סוֹעֵד מִשֶּׁחָשֵׁכָה וַדַּאי), דְּהוֹאִיל וְנַעֲשֵׂית בּוֹ מִצְוָה אַחַת — יֵשׁ לַעֲשׂוֹת בּוֹ גַם כֵּן מִצְוָה אַחֶרֶת.

וַאֲפִלּוּ נֶאֱכַל עֵרוּבוֹ בְּבֵין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת — קָנָה עֵרוּב (וּבְדִיעֲבַד), כִּי בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת הוּא סְפֵק לַיְלָה, וְשֶׁמָּא נִכְנַס כְּבָר תְּחִלַּת הַשַּׁבָּת, וּסְפֵק דִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים לְהָקֵל.

וַאֲפִלּוּ עֵרֵב לִשְׁנַיִם, לְאֶחָד עֵרֵב מִבְּעוֹד יוֹם וְנֶאֱכַל עֵרוּבוֹ בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, וּלְאֶחָד עֵרֵב בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת וְנִשְׁאַר עֵרוּבוֹ קַיָּם עַד שֶׁחָשֵׁכָה — שְׁנֵיהֶם קָנוּ עֵרוּב, שֶׁלְּאוֹתוֹ שֶׁנֶּאֱכַל עֵרוּבוֹ בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת אָנוּ חוֹשְׁבִין שֶׁבֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת הוּא לַיְלָה, וּלְאוֹתוֹ שֶׁעֵרֵב לוֹ בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת אָנוּ חוֹשְׁבִין שֶׁבֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת הוּא יוֹם, שֶׁאֵין מְעָרְבִין מִשֶּׁתֶּחְשַׁךְ. וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁעֵרֵב לִשְׁנֵיהֶם בְּבֵין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת אֶחָד, וַהֲרֵי הֵן ב' קֻלּוֹת הַסּוֹתְרוֹת זוֹ אֶת זוֹ — אֵין בְּכָךְ כְּלוּם, כֵּיוָן שֶׁהֵם לִשְׁנֵי בְּנֵי אָדָם. אֲבָל אִם עֵרֵב לְאֶחָד בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת וְנֶאֱכַל הָעֵרוּב בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת — לֹא קָנָה עֵרוּב, וְאֵין מְקִלִּין כָּל כָּךְ לוֹמַר שֶׁבְּרֶגַע שֶׁעֵרֵב הָיָה עֲדַיִן יוֹם, וּכְשֶׁנֶּאֱכַל הָיָה כְּבָר לַיְלָה וּכְבָר קָנָה עֵרוּב:

The eruv need exist only at bein hashmashos, and he may eat it once it is certainly dark (for since his dwelling was established in the place of the eruv at bein hashmashos, which is the beginning of the entry of Shabbos, it is no longer uprooted from there for all of Shabbos, even though he takes his eruv from there).

And one should break bread (livtzoa) on it on Shabbos morning (or at the night meal, if he eats once it is certainly dark): since one mitzvah was performed with it, one should perform another mitzvah with it as well.

And even if his eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos, he has acquired the eruv (and after the fact [bedi'eved]), for bein hashmashos is a doubtful night, and perhaps the beginning of Shabbos has already entered; and a doubt in [a law] of rabbinic order (sefek divrei soferim) [is decided] leniently.

And even if he made an eruv for two [people] — for one he made the eruv while it was still day, and his eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos; for the other he made the eruv during bein hashmashos, and his eruv remained intact until nightfall — both have acquired the eruv: for the one whose eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos, we consider bein hashmashos to be night; and for the one for whom he made the eruv during bein hashmashos, we consider bein hashmashos to be day, for one does not make an eruv after nightfall. And although he made the eruv for both of them during one and the same bein hashmashos, and these are two leniencies that contradict each other, it does not matter, since they are for two people. But if he made an eruv for one [person] during bein hashmashos and the eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos, he has not acquired the eruv; and we are not so lenient as to say that at the moment he made the eruv it was still day, and when it was eaten it was already night and he had already acquired the eruv:

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim 393. Sefaria gives no Kuntres Acharon entry for this siman. The text is copied as Sefaria serves it: in the heading, one letter is in square brackets; it is left as it stands, and the translation keeps the word in square brackets.

2 · The Rav's text and the Mechaber's

לשון הרב מול סעיף המחבר

Where the Rav's text differs from the Mechaber's seif

Each point sets side by side a seif of the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch (its numbering, 3 seifim, designated by their letter) and the corresponding seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (its own numbering, also 3 seifim), in the order of the Rav's seifim, and notes only what the comparison of the two texts shows; in point ד, the comparison is with a gloss of the Rema in Siman 394, introduced by הגה in seif ב. A point noted is not thereby peculiar to the Rav: the page sometimes names its source; it notes neither all the sources nor all the divergences.

נקודה א — טעם האיסור לערב ביום טוב שחל בערב שבת (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:א)

Point א — why one does not make the eruv on a Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:1)

The Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos

What the Mechaber writes: « אין מערבין עירובי חצירו' ושיתופי מבואו' בי״ט שחל להיות בע״ש » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:א). He gives no reason.

What the Rav writes: « אֵין מְעָרְבִין עֵרוּבֵי חֲצֵרוֹת וְשִׁתּוּפֵי מְבוֹאוֹת בְּיוֹם טוֹב שֶׁחָל לִהְיוֹת בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁנִּרְאֶה כִּמְתַקֵּן דָּבָר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:א).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif א states the prohibition without a reason; the Rav's text gives one: it appears as if one is fixing (mesaken) something.

Among the commentators: the Mishnah Berurah, later than the Rav, writes « מפני שנראה כמתקן ביו״ט לצורך מחר ואם שכח ועירב יש להסתפק אם מהני [פמ״ג] » (משנה ברורה שצ״ג ס״ק א). The Rav's seif 1 does not write the case of one who forgot and made the eruv on that day.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, one does not make eruvei chatzeiros or shitufei mevo'os on a Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos (393:1); for one who forgot and made it on that day, the Mishnah Berurah raises a doubt in the name of the Pri Megadim (above): consult your Rav. For one who forgot to make the eruv when two days of Yom Tov of the exile fall on Thursday and Friday, see point ב. See section 4, points ① and ②.

נקודה ב — באיזו פת יערב ביום השני (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:א)

Point ב — with which bread to make the eruv on the second day (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:1)

The bread of the second day

What the Mechaber writes: for the two days of Yom Tov that fell on Thursday and Friday, « ולמחר יאמר אם היום קודש הרי עירבתי מאתמול ואם היום חול יהא זה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:א). He does not say with which bread.

What the Rav writes: after the same formula, « וְעֵצָה טוֹבָה שֶׁיְּעָרֵב לְמָחָר בְּפַת שֶׁעֵרֵב בָּהּ אֶתְמוֹל, שֶׁאִם יְעָרֵב בְּפַת אַחֶרֶת — יֵאָסֵר לֶאֱכוֹל שְׁתֵּיהֶן קֹדֶם שֶׁחָשְׁכָה לֵיל שַׁבָּת » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:א).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif א gives the formula of the second day without saying over which bread it is said; the Rav's text writes that it is good advice, « וְעֵצָה טוֹבָה » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:א), to make the eruv on the second day with the bread of the day before, and gives the reason: with other bread, it would be forbidden to eat both before nightfall of Shabbos night.

Among the commentators: the Magen Avraham, before the Rav, reports this practice in the name of the Tur and proposes the same reason, which he introduces with ונ״ל: « על אותה הפת (טור) ונ״ל טעמו משום שאם יקח פת אחר יאסר לאכול שני לחם קודם שחשכה ליל שבת » (מגן אברהם שצ״ג ס״ק א). On whether the eruv of the second day must be made with that same bread, the commentators differ: see Level 2.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, one who makes the eruv conditionally during two days of Yom Tov of the exile that fell on Thursday and Friday does well — it is good advice — to make it on the second day with the same bread; with other bread, he would be forbidden to eat both before nightfall of Shabbos night (393:1); on this bread, the commentators differ (above). The conditional eruv does not apply to the two days of Rosh Hashanah, which are like one long day (393:1). See section 4, point ②.

נקודה ג — תוספת שבת ותפילת ערבית (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ב)

Point ג — the addition to Shabbos, and the Arvis prayer (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:2)

After accepting Shabbos

What the Mechaber writes: « א' עירובי חצירו' וא' שתופי מבואו' מערבין אותם בין השמשו' ואפילו אם כבר קיבל עליו תוספ' שבת ויש אוסרים אם קיבל עליו תוספת שבת » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ב). He reports both opinions without writing which is the main one.

What the Rav writes: « וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם כְּבָר קִבֵּל עָלָיו תּוֹסֶפֶת שַׁבָּת. וְיֵשׁ אוֹסְרִין אִם קִבֵּל עָלָיו תּוֹסֶפֶת שַׁבָּת (וְהָעִקָּר כִּסְבָרָא הָרִאשׁוֹנָה בְּקַבָּלַת תּוֹסֶפֶת שַׁבָּת בִּלְבָד. אֲבָל אִם הִתְפַּלֵּל תְּפִלַּת עַרְבִית שֶׁל שַׁבָּת — הֲרֵי קִבֵּל עָלָיו שַׁבָּת עַצְמָהּ, כֵּיוָן שֶׁהִתְפַּלֵּל תְּפִלַּת לֵיל שַׁבָּת, וְנֶאֱסַר בְּכָל הַשְּׁבוּתִים כְּמוֹ בְּשַׁבָּת עַצְמָהּ, לְבַד מִמַּה שֶּׁנַּעֲשֶׂה עַל יְדֵי אַחֵר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רס״א וְרס״ג. וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם הוּא לֹא הִתְפַּלֵּל, אִם רֹב הַצִּבּוּר הִתְפַּלְלוּ — הַמִּעוּט נִמְשָׁכִים אַחֲרֵיהֶם בְּעַל כָּרְחָן. וְלֹא מִשֶּׁגָּמְרוּ הַתְּפִלָּה, אֶלָּא אֲפִלּוּ מִשֶּׁהִתְחִילוּ אוֹתָהּ, דְּהַיְנוּ עֲנִיַּת "בָּרְכוּ", כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם) » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ב).

The difference: the Rav's text writes, in parentheses, that the main view is according to the first opinion for the acceptance of the addition to Shabbos alone; then, introduced by « אֲבָל אִם הִתְפַּלֵּל » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ב), the case of one who prayed the Shabbos Arvis, and that of the minority when the majority of the congregation prayed, from the response of Barchu. The Mechaber's seif ב does not write these two cases; the Rav refers to his Simanim 261 and 263.

Among the commentators: on the opinion « ויש אוסרים » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ב), the commentators differ: see Level 2.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, one makes eruvei chatzeiros and shitufei mevo'os at bein hashmashos, even after accepting upon oneself the addition to Shabbos; but one who prayed the Shabbos Arvis has accepted upon himself Shabbos itself, and he is forbidden in all the shevusim as on Shabbos, except for what is done through another; and the minority who did not pray is drawn after the majority of the congregation who prayed, from when it began the prayer, that is, from the response of Barchu (393:2). The Mechaber's seif ב also reports the opinion that forbids from the acceptance of the addition to Shabbos, and the commentators differ on that opinion; what the exception of what is done through another permits for the eruv, this page does not decide: consult your Rav. See section 4, point ③.

נקודה ד — העירוב אינו צריך להיות קיים אלא בין השמשות, והבציעה עליו (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג)

Point ד — the eruv need exist only at bein hashmashos, and one breaks bread on it (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:3)

What the Rav writes before the case of the two

What the Mechaber and the Rema write: the Mechaber's seif ג, in Siman 393, does not write these rules. In Siman 394, the Rema's gloss introduced by הגה in seif ב writes: « והעירוב א״צ להיות קיים רק בין השמשות (טור) ויוכל לאכלו כשודאי חשיכה (הגהות אשירי ומרדכי פרק בכל מערבין) ויש לבצוע עליו בשחרית בשבת (מנהגים) ודוקא במקום שנוהגים לערב כל ערב שבת אבל עדיף טפי לערב על כל השנה בפעם אחת כדלעיל סי' שס״ח (ב״י) » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ד:ב).

What the Rav writes: at the beginning of his seif 3, « הָעֵרוּב אֵינוֹ צָרִיךְ לִהְיוֹת קַיָּם אֶלָּא בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, וְיָכוֹל לְאָכְלוֹ כְּשֶׁוַּדַּאי חֲשֵׁכָה (שֶׁכֵּיוָן שֶׁנִּקְבְּעָה דִירָתוֹ בִּמְקוֹם עֵרוּב בְּבֵין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת, שֶׁהוּא תְּחִלַּת כְּנִיסַת הַשַּׁבָּת — שׁוּב אֵינָהּ נִפְקַעַת מִשָּׁם כָּל הַשַּׁבָּת, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁנּוֹטֵל מִשָּׁם עֵרוּבוֹ). וְיֵשׁ לִבְצוֹעַ עָלָיו בְּשַׁבָּת שַׁחֲרִית (אוֹ בִּסְעֻדַּת הַלַּיְלָה אִם סוֹעֵד מִשֶּׁחָשֵׁכָה וַדַּאי), דְּהוֹאִיל וְנַעֲשֵׂית בּוֹ מִצְוָה אַחַת — יֵשׁ לַעֲשׂוֹת בּוֹ גַם כֵּן מִצְוָה אַחֶרֶת » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג).

The difference: the Rav's text writes in Siman 393 what the Rema writes in Siman 394; it adds, in parentheses, the reason why one may eat the eruv once it is certainly dark, and the night meal for one who eats it once it is certainly dark; and it gives the reason for breaking bread on the eruv: a mitzvah was performed with it. The Rav's seif 3 does not write the end of the Rema's gloss, « ודוקא במקום שנוהגים לערב כל ערב שבת אבל עדיף טפי לערב על כל השנה בפעם אחת כדלעיל סי' שס״ח (ב״י) » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ד:ב).

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, the eruv need exist only at bein hashmashos, and one may eat it once it is certainly dark; one should break bread on it on Shabbos morning, or at the night meal if one eats that meal once it is certainly dark (393:3). What the end of the Rema's gloss, « ודוקא במקום שנוהגים לערב כל ערב שבת אבל עדיף טפי לערב על כל השנה בפעם אחת כדלעיל סי' שס״ח (ב״י) » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ד:ב), refers to, and whether it holds according to the Rav, this page does not decide: consult your Rav. See section 4, point ④.

נקודה ה — עירוב שנאכל בין השמשות, ועירוב לשנים (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג)

Point ה — the eruv eaten during bein hashmashos, and the eruv made for two (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:3)

The eruv eaten during bein hashmashos

What the Mechaber writes: « עירב לשנים לאחד מבעוד יום ונאכל העירוב בין השמשו' ולא' עירב בין השמשות שניהם קנו עירוב שלאותו שנאכל עירובו בין השמשו' אנו חושבין אותו לילה ולאותו שהניח עירובו בין השמשו' חושבים אותו יום אבל אם עירב עליו בין השמשו' ונאכל עירובו בין השמשו' אסור » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ג).

What the Rav writes: « וַאֲפִלּוּ נֶאֱכַל עֵרוּבוֹ בְּבֵין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת — קָנָה עֵרוּב (וּבְדִיעֲבַד), כִּי בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת הוּא סְפֵק לַיְלָה, וְשֶׁמָּא נִכְנַס כְּבָר תְּחִלַּת הַשַּׁבָּת, וּסְפֵק דִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים לְהָקֵל » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג); then, after the case of the two, « וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁעֵרֵב לִשְׁנֵיהֶם בְּבֵין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת אֶחָד, וַהֲרֵי הֵן ב' קֻלּוֹת הַסּוֹתְרוֹת זוֹ אֶת זוֹ — אֵין בְּכָךְ כְּלוּם, כֵּיוָן שֶׁהֵם לִשְׁנֵי בְּנֵי אָדָם. אֲבָל אִם עֵרֵב לְאֶחָד בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת וְנֶאֱכַל הָעֵרוּב בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת — לֹא קָנָה עֵרוּב, וְאֵין מְקִלִּין כָּל כָּךְ לוֹמַר שֶׁבְּרֶגַע שֶׁעֵרֵב הָיָה עֲדַיִן יוֹם, וּכְשֶׁנֶּאֱכַל הָיָה כְּבָר לַיְלָה וּכְבָר קָנָה עֵרוּב » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג).

The difference: in the case of the two, the Mechaber's seif ג writes « אנו חושבין אותו לילה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ג) and « חושבים אותו יום » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ג) without a reason, and, for the last case, writes « אסור » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:ג) without a reason. The Rav's text first writes, on its own, that the eruv eaten during bein hashmashos has acquired, with, in parentheses, « וּבְדִיעֲבַד » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג), and gives the reason: bein hashmashos is a doubtful night, and a doubt in [a law] of rabbinic order is decided leniently; in the case of the two, it writes that these are two leniencies that contradict each other and that it does not matter, since they are for two people; and, for the last case, it writes « לֹא קָנָה עֵרוּב » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ג:ג) and gives the reason.

Among the commentators: the Taz, before the Rav, writes on the last case « דכולי האי לא מקלינן לשווי' לגבי האי עירוב מקצתו יום דהיינו לענין הנחה ומקצתו לילה לענין אכילה שצ״ל בשבת » (ט״ז או״ח שצ״ג ס״ק ה). The Mishnah Berurah, later than the Rav, writes on the case of the two « ואף שהאיש הזה נעשה שליח בעד שניהם וסותרין אלו לאלו מ״מ כיון דעירובי חצרות מלתא דרבנן היא תלינן לקולא לכל אחד ואחד משום דספיקא דרבנן לקולא [גמרא] » (משנה ברורה שצ״ג ס״ק ט״ו).

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, even if the eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos, he has acquired the eruv (the Rav adds in parentheses: after the fact); if he made an eruv for two people — for one while it was still day, and his eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos; for the other during bein hashmashos, and his eruv remained intact until nightfall — both have acquired the eruv; but if he made an eruv for one during bein hashmashos and the eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos, he has not acquired the eruv (393:3). See section 4, point ⑤.

4 · Lemaaseh

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

The practical Chabad conduct

Points of halachic conduct that flow directly from the Shulchan Aruch HaRav Siman 393, in the Chabad sensibility. Each point refers to its seif of the Rav, in the order of the text.

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman 393

  • ① The Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos: one does not make eruvei chatzeiros or shitufei mevo'os on a Yom Tov that falls on Erev Shabbos, because it appears as if one is fixing something (393:1). For one who forgot and made it on that day, see section 2, point א; consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:1.
  • ② Two days of Yom Tov of the exile, on Thursday and Friday: one who forgot and did not make the eruv on Erev Yom Tov makes it on Yom Tov conditionally: on the first day he says: if today is a weekday, let this be an eruv, and if today is holy, my words are nothing; the next day: if today is holy, I made the eruv yesterday, and if today is a weekday, let this be an eruv. It is good advice to make the eruv on the second day with the same bread: with other bread, he would be forbidden to eat both before nightfall of Shabbos night. This applies only to the two days of Yom Tov of the exile; for the two days of Rosh Hashanah, one cannot make the eruv conditionally (393:1). On the bread of the second day, the commentators differ (section 2, point ב). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:1.
  • ③ At bein hashmashos, and after the addition to Shabbos: one makes eruvei chatzeiros and shitufei mevo'os at bein hashmashos, even after accepting upon oneself the addition to Shabbos: the Rav writes that the main view is according to this opinion, for the acceptance of the addition alone; the Mechaber's seif also reports the opinion that forbids, and the commentators differ on that opinion (section 2, point ג). But one who prayed the Shabbos Arvis has accepted upon himself Shabbos itself, and he is forbidden in all the shevusim as on Shabbos, except for what is done through another; and the minority who did not pray is drawn after the majority of the congregation who prayed, from when it began the prayer, that is, from the response of Barchu (393:2). What the exception of what is done through another permits for the eruv: consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:2.
  • ④ The eruv at bein hashmashos, and the bread broken on it: the eruv need exist only at bein hashmashos, and one may eat it once it is certainly dark; one should break bread on it on Shabbos morning (or at the night meal, if one eats it once it is certainly dark), for, since one mitzvah was performed with it, one should perform another mitzvah with it as well (393:3). The Rema, in Siman 394, writes at the end of his gloss what the Rav's seif 3 does not write (section 2, point ד): consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:3.
  • ⑤ The eruv eaten during bein hashmashos: even if the eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos, he has acquired the eruv (the Rav adds in parentheses: after the fact). If he made an eruv for two people — for one while it was still day, and his eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos; for the other during bein hashmashos, and his eruv remained intact until nightfall — both have acquired the eruv, although these are two leniencies that contradict each other, since they are for two people. But if he made an eruv for one during bein hashmashos and the eruv was eaten during bein hashmashos, he has not acquired the eruv (393:3). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 393:3.

⚠ This section presents the Chabad halachic conduct. For any concrete question: consult your Rav.
