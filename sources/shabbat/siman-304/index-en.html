Siman ש"ד — דין שביתת עבד

Regarding which eved a person is commanded to allow rest on Shabbos

The Alter Rebbe did not write this siman

Siman 304 traditionally deals (by the Mechaber and the Rema) with the chiyuv to give one's eved ivri rest on Shabbos — a historical topic tied to Jewish servitude as described in the Gemara.

The Alter Rebbe did not develop this siman in his Shulchan Aruch, since the subject had become inapplicable in his time (eved ivri in its halachic sense had long ceased to exist).

For academic study of this siman, refer to:

  • Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber, siman ש"ד (3 se'ifim)
  • Mishnah Berurah ad loc.

System consistency: on daattorah.com, this siman is marked "not developed by the Alter Rebbe" in order to preserve the classical numbering of the Shulchan Aruch while honoring the shitah of our Rebbe.
