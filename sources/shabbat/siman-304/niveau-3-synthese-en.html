1. The central axiom

Siman ש״ד in one sentence.
The Jewish master is responsible for the rest of his non-Jewish slave on Shabbos — but the extent of that obligation follows the degree of mitzvos the slave has accepted. Two verses are its root: « לְמַעַן יָנוּחַ עַבְדְּךָ » (the circumcised-and-immersed slave rests entirely, like his master) and « וְיִנָּפֵשׁ בֶּן אֲמָתְךָ » (one who accepted only the 7 Noahide laws does not work for the master, but may for himself). The axiom: הכל לפי דרגת קבלת המצות — everything depends on the slave's status.

3. Hierarchy of statuses

1. Slave circumcised + immersed (has accepted the mitzvos) → total rest: the master must stop him even from his own work; no Jew may order him to do a melacha; no heter outside pikuach nefesh.
↓
2. Slave of the "7 mitzvos" (ger toshav) → work for himself permitted; work for the master or a Jew forbidden (by Torah law).
↓
3. Slave (kinyan haguf) with no mitzva at all → מחלוקת: the Mechaber treats him as the preceding case (forbidden); one is machmir (Eliyahu Rabbah).
↓
4. Non-Jewish hired worker (sachir) → not concerned: the employer is not bound to his rest (seif 3).

4. Decision tree

Q1 — An owned slave (kinyan haguf) or a mere hired worker? → Hired worker → outside this siman (see the laws of amira l'akum). Slave → continue.
↓
Q2 — Was he circumcised and immersed (accepted the mitzvos)? → YES → total rest, even from his personal work. NO → continue.
↓
Q3 — Did he accept the 7 Noahide laws? → YES (ger toshav) → work for himself permitted, for the master forbidden. NO → מחלוקת, be machmir.
↓
Q4 — Illness / emergency? → Only pikuach nefesh lifts the prohibition for the eved. At the least doubt → consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic

מל — מל וטבל: total rest, even from his own work.

ז׳ — ז' מצות (גר תושב): for himself yes, for the master no.

לא — לא קיבל כלום: מחלוקת — be machmir.

שׂ — שכיר: a non-Jewish hired worker — not concerned.

Four rungs of one ladder: the more mitzvos the slave has accepted, the heavier the obligation of rest that falls upon the master.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — confusing slave and employee. Only the owned eved (kinyan haguf) is subject to שביתת עבד. A mere non-Jewish hired worker (sachir) is not concerned (seif 3) — his laws belong to amira l'akum (simanim 244, 252…).
Pitfall 2 — applying amira l'akum to one's slave. For an ordinary non-Jew, asking a melacha is a mere shevus (permitted for a sick person not in danger). For one's circumcised-and-immersed slave, it is forbidden by Torah law: no heter outside pikuach nefesh (MB ס״ק ט).
Pitfall 3 — the seal (chosam). Going out on Shabbos with a seal of servitude of metal, or fastened to the garment, or that the slave made himself — forbidden. Only the clay seal hung around the neck is permitted.
Pitfall 4 — thinking the siman "everyday". The kena'ani slave no longer exists: this siman is above all a source of principles (ger toshav, amira, hotza'ah), not a daily halacha on employing a non-Jew today.

10. The practical mitzvos of Siman ש״ד

For practice

  1. Identify the status of the non-Jew: an owned slave, or a mere hired worker?
  2. Circumcised-and-immersed slave → total rest, even from his own work; no order to do a melacha; nothing outside pikuach nefesh.
  3. Slave of the "7 mitzvos" → he may work for himself, never for the master nor for a Jew.
  4. Non-Jewish hired worker → governed by the laws of amira l'akum, not by this siman.
  5. Seal of servitude → clay hung around the neck only.
  6. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. For the pilpul, see Level 2 — Lamdan.
