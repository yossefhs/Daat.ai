Siman 336 — Walking on grass and climbing trees on Shabbos

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe across 22 se'ifim that codify actions near plants on Shabbos — no climbing trees (gezeirah of kitzur), walking on grass with nuances, smelling flowers, and modern application.
