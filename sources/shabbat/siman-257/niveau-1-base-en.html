DAAT · LEVEL 1 — INTRODUCTION

Siman רנ״ז · 8 Seifim (SA) + 13 Seifim (SHHaRav)

Hatmana (insulating/wrapping hot food) — permitted materials, forbidden ones, modern application
סימן רנ״ז
דיני הטמנת החמין
🌱 Introduction Level · Beginners
✦ ❖ ✦

The major siman of הטמנה (hatmana). How and with what may one wrap hot food on erev Shabbat so it stays hot on Shabbat? The central distinction: מוסיף הבל (a material that adds heat) versus אינו מוסיף הבל (a material that only retains heat). Direct application to modern chulent, thermal pots, and Shabbat blankets. It ends with a powerful affirmation: there is a mitzva to insulate food for Shabbat.

Topic: Wrapping hot food so it stays warm on Shabbat
Source: Shulchan Aruch Orach Chaim Siman 257 · 8 seifim · Shabbat 47b–51a.

1. The Shulchan Aruch Text

Seif 1 — General rule of הטמנה

אֵין טוֹמְנִין בְּשַׁבָּת אֲפִלּוּ בְּדָבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל. אֲבָל בִּסְפֵק חֲשֵׁכָה — טוֹמְנִין בּוֹ. וְאֵין טוֹמְנִין בְּדָבָר הַמּוֹסִיף הֶבֶל אֲפִלּוּ מִבְּעוֹד יוֹם. וְאִם הִטְמִין בְּדָבָר הַמּוֹסִיף הֶבֶל — הַתַּבְשִׁיל אָסוּר אֲפִלּוּ בְּדִיעֲבַד. וְדַוְקָא בְּצוֹנֵן שֶׁנִּתְחַמֵּם אוֹ שֶׁנִּצְטַמֵּק וְיָפֶה לוֹ. אֲבָל בְּעוֹמֵד בַּחֲמִימוּתוֹ כְּשָׁעָה רִאשׁוֹנָה — מֻתָּר.
3 distinct time regimes:
  • On Shabbat itself: forbidden to insulate even in a material that does not add heat (she-eino mosif hevel) — gezera
  • Twilight (bein hashemashot): permitted to insulate in a material that does not add heat
  • Erev Shabbat (before Shabbat): forbidden to insulate in a material that adds heat (mosif hevel); permitted in a material that does not add
Penalty if violated (with mosif hevel): the food is forbidden even bediavad. Exceptions: food that cooled and was reheated, or reduced to its benefit (mitztamek ve-yafeh lo) — forbidden. Still at its initial temperature (sha'a rishona) — permitted.

Rama gloss: (a) if forgotten beshogeg with mosif hevel — permitted (Mordechai); (b) if for the morning (not the night) — permitted lekhatchila (Shibolei Haleket) — bediavad one may rely on it; not as standard practice.
Seif 1 sets up 4 axes:
  1. When: Shabbat / twilight / erev Shabbat — 3 different regimes
  2. With what: mosif hevel / she-eino mosif hevel — 2 categories
  3. Penalty: if mosif hevel was violated — the food is forbidden
  4. Exceptions: state of the food + intention (night or morning)

Seif 2 — Distinction between insulating and protecting

אֲפִלּוּ תַבְשִׁיל שֶׁנִּתְבַּשֵּׁל כָּל צָרְכּוֹ — אָסוּר לְהַטְמִין בְּשַׁבָּת אֲפִלּוּ בְּדָבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל. וּמִכָּל מָקוֹם, לָשׂוּם כֵּלִים עַל הַתַּבְשִׁיל כְּדֵי לְשָׁמְרוֹ מִן הָעַכְבָּרִים אוֹ כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יִתְטַנֵּף בַּעֲפָרוּרִית — שָׁרֵי.
Even food cooked kol tzorko may not be insulated on Shabbat, even in a material that does not add heat. However: placing objects to protect (from mice, from dust) — permitted. That is שמירה (protection), not הטמנה (insulation). A regular pot lid — that's protection.
Conceptual distinction:
  • הטמנה = surrounding the pot with material to retain/add heat
  • שמירה = placing an object on the pot to protect (no thermal intent)
The test: what is my intent? If I want to retain heat — hatmana. If I want to protect — shemira. The standard lid of a pot is shemira (unless there's a specific intent to keep it hot).

Seif 3 — List of materials

אֵלּוּ הֵם דְּבָרִים הַמּוֹסִיפִים הֶבֶל : פְּסֹלֶת שֶׁל זֵיתִים אוֹ שֶׁל שׁוּמְשְׁמִין, וְזֶבֶל, וּמֶלַח, וְסִיד, וְחוֹל בֵּין לַחִים בֵּין יְבֵשִׁים, וְתֶבֶן, וְזַגִּים, וּמוֹכִין, וַעֲשָׂבִים בִּזְמַן שֶׁשְּׁלָשְׁתָּן לַחִין. וְאֵלּוּ דְּבָרִים שֶׁאֵינָם מוֹסִיפִים הֶבֶל : כְּסוּת, וּפֵירוֹת, וְכַנְפֵי יוֹנָה, וּנְעֹרֶת שֶׁל פִּשְׁתָּן, וּנְסֹרֶת שֶׁל חָרָשִׁין.
Materials that ADD heat (mosif hevel): olive or sesame pulp, manure, salt, lime, sand (wet or dry), straw, grape pomace, mok (fluff), wet grasses.

Materials that DO NOT add heat (she-eino mosif hevel): garments, fruits, dove feathers (and other feathers — Rama), flax tow, carpenter's sawdust.

Rama gloss: rocks — permitted even though they add heat (an exceptional case — lo shechi'a — Tosafot/Mordechai).
The defining criterion: mosif hevel materials are those that generate their own heat by fermentation, chemical reaction, or accumulation. The others only retain the heat of the food.

Seif 4 — Recovering, adding, replacing on Shabbat

אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵין טוֹמְנִין בְּשַׁבָּת אֲפִלּוּ בְּדָבָר שֶׁאֵין מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל — אִם טָמַן בּוֹ מִבְּעוֹד יוֹם וְנִתְגַּלָּה מִשֶּׁחֲשֵׁכָה — מֻתָּר לַחֲזֹר וּלְכַסּוֹתוֹ. וְכֵן אִם רָצָה לְהוֹסִיף עָלָיו בְּשַׁבָּת — מוֹסִיף. וְכֵן אִם רָצָה לִיטְּלוֹ כֻּלּוֹ וְלָתֵת אַחֵר בִּמְקוֹמוֹ — בֵּין שֶׁהָרִאשׁוֹן חַם יוֹתֵר מֵהַשֵּׁנִי בֵּין שֶׁהַשֵּׁנִי חַם יוֹתֵר מֵהָרִאשׁוֹן — אֲפִלּוּ לֹא הָיָה מְכֻסֶּה אֶלָּא בְּסָדִין — יָכוֹל לִיטְּלוֹ וּלְכַסּוֹתוֹ בִּגְלוּפְקָרִין. וְהוּא שֶׁנִּתְבַּשְּׁלָה הַקְּדֵרָה כָּל צָרְכָּה.
3 actions permitted on Shabbat on an already-existing hatmana:
  1. Re-cover what got uncovered at twilight
  2. Add to the covering (e.g. a second layer)
  3. Replace the covering entirely (sheet → thick blanket, or vice versa)
Condition: food cooked kol tzorko. If not fully cooked — forbidden even to just add to the covering (since this would promote cooking).

Seif 5 — Transferring on Shabbat

אִם פִּינָּה הַתַּבְשִׁיל בְּשַׁבָּת מִקְּדֵרָה שֶׁנִּתְבַּשֵּׁל בָּהּ לִקְדֵרָה אַחֶרֶת — מֻתָּר לְהַטְמִינוֹ בְּדָבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל.
If one transfers the food on Shabbat from the original pot it was cooked in to another pot — it is permitted to insulate it in a material that does not add heat. Why is this an exception? The transfer is a change of vessel, considered a new situation — and the prohibition of hatmana on Shabbat only applies in continuity with the original setup.

Seif 6 — Insulating a cold item

מֻתָּר לְהַטְמִין בְּשַׁבָּת דָּבָר צוֹנֵן בְּדָבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל — כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יִצְטַנֵּן בְּיוֹתֵר אוֹ כְּדֵי שֶׁתָּפֵיג צִנָּתוֹ. אֲבָל בְּדָבָר הַמּוֹסִיף הֶבֶל — וַאֲפִלּוּ לְהַטְמִין צוֹנֵן גָּמוּר וַאֲפִלּוּ מִבְּעוֹד יוֹם — נַמִּי אָסוּר.
Permitted on Shabbat: wrapping a cold item in a she-eino mosif hevel material so it doesn't get colder, or so the chill takes off. Forbidden: wrapping a fully cold item in mosif hevel — even on erev Shabbat.

Seif 7 — Food cooked kol tzorka

כָּל הֵיכָא דְאָסְרִינַן הַטְמָנָה — אֲפִלּוּ בְּקְדֵרָה מְבֻשֶּׁלֶת כָּל צָרְכָּה אָסְרִינַן ; וַאֲפִלּוּ מִצְטַמֵּק וְרַע לוֹ. הגה: וכן עיקר. ויש מקילין... ובמקום שנהגו להקל ע"פ סברא זו — אין למחות בידם, אבל אין לנהוג כן בשאר מקומות.
Whenever hatmana is forbidden — it's forbidden even for food cooked kol tzorka, even for mitztamek ve-ra lo. Rama gloss: some are lenient (treating it like the shehiya of siman 253), but this leniency should not be extended to other places.

Seif 8 — Pot + coverings + mitzva of chamin

אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁמֻּתָּר לְהַשְׁהוֹת קְדֵרָה עַל גַּבֵּי כִּירָה שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהּ גֶּחָלִים... אִם הוּא מְכֻסֶּה בִּבְגָדִים — אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁהַבְּגָדִים אֵינָם מוֹסִיפִין הֶבֶל מֵחֲמַת עַצְמָן — מִכָּל מָקוֹם מֵחֲמַת אֵשׁ שֶׁתַּחְתֵּיהֶם מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל [וְאָסוּר]. וּמִיהוּ — כָּל שֶׁאֵין הַבְּגָדִים נוֹגְעִים בַּקְּדֵרָה — אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁיֵּשׁ אֵשׁ תַּחְתֶּיהָ — שָׁרֵי. הגה: ...וְהַטְמָנָה שֶׁעוֹשִׂין בִּמְדִינוֹת אֵלּוּ שֶׁמַּטְמִינִים בְּתַנּוּר וְטָחִין פִּי הַתַּנּוּר בְּטִיט — שָׁרֵי לְכוּלֵי עָלְמָא. וּמִצְוָה לְהַטְמִין לְשַׁבָּת כְּדֵי שֶׁיֹּאכַל חַמִּין בְּשַׁבָּת ; כִּי זֶה מִכְּבוֹד וְעוֹנֶג שַׁבָּת. וְכָל מִי שֶׁאֵינוֹ מַאֲמִין בְּדִבְרֵי הַחֲכָמִים וְאוֹסֵר אֲכִילַת חַמִּין בְּשַׁבָּת — חוֹשְׁשִׁין שֶׁמָּא אֶפִּיקוֹרֵס הוּא.
Pot on a kira + coverings:
  • If the coverings touch the pot — FORBIDDEN (the air under the coverings + heat of the fire = hatmana with mosif hevel)
  • If the coverings do not touch the pot (a wide object in between) — PERMITTED (no hatmana)
Rama gloss: the practice of insulating in an oven sealed with clay (tuach be-tit) — permitted for all. It is a mitzva to insulate food for Shabbat — in order to eat chamin on Shabbat. This is part of the honor and delight of Shabbat. Anyone who doesn't believe the words of the Sages and forbids eating chamin on Shabbat — we suspect he may be an apikoros.
Cardinal affirmation of the Rama: chamin (= chulent / hamin) on Shabbat is a mitzva. It is the honor of Shabbat. The refusal is historically linked to the Karaites — it is a marker of rabbinic Orthodoxy.

1bis. Additions per Shulchan Aruch HaRav (Seifim 9–13)

Why this section? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav (Alter Rebbe) divides the siman into 13 seifim instead of the classical Shulchan Aruch's 8. The last 5 seifim add essential technical refinements. We present them here in their original formulation.

Seif 9 — Hatmana on Shabbat in a kli sheni, and for cold food

לֹא אָסְרוּ לְהַטְמִין בְּשַׁבָּת בְּדָבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל, אֶלָּא כְּשֶׁמַּטְמִין בְּאוֹתָהּ קְדֵרָה עַצְמָהּ שֶׁנִּתְבַּשֵּׁל בָּהּ הַתַּבְשִׁיל… אֲבָל אִם פִּנָּהוּ לִקְדֵרָה אַחֶרֶת — מֻתָּר לְהַטְמִינוֹ. וְכָל שֶׁכֵּן שֶׁמֻּתָּר לְהַטְמִין צוֹנֵן גָּמוּר, כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יִצְטַנֵּן בְּיוֹתֵר. וְכָל זֶה בְּדָבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל, אֲבָל בְּדָבָר הַמּוֹסִיף הֶבֶל אָסוּר לְהַטְמִין אֲפִלּוּ צוֹנֵן גָּמוּר וַאֲפִלּוּ מִבְּעוֹד יוֹם.
The Alter Rebbe's clarification regarding hatmana on Shabbat: the prohibition of insulating on Shabbat with a non-mosif-hevel material applies only to the original pot of cooking (kli rishon). If one transfers the food to another pot (kli sheni), insulating it on Shabbat is permitted. A fortiori, one may insulate completely cold food (to keep it cool or prevent over-cooling). However, with a mosif-hevel material, even cold food, even before Shabbat — it is forbidden.
Two gates for Shabbat hatmana: (1) transfer → the second pot is no longer "kli rishon" and hatmana becomes permitted; (2) cold food → even in the original pot, cold insulation is permitted because there is no risk of warming.

Seif 10 — The basket (קופה), coals beneath garments, and the covered-but-untouched pot

קֻפָּה שֶׁטָּמַן בְּתוֹכָהּ הַקְּדֵרָה בְּמוֹכִין… אָסוּר לְהַעֲמִידָהּ מִבְּעוֹד יוֹם עַל גַּבֵּי פְּסֹלֶת שֶׁל זֵיתִים, לְפִי שֶׁהֵם מַעֲלִים הֶבֶל לְמַעְלָה בְּהַמּוֹכִין… וְכֵן מֻתָּר לְהַעֲמִידָהּ בְּתוֹךְ הַכִּירָה שֶׁנִּתְבַּשְּׁלָה שָׁם לְהַטְמִינָהּ שָׁם בְּדָבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל… וּמִכָּל מָקוֹם, צָרִיךְ שֶׁתְּהֵא הַכִּירָה גְּרוּפָה מֵהַגֶּחָלִים, אֲבָל אִם יֵשׁ בָּהּ גֶּחָלִים אֲפִלּוּ הֵן קְטוּמוֹת — אָסוּר לְהַטְמִין בָּהּ הַקְּדֵרָה בִּבְגָדִים… וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁכֹּל שֶׁהַקְּדֵרָה מְגֻלָּה לְמַעְלָה — אֵין זוֹ הַטְמָנָה. וְכֵן הַמִּנְהָג.
Three refined cases:
  • Basket (kufa) with pot wrapped in mokhin: forbidden to place this basket on olive-pulp (which emits heat through the mokhin); but permitted on sesame-pulp (which does not raise the heat).
  • Pot inside the cooking kira: permitted to insulate it there if the kira is gerufa (no coals), even though the walls are hot — because that heat decays. But with coals (even ketumot), the garments become mosif hevel, and it is forbidden.
  • Pot uncovered at the top: per the "yesh omrim" view, adopted as minhag, as long as the pot is uncovered at the top — it is not hatmana — even if its walls are entirely covered.
The central principle of Seif 10: hatmana requires full enclosure. If the pot is open at the top (accepted minhag), it is not hatmana, even if its walls are fully covered. Practical application: a pot on the plata with a simple lid (not wrapped on top) is not hatmana.

Seif 11 — Prerequisites: gerufa, ketuma, or maachal ben drusai

וְכָל זֶה כְּשֶׁהַגֶּחָלִים הֵן קְטוּמוֹת, אוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבַּשֵּׁל כְּמַאֲכַל בֶּן דְּרוּסָאִי מִבְּעוֹד יוֹם, אוֹ שֶׁנָּתַן חֲתִיכַת בָּשָׂר חַיָּה בִּקְדֵרָה סָמוּךְ לַחֲשֵׁכָה מַמָּשׁ, שֶׁאִם לֹא כֵן — לְדִבְרֵי הַכֹּל אָסוּר אַף בְּלֹא שׁוּם הַטְמָנָה. בְּמִינֵי בָצֵק וּבְמִינֵי קִטְנִיּוֹת שֶׁמְּמַהֲרִים לְהִתְבַּשֵּׁל — אֵין מוֹעִיל כְּלוּם מַה שֶׁהֵן חַיִּין סָמוּךְ לַחֲשֵׁכָה.
All these permissions for hatmana on a kira presuppose one of the three Shabbat cooking conditions (siman 253):
  1. Coals ketumot (covered with ash),
  2. cooking k-maachal ben drusai (1/3 cooked) before nightfall,
  3. insertion of raw meat just before nightfall (which won't cook on Shabbat).
Restriction on legumes and doughs: they cook quickly, so even inserting them raw before nightfall does not help — they must have reached maachal ben drusai before Shabbat begins.
Connection with siman 253: this seif reminds us that hatmana does not stand alone — it fits within the general Shabbat cooking framework. Without one of the three conditions, even mere shehiya is forbidden, all the more so hatmana.

Seif 12 — Oven sealed with clay (טוח בטיט)

וְאִם הַתַּנּוּר טוּחַ בְּטִיט סְבִיב כִּסּוּיוֹ — מֻתָּר בְּכָל עִנְיָן אַף בְּהַטְמָנָה מַמָּשׁ (בְּתוֹךְ הַגֶּחָלִים), שֶׁאָז אֵין לָחוּשׁ לְחִתּוּי כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם. וְלָכֵן נוֹהֲגִים לָטוּחַ פִּי הַתַּנּוּר בְּטִיט. וּבִמְקוֹמוֹת שֶׁאֵין נוֹהֲגִים כֵּן — צָרִיךְ שֶׁלֹּא יִהְיֶה שָׁם הַטְמָנָה.
The clay-sealed oven (tuach be-tit): when one smears clay around the lid's edges, the opening becomes inaccessible. Then even actual hatmana (burying directly in the coals) is permitted — because there is no concern of stoking. That is why the custom is to seal. In places where this custom doesn't exist, all hatmana must be avoided.
The clay seal = a "behavioral barrier": the entire hatmana system rests on the fear of human intervention (chitui, stoking coals). If you physically remove this possibility (clay makes the opening inaccessible), you also remove the prohibition. Modern application: sealed ovens (modern "Shabbat mode" without access) operate on this principle.

Seif 13 — The mitzva of chamin and the marker of the apikoros

מִצְוָה לְהַטְמִין לְשַׁבָּת, כְּדֵי שֶׁיֹּאכַל חַמִּין בְּשַׁבָּת, כִּי זֶהוּ מִכְּבוֹד וְעֹנֶג שַׁבָּת. אֲבָל מִי שֶׁהַחַמִּין מַזִּיקִין לוֹ — מֻתָּר לוֹ לֶאֱכוֹל צוֹנֵן. אֲבָל מִי שֶׁאֵינוֹ מַאֲמִין בְּדִבְרֵי חֲכָמִים וְאוֹסֵר אֲכִילַת חַמִּין בְּשַׁבָּת — חוֹשְׁשִׁין שֶׁמָּא אֶפִּיקוֹרוֹס הוּא.
It is a mitzva to insulate food for Shabbat — in order to eat chamin on Shabbat, because this is part of the honor and delight of Shabbat (kavod and oneg). One to whom chamin is harmful (health) may eat cold food. But one who, out of ideological refusal, forbids chamin on Shabbat — we suspect he is an apikoros (an allusion to the Karaites who forbade all heat on Shabbat).
Three statuses of chamin: (1) a normative mitzva (honor and delight of Shabbat); (2) a tolerated health exception (not a flaw); (3) a marker of orthodoxy — principled refusal signals a theological break. This seif closes the siman by giving it its identity dimension.
Summary of the 5 additional SHHaRav seifim:
  • Seif 9: kli sheni + cold food open the door to a valid Shabbat hatmana (in non-mosif material).
  • Seif 10: "uncovered at the top" = not hatmana (accepted minhag).
  • Seif 11: the siman 253 conditions govern hatmana.
  • Seif 12: clay sealing removes the stoking concern and permits real hatmana.
  • Seif 13: chamin is a mitzva — refusal is a marker of apikoros.

What is this siman about?

3. Key Concept 1 — מוסיף הבל vs אינו מוסיף הבל

מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל (literally "adds heat/breath") = a material that generates its own heat through fermentation, chemical reaction, or accumulation. אֵינוֹ מוֹסִיף הֶבֶל = a material that only retains the heat of the food (passive insulating effect).
CategoryPhysical criterionHalachic consequence
mosif hevelActive heat: fermentation, reaction, accumulationForbidden on Shabbat AND on erev Shabbat for hatmana
she-eino mosif hevelPassive heat retention (insulator)Forbidden on Shabbat; permitted on erev Shabbat
Logic: mosif hevel = essentially quasi-cooking (heat rises, can accumulate). So even on erev Shabbat it's risky. She-eino mosif hevel = mere thermal retention. Permitted before Shabbat; forbidden on Shabbat because the act of wrapping looks like a thermal intervention.

Modern categorization (per contemporary science):
  • mosif hevel: any material that can ferment, accumulate, or generate heat (fresh straw, compost, certain wet fabrics)
  • she-eino mosif hevel: dry fabrics, passive insulators (fiberglass, polystyrene, aluminum foil)
  • Modern cases: towel, blanket, aluminum foil = she-eino mosif hevel; electric heating blanket = mosif hevel (forbidden)

5. Key Concept 3 — Pre-Shabbat Regime vs Shabbat

Bein hashemashot (twilight)PermittedForbidden
Shabbat itselfForbidden (except cases of Seif 4: re-cover, add, replace)Forbidden
Logic of the three regimes:
  1. Erev Shabbat = still weekday → permitted except for the active risk (mosif hevel)
  2. Twilight = buffer zone — ancient practice permits what doesn't add heat
  3. Shabbat = holy → hatmana is in itself forbidden, since it gives the appearance of thermal activity

6. Detailed Walk-Through of the 8 Seifim

Seif 1 — General rule + 3 time regimes

Recap: Shabbat — forbidden in all cases; twilight — permitted she-eino mosif hevel; erev Shabbat — permitted she-eino mosif hevel only. Bediavad if mosif hevel was violated: food is forbidden. Rama gloss: nuance (forgetting + morning).

Seif 2 — Distinction insulating/protecting

Standard lid = protection (shemira), not hatmana. Aluminum foil for protection against contamination = shemira. But aluminum tightly wrapped to keep the heat = hatmana. Intent matters.

Seif 3 — Catalog of materials

The big table (see Key Concept 2). In the Mechaber's time: agricultural materials. Today: adaptation to modern materials (blanket, foil, polystyrene = she-eino mosif hevel; heating blanket = mosif hevel).

Seif 4 — 3 actions permitted on Shabbat on existing hatmana

ActionCondition
Re-cover what got uncoveredFood cooked kol tzorko
Add to the coveringFood cooked kol tzorko
Replace entirelyFood cooked kol tzorko, no matter if the new one is hotter or colder

Seif 5 — Transferring on Shabbat permits hatmana

Practical case: serving the chulent in a large dish on the table. It's then permitted to wrap it (e.g. with a towel) so it doesn't cool down too much before the end of the meal. Not in a material that adds heat.

Seif 6 — Insulating a cold item on Shabbat

Permitted on Shabbat to put a bottle of cold water in a towel so it doesn't get colder. Forbidden to put it in sand (mosif hevel) — even fully cold, even on erev Shabbat.

Seif 7 — Strict even for kol tzorka

The severity of the hatmana prohibition (Shabbat) does not depend on the state of the food. Even kol tzorko, even mitztamek ve-ra lo — forbidden. Different from siman 253 (shehiya), where these states were grounds for leniency.

Seif 8 — Coverings and the mitzva

Combination kira + coverings:
  • Coverings touching the pot + fire underneath = FORBIDDEN (equivalent to hatmana with mosif hevel)
  • Coverings separated by a wide object = OK (no hatmana)
  • Oven sealed with clay (tuach be-tit) = permitted for all
+ Mitzva to insulate for Shabbat (Rama) — it is the honor of Shabbat.

8. Modern Practical Cases

Case 1 — Thermal pot (slow cooker / Crock-Pot)

Case: chulent in a Crock-Pot turned on erev Shabbat for Shabbat lunch.
Guidance:
  • The Crock-Pot itself = heat source (≈ plata) — so it falls under siman 253, not siman 257
  • No material wrapping the pot — no direct hatmana
  • But: wrapping a blanket around the Crock-Pot to keep in heat = hatmana with mosif hevel (Crock heat + insulator) → forbidden

Case 2 — Aluminum foil over the pot

Case: covering the chulent on erev Shabbat with aluminum foil, then placing it on the plata.
Guidance:
  • Aluminum foil = she-eino mosif hevel (passive material)
  • If the foil is placed as a lid = shemira / shehiya — permitted
  • If the foil is tightly wrapped around with thermal intent = hatmana she-eino mosif hevel — permitted on erev Shabbat only

Case 3 — Blankets over the pot + plata

Case: covering the chulent on the plata with a thick blanket to keep it hot until lunch.
Guidance:
  • Blanket touching the pot + plata = FORBIDDEN (Seif 8 — air under the blanket + heat of plata = hatmana with mosif hevel)
  • Solution: cover with a wide object (e.g. an upside-down dish) between pot and blanket = OK
  • Alternative: use a blech (metal plate) on the pot, blanket over the blech

Case 4 — Classic thermal pot (no electricity)

Case: putting the chulent in a double-walled thermal pot to keep it hot until lunch.
Guidance:
  • Thermal pot = passive insulation (≈ she-eino mosif hevel)
  • If closed on erev Shabbat with hot food = permitted (pre-Shabbat regime)
  • If transferred on Shabbat into the thermal pot = permitted per Seif 5

Case 5 — Heating blanket / electric heating pad

Case: placing the chulent on an electric heating pad with a blanket on top.
Guidance:
  • Heating pad = active heat source = equivalent to mosif hevel
  • With a blanket that touches = hatmana with mosif hevel = FORBIDDEN, even on erev Shabbat
  • Advice: use a regular plata (diffuse heat) without a blanket that touches

Case 6 — Serving the chulent in a large dish on the table

Case: taking the chulent off the plata, serving it in a deep dish, wrapping it in a towel so it doesn't cool down before the end of the meal.
Guidance:
  • Transferring = activates Seif 5
  • Permitted on Shabbat to insulate with a towel (she-eino mosif hevel)
  • No covering that adds heat

9. Practical Synthesis of the Siman

The 5 takeaways of Siman רנ״ז:
  1. 3 time regimes: Shabbat (totally forbidden) / Twilight (permitted she-eino mosif hevel) / Erev Shabbat (permitted she-eino mosif hevel only)
  2. 2 categories of materials: mosif hevel (forbidden even on erev Shabbat) / she-eino mosif hevel (permitted on erev Shabbat)
  3. Distinction insulating / protecting: lid = shemira (permitted); wrapping with thermal intent = hatmana
  4. Coverings + plata = trap: if they touch the pot, it becomes forbidden hatmana with mosif hevel
  5. Mitzva of chamin on Shabbat — strong theological affirmation (Rama)

The 5 practical commands of Siman רנ״ז

  1. Distinguish what adds heat (mosif hevel) from what only retains it — never mosif hevel in hatmana, even on erev Shabbat
  2. Erev Shabbat = insulating with she-eino mosif hevel permitted; Shabbat = no new hatmana (except Seifim 4-5-6)
  3. Coverings on pot + plata = trap — they must not touch the pot directly if the plata is heating
  4. Aluminum foil or lid = shemira (permitted) as long as the intent is not "to make it hotter"
  5. Mitzva of chamin on Shabbat — eating hot = honor of Shabbat. Halachic identity.
