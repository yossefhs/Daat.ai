1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שפ״ב contains 20 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo). Sefaria also prints passages in small type in it: glosses of the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles), introduced by the word הגה, in seifim א, יד, טו, יח and כ; and, without that word, in seifim ה, ו, יא and טז, passages that the text does not expressly attribute to the Rema. Topic of the siman, according to its title: whether a non-Jew's dwelling hinders the eruv. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe has 23 seifim for this siman in the text Sefaria serves: its numbering is that of Level 4, not of this page.

Seif א

אם דירת עכו״ם מעכב' בעירובו. ובו כ סעיפים:
הדר עם העכו״ם בחצר אינו אוסר עליו עד שיהיו שני ישראלים דרים בשני בתים ואוסרים זה על זה אז העכו״ם אוסר עליהם ואינו מועיל שיבטל העכו״ם רשותו אלא צריך שישכירו ממנו: הגה ישראל שהשכיר או השאיל ביתו לעכו״ם אינו אוסר עליו דלא השכיר או השאיל לו ביתו כדי שיאסור עליו (המגיד פרק ב') אבל אם הבית של עכו״ם ושכרו ישראל ממנו ועכו״ם דר בבית עמו אין שכירות הבית מועיל לענין שכירות העירוב (א״ז) ישראל שהשאיל או השכיר בית לחבירו (במקום שדרים עכו״ם) אע״פ שיש לו תפיסה בבית לא מהני וצריכים לשכור מעכו״ם (ב״י בשם מצאתי כתוב):
Translation: One who lives with a non-Jew (akum) in a courtyard: [the non-Jew] does not forbid [carrying] to him, until two Jews live in two houses and forbid [carrying] to each other; then the non-Jew forbids them, and it does not help for the non-Jew to renounce his domain: they must rent [his domain] from him. Gloss of the Rema: a Jew who rented or lent his house to a non-Jew: [the non-Jew] does not forbid him, for he did not rent or lend him his house so that he should forbid him [reference as printed: המגיד פרק ב׳]; but if the house belongs to the non-Jew, and a Jew rented it from him, and the non-Jew lives in the house with him, the rental of the house does not count as the rental [required] for the eruv [א״ז]; a Jew who lent or rented a house to his fellow (in a place where non-Jews live), even though he keeps a hold (tefisah) in the house, it does not help, and they must rent from the non-Jew [ב״י בשם מצאתי כתוב].
What this seif says:
  • The non-Jew does not forbid until two Jews live in two houses and forbid each other: הדר עם העכו״ם בחצר אינו אוסר עליו עד שיהיו שני ישראלים דרים בשני בתים ואוסרים זה על זה אז העכו״ם אוסר עליהם. The reason, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק א): by strict law the dwelling of a non-Jew is not considered a dwelling; the Sages decreed that it should forbid, so that one should not live with him and learn from his deeds, and they decreed it only in the common case, where several Jews live.
  • His renunciation does not help: his domain must be rented: ואינו מועיל שיבטל העכו״ם רשותו אלא צריך שישכירו ממנו. The Sages, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ה), wanted to distance the Jew's dwelling from the non-Jew, and were therefore strict: there is no remedy without a rental.
  • Gloss of the Rema: a Jew who rented or lent his house to a non-Jew: ישראל שהשכיר או השאיל ביתו לעכו״ם אינו אוסר עליו דלא השכיר או השאיל לו ביתו כדי שיאסור עליו.
  • Gloss of the Rema: the house belongs to the non-Jew, who lives in it with the Jew: אבל אם הבית של עכו״ם ושכרו ישראל ממנו ועכו״ם דר בבית עמו אין שכירות הבית מועיל לענין שכירות העירוב.
  • Gloss of the Rema: a hold in a house lent or rented to one's fellow does not exempt from the rental: ישראל שהשאיל או השכיר בית לחבירו (במקום שדרים עכו״ם) אע״פ שיש לו תפיסה בבית לא מהני וצריכים לשכור מעכו״ם.

Seif ב

אם בטלו הישראלים רשותם לאחד מהם כדי שיחשב כיחידי אצל העכו״ם אינו מועיל:
Translation: If the Jews renounced their domain in favor of one of them, so that he be counted as alone with the non-Jew, it does not help.
What this seif says:
  • Renouncing in favor of one of them, so that he be alone with the non-Jew, does not help: אם בטלו הישראלים רשותם לאחד מהם כדי שיחשב כיחידי אצל העכו״ם אינו מועיל. Otherwise, explains the Mishnah Berurah following the Gemara (ס״ק י״ג), one would never need to rent: one could always renounce, and the law of eruv would be forgotten.

Seif ג

אם אין לעכו״ם דריסת רגל על הישראלים אינו אוסר כגון שני חצירות הפתוחו' זו לזו ואין להם דריסת רגל זה על זה ובחצר א' דר עכ״ם ובחצר א' דרים ב' ישראלים או יותר מכניסין ומוציאין מחצר זו לחצר זו בשבת דרך חלון שביניהם ואין צריך לשכור מהעכו״ם:
Translation: If the non-Jew has no right of passage (derisas regel) over the Jews, he does not forbid; for example, two courtyards open to each other, without a right of passage of one over the other, in one of which a non-Jew lives and in the other two Jews or more: one brings in and takes out [objects] from one courtyard to the other on Shabbos, through the window between them, and there is no need to rent from the non-Jew.
What this seif says:
  • Without a right of passage over the Jews, the non-Jew does not forbid: אם אין לעכו״ם דריסת רגל על הישראלים אינו אוסר.
  • Two courtyards open to each other, the non-Jew in one, two Jews or more in the other: כגון שני חצירות הפתוחו׳ זו לזו ואין להם דריסת רגל זה על זה ובחצר א׳ דר עכ״ם ובחצר א׳ דרים ב׳ ישראלים או יותר מכניסין ומוציאין מחצר זו לחצר זו בשבת דרך חלון שביניהם ואין צריך לשכור מהעכו״ם. According to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ט״ז), this concerns objects that rested in their houses for Shabbos, and the Jews made an eruv among themselves. When both courtyards open onto one alley where the Jews forbid one another (two courtyards of Jews), the commentators diverge: see Level 2, section 1.

Seif ד

השוכר מן העכו״ם סתם מועיל וא״צ לפרש לו שהוא להתיר הטלטול וא״צ לכתוב שום כתיבה על השכירו':
Translation: One who rents from the non-Jew without specifying (stam): it works; there is no need to explain to him that it is to permit carrying, nor to write anything about the rental.
What this seif says:
  • An unspecified rental works, with no explanation and no writing: השוכר מן העכו״ם סתם מועיל וא״צ לפרש לו שהוא להתיר הטלטול וא״צ לכתוב שום כתיבה על השכירו׳. A weak rental suffices, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ); a firm rental is not required.

Seif ה

שוכרין מעכו״ם אפילו בפחו' משוה פרוטה ומותר לשכור ממנו בשבת (מרדכי ריש פרק הדר) (וע״ל סימן שפ״ג):
Translation: One rents from a non-Jew even for less than the value of a perutah. In small print, without the word הגה: and it is permitted to rent from him on Shabbos [reference as printed: מרדכי ריש פרק הדר] (and see below siman 383).
What this seif says:
  • Even for less than a perutah: שוכרין מעכו״ם אפילו בפחו׳ משוה פרוטה. For with regard to a non-Jew it counts as money, explains the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״א); it adds that from a Jew, renting his domain for less than a perutah does not help.
  • In small print: it is permitted to rent on Shabbos: ומותר לשכור ממנו בשבת. This is not, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ב), a definite rental but like a renunciation and a mere sign; one gives him food or anything permitted to be moved, saying: this is for the rent of your courtyard — but money may not be moved; and it refers to siman 383: the eruv is then void, and the residents must renounce their domain in favor of one of them.

Seif ו

כל זמן שאין העכו״ם חוזר בו מועיל השכירו' ואפילו לזמן מרובה (ואינו יכול לחזור משכירתו עד שיחזור הדמים) (ב״י בשם רש״י פרק הדר):
Translation: As long as the non-Jew does not retract, the rental works, even for a long time. Parenthesis in small print, without the word הגה: (and he cannot retract his rental until he returns the money) [reference as printed: ב״י בשם רש״י פרק הדר].
What this seif says:
  • As long as the non-Jew does not retract, the rental works: כל זמן שאין העכו״ם חוזר בו מועיל השכירו׳ ואפילו לזמן מרובה. According to the Mishnah Berurah, this concerns a rental with no stated period (ס״ק כ״ג), and the non-Jew may then retract right after the first Shabbos (ס״ק כ״ד).
  • Parenthesis: he does not retract without returning the money: ואינו יכול לחזור משכירתו עד שיחזור הדמים. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ה) explains it and records a disagreement on it: see Level 2.

Seif ז

אם שכרו מהעכו״ם לזמן ידוע לכשיכלה הזמן צריך לחזור ולשכור שנית וצריך לחזור ולערב דאין עירוב ראשון חוזר וניעור:
Translation: If they rented from the non-Jew for a fixed period, when the period ends one must rent a second time, and one must make the eruv again, for the first eruv does not revive.
What this seif says:
  • A rental for a fixed period: when it ends, one must rent again: אם שכרו מהעכו״ם לזמן ידוע לכשיכלה הזמן צריך לחזור ולשכור שנית. Even if the non-Jew does not retract, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ו): the first rental lapsed of itself.
  • And one must make the eruv again: וצריך לחזור ולערב דאין עירוב ראשון חוזר וניעור. When the rental lapses, the eruv lapses with it, for an eruv does not work where there is a non-Jew (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ז).

Seif ח

אם שכרו מהעכו״ם לזמן ידוע ובתוך הזמן השכיר העכו״ם דירתו לאחר די בשכירו הראשון:
Translation: If they rented from the non-Jew for a fixed period, and within that period the non-Jew rented his dwelling to another, the first rental suffices.
What this seif says:
  • Within the fixed period, the non-Jew rents his dwelling to another: the first rental suffices: אם שכרו מהעכו״ם לזמן ידוע ובתוך הזמן השכיר העכו״ם דירתו לאחר די בשכירו הראשון.

Seif ט

חמשה הדרים בחצר אחד שוכר מהעכו״ם בשביל כולם:
Translation: Five who live in one courtyard: [one of them] rents from the non-Jew on behalf of all of them.
What this seif says:
  • One rents on behalf of all: חמשה הדרים בחצר אחד שוכר מהעכו״ם בשביל כולם. There is no need for each one to rent: once one of them has rented, all are permitted (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ל).

Seif י

אם שכרו ממנו בעל כרחו אינו מועיל אף ע״פ שהיה רגיל להשכיר מקודם:
Translation: If they rented from him against his will, it does not help, even though he used to rent previously.
What this seif says:
  • A rental against his will does not help: אם שכרו ממנו בעל כרחו אינו מועיל אף ע״פ שהיה רגיל להשכיר מקודם. One cannot, explains the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ל״א), place the rent for his domain before him and say: here is your rent, so that we may carry in your courtyard.

Seif יא

אבל מאשתו או משכירו ולקיטו שוכרים אע״פ שהוא מוחה (ושכירו של שכירו ולקיטו של בעל הבית הוא כשכיר בעל הבית עצמו) (ריב״ש סי' תכ״ז):
Translation: But from his wife, or from his hired worker or seasonal worker (sechiro u'lekito), one rents even if he protests. Parenthesis in small print, without the word הגה: (and the hired worker of the householder's hired or seasonal worker is like the hired worker of the householder himself) [reference as printed: ריב״ש סי׳ תכ״ז].
What this seif says:
  • From his wife, his hired or seasonal worker, even if he protests: אבל מאשתו או משכירו ולקיטו שוכרים אע״פ שהוא מוחה. The same applies to one of the members of his household (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ל״ב). A hired worker is engaged for the work of the whole year, a seasonal worker for the days of the harvest (ס״ק ל״ג). And since by strict law his dwelling forbids only so that one not learn from his deeds, the Sages were lenient (ס״ק ל״ד); it also records, in the name of the Vilna Gaon, a reservation about renting despite the householder's protest, which holds even for the wife: see Level 2, section 4.
  • Parenthesis: the hired worker of the hired worker: ושכירו של שכירו ולקיטו של בעל הבית הוא כשכיר בעל הבית עצמו.

Seif יב

אם אינו רוצה להשכיר יתקרב לו א' מבני החצר עד שישאיל לו רשותו שיהא לו רשות להניח בו שום דבר דהוי ליה כשכירו ולקיטו ומשכיר שלא מדעת העכו״ם ויש אומרים שאינו צריך להשכיר אלא נותן עירובו ודיו:
Translation: If he does not want to rent, one of the residents of the courtyard should become close to him until he lends him his domain, so that he has the right to put something there; for he is then like his hired or seasonal worker, and he rents [the domain] out without the non-Jew's knowledge. And some say that he need not rent it out, but gives his share of the eruv, and that suffices.
What this seif says:
  • If he refuses: have him lend the right to put something there, and rent it out without his knowledge: אם אינו רוצה להשכיר יתקרב לו א׳ מבני החצר עד שישאיל לו רשותו שיהא לו רשות להניח בו שום דבר דהוי ליה כשכירו ולקיטו ומשכיר שלא מדעת העכו״ם. He acquires the place, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ל״ז), by putting an object there on Erev Shabbos.
  • And some say he need not rent it out: he gives his share of the eruv: ויש אומרים שאינו צריך להשכיר אלא נותן עירובו ודיו. For the practice between these two views, see Level 2; consult your Rav.

Seif יג

אם ייחד לו מקום בבית שמשאיל לו כיון שאינו כשלוחו בכל הבית לא מהני:
Translation: If he assigned him a [particular] place in the house he lends him: since he is not like his agent in the whole house, it does not help.
What this seif says:
  • An assigned place does not suffice: אם ייחד לו מקום בבית שמשאיל לו כיון שאינו כשלוחו בכל הבית לא מהני. According to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק מ״ג), the Acharonim limited this to where he has no right at all to use the assigned place and cannot remove the Jew; if he has that right, or can remove him, he is still like his agent.

Seif יד

שכרו משכירו ולקיטו לזמן אע״פ שסילקו תוך הזמן מהיות שכירו ולקיטו מותרים עד תום הזמן אבל אם שכרו ממנו סתם כיון שסילקו נתבטל השכירו': הגה וה״ה אם שכרו מגזבר המלך ונסתלק לגמרי מן המלך אבל אם לא סלקו רק מן הגזברות ועדיין אוכל פרס המלך עדיין מיקרי שכירו ולקיטו ויוכל לשכור ממנו ולכן גם שכירות הראשון עדיין קיים (ב״י בשם תשובת רשב״א):
Translation: If they rented from his hired or seasonal worker for a [fixed] period, even if he dismissed him within the period from being his hired or seasonal worker, they are permitted until the end of the period; but if they rented from him without specifying, once he dismissed him, the rental is void. Gloss of the Rema: and the same applies if they rented from the king's treasurer and he was entirely removed from the king; but if he was removed only from the treasury and still eats the king's allowance (peras), he is still called his hired or seasonal worker, and one may rent from him; and therefore the first rental also still stands [reference as printed: ב״י בשם תשובת רשב״א].
What this seif says:
  • Rented from the hired worker for a period: it holds until the end, even if he is dismissed: שכרו משכירו ולקיטו לזמן אע״פ שסילקו תוך הזמן מהיות שכירו ולקיטו מותרים עד תום הזמן.
  • An unspecified rental: void once he is dismissed: אבל אם שכרו ממנו סתם כיון שסילקו נתבטל השכירו׳.
  • Gloss of the Rema: the king's treasurer entirely removed: וה״ה אם שכרו מגזבר המלך ונסתלק לגמרי מן המלך.
  • Gloss of the Rema: removed only from the treasury, he is still his hired worker: אבל אם לא סלקו רק מן הגזברות ועדיין אוכל פרס המלך עדיין מיקרי שכירו ולקיטו ויוכל לשכור ממנו ולכן גם שכירות הראשון עדיין קיים.

Seif טו

אם ישראל ועכו״ם דרים בבית א' צריך לשכור מהעכו״ם ולערב עם הישראל: הגה אם יש לכל א' דירה בפני עצמו וה״ה שני (עכו״ם) הדרים בכה״ג בבית אחד צריך לשכור (משניהם) (ב״י בשם הג״א):
Translation: If a Jew and a non-Jew live in one house, one must rent from the non-Jew and make the eruv with the Jew. Gloss of the Rema: if each has a dwelling of his own; and the same applies to two (non-Jews) who live thus in one house: one must rent (from both) [reference as printed: ב״י בשם הג״א].
What this seif says:
  • A Jew and a non-Jew in one house: rent from the non-Jew and make the eruv with the Jew: אם ישראל ועכו״ם דרים בבית א׳ צריך לשכור מהעכו״ם ולערב עם הישראל.
  • Gloss of the Rema: if each has a dwelling of his own; and two (non-Jews) thus: אם יש לכל א׳ דירה בפני עצמו וה״ה שני (עכו״ם) הדרים בכה״ג בבית אחד צריך לשכור (משניהם). On this gloss the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק נ״א) records a disagreement: see Level 2.

Seif טז

אם יש לעכו״ם ה' שכירים או לקיטים ישראלים דרים בביתו אין דירתם חשובה דירה שיאסרו זה על זה (דשכירות של עכו״ם אין אוסר אם שכרו מבעל הבית) (ב״י):
Translation: If the non-Jew has five Jewish hired or seasonal workers living in his house, their dwelling is not counted as a dwelling such that they forbid one another. Parenthesis in small print, without the word הגה: (for the rental [printed word: « שכירות »] of a non-Jew does not forbid, if one rented from the householder) [reference as printed: ב״י]. [The Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״ה, reads « ושכירו »: and his hired worker does not forbid.]
What this seif says:
  • The non-Jew's Jewish hired workers do not forbid one another: אם יש לעכו״ם ה׳ שכירים או לקיטים ישראלים דרים בביתו אין דירתם חשובה דירה שיאסרו זה על זה. For their dwelling is not truly theirs: the non-Jew has a right in it and can remove them (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״ג).
  • Parenthesis: the rental from the householder suffices: דשכירות של עכו״ם אין אוסר אם שכרו מבעל הבית. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק נ״ה) writes that the word דשכירות is a scribal error for ושכירו: if one rented the domain from the non-Jew, there is no need to rent it also from his hired or seasonal worker, whether Jewish or not.

Seif יז

שתי חצירו' זו לפנים מזו ישראל ועכו״ם בפנימי' וישראל בחיצונה או שישראל ועכו״ם בחיצונה וישראל בפנימי' ואפילו עכו״ם בפנימי' ושני ישראלים בחיצונה אוסר ויש מי שאומר שאפי' עכו״ם בפנימי' וישראל א' בחיצונה אוסר:
Translation: Two courtyards, one inside the other: a Jew and a non-Jew in the inner one and a Jew in the outer one; or a Jew and a non-Jew in the outer one and a Jew in the inner one; and even a non-Jew in the inner one and two Jews in the outer one: he forbids. And there is one who says that even a non-Jew in the inner one and a single Jew in the outer one, he forbids.
What this seif says:
  • Two courtyards, one inside the other: the cases where he forbids: שתי חצירו׳ זו לפנים מזו ישראל ועכו״ם בפנימי׳ וישראל בחיצונה או שישראל ועכו״ם בחיצונה וישראל בפנימי׳ ואפילו עכו״ם בפנימי׳ ושני ישראלים בחיצונה אוסר.
  • And according to one view, even with a single Jew in the outer one: ויש מי שאומר שאפי׳ עכו״ם בפנימי׳ וישראל א׳ בחיצונה אוסר. For the practice between these two views, see Level 2; consult your Rav.

Seif יח

עכו״ם שהשכיר ביתו לחבירו עכו״ם אם נשאר לו שום תפיסה בבי' שיש לו רשות להניח שם שום כלים יכולים לשכור ממנו ואם לא נשאר לו שום תפיס' אם יכול המשכיר לסלק השוכר תוך זמנו יכולים לשכור ממנו ואם לאו אינו מועיל אלא אם כן ישכרו מהשוכר: הגה וה״ה איש א' שיש לו תפיסה בבית יכולין לשכור ממנו דלא גרע משכירו ולקיטו (ב״י בשם ריב״ש סי' תנ״ז):
Translation: A non-Jew who rented his house to a fellow non-Jew: if he keeps some hold (tefisah) in the house — that he has the right to put utensils there —, one may rent from him; and if he keeps no hold at all: if the lessor can remove the lessee within his term, one may rent from him; and if not, it does not help, unless one rents from the lessee. Gloss of the Rema: and the same applies to a person (ish a׳) who has a hold in the house: one may rent from him, for he is no less than a hired or seasonal worker [reference as printed: ב״י בשם ריב״ש סי׳ תנ״ז].
What this seif says:
  • If he keeps a hold in the house, one rents from him: עכו״ם שהשכיר ביתו לחבירו עכו״ם אם נשאר לו שום תפיסה בבי׳ שיש לו רשות להניח שם שום כלים יכולים לשכור ממנו.
  • With no hold: depending on whether he can remove the lessee: ואם לא נשאר לו שום תפיס׳ אם יכול המשכיר לסלק השוכר תוך זמנו יכולים לשכור ממנו ואם לאו אינו מועיל אלא אם כן ישכרו מהשוכר. For example, explains the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ס״א), if he stipulated with him that he may remove him whenever he wishes.
  • Gloss of the Rema: a person who has a hold in the house: וה״ה איש א׳ שיש לו תפיסה בבית יכולין לשכור ממנו דלא גרע משכירו ולקיטו. The Mishnah Berurah reads here איש אחר, another person (ס״ק ס״ג).

Seif יט

חצר שישראל ועכו״ם דרים בה ובית א' של ישראל אצל ביתו של זה ואינו פתוח לחצר וחלונו' ביניהם אינו יכול לערב עמו דרך החלון שביניהם כדי להוציא כליו דרך בית שכנו הפתוח לחצר אבל אם פתח פתוח ביניהם מותר ויש מי שאומר דאף אם פתח פתוח ביניהם אסור:
Translation: A courtyard in which a Jew and a non-Jew live; and the house of [another] Jew is next to this one's house, it does not open onto the courtyard, and there are windows between them: he cannot make an eruv with him through the window between them in order to take his utensils out through his neighbor's house, which opens onto the courtyard; but if there is an open doorway (pesach) between them, it is permitted. And there is one who says that even if there is an open doorway between them, it is forbidden.
What this seif says:
  • Through the window, he cannot make an eruv to take his utensils out through the neighbor's house: חצר שישראל ועכו״ם דרים בה ובית א׳ של ישראל אצל ביתו של זה ואינו פתוח לחצר וחלונו׳ ביניהם אינו יכול לערב עמו דרך החלון שביניהם כדי להוציא כליו דרך בית שכנו הפתוח לחצר. According to the Mishnah Berurah, this concerns a single Jew with the non-Jew in the courtyard (ס״ק ס״ה), and a second Jew in the adjoining house (ס״ק ס״ו), whose house opens onto another courtyard or an alley (ס״ק ס״ז).
  • But with an open doorway between them, it is permitted: אבל אם פתח פתוח ביניהם מותר.
  • And according to one view, even with an open doorway it is forbidden: ויש מי שאומר דאף אם פתח פתוח ביניהם אסור. For the practice between these two views, see Level 2; consult your Rav.

Seif כ

חצר שישראל ועכו״ם שרוים בה והיו חלונות פתוחות מבית ישראל זה לבית ישראל זה ועשו עירוב דרך חלונות אף ע״פ שהם מותרים להוציא מבית לבית דרך חלונות הרי הם אסורים להוציא מבית לבית דרך פתחים מפני העכו״ם עד שישכיר: הגה ספינה שיש בה ישראלים רבים ויש להם בתים מיוחדים צריכים לערב ולשכור רשות מן עכו״ם בעל הספינה או להבליעו בשכר הספינה (ב״י בשם שבולי לקט) וע״ל סי' שס״ו ס״ב:
Translation: A courtyard in which Jews and a non-Jew dwell, with windows open from the house of one Jew to the house of the other, and they made the eruv through the windows: although they are permitted to carry from house to house through the windows, they are forbidden to carry from house to house through the doorways (pesachim), because of the non-Jew, until there is a rental [of his domain]. Gloss of the Rema: a ship with many Jews on it, who have separate quarters there: they must make an eruv and rent the domain from the non-Jew who owns the ship, or include it in the fare [reference as printed: ב״י בשם שבולי לקט]; and see above siman 366, seif ב.
What this seif says:
  • An eruv through the windows: permitted through the windows, forbidden through the doorways because of the non-Jew: חצר שישראל ועכו״ם שרוים בה והיו חלונות פתוחות מבית ישראל זה לבית ישראל זה ועשו עירוב דרך חלונות אף ע״פ שהם מותרים להוציא מבית לבית דרך חלונות הרי הם אסורים להוציא מבית לבית דרך פתחים מפני העכו״ם עד שישכיר. According to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ע״ד), « through the doorways » means through the air of the courtyard; through an opening between one house and the other it is permitted thanks to the eruv they made, according to all views.
  • Gloss of the Rema: the ship: ספינה שיש בה ישראלים רבים ויש להם בתים מיוחדים צריכים לערב ולשכור רשות מן עכו״ם בעל הספינה או להבליעו בשכר הספינה. Separate quarters, explains the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ע״ה): two Jews or more, each with a room of his own for eating; if they ate in one place, even many, they would count as one.

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Siman 381 dealt with the renunciation of domain (bitul reshus) between Jews. Siman שפ״ב deals with the non-Jew who lives in the courtyard: when he forbids the Jews to carry (seifim א to ג); renting his domain, its form, its price, its period and from whom one rents (seifim ד to יד); a Jew and a non-Jew in one house (seif טו); the non-Jew's Jewish hired workers (seif טז); two courtyards, one inside the other (seif יז); the non-Jew who rented his house to another (seif יח); houses connected by a window or a doorway (seifim יט and כ).

Why does the non-Jew forbid? The Mishnah Berurah explains on seif א: « דמעיקר הדין דירת עכו״ם אינה חשובה דירה אלא דחכמים גזרו דליאסר עליו כדי שלא ידור עמו וילמוד ממעשיו ולא גזרו אלא בדבר דשכיח דהיינו במקום שדרים כמה ישראלים אבל לא בחד בית דלא שכיח שידור בין העכו״ם דהיו חשודין אש״ד » (משנה ברורה שפ״ב ס״ק א).

The fundamental question: when does a non-Jew's dwelling in the courtyard hinder the Jews' eruv, and what is the remedy? The Mechaber answers that he forbids only if two Jews live in two houses and forbid each other, that his renunciation does not help and that his domain must be rented (seif א); the following seifim detail this rental.

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. Siman שפ״ב follows siman שפ״א · 381, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דיני המבטל רשותו ועבר והוציא » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״א:א), and precedes siman שפ״ג · 383, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « כשאין העכו״ם בבית אינו מעכב » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ג:א). The small-print passage of seif ה itself refers to siman 383, and the Rema's gloss on seif כ to siman 366, seif ב.

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman:

The essential notions of siman שפ״ב:
  • דירת עכו״ם — the non-Jew's dwelling: according to the Mishnah Berurah, by strict law it is not a dwelling, but the Sages decreed that it should forbid (ס״ק א; section 2).
  • שכירות רשות — renting the domain: the non-Jew's renunciation does not help, his domain must be rented (seif א). The reason: « דרצו חכמים לארחוקי דירת ישראל מן העכו״ם ולהכי החמירו דאין לו תקנה עד שישכור ממנו » (משנה ברורה שפ״ב ס״ק ה).
  • שכירות חלושה — a weak rental: on seif ד, « דדי בשכירות חלושה ולא בעינן שכירות בריאה » (משנה ברורה שפ״ב ס״ק כ).
  • דריסת רגל — the right of passage: « אם אין לעכו״ם דריסת רגל על הישראלים אינו אוסר » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ב:ג); see also the two courtyards, one inside the other (seif יז).
  • שכירו ולקיטו — his hired worker and seasonal worker, from whom one may rent (among others in seifim יא, יב and יד): « שכיר לעבודת כל השנה לקיטו לימות הקציר » (משנה ברורה שפ״ב ס״ק ל״ג).
  • תפיסה — a hold in the house: in seif יח, « שיש לו רשות להניח שם שום כלים » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ב:יח).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אThe non-Jew living in the courtyard does not forbid until two Jews live in two houses and forbid each other; then he forbids, his renunciation does not help, and his domain must be rented. Rema: a Jew who rented or lent his house to a non-Jew; a house belonging to the non-Jew; a hold in a house lent or rented to one's fellow does not exempt from the rental.
בIf the Jews renounce their domain in favor of one of them, so that he be alone with the non-Jew, it does not help.
גWithout a right of passage over the Jews, the non-Jew does not forbid: two courtyards open to each other, without a right of passage, the non-Jew in one, two Jews or more in the other — one carries from courtyard to courtyard through the window, without renting.
דAn unspecified rental works, without saying it is for carrying and without writing.
הOne rents even for less than a perutah; in small print: it is permitted to rent on Shabbos (reference to siman 383).
וAs long as the non-Jew does not retract, the rental works, even for a long time; parenthesis: he does not retract the rental without returning the money.
זA rental for a fixed period: when it ends, one must rent again and make the eruv again, for the first eruv does not revive.
חA rental for a fixed period: if, within the period, the non-Jew rents his dwelling to another, the first rental suffices.
טFive residents of one courtyard: one rents on behalf of all.
יA rental against his will does not help, even if he used to rent.
יאFrom his wife, his hired or seasonal worker, one rents even if he protests; parenthesis: the hired worker's hired worker is like the householder's hired worker.
יבIf he refuses to rent, a resident of the courtyard has him lend the right to put something there, becomes like his hired worker and rents it out without his knowledge; some say he need not rent it out and gives his share of the eruv.
יגIf the non-Jew assigns him a place in the house he lends him, it does not help: he is not like his agent in the whole house.
ידA rental from the hired or seasonal worker: for a period, it holds until the end even if he is dismissed; unspecified, it is void once he is dismissed. Rema: the same for the king's treasurer entirely removed; removed only from the treasury, if he still eats the king's allowance, he is still his hired worker.
טוA Jew and a non-Jew in one house: rent from the non-Jew and make the eruv with the Jew. Rema: if each has a dwelling of his own; and two (non-Jews) thus: rent (from both).
טזFive Jewish hired or seasonal workers living in the non-Jew's house do not forbid one another; parenthesis: the rental from the householder suffices.
יזTwo courtyards, one inside the other: a Jew and a non-Jew in the inner one and a Jew in the outer one, or the reverse, and even a non-Jew in the inner one and two Jews in the outer one — he forbids; according to one view, even with a single Jew in the outer one.
יחA non-Jew who rented his house to a fellow non-Jew: if he keeps a hold, one rents from him; if not, if he can remove the lessee within his term, one rents from him; otherwise, from the lessee. Rema: the same for any person who has a hold in the house.
יטThe house of another Jew, adjoining, not opening onto the courtyard where a Jew and a non-Jew live, connected by windows: no eruv through the window to take his utensils out through the neighbor's house; with an open doorway between them, it is permitted; according to one view, even then it is forbidden.
כJews living in a courtyard with a non-Jew, who made the eruv through the windows between their houses: they carry through the windows, but not through the doorways, because of the non-Jew, until there is a rental. Rema: the ship on which Jews have separate quarters.
The heart of the siman: the non-Jew living in the courtyard forbids the Jews to carry only if two Jews live in two houses and forbid each other; his renunciation does not help, his domain must be rented (seif א). The rest of the siman tells how this rental is made, how long it holds, and from whom one may rent when the non-Jew refuses (seifim ד to יד, יח), and in which arrangements of houses and courtyards he forbids (seifim ג, טו to יז, יט and כ).

6. Position of the Rema

In this siman, the glosses of the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles, הגה) are in seifim א, יד, טו, יח and כ; in seifim ה, ו, יא and טז, Sefaria prints passages in small type without the word הגה. The Mechaber himself records a second view in seifim יב, יז and יט. Here they are, in the order of the seifim.

The glosses of the Rema, and the views the Mechaber records:
  • Seif א, gloss of the Rema — a Jew who rented or lent his house to a non-Jew: the non-Jew does not forbid him; a house belonging to the non-Jew which a Jew rented from him, the non-Jew living in it with him: the rental of the house does not count as the rental for the eruv; a hold in a house lent or rented to one's fellow does not exempt from renting from the non-Jew.
  • Seif ה, in small print — it is permitted to rent on Shabbos; the Mishnah Berurah specifies what one then gives him, and that money may not be moved; it refers to siman 383: the eruv is then void, and the residents must renounce their domain in favor of one of them (ס״ק כ״ב; section 1).
  • Seif ו, parenthesis — the non-Jew does not retract the rental without returning the money; the Mishnah Berurah records a disagreement on this (ס״ק כ״ה): see Level 2.
  • Seif יא, parenthesis — the hired worker of the householder's hired or seasonal worker is like the householder's own hired worker.
  • Seif יב, second view in the Mechaber — some say that the one to whom the domain was lent need not rent it out: he gives his share of the eruv. For the practice, see Level 2.
  • Seif יד, gloss of the Rema — the king's treasurer: entirely removed, the unspecified rental is void; removed only from the treasury, if he still eats the king's allowance, he is still his hired worker.
  • Seif טו, gloss of the Rema — if each has a dwelling of his own; and two (non-Jews) thus: rent (from both). The Mishnah Berurah records a disagreement on this gloss (ס״ק נ״א): see Level 2.
  • Seif טז, parenthesis — the rental from the householder suffices; the Mishnah Berurah reads « ושכירו » instead of « דשכירות » (ס״ק נ״ה).
  • Seif יז, second view in the Mechaber — according to one view, he forbids even with a non-Jew in the inner courtyard and a single Jew in the outer one. For the practice, see Level 2.
  • Seif יח, gloss of the Rema — any person who has a hold in the house: one may rent from him; the Mishnah Berurah reads « איש אחר » (ס״ק ס״ג).
  • Seif יט, second view in the Mechaber — according to one view, it is forbidden even with an open doorway between the houses. For the practice, see Level 2.
  • Seif כ, gloss of the Rema — the ship on which Jews have separate quarters: make an eruv and rent from the non-Jew who owns the ship, or include it in the fare; reference to siman 366, seif ב.
  • Chabad → Level 4 — Daat HaRav studies this siman in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שפ״ב, under its own numbering, in 23 seifim in the text Sefaria serves.

Seif יא: from his wife, his hired or seasonal worker, one rents even if he protests (on renting despite the householder's protest, the commentators diverge: see Level 2, section 4). Seif יב: a resident of the courtyard may have him lend the right to put something there, become like his hired worker and rent it out without his knowledge; some say it then suffices that he gives his share of the eruv — for the practice, see Level 2. Consult your Rav.
The rental was for a fixed period, and the period endsSeif ז: one must rent again and make the eruv again, for the first eruv does not revive. Consult your Rav.
The rental was not made before ShabbosSeif ה, the small-print passage: it is permitted to rent on Shabbos; the Mishnah Berurah writes that one gives him food or an object permitted to be moved, and that money may not be moved (ס״ק כ״ב), and it refers to siman 383: the eruv is then void, and the residents must renounce their domain in favor of one of them. Consult your Rav.
Jews each have their own cabin on a ship whose owner is not JewishThe site's application, the Rema's gloss on seif כ: if one sees them as separate quarters in the sense of the gloss, one must make an eruv and rent from the ship's owner, or include it in the fare; the Mishnah Berurah speaks of two Jews or more, each with a room of his own for eating (ס״ק ע״ה). Whether a present-day cabin is such a quarter is for your Rav to say.
For precise practical cases: the seifim make the rule depend, among other things, on the number of Jews and houses (seif א), on the right of passage (seif ג), on the form and period of the rental (seifim ד to ח, יד) and on the person from whom one rents (seifim יא to יד, יח). For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שפ״ב:
  1. The non-Jew forbids only if two Jews live in two houses and forbid each other; his renunciation does not help: his domain must be rented (seif א). Renouncing in favor of one of the Jews, so that he be alone with him, does not help (seif ב).
  2. Without a right of passage over the Jews, he does not forbid (seif ג).
  3. The rental works unspecified and without writing (seif ד), even for less than a perutah (seif ה); it works as long as the non-Jew does not retract (seif ו); for a fixed period, when it ends one must rent again and make the eruv again (seif ז), and if he rents his dwelling to another within that period, the first rental suffices (seif ח); one rents on behalf of all the residents (seif ט).
  4. From whom: not against his will (seif י); from his wife, his hired or seasonal worker, even if he protests (seif יא; on renting despite the householder's protest, the commentators diverge: Level 2, section 4); if he refuses, a resident of the courtyard has him lend the right to put something there and rents it out without his knowledge, and some say he then gives his share of the eruv (seif יב); an assigned place does not suffice (seif יג); from the hired worker, a rental for a period holds until its end even if he is dismissed, and an unspecified rental is void once he is (seif יד).
  5. A Jew and a non-Jew in one house: rent from the non-Jew and make the eruv with the Jew (seif טו); the non-Jew's Jewish hired or seasonal workers living in his house do not forbid one another (seif טז).
  6. Two courtyards, one inside the other: he forbids in the cases of the seif, and according to one view even with a single Jew in the outer one (seif יז).
  7. The non-Jew who rented his house to a fellow non-Jew: one rents from him if he keeps a hold, or if he can remove the lessee within his term; otherwise, from the lessee (seif יח).
  8. Houses connected by windows: seifim יט and כ, with the second view of seif יט.
  9. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Why does the non-Jew not forbid a single Jew in the courtyard (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א)?
  2. Why does the non-Jew's renunciation not help, and why is a rental needed (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה)?
  3. Why can the Jews not renounce in favor of one of them (seif ב; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ג)?
  4. Why does a rental for less than a perutah suffice with a non-Jew (seif ה; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״א)?
  5. Why must the eruv be made again when a rental for a fixed period ends (seif ז; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ז)?
  6. Why may one rent from his hired worker even if he protests (seif יא; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ל״ד)?
  7. In seif יד, what difference does it make whether the rental from the hired worker is for a period or unspecified?
