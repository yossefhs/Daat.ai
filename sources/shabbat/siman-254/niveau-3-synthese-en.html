1. The Central Axiom

שמא יחתה declined according to the user's intention.
Siman רנ״ד particularizes siman 253 over 3 modes of cooking. The common thread: look for where the human would have no interest in stoking. Three different mechanisms:
  1. Intervention would be counter-productive (gedi/chicken near the fire — would burn; tannur טוח בטיט — would cool down)
  2. Minimum cooking threshold reached (קרימת פנים for bread, maachal ben Drosai for the pâté)
  3. Intention contradicts stoking (but also: sometimes intention reinstates the risk — legumes + לאכלם לאלתר)
The distinction is more subtle than siman 253: here we read the user's intention, not only the objective characteristics of the device.

2. The 3 Cases of the Siman

Case A — Roast (Seifim 1-3)

  • Near the fire (אצל האש): gedi/עוף permitted; beef/שור forbidden
  • Tannur טוח בטיט: all permitted
  • On the coals: maachal ben Drosai on both sides required for all types

Case B — Bread and Pâtés (Seifim 5-7)

  • Bread: קרימת פנים on the oven side before Shabbat
  • Cake on coals: crust on the fire side
  • Pâté in crust: top + bottom crust + inside at maachal ben Drosai
  • Removing from oven on Shabbat: with knife/pointed object (not with a paddle)

Case C — Legumes and Water (Seifim 8-9)

  • דעתו לאכלם לאלתר = "fully raw" exemption falls
  • Lentils, lupines, jar water — forbidden without prior cooking
  • Application: modern slow cooker with fast-cooking foods

4. Mnemonic — G-T-K

G — Gedi (גדי): tender meat — exemption near the fire (Seif Alef). Beef = no exemption.

T — Tuach (טוח בטיט): oven sealed with clay = universal exemption. Modern application: oven Shabbat mode.

K — Kerima (קרימת פנים): minimum cooking threshold for bread. Before Shabbat = permitted.

To memorize: "G-T-K for siman 254: Gedi (tender meat permitted), Tuach (sealed oven permitted), Kerima (crust = bread permitted)."
+ Bonus L: Lealtar (לאלתר) — the intention to eat immediately cancels the "fully raw" exemption (Seif Het).

5. Decision Tree

Question 1: Is it roasted meat, bread, or legumes/water?
↓
If roasted meat: tannur טוח בטיט (Shabbat mode)? → permitted. Otherwise: gedi/chicken → permitted; beef → forbidden (unless cooked maachal ben Drosai)
↓
If bread: crust (קרימת פנים) formed before Shabbat? → permitted. Pâté = top+bottom crust + interior maachal ben Drosai
↓
If legumes/water: cooked at least to maachal ben Drosai before Shabbat? → permitted. Otherwise → forbidden (Seif Het — "fully raw" exemption does not apply)
↓
Removing from oven on Shabbat: with knife/fork; never with a paddle (Seif Zayin)
↓
✓ PERMITTED if all conditions answered correctly

7. 5 Pitfalls to Avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that "chicken or beef, it's the same". False — the distinction gedi/עוף vs בשר שור is structural in Seif Alef. For a beef roast in an open oven, you need a sealed tannur or prior maachal ben Drosai cooking.
Pitfall 2: Putting bread in the oven 5 minutes before the entry of Shabbat. Probably insufficient — קרימת פנים requires visible cooking (formed crust, firm crumb). Put in early and check.
Pitfall 3: Putting lentils in the slow cooker just before Shabbat with the thought "they will cook for the night". Forbidden (Seif Het) — the intention of immediate cooking cancels the exemption.
Pitfall 4: Turning on the electric kettle late Friday to have hot water for the night. Forbidden (Seif Tet) — water not boiled before the entry + need for the same night.
Pitfall 5: Removing a dish from the oven with a wooden paddle (because it's more convenient). Forbidden (Seif Zayin) — the paddle = עובדא דחול. Use a knife or fork.

10. The 5 Practical Commandments of Siman רנ״ד

For daily conduct

  1. Identify the type of meat — gedi/chicken (tender, exemption near the fire) vs beef/adult goat (tough, maachal ben Drosai required or sealed tannur).
  2. Oven Shabbat mode = טוח בטיט — exemption for all types of meat and pastry.
  3. Bread: crust (קרימת פנים) visibly formed before Shabbat. Pâté in crust = top+bottom crust + inside at maachal ben Drosai.
  4. Legumes + fast cooking + immediate need = the "fully raw" exemption of siman 253 does not apply. Cooked to maachal ben Drosai before the entry.
  5. Removing from oven on Shabbat: knife/fork/pointed object — never with a paddle (עובדא דחול).
