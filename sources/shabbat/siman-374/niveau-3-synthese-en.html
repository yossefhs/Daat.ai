The seif numbers on this page are those of the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber (4 seifim). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav, studied in Level 4, also has 4 seifim, and each of its seifim 1 to 4 deals with the case of the Mechaber's seif of the same rank: when it is cited, its name is given next to the number.

1. The central axiom

Siman שע״ד in one sentence.
When the opening through which two courtyards made an eruv is closed up, or a breach opens between two domains, does the permission to carry remain? For the opening closed up on Shabbos, the Mishnah Berurah gives the reason of seif א: « דכיון שהותר בתחלת השבת הותר לכל השבת » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ג); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes it in its seif 1: « שֶׁשַּׁבָּת כֵּיוָן שֶׁהֻתְּרָה — הֻתְּרָה » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:א). The siman sets out the cases, each in its seif: the eruv opening closed up on Shabbos, the eruv made for the year and the view of the Rambam (seif א); the wall between two courtyards breached on Shabbos, and the courtyard breached onto the public domain or onto a karmelis (seif ב); the small courtyard breached before Shabbos onto a large one (seif ג); the small roof breached before Shabbos onto a large one (seif ד).

3. Structure of the siman — from the closed-up opening to the roof

Seif א — the eruv opening closed up on Shabbos: two courtyards that made an eruv through a window or a doorway between them may make use [from one to the other, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב] by way of the top of the wall and its holes. The eruv made for the year returns to its permission when the doorway, closed up on a weekday, opens on Shabbos; the note in parentheses adds: even if he closed it deliberately (Agur). According to the Rambam, each one by itself only; for the halachah, the main view is the first one, according to the Acharonim (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו).
↓
Seif ב — the wall between two courtyards breached on Shabbos: if each had made an eruv by itself, each one may carry in his courtyard, even the utensils of the house; the courtyard breached on Shabbos onto the public domain or onto a karmelis is forbidden.
↓
Seif ג — the small courtyard breached before Shabbos onto a large one: along its full width, by a breach of at most ten amos. The large one, if it made an eruv by itself, may take out into its courtyard utensils that were in the house at the start of Shabbos; the small one may do so only with a joint eruv — when its walls enter the large one and are three tefachim away from the walls of the large one; otherwise, the small one is permitted by the principle of seen from outside and even from inside.
↓
Seif ד — the small roof breached before Shabbos onto a large one: along its full width (without a joint eruv, the small roof not being more than ten amos; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ג). One does not bring up onto the small one utensils that were in the house at the start of Shabbos; one may bring them up onto the large one — provided that the walls of the house are recognizable to one who stands on the roof; otherwise, for example if the roof juts out, it is a karmelis, unless a window of four by four opens to it from the house; even then, bringing up onto that roof utensils that were in the house at the start of Shabbos remains forbidden without a joint eruv (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ט).

4. Decision tree — does the permission to carry remain?

Q1: Was the opening through which two courtyards made an eruv closed up? (seif א)
↓ On Shabbos → one may make use from one to the other by way of the top of the wall and its holes (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב and ג). While it was still day → forbidden, even if it reopened on Shabbos, write the Biur Halachah and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (seif 1) — except for the eruv made for several Shabbosos or for the year, already acquired on an earlier Shabbos while the doorway was open: the doorway closed up on a weekday and opened on Shabbos, the eruv returns to its permission (seif א). Closed up deliberately: see section 5.
Q2: Was a wall breached on Shabbos? (seif ב)
↓ Between two courtyards that each made an eruv by itself → each one may carry in his courtyard, even the utensils of the house. Onto the public domain or onto a karmelis → forbidden (seif ב); for the four amos, see pitfall 4.
Q3: Had a small courtyard been breached before Shabbos onto a large one? (seif ג)
↓ Along its full width, by a breach of at most ten amos → the large one, which made an eruv by itself, is permitted; the small one is forbidden without a joint eruv, if its walls enter the large one and are three tefachim away from its walls; otherwise, it too is permitted (seif ג). Beyond ten amos, the Mishnah Berurah writes, one cannot say that the small one is like the doorway of the large one (ס״ק י״ב).
Q4: Had a small roof been breached before Shabbos, along its full width, onto a large one? (seif ד)
↓ Without a joint eruv, the small roof not being more than ten amos (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ג), the walls of the house being recognizable to one who stands on the roof → one does not bring up onto the small one utensils that were in the house at the start of Shabbos; onto the large one, it is permitted. They are not, for example if the roof juts out → it is a karmelis, unless a window of four by four opens to it from the house (seif ד); even then, bringing up onto that roof utensils that were in the house at the start of Shabbos remains forbidden without a joint eruv, the Mishnah Berurah adds (ס״ק כ״ט).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

5. On Shabbos or before Shabbos — seifim א to ד

The first two seifim speak notably of what happens on Shabbos — ונסתם בשבת (seif א), ונפרץ הכותל בשבת (seif ב); seif א also deals with the doorway closed up on a weekday and reopened on Shabbos, for the eruv made for the year —; the last two, of a breach opened before Shabbos — שנפרצ׳ קודם שבת (seif ג), שנפרץ קודם שבת (seif ד). summary: the table condenses these seifim; the full text and its translation are in Level 1.

The doorway closed up deliberately (seif א) — what each one writes, and to what degree:
  • Seif א: in the case of the eruv made for the year, the note in parentheses, in small print, without the word הגה, « ואפילו סתמה במזיד » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:א), followed by its source, the Agur; the text does not name its author, and the Biur Halachah attributes it to the Rema.
  • The Magen Avraham, relying on a responsum of the Maharil, explains that the leniency applies when the doorway was reopened before Shabbos, and that this is what the Agur, who writes in the name of the Maharil, meant; reopened on Shabbos, it is forbidden: « א״כ כשנפתח בשבת אסור דכיון דסתמו במזיד גלי דעתי׳ שאינו חפץ בעירו׳ » (מגן אברהם שע״ד ס״ק א); he ends this point with a צ״ע (it remains to be examined): « ומיהו בסתמא במזיד לא מהני נפתח בשבת וצ״ע » (מגן אברהם שע״ד ס״ק א).
  • The Mishnah Berurah: the Magen Avraham and several Acharonim lean towards forbidding, « ודע דמ״א וכמה אחרונים צידדו לאסור בזה כשחזר ונפתח בשבת » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ה); it writes: « ואין להקל כשסתמו במזיד אלא כשנפתח קודם השבת » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ה); and if, when he closed it, he intended to close it only for a time and to open it again, it stands to reason, it writes, that it is permitted: « מסתברא דמותר אף כשחזר ונפתח בשבת » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ה).
  • The Biur Halachah: « ומ״מ לענין סתמו במזיד שהמ״א הביא ממהרי״ל שלא התיר אלא דוקא בשנפתח קודם השבת וכן הסכים בא״ר ותו״ש בודאי אין להקל בזה » (ביאור הלכה שע״ד); and: « ואף שיש אחרונים שמיישבין בדוחק את סברת הרמ״א דמתיר בכל גווני מ״מ למעשה נראה שאין להקל » (ביאור הלכה שע״ד).
  • The Shulchan Aruch HaRav (seif 1), for an eruv made for many Shabbosos: « וַאֲפִלּוּ סְתָמוֹ בְּמֵזִיד » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:א); it reports « וְיֵשׁ מִי שֶׁחוֹשֵׁשׁ לְהַחֲמִיר אִם סְתָמוֹ בְּמֵזִיד » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:א), and writes: « וּלְעִנְיַן הֲלָכָה, בְּדִבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים הַלֵּךְ אַחַר הַמֵּקֵל » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:א).
For a doorway closed up deliberately and reopened on Shabbos, in the case of an eruv made for many Shabbosos, the Rav's text is lenient; the Mishnah Berurah and the Biur Halachah are not; where it was closed up only for a time, the Mishnah Berurah holds that it stands to reason that it is permitted (Level 4, point ג). Consult your Rav.
What the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes in addition (its own numbering, Level 4):
  • Seif 1 (the Mechaber's seif א): it writes that one may make use מִזֶּה לְזֶה, with the reason; it also writes the opposite case: a doorway closed up while it was still day remains forbidden even if it reopened on Shabbos, « לְפִי שֶׁעֲדַיִן לֹא קָנוּ עֵרוּב מֵעוֹלָם, שֶׁקְּנִיַּת הָעֵרוּב הוּא בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:א), and an eruv made before a past Shabbos does not help. The Biur Halachah, later than the Rav, writes the same rule in very close words, without naming a source (Level 4, point ב). The Rav's seif 1 does not report the view of the Rambam.
  • Seif 2 (the Mechaber's seif ב): each one may carry in his courtyard עַד מְקוֹם הַכֹּתֶל; for the courtyard breached onto the public domain or onto a karmelis, it specifies פִּרְצָה הָאוֹסַרְתָּהּ and refers to siman 362. The limit up to the wall, and that for the courtyard breached onto the public domain one does not say that the permitted Shabbos remains permitted, are already found in the Beis Yosef; for the size of the breach, the Beis Yosef for its part refers to siman 360 (Level 4, point ד).
  • Seif 3 (the Mechaber's seif ג): the small one does not forbid the large one, even without an eruv between them, « מִפְּנֵי שֶׁפִּרְצָה זוֹ הִיא לַגְּדוֹלָה כְּפֶתַח שֶׁאֵין לָהּ בְּמִלּוּאָהּ » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:ג); Level 4, point ה. For the condition of the walls, it gives the same reason as the Mechaber, נראה מבחוץ ושוה מבפנים (seen from outside and even from inside), and the Beis Yosef brings the three-tefachim gap (lavud) back to that reason: this is not a point where the Rav writes more.
  • Seif 4 (the Mechaber's seif ד): it first sets out the case of roofs surrounded by a parapet, « וְכֵן גַּג קָטָן הַמֻּקָּף מַעֲקֶה סְבִיבוֹ גְּבוֹהָה עָלָיו עֲשָׂרָה » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:ד), the houses not having made an eruv together, and forbids the small one « אִם מְחִצּוֹתָיו נִכְנָסוֹת לְתוֹךְ הַגָּדוֹל » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:ד); then the case without a parapet, when the walls of the house are visible — the very condition of the Mechaber's seif ד, which the Rav makes precise: « לְמִי שֶׁעוֹמֵד עַל שְׂפַת הַגַּג כְּשֶׁמִּסְתַּכֵּל תַּחַת רַגְלָיו » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:ד), as the Mishnah Berurah also writes (see Level 1); and, after the karmelis and the window: « אֲבָל מִכָּל מָקוֹם אִם לֹא עֵרְבוּ יַחַד אַף הַגָּדוֹל אָסוּר בְּכֵלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בַּבַּיִת » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:ד). The sentence on the large roof has its parallel, after the Rav, in the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ט; Level 4, point ו).

6. Mnemonic — on Shabbos, before Shabbos

The four seifim go in pairs, in their order: the first two speak notably of what happens on Shabbos (seif א also deals with the doorway closed up on a weekday and reopened on Shabbos, for the eruv made for the year), the last two of a breach opened before Shabbos. One letter per seif:

א — ונסתם בשבת: the eruv opening closed up on Shabbos — one may make use from one to the other (according to the Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב) by way of the top of the wall and its holes.

ב — ונפרץ הכותל בשבת: the wall between two courtyards breached on Shabbos — if each had made an eruv by itself, each one may carry in his courtyard, even the utensils of the house; but the courtyard breached onto the public domain or onto a karmelis is forbidden.

ג — שנפרצ׳ קודם שבת: the small courtyard breached before Shabbos, along its full width, onto a large one — the large one, which made an eruv by itself, is permitted; the small one is forbidden without a joint eruv, under the conditions of the seif.

ד — שנפרץ קודם שבת: the small roof breached before Shabbos, along its full width, onto a large one, without a joint eruv and under the conditions of the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ג) — one does not bring up onto the small one utensils that were in the house at the start of Shabbos; onto the large one it is permitted, if the walls of the house are recognizable.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that the opening closed up on Shabbos cancels the eruv of that Shabbos. Seif א: « ונסתם בשבת מותרים להשתמש דרך גובה הכותל וחוריו » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:א); although the holes do not have the size of a window, the Mishnah Berurah writes, « לא נתבטל העירוב על שבת זו » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ג). Conversely, do not judge in the same way an opening closed up while it was still day: « אבל אם נסתם מבעוד יום אפילו חזר ונפתח בשבת אסורין לפי שעדיין לא קנו עירוב מעולם » (ביאור הלכה שע״ד) — except for the eruv made for several Shabbosos or for the year, already acquired on an earlier Shabbos while the doorway was open (seif א).
Pitfall 2: Reading the note in parentheses of seif א as a permission without reservation. « ואפילו סתמה במזיד » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:א) is not introduced by הגה, and the Biur Halachah attributes it to the Rema. The Mishnah Berurah: « ואין להקל כשסתמו במזיד אלא כשנפתח קודם השבת » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ה) (and if he closed it only for a time, it holds that it stands to reason that it is permitted); the Biur Halachah: « מ״מ למעשה נראה שאין להקל » (ביאור הלכה שע״ד); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (seif 1): « וּלְעִנְיַן הֲלָכָה, בְּדִבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים הַלֵּךְ אַחַר הַמֵּקֵל » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:א). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 3: Taking the view of the Rambam as the ruling. Seif א reports it at its end: « ולהרמב״ם אין מותר לטלטל אלא כל אחת לעצמה אבל מזו לזו לא » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:א). The Mishnah Berurah relates it to the beginning of the seif, the doorway closed up on Shabbos; if it reopened, « ומ״מ אם חזר ונפתח לכו״ע מותר לטלטל מזו לזו כמו מתחלה » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ו); and « ולענין הלכה העיקר כסברא הראשונה כ״כ האחרונים » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ו).
Pitfall 4: Extending to the courtyard breached onto the public domain the permission given for the wall between two courtyards. Seif ב: « אבל חצר שנפרצה בשבת לרשו׳ הרבים או לכרמלית אסור » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:ב); here, the Mishnah Berurah writes, « אבל זה שנפרץ לרשות אחרת ממש לא אמרינן ביה הואיל והותרה הותרה » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק ט). On what the Beis Yosef permits within four amos, even towards the karmelis, the Biur Halachah reports the doubt of the Magen Avraham and views in both directions, and concludes: « וע״כ נראה שאין להקל » (ביאור הלכה שע״ד).
Pitfall 5: Judging the small courtyard like the large one. Seif ג: « גדול׳ מותרת להוציא כלי׳ ששבתו בבית לחצרה אם עירבה לעצמה » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:ג), but « וקטנה אסור׳ להוציא כלים ששבתו בבית לחצירה אלא אם כן עירבו יחד » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:ג) — and this « כשנכנסין כותלי קטנה לגדולה וכשכתלי קטנה מופלגים ג״ט מכותלי אורך הגדולה » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:ג); otherwise, the small one too is permitted. The seif speaks of a breach opened before Shabbos, along the full width of the small one, not exceeding ten amos.
Pitfall 6: Thinking that a window of four by four permits everything on the roof. Seif ד: « אבל אם אינם ניכרות כגון שהגג בולט עליהם הוי כרמלית אלא אם כן פתוח לו מהבית חלון ארבע על ארבע » (שו״ע או״ח שע״ד:ד); the Mishnah Berurah adds: « אבל להעלות עליו כלים ששבתו בבית אסור כ״ז שלא עירבו יחד כיון שאין המחיצות ניכרות עליו » (משנה ברורה שע״ד ס״ק כ״ט). And the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (seif 4), after the window: « אֲבָל מִכָּל מָקוֹם אִם לֹא עֵרְבוּ יַחַד אַף הַגָּדוֹל אָסוּר בְּכֵלִים שֶׁשָּׁבְתוּ בַּבַּיִת » (שו״ע הרב שע״ד:ד).
Pitfall 7: Mixing up the two numberings, or reading into the Mechaber's seif what the Rav writes and that seif does not. Levels 1 to 3 follow the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber; Level 4 follows the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, which also has 4 seifim, of the same rank. But its seifim write more — among other things, the doorway closed up while it was still day (seif 1) and the parapet of the roofs (seif 4) — and its seif 1 does not report the view of the Rambam: see section 5.

10. The practical commandments of Siman שע״ד

For daily conduct

  1. The eruv opening closed up on Shabbos: one may make use from one to the other by way of the top of the wall and its holes (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב and ג). Closed up while it was still day, it remains forbidden even if it reopened on Shabbos (Biur Halachah; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, seif 1), except for the eruv made for several Shabbosos or for the year, already acquired on an earlier Shabbos while the doorway was open.
  2. The eruv made for the year: the doorway closed up on a weekday and opened on Shabbos, the eruv returns to its permission (seif א). Closed up deliberately: the note in parentheses includes it; the Mishnah Berurah is lenient only if it reopened before Shabbos, and holds that it stands to reason to permit if he closed it only for a time (ס״ק ה); the Biur Halachah concludes that in practice it seems one should not be lenient; the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes that, in the words of the Sofrim, one follows the lenient view (seif 1). Consult your Rav.
  3. The view of the Rambam: when the opening is closed up on Shabbos, each one by itself only (seif א); for the halachah, the main view is the first one, according to the Acharonim (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו).
  4. The wall between two courtyards breached on Shabbos: if each had made an eruv by itself, each one may carry in his courtyard, even the utensils of the house (seif ב) — up to the place of the wall, writes the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (seif 2).
  5. The courtyard breached on Shabbos onto the public domain or onto a karmelis is forbidden (seif ב); the Biur Halachah concludes that it seems one should not be lenient to carry from the courtyard to the karmelis within four amos.
  6. The small courtyard breached before Shabbos onto a large one, along its full width, by a breach of at most ten amos: the large one, which made an eruv by itself, is permitted; the small one is forbidden without a joint eruv, when its walls enter the large one and are three tefachim away from its walls; otherwise, it too is permitted (seif ג).
  7. The small roof breached before Shabbos, along its full width, onto a large one (without a joint eruv, the small roof not being more than ten amos; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ג): one does not bring up onto the small one utensils that were in the house at the start of Shabbos, one may bring them up onto the large one, provided that the walls of the house are recognizable; otherwise, for example if the roof juts out, it is a karmelis, unless a window of four by four opens to it from the house (seif ד); even then, bringing up onto that roof utensils that were in the house at the start of Shabbos remains forbidden without a joint eruv (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ט).
  8. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
