The seif numbers on this page are those of the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber (2 seifim, with no gloss of the Rema). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav, studied in Level 4, also writes this siman in 2 seifim, under its own numbering: when it is cited, its name is given next to the number.

1. The central axiom

Siman ש״צ in one sentence.
When a non-Jew lives in the alley, the eruv made among Jews does not always let them use the alley. Another Jew, whose house does not open onto the alley, has a window between him and the Jew of the alley: « אינו יכול לערב דרך החלון שביניהם להוציא כליו למבוי דרך בית ישראל שכנו הפתוח למבוי » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א); with a door between them, the seif permits, then reports an opinion that forbids (seif א; section 5). The Jews who made an eruv among themselves through windows take [objects] out and bring them in through the windows, but may not use the alley through the doors until they rent from the non-Jew (seif ב).

3. The course of the seifim — from the window to the rental

Seif א, the case: an alley one side of which is [inhabited by a] non-Jew and the other side by a Jew; the house of another Jew, next to him, opens onto the public domain and not onto the alley, and there is a window between him and the Jew who lives in the alley.
↓
Seif א, the window: he cannot make an eruv through the window that is between them to take his objects out to the alley through the house of the Jew his neighbor, which opens onto the alley.
↓
Seif א, the door: if there is a door between them, it is permitted; and there is one who forbids, even when a door is open between them (section 5).
↓
Seif ב, the eruv through the windows: in an alley one side of which is [inhabited by a] non-Jew and the other side by Jews, these all made an eruv through the windows open between their courtyards: they became like the members of one household, and take [objects] out and bring them in through the windows.
↓
Seif ב, the rental: but they are forbidden to use the alley through the doors, until they rent from the non-Jew.

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes this siman in two seifim: its seif 1 corresponds to seif א of the Mechaber, its seif 2 to seif ב. Level 4 notes where its text differs: a single Jew, and many Jews in one courtyard (point א); the reason for the prohibition through the window (point ב); the door, where its seif does not report the opinion that forbids (point ג); the case where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on them in the alley, and the reason (point ד).

4. Decision tree — does the eruv made among Jews let them use the alley?

Q1: What is the case?
↓ Another Jew, whose house does not open onto the alley, next to the Jew of the alley (seif א) → Q2. Jews of the alley who all made an eruv through the windows between their courtyards (seif ב) → Q3.
Q2: What is between them? (seif א)
↓ A window → he cannot make an eruv through it to take his objects out to the alley through his neighbor's house (seif א). A door → the seif permits, then reports an opinion that forbids even then (section 5).
Q3: What does this eruv allow them? (seif ב)
↓ They take [objects] out and bring them in through the windows; but they may not use the alley through the doors: « עד שישכרו מן העכו״ם » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:ב). Seif 2 of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav specifies: in a case where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on them in the alley (390:2).
→ When in any doubt: consult your Rav.

5. A door between them — the permission and the opinion that forbids

The seif first writes the permission without a name, then the opinion that forbids: « אבל אם פתח ביניהם מותר ויש מי שאוסר אף בפתח פתוח ביניהם » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א).

summary: the table takes up the voices that Level 2 (section 3) cites in their own words; the commentators diverge.

WorkWhat it writes
Rashi, according to Tosafos and the Beis Yosef (Level 2, section 3)Tosafos bring his explanation of the windows: « והא דנקט חלונות פי׳ בקונט׳ לפי שאין דרך בני אדם לעשות פתחים גדולים מבית לבית » (תוספות עירובין ע״ד ע״ב) — people are not accustomed to make large openings from one house to another; the Beis Yosef infers: « ומשמע שאם היה פתח פתוח מבית לבית ה״נ דאין מערבין דרך הפתח » (בית יוסף או״ח שפ״ב) — through a doorway, too, one would not make the eruv
The Ri, according to Tosafos (Level 2, section 3)« אבל אם יש פתחים ביניהן מותר לערב » (תוספות עירובין ע״ד ע״ב) — if there are doors between them, it is permitted to make the eruv; the Beis Yosef: « אבל התוס׳ והרא״ש כתבו בשם ר״י » (בית יוסף או״ח שפ״ב) — Tosafos and the Rosh wrote it in the name of the Ri
Tur, as Sefaria serves it (Level 1, section 2)« אבל אם פתח ביניהם מותר לר״י ולרש״י אף אם פתח ביניהם אסור » (טור או״ח ש״צ) — the permission according to the Ri, the prohibition according to Rashi
Taz (ס״ק א; Level 1, section 2)On “ויש מי שאוסר”: « היינו רש״י גם כאן יש ט״ס בטור ברוב ספרים בין רש״י לר״י כמו בסי׳ שס״ג » (ט״ז או״ח ש״צ ס״ק א) — that is Rashi; here too, in most editions of the Tur, there is a copyist's error between Rashi and Ri, as in siman 363
Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ו; Level 2, section 3)It explains the permission, limits it to the properly fixed alley, then refers to what it wrote in siman 382, seif 19, in the Mishnah Berurah and the Biur Halachah, « ושייך ג״כ לכאן » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק ו); there, on ויש מי שאומר: « ויש להחמיר כדעה זו ועיין ביאור הלכה » (משנה ברורה שפ״ב ס״ק ס״ט) — one should be stringent according to this view; and see the Biur Halachah

6. Mnemonic ח-פ-ע-ש

Four words of the seifim, in their order:

ח — חלון: through the window between them, he cannot make an eruv to take his objects out to the alley through his neighbor's house (seif א).

פ — פתח: with a door between them, the seif permits, then reports an opinion that forbids (seif א; section 5).

ע — ועירבו: the Jews who made an eruv through the windows are like the members of one household, and take [objects] out and bring them in through the windows (seif ב).

ש — שישכרו: but they may not use the alley through the doors until they rent from the non-Jew (seif ב).

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that the window is judged like the door. Through the window, he cannot make an eruv to take his objects out to the alley through his neighbor's house; with a door, the seif permits, then reports an opinion that forbids (seif א; section 5).
Pitfall 2: Giving only one rule for the door. The seif reports an opinion that forbids even with a door, under “ויש מי שאוסר”; the commentators diverge: see section 5 and Level 2 (section 3). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 3: Writing that the Shulchan Aruch HaRav rejects the opinion that forbids. Its seif 1 writes that if there is a door between them, he may make an eruv with him, and does not report that opinion; Level 4 does not say why (point ג). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 4: Thinking that the eruv they made through the windows lets them use the alley. It makes them like the members of one household, and they take [objects] out and bring them in through the windows; but « הרי אלו אסורי׳ להשתמש במבוי דרך פתחי׳ עד שישכרו מן העכו״ם » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:ב). Seif 2 of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav specifies: in a case where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on them in the alley (390:2).

10. The practical commandments of Siman ש״צ

For daily conduct

  1. The window: the Jew whose house does not open onto the alley, with a window between him and the Jew of the alley, neighbor of a non-Jew, cannot make an eruv through that window to take his objects out to the alley through his neighbor's house (seif א).
  2. The door: if there is a door between them, the seif permits, then reports an opinion that forbids even then (seif א; section 5): consult your Rav.
  3. The eruv through the windows: the Jews who made it take [objects] out and bring them in through the windows, but may not use the alley through the doors until they rent from the non-Jew (seif ב); seif 2 of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav specifies: in a case where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on them in the alley (390:2).
  4. When in doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
