1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman ש״צ contains 2 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo), without a gloss of the Rema. Topic of the siman, according to its title: an alley one side of which is [inhabited by a] non-Jew and the other side [by a] Jew. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe also has 2 seifim for this siman: its numbering is that of Level 4, not that of this page.

Seif א

מבוי שצדו אחד עכו״ם וצדו אחד ישראל. ובו ב סעיפים:
מבוי שצדו א' עכו״ם וצדו א' ישראל ובית של ישראל אחר אצלו פתוח לר״ה ולא למבוי וחלון בינו לבין ישראל הדר במבוי אינו יכול לערב דרך החלון שביניהם להוציא כליו למבוי דרך בית ישראל שכנו הפתוח למבוי אבל אם פתח ביניהם מותר ויש מי שאוסר אף בפתח פתוח ביניהם:
Translation: An alley (mavoy) one side of which is [inhabited by a] non-Jew (akum) and the other side by a Jew; and the house of another Jew, next to him, opens onto the public domain (reshus harabim) and not onto the alley, and there is a window between him and the Jew who lives in the alley: he cannot make an eruv through the window that is between them, in order to take his objects out to the alley through the house of the Jew his neighbor, which opens onto the alley. But if there is a door between them, it is permitted. And there is one who forbids, even when a door is open between them.
What this seif says:
  • The case: a Jew in the alley, facing a non-Jew, and another Jew next to him whose house opens only onto the public domain: מבוי שצדו א׳ עכו״ם וצדו א׳ ישראל ובית של ישראל אחר אצלו פתוח לר״ה ולא למבוי וחלון בינו לבין ישראל הדר במבוי. According to the Mishnah Berurah, this is the case where only one Jewish courtyard opens onto the alley (ס״ק א), and the other Jew's “house” means a courtyard (ס״ק ב); if that second courtyard also opened onto the alley, there would be two Jewish courtyards, which would need to rent the alley from the non-Jew and make a shituf between them (ס״ק ג).
  • Through the window, he cannot make an eruv to take his objects out to the alley: אינו יכול לערב דרך החלון שביניהם להוציא כליו למבוי דרך בית ישראל שכנו הפתוח למבוי. That is, to make an eruv relying on the window open between them (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד). The reason it gives: although there is only one Jew here whose courtyard opens onto the alley, the Sages forbade this one to carry through his fellow's courtyard, even by an eruv, so that that Jew should not rely on him, who goes out and comes in often by means of his eruv, and remain living here alone in the place of a non-Jew, and learn from his deeds (ס״ק ה).
  • If there is a door between them, it is permitted: אבל אם פתח ביניהם מותר. For, explains the Mishnah Berurah, even without the eruv this Jew would not be afraid to live here: with a real door, his fellow can come in at any time, and so the Sages did not decree; and all this when the alley is properly fixed, with a lechi and a beam or with a tzuras hapesach (ס״ק ו). It refers to what it wrote in siman 382, seif 19, as applying here too; there, it writes to be stringent according to the view that forbids (section 2). The commentators diverge on the door: see Level 2, section 3.
  • An opinion, reported by the seif, forbids even with a door: ויש מי שאוסר אף בפתח פתוח ביניהם. The seif first writes the permission without a name, then this opinion under “יש מי שאוסר”. The Taz writes that this is Rashi (ס״ק א; section 2). The commentators discuss it: see Level 2. Consult your Rav.

Seif ב

מבוי שצדו א' עכו״ם וצדו א' ישראלי' והיו חלונות פתוחות מחצרו לחצר של ישראל ועירבו כולם דרך חלונות אע״פ שנעשו כאנשי בית א' ומותרי' להוצי' ולהכניס דרך חלונו' הרי אלו אסורי' להשתמש במבוי דרך פתחי' עד שישכרו מן העכו״ם:
Translation: An alley one side of which is [inhabited by a] non-Jew and the other side by Jews, and there were windows open from his courtyard to the courtyard of a Jew, and they all made an eruv through the windows: even though they have become like the members of one household, and are permitted to take out and bring in [objects] through the windows, they are forbidden to use the alley through the doors, until they rent from the non-Jew.
What this seif says:
  • The Jews on the other side of the alley all made an eruv through the windows between their courtyards: מבוי שצדו א׳ עכו״ם וצדו א׳ ישראלי׳ והיו חלונות פתוחות מחצרו לחצר של ישראל ועירבו כולם דרך חלונות. Their courtyards open onto the alley (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ז); and all the more so does the eruv work if there were doors between them (ס״ק ח).
  • They are like the members of one household, and take out and bring in [objects] through the windows: אע״פ שנעשו כאנשי בית א׳ ומותרי׳ להוצי׳ ולהכניס דרך חלונו.
  • But they may not use the alley through the doors until they have rented from the non-Jew: הרי אלו אסורי׳ להשתמש במבוי דרך פתחי׳ עד שישכרו מן העכו״ם. Through the doors that open onto the alley (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט); for the eruv does not serve to make them like an individual in the place of a non-Jew, and it refers to siman 382 (ס״ק י).

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

The Beit Yosef writes, on this siman of the Tur: « כבר נתבאר כל זה בס״ס שפ״ב ושם כתבו רבינו לענין חצר שפתוחים לו בתים בענין זה וכאן כתבו לענין מבוי שפתוחים לו חצירות בענין זה » (בית יוסף או״ח ש״צ) — all this has already been explained at the end of siman 382: there, our master [the Tur] wrote it for a courtyard onto which houses open, and here for an alley onto which courtyards open. The Mishnah Berurah likewise refers to what it wrote in siman 382, seif 19, in the Mishnah Berurah and the Biur Halachah, « ושייך ג״כ לכאן »: it is one rule, which the Mechaber wrote there for a courtyard and here for an alley (ס״ק ו). That seif ends: « אבל אם פתח פתוח ביניהם מותר ויש מי שאומר דאף אם פתח פתוח ביניהם אסור » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ב:י״ט); and there, on ויש מי שאומר, the Mishnah Berurah writes: « ויש להחמיר כדעה זו ועיין ביאור הלכה » (משנה ברורה שפ״ב ס״ק ס״ט) — one should be stringent according to this view; and see the Biur Halachah (Level 2, sections 3 and 4).

The Tur, as Sefaria serves it, names those who differ about the door: « אבל אם פתח ביניהם מותר לר״י ולרש״י אף אם פתח ביניהם אסור » (טור או״ח ש״צ) — the permission is the view of the Ri, the prohibition that of Rashi. The Taz writes, on “ויש מי שאוסר”: « היינו רש״י גם כאן יש ט״ס בטור ברוב ספרים בין רש״י לר״י כמו בסי׳ שס״ג » (ט״ז או״ח ש״צ ס״ק א) — this is Rashi; and here too, in most editions of the Tur, there is a scribal error between Rashi and the Ri, as in siman 363.

The Beit Yosef also recalls that the alley of this siman, onto which only one Jewish courtyard opens, is permitted by a lechi or a beam: « וכבר כתב רבינו בסימן שס״ג שמבוי זה אע״פ שאין בו אלא חצר אחד של ישראל ניתר בלחי או קורה כיון שפתוח לו חצר אחד ואע״ג שדר בו הכותי דירת הכותי חשיבא דירה לקולא » (בית יוסף או״ח ש״צ).

The fundamental question: when a non-Jew lives in the alley, what does the eruv that the Jews make among themselves permit them? The siman answers for two cases: a Jew whose house does not open onto the alley cannot make an eruv through a window with the Jew of the alley in order to take his objects out to the alley through his house; with a door between them it is permitted, and the seif reports an opinion that forbids it (seif א). Jews who made an eruv among themselves through windows take out and bring in [objects] through the windows, but may not use the alley through their doors until they have rented from the non-Jew (seif ב).

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. Siman 382 (seif 1) explains when a non-Jew who lives in a courtyard with Jews forbids [carrying] for them, and that one must then rent from him; the Mishnah Berurah of this siman refers to it (ס״ק ו and י). Siman 391 follows; its title is « דיני ביטול רשות למי ששכחו לערב » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א).

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman, in the order of the seifim:

The essential notions of siman ש״צ:
  • מבוי שצדו אחד עכו״ם — the alley one side of which is [inhabited by a] non-Jew. In seif א, by a single Jew: « ומיירי שאין כאן אלא חצר אחד של ישראל הפתוח למבוי » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק א); two Jewish courtyards opening onto the alley, by contrast, « וצריכים לשכור המבוי מן העכו״ם ולשתף בינם » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק ג). In seif ב, by several Jews.
  • דרך החלון שביניהם — the eruv through the window: « ר״ל שיערבו על סמך שיש חלון פתוח ביניהם » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק ד).
  • יחידי במקום עכו״ם — alone in the place of a non-Jew: the reason the Mishnah Berurah gives for the prohibition of seif א, « כדי שלא יסמוך האי ישראל עליו שיוצא ונכנס תדיר ע״י עירובו וישאר לדור כאן יחידי במקום עכו״ם וילמוד ממעשיו » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק ה); and in seif ב, « דאין עירוב מועיל לעשותם כיחיד במקום עכו״ם » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק י).
  • פתח גמור — the real door, with which seif א permits (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו). The seif also reports an opinion that forbids even with a door; the commentators diverge (seif א; Level 2, section 3).
  • עד שישכרו מן העכו״ם — until they rent from the non-Jew: although the eruv has made them like the members of one household, they may not use the alley through their doors before this rental (seif ב).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אAn alley one side of which is [inhabited by a] non-Jew and the other by a Jew; another Jew, next to him, has a house opening onto the public domain and not onto the alley, with a window between him and the Jew of the alley: he cannot make an eruv through that window to take his objects out to the alley through his neighbor's house. If there is a door between them, it is permitted; and the seif reports an opinion that forbids even with a door.
בAn alley one side of which is [inhabited by a] non-Jew and the other by Jews, with windows open from one courtyard to another, through which they all made an eruv: although they have become like the members of one household and take out and bring in [objects] through the windows, they are forbidden to use the alley through the doors until they rent from the non-Jew.
The heart of the siman: when a non-Jew lives in the alley, the eruv made between Jews is not always enough to permit them to use the alley: the Jew whose house does not open onto the alley does not take his objects out to the alley through his neighbor's house (seif א), and the Jews who made an eruv through windows do not use the alley through their doors before renting from the non-Jew (seif ב). With a door between them, seif א permits, then it reports an opinion that forbids; the commentators diverge (Level 2).

5. The Mishnah Berurah — first entries

The Mishnah Berurah of Rabbi Yisrael Meir Kagan (the Chafetz Chaim) has 10 ס״ק on this siman: six on seif א, four on seif ב. Here they all are, in order — to better understand the meaning of the seifim:

משנה ברורה (א) — (א) וצדו אחד ישראל - ומיירי שאין כאן אלא חצר אחד של ישראל הפתוח למבוי:
משנה ברורה (ב) — (ב) ובית של ישראל אחר - ר״ל חצר:
משנה ברורה (ג) — (ג) ולא למבוי - דאלו היה גם חצר השני פתוח למבוי א״כ הרי יש כאן שתי חצרות של ישראלים וצריכים לשכור המבוי מן העכו״ם ולשתף בינם:
משנה ברורה (ד) — (ד) דרך החלון - ר״ל שיערבו על סמך שיש חלון פתוח ביניהם:
משנה ברורה (ה) — (ה) להוציא כליו למבוי - ואע״ג דאין כאן אלא חד ישראל שחצירו פתוח למבוי מ״מ אסרו חכמים לזה לטלטל דרך חצירו של חבירו אפילו ע״י עירוב כדי שלא יסמוך האי ישראל עליו שיוצא ונכנס תדיר ע״י עירובו וישאר לדור כאן יחידי במקום עכו״ם וילמוד ממעשיו:
משנה ברורה (ו) — (ו) מותר - דבלאו הערוב ג״כ לא מסתפי האי ישראל לדור כאן דכיון שיש פתח גמור מצוי בכל עת ובכל שעה שיכנס האי ישראל חבירו ולהכי לא גזרו. וכ״ז כשהמבוי מתוקן כדין בלחי וקורה או בצוה״פ ועיין לעיל בסימן שפ״ב סי״ט במ״ב מש״כ שם ושייך ג״כ לכאן וכן מה שכתבנו שם בבה״ל כי הוא ענין אחד רק דשם כתב המחבר דין זה לענין חצר וכאן כתבו לענין מבוי:
משנה ברורה (ז) — (ז) וצדו אחד ישראלים - וחצריהם פתוחין למבוי:
משנה ברורה (ח) — (ח) דרך חלונות - וכ״ש אם היו פתחים ביניהם דמהני העירוב:
משנה ברורה (ט) — (ט) דרך פתחים - הפתוחים למבוי:
משנה ברורה (י) — (י) עד שישכרו וכו' - דאין עירוב מועיל לעשותם כיחיד במקום עכו״ם כדאיתא לעיל סי' שפ״ב ס״ז:

6. Position of the Rema

In the text Sefaria serves (Maginei Eretz edition), the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) adds no gloss (הגה) to the two seifim of this siman.

Without a gloss of the Rema:
  • Seif א — no gloss of the Rema; the opinion “ויש מי שאוסר” is written in the Mechaber's seif itself (sections 1 and 2, and Level 2).
  • Seif ב — no gloss of the Rema; the Mishnah Berurah explains it (sections 1 and 5).
  • Chabad → the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman ש״צ, in 2 seifim, with its own numbering (see Level 4 — Daat HaRav).

7. Modern practical cases

The situations below are applications of the site: each is tied to the statement of the seif that it brings into play.

SituationQuick analysis
A Jew whose house opens only onto the public domain wants to pass his objects to the alley through the house of his Jewish neighbor, the only Jew of the alley, facing a non-JewApplication of the site, seif א: through a window between them, he cannot make an eruv for this; if there is a door between them, the seif permits, then reports an opinion that forbids it; the commentators diverge (Level 2). Consult your Rav.
Several Jewish courtyards open onto the alley, where a non-Jew also lives, made an eruv among themselves through windows from one courtyard to anotherApplication of the site, seif ב: they take out and bring in [objects] through the windows, but may not use the alley through their doors until they rent from the non-Jew; the eruv does not make them like an individual in the place of a non-Jew (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav specifies: « בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁהַנָּכְרִי אוֹסֵר עֲלֵיהֶם בַּמָּבוֹי » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:ב; Level 4). Consult your Rav.
For precise practical cases: the seifim make the rule depend, among other things, on what connects the two Jews — a window or a door (seif א) — and on the rental from the non-Jew (seif ב); and seif א reports an opinion that forbids even with a door. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman ש״צ:
  1. Through a window, the Jew whose house does not open onto the alley cannot make an eruv with the Jew of the alley, neighbor of a non-Jew, to take his objects out to the alley through his house (seif א) — so that that Jew should not remain living alone in the place of a non-Jew, explains the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ה).
  2. With a door between them, the seif permits, then reports an opinion that forbids it; the commentators diverge (seif א; Level 2).
  3. The Jews who made an eruv through windows are like the members of one household, but may not use the alley through their doors until they rent from the non-Jew (seif ב).
  4. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. What case does seif א speak of: how many Jewish courtyards open onto the alley, and what would be needed if there were two (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א and ג)?
  2. Why did the Sages forbid, in seif א, the eruv made through the window (ס״ק ה)?
  3. Why does a door change the rule according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ו), and what opinion does the seif report?
  4. In seif ב, what does the eruv made through the windows permit, and what does it not permit (ס״ק י)?
