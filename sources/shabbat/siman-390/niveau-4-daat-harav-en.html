דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on Siman 390

A mavoi where a non-Jew and a single Jew live: may the one who lives there make an eruv, through a window or through a doorway, with the neighbouring house that does not open onto this mavoi? And, in a mavoi where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on them, may the Jews who made an eruv through the windows between their houses or courtyards use the mavoi? The two seifim of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, compared with the Mechaber's two seifim.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav is not a commentary on the Mechaber: it is a Shulchan Aruch in its own right, written by the Alter Rebbe, with its own division and numbering of the seifim. Here the Shulchan Aruch HaRav announces 2 seifim at the head of the siman, and the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber has 2 as well. Sefaria serves the Rav's text in 2 blocks, which this page reproduces and numbers as they stand (section 1). The Rav's seif 1 corresponds to the Mechaber's seif א, and his seif 2 to seif ב. In the English text of this page, the Rav's seifim are designated by their numeral (390:1, seif 1), the Mechaber's by their Hebrew letter (the Mechaber's seif א); in the Hebrew references in parentheses, both numberings are in letters, and the reference always names the work (שו״ע או״ח for the Mechaber, שו״ע הרב for the Rav).

This page gathers, for Siman 390, the full text of the Rav with its translation, where his text differs from the Mechaber's, and the practical conduct that follows from it.

1 · Text of the Alter Rebbe

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן ש״צ

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav of the Alter Rebbe

סימן ש״צ — מָבוֹי שֶׁצִּדּוֹ אֶחָד נָכְרִי וְצִדּוֹ אֶחָד יִשְׂרָאֵל — וּבוֹ ב׳ סְעִיפִים
Source: Kehot edition, as reproduced on Sefaria. The heading announces 2 seifim; Sefaria serves the text in 2 blocks (the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has 2 seifim, under its own numbering).

סעיף א מָבוֹי שֶׁצִּדּוֹ אֶחָד נָכְרִי וְצִדּוֹ אֶחָד יִשְׂרָאֵל וּבוֹ ב' סְעִיפִים: מָבוֹי שֶׁדָּרִים בּוֹ נָכְרִי וְיִשְׂרָאֵל אֶחָד…

מָבוֹי שֶׁצִּדּוֹ אֶחָד נָכְרִי וְצִדּוֹ אֶחָד יִשְׂרָאֵל וּבוֹ ב' סְעִיפִים:

מָבוֹי שֶׁדָּרִים בּוֹ נָכְרִי וְיִשְׂרָאֵל אֶחָד — שֶׁמֻּתָּר לְטַלְטֵל בַּמָּבוֹי, כְּמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שצ״א — וְיֵשׁ בַּיִת אֶחָד אֵצֶל בֵּיתוֹ שֶׁל זֶה, וְאֵינוֹ פָתוּחַ לְמָבוֹי זֶה אֶלָּא לְמָבוֹי אַחֵר אוֹ לִרְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים, וְיֵשׁ חַלּוֹן בֵּינוֹ לְיִשְׂרָאֵל — הַדָּר בְּמָבוֹי זֶה אֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לְעָרֵב עִמּוֹ עַל יְדֵי הַחַלּוֹן שֶׁבֵּינֵיהֶם כְּדֵי לְהוֹצִיא כֵּלָיו לְמָבוֹי זֶה דֶּרֶךְ בֵּיתוֹ שֶׁל זֶה הַפָּתוּחַ לְמָבוֹי זֶה, לְפִי שֶׁאָסוּר לַעֲשׂוֹת סִיּוּעַ לְיָחִיד הַדָּר עִם נָכְרִי, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ב. וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם יִשְׂרְאֵלִים רַבִּים דָּרִים בְּחָצֵר אַחַת — הֲרֵי הֵם כְּיָחִיד לְעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל בַּמָּבוֹי, כְּמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שצ״א. אֲבָל אִם יֵשׁ פֶּתַח בֵּינֵיהֶם — יָכוֹל לְעָרֵב עִמּוֹ, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ב:

A mavoi one side of which is [inhabited by] a non-Jew and one side by a Jew. It contains two seifim:

A mavoi (alley) in which a non-Jew and a single Jew live — in which it is permitted to move [objects] in the mavoi, as will be explained in siman 391 — and there is a house next to this one's house, which does not open onto this mavoi but onto another mavoi or onto the public domain (reshus harabim), and there is a window between it and the Jew: the one who lives in this mavoi cannot make an eruv with him by means of the window that is between them, in order to take out his objects (keilim) into this mavoi through the house of this one, which opens onto this mavoi, because it is forbidden to give assistance (siyua) to an individual (yachid) who lives with a non-Jew, as was explained in siman 382. And even if many Jews live in one courtyard, they are like an individual with regard to moving [objects] in the mavoi, as will be explained in siman 391. But if there is a doorway (pesach) between them, he may make an eruv with him, as was explained in siman 382.

סעיף ב מָבוֹי שֶׁדָּרִים בּוֹ יִשְׂרְאֵלִים וְנָכְרִי, בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁהַנָּכְרִי אוֹסֵר עֲלֵיהֶם בַּמָּבוֹי…

מָבוֹי שֶׁדָּרִים בּוֹ יִשְׂרְאֵלִים וְנָכְרִי, בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁהַנָּכְרִי אוֹסֵר עֲלֵיהֶם בַּמָּבוֹי, וְיֵשׁ חַלּוֹנוֹת פְּתוּחוֹת בִּשְׁכוּנוֹת הַיִּשְׂרְאֵלִים מִבַּיִת לְבַיִת אוֹ מֵחָצֵר לְחָצֵר, וְעֵרְבוּ יַחַד עַל יְדֵי הַחַלּוֹנוֹת שֶׁבֵּינֵיהֶם, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁנַּעֲשׂוּ כְּאַנְשֵׁי בַּיִת אֶחָד וּמֻתָּרִים לְהוֹצִיא וּלְהַכְנִיס דֶּרֶךְ חַלּוֹנוֹת מִבַּיִת לְבַיִת וּמֵחָצֵר לְחָצֵר — הֲרֵי אֵלּוּ אֲסוּרִים לְהִשְׁתַּמֵּשׁ בַּמָּבוֹי דֶּרֶךְ פִּתְחֵיהֶם עַד שֶׁיִּשְׂכְּרוּ מִן הַנָּכְרִי, שֶׁעַל יְדֵי עֵרוּב אֵינָן נַעֲשִׂים כְּיָחִיד בִּמְקוֹם נָכְרִי, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ב:

A mavoi in which Jews and a non-Jew live, in a case where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on them in the mavoi, and there are windows open in the dwellings (shechunos) of the Jews, from house to house or from courtyard to courtyard, and they made an eruv together by means of the windows that are between them: even though they have become like the members of one household, and they are permitted to take [objects] out and bring them in through the windows, from house to house and from courtyard to courtyard, they are forbidden to use the mavoi through their doorways until they rent from the non-Jew, since through an eruv they do not become like an individual in the place of a non-Jew, as was explained in siman 382.

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim 390. Sefaria gives no Kuntres Acharon entry for this siman.

2 · The Rav's text and the Mechaber's

לשון הרב מול סעיף המחבר

Where the Rav's text differs from the Mechaber's seif

Each point sets side by side a seif of the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch (its numbering, 2 seifim, designated by their letter; this siman has no gloss of the Rema) and the corresponding seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (its own numbering, also 2 seifim), in the order of the Rav's text, and notes only what the comparison of the two texts shows; this section does not note every difference between the two texts. A point noted is not thereby unique to the Rav: the page sometimes names its source; it notes neither every source nor every divergence. It also sometimes names the Mishnah Berurah, later than the Rav, when it writes the same thing, without noting it everywhere. Where the Mechaber's seif reports a view that the Rav's seif does not report, it does not decide.

נקודה א — ישראל אחד, וישראלים רבים בחצר אחת (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א)

Point א — a single Jew, and many Jews in one courtyard (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:1)

The single Jew in the mavoi

What the Mechaber writes: « מבוי שצדו א' עכו״ם וצדו א' ישראל ובית של ישראל אחר אצלו פתוח לר״ה ולא למבוי » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א).

What the Rav writes: « מָבוֹי שֶׁדָּרִים בּוֹ נָכְרִי וְיִשְׂרָאֵל אֶחָד — שֶׁמֻּתָּר לְטַלְטֵל בַּמָּבוֹי, כְּמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שצ״א » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א); then « וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם יִשְׂרְאֵלִים רַבִּים דָּרִים בְּחָצֵר אַחַת — הֲרֵי הֵם כְּיָחִיד לְעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל בַּמָּבוֹי » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א).

In the commentators: after the Rav, the Mishnah Berurah writes on the Mechaber's words « וצדו א' ישראל » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א): « ומיירי שאין כאן אלא חצר אחד של ישראל הפתוח למבוי » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק א).

The difference: the Mechaber writes « וצדו א' ישראל » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א); the Rav writes « וְיִשְׂרָאֵל אֶחָד » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א), states that it is permitted to move [objects] in this mavoi, referring to siman 391, and adds that many Jews who live in one courtyard are like an individual with regard to moving objects in the mavoi (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א). The Rav also writes that the neighbouring house opens « וְאֵינוֹ פָתוּחַ לְמָבוֹי זֶה אֶלָּא לְמָבוֹי אַחֵר אוֹ לִרְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א), where the Mechaber writes « פתוח לר״ה ולא למבוי » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א).

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, even when many Jews live in one courtyard, they are like an individual with regard to moving objects in the mavoi (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:1).

נקודה ב — לפי שאסור לעשות סיוע ליחיד הדר עם נכרי (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א)

Point ב — the reason for the prohibition (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:1)

Why the eruv through the window is forbidden

What the Mechaber writes: « אינו יכול לערב דרך החלון שביניהם להוציא כליו למבוי דרך בית ישראל שכנו הפתוח למבוי » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א) (for the doorway, see point ג).

What the Rav writes: « הַדָּר בְּמָבוֹי זֶה אֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לְעָרֵב עִמּוֹ עַל יְדֵי הַחַלּוֹן שֶׁבֵּינֵיהֶם כְּדֵי לְהוֹצִיא כֵּלָיו לְמָבוֹי זֶה דֶּרֶךְ בֵּיתוֹ שֶׁל זֶה הַפָּתוּחַ לְמָבוֹי זֶה » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א), then « לְפִי שֶׁאָסוּר לַעֲשׂוֹת סִיּוּעַ לְיָחִיד הַדָּר עִם נָכְרִי, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ב » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א) (for the doorway, see point ג).

In the commentators: after the Rav, the Mishnah Berurah writes: « ואע״ג דאין כאן אלא חד ישראל שחצירו פתוח למבוי מ״מ אסרו חכמים לזה לטלטל דרך חצירו של חבירו אפילו ע״י עירוב כדי שלא יסמוך האי ישראל עליו שיוצא ונכנס תדיר ע״י עירובו וישאר לדור כאן יחידי במקום עכו״ם וילמוד ממעשיו » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק ה).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif states the prohibition without giving its reason; the Rav gives the reason and refers to siman 382 (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א). The Mishnah Berurah, after him, gives a reason in its own words (above).

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, the one who lives in this mavoi does not make an eruv with the neighbouring house through the window between them in order to take out his objects into this mavoi through the house that opens onto the mavoi, because it is forbidden to give assistance to an individual who lives with a non-Jew (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:1); if there is a doorway between them, see point ג.

נקודה ג — אבל אם יש פתח ביניהם: הדעה השנייה שבסעיף המחבר (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א)

Point ג — the doorway between them: the second view in the Mechaber's seif (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:1)

The view the Rav's seif does not report

What the Mechaber writes: « אבל אם פתח ביניהם מותר ויש מי שאוסר אף בפתח פתוח ביניהם » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א).

What the Rav writes: « אֲבָל אִם יֵשׁ פֶּתַח בֵּינֵיהֶם — יָכוֹל לְעָרֵב עִמּוֹ, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ב » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif writes « מותר », then reports « ויש מי שאוסר אף בפתח פתוח ביניהם » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א); the Rav's seif writes « יָכוֹל לְעָרֵב עִמּוֹ » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:א), does not report this view, and refers to siman 382. This page does not say why; consult your Rav.

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, if there is a doorway between them, he may make an eruv with him (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:1); the Mechaber's seif also reports a view that forbids it even then (above); the commentators diverge: Level 2, section 3; consult your Rav.

נקודה ד — בענין שהנכרי אוסר עליהם במבוי, וטעם הדין (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:ב)

Point ד — the case where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on them, and the reason (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:2)

The eruv made through the windows

What the Mechaber writes: « מבוי שצדו א' עכו״ם וצדו א' ישראלי' והיו חלונות פתוחות מחצרו לחצר של ישראל ועירבו כולם דרך חלונות » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:ב), then « הרי אלו אסורי' להשתמש במבוי דרך פתחי' עד שישכרו מן העכו״ם » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:ב).

What the Rav writes: « מָבוֹי שֶׁדָּרִים בּוֹ יִשְׂרְאֵלִים וְנָכְרִי, בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁהַנָּכְרִי אוֹסֵר עֲלֵיהֶם בַּמָּבוֹי » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:ב), then « עַד שֶׁיִּשְׂכְּרוּ מִן הַנָּכְרִי, שֶׁעַל יְדֵי עֵרוּב אֵינָן נַעֲשִׂים כְּיָחִיד בִּמְקוֹם נָכְרִי, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ב » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:ב).

In the commentators: after the Rav, the Mishnah Berurah writes on « עד שישכרו » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:ב): « דאין עירוב מועיל לעשותם כיחיד במקום עכו״ם כדאיתא לעיל סי' שפ״ב ס״ז » (משנה ברורה ש״צ ס״ק י).

The difference: the Rav states that the seif concerns the case « בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁהַנָּכְרִי אוֹסֵר עֲלֵיהֶם בַּמָּבוֹי » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:ב), which the Mechaber's seif does not write; and he gives the reason, « שֶׁעַל יְדֵי עֵרוּב אֵינָן נַעֲשִׂים כְּיָחִיד בִּמְקוֹם נָכְרִי » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:ב), which the Mechaber's seif does not give. The Mishnah Berurah, after him, gives the same reason (above).

Practical consequence: according to the Rav's text, in a mavoi where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on the Jews, those who made an eruv together through the windows between their houses or courtyards may take objects out and bring them in through these windows, but do not use the mavoi through their doorways until they have rented from the non-Jew (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:2).

4 · Lemaaseh

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

The practical Chabad conduct

Points of halachic conduct that flow directly from the Shulchan Aruch HaRav Siman 390, in the Chabad sensibility. The siman has two seifim: each point refers to its seif, in the order of the text.

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman 390

  • ① The single Jew in the mavoi, and the neighbouring house that opens elsewhere: in a mavoi where a non-Jew and a single Jew live — in which it is permitted to move objects in the mavoi (the Rav refers to siman 391) —, if a house next to his does not open onto this mavoi but onto another mavoi or onto the public domain, and a window separates them, the one who lives in this mavoi cannot make an eruv with him through this window in order to take out his objects into this mavoi through the house that opens onto the mavoi; many Jews who live in one courtyard are, with regard to moving objects in the mavoi, like an individual. If there is a doorway between them, he may make an eruv with him; the Mechaber's seif also reports a view that forbids it even then; the commentators diverge: see section 2, point ג, and Level 2, section 3; consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:1.
  • ② The windows between the houses of the Jews: in a mavoi where Jews and a non-Jew live, in a case where the non-Jew imposes a prohibition on them in the mavoi, the Jews who made an eruv together through the windows between their houses or courtyards may take objects out and bring them in through these windows, but they are forbidden to use the mavoi through their doorways until they rent from the non-Jew: « שֶׁעַל יְדֵי עֵרוּב אֵינָן נַעֲשִׂים כְּיָחִיד בִּמְקוֹם נָכְרִי » (שו״ע הרב ש״צ:ב). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 390:2.

⚠ This section presents the Chabad halachic conduct. For any concrete question: consult your Rav.
