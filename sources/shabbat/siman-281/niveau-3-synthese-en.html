1. The central Axiom

Siman רפ"א in one sentence.
Remarkable case where the Mechaber writes a single technical sentence (no bowing at "וְאָנוּ" of Modim deRabbanan) and the Rema writes 5 practical rules in one Hagaha that structure all of Shabbat morning: late arrival, extended zemiros, forgetting "לאל אשר שבת", permitted extension, chatzos limit.

5. Forgetting לָאֵל אֲשֶׁר שָׁבַת — the heart of the siman

Of the five rules of siman רפ"א, four are quiet permissions: one may arrive later, one may extend the zemiros, one must not bow at "וְאָנוּ". Only one requires corrective action — and it is the heart of the siman: what to do when one has omitted the passage לָאֵל אֲשֶׁר שָׁבַת in the berachah of Yotzer Or?

Why this passage is "structural". לָאֵל אֲשֶׁר שָׁבַת is not a liturgical ornament: it is the proper mention of Shabbat inserted in the morning berachah. To omit it is to deprive the berachah of its character of the day. Hence the reflex: one should restart Yotzer Or from the beginning.
↓
The exact rule — one does not repeat the berachah. The Mishnah Berurah (s.k. 3) rules: one continues and completes the berachah as is. The forgotten passage is made up after the Amidah, by reciting it alone, without a berachah. Repeating Yotzer Or would entail a repeated berachah — a בְּרָכָה לְבַטָּלָה — worse than the omission.
The borderline case to understand. The pitfall: equating this omission with that of an essential word of a berachah (where one repeats). The difference: לָאֵל אֲשֶׁר שָׁבַת is an addition of Shabbat, not the obligatory framework of the berachah. The berachah of Yotzer Or, valid on weekdays, remains valid without it; only the coloring of the day is missing, which is supplied separately. The golden rule: one corrects the omission by the least means — never by creating a superfluous berachah.
In practice. Omission noticed mid-Yotzer Or → do not stop, complete the berachah, then say the passage after the Amidah without berachah. And keep the framework of the siman: only 4 bows (not at "וְאָנוּ"), late arrival and extended zemiros permitted (do not rebuke long singers — בִּטּוּל תּוֹרָה), seudas shacharis before חֲצוֹת. For the SA HaRav and Chabad practice — tendency to shorten the piyutim; see Level 4.

6. Mnemonic — A.Z.O.

A — Arrival later on Shabbat morning (legitimate, Rema).

Z — Zemiros extended permitted (no rebuke).

O — Omission of "לאל אשר שבת" = only case of repetition. Eat before chatzos.

7. The 4 pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — Bowing at "וְאָנוּ" out of enthusiasm. Error. Chazal established only 4 places.
Pitfall 2 — Repeating Yotzer Or because of forgetting "לאל אשר שבת". Error (MB s.k. 3). Say the passage after the Amidah without berachah.
Pitfall 3 — Eating after chatzos on Shabbat morning. To avoid. Better to shorten the tefilah.
Pitfall 4 — Censuring the long singers. The Or Zarua explicitly forbids reproaching them (bitul Torah).

10. The 5 practical imperatives of Siman רפ"א

🕊️ The rules of Shabbat morning — in 5 imperatives

  1. Only 4 bows in the Amidah — not at "וְאָנוּ" of Modim deRabbanan.
  2. Late arrival permitted on Shabbat morning (Rema). But not to exceed zman Shema.
  3. Extended zemiros = permitted. No rebuke for long singers.
  4. Forgetting "לאל אשר שבת": say after the Amidah without berachah.
  5. Eat the seudas shacharis before chatzos midday. Limits the extension.
