Personal preparations for entering Shabbat — washing, nails, 3 questions

כמה דיני הכנסת שבת
🌱 Introductory Level · מתחילים
✦ ❖ ✦

The first siman on personal preparations for entering Shabbat — the positive dimension. Mitzvah to wash the body in hot water, to wash the hair, to cut the nails according to a traditional order. Close to twilight, the head of the household asks his family in a gentle voice the 3 questions (maaser, eruv, challah) and gives the order to light the candles.

Topic: Personal preparations before Shabbat
Source: Shulchan Aruch Orach Chaim siman ר״ס · 2 seifim · Shabbat 25b – 34a.

Compilation: Rav Yossef Haim Samama

1. The text of the Shulchan Aruch

Seif Alef — Washing, hair, and nails

דִּינֵי הַכְנָסַת שַׁבָּת. וּבוֹ ב' סְעִיפִים: מִצְוָה לִרְחוֹץ הגה: כָּל גּוּפוֹ, וְאִם אִי אֶפְשָׁר לוֹ — יִרְחַץ (טור) פָּנָיו, יָדָיו וְרַגְלָיו בְּחַמִּין בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת. וּמִצְוָה לָחוֹף הָרֹאשׁ וּלְגַלֵּחַ הַצִּפָּרְנַיִם בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת. הגה: וְאִם הָיוּ שַׂעֲרוֹת רֹאשׁוֹ גְּדוֹלוֹת — מִצְוָה לְגַלְּחָן (א"ז פ' אלו מגלחין). וּכְשֶׁנּוֹטֵל צִפָּרְנָיו לֹא יִטּוֹל אוֹתָן כְּסִדְרָן, וְיַתְחִיל בִּשְׂמֹאל בִּקְמִיצָה וּבְיָמִין בָּאֶצְבַּע, וְסִימָן לְזֶה: דבהג"א בִּשְׂמֹאל וּבדאג"ה בְּיָמִין (אבודרהם בספר המוסר).
The laws of ushering in Shabbos — 2 se'ifim. Mitzvah to wash (Rema's gloss: the entire body; if not possible — at least:) face, hands, and feet in hot water on erev Shabbat. Mitzvah to wash the hair (לחוף הראש) and to cut the nails (לגלח הצפרנים) on erev Shabbat.

Rema gloss (continued):
  • If the hair is long — mitzvah to cut it
  • When cutting the nails — do not follow the natural order (= do not go finger by finger in anatomical sequence)
  • Begin with the ring finger (קמיצה) of the left hand, then the index (אצבע) of the right hand
  • Mnemonic: דבהג״א for the left hand, בדאג״ה for the right hand
Seif Alef sets out 4 positive mitzvot:
  1. רחיצה (washing) — face, hands, feet in hot water (Mechaber); the entire body (Rema)
  2. חפיפה (washing the hair)
  3. גילוח צפרניים (cutting the nails) — according to the traditional non-sequential order
  4. גילוח שערות (cutting long hair if necessary — Rema)

Seif Beit — The 3 questions and the order to light

כְּשֶׁיִּהְיֶה סָמוּךְ לַחֲשֵׁיכָה — יִשְׁאַל לְאַנְשֵׁי בֵיתוֹ בְּלָשׁוֹן רַכָּה :
"עִשַּׂרְתֶּם ? עֵרַבְתֶּם ? הִפְרַשְׁתֶּם חַלָּה ?"
וְיֹאמַר לָהֶם : "הַדְלִיקוּ אֶת הַנֵּר". וּבְמָקוֹם שֶׁאֵין מְעַשְּׂרִין — אֵין צָרִיךְ לוֹמַר עִשַּׂרְתֶּם (טור).
Near twilight, the head of the household asks the members of the house in a gentle voice (לשון רכה):
  1. "Have you taken maaser?" (עִשַּׂרְתֶּם) — for fruits and vegetables in Eretz Yisrael
  2. "Have you made the eruv?" (עֵרַבְתֶּם) — eruv chatzeirot (shared courtyards) or eruv tavshilin (Yom Tov + Shabbat)
  3. "Have you separated challah?" (הִפְרַשְׁתֶּם חַלָּה) — for dough/bread prepared at home
And he tells them: "Light the candles" (הַדְלִיקוּ אֶת הַנֵּר).

Rema gloss / Tur: in places where maaser is not taken (= outside Eretz Yisrael), there is no need to ask. And in a place where tithes are not separated — there is no need to ask "have you tithed" [Tur].
Seif Beit codifies 4 structured verbal acts:
  1. Gentle voice (לשון רכה) — mode of communication so it will be accepted
  2. 3 questions in a precise order
  3. 1 final command (lighting)
  4. Local adaptation (according to region)

2. General context

What is this siman about?

Siman ר״ס opens the "positive" section of Hilchot Shabbat. Until now (sim. 252-259), the prohibitions and their technical frameworks were codified. Now we enter the positive mitzvah: how to honor Shabbat through personal preparations.

AspectPracticeSource
BodyWashing, cutting hair and nailsShabbat 25b · "כך הוא מנהגו של ר' יהודה ב"ר אילעאי"
Family / home3 questions at twilightShabbat 34a · "שלושה דברים צריך אדם לומר"
CandlesExplicit order to lightIbid. 34a
The founding talmudic sugya — Shabbat 25b: "כך היה מנהגו של ר' יהודה ב"ר אילעאי" — the custom of R' Yehuda son of R' Ilai: to wash his face, hands, and feet in hot water on Friday, and to receive Shabbat. Shabbat 34a: "שלושה דברים צריך אדם לומר בתוך ביתו ערב שבת עם חשיכה" — three things one must say at home near twilight.

3. Key concept 1 — Mitzvah of רחיצה

רְחִיצָה = "washing". The siman codifies a positive mitzvah to wash on erev Shabbat. Hierarchy:
  • Ideal (Rema, Tur): entire body in hot water (= mikvah or hot bath)
  • Minimum (Mechaber): face, hands, and feet in hot water
  • If not possible: do what one can
Hot water is essential — this is the chiddush of the seif. Not just washing, but in hot water (בחמין). Symbolically: the warmth represents dignity, the personal attention given to honor Shabbat.

4. Key concept 2 — The order of the nails

The Rema codifies a traditional non-sequential order for cutting nails: "לא יטול אותן כסדרן" (do not take them in order). Mnemonic: דבהג״א for the left hand, בדאג״ה for the right hand.
PositionLeft hand: דבהג״אRight hand: בדאג״ה
1stד = ring finger (קמיצה)ב = index (אצבע)
2ndב = index (אצבע)ד = ring finger (קמיצה)
3rdה = pinky (זרת)א = thumb (גודל)
4thג = middle (אמה)ג = middle (אמה)
5thא = thumb (גודל)ה = pinky (זרת)
Why this order? The commentators (Magen Avraham, Mishnah Berurah) explain: there is a kabbalistic belief that cutting nails in sequential order brings misfortune (סכנה). So we alternate. The prescribed order avoids both a purely sequential order and total disorder.

5. Key concept 3 — The 3 questions at twilight

Near twilight, the head of the household recalls 3 critical mitzvot that can still be done before Shabbat (and not after). All connected to food (maaser, eruv, challah):
QuestionMeaningConcerns
עִשַּׂרְתֶּם?Have you taken maaser?Fruits / vegetables (Eretz Yisrael only)
עֵרַבְתֶּם?Have you made the eruv?Eruv chatzeirot (shared courtyards) or Yom Tov
הִפְרַשְׁתֶּם חַלָּה?Have you separated challah?Dough / bread prepared at home
The art of "לשון רכה" (gentle voice): this is not a strict command, it is a question. The Mechaber emphasizes this mode because:
  • The non-authoritarian aspect favors listening
  • Questions invite awareness rather than obedience
  • "לשון רכה" is consistent with shalom bayit (peace in the home)
+ The seif ends with a brief command: "הדליקו את הנר" (light the candles). It is the only direct order.

6. Detail of the 2 seifim

Seif Alef — 4 personal mitzvot

Recap:
  1. Washing — face / hands / feet in hot water (Mechaber). Entire body (Rema, ideal). If impossible — do what one can.
  2. Hair — wash and cut if long (Rema).
  3. Nails — cut according to alternating order (mnemonic דבהג״א / בדאג״ה).

7. The Rema's position

SubjectMechaberRema
WashingFace, hands, feet in hot waterIdeal: entire body; if not possible, like Mechaber
Long hairNot explicitMitzvah to cut if long
Order of nailsNot explicitMnemonic דבהג״א / בדאג״ה (Avudraham)
Diaspora and maaser—No need to ask עשרתם (Tur)
The Rema's major chiddush: integration of the Avudraham and the Sefer haMussar on the kabbalistic order of nails. This is one of the rare passages of the Shulchan Aruch where a kabbalistic practice is halachically codified.

8. Modern practical cases

Case 1 — Mikvah before Shabbat

Case: going to the men's mikvah Friday afternoon.
Conduct:
  • Chabad and chassidic practice: strong minhag (= "מקווה דערב שבת")
  • Fulfills the Rema's ideal: "כל גופו" in hot water
  • Symbolic: passing from the mundane to the sacred through water (similar to a niddah → sanctification)
  • If no mikvah is accessible — hot bath or hot shower covering the entire body

Case 2 — Shower / bath at minimum

Case: no mikvah but a hot shower is available.
Conduct:
  • Cover the entire body (Rema) — prolonged hot shower
  • At minimum (Mechaber): face, hands, and feet in hot water
  • Hot water essential — not a simple rinse

Case 3 — Cutting nails according to the order

Case: cutting nails Friday afternoon.
Conduct:
  • Left hand mnemonic: D-B-H-G-A (ring → index → pinky → middle → thumb)
  • Right hand mnemonic: B-D-A-G-H (index → ring → thumb → middle → pinky)
  • Do not cut them on Shabbat itself (forbidden מדאורייתא)
  • Preferably erev Shabbat / Friday afternoon

Case 4 — Doing challah / the 3 questions

Case: before Shabbat, the head of household reminds his family.
Conduct:
  • Challah: if bread was made at home this week — verify it was done. If not, still possible before Shabbat.
  • Eruv: if sharing a courtyard with non-Jews or doing Yom Tov-Shabbat (eruv tavshilin) — verify.
  • Maaser: for fruits/vegetables from Eretz Yisrael only.
  • Gentle voice — the tone matters as much as the content

Case 5 — Shabbat clothing

Case: changing clothes before Shabbat.
Conduct:
  • Although explicitly codified at sim. רס״ב, the idea is implicit here (personal preparations)
  • Clothes distinct from weekday wear — honor of Shabbat (כבוד שבת)
  • Chabad tradition: "kapota" / "bekishe" (long white/black coat) according to custom

9. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The 5 teachings of Siman ר״ס:
  1. Mitzvah of רחיצה in hot water — entire body ideal, otherwise face/hands/feet
  2. Hair and nails cut on erev Shabbat — not on Shabbat itself
  3. Kabbalistic order for the nails: left hand דבהג״א, right hand בדאג״ה
  4. 3 questions at twilight (maaser, eruv, challah) in a gentle voice
  5. Command to light the candles — the final ritual transition

The 5 practical commands of Siman ר״ס

  1. Wash in hot water before Shabbat — entire body (ideal: mikvah) or minimum face/hands/feet
  2. Cut hair and nails on erev Shabbat — not on Shabbat (Toraitic prohibition)
  3. Follow the traditional order of nails: דבהג״א for the left, בדאג״ה for the right
  4. Remind family of the 3 questions in a gentle voice — maaser, eruv, challah
  5. Command the lighting of the candles — moment of ritual transition
