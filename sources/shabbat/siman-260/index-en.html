Siman 260 — Laws of welcoming Shabbos (mikveh, garments, nails)

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe across 5 se'ifim that codify the personal preparations for entering Shabbos: washing the entire body in hot water, cutting one's nails, and putting on special Shabbos garments.
