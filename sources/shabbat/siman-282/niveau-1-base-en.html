1. The text of the Shulchan Aruch

Siman רפ"ב contains 7 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo) which codify the halachos relating to krias haTorah and maftir on Shabbos.

Seif 1 — 7 aliyos on Shabbos, additions permitted

קריאת התורה והמפטיר בשבת. ובו ז סעיפים:
מוֹצִיאִין סֵפֶר תּוֹרָה וְקוֹרִים בּוֹ שִׁבְעָה. וְאִם רָצָה לְהוֹסִיף — מוֹסִיף. הגה : והוא הדין ביו"ט מותר להוסיף על הקרואים (רמב"ם, מהר"ם, ב"י). ויש אומרים דביו"ט אין להוסיף (ר"ן) — וכן נהגו במדינות אלו, מלבד בשמחת תורה שמוסיפין הרבה. גם בכל יו"ט אין המפטירין ממנין הקרואים.
Translation: On Shabbos morning, we take out the sefer Torah and call up 7 aliyos (those called up to read). If one wishes, one may add beyond the 7. Hagaha (Rema): also permitted on Yom Tov, but the opinion of the Ran (followed in Ashkenaz): no additions except on Simchas Torah. And on Yom Tov, the maftir does not count in the number of aliyos.

Seif 2 — Multiple olim may read the same verses

מֻתָּר לִקְרוֹת עוֹלִים הַרְבֵּה אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁקָּרָא זֶה מַה שֶּׁקָּרָא זֶה — וְחוֹזֵר וּמְבָרֵךְ. אֵין בְּכָךְ כְּלוּם. הגה : ויש אוסרים (מרדכי) — וכן נהגו במדינות אלו, חוץ משמחת תורה.
Translation: permitted (Mechaber) to call up multiple olim on the same verses — each recites the brachos anew. No issue of "bracha levatala" (since we re-read for their honor). Hagaha (Rema, Ashkenaz): some forbid — Ashkenazi minhag: do not repeat the same verses. Exception: Simchas Torah.

Seif 3 — Who can be an oleh: women, minors, etc.

הַכֹּל עוֹלִים לְמִנְיַן שִׁבְעָה — אֲפִילוּ אִשָּׁה וְקָטָן שֶׁיּוֹדֵעַ לְמִי מְבָרְכִין. אֲבָל אָמְרוּ חֲכָמִים: אִשָּׁה לֹא תִּקְרָא בְּצִיבּוּר מִפְּנֵי כְּבוֹד הַצִּיבּוּר. הגה : ואלו דוקא מצטרפים למנין הקרואים — אבל לא שיהיו כולם נשים או קטנים. ודין עבד כנעני כדין אשה. אבל אם אמו מישראל — מותר לעלות. ואסור לקרות בראש מגולה. ואין איסור לקרות עם הארץ נכבד ועשיר וגדול הדור לפני תלמיד חכם — כי אין זה בזיון לת"ח, רק כבוד לתורה. וממזר מותר לעלות לס"ת.
Translation: all can be aliyos — including women and minors (who understand to whom the bracha is addressed). HOWEVER, Chazal said: a woman does not read in public — מפני כבוד הציבור (for the honor of the tzibbur). Hagaha (Rema): women/minors may complete the 7, but not all be women/minors. Eved kena'ani = like a woman. If the mother is Jewish — may go up. No bare head. Possible to call up a respected wealthy am ha'aretz before a talmid chacham — this is kavod haTorah. A mamzer may go up.

Seif 4 — Minhag: 7 olim + separate maftir

נוֹהֲגִים לִקְרוֹת שִׁבְעָה — לִגְמוֹר עִמָּהֶם הַפָּרָשָׁה — וְאוֹמֵר קַדִּישׁ. וְחוֹזֵר וְקוֹרֵא עִם הַמַּפְטִיר מַה שֶּׁקָּרָא הַשְּׁבִיעִי. הגה : וכן נוהגים בימים טובים שאין מפטיר ממנין הקרואים. אבל בחול שאסור להוסיף — השלישי הוא מפטיר. וביום שמוציאין ב' ספרים או ג' — המפטיר קורא באחרונה. וקטן יכול לקרות בפרשת המוספין או בד' פרשיות שמוסיפין באדר.
Translation: minhag — 7 olim complete the parsha, then Kaddish, then the maftir rereads what the 7th read. So 8 olim total on Shabbos. Hagaha (Rema): same on Yom Tov (maftir not counted in the number of aliyos). But during the week (Monday/Thursday) — no additions, so the 3rd is the maftir. If 2 or 3 sifrei Torah are taken out: maftir reads in the last. A minor may be maftir of the musaf reading or of the 4 parshiyos of Adar.

Seif 5 — Maftir = one of the olim already called up

אִם לֹא נִמְצָא מִי שֶׁיּוֹדֵעַ לְהַפְטִיר אֶלָּא אֶחָד מֵאוֹתָם שֶׁעָלוּ לִקְרוֹת בַּתּוֹרָה — וּכְבָר אָמַר שְׁלִיחַ צִבּוּר קַדִּישׁ אַחַר קְרִיאַת הַפָּרָשָׁה — זֶה שֶׁרוֹצֶה לְהַפְטִיר צָרִיךְ לַחֲזוֹר וְלִקְרוֹת, וִיבָרֵךְ עַל קְרִיאָתוֹ תְּחִילָּה וְסוֹף. הגה : אבל אם לא אמר קדיש — יפטיר מי שעלה לשביעי, אם יודע. ואם יש אחרים שיודעים להפטיר — לא יפטיר מי שעלה כבר.
Translation: only one who already went up knows how to do maftir — and the chazzan has already said Kaddish after the parsha. Solution: the one who wants to do maftir must re-read the portion + recite the brachos before and after. Hagaha: if Kaddish was not yet said — the 7th himself may do the maftir (if he knows). If another reader is available — that is preferable.

Seif 6 — Chazzan's error: Kaddish too early

אִם טָעָה שְׁלִיחַ צִבּוּר וְסִיֵּם הַפָּרָשָׁה עִם הַשִּׁשִּׁי וְאָמַר קַדִּישׁ — אֵין צָרִיךְ לִקְרוֹת עוֹד אַחֵר. אֶלָּא יִקְרָא עִם הַמַּפְטִיר מַה שֶּׁקָּרָא עִם הַשִּׁשִּׁי. דְּקַיְמָא לָן: מַפְטִיר עוֹלֶה לְמִנְיַן שִׁבְעָה.
Translation: if the chazzan finished the parsha with the 6th and said Kaddish too early — no need to call a separate 7th. The maftir reads what the 6th read, and he counts as the 7th. Source: מפטיר עולה למנין שבעה (= the maftir is included in the count of 7).

Seif 7 — Verses skipped in the reading

קָרָא הַפָּרָשָׁה בִּתְפִלַּת שַׁחֲרִית בְּשַׁבָּת וְדִילֵּג פָּסוּק אֶחָד — חוֹזֵר וְקוֹרֵא הוּא וּשְׁנַיִם עִמּוֹ. אֲפִילוּ הִפְטִיר וְהִתְפַּלֵּל מוּסָף — חוֹזֵר וְקוֹרֵא.
Translation: if it is discovered afterwards that a verse was skipped in the reading — we call up 3 people (the reader + 2) to re-read the section containing that verse. Even if the haftarah was already read and musaf davened — we go back and read! Reason: the mitzva of krias haTorah is essential, not an ornament.
Overview of the 7 seifim: the siman codifies the complete structure of krias haTorah on Shabbos:
• Seifim 1-2: number of aliyos (7 minimum, additions permitted Mechaber / contested Rema)
• Seif 3: qualifications of olim (women/minors/etc.)
• Seifim 4-6: maftir/haftarah structure (timing of Kaddish, maftir = 7th or additional)
• Seif 7: remedy for a skipped verse — even belatedly

2. General background

Krias haTorah on Shabbos morning

The public reading of the Torah is an institution attributed to Moshe Rabbeinu (Megilla 32a). On Shabbos morning, we call up at least 7 aliyos — the highest of any day of the year. Monday and Thursday: 3 aliyos. Rosh Chodesh and Chol HaMoed: 4. Yom Tov: 5. Yom Kippur: 6. Shabbos: 7. The greater the kedusha, the more aliyos we add.

The fundamental question: how is the public reading of Shabbos structured? Who can go up (oleh)? How does the maftir relate to the regular aliyos? What is done in case of errors (skipped verses, Kaddish too early)?

Place in Hilchos Shabbos

Siman רפ"ב follows 281 (zemiros/Modim) and precedes 283 (short reading) + 284 (haftarah). It is the central siman of the Shabbos morning liturgy.

3. Key halachic concepts

Concept 1 — שִׁבְעָה עוֹלִים (7 aliyos): Shabbos is the only day with 7 aliyos. Source: Mishnah Megilla 4:2. Different days = different numbers of aliyos (correlated to the kedusha of the day).
Concept 2 — מַפְטִיר (Maftir): "the one who concludes" — the 8th person called up on Shabbos. Reads the final verses of the parsha (often the same as the 7th), then reads the haftarah (Nevi'im). Historical origin: in times of persecution, reading the prophets replaced the Torah.
Concept 3 — מַפְטִיר עוֹלֶה לְמִנְיַן שִׁבְעָה: "the maftir counts in the 7" — if the chazzan accidentally finished the parsha with the 6th and said Kaddish, the maftir fills in as the 7th (seif 6). But in normal minhag, we call up 7 aliyos plus a maftir (= 8 olim).
Concept 4 — כְּבוֹד הַצִּיבּוּר (Honor of the tzibbur): the reason a woman does not read publicly — even though she is halachically qualified (s.3). A "sociological" concept that codifies the minhag.
Concept 5 — חוֹזֵר וְקוֹרֵא (We go back and read): if a verse is skipped — even after haftarah, Musaf — we go back and read the parsha with at least 3 people (s.7). Emphasizes the primacy of the mitzva of krias haTorah.

6. The position of the Rema — 4 major הגהות

  1. Hagaha s.1: Yom Tov — no additions beyond the number (Ashkenaz). Exception: Simchas Torah. Maftir not counted.
  2. Hagaha s.2: In Ashkenaz, we do not call up multiple olim on the same verses. Exception: Simchas Torah.
  3. Hagaha s.3: Women/minors are only completing, never "all the olim." Eved kena'ani = like a woman. If the mother is Jewish, OK. No bare head. Mamzer permitted.
  4. Hagaha s.4: Standard minhag on YT (maftir not counted); during the week, the 3rd is maftir. If 2 or 3 sifrei Torah: maftir in the last.

7. Modern practical cases

SituationApplication
Ashkenazi shul — who is called up?7 aliyos + 1 maftir = 8 total. The maftir also reads the haftarah.
Sephardi shul — may more than 7 be called up?Mechaber: yes. Sephardi minhag: generally 7 except for special occasions (bar mitzvah, Simchas Torah).
Liberal shul calling up womenHalachically: Chazal said "no" for כבוד הציבור (s.3). Orthodox do not permit.
Bar mitzvah — the bachur does the reading and maftirPermitted (a minor may do maftir). The bachur often reads everything + maftir.
Skipped verse discovered after MusafS.7 — re-read the parsha with 3 olim. Even belatedly.
Kaddish said after the 6th by mistakeS.6 — maftir = 7th (reads the missing verses).
Oleh who cannot read (am ha'aretz)The ba'al kore reads for him — the oleh holds and recites brachos. Standard modern practice.

8. Practical synthesis of the siman

The 7 rules to remember from Siman רפ"ב:
  1. 7 aliyos on Shabbos minimum. Mechaber allows additions; Rema (Ashkenaz) restricts.
  2. Do not repeat the same verses (Ashkenaz) except on Simchas Torah.
  3. Women halachically eligible but Chazal: "כבוד הציבור" — not in practice.
  4. Shabbos minhag: 7 aliyos + Kaddish + maftir (= 8 olim).
  5. Maftir = already an oleh: must re-read + brachos if Kaddish already said.
  6. Chazzan's error: maftir fills in (משלים) if Kaddish too early.
  7. Skipped verse: re-read parsha even after Musaf.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. How many aliyos on Shabbos? Why this number?
  2. What is the dispute Mechaber/Rema about additions on Yom Tov?
  3. Halachically, may a woman be an oleh? Why not in practice?
  4. What is maftir? Does it count in the 7?
  5. What is done if the chazzan said Kaddish too early (after the 6th)?
  6. What is done if a verse was skipped — discovered after Musaf?
  7. Why may a minor be a maftir?
