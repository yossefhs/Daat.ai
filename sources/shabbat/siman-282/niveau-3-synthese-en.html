1. The central axiom

Siman רפ"ב in one sentence.
Codifies the public reading of the Torah on Shabbos morning — Mosaic institution (Megilla 32a). At least 7 aliyos (the highest number of the year), with or without additions (machlokes Mechaber/Rema). Qualifications, maftir-haftarah structure, handling of errors (Kaddish too early, skipped verses).

5. The maftir and the count of seven — the heart of the siman

Siman רפ"ב fixes seven aliyos for Shabbos morning — the largest number of the year. But the point that truly structures the siman, and is the most delicate to handle, is the dual status of the maftir: eighth oleh in addition to the seven, or one of the seven? The answer is not unique — it depends on the situation.

Normal situation — the maftir is "in addition." Seven olim read the parsha, the chazzan says half-Kaddish, then the maftir goes up as the eighth: he re-reads a few verses from the end and reads the haftarah. The maftir does not count in the seven — he is added to them.
↓
Error situation — מַפְטִיר עוֹלֶה לְמִנְיַן שִׁבְעָה. If the chazzan said Kaddish too early, after only the sixth oleh, we do not bring up the sefer again for a seventh: the maftir himself then counts as the seventh oleh. The same person fills the gap. The sefer does not need to be moved again (s.6).
The borderline case to understand. How can the same maftir sometimes "not count" and sometimes "count" in the seven? Because the count of seven is a quantity requirement of public reading, and the Kaddish is only a divider. When seven have read, the maftir is a supererogatory eighth; when only six have read and the Kaddish has already separated them — the maftir, who also reads in the Torah, is admitted to complete the quorum of seven. Another remedy in the same spirit: a verse omitted and discovered after Musaf is not "lost" — we take out the sefer again and re-read the section with three olim (s.7). The lesson: the structure of seven is rigid in requirement, flexible in repair.
In practice. Shabbos morning: seven olim + half-Kaddish + maftir = eight olim. On additions beyond seven: the Mechaber allows them (and allows re-reading the same verses); the Rema restricts them, allowing them only on Simchas Torah. Women and minors: halachically eligible, but minhag excludes them due to כְּבוֹד הַצִּיבּוּר. For the SA HaRav and Chabad practice: like the Rema, with its minhagim — see Level 4.

6. Mnemonic — 7.M.E.

7 — 7 aliyos Shabbos morning (the most of the year).

M — Maftir = 8th oleh, reads haftarah.

E — Errors remedied: maftir fills in if Kaddish too early; re-read if a verse was skipped.

7. The 4 pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — Adding olim on Yom Tov in Ashkenaz. Forbidden (Rema). Except Simchas Torah.
Pitfall 2 — Thinking that a woman may go up in an Orthodox shul. Halachically eligible but כבוד הציבור excludes her.
Pitfall 3 — Ignoring the chazzan's error (Kaddish after the 6th). Solution: maftir fills in — s.6.
Pitfall 4 — Skipped verse = ignore if Musaf is already finished. Error. Re-read parsha with 3 olim (s.7).

10. The 7 practical commandments of Siman רפ"ב

📖 The rule of the Shabbos reading — in 7 commandments

  1. 7 aliyos minimum on Shabbos morning. The highest number of the year.
  2. Additions: Mechaber permits; Ashkenaz restricts to Simchas Torah.
  3. Women/minors: halachically eligible; minhag restricts (כבוד הציבור).
  4. Shabbos structure: 7 olim + Kaddish + maftir = 8 olim.
  5. Maftir reads the haftarah (Nevi'im). May be a minor.
  6. Kaddish too early error: maftir fills in as the 7th (מפטיר עולה למנין).
  7. Skipped verse: re-read parsha with 3 olim — even after Musaf.

→ For the haftarah itself: see siman 284.
