1. The central axiom

Siman שס״ט in one sentence.
One who gives money for his share in the courtyard eruv acquires it, or does not, according to the way he went about it, among other things: what he said (זכה לי or ערב לי), what he handed over (a coin or an object), the person he handed it to (the baker or a private householder), and what the baker then did (having it acquired for him through another, buying him bread at the market with the coin) — seif א. The coin alone, given to the baker with “acquire for me”, gives him no share: « זכה לי בעירוב שיהא לי חלק עמהם בשביל מעה זו אינו עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א). The reason: money does not acquire before meshichah, as the Mishnah Berurah writes on the object, « דדוקא מעות אינן קונות עד שימשוך החפץ אבל אם מקנה לו כלי הוי חליפין » (משנה ברורה שס״ט ס״ק ד); when the baker counted him among all without naming him, the reason it gives is first that, by default, he did not mean to give it to him as a gift, and then that money does not acquire (ס״ק ב).

3. The ways of acquiring — in the order of the seif

The coin to the baker, with “acquire for me” → « זכה לי בעירוב שיהא לי חלק עמהם בשביל מעה זו אינו עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א), and this even if the baker made the eruv for everyone and « וזכה גם לזה עמהם » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א): according to the Mishnah Berurah, he was counted among everyone without being named, and, by default, the baker did not mean to give it to him as a gift (ס״ק ב).
↓
“Make the eruv for me” → « אבל אם אמר לו ערב לי קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א): he made the baker his agent (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג).
↓
“Acquire for me”, with an object → « ואפילו אם אמר זכה לי אם נתן לו כלי בתור׳ קנין שיקנה לו בו קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א): the object makes an exchange, chalifin (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד).
↓
Through another, or at the market → « וכן אם זיכה לו הנחתום ע״י אחר או שקנה במעה שנתן לו לחם מן השוק קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א). The Mishnah Berurah reads the acquisition through another as the case where the baker named him (ס״ק ה), and reports that some Acharonim disagree (ס״ק ו); at the market, the baker became like his agent (ס״ק ז).
↓
The coin to a private householder → « ואם נתן מעה לבעל הבי׳ ואמר לו זכה לי בעירוב קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א): a householder is not in the habit of selling what is his (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ח).

4. Decision tree — has the one who pays for his share acquired the eruv?

Q0: Was the coin given to the baker? If it was given to a private householder, go straight to the end of the seif, at the bottom of the tree: Q1 to Q3 concern only the baker, in the order of the seif.
↓ to the baker
Starting point (beginning of the seif): a coin given to the baker with “acquire for me” does not make the eruv, even if he was counted among everyone (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב). The seif then names what does.
↓
Q1: Did he tell the baker “make the eruv for me”?
↓ YES → he has acquired the eruv (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג); NO ↓
Q2: Having said “acquire for me”, did he hand over an object as a kinyan?
↓ YES → he has acquired the eruv (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד); NO ↓
Q3: Did the baker have it acquired for him through another, naming him (this is how the Mishnah Berurah reads the seif, ס״ק ה), or buy him bread at the market with the coin?
↓ YES → he has acquired the eruv (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה and ז; on the acquisition through another, some Acharonim disagree, ס״ק ו); NO → the rule at the start of the seif: it is not an eruv (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב); if the baker had it acquired for him through another without naming him, the Biur Halachah leaves the question open (Pitfall 4), and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav makes this case depend on the baker's intention (section 5) — consult your Rav
End of the seif (if, at Q0, the coin was given to a private householder): with “acquire for me”, he has acquired the eruv (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ח). → The Mishnah Berurah reports a disagreement on the acquisition through another (ס״ק ו); the Taz does not read the start of the seif as it does (ס״ק א); and the Biur Halachah leaves in question the boundary between the start of the seif and the clause « וכן אם זיכה לו הנחתום ע״י אחר » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א), « וע״כ צ״ע לדינא » (ביאור הלכה שס״ט): consult your Rav.

5. The Mechaber and the Alter Rebbe — where their texts differ

The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe also has a single seif for this siman. Level 4 gives its full text and the comparison; here are points where his text differs from the Mechaber's seif. These points are not unique to the Rav for all that: most of these reasons can already be read in the Tur, in Rashi, and in the Magen Avraham and the Taz, which Level 4 quotes at each point. The rows follow the order of the Mechaber's seif; in the Rav's text, the parenthesis quoted in the row “The baker, through another” belongs to his first case and comes before “make the eruv for me”.

6. Mnemonic עכב

Three terms of the seif, in the order in which it introduces them, form עכב — three of the ways by which one who has paid acquires the eruv:

ע — ערב לי: he made the baker his agent (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג).

כ — כלי: with “acquire for me”, the object handed over as a kinyan acquires (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד).

ב — בעל הבי׳: the coin given to a private householder, saying “acquire for me [a share] in the eruv”, acquires (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ח).

Between כ and ב, the seif also names the acquisition through another and the bread bought at the market. And the coin alone, given to the baker with “acquire for me”, does not acquire (seif א). For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that having paid is enough. With the coin alone given to the baker and “acquire for me”, it is not an eruv (seif א). The reason: « שֶׁמָּעוֹת אֵינָן קוֹנוֹת עַד שֶׁיִּמְשׁוֹךְ » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א); the Mishnah Berurah writes it too, « דדוקא מעות אינן קונות עד שימשוך החפץ אבל אם מקנה לו כלי הוי חליפין » (משנה ברורה שס״ט ס״ק ד). The case where the baker counted him among all is Pitfall 2.
Pitfall 2: Thinking that being counted among everyone is always enough. In the case of the seif — the coin given to the baker with “acquire for me” — even if the baker made the eruv for everyone and « וזכה גם לזה עמהם » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א), it is not an eruv for him (seif א). According to the Mishnah Berurah, he was included without being named, and, by default, the baker did not mean to give it to him as a gift but to act for his coin, and money does not acquire (ס״ק ב). The reverse is also wrong: with “make the eruv for me” (ס״ק ג) and with a private householder (ס״ק ח), the Mishnah Berurah writes that inclusion among everyone suffices. On the scope of this first case, see Pitfall 4.
Pitfall 3: Treating “acquire for me” and “make the eruv for me” as equivalent. The seif distinguishes them: « אבל אם אמר לו ערב לי קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א); these words make the baker an agent (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג).
Pitfall 4: Taking as settled the boundary between the start of the seif, where the eruv made for everyone does not help him, and the clause at the end, « וכן אם זיכה לו הנחתום ע״י אחר » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א), where he has acquired. The Mishnah Berurah distinguishes the start from the end by the name: at the end, « ר״ל שפרט שמו בהדיא ולא בסתמא עם אחרים » (משנה ברורה שס״ט ס״ק ה); it also reports « ויש מאחרונים שחולקין ע״ז » (משנה ברורה שס״ט ס״ק ו). The Taz reads the start differently: there the baker transfers by himself, « פי׳ הוא עצמו מזכה להם ולו אבל לא ע״י אחר » (ט״ז או״ח שס״ט ס״ק א). The Biur Halachah writes that, for the halachah, the Beis Yosef and the Taz hold that an acquisition made through another, even without naming him, is valid, but that they read it into the Tur, which cannot be read so; and he concludes « וע״כ צ״ע לדינא » (ביאור הלכה שס״ט). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav makes the first case depend on the baker's intention (369:1, section 5). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 5: Extending the baker's rule to the private householder. For the householder the seif rules the other way: « ואם נתן מעה לבעל הבי׳ ואמר לו זכה לי בעירוב קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א); the Mishnah Berurah gives the reason (ס״ק ח).
Pitfall 6: Attributing to the Mechaber's seif the reasons for its rules. The seif states the rules; the reasons reported on this page come from the commentators — among them Rashi, Tosafos, the Rosh, the Tur, the Magen Avraham, the Taz, the Mishnah Berurah — and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes several of these reasons into the body of the law (369:1, see section 5).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שס״ט

For daily conduct

  1. The coin given to the baker with “acquire for me” does not make the eruv, even if he made the eruv for everyone and included him (seif א); the scope of this case is disputed (Taz, ס״ק א; Biur Halachah).
  2. “Make the eruv for me” makes the eruv (seif א).
  3. “Acquire for me” with an object handed over as a kinyan makes the eruv (seif א).
  4. If the baker had it acquired for him through another (naming him, according to the Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה), or bought him bread at the market with the coin, he has acquired the eruv (seif א); on the first way, the Mishnah Berurah reports a disagreement (ס״ק ו).
  5. The coin given to a private householder with “acquire for me” makes the eruv (seif א).
  6. The Shulchan Aruch HaRav makes the first case depend on the baker's intention: in parentheses, he writes that if the baker had in mind to make the zocheh this man's agent too, he has acquired the eruv (369:1); Tosafos wrote, against Rashi's explanation on the Mishnah, that this intention does not suffice, while concluding that Rashi's wording a little further on supports them (Tosafos, Eruvin 81a; their view is cited by the Taz, ס״ק ד), and the Rosh questions them (Taz, ס״ק ד); the seif, as the Taz explains it, holds acquisition through another valid without this condition (ס״ק א) — see section 5 and Level 4.
  7. In case of doubt: consult your Rav.
  8. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
