דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on Siman 369

One who gives a coin to a baker, or to a householder, to have a share in the courtyard eruv: when does he acquire it? The single seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, compared with the single seif of the Mechaber.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav is not a commentary on the Mechaber: it is a Shulchan Aruch in its own right, written by the Alter Rebbe, with its own division and its own numbering of the seifim. Here the Shulchan Aruch HaRav has one seif, and the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has one seif too; the references on this page follow the Rav's numbering, except where the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch is named.

For Chabad, the Alter Rebbe is הפוסק האחרון (the final posek). This page gathers, for Siman 369, the full text of the Rav and its translation, what his text says and the Mechaber's text does not, and the practical conduct that follows from it.

1 · Text of the Alter Rebbe

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן שס״ט

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav of the Alter Rebbe

סימן שס״ט — בְּאֵיזֶה אֹפֶן מַקְנִין הָעֵרוּב — וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד
Source: Kehot edition, as reproduced on Sefaria. 1 seif (the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch also has 1, under its own numbering).

סעיף א בְּאֵיזֶה אֹפֶן מַקְנִין הָעֵרוּב וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד: הַנּוֹתֵן מָעוֹת לְנַחְתּוֹם, וְאָמַר לוֹ: אִם יָבֹאוּ בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר לִקְנוֹת…

בְּאֵיזֶה אֹפֶן מַקְנִין הָעֵרוּב וּבוֹ סְעִיף אֶחָד:

הַנּוֹתֵן מָעוֹת לְנַחְתּוֹם, וְאָמַר לוֹ: אִם יָבֹאוּ בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר לִקְנוֹת מִמְּךָ כִּכָּר לְעֵרוּב, זְכֵה לִי בָּעֵרוּב, שֶׁיְּהֵא לִי חֵלֶק עִמָּהֶם, בִּשְׁבִיל מָעָה זוֹ — אֵינוֹ עֵרוּב לְזֶה, שֶׁמָּעוֹת אֵינָן קוֹנוֹת עַד שֶׁיִּמְשׁוֹךְ. וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם זִכָּה הַנַּחְתּוֹם הַכִּכָּר לְכָל בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר כֻּלָּם בִּכְלָל עַל יְדֵי אַחֵר בְּמַתְּנַת חִנָּם, וַהֲרֵי גַם זֶה הוּא מִכְּלַל בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר, וְנִתְכַּוֵּן הַנַּחְתּוֹם שֶׁגַּם זֶה יִזְכֶּה וְיִהְיֶה לוֹ חֵלֶק בְּכִכָּר זֶה — מִכָּל מָקוֹם לֹא נִתְכַּוֵּן הַנַּחְתּוֹם שֶׁיִּזְכֶּה בּוֹ זֶה עַל יְדֵי הַזּוֹכֶה, שֶׁיְּהֵא נַעֲשֶׂה שְׁלוּחוֹ כְּמוֹ שֶׁנַּעֲשֶׂה שָׁלִיחַ לְכָל שְׁאָר בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר שֶׁמְּזַכֶּה לָהֶם הַנַּחְתּוֹם בְּמַתְּנַת חִנָּם, אֶלָּא דַּעַת הַנַּחְתּוֹם הוּא שֶׁזֶּה יֵשׁ לוֹ כְּבָר זְכוּת בְּכִכָּר זֶה בִּשְׁבִיל הַמָּעָה שֶׁנָּתַן לוֹ, וְלִשְׁאָר בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר — שֶׁנּוֹתֵן לָהֶם מַתְּנַת חִנָּם — לָהֶם בִּלְבַדָּם הוּא שֶׁעוֹשֶׂה אֶת הַזּוֹכֶה שָׁלִיחַ שֶׁיִּזְכֶּה בִּשְׁבִילָם. אֲבָל לְזֶה אֵינוֹ עוֹשֵׂהוּ שָׁלִיחַ כְּלָל, וְנִמְצָא שֶׁאֵין לוֹ חֵלֶק בָּעֵרוּב (אֲבָל אִם יֵשׁ בְּדַעְתּוֹ שֶׁיִּהְיֶה הַזּוֹכֶה שָׁלִיחַ לְזֶה כְּמוֹ לִשְׁאָר בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר — קָנָה עֵרוּב).

וְאִם אָמַר לוֹ "עָרֵב לִי" — קָנָה עֵרוּב בְּכָל עִנְיָן אִם זִכָּה לְכָל בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר כֻּלָּם בִּכְלָל, שֶׁהֲרֵי עֲשָׂאוֹ שָׁלִיחַ לְעָרֵב לוֹ בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁיּוֹעִיל. וְכֵיוָן שֶׁאִי אֶפְשָׁר לִהְיוֹת לוֹ עֵרוּב אֶלָּא אִם כֵּן יְזַכֶּה לוֹ עַל יְדֵי אַחֵר, כְּמוֹ לַאֲחֵרִים שֶׁלֹּא נָתְנוּ מָעוֹת — הֲרֵי זֶה כְּאִלּוּ אָמַר לוֹ שֶׁיְּזַכֶּה לוֹ עַל יְדֵי אַחֵר כְּמוֹ לַאֲחֵרִים, וְלֹא יִזְכֶּה בְּמָעָה בִּלְבַדָּהּ. וַחֲזָקָה שָׁלִיחַ עוֹשֶׂה שְׁלִיחוּתוֹ.

וַאֲפִלּוּ אִם אָמַר "זְכֵה לִי" — אִם נָתַן לוֹ כְּלִי שֶׁיַּקְנֶה לוֹ בְּתוֹרַת קִנְיַן סוּדָר, קָנָה עֵרוּב.

וְאִם נָתַן מָעָה לְבַעַל הַבַּיִת, וְאָמַר לוֹ "זְכֵה לִי בָּעֵרוּב" — קָנָה עֵרוּב, לְפִי שֶׁאֵין דֶּרֶךְ בַּעַל הַבַּיִת לִמְכּוֹר פַּת מִשֶּׁלּוֹ, וּלְדַעַת שֶׁיִּקְנֶה לוֹ בְּמָעוֹתָיו פַּת מִן הַשּׁוּק אָמַר לוֹ כֵּן, וּכְאִלּוּ אָמַר "עָרֵב לִי", שֶׁעֲשָׂאוֹ שָׁלִיחַ לְעָרֵב. וַחֲזָקָה שָׁלִיחַ עוֹשֶׂה שְׁלִיחוּתוֹ, וּמִן הַסְּתָם קָנָה לוֹ וְזָכָה בִּשְׁבִילוֹ, אוֹ זִכָּה לוֹ עַל יְדֵי אַחֵר מִשֶּׁלּוֹ:

In what manner the eruv is transferred; it contains a single se'if:

One who gives money (ma'os) to a baker (nachtom) and says to him: “If the residents of the courtyard come to buy a loaf from you for the eruv, acquire [a share] in the eruv on my behalf, so that I have a share with them, in exchange for this coin (ma'ah)” — it is not an eruv for him, for money does not acquire until one draws [the object to oneself] (meshichah). And even if the baker transferred the loaf to all the residents of the courtyard together, collectively, through another person, as an outright gift (matnas chinam) — and this man too is among the residents of the courtyard, and the baker intended that he too acquire and have a share in this loaf — nevertheless the baker did not intend that he acquire it through the one who acquires [for the residents] (ha-zocheh), so that the latter become his agent (shaliach) just as he becomes the agent of all the other residents of the courtyard to whom the baker transfers as an outright gift. Rather, the baker's thought is that this man already has a right in this loaf in exchange for the coin he gave him, and that it is for the other residents of the courtyard — to whom he gives an outright gift — for them alone, that he makes the zocheh an agent to acquire on their behalf. But for this man he does not make him an agent at all, and it turns out that he has no share in the eruv (but if [the baker] has in mind that the zocheh be an agent for this man as for the other residents of the courtyard, he has acquired the eruv).

And if he said to him “make the eruv for me” (arev li), he has acquired the eruv in any case, if [the baker] transferred [the loaf] to all the residents of the courtyard together, collectively: for he made him his agent to make the eruv for him in a way that will be effective. And since he can have an eruv only if [the baker] transfers it to him through another person, as to the others who did not give money, it is as if he had told him to transfer it to him through another person, as to the others, and not that he acquire by the coin alone. And there is a presumption that an agent carries out his mission (chazakah shaliach oseh shelichuso).

And even if he said “acquire on my behalf” (zecheh li): if he gave him an article (keli) so that he transfer to him by way of kinyan sudar, he has acquired the eruv.

And if he gave a coin to a householder (ba'al ha-bayis) and said to him “acquire [a share] in the eruv on my behalf,” he has acquired the eruv: for a householder is not accustomed to sell bread of his own; it was with the thought that he would buy bread for him in the market with his money that he spoke to him so, and it is as if he had said “make the eruv for me,” since he made him his agent to make the eruv. And there is a presumption that an agent carries out his mission: by default (min ha-stam), he bought [bread] for him and acquired it on his behalf, or he transferred to him, through another person, of what is his own [the householder's].

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim 369. Sefaria gives no Kuntres Acharon entry for this siman.

2 · Chidush of the Alter Rebbe

חידושים מיוחדים של הרב

The chidushim unique to the Rav on this siman

What the Rav's text says and the Mechaber's text does not, as shown by comparing the two texts of Siman 369, quoted side by side. Several of the reasons the Rav writes in the body of the law are already found in the Magen Avraham on this seif: this is noted each time.

חידוש א — מעות, משיכה וכוונת הנחתום (שס״ט:1)

Chidush א — money, meshichah and the baker's intention (369:1)

Why the coin does not acquire, and when the eruv is acquired nonetheless

What the Mechaber writes: « אינו עירוב אפילו עירב החנוני [לכל האחרים] וזכה גם לזה עמהם… » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א) — the seif then moves on to the case where he said “make the eruv for me” (see chidush ב) — and further on in the same seif, « וכן אם זיכה לו הנחתום ע״י אחר או שקנה במעה שנתן לו לחם מן השוק קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א). The Mechaber does not give here the reason for the first rule, nor does he say what distinguishes the two cases.

What the Rav writes: the reason, « שֶׁמָּעוֹת אֵינָן קוֹנוֹת עַד שֶׁיִּמְשׁוֹךְ » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א); and, for the case where the baker transferred the loaf to all the residents of the courtyard together, the thought he attributes to the baker: « אֶלָּא דַּעַת הַנַּחְתּוֹם הוּא שֶׁזֶּה יֵשׁ לוֹ כְּבָר זְכוּת בְּכִכָּר זֶה בִּשְׁבִיל הַמָּעָה שֶׁנָּתַן לוֹ » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א). The Magen Avraham had already given this reason on the Mechaber's words: « לפי שלא נתכוין ליתן לו מתנת חנם אלא בשביל מעות שנתן לו » (מגן אברהם שס״ט ס״ק א). The Rav then writes, in parentheses, the opposite case: « אֲבָל אִם יֵשׁ בְּדַעְתּוֹ שֶׁיִּהְיֶה הַזּוֹכֶה שָׁלִיחַ לְזֶה כְּמוֹ לִשְׁאָר בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר — קָנָה עֵרוּב » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א).

The difference: the Mechaber states the rule without its reason; the Rav's text grounds it on two points — money does not acquire without meshichah, and the baker does not make the zocheh the agent of the one who paid — and it writes the case where the baker makes the zocheh his agent too: the eruv is then acquired.

Practical consequence: one who gave a coin to the baker saying “acquire on my behalf” (zecheh li) has no share in the eruv, even if the baker transferred the loaf to all the residents of the courtyard together — unless the baker had in mind to make the zocheh his agent too (369:1).

חידוש ב — עשאו שליח, וחזקה שליח עושה שליחותו (שס״ט:1)

Chidush ב — “make the eruv for me,” the householder, and the presumption about the agent (369:1)

The presumption that an agent carries out his mission

What the Mechaber writes: « אבל אם אמר לו ערב לי קנה עירוב ואפילו אם אמר זכה לי אם נתן לו כלי בתור' קנין שיקנה לו בו קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א), and « ואם נתן מעה לבעל הבי' ואמר לו זכה לי בעירוב קנה עירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שס״ט:א) — without giving the reasons. The Magen Avraham gives them: for “make the eruv for me,” citing the Tur, « הרי עשאו שליח לערב לו באופן שיועיל » (מגן אברהם שס״ט ס״ק ב); for the householder, « דאין דרך בע״ה למכור משלו » (מגן אברהם שס״ט ס״ק ג).

What the Rav writes: he writes these reasons in the body of the law, « שֶׁהֲרֵי עֲשָׂאוֹ שָׁלִיחַ לְעָרֵב לוֹ בְּעִנְיָן שֶׁיּוֹעִיל » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א) and « לְפִי שֶׁאֵין דֶּרֶךְ בַּעַל הַבַּיִת לִמְכּוֹר פַּת מִשֶּׁלּוֹ » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א); he specifies that “make the eruv for me” acquires « קָנָה עֵרוּב בְּכָל עִנְיָן אִם זִכָּה לְכָל בְּנֵי הֶחָצֵר כֻּלָּם בִּכְלָל » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א); and in both cases he adds the presumption that an agent carries out his mission, from which he draws, for the householder, what is assumed by default: « וַחֲזָקָה שָׁלִיחַ עוֹשֶׂה שְׁלִיחוּתוֹ, וּמִן הַסְּתָם קָנָה לוֹ וְזָכָה בִּשְׁבִילוֹ, אוֹ זִכָּה לוֹ עַל יְדֵי אַחֵר מִשֶּׁלּוֹ » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א).

The difference: the Mechaber's text does not name this presumption; the Rav's text relies on it to hold the eruv acquired, both when one told the baker “make the eruv for me” and when one handed the coin to a householder.

Practical consequence: one who told the baker “make the eruv for me” (arev li) has acquired the eruv, if the baker transferred the loaf to all the residents of the courtyard together; one who gave a coin to a householder saying “acquire [a share] in the eruv on my behalf” has acquired the eruv (369:1).

4 · Lemaaseh

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

The practical Chabad conduct

Points of halachic conduct that flow directly from the Shulchan Aruch HaRav Siman 369, in the Chabad sensibility. The siman has a single seif: each point refers to that seif, in the order of the text.

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman 369

  • ① A coin given to the baker, with “acquire on my behalf”: it is not an eruv for the one who paid, for money does not acquire without meshichah; and this even if the baker transferred the loaf to all the residents of the courtyard together through another person, as an outright gift — unless the baker had in mind to make the zocheh this man's agent too. See section 2. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 369:1.
  • ② “Make the eruv for me”: the eruv is acquired, if the baker transferred [the loaf] to all the residents of the courtyard together; it is as if he had asked him to transfer it to him through another person, « וְלֹא יִזְכֶּה בְּמָעָה בִּלְבַדָּהּ » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 369:1.
  • ③ “Acquire on my behalf,” with an article: « אִם נָתַן לוֹ כְּלִי שֶׁיַּקְנֶה לוֹ בְּתוֹרַת קִנְיַן סוּדָר, קָנָה עֵרוּב » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 369:1.
  • ④ A coin given to a householder: the eruv is acquired, for it was with the thought that he would buy him bread in the market, « וּלְדַעַת שֶׁיִּקְנֶה לוֹ בְּמָעוֹתָיו פַּת מִן הַשּׁוּק אָמַר לוֹ כֵּן » (שו״ע הרב שס״ט:א); by default, the householder bought [bread] for him and acquired it on his behalf, or transferred to him, through another person, of what is his own. See section 2. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav 369:1.

⚠ This section presents the Chabad halachic conduct. For any concrete question: consult your Rav.
