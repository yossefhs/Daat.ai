Siman 271 — Friday Night Kiddush over Wine

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe on the 30 seifim that codify the great corpus of Friday night Kiddush: mitzvah min haTorah, wine (yayin) or bread (lechem mishneh), nusach, standing or sitting, Kiddush for women, and more.
