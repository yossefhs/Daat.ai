1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שכ״ב contains 6 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo). Subject of the siman: the egg laid on Shabbos (nolad).

Seif א

דין נולד בשבת. ובו ו סעיפים:
ביצה שנולדה בשבת אסור אפי' לטלטלה ואפי' נתערבה באלף כולן אסורות ויכול לכוף עליה כלי שלא תשבר ובלבד שלא יגע בה:
Translation: An egg laid on Shabbos — it is forbidden even to move it (it is מוקצה). And even mixed into a thousand [permitted eggs], all become forbidden — because it is a דבר שיש לו מתירין (something that will become permitted later, at the close of Shabbos), and such a thing is not nullified, even in a thousand. One may turn a vessel over it so that it not break, provided one does not touch it.

Seif ב

שבת שלאחר יום טוב או לפניו נולדה בזה אסורה בזה:
Translation: A Shabbos falling immediately after a Yom Tov (or immediately before): an egg laid on the one is forbidden on the other. (A gezeirah of the Sages: no distinction is drawn, to avoid confusion — and in fact an egg laid on a Yom Tov following Shabbos is forbidden by Torah law, having been "prepared" inside the hen since the day before.)

Seif ג

פירות שנשרו מן האילן בשבת אסורים בו ביום ולערב מותרים מיד:
Translation: Fruit that fell from the tree on Shabbos — forbidden that day (מוקצה, and out of concern that one might climb up to pick), but permitted as soon as night falls, immediately (without waiting "the time it would have taken to produce them", since no melacha was performed).

Seif ד

אוכלי בהמה אין בהם משום תיקון כלי ולפיכך מותר לקטום אפילו בסכין קש או תבן ולחצוץ בו שיניו אבל קיסם שאינו אוכל בהמה אפילו ליטלו כדי לחצוץ בו שיניו אסור:
Translation: Animal fodder does not fall under the prohibition of תיקון כלי (fashioning a utensil); one may therefore cut straw or hay, even with a knife, and pick one's teeth with it. But a wood chip (קיסם) which is not animal food — even picking it up to clean one's teeth is forbidden (מוקצה; and shaping it with a tool would make a "utensil" → melacha).

Seif ה

מותר לטלטל עצי בשמים להריח בהם ולהניף בהם לחולה ומוללו להריח בו אחד קשים ואחד רכים:
Translation: One may handle aromatic woods (עצי בשמים) in order to smell them, fan them towards a sick person [to refresh him], and crush them between the fingers to bring up their scent — whether they are hard or soft. (They are not מוקצה since they serve the sense of smell; but creating a new fragrance — for instance by impregnating a garment — remains forbidden.)

Seif ו

המחלק לבני ביתו מנות בשבת יכול להטיל גורל לומר למי שיצא גורל פלוני יהיה חלק פלוני שלו והוא שיהיו החלקים שוים ואינו עושה אלא כדי להשותם שלא להטיל קנאה ביניהם אבל עם אחרים אסור כיון שמקפידין זה על זה יבואו לידי מדה ומשקל אבל ליתן מנה גדולה כנגד מנה קטנה ולהטיל גורל עליהם אפילו בבני ביתו ובחול אסור משום קוביא ויש אומרים דעם בניו ובני ביתו מותר להטיל גורל אפי' על מנה גדולה כנגד מנה קטנה מפני שאין מקפידין: הגה ואסור להטיל גורל בשבת אפילו ע״י עכו״ם [מהרי״ל]:
Translation: One who distributes portions to his household on Shabbos may draw lots (גורל) — "whoever draws such a lot, such a portion shall be his" — provided the portions are equal, and that he does so only in order to even them out, so as not to arouse jealousy. But with others [outside his household] it is forbidden: being particular with one another, they would come to "measure and weight". To offer a large portion against a small one and draw lots on it — even within the family and even on a weekday — is forbidden on account of קוביא (games of chance, akin to theft). And some say that with one's children and household it is permitted even large against small, since they are not particular. Gloss (Rema): it is forbidden to draw lots on Shabbos, even through a non-Jew (Maharil).
Complete text: these 6 seifim constitute the whole of the Mechaber's codification on this subject. Each one specifies a case, a condition, or an exception.

2. The general context

What is this siman about?

Our siman deals with the egg laid on Shabbos (nolad). It belongs to the great corpus of הלכות שבת (the laws of Shabbos) in the Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chayim.

The fundamental question: what are the halachic conditions, permissions and prohibitions bearing on the egg laid on Shabbos (nolad)? Which structuring principles allow one to decide the borderline cases?

Place within Hilchos Shabbos

Siman שכ״ב is one of simanim 242-365 which cover Hilchos Shabbos. It has its own conceptual scope, but it works together with the neighbouring simanim.

3. The key halachic concepts

To understand this siman one must master a few structuring halachic concepts — which appear across the 6 seifim and the commentary of the Mishnah Berurah:

Recurring concepts in Hilchos Shabbos (worth knowing):
  • מלאכה (melacha) — the act of work forbidden on Shabbos
  • דאורייתא / דרבנן — forbidden by the Torah or by the Sages
  • פסיק רישא — an unavoidable consequence
  • דבר שאינו מתכוון — an unintended act
  • שבות — a secondary rabbinic prohibition
  • פיקוח נפש — mortal danger, which suspends the prohibition
Siman שכ״ב deploys some of these concepts in the context of the egg laid on Shabbos (nolad).

4. The seifim in detail — one by one

Let us take each seif of the Mechaber in turn and set out its practical import. For the in-depth pilpul (Talmudic sources, מחלוקות of the Rishonim, נפקא מינות), see Level 2 — Lamdan.

The siman gathers four subjects held together by a common thread: what one may or may not use and handle on Shabbos — מוקצה, נולד, תיקון כלי, and the drawing of lots.

Seifim א-ג — נולד: what "comes into being" on Shabbos

CasRègleRaison (MB)
Egg laid on Shabbos (seif 1)Forbidden even to move; mixed into 1000 → all forbiddenמוקצה + דבר שיש לו מתירין (will be permitted tonight → not nullified, ס״ק ב)
Shabbos adjoining a Yom Tov (seif 2)Egg laid on the one, forbidden on the otherGezeirah; on a Yom Tov after Shabbos, forbidden by Torah law (הכנה, ס״ק א)
Fruit fallen from the tree (seif 3)Forbidden during the day, permitted from nightfallמוקצה + concern that one might climb up to pick (ס״ק ז)

Seif ד — אוכלי בהמה and תיקון כלי

Animal food is never "a utensil in the making": cutting it (even with a knife) does not fall under תיקון כלי. But an inedible chip is מוקצה — and shaping it into a toothpick would amount to fashioning a utensil (a מחלוקת whether the prohibition is תיקון כלי or מחתך, see Level 2).

Seif ה — עצי בשמים (aromatic woods)

Permitted to handle them in order to smell them (they serve the sense of smell, hence not מוקצה), whether hard or soft; but forbidden to create a new fragrance — מוליד ריח — by impregnating a garment, for instance.

Seif ו — גורל (drawing lots for the portions)

Within the family, equal portions → permitted (no gain, no קוביא). With others, or a large portion against a small one → forbidden (קוביא / מדה ומשקל). Rema: forbidden even through a non-Jew.

6. The position of the Rema

The Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) adds here a single הגהה (gloss), on seif 6, concerning the drawing of lots:

The gloss of the Rema (seif 6) — the goral: it is forbidden to draw lots on Shabbos even through a non-Jew (Maharil). One does not circumvent the prohibition of קוביא by having a non-Jewish third party act.

Marker: Mechaber → Sephardim (Beis Yosef); Rema → Ashkenazim (Magen Avraham, Mishnah Berurah). This siman has no Level 4 Daat HaRav: the Alter Rebbe did not write it.

Siman שכ״ב touches daily Shabbos life. Here are a few concrete applications:
