1. The central axiom

Siman שכ״ב in one sentence.
The siman gathers four subjects that turn on a single question: is this object ready and designated for my use on Shabbos? — the egg and the fruit that "come into being" on Shabbos (נולד / מוקצה), cutting animal food (תיקון כלי), smelling aromatic woods, and drawing lots for the portions (גורל). The axiom: what was מוכן (prepared and designated) before Shabbos is permitted — provided one turns it neither into a tool nor into a stake of chance.

3. Hierarchy: permitted → forbidden

Permitted: smelling and crushing aromatic woods (by hand); cutting animal food, even with a knife; drawing lots for equal portions within the family.
↓
Permitted conditionally / from the evening: an egg found before dawn in the nest (presumed from yesterday); fallen fruit — from nightfall, immediately.
↓
Forbidden (rabbinically): moving or eating the egg laid on Shabbos; gathering fruit fallen that day; picking up an inedible chip; drawing lots with outsiders.
↓
Gravely forbidden: an egg of a Yom Tov following Shabbos (by Torah law — הכנה); shaping a toothpick with a knife (תיקון כלי / מחתך); creating a fragrance in a garment (מוליד ריח); a large-against-small lottery (קוביא).

4. Decision tree — the four subjects

Q1 — Did something "come into being" on Shabbos (an egg, fallen fruit)? → מוקצה: do not handle or eat; the egg is not nullified even in 1000; permitted in the evening.
↓
Q2 — Cutting or shaping something? → Animal food → permitted, even with a knife. An inedible object (a chip) → מוקצה; making a tool of it → forbidden (melacha).
↓
Q3 — Fragrant wood or spices? → Smelling / crushing by hand → permitted. Creating a scent in a fabric → forbidden (מוליד ריח).
↓
Q4 — Drawing lots? → Equal portions within the family → permitted. Large against small, or with outsiders, or through a non-Jew → forbidden. At the least doubt → consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic

נ — נולד: egg and fruit "born" on Shabbos → מוקצה, do not handle.

ת — תיקון כלי: cutting animal food yes; making a tool of it no.

ב — בשמים: smelling yes; creating a fragrance (in a fabric) no.

ג — גורל: equal portions within the family yes; lottery / outsiders / through a non-Jew no.

Four subjects, one thread: use only what was ready and designated for Shabbos, without turning it into a tool or into a stake.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — the egg "lost in the crowd". An egg laid on Shabbos is not nullified, even mixed into a thousand (דבר שיש לו מתירין): the whole stock becomes forbidden until nightfall. Nor may it be moved (מוקצה).
Pitfall 2 — gathering fallen fruit. Beyond the prohibition of the day itself, gathering several fruits at their place of growth resembles מעמר — close to a חיוב חטאת. Even if they fell the day before, take them only one by one.
Pitfall 3 — the toothpick. Shaping a wood chip with a knife in order to pick one's teeth fashions a "utensil" (תיקון כלי / מחתך) — forbidden. Prepare the toothpick before Shabbos.
Pitfall 4 — fragrance and lottery. Impregnating a garment with a scent (מוליד ריח) is forbidden, even though handling spices in order to smell them is permitted. And any drawing of lots with a stake is forbidden on Shabbos — even through a non-Jew (Rema).

10. The practical mitzvos of Siman שכ״ב

For practice

  1. Egg laid on Shabbos → do not handle or eat it; turn a vessel over it without touching it.
  2. Fallen fruit → do not gather during the day; permitted from nightfall, one by one.
  3. Animal food → cutting it even with a knife is permitted; an inedible chip → do not touch it.
  4. Aromatic woods → smell and crush them by hand; do not perfume a garment.
  5. Drawing lots → equal portions within the family / a mitzvah from the book, permitted; a lottery or through a non-Jew, forbidden.
  6. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. For the pilpul, see Level 2 — Lamdan.
