Siman שכ"ב — דין נולד בשבת

An egg laid on Shabbos (nolad) — a topic from the Mechaber.

The Alter Rebbe did not write this siman separately

The topic of nolad (an egg laid on Shabbos — forbidden as muktzeh) is treated in other simanim by the Alter Rebbe (in particular, 308 and 310 on muktzeh in general).

For this particular siman, see the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber (6 se'ifim).
