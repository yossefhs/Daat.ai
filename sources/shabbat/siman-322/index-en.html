An egg laid on Shabbos (Nolad)

The egg laid on Shabbos and the laws of Nolad, according to the Mechaber and the Mishnah Berurah.

Frequently asked questions — Siman 322

Why does siman 322 not have a Daat HaRav level (level 4)?

The Alter Rebbe (Shulchan Aruch HaRav) did not devote a separate siman to this topic; he treats Nolad in simanim 308 and 310. The study of siman 322 therefore follows the Mechaber and the Mishnah Berurah, offered at levels 1 to 3.
