דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch on Siman רצ"ו

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe on the 19 seifim codifying havdalah on wine at the conclusion of Shabbos — 4 berachos (borei pri ha-gafen, besamim, me'orei ha-eish, ha-Mavdil), ritual structure, modern application.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch is not a commentary on the Mechaber (מחבר) — it is a standalone, complete Shulchan Aruch, written by the Alter Rebbe. Its uniqueness: it combines halachah (הלכה) + טעמי המצוות + inner dimension in a single work, and rules with a unique Talmudic rigor.

For Chabad, the Alter Rebbe is הפוסק האחרון. This page gathers, for siman רצ"ו, the full text of the Rav, his originality, and the דברי הרבי that illuminate the topic.

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן רצ"ו

The full text of the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch

סימן רצ"ו — דִּינֵי הַבְדָּלָה עַל הַיַּיִן — וּבוֹ יט סְעִיפִים
Source: Kehot edition. 19 seifim + 3 entries of Kuntres Acharon (קונטרס אחרון).

סעיף א הִנְהִיגוּ חֲכָמִים לְהָרִיחַ בִּבְשָׂמִים בְּמוֹצָאֵי שַׁבָּת… וְסִמָּנָךְ יִבָּנֶ"ה

דִּינֵי הַבְדָּלָה עַל הַיַּיִן וּבוֹ י"ט סְעִיפִים:

הִנְהִיגוּ חֲכָמִים לְהָרִיחַ בִּבְשָׂמִים בְּמוֹצָאֵי שַׁבָּת, מִטַּעַם שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רצ"ז. וְצָרִיךְ שֶׁיְּבָרֵךְ קֹדֶם הָרֵיחַ. וְהִצְרִיכוּ גַם כֵּן לְבָרֵךְ עַל הָאוּר בְּכָל מוֹצָאֵי שַׁבָּת, מִטַּעַם שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן רצ"ח. וְסִדְּרוּ בְּרָכוֹת אֵלּוּ עַל הַכּוֹס אַחַר בִּרְכַּת הַיַּיִן קֹדֶם בִּרְכַּת הַבְדָּלָה, בִּתְחִלָּה עַל הַבְּשָׂמִים, וְאַחַר כָּךְ עַל הַנֵּר, וְנִמְצָא סֵדֶר הַבְדָּלָה שֶׁעַל הַכּוֹס, בִּרְכַּת הַיַּיִן וּבִרְכַּת הַבְּשָׂמִים וּבִרְכַּת הַנֵּר וּבִרְכַּת הַבְדָּלָה, וְסִמָּנָךְ יִבָּנֶ"ה, "יַיִן "בְּשָׂמִים "נֵר "הַבְדָּלָה:

Laws of Havdalah on wine — and it contains 19 seifim:

The Chachamim instituted smelling besamim on motzaei Shabbos, for the reason that will be explained in siman 297. One must recite the berachah before smelling. They likewise required reciting a berachah on the fire on every motzaei Shabbos, for the reason that will be explained in siman 298. They arranged these berachos on the kos after the berachah on the yayin and before the berachah of Havdalah: first on the besamim, then on the ner. The order of Havdalah on the kos is therefore: berachah on the yayin, berachah on the besamim, berachah on the ner, and berachah of Havdalah. The mnemonic is yavneh (יבנ"ה): Yayin, Besamim, Ner, Havdalah:

סעיף ב וְצָרִיךְ לִזָּהֵר בְּכוֹס שֶׁל הַבְדָּלָה בְּכָל הַדְּבָרִים שֶׁצָּרִיךְ לִזָּהֵר בְּכוֹס שֶׁל קִדּוּשׁ…

וְצָרִיךְ לִזָּהֵר בְּכוֹס שֶׁל הַבְדָּלָה בְּכָל הַדְּבָרִים שֶׁצָּרִיךְ לִזָּהֵר בְּכוֹס שֶׁל קִדּוּשׁ וְשֶׁל בִּרְכַּת הַמָּזוֹן, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן קפ"ג וְסִמָּן רע"א:

One must be careful with the kos of Havdalah in all the matters one must be careful with regarding the kos of Kiddush and of Birkas ha-Mazon, as explained in simanim 183 and 271:

סעיף ג נָהֲגוּ לוֹמַר קֹדֶם הַבְדָּלָה שֶׁבַּבַּיִת "הִנֵּה אֵל יְשׁוּעָתִי וְגוֹ'"…

נָהֲגוּ לוֹמַר קֹדֶם הַבְדָּלָה שֶׁבַּבַּיִת "הִנֵּה אֵל יְשׁוּעָתִי וְגוֹ'", "לַיְּהוּדִים הָיְתָה וְגוֹ'", "כּוֹס יְשׁוּעוֹת אֶשָּׂא וְגוֹ'". הַכֹּל לְסִמָּן טוֹב, וְאֵין חוֹבָה בַּדָּבָר:

The custom before Havdalah at home is to recite "Hinei Kel yeshu'ati…", "la-Yehudim hayetah…", "kos yeshu'os essa…". All this is for a good sign (siman tov); there is no obligation in it:

סעיף ד וּבִשְׁעַת בִּרְכַּת הַבְדָּלָה יִתְּנוּ הַשּׁוֹמְעִים אֶת עֵינֵיהֶם בַּכּוֹס וּבַנֵּר:

וּבִשְׁעַת בִּרְכַּת הַבְדָּלָה יִתְּנוּ הַשּׁוֹמְעִים אֶת עֵינֵיהֶם בַּכּוֹס וּבַנֵּר:

During the berachah of Havdalah, the listeners should fix their eyes on the kos and on the ner:

סעיף ה וְנוֹהֲגִין לִשְׁפּוֹךְ מְעַט יַיִן מֵהַכּוֹס עַל הָאָרֶץ לְסִמָּן טוֹב…

וְנוֹהֲגִין לִשְׁפּוֹךְ מְעַט יַיִן מֵהַכּוֹס עַל הָאָרֶץ לְסִמָּן טוֹב, שֶׁכָּל בַּיִת שֶׁאֵין יַיִן נִשְׁפָּךְ בּוֹ כַּמַּיִם אֵין בּוֹ סִמַּן בְּרָכָה, עַל כֵּן עוֹשִׂין סִמַּן בְּרָכָה בִּתְחִלַּת הַשָּׁבוּעַ. וְאֵין בָּזֶה אִסּוּר מִשּׁוּם בִּזּוּי מַשְׁקֶה שֶׁשּׁוֹפְכוֹ לְאִבּוּד, כֵּיוָן שֶׁאֵינוֹ שׁוֹפֵךְ אֶלָּא דָּבָר מוּעָט.

גַּם נוֹהֲגִין לִשְׁפּוֹךְ עַל הַשֻּׁלְחָן מִן יַיִן הַנִּשְׁאָר בַּכּוֹס אַחַר שְׁתִיַּת הַמַּבְדִּיל וּמְכַבִּין בּוֹ הַנֵּר. וְנוֹהֲגִין לִרְחוֹץ עֵינֵיהֶם מִיַּיִן שֶׁל שְׁפִיכָה זוֹ, מִשּׁוּם חִבּוּב מִצְוָה:

It is customary to pour a little yayin from the kos onto the ground as a good sign (siman tov), since any house in which wine is not spilled like water has no sign of berachah; therefore, one makes a sign of berachah at the beginning of the week. There is no prohibition of bizui mashkeh (disgracing a beverage) in this, since one only pours out a tiny amount.

It is also customary to pour some of the yayin left in the kos, after the one who made Havdalah has drunk, onto the table and to extinguish the ner havdalah with it. It is customary to wash one's eyes with the yayin of this pouring, out of chibuv mitzvah:

סעיף ו אֵין צָרִיךְ לְהַטְעִים מִכּוֹס שֶׁל הַבְדָּלָה לְהַשּׁוֹמְעִים…

אֵין צָרִיךְ לְהַטְעִים מִכּוֹס שֶׁל הַבְדָּלָה לְהַשּׁוֹמְעִים כְּמוֹ שֶׁצָּרִיךְ בְּכוֹס שֶׁל קִדּוּשׁ, אֶלָּא הַמַּבְדִּיל רָגִיל לִשְׁתּוֹתוֹ כֻלּוֹ:

One is not required to give listeners a taste from the kos of Havdalah as one is required to do with the kos of Kiddush; rather, the one making Havdalah (the Mavdil) customarily drinks it all:

סעיף ז אֵין מַבְדִּילִין עַל הַפַּת אֲפִלּוּ אֵין לוֹ יַיִן וְלֹא שְׁאָר מַשְׁקִים…

אֵין מַבְדִּילִין עַל הַפַּת אֲפִלּוּ אֵין לוֹ יַיִן וְלֹא שְׁאָר מַשְׁקִים. וְאֵינוֹ דוֹמֶה לְקִדּוּשׁ שֶׁמְּקַדְּשִׁין עַל הַפַּת, לְפִי שֶׁהַקִּדּוּשׁ יֵשׁ לוֹ עִנְיָן לְהַפַּת, שֶׁאֵין קִדּוּשׁ אֶלָּא בִּמְקוֹם סְעֻדָּה, אֲבָל הַבְדָּלָה אֵין לָהּ עִנְיָן כְּלָל לְהַפַּת:

One does not make Havdalah on bread (pas), even if one has neither yayin nor other beverages. It is not comparable to Kiddush, which one may make on bread, because Kiddush has a connection to bread — there is no Kiddush except in the place of a meal — whereas Havdalah has no connection at all to bread:

סעיף ח אֲבָל מַבְדִּילִין עַל כָּל מַשְׁקֶה שֶׁהוּא חֲמַר מְדִינָה חוּץ מִן הַמַּיִם…

אֲבָל מַבְדִּילִין עַל כָּל מַשְׁקֶה שֶׁהוּא חֲמַר מְדִינָה חוּץ מִן הַמַּיִם, אֶלָּא אִם כֵּן יֵשׁ לוֹ יַיִן, שֶׁאָז אֵין זֶה הִדּוּר מִצְוָה אִם יַבְדִּיל עַל שֵׁכָר אוֹ שְׁאָר מַשְׁקִים אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁהֵם חֲמַר מְדִינָה. אֲבָל אִם אֵין יַיִן בְּבֵיתוֹ אַף שֶׁיֶּשְׁנוֹ אֵצֶל חֶנְוָנִי — אֵין צָרִיךְ לִקְנוֹתוֹ.

וּמִכָּל מָקוֹם, מִצְוָה מִן הַמֻּבְחָר לְהַבְדִּיל עַל הַיַּיִן בְּכָל עִנְיָן:

But one may make Havdalah on any beverage that is chamar medinah (the "wine of the country") except for water, unless one has yayin — for then it would not be hiddur mitzvah to make Havdalah on beer (shechar) or other beverages, even if they are chamar medinah. But if one has no yayin at home, even if it is available at the shop, one is not obligated to go and buy it.

Nevertheless, it is mitzvah min ha-muvchar to make Havdalah on yayin in any event:

סעיף ט-יב Further seifim — pagum wine vs. chamar medinah, motzaei Pesach, Yom Tov motzaei Shabbat, no drink…

Seifim 9-12 cover: priority between pagum wine and chamar medinah, motzaei Pesach minhag to use beer if chaviv, yaknehaz on bread if Yom Tov falls motzaei Shabbat, and ruling for one who has nothing — if expects tomorrow he waits, otherwise leniency to eat. See full text on Sefaria.

Seifim 9-12 cover: priority between pagum yayin and chamar medinah, the minhag on motzaei Pesach to use beer if it is chaviv, yaknehaz on bread when Yom Tov falls on motzaei Shabbos, and the ruling for one who has nothing — if he expects to obtain a kos the next day he waits, otherwise there is leniency to eat. See the full text on Sefaria.

סעיף יג-טז Priorities — Kiddush/havdalah, Chanukah/havdalah, standing posture, hold in right hand…

Seif 15: נוֹהֲגִים בִּמְדִינוֹת אֵלּוּ לְהַבְדִּיל מְעֻמָּד לִכְבוֹד הַמֶּלֶךְ שֶׁאָנוּ מְלַוִּין אוֹתוֹ. Seif 16: כָּל דָּבָר שֶׁמְּבָרֵךְ עָלָיו צָרִיךְ לְאָחֳזוֹ בִּימִינוֹ בִּשְׁעַת הַבְּרָכָה — hold the cup in the right hand during borei pri ha-gafen, swap with besamim, return wine to the right before ha-Mavdil.

Seif 15: The minhag in these countries is to make Havdalah standing, in honor of the King whom we are escorting — and the manner of escorting is done standing. Seif 16: Anything over which one recites a berachah must be held in one's right hand at the time of the berachah. Hold the kos in the right hand during borei pri ha-gefen, switch with the besamim, then return the yayin to the right hand before ha-Mavdil.

סעיף יז שְׁלִיחַ צִבּוּר הַמַּבְדִּיל בְּבֵית הַכְּנֶסֶת… הַמַּבְדִּיל בְּבֵיתוֹ לְהוֹצִיא בְּנֵי בֵיתוֹ…

(שְׁלִיחַ צִבּוּר הַמַּבְדִּיל בְּבֵית הַכְּנֶסֶת, אַף שֶׁנִּתְכַּוֵּן לָצֵאת יְדֵי חוֹבָתוֹ — יְכוֹלִים לַחֲזוֹר וּלְהַבְדִּיל בְּבָתֵּיהֶם לְהוֹצִיא בְּנֵי בֵיתָם הַקְּטַנִּים שֶׁלֹּא הָיוּ בְּבֵית הַכְּנֶסֶת.) מִי שֶׁנִּתְכַּוֵּן שֶׁלֹּא לָצֵאת יְדֵי חוֹבָתוֹ בְּהַבְדָּלָה שֶׁבְּבֵית הַכְּנֶסֶת — צָרִיךְ לַחֲזוֹר וּלְהַבְדִּיל בְּבֵיתוֹ. לְפִיכָךְ, נָהֲגוּ הָעוֹלָם שֶׁכָּל אֶחָד חוֹזֵר וּמַבְדִּיל בְּבֵיתוֹ.

(The shaliach tzibbur who makes Havdalah in shul, even if he intended to fulfill his obligation — may go back and make Havdalah at home to bring out the minors of his household who were not in shul.) One who intended not to fulfill his obligation with the Havdalah in shul must go back and make Havdalah at home. Therefore, the practice of the world is that everyone goes back and makes Havdalah at home.

סעיף יח-יט Hearing havdalah with kavanah; obligation of women in havdalah de'oraisa per one opinion…

Seif 19: According to those who say havdalah is essentially from the Torah — women too are obligated in havdalah from the Torah. Some say women are exempt. Others disagree and say havdalah is part of remembering the kedushah of Shabbos, and so is the main view. Nevertheless, one should be concerned for the first opinion. But women themselves may make havdalah for themselves. And a man should not be yotzei by hearing havdalah from a woman.

Seif 19: According to those who say Havdalah is essentially from the Torah — women too are obligated in Havdalah from the Torah. Some say women are exempt. Others disagree and say Havdalah is part of remembering the kedushah of Shabbos, and this is the main view. Nevertheless, one should be concerned for the first opinion (a man already yotzei should not be motzi women alone). But women themselves may make Havdalah for themselves. And a man should not be yotzei by hearing Havdalah from a woman.

חידושים מיוחדים של הרב

The Rav's distinctive חידושים on this siman

Innovations that distinguish the Alter Rebbe on siman רצ"ו.

חידוש א — cosmic order of the 4 berachos (296:1-4)

Chiddush 1 — cosmic order of the 4 berachos (296:1-4)

Wine → spice → flame → separation

Classic position: —

The Rav's chiddush: the Alter Rebbe articulates the cosmic order: wine (joy), spice (consolation for the end of Shabbos), flame (creation of fire permitted again), separation (Mavdil between kodesh and chol).

Practical consequence: tool for the chossid: kavanah on each berachah according to its dimension.

חידוש ב — braided candle as principle (296:10-14)

Chiddush 2 — braided candle as principle (296:10-14)

Multiple wicks for the plural

Classic position: —

The Rav's chiddush: the Alter Rebbe articulates: the braided candle (multiple wicks) answers to the plural of the berachah. It is structural, not decorative.

Practical consequence: tool: have a dedicated braided havdalah candle.

For the Chabad chossid — Siman רצ"ו

  • ① 4 berachos in order: wine → besamim → flame → Mavdil. The Rav's chiddush א. Reference: Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch רצ"ו:1-4.
  • ② Braided candle (multiple wicks) for the flame. The Rav's chiddush ב. Reference: Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch רצ"ו:10-14.
  • ③ Full overflowing cup (abundant blessing). Reference: Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch רצ"ו:15-19.
  • ④ Look at one's nails by the flame (use the light). Reference: Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch רצ"ו:15-19.

⚠ For any concrete question: consult your Rav.
