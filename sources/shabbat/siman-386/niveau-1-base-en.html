1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שפ״ו contains 9 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo); in the text Sefaria serves (Maginei Eretz edition), the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) has glosses introduced by the word הגה in seifim ב and ג, and the text also carries parentheses in small type, which this page attributes to no one. Topic of the siman, according to its title: the laws of shituf in the eruv. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe also has 9 seifim for this siman: its numbering is that of Level 4, not that of this page.

Seif א

דיני שיתוף בעירוב. ובו ט סעיפים:
אסרו חכמים לטלטל מן החצירו' למבוי והתירוהו ע״י שיתוף שגובין פת או דבר אחר ממיני מאכל מכל חצר וחצר ונותנים אותו בא' מן החצירו' ואנו רואים כאלו פתוח כל המבוי לאותו חצר ומפני שאינו אלא לשתף החצירו' יכולים ליתנו באויר החצר או בבית שאין לו ארבע על ארבע וא״צ ליתנו בבית רק שיהי' במקום המשתמר הילכך אין נותנין אותו באויר המבוי (וע״ל סי' שס״ו):
Translation: The Sages forbade carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy, and permitted it through shituf (partnership): one collects bread, or something else among the kinds of food, from each and every courtyard, and places it in one of the courtyards; and we regard [it] as though the whole mavoy were open to that courtyard. And because [the shituf] serves only to join the courtyards, one may place it in the open air (avir) of the courtyard, or in a house that is not four [amos] by four, and one need not place it in a house, provided it is in a guarded place (makom hamishtamer); therefore one does not place it in the open air of the mavoy (and see above, siman 366).
What this seif says:
  • The Sages forbade carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy, and permitted it through shituf: אסרו חכמים לטלטל מן החצירו׳ למבוי והתירוהו ע״י שיתוף. According to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק א), because the mavoy counts as a shared domain relative to the courtyards; and just as the eruv of courtyards joins all the houses of the courtyard, the shituf of mavoyos joins all the courtyards of the mavoy, and they become like one courtyard (ס״ק ב).
  • One collects bread or another food from each courtyard, and places it in one of them: שגובין פת או דבר אחר ממיני מאכל מכל חצר וחצר ונותנים אותו בא׳ מן החצירו׳ ואנו רואים כאלו פתוח כל המבוי לאותו חצר. For the eruv of courtyards, bread is required (siman 366); here, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק ג), the shituf joins only the domain of the courtyards, not that of the houses, and a courtyard is not a dwelling: therefore any food suffices.
  • Where to place it: in the open air of the courtyard, or in a house not four by four, provided it is a guarded place: ומפני שאינו אלא לשתף החצירו׳ יכולים ליתנו באויר החצר או בבית שאין לו ארבע על ארבע וא״צ ליתנו בבית רק שיהי׳ במקום המשתמר. A courtyard is called a guarded place (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ה).
  • Not in the open air of the mavoy: הילכך אין נותנין אותו באויר המבוי. The reference to siman 366 is, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ו), to its seif ג, on the eruv of courtyards, which must be placed in a dwelling house.

Seif ב

ישראלים שדרים בג' מקומו' חלוקים ודלתות כל א' מהמקומו' נעולים בלילה והשמש גובה קמח מכולם אינו מועיל להם להשתתף ששיתוף מבוי צריך שיהי' מונח בחצר שבמבוי ואלו שנבדלים זה מזה ורחוקים אין שיתופן א' ואין מבואיהן כא': הגה ואם עירבו במקום אחד אין האחרים שלא עירבו אוסרים עליהם הואיל ואין להם דריסת הרגל זה על זה כי אין כל העיר חשובה כמבוי אחד אע״פ שיושבין בהיקף חומה אחת (ב״י סי' שצ״ב בשם חזה התנופה):
Translation: Jews who live in three separate places, and the doors of each of the places are locked at night, and the shamash collects flour from all of them: it does not help them to join in a shituf, for the shituf of a mavoy must be placed in a courtyard that is in the mavoy; and these, which are set apart from one another and distant, their shituf is not one, and their mavoyos are not as one. Gloss of the Rema: and if they made the eruv in one place, the others, who did not make the eruv, do not forbid them, since they have no right of passage (derisas haregel) over one another; for the whole city is not considered as one mavoy, even though they dwell within the enclosure of one wall [the Rema's references, as printed: ב״י סי׳ שצ״ב בשם חזה התנופה].
What this seif says:
  • Three separate places, their doors locked at night: a joint shituf does not help them: ישראלים שדרים בג׳ מקומו׳ חלוקים ודלתות כל א׳ מהמקומו׳ נעולים בלילה והשמש גובה קמח מכולם אינו מועיל להם להשתתף. According to the Mishnah Berurah, these are three distant mavoyos, separated by a place where carrying is forbidden, because non-Jews live there and their domain was not rented from them (ס״ק ז); they must make the eruv in each place by itself (ס״ק ח).
  • The reason: the shituf of a mavoy must be placed in a courtyard of the mavoy: ששיתוף מבוי צריך שיהי׳ מונח בחצר שבמבוי ואלו שנבדלים זה מזה ורחוקים אין שיתופן א׳ ואין מבואיהן כא. On the scope of the doors locked at night, the commentators differ (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט).
  • Gloss of the Rema: if they made the eruv in one place, the others, having no right of passage, do not forbid them: ואם עירבו במקום אחד אין האחרים שלא עירבו אוסרים עליהם הואיל ואין להם דריסת הרגל זה על זה. For the Mishnah Berurah, this refers to the shituf (ס״ק י); and even if they have the right to pass there, since they have another way out, they are compelled to pass [by the other way] (ס״ק י״א, in the name of the Magen Avraham, with a reference to siman 392 and to the Beis Meir).
  • Gloss of the Rema: the whole city is not as one mavoy, even within the enclosure of one wall: כי אין כל העיר חשובה כמבוי אחד אע״פ שיושבין בהיקף חומה אחת. On these words, the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק י״ג) leaves a difficulty (צ״ע), from siman 363, seif כ״ו: there a walled city is considered a courtyard of many.

Seif ג

ככל משפטי עירובי חצירו' ושיעורו ושאשתו מערבת בשבילו ושמערבין שלא לדעתו אם אין הבית פתוח אלא לחצר א' ושקטן יכול לגבותו ושיכול א' לזכות לכולם (ושאם היו שותפין א״צ לערב) כך דיני שיתוף: הגה ואפי' היה משותף עם שכיניו בסחורה לזה ביין ולזה בשמן אין צריך להשתתף והוא שיהיה בכלי אחד: (טור סי' שס״ו):
Translation: Like all the laws of the eruv of courtyards — its measure; that one's wife makes the eruv for him; that one makes the eruv without his knowledge if the house opens onto one courtyard only; that a minor may collect it; and that one person may confer it on all (and that if they were partners, there is no need to make an eruv) — so are the laws of shituf. Gloss of the Rema: and even if he was a partner with his neighbors in merchandise, with one in wine and with the other in oil, he need not make the shituf; provided that it is in one vessel [the Rema's reference, as printed: טור סי׳ שס״ו].
What this seif says:
  • The laws of the eruv of courtyards apply to the shituf: ככל משפטי עירובי חצירו׳ ושיעורו ושאשתו מערבת בשבילו ושמערבין שלא לדעתו אם אין הבית פתוח אלא לחצר א׳ ושקטן יכול לגבותו ושיכול א׳ לזכות לכולם … כך דיני שיתוף. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק י״ד) refers each of these laws to its place, in simanim 366, 367 and 368.
  • The parenthesis, in small type in the text Sefaria serves: if they were partners, no eruv is needed: ושאם היו שותפין א״צ לערב. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק י״ד) refers it to siman 366, seif יא, in the gloss.
  • Gloss of the Rema: partners in merchandise, in wine and oil, need no shituf, provided it is in one vessel: ואפי׳ היה משותף עם שכיניו בסחורה לזה ביין ולזה בשמן אין צריך להשתתף והוא שיהיה בכלי אחד. Even though it was not placed there for the sake of shituf, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק ט״ו); in two vessels it does not help (ס״ק ט״ז). On this gloss, the commentators differ (section 6).

Seif ד

משתתפין בכל מיני מאכל ואפי' ד' וה' מיני מאכל מצטרפין למזון שני סעודות:
Translation: One makes the shituf with any kind of food; and even four or five kinds of food combine toward [the measure of] food for two meals.
What this seif says:
  • Any kind of food: משתתפין בכל מיני מאכל.
  • Several kinds combine toward food for two meals: ואפי׳ ד׳ וה׳ מיני מאכל מצטרפין למזון שני סעודות. A measure that suffices even for a thousand people, the Mishnah Berurah writes (ס״ק י״ז), referring to siman 368, seif ג.

Seif ה

בכל מיני מאכל משתתפין חוץ מגודגדניו' שהוקשו לזרע ותבלין ופולין יבשים ולא בעלי בצלים שלא גדלו אורך הזרת ולא בכמהין ופטריות ולא בכפניו' ולא בעדשים ולא בחטים ושעורים ולא בירק שהוא בשיל ולא בשיל ולא במים לבדם ולא במלח לבדו אבל אם עירבם יחד משתתפין בהם וי״א דדוקא כשנתן לתוכן שמן וי״א שמערבין בתבלין:
Translation: One makes the shituf with any kind of food, except with gudgedaniyos that have hardened as they went to seed, spices (tavlin) and dry beans; nor with the ba'alei betzalim [the onion leaves: Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ב] that have not grown the length of a zeres; nor with truffles and mushrooms; nor with kafniyos [dates that have not ripened: Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ד]; nor with lentils; nor with wheat and barley; nor with a half-cooked vegetable (bashil velo bashil); nor with water alone; nor with salt alone; but if he mixed them together, one makes the shituf with them. And some say: only when oil was put into them. And some say that one makes the eruv with spices.
What this seif says:
  • Any food, except hardened gudgedaniyos, spices and dry beans: בכל מיני מאכל משתתפין חוץ מגודגדניו׳ שהוקשו לזרע ותבלין ופולין יבשים. Spices are not food (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ט); dry beans are eaten only after cooking, and the food must be fit to eat now (ס״ק כ׳).
  • Neither onion leaves too short, nor truffles and mushrooms, nor kafniyos, nor lentils, nor wheat and barley, nor a half-cooked vegetable: ולא בעלי בצלים שלא גדלו אורך הזרת ולא בכמהין ופטריות ולא בכפניו׳ ולא בעדשים ולא בחטים ושעורים ולא בירק שהוא בשיל ולא בשיל. For cooked truffles and mushrooms, the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ג) reports a contrary view, that of the Biur HaGra. Lentils, wheat and barley are excluded when raw (ס״ק כ״ה); a vegetable fully cooked or fully raw is admitted, for vegetables usually eaten raw (ס״ק כ״ו).
  • Neither water alone nor salt alone; mixed, one may use them: ולא במים לבדם ולא במלח לבדו אבל אם עירבם יחד משתתפין בהם. For the mixture is fit to accompany bread (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ח, in the name of the Beis Yosef).
  • Some say: only if oil was put into them: וי״א דדוקא כשנתן לתוכן שמן. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ט) writes that in practice we hold like the first view.
  • Some say one makes the eruv with spices: וי״א שמערבין בתבלין. On this view, and on pepper, the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ל׳) reports differing views.

Seif ו

כל דבר שרגילים ללפת בו את הפת שיעורו ללפת בו פת הנאכל לשני סעודו' וכל שאין מלפתין בו הפת שיעורו כדי לאכול ממנו ב' סעודו' ובשר חי לא הוי ליפתן וצריך כדי שיאכל ממנו ב' סעודו' אבל צלי הוי ליפתן ושיעורו ללפת בו ב' סעודו' חומץ הוי ליפתן וכן יין מבושל אבל יין חי לא הוי ליפתן ושיעורו שני רביעיו' וכן שיעור שאר משקים:
Translation: Anything with which one is accustomed to accompany bread (lelafes): its measure is enough to accompany the bread eaten in two meals; and anything with which one does not accompany bread: its measure is enough to eat two meals of it. Raw meat is not an accompaniment (lifsan), and one needs enough to eat two meals of it; but roasted meat is an accompaniment, and its measure is enough to accompany two meals. Vinegar is an accompaniment, and so is cooked wine; but uncooked wine (yayin chai) is not an accompaniment, and its measure is two revi'iyos; and so is the measure of other beverages.
What this seif says:
  • What accompanies bread, and what does not: כל דבר שרגילים ללפת בו את הפת שיעורו ללפת בו פת הנאכל לשני סעודו׳ וכל שאין מלפתין בו הפת שיעורו כדי לאכול ממנו ב׳ סעודו. This suffices: the whole meal need not be of that kind (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ל״א).
  • Raw meat and roasted meat: ובשר חי לא הוי ליפתן וצריך כדי שיאכל ממנו ב׳ סעודו׳ אבל צלי הוי ליפתן ושיעורו ללפת בו ב׳ סעודו. On “one needs enough to eat two meals of it”, the Mishnah Berurah refers to the Biur Halachah (ס״ק ל״ג). Even if there are places where roasted meat is eaten without bread, their practice is set aside relative to everyone; and cooked meat has the law of roasted meat (ס״ק ל״ד).
  • Vinegar and cooked wine are an accompaniment: חומץ הוי ליפתן וכן יין מבושל. The measure of vinegar is enough to dip into it the vegetables of two meals, eaten without bread, and the poskim explain that it is a revi'is (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ל״ה); on the measure of cooked wine, it reports views (ס״ק ל״ו).
  • Uncooked wine: two revi'iyos, like other beverages: אבל יין חי לא הוי ליפתן ושיעורו שני רביעיו׳ וכן שיעור שאר משקים. For it is not usual to accompany [bread] with it: one needs the drinking of two meals, which is two revi'iyos (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ל״ז).

Seif ז

משתתפין בשני ביצים אפילו חיים בשני רמונים בחמשה אפרסקים בליטרא ירק בין חי בין מבושל בפולים לחים מלא היד בתפוחי יער מלא הקב:
Translation: One makes the shituf with two eggs, even raw; with two pomegranates; with five peaches (afarsekim); with a litra of vegetables, whether raw or cooked; with fresh beans, a handful; with wild apples, a full kav.
What this seif says:
  • Two eggs, even raw; two pomegranates; five peaches: משתתפין בשני ביצים אפילו חיים בשני רמונים בחמשה אפרסקים. Eggs even raw, because one sometimes makes a meal of them in case of need (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ל״ח); pomegranates and peaches come as dessert, and because of their importance this measure suffices for the dessert of two meals (ס״ק ל״ט).
  • A litra of vegetables; a handful of fresh beans; a full kav of wild apples: בליטרא ירק בין חי בין מבושל בפולים לחים מלא היד בתפוחי יער מלא הקב. On whether this measure applies to all apples, the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק מ״ד) reports differing views.

Seif ח

משתתפין אפי' באוכל שאינו ראוי לו אם ראוי לשום אדם כגון לנזיר ביין ולישראל בתרומה וכן (הנודר) מאוכל או נשבע שלא יאכלנו משתתף בו ויש אומרים דהיינו דוקא כשנדר או נשבע שלא יאכלנו אבל אם נדר או נשבע שלא יהנה ממנו אין משתתפין לו בה (ואם אמר קונם הנאתו או אכילתו עלי לכ״ע אין משתתף בה) (טור):
Translation: One makes the shituf even with food that is not fit for him, if it is fit for someone: for example, for a nazir with wine, and for a Yisrael with terumah; and likewise (one who vows) [to forbid himself] a food, or swears not to eat it, makes the shituf with it. And some say this is only when he vowed or swore not to eat it; but if he vowed or swore not to benefit from it, one does not make the shituf for him with it (and if he said, “konam its benefit or its eating upon me”, according to all he does not make the shituf with it) [reference as printed: טור].
What this seif says:
  • Food not fit for him, if fit for someone: משתתפין אפי׳ באוכל שאינו ראוי לו אם ראוי לשום אדם כגון לנזיר ביין ולישראל בתרומה. Now that we are all impure from the dead, the Mishnah Berurah writes (ס״ק מ״ז), pure terumah is fit for no one, and one does not make the eruv with it. It also writes (ס״ק מ״ו) that this seif and the preceding ones apply to the eruv of boundaries (eruvei techumin) as well, not to the eruv of courtyards, which is made only with bread.
  • One who forbade himself a food by vow, or swore not to eat it, makes the shituf with it: וכן (הנודר) מאוכל או נשבע שלא יאכלנו משתתף בו. Since it is fit for others (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק מ״ח).
  • Some say: not if he forbade himself to benefit from it: ויש אומרים דהיינו דוקא כשנדר או נשבע שלא יאכלנו אבל אם נדר או נשבע שלא יהנה ממנו אין משתתפין לו בה. The reason, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק מ״ט): he benefits through this arrangement; and this applies to the shituf and the eruv of courtyards, made for a permitted purpose, not to the eruv of boundaries, made only for a mitzvah. On what the first view permits when he forbade himself benefit, it reports differing views.
  • The parenthesis, in small type: “konam”: ואם אמר קונם הנאתו או אכילתו עלי לכ״ע אין משתתף בה. On this parenthesis, the commentators differ (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ׳, which refers to the Biur Halachah).

Seif ט

אמר על ככר היום חול ולמחר קודש או קונם משתתפין לו בה שבין השמשו' עדיין לא נתקדש' ודאי וראוי היתה מבעוד יום אבל אם אמר היום קונם ולמחר חול אין משתתפין לו בה שאינה ראויה עד שתחשך חצר הפתוח לשני מבואו' ושיתף עם כל א' מהם מותר עם כל א' מהם לטלטל ממנו לחצר ומהחצר לתוכו ואסור לטלטל כלים ששבתו במבוי זה למבוי האחר דרך החצר ואם לא שיתף עם שום א' מהם אם הוא רגיל עם שניהם לצאת ולבא בחול דרך עליהם אוסר על שניהם ואם הוא רגיל עם הא' ועם השני אינו רגיל אותו שרגיל אוסר שאינו רגיל אינו אוסר ואם שיתף עם אותו שאינו רגיל הותר הרגיל לעצמו אם שיתפו ביניהם וכן אם היה רגיל עם שניהם ושיתף עם א' מהם מותר השני שהרי סילק עצמו ממנו ואם המבוי שרגיל בו שיתפו ביניהם ואותו שאינו רגיל לא שיתפו והוא לא שיתף לא עם זה ולא עם זה דוחים אותו אצל שאינו רגיל ויסתלק מאותו שרגיל כדי שיהא מותר כיון שהוא אינו מפסיד בדבר שהרי לא שיתף עמהם ויש ריוח לאחרים שע״י זה יהיו מותרים כופין אותו על מדת סדום:
Translation: If he said of a loaf, “today ordinary, and tomorrow consecrated”, or “konam”, one makes the shituf for him with it, for at twilight (bein hashmashos) it was not yet consecrated with certainty, and it was fit while it was still day; but if he said, “today konam, and tomorrow ordinary”, one does not make the shituf for him with it, for it is not fit until it gets dark. A courtyard open to two mavoyos, which made the shituf with each of them: it is permitted, with each of them, to carry from it into the courtyard and from the courtyard into it; and it is forbidden to carry objects that began Shabbos (shavsu) in this mavoy into the other mavoy by way of the courtyard. And if [the courtyard] made the shituf with neither of them: if it is accustomed, with both, to go out and come in on weekdays by way of them, it forbids both; and if it is accustomed to [pass by] one and not accustomed to [pass by] the other, the one it is accustomed to, it forbids; the one it is not accustomed to, it does not forbid. And if it made the shituf with the one it is not accustomed to, the one it is accustomed to is permitted for itself, if they made the shituf among themselves; and likewise, if it was accustomed to both and made the shituf with one of them, the second is permitted, for it has withdrawn from it. And if [the residents of] the mavoy it is accustomed to made the shituf among themselves, and [those of] the one it is not accustomed to did not, and it made the shituf with neither: one pushes it toward the one it is not accustomed to, and it withdraws from the one it is accustomed to, so that [that mavoy] may be permitted; since it loses nothing thereby — for it did not make the shituf with them — and there is gain for the others, who through this will be permitted, one compels it [to do so, for refusing would be] the trait of Sodom (kofin al middas Sedom).
What this seif says:
  • A loaf “today ordinary, tomorrow consecrated”, or “konam”: one makes the shituf with it: אמר על ככר היום חול ולמחר קודש או קונם משתתפין לו בה שבין השמשו׳ עדיין לא נתקדש׳ ודאי וראוי היתה מבעוד יום. For the essential taking effect of the shituf is at bein hashmashos, and out of doubt sanctity does not descend upon it (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״א).
  • “Today konam, tomorrow ordinary”: one does not make the shituf with it: אבל אם אמר היום קונם ולמחר חול אין משתתפין לו בה שאינה ראויה עד שתחשך. Until full night: out of doubt the sanctity does not lapse, and one does not make the eruv with consecrated things (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״ב).
  • The courtyard open to two mavoyos, joined with each: חצר הפתוח לשני מבואו׳ ושיתף עם כל א׳ מהם מותר עם כל א׳ מהם לטלטל ממנו לחצר ומהחצר לתוכו ואסור לטלטל כלים ששבתו במבוי זה למבוי האחר דרך החצר. For the mavoyos did not make the shituf together; if both mavoyos placed their shituf in this courtyard in one vessel, they are all as one and it is permitted (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״ג, referring to siman 378). This refers to objects that began Shabbos in the houses of the mavoy; those that began it in the mavoy itself are permitted (ס״ק נ״ד).
  • Joined with neither: it forbids the one or ones it is accustomed to pass through: ואם לא שיתף עם שום א׳ מהם אם הוא רגיל עם שניהם לצאת ולבא בחול דרך עליהם אוסר על שניהם ואם הוא רגיל עם הא׳ ועם השני אינו רגיל אותו שרגיל אוסר שאינו רגיל אינו אוסר. As we hold that a courtyard which did not join the mavoy forbids the whole mavoy (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״ה).
  • Joined with one: it has withdrawn from the other: ואם שיתף עם אותו שאינו רגיל הותר הרגיל לעצמו אם שיתפו ביניהם וכן אם היה רגיל עם שניהם ושיתף עם א׳ מהם מותר השני שהרי סילק עצמו ממנו. It does not forbid, for by joining the second it withdrew from the one it was accustomed to (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״ו).
  • It is compelled to withdraw from the mavoy that made the shituf: ואם המבוי שרגיל בו שיתפו ביניהם ואותו שאינו רגיל לא שיתפו והוא לא שיתף לא עם זה ולא עם זה דוחים אותו אצל שאינו רגיל ויסתלק מאותו שרגיל כדי שיהא מותר כיון שהוא אינו מפסיד בדבר שהרי לא שיתף עמהם ויש ריוח לאחרים שע״י זה יהיו מותרים כופין אותו על מדת סדום. So that it does not pass through it on Shabbos (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״ח); it is compelled to pass on Shabbos through the mavoy that did not make the shituf, even though it is not accustomed to it (ס״ק נ״ט).

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

The eruv of courtyards (eruvei chatzeiros) joins the houses of a courtyard; the shituf of mavoyos likewise joins the courtyards of a mavoy, which become like one courtyard (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב, referring to the beginning of siman 366). Siman שפ״ו gives its laws: the prohibition and its permission through the shituf, and where it is placed (seif א); a case where it does not help (seif ב); the laws of the eruv of courtyards that apply to it (seif ג); with which foods and in what measure it is made (seifim ד to ח, and the beginning of seif ט); and finally the courtyard open to two mavoyos (seif ט).

The fundamental question: how does one permit carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy onto which they open? Through the shituf: a food, bread or other, collected from each courtyard and placed in one of them, in a guarded place (seif א), according to the laws of the eruv of courtyards (seif ג) — unlike that eruv, which requires bread (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג).

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. Siman שפ״ו follows siman שפ״ה · 385, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דין צדוקי ומומר בעירוב » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ה:א), and precedes siman שפ״ז · 387, whose title, at the head of its single seif, is « שותפין במבוי צריכין לערב בחצירות » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ז:א). Seif א itself refers to siman 366.

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman:

The essential notions of siman שפ״ו:
  • מבוי — the mavoy: the alley onto which several courtyards open. Why carrying into it from the courtyard is forbidden: « מפני שהמבוי חשיב רשות משותפת כנגד החצרות שאינם מיוחדים רק לאנשי אותו החצר וגזרו אטו מר״ה לרה״י » (משנה ברורה שפ״ו ס״ק א) — because the mavoy counts as a shared domain relative to the courtyards, which are designated only for the people of each courtyard; and [the Sages] decreed on account of [carrying] from the public domain into the private domain.
  • שיתוף מבואות — the shituf of mavoyos: « כך שיתופי מבואות משתף כל חצרי המבוי יחד ונעשים כחצר אחד » (משנה ברורה שפ״ו ס״ק ב) — it joins all the courtyards of the mavoy, which become like one courtyard.
  • פת או דבר אחר — bread or another food (seif א): « משא״כ שיתופי מבואות דאינו אלא לשתף רשות החצרות שבמבוי לא רשות הבתים וחצר לאו בית דירה הוא הלכך סגי בכל דבר מאכל » (משנה ברורה שפ״ו ס״ק ג) — the shituf joins only the domain of the courtyards, not that of the houses, and a courtyard is not a dwelling; therefore any food suffices.
  • מקום המשתמר — the guarded place, where the shituf is placed (seif א): « וחצר מקרי מקום המשתמר » (משנה ברורה שפ״ו ס״ק ה).
  • דריסת הרגל — the right of passage: in the Rema's gloss, the others do not forbid « הואיל ואין להם דריסת הרגל זה על זה » (רמ״א או״ח שפ״ו:ב).
  • מזון שתי סעודות — food for two meals, the measure of the shituf (seif ד): « שהוא שיעור הראוי אף לאלף בני אדם » (משנה ברורה שפ״ו ס״ק י״ז).
  • ליפתן — the accompaniment of bread (seif ו): « כל דבר שרגילים ללפת בו את הפת שיעורו ללפת בו פת הנאכל לשני סעודו׳ וכל שאין מלפתין בו הפת שיעורו כדי לאכול ממנו ב׳ סעודו » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:ו) — what accompanies bread is measured by the bread of two meals; anything else, by two meals of that thing.
  • מדת סדום — the trait of Sodom: in seif ט, since the courtyard loses nothing and the others gain, « כופין אותו על מדת סדום » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:ט).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אThe Sages forbade carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy, and permitted it through shituf: bread, or another food, collected from each courtyard and placed in one of them. One may place it in the open air of the courtyard, or in a house that is not four [amos] by four, provided it is a guarded place; not in the open air of the mavoy.
בJews who live in three separate places, whose doors are locked at night, and whose shamash collects the flour: the joint shituf does not help them, for the shituf of a mavoy must be placed in a courtyard of the mavoy. Rema: if they made the eruv in one place, the others, who have no right of passage over one another, do not forbid them, even if they dwell within the enclosure of one wall.
גThe laws of the eruv of courtyards apply to the shituf: its measure; the wife who makes it for her husband; that one makes it without his knowledge, if the house opens onto one courtyard only; the minor who collects it; one person who confers it on all. Rema: one who is a partner with his neighbors in merchandise, with one in wine and with the other in oil, need not make the shituf, provided it is in one vessel.
דOne makes the shituf with any kind of food; several kinds combine toward food for two meals.
הOne does not make it with gudgedaniyos that have hardened as they went to seed, spices, dry beans, onion leaves that have not grown a zeres, truffles and mushrooms, kafniyos, lentils, wheat and barley, a half-cooked vegetable, water alone or salt alone; water and salt mixed are admitted — some say, only with oil; and some say one makes the eruv with spices.
וThe measures: what accompanies bread, enough to accompany the bread of two meals; what does not accompany it, enough to eat two meals of it. Raw meat is not an accompaniment, roasted meat is; vinegar and cooked wine are; uncooked wine is not, and its measure is two revi'iyos, as is that of other beverages.
זTwo eggs, even raw; two pomegranates; five peaches; a litra of vegetables, raw or cooked; a handful of fresh beans; a full kav of wild apples.
חOne makes the shituf with food not fit for him, if it is fit for someone (wine for the nazir, terumah for the Yisrael); one who forbade himself a food by vow, or swore not to eat it, makes the shituf with it; some say, not if he vowed or swore not to benefit from it; and, in a parenthesis, if he said “konam”, according to all he does not.
טA loaf of which he said “today ordinary, tomorrow consecrated”, or “konam”, serves for the shituf; “today konam, tomorrow ordinary”, does not. A courtyard open to two mavoyos: joined with both, it carries with each, but it is forbidden to carry from one mavoy to the other, through the courtyard, objects that began Shabbos in one of them; joined with neither, it forbids the one or ones through which it is accustomed to go out and come in on weekdays; joined with the one it is not accustomed to, the other is permitted if they made the shituf among themselves; if it was accustomed to both and joins one, the second is permitted; and if the mavoy it is accustomed to made the shituf, and not the other, and it joined neither, it is compelled to withdraw from the first, since it loses nothing and the others gain.
The heart of the siman: the shituf permits carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy: bread or another food, collected from each courtyard and placed in a guarded place (seif א), according to the laws of the eruv of courtyards (seif ג). It does not help for separate and distant places, with doors locked at night (seif ב). The siman sets its foods and measures (seifim ד to ח, and the beginning of seif ט), and rules on the courtyard open to two mavoyos (seif ט).

6. Position of the Rema

In the text Sefaria serves, the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) has a gloss introduced by the word הגה in seif ב and one in seif ג. The text also carries, in small type, parentheses which this page does not attribute to him; two of them, in seifim ג and ח, are read below.

The glosses of the Rema, and two parentheses of the text:
  • Seif ב, the gloss — if they made the eruv in one place, the others, who did not make the eruv, do not forbid them, since they have no right of passage over one another; the whole city is not one mavoy, even within the enclosure of one wall. The Mishnah Berurah reads it as referring to the shituf (ס״ק י); on the right of passage, it writes, in the name of the Magen Avraham, that since they have another way out, they are compelled to pass [by the other way] (ס״ק י״א); and on the wall, it leaves a difficulty (ס״ק י״ג).
  • Seif ג, the parenthesis — « ושאם היו שותפין א״צ לערב » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:ג): the Mishnah Berurah refers it to siman 366, seif יא, in the gloss (ס״ק י״ד).
  • Seif ג, the gloss — one who is a partner with his neighbors in merchandise, with one in wine and with the other in oil, need not make the shituf, provided it is in one vessel. The Mishnah Berurah writes: « ודע דהרמ״א נמשך בזה אחר דעת הטור אבל רוב הפוסקים חולקים על זה ודעתם דביין ושמן שהם שני מינים שאין ראויין להתערב יחד אפילו כשהיו מעורבין בכלי אחד ג״כ אין סומכין עליהן לענין שיתוף ועיין בבה״ל » (משנה ברורה שפ״ו ס״ק ט״ז) — the Rema follows in this the view of the Tur, but most poskim dispute it: for wine and oil, two kinds that do not mix, even when mixed in one vessel, one does not rely on them for the shituf; and it refers to the Biur Halachah. Consult your Rav.
  • Seif ח, the “konam” parenthesis — if he said “konam its benefit or its eating upon me”, according to all he does not make the shituf with it; on these words, the commentators differ (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ׳, which refers to the Biur Halachah). Consult your Rav.
  • Chabad → Level 4 — Daat HaRav studies this siman in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שפ״ו, under its own numbering, in 9 seifim.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The lessons of Siman שפ״ו:
  1. The shituf: the Sages forbade carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy, and permitted it through shituf — bread or another food, collected from each courtyard and placed in one of them, in a guarded place, not in the open air of the mavoy (seif א).
  2. Separate and distant places, with doors locked at night: the joint shituf does not help; and according to the Rema's gloss, if they made the eruv in one place, the others, having no right of passage, do not forbid them (seif ב).
  3. The laws of the eruv of courtyards apply to the shituf; and according to the Rema's gloss, a partner in merchandise, in wine and in oil, need not make the shituf, provided it is in one vessel; on this gloss, see section 6 (seif ג).
  4. Any food, and several kinds combine toward food for two meals (seif ד).
  5. The exclusions, among them water alone and salt alone — mixed, they are admitted (seif ה).
  6. The measures: according to whether the thing accompanies bread or not; uncooked wine and other beverages, two revi'iyos (seif ו); and measures set for certain foods (seif ז).
  7. Food not fit for him, if it is fit for someone; and food he forbade himself by vow or oath, according to what he forbade himself (seif ח).
  8. The loaf consecrated from tomorrow serves; the one that becomes ordinary only from tomorrow does not (seif ט).
  9. The courtyard open to two mavoyos: according to whether it joined one, both or neither, and according to its passage habits (seif ט).
  10. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Why did the Sages forbid carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק א), and how does the shituf permit it (seif א; ס״ק ב)?
  2. Why is the shituf made with any food, whereas the eruv of courtyards requires bread (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג)?
  3. Why may the shituf be placed in the open air of the courtyard, but not in that of the mavoy (seif א; ס״ק ה)?
  4. In seif ב, why does the joint shituf not help the three separate places?
  5. In seif ה, why are water alone and salt alone excluded, and why is their mixture admitted (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ז and כ״ח)?
  6. In seif ט, why does the loaf “today ordinary, tomorrow consecrated” serve, and not the loaf “today konam, tomorrow ordinary” (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק נ״א and נ״ב)?
  7. In seif ט, why can the courtyard be compelled to withdraw from the mavoy that made the shituf?
