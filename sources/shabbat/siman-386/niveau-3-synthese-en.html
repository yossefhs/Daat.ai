The Shulchan Aruch HaRav, studied in Level 4, also has 9 seifim, but does not divide them the same way (Level 4, introduction): when it is cited, its name is given next to the number.

1. The central axiom

Siman שפ״ו in one sentence.
The Sages forbade carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy, and permitted it through the shituf: « אסרו חכמים לטלטל מן החצירו׳ למבוי והתירוהו ע״י שיתוף שגובין פת או דבר אחר ממיני מאכל מכל חצר וחצר ונותנים אותו בא׳ מן החצירו׳ » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:א). The siman says where it is placed (seif א), where it does not help (seif ב), that it follows the laws of the eruv of courtyards (seif ג), with which foods and in what measure it is made (seifim ד to ח, and the beginning of seif ט), and how the courtyard open to two mavoyos is ruled (seif ט).

3. Structure of the siman — from the prohibition to the courtyard open to two mavoyos

Seif א — the prohibition and the shituf: the Sages forbade carrying from the courtyards into the mavoy, and permitted it through the shituf; it is placed in one of the courtyards, in a guarded place, not in the open air of the mavoy.
↓
Seif ב — separate places: Jews who live in three separate places, whose doors are locked at night, and whose shamash collects the flour: the joint shituf does not help them, for the shituf of a mavoy must be placed in a courtyard of the mavoy. The Rema's gloss: if they made the eruv in one place, the others, who have no right of passage over one another, do not forbid them. On the scope of the locked doors, see section 5.
↓
Seif ג — the laws of the eruv of courtyards apply to the shituf. The Rema's gloss: one who is a partner with his neighbors in merchandise, with one in wine and with the other in oil, need not make the shituf, provided it is in one vessel; on this case, see section 5.
↓
Seif ד — any food: several kinds combine toward food for two meals.
↓
Seif ה — the exclusions, among them water alone and salt alone; mixed, they are admitted — according to one « יש אומרים », only if oil was put in; and according to another « יש אומרים », one makes the eruv with spices.
↓
Seif ו — the measures: what accompanies bread, enough to accompany the bread of two meals; what does not accompany it, enough to eat two meals of it. Raw meat is not an accompaniment; roasted meat, vinegar and cooked wine are; uncooked wine, two revi'iyos, like other beverages. On the measure of cooked wine, see section 5.
↓
Seif ז — fixed measures: two eggs, even raw; two pomegranates; five peaches; a litra of vegetables, raw or cooked; a handful of fresh beans; a full kav of wild apples.
↓
Seif ח — food not fit for him, if it is fit for someone (today, pure terumah is fit for no one: Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק מ״ז; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 386:8); food he forbade himself by vow or by oath: see section 5.
↓
Seif ט — the loaf, then the courtyard open to two mavoyos: a loaf of which he said “today ordinary, tomorrow consecrated”, or “konam”, serves for the shituf; “today konam, tomorrow ordinary” does not. The courtyard open to two mavoyos is ruled according to whether it joined both, one or neither, and according to its habits of passage.

4. Decision tree — where, with what, how much?

Q1: Where is the shituf placed? (seifim א and ב)
↓ In one of the courtyards of the mavoy, in a guarded place — its open air, or a house that is not four [amos] by four (on the house, section 5) → Q2. In the open air of the mavoy → it is not placed there (seif א). Separate and distant places, whose doors are locked at night, from which a single shituf was collected → it does not help them (seif ב; on the scope of the locked doors, section 5).
Q2: With what, and in what measure? (seifim ד to ח, and the beginning of seif ט)
↓ Any food, several kinds combining toward food for two meals (seif ד); except what seif ה excludes, where water and salt are admitted only mixed (on oil and spices, section 5). The measures: according to whether the thing accompanies bread or not, and two revi'iyos for uncooked wine and other beverages (seif ו; on cooked wine, section 5); the fixed measures of seif ז. Food not fit for him, if it is fit for someone (seif ח); food he forbade himself by vow or by oath: section 5. A loaf “today ordinary, tomorrow consecrated”, or “konam”, serves; “today konam, tomorrow ordinary” does not (seif ט).
Q3: Does the courtyard open to two mavoyos? (seif ט)
↓ Joined with both → it carries with each, but it is forbidden to carry, through the courtyard, from one mavoy to the other objects that began Shabbos in one of them; if the two mavoyos placed their shituf in this courtyard in one vessel, it is permitted, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק נ״ג), as does the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (386:9). Joined with neither → it forbids the one or ones through which it is accustomed to go out and come in on weekdays. Joined with one → the seif says when the other is permitted. The mavoy it is accustomed to made the shituf, and not the other, and it joined neither → it is compelled to withdraw from the first.
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic — one marker per seif

A word taken from the text of each seif, in order (two for seif ט):

א — שיתוף: the Sages permitted through the shituf; in a courtyard, in a guarded place, not in the open air of the mavoy.

ב — חלוקים: separate places, with doors locked at night: the joint shituf does not help them (on the scope of the locked doors, section 5).

ג — משפטי: the laws of the eruv of courtyards apply to the shituf.

ד — מצטרפין: several kinds combine toward food for two meals.

ה — חוץ: except what the seif excludes; water and salt, only mixed (on oil and spices, section 5).

ו — ללפת: the measure, according to whether the thing accompanies bread or not.

ז — ביצים: two eggs, even raw, and the other fixed measures of the seif.

ח — שאינו ראוי: food not fit for him, if it is fit for someone.

ט — ככר, then חצר: the loaf consecrated only from the next day serves; then the courtyard open to two mavoyos.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Thinking that the shituf may be placed in the open air of the mavoy. Seif א: « רק שיהי׳ במקום המשתמר הילכך אין נותנין אותו באויר המבוי » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:א).
Pitfall 2: Thinking that the shituf requires bread, like the eruv of courtyards. Seif א: « שגובין פת או דבר אחר ממיני מאכל מכל חצר וחצר » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:א); seif ד: « משתתפין בכל מיני מאכל » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:ד). What the shituf does not admit is in seif ה.
Pitfall 3: Thinking that water alone, or salt alone, is enough. Seif ה: « ולא במים לבדם ולא במלח לבדו אבל אם עירבם יחד משתתפין בהם וי״א דדוקא כשנתן לתוכן שמן וי״א שמערבין בתבלין » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:ה). On the views cited at the end of the seif, see section 5.
Pitfall 4: Thinking that a loaf he declared consecrated never serves for the shituf. Seif ט: « אמר על ככר היום חול ולמחר קודש או קונם משתתפין לו בה » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:ט); but « אבל אם אמר היום קונם ולמחר חול אין משתתפין לו בה שאינה ראויה עד שתחשך » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:ט).
Pitfall 5: Thinking that a courtyard joined with both mavoyos unites the two mavoyos. Seif ט: « ואסור לטלטל כלים ששבתו במבוי זה למבוי האחר דרך החצר » (שו״ע או״ח שפ״ו:ט). If the two mavoyos placed their shituf in this courtyard in one vessel, it is permitted, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק נ״ג), as does the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (386:9).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שפ״ו

For daily conduct

  1. The shituf: bread or another food, collected from each courtyard and placed in one of them, in a guarded place; not in the open air of the mavoy (seif א). On placing it in a house, see section 5.
  2. Separate places, with doors locked at night: the joint shituf does not help them (seif ב). On the scope of the locked doors, see section 5.
  3. The laws of the eruv of courtyards apply to the shituf (seif ג). For a partner in a business of wine and oil, see section 5.
  4. With what: any food, several kinds combining toward food for two meals (seif ד); except what seif ה excludes, where water and salt are admitted only mixed (seif ה; on oil and spices, see section 5).
  5. The measures: those of seif ו (on cooked wine, see section 5) and those of seif ז.
  6. Food not fit for him, if it is fit for someone (seif ח). For food he forbade himself by vow or by oath, see section 5.
  7. The loaf “today ordinary, tomorrow consecrated”, or “konam”, serves; “today konam, tomorrow ordinary” does not (seif ט).
  8. The courtyard open to two mavoyos: according to whether it joined both, one or neither, and according to its habits of passage (seif ט).
  9. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
