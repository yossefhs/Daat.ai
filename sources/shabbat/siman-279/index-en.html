Siman 279 — Moving the Shabbos Light (Muktzeh)

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe on the 9 seifim codifying the status of the Shabbos lamp: muktzeh according to its state (lit vs. extinguished), the menorah and its base, moving the tray. The conceptual framework of kli shemelachto le-issur and basis le-muktzeh.
