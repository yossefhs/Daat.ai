The seif numbers on this page are those of the Shulchan Aruch of the Mechaber (2 seifim, with a gloss of the Rema, introduced by הגה, in seif א). The Shulchan Aruch HaRav, studied in Level 4, writes this siman in 3 seifim: when it is cited, its name is given next to the number.

1. The central axiom

Siman שצ״א in one sentence.
What counts as one. In the mavoy, a courtyard that forgot and did not join renounces its domain to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it, for « שבני החצר גבי מבוי חשובי׳ כיחיד בחצר » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א) — the residents of a courtyard, with respect to the mavoy, count as an individual in a courtyard. In the mavoy or in a city walled for dwelling, it is not forbidden to carry until there are in the city two courtyards of Jewish houses; with two, they must rent from each and every courtyard of the non-Jew, and the seif says when renting from the lord of the city is not enough, and when it is effective (seif א; on the gloss of the Rema, section 5). In a walled city of non-Jews where Jews live in a courtyard, alone, Jews passing through who lodge on Shabbos in another courtyard do not forbid, for « דאורח אינו אוסר » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:ב); on the guests themselves, and on the guest who regularly lodges there, section 5.

3. The course of the seifim — from renunciation in the mavoy to Jews passing through

Seif א, opening — renunciation in the mavoy: the laws of renunciation for one who forgot the eruv in his courtyard apply to the mavoy; a courtyard that forgot and did not join renounces to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it, "even though they are many" (section 5), for the residents of a courtyard, with respect to the mavoy, count as an individual in a courtyard.
↓
Seif א, continued — the non-Jew in the mavoy or the city: like the laws of a Jew in a courtyard with a non-Jew; it is not forbidden to carry in the mavoy, or in the walled city, until there are in the city two courtyards of Jewish houses; one courtyard, no, even if many Jewish houses open into it.
↓
Seif א, the condition: that the city be walled for dwelling; ordinary towns are, ordinary fortresses are not.
↓
Seif א, two Jewish courtyards: they must rent from each and every courtyard of the non-Jew; renting from the lord of the city is not enough.
↓
Gloss of the Rema, introduced by הגה: some say ("ויש אומרים") that this is only for carrying [things] out and in to the domain of the non-Jew; to carry in the mavoy, one may rent from the lord (section 5).
↓
Seif א, end, in ordinary type — "במה דברי׳ אמורי׳": a lord whose houses are not his and who has no right at all to use the houses of the city, even in wartime; but where all the needs of the city are done only by order of the lord or his appointee, renting from him, or from his hired worker and laborer, is effective.
↓
Seif ב — Jews passing through: in a walled city of non-Jews, Jews live in a courtyard, alone; other Jews, who passed through there on Shabbos, lodge in another courtyard: they do not forbid, for a guest does not forbid, and they are permitted to carry in the whole city (section 5). A parenthesis in small print, without הגה, refers to the end of siman 370.

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes this siman in 3 seifim: seif א of the Mechaber, except for the opinion the gloss of the Rema reports, is carried by seifim 1 and 2 of the Rav, and seif ב by seif 3 of the Rav (Level 4). Level 4 sets the two texts side by side, point by point: the courtyard counted as one dwelling with respect to the mavoy (point א); renting from the lord, where seif 2 of the Rav does not reproduce the opinion the gloss of the Rema reports (point ב); the appointee, and anyone who has the right to deposit his things with the non-Jew (point ג); the guests (point ד); the other Jew who does not lodge in the courtyard where a single Jew lives with non-Jews, a case seif ב of the Mechaber does not write (point ה).

4. Decision tree — in a walled city, must one rent the non-Jew's domain, and from whom?

Q1: Is the city walled for dwelling? (seif א)
↓ An ordinary town is → Q2. An ordinary fortress is not → the condition of the seif is not met: consult your Rav.
Q2: How many courtyards of Jewish houses are there in the city? (seif א)
↓ One, even if many Jewish houses open into it → it is not forbidden to carry (seif א). Two → Q3.
Q3: From whom is the non-Jew's domain rented? (seif א)
↓ From each and every courtyard of the non-Jew → this is what the seif requires (seif א). From the lord of the city → the seif writes that this is not enough (seif א); the gloss of the Rema reports an opinion for carrying in the mavoy, and the commentators diverge (section 5); then the seif specifies which lord it means → Q4.
Q4: What power does the lord have over the houses of the city? (seif א)
↓ The houses are not his, and he has no right at all to use the houses of the city, even in wartime → renting from him is not enough (seif א). All the needs of the city are done only by his order or that of his appointee → renting from him, or from his hired worker and laborer, is effective (seif א).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

6. Mnemonic ב-ח-ש-א

Four words of the seifim, in their order:

ב — ביטול: in the mavoy, the courtyard that did not join renounces to the other residents, or they to it (seif א).

ח — חומה: in the walled city — walled for dwelling —, a single Jewish courtyard does not forbid (seif א).

ש — שני: with two Jewish courtyards, rent from each and every courtyard of the non-Jew; the lord, according to his power over the houses of the city (seif א).

א — אורח: Jews passing through, in the walled city of non-Jews, do not forbid, for a guest does not forbid (seif ב; section 5).

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Mixing the two numberings. The Mechaber writes this siman in 2 seifim, the Shulchan Aruch HaRav in 3: seif א of the Mechaber, except for the opinion the gloss of the Rema reports, is carried by seifim 1 and 2 of the Rav, and seif ב of the Mechaber by seif 3 of the Rav (Level 4).
Pitfall 2: Counting houses instead of courtyards. In a city walled for dwelling, a single Jewish courtyard does not forbid, « אפילו אם הרבה בתי׳ של ישראל פתוחי׳ לתוכה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א) — even if many Jewish houses open into it.
Pitfall 3: Forgetting the condition of the single-courtyard rule: « והוא שתהא העיר מוקפת חומה לדירה וסתם עיירות הן מוקפות לדירה וסתם מבצרי׳ אינם מוקפי׳ לדירה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א).
Pitfall 4: Presenting the gloss of the Rema as the settled rule. It reports an opinion, introduced by "ויש אומרים", and the commentators diverge on this gloss (section 5). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 5: Reading "renting from the lord of the city is not enough" as a rule without limit. The seif continues: « במה דברי׳ אמורי׳ בשר שאין הבתי׳ שלו וגם אין לו רשות להשתמש בבתי בני העיר כלל אפי׳ בשעת מלחמה אבל במקו׳ שכל צרכי העיר אינם נעשי׳ אלא על פי השר או הממונה שלו ודאי ששכירו׳ מהשר ההוא או משכירו ולקיטו מהני » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א).
Pitfall 6: Thinking that "ומותרי׳ לטלטל בכל העיר" settles everything for the guests. On the guests themselves, and on the guest who regularly lodges there, the commentators diverge (section 5); the Rav's text writes that these guests do not forbid in the city until after thirty days, and reports, under "יש אומרים", that they may not carry from their courtyard to the mavoy (Level 4, point ד). Consult your Rav.

10. The practical commandments of Siman שצ״א

For daily conduct

  1. The courtyard that forgot and did not join the shituf of the mavoy renounces its domain to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it (seif א; on the words "אע״פ שהם רבי׳", section 5).
  2. A single Jewish courtyard, even of many houses, does not forbid carrying in the mavoy or in the walled city — provided the city is walled for dwelling; with two Jewish courtyards, they must rent from each and every courtyard of the non-Jew (seif א).
  3. The lord of the city: with two Jewish courtyards, renting from him is not enough (seif א); on the gloss of the Rema, which reports an opinion for carrying in the mavoy, the commentators diverge (section 5); then the seif specifies that it speaks of a lord whose houses are not his and who has no right at all to use the houses of the city, even in wartime, and that where all the needs of the city are done only by his order or that of his appointee, renting from him, or from his hired worker and laborer, is effective (seif א). Consult your Rav.
  4. Jews passing through who lodge on Shabbos in another courtyard, in a walled city of non-Jews where Jews live in a courtyard, alone, do not forbid, for a guest does not forbid (seif ב); the Rav's text writes that they do not forbid in the city until after thirty days, and reports, under "יש אומרים", that they may not carry from their courtyard to the mavoy (391:3; Level 4, point ד). On the guests themselves, and on the guest who regularly lodges there, section 5: consult your Rav.
  5. The other Jew who does not lodge in the courtyard where a single Jew lives with non-Jews: the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes that he may carry out and in [things] from the houses of the non-Jew or from the house of the Jew to the courtyard (391:3; Level 4, point ה).
  6. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
