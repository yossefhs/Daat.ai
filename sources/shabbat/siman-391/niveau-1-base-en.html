1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שצ״א contains 2 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo); the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) adds a gloss to it, introduced by הגה in seif א. At the end of seif ב, a parenthesis in small print, without הגה, refers to the end of siman 370. Title of the siman: the laws of renunciation of domain for those who forgot to make the eruv. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe has 3 seifim for this siman: its numbering is that of Level 4, not that of this page.

Seif א

דיני ביטול רשות למי ששכחו לערב. ובו ב סעיפים:
ככל משפטי ביטול למי ששכח ולא עירב בחצירו כן היא במבוי שאם שכחה חצר אחת ולא נשתתפה מבטלת רשותה לשאר בני המבוי או הם לה אע״פ שהם רבי' שבני החצר גבי מבוי חשובי' כיחיד בחצר וככל משפטי ישראל בחצר עם העכו״ם כן הוא במבוי או בעיר שאין אסור לטלטל במבוי או בעיר המוקפת חומה עד שיהיו שני חצרות של בתי ישראל בעיר אבל חצר אחת לא אפילו אם הרבה בתי' של ישראל פתוחי' לתוכה והוא שתהא העיר מוקפת חומה לדירה וסתם עיירות הן מוקפות לדירה וסתם מבצרי' אינם מוקפי' לדירה וכשיש ב' חצרו' של בתי ישראל בעיר צריכי' לשכור מכל חצר וחצר של עכו״ם ואין מספיק במה שישכור משר העיר: הגה ויש אומרים דדוקא לענין להוציא ולהכניס לרשות העכו״ם אבל לטלטל במבוי יכול לשכור מן השר שהרי דרך המבוי הוא של השר ויכול לסלק כל העכו״ם משם: (ריב״ש סי' תש״י) במה דברי' אמורי' בשר שאין הבתי' שלו וגם אין לו רשות להשתמש בבתי בני העיר כלל אפי' בשעת מלחמה אבל במקו' שכל צרכי העיר אינם נעשי' אלא על פי השר או הממונה שלו ודאי ששכירו' מהשר ההוא או משכירו ולקיטו מהני שהרי יש לו רשות להושיב אנשיו וכלי מלחמתו בבתי בני העיר בשעת מלחמה שלא מדעתם:
Translation: Like all the laws of renunciation [of domain] for one who forgot and did not make the eruv in his courtyard, so it is in the mavoy: if one courtyard forgot and did not join [in the shituf of the mavoy], it renounces its domain to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it, even though they are many, for the residents of a courtyard, with respect to the mavoy, count as an individual in a courtyard. And like all the laws of a Jew in a courtyard with a non-Jew (akum), so it is in the mavoy or in the city: it is not forbidden to carry in the mavoy, or in the walled city, until there are in the city two courtyards of Jewish houses; but one courtyard, no, even if many Jewish houses open into it; and this, provided the city is walled for dwelling. Ordinary towns are walled for dwelling, and ordinary fortresses are not walled for dwelling. And when there are in the city two courtyards of Jewish houses, they must rent from each and every courtyard of the non-Jew, and it is not enough to rent from the lord of the city (sar ha'ir). Gloss of the Rema: and some say that this is only with regard to carrying [things] out and in to the domain of the non-Jew [that renting from the lord is not enough]; but to carry in the mavoy, one may rent from the lord, for the road of the mavoy belongs to the lord, and he can remove all the non-Jews from there [the Rema's reference, as printed: ריב״ש סי׳ תש״י]. [The text resumes in ordinary type:] Of what are these words said? Of a lord whose houses are not his, and who has no right at all to use the houses of the townspeople, even in wartime; but where all the needs of the city are done only by order of the lord or his appointee, certainly renting from that lord, or from his hired worker and laborer (sechiro u'lekito), is effective, for he has the right to quarter his men and his weapons in the houses of the townspeople in wartime without their consent.
What this seif says:
  • In the mavoy, the laws of renunciation are those of the courtyard: ככל משפטי ביטול למי ששכח ולא עירב בחצירו כן היא במבוי. For these laws, the Mishnah Berurah refers to simanim 380 and 381 (ס״ק א).
  • The courtyard that did not join renounces to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it: שאם שכחה חצר אחת ולא נשתתפה מבטלת רשותה לשאר בני המבוי או הם לה אע״פ שהם רבי׳ שבני החצר גבי מבוי חשובי׳ כיחיד בחצר. The commentators differ on whom the words "even though they are many" refer to (Taz, ס״ק א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב; section 6).
  • As the courtyard where a non-Jew lives, so the mavoy or the city: וככל משפטי ישראל בחצר עם העכו״ם כן הוא במבוי או בעיר. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ד) refers to the beginning of siman 382: the non-Jew does not forbid until there are two Jewish houses in the courtyard; likewise, it is not forbidden to carry out from the courtyard to the mavoy, fitted with a lechi or a beam, until there are two Jewish courtyards in the mavoy.
  • One Jewish courtyard does not forbid, even if many Jewish houses open into it: שאין אסור לטלטל במבוי או בעיר המוקפת חומה עד שיהיו שני חצרות של בתי ישראל בעיר אבל חצר אחת לא אפילו אם הרבה בתי׳ של ישראל פתוחי׳ לתוכה. With two courtyards, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק ו), they forbid each other to carry the household utensils out to the mavoy as long as they have not joined together, and the non-Jew also forbids them: their shituf does not help until they have rented from him his domain in the mavoy.
  • Provided the city is walled for dwelling: והוא שתהא העיר מוקפת חומה לדירה וסתם עיירות הן מוקפות לדירה וסתם מבצרי׳ אינם מוקפי׳ לדירה. Otherwise, writes the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ח), it is forbidden anyway if it is larger than a beis sa'tayim, like the karpef of siman 358; and the ordinary town is walled for dwelling because the houses are built first and the wall is put around them afterwards, whereas the fortress is first surrounded by a wall and the houses are built inside afterwards (ס״ק ט).
  • Two Jewish courtyards in the city: rent from each courtyard of the non-Jew; renting from the lord of the city is not enough: וכשיש ב׳ חצרו׳ של בתי ישראל בעיר צריכי׳ לשכור מכל חצר וחצר של עכו״ם ואין מספיק במה שישכור משר העיר. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק י) writes that it seems to him to be so even if each courtyard is in a different mavoy, since in a walled city all the mavoys count as one — unless the places are separated from each other by a tzuras hapesach or by two posts (it refers to simanim 386 and 392).
  • Gloss of the Rema: some say that to carry in the mavoy one may rent from the lord: ויש אומרים דדוקא לענין להוציא ולהכניס לרשות העכו״ם אבל לטלטל במבוי יכול לשכור מן השר שהרי דרך המבוי הוא של השר ויכול לסלק כל העכו״ם משם. "To carry in the mavoy", writes the Mishnah Berurah, means carrying out of the Jews' houses and into them (ס״ק י״ג). The commentators differ on this gloss (section 6).
  • Which lord is meant: one whose houses are not his and who has no right at all over the houses of the city, even in wartime: במה דברי׳ אמורי׳ בשר שאין הבתי׳ שלו וגם אין לו רשות להשתמש בבתי בני העיר כלל אפי׳ בשעת מלחמה. In the printed text this passage follows the gloss of the Rema, in ordinary type.
  • But where everything is done by order of the lord, renting from him or from his hired worker is effective: אבל במקו׳ שכל צרכי העיר אינם נעשי׳ אלא על פי השר או הממונה שלו ודאי ששכירו׳ מהשר ההוא או משכירו ולקיטו מהני שהרי יש לו רשות להושיב אנשיו וכלי מלחמתו בבתי בני העיר בשעת מלחמה שלא מדעתם. It is effective, writes the Mishnah Berurah, even for carrying [things] out and in to the houses of the non-Jew (ס״ק ט״ז).

Seif ב

ישראלי' הדרי' בחצר יחידי בעיר של עכו״ם שהיא מוקפת חומה ועברו יהודי' אחרי' דרך שם בשבת ונתאכסנו בחצר אחרת אינם אוסרי' עליה' דאורח אינו אוסר ומותרי' לטלטל בכל העיר: (וע״ל סוף סי' ש״ע):
Translation: Jews who live in a courtyard, alone (b'chatzer yechidi), in a city of non-Jews that is walled, and other Jews passed through there on Shabbos and lodged in another courtyard: they do not forbid them, for a guest (ore'ach) does not forbid; and they are permitted to carry in the whole city. [Parenthesis in small print, without הגה, as printed: וע״ל סוף סי׳ ש״ע — and see above, the end of siman 370.]
What this seif says:
  • Jews live in a courtyard, alone, in a walled city of non-Jews; other Jews, passing through on Shabbos, lodge in another courtyard: ישראלי׳ הדרי׳ בחצר יחידי בעיר של עכו״ם שהיא מוקפת חומה ועברו יהודי׳ אחרי׳ דרך שם בשבת ונתאכסנו בחצר אחרת. Although several Jews live in the courtyard, writes the Mishnah Berurah, they were permitted to carry out from the courtyard to the mavoys of the city, for with respect to the shituf of mavoys all the residents of the courtyard count as one, where they made an eruv (ס״ק י״ט).
  • Those passing through do not forbid them, for a guest does not forbid: אינם אוסרי׳ עליה׳ דאורח אינו אוסר. The guests, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק כ״ב), are nullified relative to the Jew settled in the city, and there is only one Jew there. On a guest who regularly lodges there, there are differing views, which it reports (ס״ק כ״ד; section 6).
  • And they are permitted to carry in the whole city: ומותרי׳ לטלטל בכל העיר. Are the guests themselves permitted? The commentators differ (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ג; section 6).
  • A parenthesis in small print, without הגה, refers to the end of siman 370: וע״ל סוף סי׳ ש״ע. Seif ח of siman 370 deals with one who lodges in a courtyard (hamisare'ach).

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Siman שצ״א applies to the mavoy and to the city two sets of laws stated for the courtyard: those of renunciation of domain (beginning of seif א; the Mishnah Berurah refers to simanim 380 and 381, ס״ק א), and those of a Jew who lives in a courtyard with a non-Jew (continuation of seif א; the Mishnah Berurah refers to the beginning of siman 382, ס״ק ד). It then says from whom the non-Jew's domain must be rented when there are two Jewish courtyards in the city (end of seif א, and the gloss of the Rema), and finally deals with Jews passing through who lodge in another courtyard on Shabbos (seif ב).

The beginning of seif א is the sentence of the Tur: « ככל משפטי ביטול למי ששכח ולא עירב בחצירו כן הוא במבוי » (טור או״ח שצ״א).

The fundamental question: in a mavoy or a city, what counts as "one" and what counts as "many"? Seif א answers that the residents of a courtyard count, with respect to the mavoy, as an individual in a courtyard, and that a single Jewish courtyard, in a city walled for dwelling, does not require renting from the non-Jew; seif ב, that a guest does not forbid.

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. Siman שצ״א follows siman ש״צ · 390, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « מבוי שצדו אחד עכו״ם וצדו אחד ישראל » (שו״ע או״ח ש״צ:א), and precedes siman שצ״ב · 392, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דיני עירובין לעיר » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:א).

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman:

The notions of siman שצ״א:
  • ביטול רשות — renunciation of domain (simanim 380 and 381): in seif א, the courtyard that did not join renounces to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it.
  • שיתוף — the joining of the courtyards of a mavoy; seif א speaks of the courtyard that « שכחה חצר אחת ולא נשתתפה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א).
  • שכירות רשות — renting his domain from the non-Jew: with two Jewish courtyards in the city, « צריכי׳ לשכור מכל חצר וחצר של עכו״ם » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א).
  • מוקפת חומה לדירה — the city walled for dwelling, a condition stated in seif א; the ordinary town is, the ordinary fortress is not (same seif; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט).
  • שכירו ולקיטו — the lord's hired worker and laborer, from whom renting is effective where all the needs of the city are done only by order of the lord or his appointee (seif א).
  • אורח — the guest: « דאורח אינו אוסר » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:ב).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אIn the mavoy, the laws of renunciation are those of the courtyard: the courtyard that forgot and did not join renounces to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it, even though they are many, for the residents of a courtyard count, with respect to the mavoy, as an individual in a courtyard. The laws of a Jew in a courtyard with a non-Jew apply to the mavoy or the city: it is not forbidden to carry in the mavoy or in the walled city until there are in the city two courtyards of Jewish houses; one courtyard, no, even if many Jewish houses open into it — provided the city is walled for dwelling (ordinary towns are, ordinary fortresses are not). With two Jewish courtyards, one must rent from each courtyard of the non-Jew; renting from the lord of the city is not enough. Rema: some say this is only for carrying out and in to the domain of the non-Jew, and to carry in the mavoy one may rent from the lord. The seif specifies which lord it means: one whose houses are not his and who has no right at all over the houses of the city, even in wartime; where all the needs of the city are done only by order of the lord or his appointee, renting from him or from his hired worker is effective.
בJews live in a courtyard, alone, in a walled city of non-Jews; other Jews, who passed through on Shabbos, lodge in another courtyard: they do not forbid them, for a guest does not forbid, and they are permitted to carry in the whole city. A parenthesis, without הגה, refers to the end of siman 370.
The heart of the siman: what counts as one. With respect to the mavoy, the residents of a courtyard count as an individual: the courtyard renounces, or one renounces to it (seif א). In a city walled for dwelling, a single Jewish courtyard does not require renting from the non-Jew; with two, one must rent from each of his courtyards, and the seif says when renting from the lord is not enough and when it is effective (seif א; for the gloss of the Rema, section 6). And in a walled city of non-Jews, where Jews live in a courtyard, alone, Jews passing through who lodge in another courtyard on Shabbos do not forbid, for a guest does not forbid (seif ב).

6. Position of the Rema

In this siman, the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) has a gloss, introduced by הגה in seif א, on renting from the lord of the city. The parenthesis of seif ב, in small print and without הגה, is attributed to no one on this page. The commentators also discuss the seifim of the Mechaber; here are several points, seif by seif.

The gloss of the Rema, and what the commentators discuss:
  • Seif א, "even though they are many" — the commentators differ on whom these words refer to (Level 2, section 2).
  • Seif א, the gloss of the Rema — « ויש אומרים דדוקא לענין להוציא ולהכניס לרשות העכו״ם אבל לטלטל במבוי יכול לשכור מן השר שהרי דרך המבוי הוא של השר ויכול לסלק כל העכו״ם משם » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״א:א). The commentators differ on this gloss (Level 2, section 4). Consult your Rav.
  • Seif ב, the guests themselves — the commentators differ on what the guests themselves may carry (Level 2, section 6). Consult your Rav.
  • Seif ב, a guest who regularly lodges there — the commentators differ on a guest who regularly lodges there (Level 2, section 5). Consult your Rav.
  • Chabad → Level 4 — Daat HaRav studies this siman in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שצ״א, under its own numbering, in 3 seifim.

On the guests themselves, and on a guest who regularly lodges there, the commentators differ (section 6). Consult your Rav.
For precise practical cases: the seifim make the rule depend, among other things, on what counts as one (seif א), on the city wall, whether or not it surrounds the city for dwelling (seif א), on the lord's power over the houses of the city (seif א), and on the status of a guest (seif ב); and the commentators discuss the gloss of the Rema and the case of the guests (section 6). For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שצ״א:
  1. Renunciation in the mavoy: the courtyard that forgot and did not join renounces to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it, for the residents of a courtyard count, with respect to the mavoy, as an individual in a courtyard (seif א; on "even though they are many", section 6).
  2. A single Jewish courtyard in the mavoy or in the walled city does not forbid, even if many Jewish houses open into it — provided the city is walled for dwelling (seif א).
  3. Two Jewish courtyards in the city: one must rent from each courtyard of the non-Jew; renting from the lord of the city is not enough when his houses are not his and he has no right at all over the houses of the city, even in wartime; where all the needs of the city are done only by order of the lord or his appointee, renting from him or from his hired worker is effective (seif א). The gloss of the Rema, on carrying in the mavoy, is discussed by the commentators (section 6).
  4. Jews passing through who lodge on Shabbos in another courtyard of a walled city of non-Jews, where Jews live in a courtyard, alone, do not forbid, for a guest does not forbid (seif ב; on the guests themselves, section 6).
  5. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Why can a courtyard that did not join renounce to the other residents of the mavoy, or they to it (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ב; Taz, ס״ק א)?
  2. Why does a single Jewish courtyard, even of many houses, not require renting from the non-Jew, and why do two courtyards require it (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ו)?
  3. What difference does seif א make between the ordinary town and the fortress (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט)?
  4. When is renting from the lord of the city not enough, and when is it effective (seif א)?
  5. What does the view quoted by the Rema distinguish, and what does the Taz object to it (ס״ק ג)?
  6. Why do the Jews passing through in seif ב not forbid (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ב)?
