דעת הרב

Daat HaRav — The Shulchan Aruch HaRav on Siman 391

A courtyard that forgot to join the shituf of the mavoi renounces its domain; a walled city of non-Jews; renting from the non-Jew of each courtyard, from the lord or from his appointee; Jews passing through who lodged in another courtyard. The 3 seifim of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, compared with the Mechaber's 2 seifim.

Why a Level 4 dedicated to the Alter Rebbe? The Shulchan Aruch HaRav is not a commentary on the Mechaber: it is a Shulchan Aruch in its own right, written by the Alter Rebbe, with its own division and numbering of the seifim. Here the Shulchan Aruch HaRav announces 3 seifim at the head of the siman, and Sefaria serves its text in 3 blocks, which this page reproduces and numbers as they stand (section 1); the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has 2. The Rav's seifim do not correspond one to one to the Mechaber's: what the Mechaber's seif א writes, except for the opinion the Rema's gloss reports (point ב), is carried by the Rav's seifim 1 and 2 — the renunciation and the walled city in seif 1; the rental, from the two courtyards of Jews in the city onwards, in seif 2 —, and the Mechaber's seif ב corresponds to the Rav's seif 3. In the English text of this page, the Rav's seifim are designated by their number (391:2, seif 2), the Mechaber's by their Hebrew letter (the Mechaber's seif א); in the Hebrew addresses in parentheses, both numberings are in letters, and the address always names the work (שו״ע או״ח for the Mechaber, שו״ע הרב for the Rav).

This page gathers, for Siman 391, the full text of the Rav with its translation, where his text differs from that of the Mechaber, and the practical conduct that follows from it.

1 · Text of the Alter Rebbe

שלחן ערוך הרב — סימן שצ״א

The full text of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav of the Alter Rebbe

סימן שצ״א — דִּין בִּטּוּל רְשׁוּת לְאוֹתָן שֶׁשָּׁכְחוּ לְעָרֵב — וּבוֹ ג׳ סְעִיפִים
Source: Kehot edition, as reproduced on Sefaria. The heading announces 3 seifim; Sefaria serves the text in 3 blocks (the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch has 2 seifim, under its own numbering).

סעיף א דִּין בִּטּוּל רְשׁוּת לְאוֹתָן שֶׁשָּׁכְחוּ לְעָרֵב וּבוֹ ג' סְעִיפִים: כְּכָל מִשְׁפְּטֵי…

דִּין בִּטּוּל רְשׁוּת לְאוֹתָן שֶׁשָּׁכְחוּ לְעָרֵב וּבוֹ ג' סְעִיפִים:

כְּכָל מִשְׁפְּטֵי בִטּוּל לְמִי שֶׁשָּׁכַח וְלֹא עֵרֵב בַּחֲצֵרוֹ — כֵּן הוּא בַּמָּבוֹי. שֶׁאִם שָׁכְחָה חָצֵר אַחַת וְלֹא נִשְׁתַּתְּפָה, מְבַטֶּלֶת רְשׁוּתָהּ לִשְׁאָר בְּנֵי הַמָּבוֹי אוֹ הֵם לָהּ, וּכְשֶׁהִיא מְבַטֶּלֶת לָהֶם הִיא נַעֲשֵׂית כְּאוֹרֵחַ אֶצְלָם, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהּ דִּיּוּרִים הַרְבֵּה וְאֵין הָרַבִּים נַעֲשִׂים אוֹרְחִים — מִכָּל מָקוֹם לְעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל בַּמָּבוֹי כָּל הַדִּיּוּרִים שֶׁבֶּחָצֵר נֶחְשָׁבִים כְּדִירָה אַחַת, כֵּיוָן שֶׁכֻּלָּם יוֹצְאִים לַמָּבוֹי בְּפֶתַח אֶחָד, דְּהַיְנוּ בְּפֶתַח הֶחָצֵר, וּבְפֶתַח זֶה הוּא שֶׁאוֹסְרִים בַּמָּבוֹי. וְלָכֵן נַעֲשֵׂית הֶחָצֵר לְעִנְיַן לֶאֱסוֹר בַּמָּבוֹי כְּאִלּוּ הוּא בַּיִת אֶחָד שֶׁדָּרִים בּוֹ רַבִּים.

לְפִיכָךְ כְּכָל מִשְׁפְּטֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל עִם הַנָּכְרִי בֶּחָצֵר — כֵּן הוּא בַּמָּבוֹי אוֹ בְּעִיר הַמֻּקֶּפֶת חוֹמָה, שֶׁאֵין אִסּוּר לְטַלְטֵל בְּמָבוֹי אוֹ בְּעִיר שֶׁל נָכְרִים עַד שֶׁיִּהְיוּ שְׁתֵּי חֲצֵרוֹת שֶׁל יִשְׂרָאֵל פְּתוּחוֹת לְתוֹכָן וְהֵן אוֹסְרוֹת זוֹ עַל זוֹ, אֲזַי צָרִיךְ לִשְׂכּוֹר מִן הַנָּכְרִים. אֲבָל בְּחָצֵר אַחַת שֶׁל יִשְׂרָאֵל, אֲפִלּוּ יֵשׁ בָּהּ הַרְבֵּה בָתִּים — הֵם חֲשׁוּבִים כְּיָחִיד לְעִנְיַן טִלְטוּל בָּעִיר, וְאֵין צָרִיךְ לִשְׂכּוֹר מִן הַנָּכְרִים.

וְהוּא שֶׁתְּהֵא הָעִיר מֻקֶּפֶת לְדִירָה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שס״ג. וּסְתָם עֲיָרוֹת מֻקָּפוֹת לְדִירָה, שֶׁבּוֹנִין בָּתִּים תְּחִלָּה וְאַחַר כָּךְ מַקִּיפִים אוֹתָם. אֲבָל סְתָם מִבְצָרִים (שֶׁקּוֹרִין שְׁלָא״ס) אֵינָן מֻקָּפִים לְדִירָה:

The law of renouncing one's domain for those who forgot to make an eruv; it contains 3 seifim:

All the laws of renunciation (bittul) for one who forgot and did not make an eruv in his courtyard apply likewise to the mavoi. If one courtyard forgot and did not join [the shituf], it renounces its domain in favor of the other residents of the mavoi, or they in its favor; and when it renounces in their favor, it becomes like a guest with them. Although it contains many dwellings, and many do not become guests, nevertheless, with regard to carrying in the mavoi, all the dwellings of the courtyard are counted as a single dwelling, since they all go out to the mavoi through a single opening, that is, through the opening of the courtyard, and it is through this opening that they forbid in the mavoi. Therefore the courtyard becomes, with regard to forbidding in the mavoi, like a single house in which many people live.

Therefore all the laws of a Jew with a non-Jew in a courtyard apply likewise to the mavoi or to a city surrounded by a wall: there is no prohibition to carry in a mavoi or in a city of non-Jews until there are two courtyards of Jews that open into it and that forbid each other; then one must rent [their domain] from the non-Jews. But a single courtyard of Jews, even if it has many houses, is considered as an individual with regard to carrying in the city, and there is no need to rent [their domain] from the non-Jews.

This is provided that the city is enclosed [by a wall] for dwelling, as was explained in siman 363. Ordinary towns are enclosed for dwelling, since one first builds the houses and afterwards encloses them. But ordinary fortresses (called schloss) are not enclosed for dwelling.

סעיף ב כְּשֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּעִיר ב' חֲצֵרוֹת שֶׁל בָּתֵּי יִשְׂרָאֵל…

כְּשֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּעִיר ב' חֲצֵרוֹת שֶׁל בָּתֵּי יִשְׂרָאֵל — צְרִיכִים לִשְׂכּוֹר מִכָּל חָצֵר וְחָצֵר שֶׁל נָכְרִי, וְאֵין מוֹעִיל מַה שֶּׁיִּשְׂכְּרוּ מִשַּׂר הָעִיר.

בַּמֶּה דְּבָרִים אֲמוּרִים? בְּשַׂר שֶׁאֵין הַבָּתִּים שֶׁלּוֹ, וְגַם אֵין לוֹ רְשׁוּת לְהִשְׁתַּמֵּשׁ בְּבָתֵּי בְּנֵי הָעִיר כְּלָל, וַאֲפִלּוּ בִּשְׁעַת מִלְחָמָה, שֶׁצָּרִיךְ לְהוֹשִׁיב אַנְשֵׁי הַמִּלְחָמָה וּכְלֵי מִלְחַמְתָּם בְּבָתֵּי בְּנֵי הָעִיר, אֵינוֹ נַעֲשֶׂה עַל פִּי הַשָּׂר אֶלָּא עַל פִּי עֵצָה וְהַנְהָגַת יוֹעֲצֵי הַמְּדִינָה, כְּמִנְהַג קְצָת עֲיָרוֹת שֶׁאֵין לַמֶּלֶךְ וְלַשָּׂר עֲלֵיהֶם שְׂרָרָה וְשֻׁלְטָנוּת כִּי אִם לִגְבּוֹת מִסִּים יְדוּעִים וְלִשְׁפּוֹט מִשְׁפְּטֵיהֶם, אֲבָל שְׁאָר צָרְכֵי הָעִיר אֵינָם נַעֲשִׂים רַק עַל פִּי יוֹעֲצֵי הַמְּדִינָה וּמַנְהִיגֶיהָ. אֲבָל עֲיָרוֹת שֶׁצָּרְכֵיהֶן אֵינָם נַעֲשִׂים אֶלָּא עַל פִּי הַמֶּלֶךְ אוֹ הַשָּׂר וְהַנְהָגָתָם, אוֹ עַל פִּי הַמְּמֻנִּים שֶׁלָּהֶם, וַדַּאי שֶׁשְּׂכִירוּת מֵהַשָּׂר הַהוּא אוֹ מֵהַמְּמֻנֶּה אוֹ אֲפִלּוּ מִשְּׂכִירוֹ וּלְקִיטוֹ (שֶׁבְּבֵיתוֹ) מוֹעִיל, שֶׁהֲרֵי יֵשׁ לוֹ רְשׁוּת לְהוֹשִׁיב אֲנָשָׁיו וּכְלֵי מִלְחַמְתָּם בְּבָתֵּי בְּנֵי הָעִיר בִּשְׁעַת מִלְחָמָה שֶׁלֹּא מִדַּעְתָּם, וְכָל מִי שֶׁיֵּשׁ לוֹ רְשׁוּת לְהַנִּיחַ חֲפָצָיו בְּבֵית הַנָּכְרִי אוֹ בַּחֲצֵרוֹ — נַעֲשֶׂה כִּשְׂכִירוֹ וּלְקִיטוֹ, וְיָכוֹל לְהַשְׂכִּיר רְשׁוּתוֹ שֶׁלֹּא מִדַּעְתּוֹ. וַאֲפִלּוּ שְׂכִירוֹ וּלְקִיטוֹ שֶׁל זֶה הַנַּעֲשֶׂה כִּשְׂכִירוֹ וּלְקִיטוֹ יָכוֹל לְהַשְׂכִּיר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ב:

When there are in the city two courtyards of Jewish houses, one must rent [his domain] from the non-Jew of each courtyard, and what one would rent from the lord of the city is of no avail.

When is this said? Of a lord to whom the houses do not belong and who also has no right at all to use the houses of the townspeople; and even in wartime, when the men of war and their weapons must be quartered in the houses of the townspeople, this is not done by order of the lord but by the counsel and direction of the advisers of the land — as is the custom of some towns over which the king and the lord have authority and power only to collect fixed taxes and to judge their cases, while the other needs of the town are carried out only by the advisers of the land and its leaders. But towns whose needs are carried out only by order of the king or the lord and under their direction, or by order of their appointees — certainly a rental from that lord, or from the appointee, or even from his hired worker and laborer (sechiro u'lekito) (who is in his house), is effective, since he has the right to quarter his men and their weapons in the houses of the townspeople in wartime without their consent; and anyone who has the right to place his belongings in the non-Jew's house or in his courtyard becomes like his hired worker and laborer, and can rent out his domain without his consent. And even the hired worker and laborer of the one who became like his hired worker and laborer can rent it out, as was explained in siman 382.

סעיף ג יִשְׂרְאֵלִים הַדָּרִים בְּחָצֵר אַחַת בְּעִיר שֶׁל נָכְרִים…

יִשְׂרְאֵלִים הַדָּרִים בְּחָצֵר אַחַת בְּעִיר שֶׁל נָכְרִים הַמֻּקֶּפֶת חוֹמָה, וְעָבְרוּ יִשְׂרְאֵלִים אֲחֵרִים דֶּרֶךְ שָׁם וְנִתְאַכְסְנוּ בְּחָצֵר אַחֶרֶת שֶׁל נָכְרִי — אֵינָם אוֹסְרִים בָּעִיר עַד לְאַחַר ל' יוֹם, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן ש״ע בְּאוֹרֵחַ שֶׁנִּתְאָרַח בֶּחָצֵר. אֶלָּא שֶׁהָאוֹרְחִים — יֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁהֵם אֲסוּרִים לְטַלְטֵל מֵחֲצֵרָן לַמָּבוֹי, שֶׁיִּשְׂרָאֵל הַדָּר בְּעִיר אוֹסֵר עֲלֵיהֶם כָּל מְבוֹאוֹת הָעִיר מִטַּעַם שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם.

אֲבָל יִשְׂרָאֵל אֶחָד הַדָּר בְּחָצֵר שֶׁדָּרִים בָּהּ נָכְרִים — מֻתָּר לְיִשְׂרָאֵל אַחֵר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מִתְאַכְסֵן בְּחָצֵר זוֹ לְהוֹצִיא וּלְהַכְנִיס מִבָּתֵּי הַנָּכְרִי אוֹ מִבֵּית הַיִּשְׂרָאֵל לֶחָצֵר, וְאֵין יִשְׂרָאֵל הַדָּר שָׁם אוֹסֵר עָלָיו, הוֹאִיל וְהוּא אֵינוֹ שׁוֹבֵת כְּלָל בְּחָצֵר זוֹ וְאוֹרֵחַ גָּמוּר הוּא אֵצֶל זֶה:

Jews live in one courtyard in a walled city of non-Jews, and other Jews passed through there and lodged in another courtyard, that of a non-Jew: they do not forbid in the city until after thirty days, as was explained in siman 370 regarding a guest who lodged in a courtyard. However, as for the guests, some say (yesh omrim) that they are forbidden to carry from their courtyard to the mavoi, since the Jew who lives in the city forbids to them all the alleys (mevo'os) of the city, for the reason explained there.

But if a single Jew lives in a courtyard in which non-Jews live, another Jew, who does not lodge in this courtyard, is permitted to carry out and bring in [objects] from the houses of the non-Jew or from the house of the Jew to the courtyard, and the Jew who lives there does not forbid him, since he does not make his Shabbos residence in this courtyard at all and is a complete guest with him.

Source: Sefaria — Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chayim 391. Sefaria gives no Kuntres Acharon entry for this siman.

2 · The Rav's text and the Mechaber's

לשון הרב מול סעיף המחבר והרמ״א

Where the Rav's text differs from the Mechaber's seif and the Rema's

Each point sets side by side a seif of the Mechaber's Shulchan Aruch (its numbering, 2 seifim, designated by their letter), or the Rema's gloss, and the corresponding seif of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav (its own numbering, 3 seifim), in the order of the Rav's seifim, and notes only what the comparison of the two texts shows. A point noted is not thereby unique to the Rav: the page sometimes names its source; it notes neither all the sources nor all the divergences.

Not noted here as separate points are clarifications of the Rav that explain the Mechaber's seif א and that his commentators also write. In seif 1, the Rav writes why ordinary towns are enclosed for dwelling, « שֶׁבּוֹנִין בָּתִּים תְּחִלָּה וְאַחַר כָּךְ מַקִּיפִים אוֹתָם » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:א); the Taz, before the Rav, writes « שהוא מוקף תחלה בחומה ואח״כ בונים בתוכו בתים משא״כ בכל העיירות שבונין בתים תחלה ואחר כך מקיפין חומה סביבם » (ט״ז או״ח שצ״א ס״ק ב). In seif 2, regarding a lord who has no right to use the houses, the Rav writes that in wartime the quartering of the men of war « אֵינוֹ נַעֲשֶׂה עַל פִּי הַשָּׂר אֶלָּא עַל פִּי עֵצָה וְהַנְהָגַת יוֹעֲצֵי הַמְּדִינָה » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ב); the Magen Avraham, before the Rav, writes « אלא ע״פ יועצי המדינה » (מגן אברהם שצ״א ס״ק א).

נקודה א — החצר כדירה אחת לגבי המבוי (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:א)

Point א — the courtyard counted as a single dwelling with regard to the mavoi (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 391:1)

The courtyard that renounces, and why it counts as one

What the Mechaber writes: « מבטלת רשותה לשאר בני המבוי או הם לה אע״פ שהם רבי׳ שבני החצר גבי מבוי חשובי׳ כיחיד בחצר » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א). He does not say to whom the words “אע״פ שהם רבי׳” refer, nor why the residents of the courtyard count as an individual with regard to the mavoi.

What the Rav writes: « וּכְשֶׁהִיא מְבַטֶּלֶת לָהֶם הִיא נַעֲשֵׂית כְּאוֹרֵחַ אֶצְלָם, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהּ דִּיּוּרִים הַרְבֵּה וְאֵין הָרַבִּים נַעֲשִׂים אוֹרְחִים » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:א); then the reason: all the dwellings of the courtyard count as one, « כֵּיוָן שֶׁכֻּלָּם יוֹצְאִים לַמָּבוֹי בְּפֶתַח אֶחָד, דְּהַיְנוּ בְּפֶתַח הֶחָצֵר, וּבְפֶתַח זֶה הוּא שֶׁאוֹסְרִים בַּמָּבוֹי » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:א).

The difference: the Rav's text writes of the courtyard that renounces that it becomes like a guest with the residents of the mavoi, « אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהּ דִּיּוּרִים הַרְבֵּה », and gives the reason why its dwellings count as one: they all go out to the mavoi through the courtyard's opening. The Mechaber's seif א states the rule without this reason. The Mechaber's commentators read the words “אע״פ שהם רבי׳” differently (see Level 2).

נקודה ב — השכירות מן השר והגהת הרמ״א (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ב)

Point ב — renting from the lord, and the Rema's gloss (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 391:2)

Renting from the lord of the city

What the Mechaber writes: « וכשיש ב׳ חצרו׳ של בתי ישראל בעיר צריכי׳ לשכור מכל חצר וחצר של עכו״ם ואין מספיק במה שישכור משר העיר » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א); then he restricts its scope, “במה דברי׳ אמורי׳” (see point ג).

What the Rema writes: in a gloss, introduced by הגה, at this place in the Mechaber's seif א: « ויש אומרים דדוקא לענין להוציא ולהכניס לרשות העכו״ם אבל לטלטל במבוי יכול לשכור מן השר שהרי דרך המבוי הוא של השר ויכול לסלק כל העכו״ם משם » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״א:א).

What the Rav writes: « וְאֵין מוֹעִיל מַה שֶּׁיִּשְׂכְּרוּ מִשַּׂר הָעִיר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ב), and immediately « בַּמֶּה דְּבָרִים אֲמוּרִים? בְּשַׂר שֶׁאֵין הַבָּתִּים שֶׁלּוֹ, וְגַם אֵין לוֹ רְשׁוּת לְהִשְׁתַּמֵּשׁ בְּבָתֵּי בְּנֵי הָעִיר כְּלָל » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ב).

The difference: in this seif, the Rav's text does not reproduce the view reported in the Rema's gloss — the distinction between carrying out or bringing in [objects] to the non-Jew's domain and carrying in the mavoi. The commentators diverge on this gloss (see Level 2); for practice, consult your Rav.

נקודה ג — הממונה, וכל מי שיש לו רשות להניח חפציו (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ב)

Point ג — the appointee, and anyone who has the right to place his belongings (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 391:2)

From whom the rental is effective

What the Mechaber writes: « אבל במקו׳ שכל צרכי העיר אינם נעשי׳ אלא על פי השר או הממונה שלו ודאי ששכירו׳ מהשר ההוא או משכירו ולקיטו מהני » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א). He names the appointee in the condition (the town's needs are carried out by order of the lord or his appointee), and, for the rental, the lord or his hired worker and laborer.

What the Rav writes: for towns whose needs are carried out only by order of the king or the lord and under their direction, or by order of their appointees, « וַדַּאי שֶׁשְּׂכִירוּת מֵהַשָּׂר הַהוּא אוֹ מֵהַמְּמֻנֶּה אוֹ אֲפִלּוּ מִשְּׂכִירוֹ וּלְקִיטוֹ (שֶׁבְּבֵיתוֹ) מוֹעִיל » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ב); then « וְכָל מִי שֶׁיֵּשׁ לוֹ רְשׁוּת לְהַנִּיחַ חֲפָצָיו בְּבֵית הַנָּכְרִי אוֹ בַּחֲצֵרוֹ — נַעֲשֶׂה כִּשְׂכִירוֹ וּלְקִיטוֹ, וְיָכוֹל לְהַשְׂכִּיר רְשׁוּתוֹ שֶׁלֹּא מִדַּעְתּוֹ » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ב); and « וַאֲפִלּוּ שְׂכִירוֹ וּלְקִיטוֹ שֶׁל זֶה הַנַּעֲשֶׂה כִּשְׂכִירוֹ וּלְקִיטוֹ יָכוֹל לְהַשְׂכִּיר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן שפ״ב » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ב).

The difference: the Rav's text also names the appointee among those from whom one may rent, carries in parentheses, after the hired worker and laborer, “(שֶׁבְּבֵיתוֹ)” (“who is in his house”), and writes the general rule: anyone who has the right to place his belongings in the non-Jew's house or in his courtyard becomes like his hired worker and laborer; and the hired worker and laborer of that one can also rent it out, with a reference to siman 382. The Mechaber's seif א does not write this general rule.

נקודה ד — האורחים (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ג)

Point ד — the guests (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 391:3)

Jews passing through, lodged in another courtyard

What the Mechaber writes: « ונתאכסנו בחצר אחרת אינם אוסרי׳ עליה׳ דאורח אינו אוסר ומותרי׳ לטלטל בכל העיר » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:ב), followed by a parenthesis in small print, without הגה, which refers to the end of siman 370, “(וע״ל סוף סי׳ ש״ע)”.

What the Rav writes: « אֵינָם אוֹסְרִים בָּעִיר עַד לְאַחַר ל' יוֹם, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר בְּסִמָּן ש״ע בְּאוֹרֵחַ שֶׁנִּתְאָרַח בֶּחָצֵר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ג); then « אֶלָּא שֶׁהָאוֹרְחִים — יֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים שֶׁהֵם אֲסוּרִים לְטַלְטֵל מֵחֲצֵרָן לַמָּבוֹי, שֶׁיִּשְׂרָאֵל הַדָּר בְּעִיר אוֹסֵר עֲלֵיהֶם כָּל מְבוֹאוֹת הָעִיר מִטַּעַם שֶׁנִּתְבָּאֵר שָׁם » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ג).

The difference: the Mechaber's seif ב writes “דאורח אינו אוסר” without stating a time limit in the seif itself; the Rav's text writes in the seif that the guests do not forbid in the city until after thirty days, referring to siman 370. The Mechaber's seif ב also writes “ומותרי׳ לטלטל בכל העיר”; the Rav's text does not take up these words, and reports in the name of “יֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים” that the guests themselves may not carry from their courtyard to the mavoi. The commentators diverge on what the guests themselves may carry (see Level 2); for practice, consult your Rav. On a guest who regularly lodges there, the commentators also diverge (Level 2, section 5); consult your Rav.

נקודה ה — ישראל אחד הדר בחצר עם נכרים (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ג)

Point ה — a single Jew living in a courtyard with non-Jews (Shulchan Aruch HaRav 391:3)

Another Jew who does not lodge in this courtyard

What the Mechaber writes: the Mechaber's seif ב does not write this case.

What the Rav writes: « אֲבָל יִשְׂרָאֵל אֶחָד הַדָּר בְּחָצֵר שֶׁדָּרִים בָּהּ נָכְרִים — מֻתָּר לְיִשְׂרָאֵל אַחֵר שֶׁאֵינוֹ מִתְאַכְסֵן בְּחָצֵר זוֹ לְהוֹצִיא וּלְהַכְנִיס מִבָּתֵּי הַנָּכְרִי אוֹ מִבֵּית הַיִּשְׂרָאֵל לֶחָצֵר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ג), with the reason, « הוֹאִיל וְהוּא אֵינוֹ שׁוֹבֵת כְּלָל בְּחָצֵר זוֹ וְאוֹרֵחַ גָּמוּר הוּא אֵצֶל זֶה » (שו״ע הרב שצ״א:ג).

The difference: after the case of the guests, the Rav's text writes the case of another Jew who does not lodge in the courtyard where a single Jew lives with non-Jews; the Mechaber's seif ב does not write it.

4 · Lemaaseh

הלכה למעשה — מנהג חב״ד

The practical Chabad conduct

Points of halachic conduct that flow directly from the Shulchan Aruch HaRav Siman 391, in the Chabad sensibility. Each point refers to its seif of the Rav, in the order of the text.

For the Chabad Chassid — Siman 391

  • ① The courtyard that forgot to join the shituf of the mavoi: it renounces its domain in favor of the other residents of the mavoi, or they in its favor; with regard to carrying in the mavoi, all the dwellings of the courtyard count as one (391:1). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav שצ״א:1.
  • ② A walled city of non-Jews: there is no prohibition to carry in the mavoi or in the city until there are two courtyards of Jews that open into it and forbid each other; then one must rent [their domain] from the non-Jews; a single courtyard of Jews, even of many houses, need not rent. This is provided that the city is enclosed for dwelling (391:1). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav שצ״א:1.
  • ③ Renting from the lord: when there are two courtyards of Jews in the city, one must rent from the non-Jew of each courtyard; renting from the lord of the city is of no avail if the houses are not his and he has no right at all to use them, even in wartime. But where the town's needs are carried out only by order of the king or the lord, or of their appointees, a rental from that lord, from the appointee, or even from his hired worker and laborer (who is in his house), is effective (391:2). The view reported in the Rema's gloss, which this seif of the Rav does not reproduce, is discussed by the commentators: see section 2, point ב, and consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav שצ״א:2.
  • ④ Jews passing through: Jews passing through who lodged in another courtyard, that of a non-Jew, in a walled city of non-Jews where other Jews live in one courtyard, do not forbid in the city until after thirty days (391:3, referring to siman 370). The Rav reports in the name of “יֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים” that the guests themselves may not carry from their courtyard to the mavoi; the commentators diverge on this (section 2, point ד): consult your Rav. On a guest who regularly lodges there, the commentators diverge (Level 2, section 5); consult your Rav. Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav שצ״א:3.
  • ⑤ Another Jew, in a courtyard where a single Jew lives with non-Jews: if he does not lodge in this courtyard, he may carry out and bring in [objects] from the houses of the non-Jew or from the house of the Jew to the courtyard (391:3). Reference: Shulchan Aruch HaRav שצ״א:3.

⚠ This section presents the Chabad halachic conduct. For any concrete question: consult your Rav.
