1. The Central Axiom

Siman ש in one sentence.
Melaveh Malka: "le-olam yesader adam shulchano" — always set the table at motzaei Shabbos to escort the Shabbos Queen, even with a single kezayis. This is the 4th seudah of Shabbos. Kabbalistic meaning: nourishes the luz bone / nesko'i which will revive the body at techiyas ha-meisim.

5. "Le-olam" — the force of a 4th seudah

The heart of the siman is in one word of the text: "Le-olam yesader adam shulchano" — "always, a person should set his table at motzaei Shabbos." The whole meaning of Melaveh Malka is played out in the tension of this "always": a 4th seudah that is not a chiyuv gamur like the three seudos of Shabbos, and yet asserts itself at all times and in all circumstances.

Why "escort" rather than simply "eat"? One escorts a departing king — out of honor. The 4th seudah does not introduce anything: it escorts the Shabbos Queen as she withdraws. It is a gesture of love, not a formal obligation of sustenance.
↓
Hence the flexibility of quantity. Where the three seudos require bread in quantity, Melaveh Malka is satisfied — bedieved — by a single kezayis of something. The essence is the intention of escort, not volume.
↓
And yet "le-olam." Even sated from the day's meals, even without appetite, one sets the table. The weakness of the formal obligation is compensated by the universality of the practice.

The limit case that illuminates the entire halacha is the kabbalistic meaning: this seudah nourishes the luz bone (the nesko'i), the mystical bone that is nourished only by Melaveh Malka and from which the body will be revived at techiyas ha-meisim. This is why "le-olam": missing this meal is not transgressing a prohibition — it is depriving of nourishment the part of you destined for techiyas ha-meisim. The 4th seudah thus connects motzaei Shabbos to the end of days: we escort the Queen, and sing Eliyahu ha-Navi, the herald of redemption.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — Dismissing Melaveh Malka as "not obligatory": while technically not a chiyuv gamur, it is mitzvah min ha-muvchar. Missing this opportunity = losing the zechus of the luz.
Pitfall 2 — Eating a snack instead of a seudah: lechatchilah, a kezayis of bread, at the table, with respect.
Pitfall 3 — Doing Melaveh Malka late at night: Sha'arei Teshuvah: before chatzos. Earlier = better.
Pitfall 4 — Not singing: the songs (Eliyahu, Dovid) are an essential part of the experience.
Pitfall 5 — Eating alone, without family: ideally with family, transmitting to children the centrality of Shabbos.

10. The 4 practical commandments of Siman ש

For Melaveh Malka

  1. Always set a small table after Havdalah — at least a kezayis.
  2. Lechatchilah on bread with a dish (meat, fish, or mezonos).
  3. Sing the piyutim: Hamavdil, Eliyahu ha-Navi, Dovid Melech Yisrael.
  4. Before chatzos; as close as possible to Havdalah.
