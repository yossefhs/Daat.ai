1. The central axiom

Siman רצ"ה in one sentence.
On motzaei Shabbat in shul one says Vihi noam + Seder Kedushah prolonged (to delay the return of souls to gehinnom — Kabbalah), the chazzan makes public havdalah to be motzi those without wine, one mentions Eliyahu HaNavi (Moshiach). If Yom Tov in the week: no Vihi noam; one says V'yitein lecha instead.

4. Decision tree — Vihi noam or V'yitein lecha?

Q1: Is there a Yom Tov gamur (Pesach, Sukkos, Shavuos, R"H, YK) in the 6 days that follow?
↓ No → Vihi noam + Seder Kedushah
↓ Yes → next
Q2: Purim or Erev Pesach? (Not Yom Tov gamur)
↓ Yes → Vihi noam anyway
↓ No (real YT) → V'yitein lecha instead
Q3: In all cases: chazzan's havdalah + Eliyahu HaNavi.

5. The chazzan's havdalah — a collective net

The central text of the siman — the very reason for its existence — is the institution by which the chazzan makes public havdalah in shul. But a question immediately arises: why this communal havdalah, when each one is anyway obligated to do havdalah at home on his own cup?

The origin: in the time of the Gemara, not everyone had wine at home. The havdalah said in the beis ha-knesses was motzi the poor and travelers who, without it, would have remained without havdalah at all.
↓
The condition of efficacy: to be yotzei through the chazzan's havdalah is not automatic. One needs the kavanah to be yotzei (principle of "שומע כעונה"), and not to have drunk or eaten before. Without this intention, one has heard — but is not yotzei.
↓
The modern situation: today almost every home has wine. The obligatory havdalah remains thus that of the home; the chazzan's becomes a collective safety net — a supplement, not the "real" havdalah.
The central pitfall: treating the chazzan's havdalah as sufficient and omitting that of the home. It is motzi only one who really has no cup and had kavanah. For all others, the obligation is fulfilled at home, on one's own cup, with family.

This siman thus articulates siman 294 (havdalah in tefillah) and siman 296 (havdalah on wine): the chazzan does not eliminate either — he guarantees that no one in the community leaves Shabbat without havdalah.

6. Mnemonic — "V.S.H.E"

V — Vihi noam (prolonged)

S — Seder Kedushah ("Atah Kadosh")

H — Havdalah by the chazzan

E — Eliyahu HaNavi + V'yitein lecha

Exception mnemonic:

YT = Yom Tov gamur in the week → no Vihi noam

P = Purim in the week → yes Vihi noam (not YT gamur)

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1 — Reciting Vihi noam at a quick pace: contradicts the purpose ("arichus"). One must sing, prolong, delay.
Pitfall 2 — Saying Vihi noam in the week with Pesach/Sukkos: wrong. One says V'yitein lecha.
Pitfall 3 — Being yotzei the chazzan's havdalah without kavanah: does not discharge the obligation. One needs awareness of being yotzei + not having drunk/eaten.
Pitfall 4 — Considering the chazzan's havdalah as the "real" havdalah: today, almost everyone has wine at home — the obligatory havdalah is at home. The chazzan's remains a supplement/net.
Pitfall 5 — Skipping Eliyahu HaNavi on motzaei Shabbat: minhag strongly codified in all communities. Breaking a traditional chain.

10. The 5 practical commandments of Siman רצ"ה

For motzaei Shabbat in shul

  1. Prolonged Vihi noam + Seder Kedushah — to delay the return of souls to gehinnom.
  2. Yom Tov in the week? Replace with V'yitein lecha.
  3. Chazzan's havdalah with kavanah to be motzi those without wine (but halachah also wants one to redo it at home).
  4. Mention Eliyahu HaNavi — traditional songs calling for the redemption.
  5. V'yitein lecha recited after havdalah — with family at home, for blessings.
