Siman 275 — Activities Forbidden by Shabbos Candlelight

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe on the 13 seifim that codify acts forbidden by the light of the Shabbos candle — reading (the gezeirah of shema yateh, lest one tilt the wick), checking, examining. Conceptual framework: chashash of tilting the wick and Rabbinic decrees.
