Siman 344 — Lost in the Desert: When Does Shabbos Begin?

The shitah of the Alter Rebbe on the 2 se'ifim codifying the case of the holech bamidbar — a traveler lost in the desert who no longer knows which day is Shabbos counts 7 days and keeps the 7th as Shabbos. A fascinating edge-case in hilchos safek.
