1. The central axiom

Siman שצ״ב in one sentence.
Can a whole city join in a single shituf? The city that was the acquisition of an individual, even if it became the city of many, and the city of many with a single entrance: yes, all make a single shituf (seif א). The city of many with two entrances, through which people enter and go out, even if it became an individual's: « אין מערבין את כולה אלא מניחין ממנה מקום א׳ אפילו חצר אחת ובית לתוכה ומשתתפין השאר » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:א) — one leaves a place out of it, as a sign (seif א; on its measure, the commentators differ: Level 2, section 2); unless it was reduced and remained at fifty residents (seif ז; on a city that keeps fifty residents, see Level 2, section 9). The city that was the acquisition of an individual is not given an eruv in halves, but either all of it or alley by alley, with a monument at the entrance of each alley (seif ד) — a rule stated for a city surrounded by a wall ten tefachim high and which has doors (seif ה); what separates: not a lechi or a korah, but a post of four or two posts, and beyond ten amos a doorway form (seif ו). On the view the gloss of the Rema brings in seif ו, the commentators differ (section 5).

3. The course of the seifim — from the city of an individual to the one who confers the shituf

Seif א — a single shituf: the city that was the acquisition of an individual, even if it became the city of many, and the city of many with a single entrance: all join in a single shituf and carry in the whole town.
↓
Seif א — the shiyur: the city of many with two entrances, through which people enter and go out, even if it became an individual's: one leaves a place out of it, and joins the rest. Those who joined are permitted in the whole city except that place; those who remained, in their place, with their own shituf; according to the printed text, if they are many, the rest of the city is forbidden to them (on the reading: Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ח, in the name of the Taz).
↓
Seif א — the reason: a sign, so that people know that it is the eruv that permits carrying in a city through which the many pass. Making the eruv alley by alley works all the more so, for there is no greater shiyur.
↓
Seif א — the gloss of the Rema, introduced by הגה: they must make between them two posts of two mashehuyin, if it is ten amos wide; beyond ten, a doorway form. On the case this gloss refers to, see Level 2 (section 3) and Level 4 (point ז).
↓
Seif ב — the entrances: the ladder in the wall is not an entrance, even many ladders side by side; a refuse heap in front of one of the two entrances, and it is as if there were only one (according to the Mishnah Berurah, a public refuse heap: ס״ק י״ז).
↓
Seif ג — the shiyur itself: houses facing outward, and even a cattle barn or a straw barn, which do not need an eruv: one makes them the shiyur, and makes the eruv for the rest.
↓
Seif ד — the alley that does not join: it forbids to all, unless a monument was built at its entrance. Therefore the city that was the acquisition of an individual, even if it became the city of many, is not given an eruv in halves, but either all of it or alley by alley, with a monument at the entrance of each alley.
↓
Seif ה — in what case: in a city surrounded by a wall ten tefachim high and which has doors; if the city was not made wholly fit by partitions, one may make half of it fit and make the eruv for it.
↓
Seif ו — what separates: a lechi and a korah do not separate; a post of four, or two posts of two mashehuyin, separate, and one makes the eruv in halves; beyond ten amos, a doorway form. Gloss of the Rema, introduced by הגה: or a partition ten high at the entrance of the alley; then the view “י״א” on the length and the width (section 5).
↓
Seif ז — the reduced city: a city of many that was reduced and remained at fifty residents does not need a shiyur (on a city that keeps fifty residents: Level 2, section 9).
↓
Seif ח — the one who confers the shituf: if they all made a single eruv, he need not inform the people of the city; the one who forgot, the one who went to spend Shabbos in another city, the non-Jew: as their law in the courtyard and the alley.

The Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes this siman in 9 seifim, under its own division. Level 4 notes, point by point, where its text differs from that of the Mechaber and the Rema; it notes neither all the sources nor all the divergences.

4. Decision tree — can the whole city join in a single shituf?

Q1: Was the city the acquisition of an individual? (seif א)
↓ YES, even if it became the city of many → all make a single shituf (seif א); to divide it, see Q4. NO, it is a city of many → Q2.
Q2: Does it have two entrances, through which people enter and go out? (seifim א and ב)
↓ A single entrance → all make a single shituf (seif א); the ladder in the wall does not count as an entrance, and a refuse heap in front of one of the two makes the city count as having only one (seif ב; according to the Mishnah Berurah, a public refuse heap: ס״ק י״ז). Two entrances → Q3.
Q3: Was it reduced, and did it remain at fifty residents? (seif ז)
↓ YES → no shiyur is needed (seif ז; on a city that keeps fifty residents, see Level 2, section 9; consult your Rav). NO → one leaves a place out of it (seif א); houses facing outward, a cattle barn or a straw barn can be the shiyur (seif ג). On the custom of not making a shiyur: Level 4, point ב; this page does not decide.
Q4: Does part of a city that was the acquisition of an individual want to make its own eruv? (seifim ד to ו)
↓ In a city surrounded by a wall ten tefachim high and which has doors (seif ה) → not in halves: either all of it, or alley by alley with a monument at the entrance of each alley (seif ד); or separate by a post of four or two posts, beyond ten amos by a doorway form, and not by a lechi or a korah (seif ו); on the gloss of the Rema, section 5. The city was not made wholly fit by partitions → one may make half of it fit and make the eruv for it (seif ה).
→ At the slightest doubt: consult your Rav.

5. Dividing the city — seifim ד to ו and the gloss of the Rema

Seif ד writes: « לפיכך עיר שהיתה קנין יחיד אפילו נעשי׳ של רבים אין מערבין אותה לחצאין אלא או כולה או מבוי ומבוי ובונה כל מבוי ומבוי מצבה על פתחו אם רצה לחלוק רשותו מהם כדי שלא יאסור על שאר המבואו׳ » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:ד); seif ה, in what case: « במה דברים אמורי׳ שאין מערבין אותה לחצאין בעיר מוקפת חומה גבוה י״ט ויש לה דלתו׳ אבל אם לא היתה העיר כולה מוכשרת במחיצו׳ ובאו להכשיר חציה ולערבה הרשו׳ בידם » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:ה); seif ו, what “not in halves” means: a lechi and a korah do not separate, a post of four or two posts separate, and beyond ten amos a doorway form (seif ו).

summary: the table gives what Levels 1 and 4 carry; the commentators differ: in Level 2 (sections 7 and 8), each is quoted in his own words.

WorkWhat it writes

Then, in the name of “י״א”: the prohibition to divide the city of an individual applies along its length; across its width, when these go out by one gate and those by another, with no right of passage over each other, one makes the eruv in halves, with a repair between them — up to ten, a lechi or a korah; beyond ten, the doorway form (reference to siman 363)

On a lechi and a korah between alleys, it distinguishes according to the city (392:6 and 392:7)

6. Mnemonic ה-ס-ש-מ-ח-פ-ח-מ

One word from each seif, in their order:

ה — היכר (seif א): the city of many with two entrances leaves a shiyur, as a sign — except the city reduced to fifty residents (see the letter ח of חמשים).

ס — סולם (seif ב): the ladder in the wall is not an entrance; a refuse heap in front of one of the two entrances, and it is as if there were only one.

ש — שיור (seif ג): houses facing outward, and even a cattle barn or a straw barn — one makes them the shiyur.

מ — מצבה (seif ד): the alley that does not join forbids to all, unless a monument was built at its entrance.

ח — חומה (seif ה): “not in halves” is stated for a city surrounded by a wall ten tefachim high and which has doors.

פ — פסים (seif ו): a post of four or two posts separate, beyond ten amos a doorway form; a lechi and a korah do not separate.

ח — חמשים (seif ז): a city of many reduced to fifty residents does not need a shiyur; on a city that keeps fifty residents, see Level 2, section 9.

מ — המזכה (seif ח): if they all made a single eruv, he need not inform them.

7. Pitfalls to avoid

Pitfall 1: Swapping the two cities. Seif א writes « עיר שהיתה קנין יחיד אפילו נעשי׳ של רבי׳ משתתפי׳ כולם שיתוף א׳ ויטלטלו בכל המדינה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:א); it is the city of many with two entrances, « אפילו נעשי׳ של יחיד » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:א), that leaves a shiyur.
Pitfall 2: Miscounting the entrances. The ladder in the wall is not an entrance (seif ב); for « עד רוחב עשרה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:ב), the Mishnah Berurah writes that one must read « עד יותר מעשר » (משנה ברורה שצ״ב ס״ק ט״ז), and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes « בְּרֹחַב יוֹתֵר מֵעֶשֶׂר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ב:ב). The refuse heap in front of one of the two entrances: « ואם יש לה שני פתחים ויש אשפה לפני אחד מהם כאילו אין שם אלא פתח א׳ » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:ב); according to the Mishnah Berurah, only a public refuse heap, which is not likely to be removed (ס״ק י״ז), and if it was cleared, the entrance returns (ס״ק י״ח).
Pitfall 3: Thinking that the alley that does not join forbids only to itself: « הרי זה אוסר על כולם » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:ד), unless a monument was built at its entrance (seif ד). On the dimensions of the monument, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 6; Level 4, point ה); the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes an itztaba four tefachim high (392:4). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 4: Extending “not in halves” to every city. Seif ה states this rule for a city surrounded by a wall ten tefachim high and which has doors; if the city was not made wholly fit by partitions, one may make half of it fit and make the eruv for it. On what the Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes about the city that has always belonged to the many, see section 5.
Pitfall 5: Separating the two parts by a lechi or a korah: seif ו writes that they do not separate, and names a post of four or two posts, beyond ten amos a doorway form. The view the gloss of the Rema brings allows, across the width, up to ten, a lechi or a korah; the commentators differ (section 5). Consult your Rav.
Pitfall 6: Reading in the present the custom the Shulchan Aruch HaRav reports. According to the view that a public domain through which sixty myriads do not pass every day is not a full public domain, it writes « אֵין צָרִיךְ לַעֲשׂוֹת שִׁיּוּר לְעִיר שֶׁל רַבִּים, אֶלָּא אִם כֵּן שִׁשִּׁים רִבּוֹא בּוֹקְעִים בָּהּ בְּכָל יוֹם » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ב:א), then « וְעַל זֶה סוֹמְכִין עַכְשָׁו, שֶׁאֵין נוֹהֲגִין לַעֲשׂוֹת שִׁיּוּר לְשׁוּם עִיר » (שו״ע הרב שצ״ב:א) — in his time. For today, consult your Rav (Level 4, point ב).

10. The practical commandments of Siman שצ״ב

For daily conduct

  1. A single shituf: the city that was the acquisition of an individual, even if it became the city of many, and the city of many with a single entrance — all join in a single shituf and carry in the whole town (seif א), once its alleys have been made fit according to their law (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ד; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 392:1).
  2. The shiyur: the city of many with two entrances, through which people enter and go out, even if it became an individual's — one leaves a place out of it, as a sign (seif א); it can be houses facing outward, a cattle barn or a straw barn (seif ג). On the custom of not making a shiyur: Level 4, point ב; consult your Rav.
  3. The entrances: the ladder in the wall is not one; a refuse heap in front of one of the two makes the city count as having only one (seif ב; according to the Mishnah Berurah, a public refuse heap: ס״ק י״ז).
  4. The alley that does not join forbids to all, unless a monument was built at its entrance; the city that was the acquisition of an individual is not given an eruv in halves, but either all of it or alley by alley, with a monument at the entrance of each alley (seif ד) — in a city surrounded by a wall ten tefachim high and which has doors (seif ה).
  5. What separates: a post of four or two posts, beyond ten amos a doorway form; not a lechi or a korah (seif ו). On the view the gloss of the Rema brings, the commentators differ (section 5): consult your Rav.
  6. The reduced city of fifty residents does not need a shiyur (seif ז; on a city that keeps fifty residents, see Level 2, section 9; consult your Rav).
  7. The one who confers the shituf on all the people of the city need not inform them, if they all made a single eruv; the one who forgot, the one who left, and the non-Jew follow their law in the courtyard and the alley (seif ח).
  8. In case of doubt — consult your Rav. In-depth pilpul — Level 2; Chabad shitah — Level 4.
