1. The Shulchan Aruch text

Siman שצ״ב contains 8 seifim of the Mechaber (Rabbi Yosef Karo); glosses of the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles) are introduced in it by הגה in seifim א and ו. In seif ג, the printed text also carries a parenthesis in small type, without הגה. The subject of the siman, according to its title: the laws of eruvin for a city. The Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe has 9 seifim for this siman: its numbering is that of Level 4, not of this page.

Seif א

דיני עירובין לעיר. ובו ח סעיפים:
עיר שהיתה קנין יחיד אפילו נעשי' של רבי' משתתפי' כולם שיתוף א' ויטלטלו בכל המדינה וכן אם היתה של רבי' ויש לה פתח אחד משתתפי' כולם שיתוף א' אבל אם היתה של רבי' ויש לה שני פתחים שהעם נכנסי' בזה ויוצאי' בזה אפילו נעשי' של יחיד אין מערבין את כולה אלא מניחין ממנה מקום א' אפילו חצר אחת ובית לתוכה ומשתתפין השאר ויהיו אלו המשתתפי' כולם מותרים בכל העיר חוץ מאותו מקום ששיירו ויהיו אותם הנשארי' מותרי' במקומם בשיתוף שעושים לעצמם ואם היו הנשארים רבים אסורים לטלטל בשאר כל העיר ודבר זה משום היכר הוא כדי שידעו שהעירוב התיר להם לטלטל בעיר זה שרבים בוקעים בה שהרי המקום שנשאר ולא נשתתף עמהם אין מטלטלין בו אלא אלו לעצמן ואלו לעצמן ואם רצו לערב מבוי מבוי בפני עצמו כל שכן דמהני דאין שיור גדול מזה: הגה וצריך שיעשו ביניהם שני פסין של שני משהויין אם הוא רחב עשר אמות וביתר מי' צריך צורת פתח (טור):
Translation: A city that was the acquisition of an individual (kinyan yachid), even if it became [the city] of many (rabim): they all join in a single shituf (shituf), and they will carry in the whole town (medinah). And likewise, if it was [the city] of many and it has a single entrance, they all join in a single shituf. But if it was [the city] of many and it has two entrances, through which the people enter by one and go out by the other, even if it became [the city] of an individual, one does not make the eruv for all of it; rather one leaves out a place from it, even a single courtyard and a house opening inside, and one joins the rest; and those who joined will all be permitted in the whole city, except for that place which they left out; and those who remained will be permitted in their place, with the shituf they make for themselves; and if those who remained are many, they are forbidden to carry in all the rest of the city [on the reading of this passage, see the box below]. And this is because of a sign (heker): so that they know that it is the eruv that permitted them to carry in this city through which the many pass, since the place that remained and did not join with them, one does not carry in it except these by themselves and those by themselves. And if they want to make the eruv alley (mavoi) by alley, each by itself, all the more so it works, for there is no shiyur (shiyur) greater than this. Gloss of the Rema: and they must make between them two posts (pasin) of two mashehuyin [of minimal thickness], if it is ten amos wide; and beyond ten, a doorway form (tzuras hapesach) is required [the Rema's reference, as printed: טור].
What this seif says:
  • The city acquired by an individual, even if it became the city of many: one shituf for the whole city: עיר שהיתה קנין יחיד אפילו נעשי׳ של רבי׳ משתתפי׳ כולם שיתוף א׳ ויטלטלו בכל המדינה. According to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק א), this is for example one that an individual built for himself and rented out to many; and some say: even if he sold it to many, as long as the name of the first owners has not faded from it. A single shituf specifically, it writes, since, having first been the acquisition of an individual, it is like one courtyard (ס״ק ג); and one carries in the whole town once its alleys have been made fit according to their law (ס״ק ד).
  • The city of many with a single entrance: likewise: וכן אם היתה של רבי׳ ויש לה פתח אחד משתתפי׳ כולם שיתוף א׳. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ה) explains that the shiyur is required in a city of many so that the law of the public domain not be forgotten; but this one, having only one entrance and being closed on the other side, does not resemble a public domain, and its law is that of a courtyard.
  • The city of many with two entrances, even if it became an individual's: one leaves a place out of the shituf: אבל אם היתה של רבי׳ ויש לה שני פתחים שהעם נכנסי׳ בזה ויוצאי׳ בזה אפילו נעשי׳ של יחיד אין מערבין את כולה אלא מניחין ממנה מקום א׳ אפילו חצר אחת ובית לתוכה ומשתתפין השאר. According to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ז), this is a decree of the Sages, lest it revert to being a public domain and people be lenient there too. It adds, in the name of the Magen Avraham, that the common practice is not to be careful about this, even in a city of many, and that it is possible that people hold like Rashi, for whom one does not speak of "many" with fewer than six hundred thousand. On the measure of the shiyur — a courtyard with a house, or a house alone — the commentators differ (Level 2, section 2).
  • Those who joined are permitted in the whole city, except the place left out: ויהיו אלו המשתתפי׳ כולם מותרים בכל העיר חוץ מאותו מקום ששיירו.
  • Those who remained: in their place, with their own shituf; and the rest of the city: ויהיו אותם הנשארי׳ מותרי׳ במקומם בשיתוף שעושים לעצמם ואם היו הנשארים רבים אסורים לטלטל בשאר כל העיר. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ח) brings from the Taz the reading « בשיתוף שעושים לעצמם אם היו הנשארים רבים » (משנה ברורה שצ״ב ס״ק ח), and then « ואסורים » with a vav: the condition "if they are many" then bears on the shituf they make for themselves; and it writes that if the shiyur is of only one, he needs no shituf (ס״ק ט).
  • The reason: a sign: ודבר זה משום היכר הוא כדי שידעו שהעירוב התיר להם לטלטל בעיר זה שרבים בוקעים בה שהרי המקום שנשאר ולא נשתתף עמהם אין מטלטלין בו אלא אלו לעצמן ואלו לעצמן. Without a shiyur, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק י), the eruv would be forgotten, and the law of the public domain would be forgotten as well, when people see carrying in a city that resembles a public domain.
  • The eruv alley by alley works all the more so: ואם רצו לערב מבוי מבוי בפני עצמו כל שכן דמהני דאין שיור גדול מזה. "Making the eruv" here means the shituf (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״א).
  • Gloss of the Rema: what must be made between them: וצריך שיעשו ביניהם שני פסין של שני משהויין אם הוא רחב עשר אמות וביתר מי׳ צריך צורת פתח. Otherwise, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק י״ב), [the place] is breached into a place forbidden to it. It writes (ס״ק י״ג) that the same applies to a monument (matzeivah) set up at that spot to divide, but that a lechi and a korah do not work to divide; and it brings from the Acharonim that this gloss does not refer to the case right before it — the eruv alley by alley, where a lechi or a korah at the end of the alley certainly suffices —, but to the case above, where within the alley itself a house and a courtyard were left out.

Seif ב

עיר של רבים שיש לו פתח א' וסולם במקום אחר מערבין את כולה ואינה צריכ' שיור שאין הסולם שבחומה חשוב כפתח ואפילו העמיד הרבה סולמות זה בצד זה עד רוחב עשרה לא חשיב כפתח ואם יש לה שני פתחים ויש אשפה לפני אחד מהם כאילו אין שם אלא פתח א':
Translation: A city of many which has a single entrance, and a ladder in another place: one makes the eruv for all of it, and it does not need a shiyur, for the ladder in the wall is not considered an entrance. And even if he set up many ladders side by side, up to a width of ten, it is not considered an entrance. And if it has two entrances and there is a refuse heap (ashpah) in front of one of them, it is as if there were only one entrance there.
What this seif says:
  • One entrance and a ladder: no shiyur: עיר של רבים שיש לו פתח א׳ וסולם במקום אחר מערבין את כולה ואינה צריכ׳ שיור שאין הסולם שבחומה חשוב כפתח. The ladder is on the opposite side, where one climbs up and down outside the wall; one might have thought it counts as an entrance (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ד).
  • Even many ladders side by side: ואפילו העמיד הרבה סולמות זה בצד זה עד רוחב עשרה לא חשיב כפתח. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ט״ז) writes that this is a scribal error, and one must read "up to more than ten"; and the same applies even if the ladders cover the whole width of the wall.
  • Two entrances, and a refuse heap in front of one: ואם יש לה שני פתחים ויש אשפה לפני אחד מהם כאילו אין שם אלא פתח א׳. The refuse heap blocks the place of the entrance, and the public does not pass there; and only a public refuse heap, which is not likely to be removed, but not that of an individual (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ז); if the refuse heap was cleared, the entrance returns to its place (ס״ק י״ח).

Seif ג

הבתים שמניחים אותם שיור אע״פ שאינם פתוחים לעיר אלא אחוריהם לעיר ופניהם לחוץ ואפילו הוא (רק בית אחד אפי') בית הבקר או בית התבן שאינם צריכים לערב עושים אותם שיור ומערבין את השאר:
Translation: The houses that are left as a shiyur: even though they are not open to the city, but have their backs to the city and their fronts outward, and even if it is — (only one house, even) [a parenthesis of the printed text] — a cattle barn or a straw barn, which do not need an eruv, one makes them the shiyur, and one makes the eruv for the rest.
What this seif says:
  • Houses facing outward can be the shiyur: הבתים שמניחים אותם שיור אע״פ שאינם פתוחים לעיר אלא אחוריהם לעיר ופניהם לחוץ. One might have thought, the Mishnah Berurah explains (ס״ק י״ט), that since they could not in any case join with the people of the city, they are not counted among them, and their shiyur does not work.
  • Even a cattle barn or a straw barn: בית הבקר או בית התבן שאינם צריכים לערב עושים אותם שיור ומערבין את השאר. The printed text carries before these words, in small type and in parentheses, without attribution: "רק בית אחד אפי׳". On "which do not need an eruv", the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ) writes that siman 370, where it is ruled that they do need one, refers to where one also eats there, as the Beis Yosef writes.

Seif ד

עיר שנשתתפו כל יושביה חוץ במבוי א' הרי זה אוסר על כולם ואם בנו מצבה על פתח המבוי אינו אוסר עליהם לפיכך עיר שהיתה קנין יחיד אפילו נעשי' של רבים אין מערבין אותה לחצאין אלא או כולה או מבוי ומבוי ובונה כל מבוי ומבוי מצבה על פתחו אם רצה לחלוק רשותו מהם כדי שלא יאסור על שאר המבואו':
Translation: A city all of whose inhabitants joined, except for one alley: it [the alley] forbids to all of them. And if they built a monument (matzeivah) at the entrance of the alley, it does not forbid to them. Therefore a city that was the acquisition of an individual, even if it became [the city] of many, one does not make the eruv for it in halves, but either all of it or alley by alley; and one builds, for each alley, a monument at its entrance, if he wants to separate his domain from theirs, so as not to forbid to the other alleys.
What this seif says:
  • An alley that does not join forbids to the whole city: עיר שנשתתפו כל יושביה חוץ במבוי א׳ הרי זה אוסר על כולם. According to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״א), this deals with a city that was at first the acquisition of one, as the seif says further on: seif א taught that one makes a single shituf in it without a shiyur, and this one teaches that it must be a single shituf specifically, and that division makes them forbid one another.
  • A monument at the entrance of the alley: ואם בנו מצבה על פתח המבוי אינו אוסר עליהם. For by this he shows that he withdraws from the city (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ג). On the dimensions of the monument, the commentators differ (Level 2, section 6).
  • Therefore: no eruv in halves, but the whole city or alley by alley: לפיכך עיר שהיתה קנין יחיד אפילו נעשי׳ של רבים אין מערבין אותה לחצאין אלא או כולה או מבוי ומבוי ובונה כל מבוי ומבוי מצבה על פתחו אם רצה לחלוק רשותו מהם כדי שלא יאסור על שאר המבואו׳. The reason, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ה): since it was at first the acquisition of an individual, they grew used to being together, are like one courtyard, and forbid one another. Seif ה says in which case this rule is stated; seif ו, what separates. On its scope — along the length of the city, or across its width as well —, the commentators differ (section 6; Level 2).

Seif ה

במה דברים אמורי' שאין מערבין אותה לחצאין בעיר מוקפת חומה גבוה י״ט ויש לה דלתו' אבל אם לא היתה העיר כולה מוכשרת במחיצו' ובאו להכשיר חציה ולערבה הרשו' בידם:
Translation: In what case was it said that one does not make the eruv for it in halves? In a city surrounded by a wall ten tefachim high and which has doors; but if the whole city was not made fit by partitions, and they came to make half of it fit and make the eruv for it, they may do so.
What this seif says:
  • The rule refers to a city surrounded by a wall ten high, which has doors: במה דברים אמורי׳ שאין מערבין אותה לחצאין בעיר מוקפת חומה גבוה י״ט ויש לה דלתו׳. The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ז) writes that the seif mentions doors because it speaks of the city that became the city of many, which is a public domain, permitted only with doors (siman 364, seif ב); and the same applies, it writes, to a city that is not a public domain by law and that a doorway form permits: since one makes all of it fit by such partitions, one does not divide its shituf into halves.
  • A city that partitions did not make wholly fit: one may make half of it fit and make the eruv for it: אבל אם לא היתה העיר כולה מוכשרת במחיצו׳ ובאו להכשיר חציה ולערבה הרשו׳ בידם. That is, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק כ״ח), making half of it fit by the repairs of the partitions and making its eruv by a shituf, leaving the rest with no repair at all.

Seif ו

זה שאמרנו שאין מערבין אותה לחצאין היינו לומר שאין לחי וקורה מועיל לסלקם זה מזה אבל בפס ארבעה או בשני פסים שני משהויין נחלקי' אלו מאלו ומערבין לחצאין ואם הוא רחב מעשר אמות עושה צורת פתח: הגה או יעשה מחיצה גבוה עשרה לפתח מבואו (טור) י״א הא דעיר של יחיד אסור לחלק היינו לארכה מאחר דשניהם צריכים לילך לרה״ר שבתוכה אבל לרחבה ואלו יוצאים בשער זה ואלו יוצאים בשער זה ואין להם דריסת הרגל זה על זה מערבין לחצאין ועושין תיקון ביניהם כמו שנתבאר לעיל סי' שס״ג דעד עשרה מהני לחי או קורה וביתר מעשרה צורת הפתח (הגהות מיימוני פ״ה מהלכות עירובין והרא״ש והטור):
Translation: What we said, that one does not make the eruv for it in halves, means that a side post (lechi) and a beam (korah) do not work to separate them from one another; but with a post (pas) of four, or with two posts of two mashehuyin, these are separated from those, and one makes the eruv in halves; and if it is wider than ten amos, one makes a doorway form. Gloss of the Rema: or one makes a partition ten high at the entrance of his alley [reference: טור]. Some say that [the rule that] it is forbidden to divide a city of an individual applies along its length, since both need to go to the public domain within it; but across its width, when these go out by this gate and those go out by that gate, and they have no right of passage (derisas haregel) over one another, one makes the eruv in halves, and one makes a repair between them, as explained above in siman 363: up to ten, a lechi or a korah works, and beyond ten, the doorway form [references: הגהות מיימוני פ״ה מהלכות עירובין והרא״ש והטור].
What this seif says:
  • A lechi and a korah do not separate: זה שאמרנו שאין מערבין אותה לחצאין היינו לומר שאין לחי וקורה מועיל לסלקם זה מזה.
  • A post of four, or two posts, separate; beyond ten amos, a doorway form: אבל בפס ארבעה או בשני פסים שני משהויין נחלקי׳ אלו מאלו ומערבין לחצאין ואם הוא רחב מעשר אמות עושה צורת פתח.
  • Gloss of the Rema: or a partition ten high: או יעשה מחיצה גבוה עשרה לפתח מבואו.
  • Gloss of the Rema: according to some, across the width one makes the eruv in halves: י״א הא דעיר של יחיד אסור לחלק היינו לארכה מאחר דשניהם צריכים לילך לרה״ר שבתוכה אבל לרחבה ואלו יוצאים בשער זה ואלו יוצאים בשער זה ואין להם דריסת הרגל זה על זה מערבין לחצאין ועושין תיקון ביניהם כמו שנתבאר לעיל סי׳ שס״ג דעד עשרה מהני לחי או קורה וביתר מעשרה צורת הפתח. "Along its length": the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ל) explains that cities usually have their gates open straight through along their length, the public domain passing from gate to gate and connecting them all. On this view — its scope, and the lechi or korah it allows —, the commentators differ (section 6; Level 2).

Seif ז

עיר של רבים ונתמעטה ועמדה על חמשים דיורים א״צ שיור:
Translation: A city of many that diminished and came down to fifty residents (dayorim) does not need a shiyur.
What this seif says:
  • The city of many reduced to fifty residents: no shiyur: עיר של רבים ונתמעטה ועמדה על חמשים דיורים א״צ שיור. On a city that keeps fifty residents, see Level 2, section 9; consult your Rav.

Seif ח

המזכה בשיתוף לכל בני העיר אם עירבו כולם עירוב א' א״צ להודיעם שזכו' הוא להם ודין מי ששכח ולא נשתתף עם בני העיר או מי שהלך לשבו' בעיר אחרת או עכו״ם שהיה עמהם בעיר דין הכל כדינה בחצר ומבוי:
Translation: One who confers (mezakeh) the shituf on all the people of the city: if they all made a single eruv, he need not inform them, for it is a benefit (zechus) for them. And the law of one who forgot and did not join with the people of the city, or of one who went to spend Shabbos in another city, or of a non-Jew (akum) who was with them in the city: the law of all is as their law in the courtyard and the alley.
What this seif says:
  • Conferring the shituf without informing them, if they all made a single eruv: המזכה בשיתוף לכל בני העיר אם עירבו כולם עירוב א׳ א״צ להודיעם שזכו׳ הוא להם. "If they all made a single eruv" excludes, according to the Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ל״ד), the case where they made the eruv in halves: they have then shown that they do not want to be joined.
  • The one who forgot, the one who left, the non-Jew: as in the courtyard and the alley: ודין מי ששכח ולא נשתתף עם בני העיר או מי שהלך לשבו׳ בעיר אחרת או עכו״ם שהיה עמהם בעיר דין הכל כדינה בחצר ומבוי.

2. General context

What does this siman discuss?

Siman שצ״ב brings the eruv to the level of the city. It distinguishes the city that was the acquisition of an individual from the city of many, and says when one makes a single shituf in it and when one must leave a place out of it, the shiyur (seif א); what counts as an entrance (seif ב); which houses can be the shiyur (seif ג); the alley that does not join, and the eruv in halves (seif ד), in which case this rule is stated (seif ה) and what separates (seif ו); the city reduced to fifty residents (seif ז); one who confers the shituf, with a reference for the rest to the laws of the courtyard and the alley (seif ח).

The mishnah of tractate Eruvin teaches: « עִיר שֶׁל יָחִיד, וְנַעֲשֵׂית שֶׁל רַבִּים — מְעָרְבִין אֶת כּוּלָּהּ. וְשֶׁל רַבִּים וְנַעֲשֵׂית שֶׁל יָחִיד — אֵין מְעָרְבִין אֶת כּוּלָּהּ אֶלָּא אִם כֵּן עָשָׂה חוּצָה לָהּ. כָּעִיר חֲדָשָׁה שֶׁבִּיהוּדָה, שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהּ חֲמִשִּׁים דָּיוֹרִין, דִּבְרֵי רַבִּי יְהוּדָה. רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן אוֹמֵר: שָׁלֹשׁ חֲצֵירוֹת שֶׁל שְׁנֵי בָתִּים » (עירובין נ״ט ע״א). Seif א, for its part, writes that one leaves out « מקום א׳ אפילו חצר אחת ובית לתוכה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:א).

The fundamental question: when can a whole city join in a single shituf, and when must a place be left out of the shituf? Seif א answers according to whether the city was the acquisition of an individual or that of many, and whether it has one entrance or two; and it gives the reason: a sign, so that people know that it is the eruv that permits carrying in a city through which the many pass.

Place in Hilchos Eruvin

In Sefaria's index, the section הלכות עירובין of the Shulchan Aruch begins at siman 345. Siman שצ״ב follows siman שצ״א · 391, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דיני ביטול רשות למי ששכחו לערב » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״א:א), and precedes siman שצ״ג · 393, whose title, at the head of its seif א, is « דיני עירוב ביום טוב שחל בערב שבת ודין בין השמשו׳ לערב » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ג:א). Seif ח refers, for the one who forgot, the one who left and the non-Jew, to their law in the courtyard and the alley.

3. Key halachic concepts

A few notions structure this siman:

The essential notions of siman שצ״ב:
  • שיתוף — the shituf: seif א writes that all join « שיתוף א׳ » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:א); and « לערב », in that seif, means the shituf (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״א).
  • קנין יחיד / של רבים — the city acquired by an individual, and that of many: seif א distinguishes them, together with the number of entrances, to say when a shiyur is needed.
  • שיור — the shiyur: the place left out of the shituf, « אפילו חצר אחת ובית לתוכה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:א; on this measure, the commentators differ: Level 2, section 2); houses facing outward, and even a cattle barn or a straw barn, can be it (seif ג).
  • היכר — the sign: the reason for the shiyur, according to seif א, « כדי שידעו שהעירוב התיר להם לטלטל בעיר זה שרבים בוקעים בה » (שו״ע או״ח שצ״ב:א).
  • לחצאין — in halves: the city that was the acquisition of an individual, one does not make its eruv in halves (seif ד), in a city surrounded by a wall ten high and which has doors (seif ה).
  • מצבה, פס, צורת פתח — what separates: the monument at the entrance of the alley (seif ד); a post of four, or two posts, and beyond ten amos a doorway form (seif ו), whereas a lechi and a korah do not separate (seif ו).

4. Detail of the seifim — one by one

SeifContent
אThe city that was the acquisition of an individual, even if it became the city of many, and the city of many with a single entrance: all make a single shituf. The city of many with two entrances, through which people enter and go out, even if it became an individual's: one leaves a place out of it; those who joined are permitted in the whole city except that place, those who remained in their place with their own shituf; and, according to the printed text, if they are many, the rest of the city is forbidden to them (on the reading, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ח, in the name of the Taz). The reason: a sign. Making the eruv alley by alley works all the more so. Rema: between them, two posts if it is ten amos wide; beyond ten, a doorway form.
בA city of many with a single entrance, and a ladder elsewhere: a single eruv, without a shiyur, the ladder in the wall not being an entrance, even many ladders up to a width of ten (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ט״ז: read "more than ten"). Two entrances with a refuse heap in front of one: as a single entrance.
גThe houses left as a shiyur can face outward, and can even be a cattle barn or a straw barn, which do not need an eruv; one makes the eruv for the rest.
דThe whole city joined except one alley: it forbids to all, unless a monument was built at its entrance. Therefore the city that was the acquisition of an individual, even if it became the city of many, one does not make its eruv in halves: either all of it, or alley by alley, with a monument at the entrance of each alley — in a city surrounded by a wall ten high and which has doors (seif ה).
הThis rule is stated for a city surrounded by a wall ten tefachim high and which has doors; if the city was not made wholly fit by partitions, one may make half of it fit and make the eruv for it.
ו"Not in halves" means that a lechi and a korah do not separate; a post of four or two posts separate, and beyond ten amos a doorway form. Rema: or a partition ten high at the entrance of the alley; and according to some, the prohibition to divide applies along the length, but across the width, when each part goes out by its own gate with no right of passage over the other, one makes the eruv in halves, with a repair between them (siman 363); on this view, the commentators differ (section 6).
זThe city of many reduced to fifty residents does not need a shiyur (on a city that keeps fifty residents: Level 2, section 9).
חOne who confers the shituf on all the people of the city need not inform them, if they all made a single eruv. The one who forgot, the one who went to spend Shabbos elsewhere, the non-Jew: as their law in the courtyard and the alley.
The heart of the siman: a city of many with two entrances, through which people enter and go out, does not join entirely in a single shituf: one leaves a place out of it, so that people know that it is the eruv that permits carrying in a city through which the many pass (seif א) — unless it was reduced to fifty residents (seif ז; on a city that keeps fifty residents, see Level 2, section 9). Conversely, the city that was the acquisition of an individual makes a single shituf, and one does not make its eruv in halves (seifim א and ד), in a city surrounded by a wall ten high and which has doors (seif ה), unless one separates it by what separates (seif ו); on the view the Rema brings in seif ו, the commentators differ (section 6).

6. Position of the Rema

In this siman, glosses of the Rema (Rabbi Moshe Isserles, הגה) are introduced by הגה in seifim א and ו. The parenthesis in small type in seif ג is not introduced by הגה, and this page attributes it to no one.

The glosses of the Rema:
  • Seif א — « וצריך שיעשו ביניהם שני פסין של שני משהויין אם הוא רחב עשר אמות וביתר מי׳ צריך צורת פתח » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ב:א). The Mishnah Berurah brings from the Acharonim that it refers to the case where, within the alley itself, a house and a courtyard were left out, and not to the eruv alley by alley (ס״ק י״ג; see the box of seif א).
  • Seif ו, the partition — « או יעשה מחיצה גבוה עשרה לפתח מבואו » (רמ״א או״ח שצ״ב:ו).
  • Seif ו, the view brought with "י״א" — according to some, the prohibition to divide the city of an individual applies along its length; across its width, when each part goes out by its own gate with no right of passage over the other, one makes the eruv in halves, with a repair between them: up to ten, a lechi or a korah, beyond ten, the doorway form (siman 363). On this view — its scope, its relation to seifim ד and ו of the Mechaber, and the lechi or korah it allows —, the commentators differ: see Level 2.
  • Chabad → Level 4 — Daat HaRav studies this siman in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, siman שצ״ב, under its own numbering, in 9 seifim.

7. Modern practical cases

Each situation below ties a case to a named seif; when the situation is a present-day one, the row says so: an application by the site, and not the words of the source.

SituationQuick analysis
The people of a city all want to join in a single shitufSeif א: the city that was the acquisition of an individual, or the city of many with a single entrance, makes a single shituf; the city of many with two entrances, through which people enter and go out, leaves a place out of it — unless it was reduced to fifty residents (seif ז; on a city that keeps fifty residents, see Level 2, section 9). The Mishnah Berurah (ס״ק ז) reports, in the name of the Magen Avraham, that the common practice is not to be careful about this. An application by the site: whether a present-day city is a "city of many" in the sense of the seif, and what applies to it, is for your Rav to say.
A city has two entrances, but a refuse heap blocks one of themSeif ב: it is as if there were only one entrance. Only a public refuse heap, which is not likely to be removed, and not that of an individual (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ז); if it is cleared, the entrance returns (ס״ק י״ח). Consult your Rav.
Part of the city wants to make its own shitufSeifim ד to ו: in a city that was the acquisition of an individual, one does not make the eruv in halves, but the whole city or alley by alley, with a monument at the entrance of each alley — a rule stated for a city surrounded by a wall ten high and which has doors (seif ה); a lechi and a korah do not separate, a post of four or two posts separate, and beyond ten amos a doorway form (seif ו). On the view the Rema brings in seif ו, the commentators differ (section 6). Consult your Rav.
A resident forgot to join, or spends Shabbos in another citySeif ח: his law is as in the courtyard and the alley. Consult your Rav.
For precise practical cases: the seifim make the rule depend, among other things, on whether the city was the acquisition of an individual or that of many, and on the number of its entrances (seifim א and ב), on the number of its residents (seif ז), on the wall and the doors (seif ה), and on what separates one part from another (seifim ד and ו). For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

8. Practical synthesis of the Siman

The teachings of Siman שצ״ב:
  1. The city that was the acquisition of an individual, even if it became the city of many, and the city of many with a single entrance: all make a single shituf (seif א).
  2. The city of many with two entrances, through which people enter and go out, even if it became an individual's: one leaves a place out of it, as a sign; those who joined are permitted in the whole city except that place; those who remained, in their place, with their own shituf (seif א; on the reading, Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ח).
  3. The ladder is not an entrance; a refuse heap in front of one of the two entrances makes it count as absent (seif ב).
  4. The shiyur can be houses facing outward, and even a cattle barn or a straw barn (seif ג).
  5. The alley that does not join forbids to the whole city, unless a monument was built at its entrance; the city that was the acquisition of an individual is not done in halves, but whole or alley by alley, with a monument at the entrance of each alley (seif ד) — in a city surrounded by a wall ten high and which has doors (seif ה).
  6. What separates: not a lechi or a korah, but a post of four or two posts, and beyond ten amos a doorway form (seif ו); on the view the Rema brings, the commentators differ (section 6).
  7. The city reduced to fifty residents does not need a shiyur (seif ז; on a city that keeps fifty residents, see Level 2, section 9; consult your Rav).
  8. One who confers the shituf on all need not inform them, if they all made a single eruv; the one who forgot, the one who left and the non-Jew follow the laws of the courtyard and the alley (seif ח).
  9. For halachah lema'aseh, consult your Rav.

9. Comprehension questions

Check your understanding:
  1. Why does the city that was the acquisition of an individual make a single shituf, and why specifically one (seif א; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ג and ס״ק כ״א)?
  2. What is the reason for the shiyur according to seif א, and what is feared without it (Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י)?
  3. Why does a ladder not prevent the city from having a single entrance, and which refuse heap counts (seif ב; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק י״ז)?
  4. Why does a monument at the entrance of the alley prevent it from forbidding to the city (seif ד; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק כ״ג)?
  5. In which city was it said that one does not make the eruv in halves, and what does the rule mean (seifim ה and ו)?
  6. Why does one who confers the shituf not have to inform the people of the city, and when is this not so (seif ח; Mishnah Berurah, ס״ק ל״ד)?
