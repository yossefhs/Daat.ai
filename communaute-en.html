Why study with a partner?

The Talmud (Taanit 7a) compares the study of Torah to fire: "Just as fire does not burn alone, so too Torah is not sustained in one who learns alone." The khavroutha — paired study — is the quintessential mode of halakhic transmission.

In the DAAT community, the goal is simple: to allow every student to find a partner matched to their level and availability. Whether you are learning the Shulchan Arukh for the first time or looking for a pilpul partner, the team will match you with a compatible student.
